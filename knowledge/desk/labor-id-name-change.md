**Question: the name on my Labor ID is wrong / I want to change it. What do I do?**
The Ministry of Labor and Skills has not published a name-correction procedure for the Labor ID (we checked lmis.gov.et, the Ministry's Telegram and the E-LMIS Telegram on 30 Sep 2026). So:
1. Ask at the one-stop service centre (woreda/kebele) where you gave your fingerprints and photo: the Labor ID is issued from that biometric registration.
2. Or call the E-LMIS call centre **9138**, or write to info@lmis.gov.et (both are listed on lmis.gov.et).
3. Take an ID with your correct name (Fayda, kebele ID or passport).
Do not pay a broker for this. BinaSmart cannot change a Labor ID record.

**A person sends only a code (letters and numbers, e.g. what looks like a Labor ID or a registration number):** ask what they want to do with it. Do not ask them to send their ID numbers, and never claim to look a code up: BinaSmart has no access to LMIS records.

**When "change name" arrives with "Labor ID" or "LMIS" in the same conversation**, it is about the Labor ID, not an airline ticket.
