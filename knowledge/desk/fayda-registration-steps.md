# ፋይዳ መታወቂያ (Fayda / National ID) — እንዴት ይወጣል?

**ምዝገባው ነፃ ነው።** በኢትዮጵያ የሚኖሩ ዜጎችና ሕጋዊ ነዋሪዎች ይመዘገባሉ። ኢትዮ ቴሌኮም እንደሚለው **ከ5 ዓመት በላይ** የሆኑ ልጆች መመዝገብ ይችላሉ፤ ከ5 ዓመት በታች ላሉት ልዩ
የምዝገባ ፕሮግራም «በቅርቡ» እንደሚጀመር ገልጿል። ባዮሜትሪክ መስጠት ለማይችሉ ልጆች የአሳዳጊያቸው የፋይዳ ቁጥር በመገለጫቸው ይመዘገባል (id.gov.et)።

2. **ሰነድ ይዘው ይሂዱ** — የሚያገለግል መታወቂያ ወይም የነዋሪነት ማረጋገጫ (እንደ ሁኔታው ፓስፖርት፣ የነዋሪነት ፈቃድ ወይም የአካባቢ
   መታወቂያ)። **መደበኛ ሰነድ ከሌለዎት** በፋይዳ የተመዘገበ ሰው እንደ ምስክር («Introducer») ይዘው መመዝገብ ይችላሉ።
3. **ባዮሜትሪክ ይስጡ** — በአካል ቀርበው የጣት አሻራ፣ የዓይን (አይሪስ) ቅኝትና የፊት ፎቶ ይሰጣሉ፤ ከነመረጃዎትም ጋር።
4. **FIN ይደርስዎታል** — ከማጣራትና ድግግሞሽ ከማስወገድ በኋላ የ**12 አኃዝ** የፋይዳ መለያ ቁጥርዎ በኤስኤምኤስ በነፃ ወደ ስልክዎ

5. **ዲጂታል ወይም የታተመ ካርድ** — ዲጂታሉን በቴሌብር ሚኒ አፕ ማየት ይችላሉ። የታተመ ካርድ በid.gov.et/cardprint ላይ ከ**ኢትዮ ቴሌኮም**

## Never say this

Do not write "በጣም ቀላል ነው" — it is not our place to tell a person a government process will be easy. Give
the steps, the documents and the fee, and let them decide. If it turns out to be hard for them, a
sentence like that makes the whole answer look dishonest.

## In English

Registration is free for citizens and legal residents living in Ethiopia. Children above the age of 5 can register; Ethio
telecom says a special programme for children under 5 will be rolled out soon. For minors who cannot give biometrics, the
guardian's Fayda number is recorded on their profile (id.gov.et). Find an approved centre (an Ethio telecom centre or a
partner site; "Fayda Near Me" on id.gov.et); bring valid identification or proof of residence — passport, resident permit
or local ID depending on status — and if you have no standard document, bring a Fayda-registered witness ("Introducer");
attend in person to give fingerprints, an iris scan and a facial photograph with your details. The 12-digit Fayda
Identification Number (FIN) arrives free by SMS from "9779" or "National ID Ethiopia" after processing and deduplication,
within hours or a few days (Ethio telecom). The digital ID is in the telebirr mini app. A printed card is ordered at
id.gov.et/cardprint from ONE provider, Ethio telecom or Ethiopost (ordering from both blocks it); it costs money, but no
price is published there.

Help: if your Fayda number did not arrive, you lost it, or the digital ID does not show in telebirr, Ethio telecom says to
dial 9779 or visit id.et/help. (The owner's 2026-09-20 note said *9779#; the official instruction is "dial 9779".)
NIDP support: info@id.et, +251 113 720 006.
