# TIN registration and CBE account opening — what to bring

## TIN (Taxpayer Identification Number)

The documents differ for a person and for a business, and answering with one list for both is the mistake
this note exists to fix.

**An individual** needs a valid national identity card **or** a passport.

**A business** needs:

- the business registration certificate
- the trade licence
- the memorandum and articles of association (for a company)
- the identity document of the owner or manager

### በአማርኛ — TIN ለማውጣት ምን ያስፈልጋል?

የግለሰብና የድርጅት TIN የሚጠይቀው ሰነድ **አንድ አይደለም**። ሁለቱን ለያይቶ መመለስ ያስፈልጋል።

**ለግለሰብ TIN ለማውጣት፦**

- የሚያገለግል (የታደሰ) ብሔራዊ መታወቂያ **ወይም** ፓስፖርት

**ለድርጅት TIN ለማውጣት፦**

- የንግድ ምዝገባ ምስክር ወረቀት
- የንግድ ፈቃድ
- የመመሥረቻ ጽሑፍና መተዳደሪያ ደንብ (ለኩባንያ)
- የባለቤቱ ወይም የሥራ አስኪያጁ መታወቂያ

TIN ማውጣት ራሱ ክፍያ የለውም።

Registering for a TIN is itself free.

## Commercial Bank of Ethiopia — opening an account

**This is not the same thing as CBE Birr.** CBE Birr is the mobile wallet, opened with a phone number at
a branch or an agent. What follows is opening a bank **account**. When someone asks about "CBE Birr",
answer about the wallet; when they ask about an account, answer with this. If it is not clear which they
mean, ask.

### A local account

- **Age:** 18 or older
- **Identity:** a renewed national identity card, or a kebele ID
- **Photographs:** two recent passport-size photographs
- **Opening deposit:** a minimum balance, and some accounts open at zero with 15 days to fund them

### A diaspora or non-resident account

- **Who:** an Ethiopian citizen living abroad, a person of Ethiopian origin, or a foreign national with
  valid credentials
- **Identity:** a renewed passport, and the Yellow Card where it applies
- **Proof of income:** an employment letter, a tax return, or business financial statements

### The two ways to open it

**Online** — through the official CBE documents portal: upload the identity documents, complete the
remote verification, and sign the onboarding forms digitally.

**At a branch** — walk into any Commercial Bank of Ethiopia branch, fill in the paper application,
hand over the copies of the identity documents and the photographs, and deposit the opening amount to
activate the account.
