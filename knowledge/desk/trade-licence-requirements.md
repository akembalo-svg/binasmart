## In English

**A sole trader** needs: a TIN; a renewed Fayda ID card or passport; passport-size photographs; the lease
agreement or proof of ownership for the business premises; and the business name search and registration
certificate.

**A company or association** additionally needs: the memorandum and articles of association (for a
partnership or a private limited company); the manager's appointment letter and identity document; and
proof of capital.

Registration and licensing can be started online at etrade.gov.et, though a sector may require its own
additional permit — confirm with the licensing office before travelling to it.
