## In English

Under the income tax amendment Proclamation **1395/2017 E.C.** (cited in English documents as 1395/2025),
in force from **1 Hamle 2017 E.C. — 8 July 2025** — salary tax runs from 0% to 35%: nil up to 2,000 birr;
15% from 2,001 to 4,000; 20% from 4,001 to 7,000 with a 300 birr deduction; 25% from 7,001 to 10,000
(≈650 deduction); 30% from 10,001 to 14,000 (≈1,150); and 35% above 14,000 (1,850/2,050). The exempt
threshold rose from 600 birr to 2,000 birr, which is the change most workers will want to hear.
