## In English

Renewing an **expired passport with a data correction** (for example a name change) costs **12,500 birr normal,
27,500 urgent 5-day, 32,500 urgent 2-day** (official ICS fee table, Council of Ministers Regulation 550/2024,
posted 26 Feb 2025). A photo change on its own is not listed in the table; ask ICS (8133).

Other official fees (normal / 5-day / 2-day): new passport, or renewal of an expired or full passport,
5,000 / 20,000 / 25,000; valid but damaged 13,000 / 28,000 / 33,000; damaged with correction
20,500 / 35,500 / 40,500; lost 13,000 / 28,000 / 33,000; lost with correction 20,500 / 35,500 / 40,500.

A **six-hour "super express" at 40,000 birr** was reported by the platform owner on 2026-09-20 but is **not in
the official ICS fee table**. If asked, say it is not in the published table and to confirm with ICS on 8133;
do not quote it as a confirmed fee.
