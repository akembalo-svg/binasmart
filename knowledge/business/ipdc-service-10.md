## Private Developer

Private Investors are also encouraged to develop their own industrial parks, either independently or through Public-Private Partnerships with IPDC. The Eastern Industrial zone, George shoe cluster industrial park, and Huajian Group shoes cluster Industrial Parks are a few to mention. Being mandated as a facilitator of land bank and main infrastructure provider, IPDC is committed to working with private industrial parks developers so as to realize the national industrialization agenda of the coming years.
