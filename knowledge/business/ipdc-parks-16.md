## Addis Industry Village

Addis Industrial Village is established in 1980s at heart of Ethiopia's capital city, Addis Ababa. The pioneer industrial village in the country has 10 government-built factory buildings rented to investors, and 9 privately-built factory buildings developed on leased land. Covering eight hectares of land, it is an ideal place to manfacture fast moving consumer producst. It has acess to international airport and rail way.
