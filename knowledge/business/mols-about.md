## Profile

### The Ministry of labour and Skills was established Following the National Reform, the Ministry of Labor and Skills was founded with Proclamation No. 1263/2022, which oversees national job creation, skills development, and labor concerns.

## Vision

Aspires to see Ethiopia labor market demand and supply harmonized, and access to descent work by 2030.

## Mission

Ensure equitable access to sustainable employment opportunities through demand responsive skill development.
