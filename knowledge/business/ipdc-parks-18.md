## Arerti

Arerti Industrial Park is located in the North Shoa Zone of the Amhara Regional State town called Arerti. The park is located 130 kilo meter away from Ethiopia's capital city, Addis Ababa. The park has started operations with investors engaged in the production of textiles, ceramics, and woodwork. It has serviced land waiting for willing and able to invest. (Invest here)
