Message From the
President

Welcome to the official website of the Ethiopian Chamber of Commerce and Sectoral Associations (ECCSA), a platform dedicated to fostering economic growth, trade, and investment in Ethiopia.

For decades, ECCSA has played a pivotal role in representing and advocating for the Ethiopian business community. Our chamber’s historical journey reflects a commitment to strengthening the private sector, facilitating business networking, and influencing policies that create a conducive environment for economic development.

Years Of Experience

ECCSA traces its roots back to 1947, when Ethiopia’s first chamber of commerce was established under General Notice No. 90/1947, operating on a voluntary membership basis. Over the decades, ECCSA has evolved in response to changing economic policies
