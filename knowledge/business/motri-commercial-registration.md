# Commercial Registration

1
- Commercial Registration

Description Customers must have a business registration certificate before requesting any services

Requirement: Certificate of Tax Identification Number (TIN);

Original and necessary copies of valid identity card or passport of the applicant;

where the application is submitted by an attorney; original and copy of power of attorney given by all of the founders, copies of valid identity card or passport of the attorney and the manager;'

certificate of proof of legal existence of the business organization;

approved resolution minute or similar document showing that the business organization has decided to open branch office in Ethiopia and assign a manager;

support letter of registration requirement;

Processing Time: Online

2
- Commercial Registration Amendment

Description Customers can alter or amend their commercial registration

Requirement: Where the amendment is to the memorandum and articles of association, minutes of resolution of the share holders of the business organization to make the amendment; the minutes of resolution of the share holders of the business organization

Where a new member is a business organization its original copies of memorandum and article of association, certificate of incorporation or similar document, a notarized minutes of resolution or letter of its decision by legally authorized body in Ethiopia to join the business organization passed by its authorized organ, if it is a foreign company, investment license or authorization letter issued to buy shares of an existing company

Bank proof If the amendment is capital and in cash

Necessary copies of valid identity card or passport, If it is manager amendment

Audit report

Processing Time: Online

3
- Commercial Registration Cancellation

Description Any business person whose certificate of commercial registration is lost or damaged may obtain a substitute certificate

Requirement: Evidence showing return of previously issued business license and certificate of Trade name.

The resolution minutes of cancellation decision

Audit report

Processing Time: Online
