## Adama

Adama Special Economic Zone is located in Adama city which is 74 kilo meter away to the south east of Ethiopia's capital city, Addis Ababa. The park started operations in October 2018 with 19 factory sheds that lies over 120 hectares of land. The factory sheds are already rented out to different investors from Japan, China & Ethiopia. It specializes in Machinery, Apparel, and Garment.
