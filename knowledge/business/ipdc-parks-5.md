## Bahir-Dar

Bahir-Dar Special Economic Zone is located in the Amahara Regional Sate capital city,Bahir-Dar. It is 560 km from Ethiopia's capital city, Addis Ababa. The park started operations in 2020 with 8 factory sheds that lies over 75 hectares. It has access to domestic airport.
