Addis Ababa Chamber of Commerce and Sectoral Associations (AACCSA)

Trade and International Business Services

AACCSA promotes domestic, regional, and international trade by connecting Ethiopian businesses with global markets, facilitating investment, and supporting export competitiveness.

1. Trade Missions

AACCSA organizes and coordinates business delegations to local and international markets.

Services include:

• Outbound trade missions.

• Inbound business delegations.

• Business networking events.

• Company visits.

• Government and institutional meetings.

• Investment forums.

• Market exploration visits.

• Trade partnership facilitation.

• Memoranda of Understanding (MoU) support.

• Follow up business matchmaking.

2. Export Promotion

AACCSA helps businesses expand into international markets.

Services include:

• Export readiness assessment.

• Export market information.

• Product promotion.

• Export regulations guidance.

• Buyer identification.

• Trade documentation guidance.

• Export training.

• Packaging and labeling information.

• Quality standards awareness.

• Trade finance information.

• Logistics and shipping information.

• Market entry support.

3. Import Support

AACCSA provides information to businesses importing goods and services.

Support includes:

• Import procedures.

• Customs information.

• Trade regulations.

• Import documentation.

• Supplier identification.

• International sourcing.

• Trade finance information.

• Risk management guidance.

• International standards awareness.

4. African Continental Free Trade Area (AfCFTA)

AACCSA promotes opportunities under the African Continental Free Trade Area.

Services include:

• AfCFTA awareness programs.

• Rules of Origin guidance.

• Market access information.

• Tariff reduction information.

• Cross border trade opportunities.

• Trade policy updates.

• Capacity building.

• Business advisory services.

• Networking with African partners.

• Regional trade promotion.

5. Business to Business (B2B) Matchmaking

AACCSA facilitates business partnerships between local and international companies.

Services include:

• Buyer and seller meetings.

• Supplier matching.

• Distributor identification.

• Investor matchmaking.

• Strategic partnership development.

• Joint venture facilitation.

• Franchise opportunities.

• Technology transfer partnerships.

• Digital B2B meetings.

6. International Exhibitions and Trade Fairs

AACCSA supports participation in trade fairs and exhibitions.

Services include:

• Sector specific exhibitions.

• Exhibition coordination.
