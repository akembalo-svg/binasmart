## Jimma

Jimma Special Economic Zone is located in Oromia Regional State city called Jimma. It is 352 kilometer away from Ethiopia's capital city,Addis Ababa, to the west of the country. It has started operations in 2019 with 9 factory sheds that lies over 75 hectares. So far four factory sheds are rented out to investors from india and Ethiopia. It has access to a domestic airport.
