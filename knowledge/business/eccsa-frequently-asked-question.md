Ask Us What you want to know from us

What is ECCSA?
The Ethiopian Chamber of Commerce and Sectoral Associations (ECCSA) is a 75-year-old umbrella organization that represents the private sector in Ethiopia. It facilitates public-private dialogue, promotes trade, and supports business development.

Where is ECCSA located?
ECCSA is located at Mexico Square, Chamber Building, Addis Ababa, Ethiopia.

What are the main objectives of ECCSA?
• Advocate for a favorable business environment.

• Promote trade and investment.

• Provide capacity-building support to members.

• Foster public-private dialogue.

Who can become a member of ECCSA?
ECCSA welcomes businesses, sectoral associations, and regional chambers of commerce to become members.

What services does ECCSA provide?
ECCSA offers policy advocacy, business networking, capacity-building training, trade fair organization, dispute resolution, and certification services, among others.

How can I participate in ECCSA’s training programs?
ECCSA organizes regular training sessions. Check the Events section on the website or contact the office for schedules.

Does ECCSA help businesses find market opportunities?
Yes, ECCSA facilitates local and international market linkages, organizes trade fairs, and promotes business partnerships.

How often does ECCSA organize trade fairs?
ECCSA hosts multiple trade fairs annually, including the Ethio Chamber International Trade Fair, to promote Ethiopian products and services.

How can I exhibit at ECCSA trade fairs?
Businesses can apply online via the ECCSA website or visit the office for registration.

Are trade fair opportunities open to non-members?
Yes, non-members can also participate, but members receive discounted rates and priority services.

How does ECCSA advocate for businesses?
ECCSA collects feedback from the private sector, conducts research, and engages in dialogue with government authorities to influence policies favorable to businesses.

How can businesses share concerns with ECCSA?
Members can submit their issues through direct contact, email, or regional chamber representatives.

What certification services does ECCSA offer?
ECCSA issues Certificates of Origin and other trade-related certifications.

How do I apply for a Certificate of Origin?
Visit ECCSA’s office with the required documents or apply online Via https://esw.et/esw-trd/

Does ECCSA offer dispute resolution services?
Yes, ECCSA provides arbitration and mediation services to resolve business disputes.

How can I contact ECCSA?
• Phone: +251-115-514005

• Email: info@ethiopianchamber.com

• Telegram: t.me/ethiopiachamber

What are ECCSA’s working hours?
ECCSA operates Monday to Friday, from 8:30 AM to 5:00 PM (local time).

Does ECCSA offer support for women entrepreneurs?
Yes, ECCSA actively supports women entrepreneurs through tailored training programs, networking opportunities, and access to funding.
