## Mekelle

Mekelle Special Economic Zone is located in the capital city of Tigray Regional State, Mekelle. It is located 760 kilo meter from Ethiopia's capital, Addis Ababa. The park started operations in 2017 with 15 factory sheds that lies over 75 hectares of land. It has access to an International airport.
