## Kombolcha

Kombolcha Special Economic Zone is located in Amhara Regional State's Kombolcha city. It is 380 kilometer away from Ethiopia's capital city, Addis Ababa, to the north east part of the country. The park started operations in 2017,with 9 factory sheds that lies over 75 hectares. Eight factory shed are already rented out to investors from the USA,Turkey, China, South Korea, and Italy. It has access to a domestic airport.
