## Bole Lemi

Bole Lemi Special Economic Zone

Bole Lemi Phase is located in Ethiopia's capital city,Addis Ababa. It is the firts industrial park in Ethiopia. The park has started operations in 2014 with 20 factory sheds that lies over 353 hectares of land currently rented out to different investors from India, China, and South Korea. It has ample developd land that awaits willing and able investors. It is an ideal place to invest.
