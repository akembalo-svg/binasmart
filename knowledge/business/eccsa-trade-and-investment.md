In every part of the world building reliable and credible business networks is not an easy task. This is why the Chamber steps in to fill such gaps for the business communities so as to help them grow by creating business links with potential partners to bolster mutual benefits. Business communities can testify to a variety of opportunities that have come their way through international trade fairs organized by the chamber while networking at such events. Interaction and exchange of ideas is an essential ingredient to keeping up with the dynamic and ever changing face of the business scene and if one is to keep on track the most appropriate mode would be to take advantage of the networking platforms that the chamber offers to its members. The network is being created by organizing and participating in trade fair, exchange of delegation, B2B, among others.

As part of ECCSA’s Core functions,
The trade Facilitation Division supports the private sector particularly Exporters relating to export trade. The Division work with exporters, whether first-time or experienced, to identify their opportunities and to solve their challenges. Moreover the Trade Facilitation Division supports the simpliﬁcation and associated information ﬂows required to move goods from seller to buyer. The Division desire to improve the trade environment and reduce transaction cost of exporters. In order to achieve these activities, Chamber works closely with Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Customs Commission and other relevant organizations.
Major services offered:-

- Certificate of Origin and COMESA certificate of Origin
- Commercial Invoice
- Packing list
- Document authentication
- Recommendation letter to Embassy
- Recommendation letter to Ministry of Foreign Affairs
- Free sale certificate
- Information sharing to private sectors
- Information sharing to relevant organizations
