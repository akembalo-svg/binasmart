# How to transfer money using Dashen Mobile Plus

### Option 1: USSD *996#

Step 1: Dial *996#

Step 2: Select option number one (login) and enter PIN to login

Step 3: Choose option number one (transfer) from the listed service to transfer Money

Step 4: And select option number three *within Dashen Bank*

Step 5: Choose the sending/ source account “account from”

Step 6: Enter the recipient account number on the field

Step 7: Check the account name and insert small “s” to continue

Step 8: Enter payment amount with reference/narrative

Step 9: Check the recipient account name and insert 1 to submit a final payment

### Option 2: Dashen Mobile Plus (Application)

Step 1: Enter your username and password to login to the system

Step 2: Select transfers within Dashen to make a transaction

Step 3: Then select new account transfer

Step 4: Write the following necessary information on the blank page

- Recipient name

- Select sending/ source account

- Enter the recipient account number and check the name look up appear

- Enter the transfer amount

- Enter your and recipient reference to be appear on balance statement( if any)

- Finally choose among pay once, save & pay and save to make payments

Step 5: Finally; check the credited account number and recipient name again, the click on PAY butt

### Option 3: Dashen Web

Step 1: Enter your username and password to login to the system

Step 2: Select and click on payment and transfer from the page

Step 3: Choose transfer within Dashen from the given service items

Step 4: Select new account transfers and fill the following information on the field

- Recipient name

- Select sending/ source account

- Enter the recipient account number and check the name look up appear

- Enter the transfer amount

- Enter your and recipient reference to be appear on balance statement( if any)

Step 5: Finally, choose among pay once, save & pay and save to make payments
