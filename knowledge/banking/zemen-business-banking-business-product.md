Zemen’s Business Basic Account is designed to meet the foundational banking needs of businesses with simplicity and flexibility.
To open this account, an initial deposit of ETB 5,000 is required. While the account does not bear interest, it maintains an average monthly minimum balance requirement of ETB 25,000.
Account holders enjoy unlimited check-writing privileges and full access to our omni-channel banking platforms, including Internet Banking, ATM services, Mobile Banking, and more—ensuring convenient and seamless business transactions anytime, anywhere

Our Business Saving and Business Advantage Accounts are hybrid solutions that offer the flexibility of a current account.
This account requires an initial deposit of ETB 5,000, with an average daily balance requirement of ETB 100,000. A grace period of 10 days is provided from the date of account opening to meet the required balance. The account earns an interest rate of 7% per annum, calculated on the monthly minimum balance.
With this account, businesses benefit from the combined features of both savings and checking accounts—allowing them to earn interest while also ensuring the smooth processing of checks and other transactions. Clients also enjoy seamless access to our full suite of omni-channel banking platforms and digital banking services for enhanced operational efficiency

Zemen Bank’s Overdraft Protection offers peace of mind for customers who maintain both checking and savings accounts with us.
A customer who has a checking and a savings account has the option of using the savings account as an overdraft protection towards the checking account. If a customer writes a check on his/her checking account and it has an insufficient fund, then the available fund from the savings account may be used to cover the difference. There will be a fee of Birr 20 per transaction.
