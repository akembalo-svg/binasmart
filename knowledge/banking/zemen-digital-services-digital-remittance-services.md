## Digital Remittance Services

Zemen Bank has introduced a digital remittance service, developed in partnership with leading fintech companies and payment instrument issuers. This service allows individuals living abroad to easily send money to their loved ones in Ethiopia, using their MasterCard or Visa cards, all from the comfort of their home.
Our partnership with fintech companies and payment issuers delivers a cutting-edge solution for faster, more affordable, and simpler inbound remittances, ensuring a seamless experience for both senders and receivers.

## Key Features

Instantaneous Transfers: With the click of a button, funds are transferred in real-time, ensuring that your money reaches its destination instantly.

Global Reach: Thanks to our partnerships with VISA and MasterCard, this service is accessible through a wide network of financial institutions, enabling seamless transfers to Ethiopia from countries worldwide with VISA and MasterCard access.

Zero Fees: Enjoy transfers without worrying about hidden charges. No service charges or commissions. Recipients enjoy the benefit of receiving money with no deductions, ensuring they get the full amount sent, making it an affordable option.

User-Friendly: The platforms are designed with ease of use in mind, allowing users to send or receive money in just a few simple steps.
