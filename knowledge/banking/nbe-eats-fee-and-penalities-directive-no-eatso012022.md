EATS Fees and Penalties Directive No. EATSO/01/2022
Whereas, it is important to ensure the safety, efficiency, reliability and smooth operation of
Ethiopian Automated Transfer System (EATS); -
Whereas, it is essential that the National Bank has to improve its service quality with a view to -
satisfy its customers; '
Whereas, it is important to share costs related to EATS among participants; 5.
Whereas, it is important to ensure that all participants comply with rules and guidelines
governing EATS;
Now, therefore in accordance with Article 4(2)(d)/2/, 30, 34 and 37(2) of the National Payment
System Proclamation No. 718/2011, the National Bank of Ethiopia has issued this Directive.
1. Short title
This Directive may be cited as “EATS Fees-and Penalties Directive No. EATSO/01/2022”
3. Definitions

2.1. “Addis Ababa Check Clearing Office (ACCO)” means a clearing house located in
the National Bank where participants exchange physical checks during the operating
day;
2.2. “end of settlement cycle” means an EATS event where the daily EATS cycle is
ended and activities listed under Article 71 Nules are executed;

2.3. “EATS” means a clearing and settlement system that combines the functions of Real

Automated Clearing House (ACH) for bulk/low value payments;
2.4. “e-token” means a device in which digital certificate is configured by the National
Bank and enables the user to access EATS;
2.5. “low value payment” means payment instruction via credit transfers, direct debits,
and cheques) which can be submitted to EATS individually or in bulk;
2.6. “net settlement instruction (NSI)” means a payment instruction that determines the
net settlement position of two or more participants;
2.7.“operating day” means any day in which EATS is open for clearing and settlement
of transactions between participants according to the system rules;
2.8.“operating hours” means the hours in which the EATS is operational during the
operating day.
2.9. “participant” means a financial institution which, in accordance with EATS rules, is
making direct or indirect participation in the System;
2.10.“real-time gross settlement systems (RTGS)” means the system that processes real-
time settlement of payments, transfer instructions or other obligations individually on
a transaction-by-transaction basis, which includes customer to customer payment
(MT103), financial institution to financial institution (MT202), participant debit and
credit (MT204 & MT205);
2.11.“system” means EATS; |

3. Scope of the Directive
This Directive shall apply to all participants of EATS and ACCO.

4.2. A participant shall pay annual EATS membership fee of Birr 30,000 (Thirty

4.3. A participant sending RTGS transaction shall pay standard RTGS transaction fee of
Birr 50 (Fifty Birr) per transaction.

4.4. A participant sending low value payment shall pay standard bulk transaction fee of
Birr 5 (Five Birr) per item.

4.5. A participant sending a net settlement instruction (NSIJ) shall pay a net settlement
instruction processing fee of Birr 250 (Two Hundred Fifty Birr) per NSI.

4.6. A participant requesting for new EATS e-token certificate configuration shall pay e-
token certificate configuration fee of Birr 10,000 (Ten Thousand Birr) per e-token
certificate.

4.7. A participant requesting for EATS e-token certificate renewal or password reset
services shall pay EATS e-token certificate renewal or password reset fee of Birr
5,000 (Five Thousand Birr)

4.8. A participant requesting for EATS user password reset shall pay EATS user password
reset fee of Birr 20,000 (Twenty Thousand Birr) per user.

4.9, A participant participating in ACCO shall pay annual membership fee of Birr
100,000 (One Hundred Thousand Birr).

5. Incentives

5.1. A participant sending and settling RTGS and low value transactions before 10:30AM
of the operating day may be entitled to early hour settlement discount of 10% (ten
percent) of the standard fee per transaction.

5.2. A participant sending and settling more than 10,000 (Ten Thousand) RTGS
transactions and 50,000 (Fifty Thousand) low value payment items during the
operating day may be entitled to high volume payment instruction discount of 10%
(ten percent) of the standard fee per transaction.

6. Penalties

6.1. Unless and otherwise a specific financial penalty is provided for the violation of
article of the system rules a participant violating the System rules shall pay a penalty
of Birr 30,000 (Thirty Thousand Birr) pezwiglated rule.

6.2. The National Bank may apply its own highest short term interest rate in the absence
of overnight loan or deposit interest rate to calculate for the penalty stated under
Articles 85(2&3 ) and 102(d) of the system rules.

6.3. A participant sending RTGS transaction to the system without having sufficient fund
and if the transaction is cancelled the participant shall pay transaction cancellation
charge of Birr 30,000 (Thirty Thousand Birr) per cancelled transaction.

6.4. A participant delaying to credit the beneficiary’s account for more than 24 (Twenty
four) operating hours after receiving a payment instruction from EATS shall pay
transaction delaying charge of Birr 30,000 (Thirty Thousand Birr) per transaction.

6.5. A participant delaying to send a payment instruction to the EATS for more than 6
(six) operating hours after receiving payment instruction from customer shall pay
transaction delaying charge of Birr 20,000 (Twenty Thousand Birr) per day and
transaction.

6.6. A participant returning payment instruction due to unjustifiable and/or payment return
other than return reasons stipulated by the National Bank of Ethiopia shall pay a
penalty of Birr 30,000 (Thirty Thousand Birr) per transaction.

7, Surcharge

7.1. A participant sending RTGS transaction after 4:00 PM of the operating day shall pay
late hour settlement charge of 20% (twenty percent) of the standard transaction fee.

7.2. A participant requesting system operating hour extension shall pay operating hour
extension fee of Birr 10,000 (Ten Thousand Birr).

7.3. No participant shall send any transaction to the system during extended operating
hour, except a participant who requested the extension of operating hour or the

National Bank.
