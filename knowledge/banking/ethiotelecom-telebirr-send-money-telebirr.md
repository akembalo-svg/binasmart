Send and Receive Money

You can quickly send money to your family, friends or beloved ones anywhere whether they are users or non-users of telebirr service living in any corner of the country.

- Dial *127#Or login to telebirr mobile app
- Select ‘Send Money’ from the menu
- Enter/Select recipient’s phone number
- Enter amount you want to send
- Then Press/Click OK
- After confirming the amount and who you are sending to, press “Send” and enter PIN
- You will get confirmation message instantly for the transaction.
