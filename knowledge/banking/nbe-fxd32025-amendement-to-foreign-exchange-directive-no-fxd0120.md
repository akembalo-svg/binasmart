Amendment to Foreign Exchange Directive No. FXD/01/2024

Whereas, it has become necessary to further amend the Foreign Exchange Directive No.
FXD/01/2024 by improving the value limits set in the directive for foreign exchange
advance payments and travel abroad; expanding the utilization of foreign exchange funds
for traveller to incentivize foreign account holders and boost the country's foreign
currency earnings; and allowing outgoing family remittance payments to strengthen future
family relationships among Ethiopians living abroad, thereby ultimately enhancing
remittance inflows over time;
Now, therefore, in accordance with Articles 6(2 and 17), 39(3, 4 and 6), and 53(2) of the
National Bank of Ethiopia Proclamation No. 1359/2025, the National Bank of Ethiopia has
issued this Directive.
1. Short Title
This Directive may be cited as the “Foreign Exchange (Amendment) Directive No.
FXD/03/2025” ፤
2. Amendments
The Foreign Exchange Directive No. FXD/01/2025 is hereby amended as Follows:

1). Annex 2, sub-article 1.4.3(b) of the Directive is deleted and replaced by the following

new sub-article 1.4.3(b):

b. The amount to be approved for advance payment from one supplier shall not
exceed USD 50,000.00 (USD Fifty Thousand) or equivalent in other currencies. In
case of value exceeding USD 50,000.00 (USD Fifty Thousand) or equivalent in
other currencies, an importer should submit foreign bank guarantee confirmed
by local Authorized Banks.

2). Annex 4, sub-article 9.1 (paragraphs 1 and 2) of the Directive is deleted and replaced
by the following new sub-article 9,1(paragraphs 1 and 2): ሩፍ

1. Personal traveller is entitled to USD 10,000 (USD Ten thousand) or its equivalent
in other convertible currencies, which may be provided as cash notes or via a
debit card.
2. For personal traveller FCY Account holder, up to USD 10,000 (USD Ten
Thousand) or its equivalent in other convertible currencies may be taken in cash
notes or via a debit card. In addition, the account holder can take up to 20% of his
outstanding FCY Account balance via a debit card.
3) Annex 4; sub-article 9.2 (paragraphs 1, 2 and 4) of the Directive is deleted and
replaced by the following new sub-article 9.2(paragraphs 1, 2, and 4):
1. A Forex Bureau may sell foreign exchange up to USD 15,000.00 (USD Fifteen
Thousand) or its equivalent in other convertible currencies, which may be
provided USD 10,000(USD Ten Thousand) as cash notes or via a debit card,
and USD 5,000(USD Five Thousand) by debit card only for a bona-fide
business traveller to representatives of business organization, upon
presentation of valid licenses together with passport, if applicable, and air
ticket.
2. A Forex Bureau may also sell foreign exchange up-to USD 15,000.00 (USD
Fifteen Thousand) or its equivalent in other convertible currencies, which
may be provided USD 10,000(USD Ten Thousand) as cash notes or via a
debit card, and USD 5,000(USD Five Thousand) by debit card only for 8 bona-
fide business traveller to business associations, welfare institutions, and
religious associations, organizers of trade exhibitions, tourism, cultural show
and sports.
3. A Business Traveller FCY account holder is entitled up-to USD 10,000 (Ten-
thousand USD) or its equivalent in other convertible currencies, to be taken
in cash notes or via a debit card. In addition, the account holder can take up-
to 20% of his outstanding FCY account balance via a debit card.
3. Effective Date
This Directive shall enter into force as of May 20, 2025. |
