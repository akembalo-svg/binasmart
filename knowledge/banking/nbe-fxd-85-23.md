7. Crediting the Account

7.3. Foreign currency cash notes for exceeding USD 10,000 (USD Ten Thousand) or
equivalent in any other convertible foreign currency can be deposited in the account
provided that the account holder presents a signed and sealed foreign currency declaration
from Ethiopian Custom Commission.

7.4 Depositing foreign currency cash notes for amount less than USD 10,000 (USD Ten
Thousand)can only be allowed upon presentation of entry Visa not exceeding 30 days for
residents other than neighboring and Middle East.

7.5 Notwithstanding to the provisions stated under sub article 7.3 of this article, residents
living in neighboring and Middle East countries are required to present signed and sealed
foreign currency declaration from Ethiopian Custom Commission for less or equal to USD
10,000 (USD Ten Thousand) foreign currency cash notes or equivalent in any other
convertible foreign currency to deposit in the account.”

3. Repealed
Article 7.3, 7.4 and 7.5 of the Directive No. FXD/69/2021, to “Establish and
Operation of Foreign Currency account for Non Resident Ethiopian and Non
Resident Ethiopian Origin” is repealed and replaced by this Directive.

4. Effective Date

This Directive shall enter into force as of September 08, 2023.”
