. Directive No. FXD/80/2022
Amendment to Directive No. FXD/65/2020 on Flower
Export and Repatriations of the Export Proceeds
Whereas the price of flower quoted at various international markets vary duc to various
factors which requires regular monitoring and follow-up the international flora market and
proceeds repatriation by setting an appropriate mechanism and monitoring system:
Whereas, it has become necessary to amend further the minimum net price set under Flower
Export and Repatriations of the Export Proceed Directive No. FXD/41/2012 as amended by
Directive No. FXD/65/2020, in accordance to Agro Ecology classification of each flower.
Now, therefore in accordance with article 20(3) of the National Bank of Ethiopia
Establishment (as amended) Proclamation No. 591/2008, this Directive is hereby issued to
monitor the flower export, international price of the product and export proceeds repatriation.
Short Title
This directive may be cited as “Flower Export and Foreign Exchange Repatriation of Export
Proceeds” (amendment) Directive No. FXD/80/2022.
