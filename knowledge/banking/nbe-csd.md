# WELCOME TO THE CENTRAL SECURITIES DEPOSITORY (CSD)

The Central Securities Depository (CSD) of Ethiopia is a robust, automated platform that provides secure and efficient primary market post-trade services for the capital market ecosystem in Ethiopia. As Ethiopia recently launched its capital market with the Ethiopian Securities Exchange (ESX), the CSD is essential in facilitating transparent and seamless securities settlement, registration, and custody services. The National Bank of Ethiopia (NBE) oversees the CSD's operations, ensuring it meets global standards for securities settlement and risk management, safeguarding both investors and issuers.

### Capital Market Infrastructure

Support the country’s capital market through private securities services:

- Private securities dematerialization
- Centralized custody with secure electronic safekeeping
- Post-trade orchestration for efficient clearing and settlement
- Corporate actions processing including dividends, bonus issues, splits, and more.

## VISION

To support the growth and modernization of Ethiopia’s capital market by providing a world-class, automated post-trade infrastructure that increases market security, efficiency, and accessibility.

## MISSION

The CSD aims to provide comprehensive and cutting-edge solutions for securities settlement and depository services. This includes dematerializing securities, enabling seamless trade processing, and supporting all transactions with increased security and transparency.

📥

### Depository of Securities

Securities are registered, verified, and lodged at the Central Securities Depository in strict compliance with regulations.

🧾

### Corporate Actions

The CSD authenticates amendments including stock splits, mergers, capital adjustments, and updates by issuers.

🔄

### Clearing & Settlement

The CSD manages clearance procedures and ensures all settlements comply with custodian agreements.

🛡️

### Primary Market Auction Platform

Executes IPOs for both debt and equity financial instruments through an auction function.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Your guide to understanding Ethiopia’s Central Securities Depository.

What is CSD Ethiopia?▾CSD Ethiopia refers to the Central Securities Depository operated by the National Bank of Ethiopia (NBE). It is a secure and centralized digital platform responsible for the registration, clearing, settlement, and custody of securities such as bonds and equities in Ethiopia’s capital market.

What services does CSD Ethiopia provide?▾CSD Ethiopia offers a range of services including securities registration, custody, trade settlement, corporate action processing (such as dividend payments), and managing Delivery vs. Payment (DvP) systems to ensure secure transaction settlements.

How does CSD Ethiopia support the capital market?▾CSD Ethiopia supports the capital market by enabling the seamless settlement of trades, reducing operational risks, and ensuring that transactions are processed in a secure and timely manner. This is key to the development of the Ethiopian Securities Exchange (ESX).

What is the difference between CSD and a stock exchange? ▾While a stock exchange like the Ethiopian Securities Exchange (ESX) provides a platform for trading securities, the CSD focuses on the post-trade process. CSD handles the registration, custody, and settlement of securities after trades have been executed on the Exchange.

Is CSD Ethiopia compliant with international standards? ▾Yes, CSD Ethiopia adheres to international standards for securities settlement, clearing, and custody. This ensures that Ethiopia’s capital market is in line with global best practices, making it a secure and reliable platform for both domestic and international investors.
