## Accept digital payments securely

Take your business to the next level. Receive electronic payments and use collected funds to make payments all on your M‑PESA payments suite. With complete visibility on business performance, you get to have a peace of mind.

This platform is ideal for retail businesses like supermarkets, restaurants, hardware stores, pharmacies, boutiques, souks, and salons that frequently collect payments from customers.

## M‑PESA allows you to:

### Pay other Businesses

- Pay utility bills

- Pay suppliers

### Withdraw from merchant account

- Withdraw to owner’s mobile number

- Withdraw from M‑PESA Agent

- Withdraw to bank account

### Send to mobile numbers

- Make real-time payments directly to people's mobile phones.

### Transfer to other bank accounts via M‑PESA

- Transfer money from your M‑PESA wallet directly to a bank account.

- Increase your sales by providing your customers with a convenient alternative payment method.

- Enjoy lower fees than other digital payment solutions and improve your overall profitability.

- Improve your cash flow and enhance liquidity with instant mobile money payments.

- Improved security due to low risk of theft and fraud during financial transactions.

- Increased customer loyalty resulting from trust, leading to repeat business and referrals.
