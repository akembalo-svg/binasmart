# 7. Loading E-Money

7.1 You may load E-Money into your M‑PESA Account only and not directly into another M‑PESA participant’s M‑PESA Account.

7.2 In order to load E-Money, you must complete the information requested, including passing the identity and security validation and verification procedures to ascertain that you are the owner of the account and then hand over Cash equivalent to the E-Money sought to be loaded to the M‑PESA Agent.

7.3 After handing over the Cash to the M‑PESA Agent, you must ensure that you receive an SMS confirmation receipt that will serve as conclusive evidence that you have successfully completed the deposit Transaction.

# 8. Transferring E-Money

8.1 Transaction limits and charges as prescribed by Safaricom M‑PESA shall apply each time you use the “Send Money”, “Pay Bill”, “Buy Goods and Services” or other functionalities that effect the transfer of funds from your M‑PESA Account.

8.2 Your Transaction request will not be completed:

8.2.1 If you have insufficient funds in your M‑PESA Account to complete a Transaction or to cover the charges for a Transaction;

8.2.2 If you have reached the maximum Account balance limit prescribed by Safaricom M‑PESA and the law;

8.2.3 If you have reached the daily limit prescribed by Safaricom M‑PESA and the law;

8.2.4 If your Account has been temporarily suspended or permanently frozen on valid grounds;

8.2.5 If there are any other compelling reasons such as temporary system delay or outage;

8.2.6 If you cancel the Transaction request within such timeline indicated to You and use such functionality made available for that purpose.

8.3 You may send E-Money to any Participant on the M‑PESA System as well as any mobile telephone user on any network in Ethiopia by following the instructions on your phone’s M‑PESA menu.

8.4 You will be charged a fee for any transactions you undertake. The fee will be in accordance with applicable tariffs and charges, to be published on the Safaricom M‑PESA website and/or any other means of communication.

8.5 If the E-Money in your M‑PESA Account is not enough to carry out your Transaction (including covering the Transaction cost) request in full, the Transaction will not be completed, and no E-Money will be debited from your M‑PESA Account.

8.6 Upon the successful completion of your Transaction to another M‑PESA Participant, Safaricom M‑PESA will immediately cause the transfer of the E-Money to the other Participant’s M‑PESA Account. You and the recipient will both be notified by SMS as to the completed status of your transaction.

8.7 Where Safaricom M‑PESA is unable to complete a transaction, you will be notified by SMS as to the reasons for not completing the transaction.

8.8 Each Transaction you undertake will be accompanied by a unique receipt number that will appear in the confirmation SMS sent to you and will include an updated balance of your Account. This receipt number may be used to track and identify the Transactions carried out on your Account and the SMS should therefore not be deleted if reliance on it will be required.

8.9 Upon the successful transmission of E-Money from your Account to the registered recipient’s M‑PESA account, the title to the E-Money will pass to the recipient.

8.10 Where the recipient is not registered on the M‑PESA System, the title to the E-Money will remain with you. However, you will not be able to withdraw the funds or send the funds to any other recipient or otherwise reverse the Transaction unless the initial Transaction is canceled by Safaricom M‑PESA upon the failure of the recipient Participant to accept the remittance within the stipulated time frame as stated under clause 8.12.

8.11 If the recipient does not have an M‑PESA account, Safaricom M‑PESA will send the recipient an SMS containing a one-time voucher to enable them to cash the value sent to them.

8.12 If within 7 days (or such shorter or longer period as may be determined by Safaricom M‑PESA) of the Transaction being made to a non-registered recipient, the recipient has not opened or cashed their voucher, Safaricom M‑PESA will cancel the Transaction and the E-Money will be sent back to your Account. You will be notified by SMS of the cancellation and reversion of the E-Money.

8.13 A Transaction once completed is final and irrevocable. As such, Safaricom M‑PESA shall not be under any compulsion to reverse any Transaction.

8.14 Notwithstanding clause 8.13, Safaricom M‑PESA reserves the right, at its sole discretion, to cancel or reverse a Transaction if reasonable grounds such as manifest error or fraud, or duress or criminal force are shown and PROVIDED that the recipient has not redeemed the E-Money and the reversal claim is made within one (1) month of the erroneous Transaction date.

8.15 Without prejudice to clause 8.14, Safaricom M‑PESA will not be compelled to refund or compensate you as a sender if the E-Money is sent to a recipient mistakenly or in error or fraudulently or under duress and subsequently redeemed for Cash or otherwise spent by the recipient of the mistaken, erroneous, or fraudulent Transaction and you hereby agree to hold Safaricom M‑PESA harmless from any losses arising from a mistaken, erroneous or fraudulent transfer of funds or any transfer of funds from your Account effected under duress or under coercion or criminal force.

9.3 You may be liable to face criminal prosecution (based on applicable laws of Ethiopia) for consuming or utilizing funds sent to you in error.
