Fees on Cash Processing, Sale of Gold/Silver_and Cost of Boxes |
Directive No. CMD/01/ 2022
Whereas; it is imperative to improve the quality of services related to cash notes processing
tasks, currency supply and others associated with the sale of gold and silver coins to smithery
and lapidaries;
Whereas; it is found necessary to revise fees related to cash processing and fees on sale of gold
and silver as well as costs associated with cash notes/coins packing and transportation materials
to the user banks located in regions and cities;
Now, therefore, pursuant to the authority vested in it by Article 27(2) of the National Bank of |
Ethiopia Establishment (as amended) Proclamation No. 591/2008, the National Bank of Ethiopia
hereby issues the following Directive.
1) Short Title
This Directive may be cited as “Fees on Cash Processing, Sale of Gold/Silver _and Cost of
Boxes Directive No. CMD /01/2022”
2) Definition
For the purpose of this Directive, unless the context provides otherwise:
2.1. “National Bank” means the National Bank Ethiopia;
2.2. “bank” means a company licensed by the National Bank to undertake banking business or a
bank owned by the government;
2.3. “user bank” means a bank which demands withdrawal, deposit or transfer services from the
bank or its agent;
2.4. “brick” means packed cash notes containing 10 bundles;
2.5. “packing and transportation materials” means cash notes and coins boxes/bags used to
transport currencies;

3) Scope of the Directive
Provisions of this Directive shall be applicable to a person and all banks operating in Ethiopia.
4) Fees
4.1) The National Bank shall charge any person or association that has relevant artisan mines,
and lapidary trade license from the concerned authority:
i) 2% (two percent) service fees on a sale of Maria Theresa Taller silver coins/Minilik
Silver coins ;
ii) 2% (two percent) fees on a local gold sale.
5) Cash Processing Fees and Cost of Boxes
5.1) A user bank depositing unsorted notes shall pay sorting fee of Birr 50 (fifty birr) per brick;
5.2) A user bank depositing deteriorated notes shall pay counting fee of Birr 50 (fifty birr) per
brick;
5.3) A bank applying for currency supply shall pay:
i) Birr 50(fifty birr) per bank note box;
ii) Birr 7(seven birr) per coin box;
iii) Birr 30(thirty birr) per piece of hessian bag.
6) Repeal
6.1) Number 3 of Maria Theresa Taller Silver Coins Sales Procedure manual approved on
January 11, 2011 (03/05/03 E.C) is hereby repealed.
6.2) Number 6.3 of local gold sales procedure manual issued on October 30, 2010 is hereby
repealed.
6.3) Sub-article 4.1 (a) and 4.2 of Directive No.CMD/04/2012 are hereby repealed.
7) Effective Date /
This Directive shall enter into effect as of October 4, 2022.
