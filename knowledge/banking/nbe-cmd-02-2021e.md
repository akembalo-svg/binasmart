በአማርኛ፦ ገንዘብና የባንክ ኖቶች — Legal Tender Protection (Amendment) Directive No. CMD/02/2021። ይህ ገጽ ከኢትዮጵያ ብሔራዊ ባንክ ኦፊሴላዊ ድረ-ገጽ በእንግሊዝኛ የተወሰደ ነው። የወለድ መጠን፣ የአገልግሎት ክፍያ፣ ታሪፍና የምንዛሪ ተመን ያለማስታወቂያ ይለወጣሉ። ይህ ገጽ ከላይ በተጠቀሰው ቀን ተቋሙ ባሳተመው መልኩ ነው። በማንኛውም ቁጥር ላይ ከመወሰንዎ በፊት ባንኩን ያረጋግጡ።

ይህ መመሪያ የገንዘብና የባንክ ኖቶች ጥበቃ (ማሻሻያ) መመሪያ ቁጥር CMD/02/2021 ሲሆን፣ የባንክ ኖቶች ክምችት ገደብን የበለጠ ለመቀነስ ታስቦ የወጣ ነው። መመሪያው አንድ ግለሰብ በአንድ ጊዜ እስከ 100,000 ብር፣ ህጋዊ ሰው ደግሞ እስከ 200,000 ብር የባንክ ኖቶች መያዝ እንደሚችል ይደነግጋል። ከዚህ ገደብ በላይ የተከማቸ ገንዘብ በህግ ቁጥር 591/2008 ንዑስ አንቀጽ 26(1)(መ) መሰረት እንደሚወረስ ይገልጻል።

ይህ ሰነድ ከብሔራዊ ባንክ የተገኘው በስካን (በምስል) መልክ ብቻ ስለሆነ በኦሲአር (OCR) ተነቦ ወደ ጽሑፍ ተቀይሯል፤ የገጽ ብዛት፦ 1፤ የንባቡ ጥራት «good» ተብሎ ተመዝኗል። ቃላቱ የብሔራዊ ባንክ ናቸው፤ ንባቡ ግን የማሽን ነው፤ ስለዚህ ማንኛውንም ቁጥር ከመጠቀምዎ በፊት ከዋናው ሰነድ ጋር ያመሳክሩ።

“Legal Tender Protection (Amendment) Directive No. CMD/02/2021”
Whereas, it is found necessary to further reduce hoarding limit of banknotes to discourage illegal
activities;
Now, therefore, the National Bank has issued this Directive in accordance with the power vested
on it by article 27(2) of the National Bank of Ethiopia Establishment (as Amended) Proclamation
No. 591/2008 to protect the country’s legal tender.
1. Short Title |
This Directive may be cited as “Legal Tender Protection (Amendment) Directive No.
CMD/02/2021”. "
2. Amendment |
Legal Tender Protection Directive No. CMD/1/2020 is hereby amended as follows:
~-
ቃ 1. Article 3.7 of the Directive is deleted and replaced by the following new article 3.7:

“3.7 Notwithstanding sub-article 3.6 of this Article, a natural person, at one time, may
hold banknotes up to Birr 100,000 (one hundred thousand Birr) and a juridical
person up to Birr 200,000 (two hundred thousand Birr).”

2. A new article 4.5 is added following article 4.4 of the Directive:

“4 Any excessive hoarding of banknotes in violation of Article 3.7 hereinabove shall be
confiscated in accordance with sub-article 26(1)(d) of Proclamation No. 591/2008.”

3. ላ new Article 5 is added following article 4 of the Directive and existing Article 5 is

‘renumbered as Article 6:

{~~ “5. Transitional Provision |
88 Any excessive hoarding of banknotes shall be reduced to the limits stipulated in 3.7
above within 3 (three) calendar days from effective date of this Directive.”
