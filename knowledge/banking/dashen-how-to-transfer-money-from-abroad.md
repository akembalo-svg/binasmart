### How to Send Money Online Through Thunes:

- Customers shall visit international money transfer agent’s official website at first (Western Union, Ria, World Remit……etc.) and login to the system with their credential. Customers need to have online account at international money transfer agents.

- Then, customers shall select “send money” option from the given list of options.

- Customers are required to fill where to send money and how much they need to send.

- Then, customers are required to select how the receiver wants the money (cash pickup, mobile wallet, or bank account).

- Customers are required to fill the detail information of the receiver (name, telephone number, or bank account…etc.)

- After selecting the receiving option, they need to select how they want to send the money (from their credit card, bank account, or debit card).

- After filling the mandatory options and submitting the request, the system displays money transfer aggregators to select

- Customers are required to select Thunes from the list to send money online to Ethiopia.

- Then, money automatically gets debited from the sender and credits the receiver’s bank account or wallet, If it is cash pickup, it will provide reference number.

- Customers are required to select Thunes from the list to send money online to Ethiopia.

Remember, all the transactions occur within the official websites of international money transfer agents. There is no need to visit THUNE’s official website to send money.
