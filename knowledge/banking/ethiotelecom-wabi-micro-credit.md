Wabii Salary Credit

## Wabii Salary credit

Offers employees of private and public institutions whose monthly salaries are paid through telebirr or Siinqee Bank access to loans equivalent to five months’ salary, up to 1 million Birr. The loan is repayable over 14 months. This is expected to generate up to 1.3 billion Birr in loan disbursement annually.

Range | Loan Type | Facilitation fee | Fee per Month | Repayment period | Penalty |

- Credit Amount and Fees

- Based on your transaction activity , you can borrow any amount between the minimum and maximum permitted amount loan limits.

- You should repay your previous credit first to get another credit.
- You can pay your credit partially or fully at a time.
- Credit repayment deadline will be 5 days, 10 days, 30 days, 45 days and 14 months from the date of taking the loan, depending on the type of loan.
- The maximum credit amount for 5 Days, 10 Days, 30 Days, and 45 Days is 5,000 Br, 6,000 Br, 15,000 Br, and 30,000 Br, respectively. Whereas the minimum credit amount for the mentioned credit days is 100 Br, 100 Br 200 Br, and 2,500 Br, respectively.
- If you repay your 5 days Wabi credit within the due date, you will be charged a facilitation fee of 1.5% of the loan amount on day one and you will be charged a daily fee ranging from 0.35% to 1.2% for the rest of the repayment period depending on your credit limit.
- If you repay your Wabi 10 days credit, you will be charged a facilitation fee of 2% of the loan amount on first day, and you will be charged a daily fee ranging from 0.3% to 0.9% for the rest of the repayment period depending on your credit limit.
- If you repay your monthly Wabi credit within 30 days, you will be charged a facilitation fee of 4% of the loan amount on first day, and you will be charged a daily fee ranging from 0.3% to 1% for the rest of the repayment period depending on your credit limit.
- If you repay your 45 days of Wabi credit within 45 days, you will be charged a facilitation fee of 6.5% of the loan amount on first day. and you will be charged a daily fee ranging from 0.5% to 0.67% for the rest of the repayment period depending on your credit limit.
- For wabi salary micro credit service , the repayment will be installment base (outstanding credit amount will be prorated for repayment period) that will be settled every month.
- A 5 months’ salary Wabi credit service has 14 months repayment period and has a 19.5% facilitation fee.
- A 3 months’ salary Wabi credit service has 9 months repayment period and has a 14% facilitation fee.
- A 1 month’s salary Wabi credit service has 3 months repayment period and has a 12% facilitation fee.
- After the due date, you will be charged a penalty fee of 0.11% per day on the outstanding credit amount.
