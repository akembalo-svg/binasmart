1
ተከታታይ ቁጥሮች | Serial Numbers
Each banknote bears a unique serial number that exhibits a
black-to-green fluorescence when viewed under ultraviolet (UV) light.

2
መለያ ምልክት | Identification Mark
A tactile recognition feature allows visually impaired individuals
to identify the denomination of the Birr through touch.

3
አንጸባራቂ ምልክት | Reflective Symbol
Tilt the banknote to observe the SPARK feature — a bright,
color-shifting patch that moves as the note is rotated.

4
የገዥ ፊርማ | Governor Signature
Circulating banknotes bear the signature of the serving Governor
at the time of issuance.

5
የደህንነት ገመድ | Security Ribbon
When tilted, the security thread changes color and reveals inscriptions
such as “NBE” and the denomination.

6
ውሃ ምልክት | Watermark
When held up to the light, a faint watermark matching the main
portrait becomes visible.

7
ትይዩ ምልክት | See-Through Feature
When held to the light, the front and back symbols align
perfectly at the same position.

8
ፍሎረሰንት ምልክት | Fluorescent Feature
Under UV light, a fluorescent mark appears on the lion’s head
and shifts to a yellowish color.
