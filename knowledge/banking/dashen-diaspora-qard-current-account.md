# Diaspora Qard Current Account

The Current Account product, which is a foreign currency account for Ethiopian Diaspora offered based on the contract of Qard that allows account holders ready access to their money placed with the Bank.

- Safe custody

- Effect transfers abroad/make foreign payments, such as import and other foreign service payments

- Convert into a Birr account at the prevailing exchange rate for local payment

- Limitless inter and intra-bank transfer of funds

- Serve as collateral or guarantee for loans or bids.

- Make local payments in Birr

- Free monthly account statement

- Initial deposit USD 100 and minimum deposit is set to be USD 100 with no maximum deposit and withdrawal limit

Basic Requirements

- Tin registration certificate, Renewed trade license and commercial registration certificate (For MSME and Corporate)

- One recent photograph (3×4)
