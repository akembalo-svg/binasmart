### Interest Free Banking Diaspora Banking Products and Services

Coupled with continuous development in the provision of customers’ own used modern banking services, its service excellence also enables the Bank to gain momentum in having public confidence and maintaining its preeminence in operational and financial performances among the available private banks in Ethiopia. In doing so, the Bank is commenced rendering Shari’ah complaint full-fledged interest free banking services dubbed ‘’Sharik’’.

Dashen Bank is delivering interest free Banking service to its esteemed customers through full-fledged Branches and in dedicated windows of conventional Branches. In addition the core-banking system treated the window as a branch, which enables the bank to segregate fund from the conventional bank wing.

The interest free banking services include but not limited Deposit and Investment accounts, Financing contract, Letter of Guarantee , International Trade Service, Money Transfer .

Specific to the Ethiopian Diaspora communities, Dashen Bank caters the following banking services under Interest Free banking segments:-

1. Interest Free Banking (IFB) Deposit and investment Accounts
It is a type of bank account that will be opened for the Diaspora community in accordance with the laws and regulations of the National Bank of Ethiopia (NBE) and Shari’ah governing principles:-

A. Diaspora Qard Current Account

- It is FCY account operated by cheque book and/or pre-arranged procedures.

- An individual may open current account in only one of the domestic authorized bank. Thus, the customer shall give its confirmations to this effect.

- The minimum balance to open this account is USD 100(One hundred US Dollar only) or equivalent in any of the eligible currencies (USD, GBP &EUR).

B. Diaspora Wadi’ah Saving Account

- It is non-repatriate Birr saving account that can be used for local payments only.

- It is an account that may take the form of Wadi’ah saving deposit that can be used for local payments only.

- The option of passbook or statement may be offered to the customer(s) when this account opened.

- The minimum balance to open this account is USD 100(One hundred US Dollar only) or equivalent in any of the eligible currencies

C. Diaspora Mudarabe Fixed (time) Deposit Account

- The bank set to introducing the product for its clients in the near future. And the account will be opened based on profit and loss sharing principle.

To open accounts at our Bank, you can use one of the following options:-

- Individuals living abroad can use selected international money transfer agents working with Dashen Bank or you can directly fill out the account opening application form posted at our Bank’s websitedashenbanksc.com and present documents to Ethiopian embassies in their domain for subsequent authentication and sent to Dashen Bank S.C. via courier or our corporate mail address.

- If the customer is being able to physically present, he/she can appeal his application at all branches of our bank across the country.

Documents required

- Application forms properly filled, signed by the applicant and specimen signature with two recent photographs of the account holder.

- For Ethiopians or foreign nationals of Ethiopian origin individuals, valid passport and/or identification card and/or residence and work permit.

2. IFB financing facilities for Diaspora communities
a. Murabah Residential Home/Mortgage financing :-

- This type of financing is a mortgage for a home buyer, with an equity contribution starting from 10%.

- Very attractive and competitive pricing (profit mark-up) that depend up on the customer’s contribution and mode of re-payments, while the repayment period may last up to 20 years.

b. Murabah Car Financing (New & Used):-

- A type of IFB Financing availed for the Diaspora communities to purchase a new/used car with an equity contribution starting from 10%.

- Very attractive and competitive pricing ( profit mark-up ) that depend up on the customer’s contribution and mode of re-payments, while the repayment period may last up to 8 years for brand new cars and up to 5 years for used cars.

Murabah Personal Financing :-

- This type of IFB Financing is has been availed for the diaspora that can be used for a variety of personal issues such as fulfillment of home supplements and so on upon presentation of acceptable collaterals.

Murabah Investment Financing:–

- This is a type of IFB Financing that has been availed to the diaspora for a wide range of potential investment activities that provides the customer with a attractive profit mark-up charges and with an equity contribution starting from 30% of the project cost.
