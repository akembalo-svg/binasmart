Convenient Payment Process

Parents can securely and conveniently make payments for school fees and other educational expenses online with just few minutes.

## Stay informed anytime, anywhere

The Zemen School Pay system sends notifications through multiple channels to remind parents of payment deadlines, parent meetings, holidays, and other important information.

## Accessible at your fingertips

The system is available 24/7, allowing parents to access it whether you're at home, at work or on the go. It ensures seamless transactions without the hassle of standing in long queues or dealing with complicated payment processes.

Multiple payment options

The system enables parents to conveniently make payments through Zemen mobile and internet banking or in person over the counter.
Stay informed about your child’s academic progress: Parents receive real-time updates on children’s quizzes, exams, and grade results, as well as their child’s daily attendance status via Telegram Bot. This platform also facilitates direct communication with teachers regarding the child’s academic progress.

Free service

Zemen Bank provides the school pay system to parents free of charge.

Real-time tracking and transparency

Parents can monitor payments, track transaction history, and receive instant notifications, ensuring transparency and up-to-date financial reports.

Secure and reliable Transactions

Zemen Bank’s School Payment System ensures the protection of your financial information with advanced security measures.
