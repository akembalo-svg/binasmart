## Loan Services for Micro and Small Enterprises (MSEs):

Enables Micro and Small Enterprises, merchants, and agents to start or expand their small businesses through both collateralized and non-collateralized loans up to 1.2 million Birr. It is planned to disburse up to 10 billion Birr annually through this service. This includes:
The project aims to provide merchant and agent loans digitally by collecting all the required documents, reviewing, and approving the loan digitally (online), then the merchant and agent will take the loan digitally by accepting the T&Cs (i.e., without signing a physical contract).
 Loan approval is after evaluating the submitted documents.
o Required documents & information from Sole Proprietorship:
 License
 TIN
 Marital Certificate
 Kebele & National ID
 Asset ownership certificate for collateral loan
 Marital status of the merchant.
 If not married  submit non-marital certificate.
 If married  submit marriage certificate.
 If the merchant is married, the authorization given by spouse which is authenticated by document registration and authentication office.
o Required document from PLC, Share company, etc
 License
 TIN
 Memorandum & Article of Association
 Members Kebele & National ID
 Asset ownership certificate for collateral loan
