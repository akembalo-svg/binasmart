telebirr Sanduq (Saving)

It is a method of saving small sum of e-money to individuals telebirr account using telebirr app or
USSD (*127#).

###
Interest bearing Saving Account

It allows customers to securely store money anytime from anywhere and any amount that enables customers to get an interest fee.

###
Non-interest-bearing saving service:

Interest will not be applicable.
Customers can save and withdraw anytime.

## Deposit Type

### On demand saving

- Customers can save any amount any time and can withdraw anytime.
- Customers can choose between an interest free offer (0% interest) and a 7% interest rate offer.

### Locked saving

- Customers can save specific amount for defined period i.e. customers can not withdraw before defined period. The interest fee will be 8% per year (0.67% per Month).

### Installment saving

- Customers can save periodically. The interest fee will be 7.5% per year (0.63% per Month).

telebirr Sanduq activation requirement

- 18-year-old and Above
- Have an Ethio telecom SIM card
- Active user of telebirr and Ethio telecom products such as Data/Voice/SMS
- You can use USSD (*127#) or telebirr app to register

###
How to activate and use the service

### To Activate

- Go to Financial Service
- Select Sanduq
- Read the terms and conditions
- tick the check box
- Select annual interest rate and press activate.

### To deposit money

- Go to On-Demand Saving under telebirr sanduq
- Select Deposit
- Enter the amount to be saved and press Deposit

### To withdraw money

- Go to On-Demand Saving under telebirr sanduq
- Select Withdraw
- Enter the amount to be withdrawn and press Withdraw
