## Card Services

Zemen Bank has consistently continued to provide card personalization services, setting itself apart as one of the few banks in the industry with its own card personalization office that adheres to PCI DSS standards. As the first bank in Ethiopia to achieve PCI DSS compliance, Zemen Bank has maintained this position and obtained a renewed compliance certificate.
The bank’s in-house card personalization service offers a variety of products, including the Platinum Prepaid Travel MasterCard, making Zemen Bank the pioneering Ethiopian bank to introduce an international travel card. Moreover, Zemen Bank personalizes a range of cards for its clients, such as debit cards, prepaid local cards, and gift cards, at its dedicated personalization office, catering to a diverse set of customer requirements.

## Debit Cards

Zemen’s debit cards offer 24-hour access to your account. Our contactless debit cards are more than simply an ATM card; it allows you to make cashless transactions at POS terminals, online shopping and ATMs. We provide Basic, Personal, Prestige, and Z-club debit cards.

## Payroll cards

Designed to meet the needs of modern businesses, our payroll card offers a secure and efficient way to distribute employee wages via their payroll card. You can streamline your payroll process while providing your employees with a flexible and accessible payment option.

## Prepaid cards

With the Zemen’s Prepaid Card, you have full control over how much you spend each day. The amount of money on the prepaid card is the maximum amount that can be paid out. To use the Prepaid card for all of your payment needs, just load the card via our internet & mobile banking with the money you will need for travel or shopping.

Ethiopia’s first prepaid and contactless travel card will enable Ethiopian Travelers to travel with USD or EUR currencies in partnership with Mastercard by introducing contactless features.
This product address our customers’ needs for overseas travel, without the need to carry cash. ## Cardless ATM Withdrawal

Through our Card Less Cash Withdrawal service, you can also withdraw cash without using a card at Zemen bank’s ATMs. The cardless solution allows you to leave your card in your wallets and withdraw cash using your mobile phones.
