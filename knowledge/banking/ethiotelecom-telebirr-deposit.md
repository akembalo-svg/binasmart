## How to deposit cash with telebirr

- Visit nearby telebirr agent or Ethio telecom shops
- Provide cash to the agent or Ethio telecom sales representative
- The agent receives cash and transfers equivalent e-money to your telebirr account
- You will receive telebirr transaction notification
ORIf you have a linked bank account, you can transfer directly from your bank account to telebirr

- Open your bank account app or USSD
- Choose transfer to telebirr,
- Then, enter mobile number and amount,
- Confirm using your bank PIN

How to Deposit Cash with Telebirr

##
Visit Nearby Telebirr Agent or Ethio Telecom Shops

- Visit nearby Telebirr agent or Ethio Telecom shops

- Provide cash to the agent or Ethio Telecom sales representative

- The agent receives cash and transfers equivalent e-money to your Telebirr account

- You will receive Telebirr transaction notification

##
Transfer Directly from Your Linked Bank Account

If you have a linked bank account, you can transfer directly to your Telebirr account by following these steps:

- Open your bank account app or USSD.

- Choose the option "Transfer to Telebirr."

- Enter the mobile number and amount.

- Confirm the transfer using your bank PIN.
