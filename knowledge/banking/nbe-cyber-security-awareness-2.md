_ TE Us .. NATIONAL BANK OF ETHIOPIA
ISMD/D/88/2023
October 30, 2023
All Banks
Addis Ababa
Dear Sir;
Subject: Notification to Give Cyber Security Awareness to Your
Customers
Cybersecurity threats and attacks are evolving rapidly and are affecting all
sectors of the economy. The frequency and potential impact of cyberattacks
continue to increase as cyber attackers gain skills and technological
sophistication. The financial sectors, together with their customers, are among
the primary targets of cyberattacks.
At this time, there are many more cyberattacks targeting Ethiopian bank
customers. As a result, the need for cybersecurity awareness and skills is
becoming increasingly urgent due to our dependence on information technology
(IT) across all aspects of our society. Recent technological advances and the
introduction of smart devices have forced both the government and private
organizations like banks to create awareness of cyber threats and cybersecurity.
Just as you pay attention and are aware of the services you provide for your
customers through various media, please provide cyber security awareness
through your website, ATM machines, social media, and other methods to your
customers about what they should do to prevent or reduce the risk of a
cyberattack.
