Requirements for Undertaking Account Based Transactions and
Ensuring of Regulatory Limits (Amendment
Directive No. FIS/05/2022
Whereas, it has become necessary to amend the Requirements for Undertaking
Account Based Transactions and Ensuring of Regulatory Limits Directive No.
FIS/04/2021;
Now, therefore, in accordance with article 60 and article 66(2) of the Banking Business
Proclamation No. 592/208 (as amended by Proclamation No. 1159/2019), article 24 and
article 34(2) of the Microfinancing Business Proclamation No. 626/2009 (as amended by
Proclamation No. 1164/2019), and article 4(2) and article 37(2) of the National Payment
System Proclamation No, 718/2011, the National Bank of Ethiopia has issued this
Directive.
1. Short Title
This Directive may be cited as “Requirements for Undertaking Account Based
Transactions and Enduring of Regulatory Limits (Amendment) Directive No.
FIS/ 05/2022."
2. Amendment
Requirements for Undertaking Account Based Transactions and Ensuring of
Regulatory Limits Directive No, FIS/04/2021 is hereby amended as follows:
1) Article 6 of the Directive is deleted and Article 7-12, are renumbered as Article 6-
11, respectively.
2) Article 10(2) of the Directive (as renumbered) is deleted and replaced by the
following new sub-article (2):
10.2 Birr 50,000 (Fifty Thousand Birr) for violations of each of Article 4.3, 5.1,
5.2, 5.4, 5.5, 5.8, 6 and 7 (as renumbered) of the Directive”
3) Effective Date
This Directive shall enter into force as of the 5” day of January 2022.
