### Electronic Retail Payment Switching and Clearing

We provide all Financial Service Providers (FSPs) interoperability with their payment systems and help them serve their customers with convenience. Be it ATM, POS, Mobile or Internet banking, Ethswitch’s mission is to integrate these independent systems to create seamless collaboration and make payments simple and affordable for every user. The value we create work for financial service providers, merchants and the end users. We enable domestic P2P & P2G transactions & payments enabling our partners and bill payment aggregators and processors to provide convenient services to their customers. Our system will also enable e-commerce platforms operate with an embedded secure payment systems.
