## Fast Loan by CBE-Mobile Banking

### 1. Introduction

This document outlines the Terms and Conditions for the Fast Loan CBE Mobile Banking, designed by the Commercial Bank of Ethiopia (CBE). The product is aimed at providing a convenient credit facility to CBE account holders having Mobile Banking user, allowing them to access short-term funds.

### 2. Definitions

- a. Agreement: Refers to this Terms and Conditions document, including any amendments or variations made from time to time.
- b. CBE: Refers to the Commercial Bank of Ethiopia.
- c. CBE Mobile Banking User: Refers to the customer having active Mobile Banking product maintained with CBE.
- d. Charge: means any fee imposed by the bank for processing, administering, or servicing the Loan.
- e. Customer or You: Refers to any person who is a registered CBE Mobile Banking User and has accepted these Terms and Conditions.
- f. Credit Scoring: The evaluation method used to determine the Fast Loan Limit based on the customer’s transaction history and creditworthiness.
- g. Facility Term: The period within which the loan amount must be repaid.
