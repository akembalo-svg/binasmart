## Diaspora Savings Account

This account is designed to make your money more productive back home. It is a deposit account with attractive interest rates and is available in both ETB and foreign currency (USD, GBP & EURO).

Features

- Attractive interest rates for all applicable currencies,

- Funds are non-repatriatable,

- Standing orders for transactions.

Benefits

- Safe and secure investment option,

- Designated Personal Banker,

- The deposit can serve as collateral for personal loans and advances.
