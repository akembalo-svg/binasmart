## Personal Checking

It is a non-interest bearing account. The account is established as a package account with their Personal Saving Account, without additional deposit requirements, to enable the respective account holder to operate the account with check. The deposit required to open the account is Birr 5,000.

## Prestige Checking

It is a non-interest bearing account. The account is established as a package account with their Prestige Saving Account, without additional deposit requirements, to enable the respective account holder to operate the account with check. The deposit required to open the account is Birr 5,000. The account holder has the privilege to keep the money under the saving account earning interest on daily basis while effecting payment from the checking account.

## Z-Club Checking

The account holder is also entitled to open packing z club checking account as a package without additional deposit requirement. It gives the privilege like any other deposit accounts to operate the account with check while earning better interest on the deposit, which is accrued on daily basis. Minimum deposit required to open the account is Birr 5,000.
