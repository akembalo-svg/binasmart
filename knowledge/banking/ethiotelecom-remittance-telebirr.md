## telebirr Remit

With the convenient and secure telebirr Remit App, you can receive money sent from abroad directly to your telebirr account.

What is telebirr Remit?
telebirr Remit allows customers residing abroad to send money instantly to telebirr users in Ethiopia using telebirr Remit App using Bank of Abyssinia Cyber Source payment gateway.

## Steps to Use telebirr Remit

Install App
- The sender should download and install telebirr Remit App on his/her mobile device.
- Ensure your device is compatible with the app.

Setup Account:
-Create an account using your email (currently setup using gmail)
-Provide necessary identification and account details on the app.

Verify your email
- Check your email for a verification OTP (one-time password) after account setup.
- Enter the verification OTP from your email inbox or spam message to complete the signup process.

Add Recipient Details:
-Enter the recipient’s telebirr mobile number.
-Ensure the recipient’s telebirr number is correct.

Initiate Transfer
- Input the amount to be sent and complete the transfer.
- Ensure sufficient funds and accurate recipient details.

Confirm Receipt
-The recipient receives the funds instantly in their telebirr account.
