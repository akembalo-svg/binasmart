## How to pay with telebirr

Pay Ethio telecom bill

- Select "Pay Bill" from the menu;
- Select "For self" or
- "For other" (for another person)
- Enter/ Select receiver’s phone number

- Select/Enter the amount you want to pay.
- Enter PIN
- Confirm transaction

Buy Unity Park ticket

- Select "buy ticket” option from the menu
- Select ticket type
- Enter number of tickets
- Enter entrance date and time
- Enter PIN to confirm

Settle your monthly DSTV payment

Via telebirr App:

- Go to telebirr App home page and select Pay with telebirr
- Select Utility payment
- Select Pay to Entertainment
- Enter Smartcard number and follow the instructions to make your payment.

Settle your passport service fees

- Via telebirr App:

- Click pay with telebirr
- Open Utility payment
- Select Immigration and citizenship service
- Enter Payment Reference Number & Press Next
- Enter PIN number, press Finish and Confirm
Note: - Note: you will receive a Payment Reference Number to be entered in telebirr App as you register for passport service via Immigration & Citizenship Service Website

- Via short number (*127):

- Dial *127#
- Enter # to proceed to the next menu
- Enter 7 to select Pay with telebirr
- Enter 2 to select Utility Bill Payment
- Enter 1 to select By Utility Company
- Enter 8 to select Immigration and citizenship
- Enter your Reference Number
- Enter Your PIN & Enter 1 to Confirm and you will get short message

Buy Admas Digital lottery via telebirr or Send SMS to 605

Buy Admas Digital lottery via telebirr USSD (*127#) or Send SMS to 605

- Dial *127#
- Enter #
- enter 7 (National Lottery)
- enter 1 (Admas lottery 3 ETB)
- Enter 1 for confirmation
- Enter PIN and yow will receive confirmation SMS
Note

- A confirmation SMS from 127 and lottery ticket number from 605 will be sent.
- Buy many more tickets to increase your winning chance.
- keep the lottery number safely.

Settle Your Annual Tax Payment via telebirr

- Settle Your Annual Tax Payment Easily via telebirr App

- Press pay with telebirr
- Select Utility payment
- Select Addis Ababa City Administration Revenue Bureau
- Enter your payment Order Number and press Next
- Check the detail information and press Next
- Enter PIN and settle the payment (you will receive a confirmation message soon)

- Settle Your Annual Tax Payment Easily via telebirr (*127#)

- Dial *127#
- Enter 4 (Tax Payment)
- Select 1 (Addis Ababa)
- Enter payment order Number
- Check the detail information, enter 1 (ok) and press send
- Enter reason
- Enter Your PIN and settle the payment (you will receive a confirmation text)

Settle payments of Ministry of Foreign Affairs & Accounting & Auditing Board of Ethiopia via telebirr

- Go to www.eservices.gov.et & login
- Select service provider & service type
- Fill the application form
- Choose the payment options (scan telebirr QR code or mobile app)

Via telebirr App:

- Go to telebirr App home page and select Pay with telebirr
- Select Utility payment
- Select Electric Utility Payment
- Enter your Contract Account No.
- Check Your Name & Contract Acc. No.
- Enter Your PIN number & follow the instructions to settle the payment
