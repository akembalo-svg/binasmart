Loans & Credit Facilities

Line of Credit (Overdraft Facility)

A Line of Credit gives your business continuous access to funds up to a pre-approved limit—providing essential flexibility to manage day-to-day operational expenses or unexpected shortfalls. This facility is linked to your business current account, allowing you to draw funds as needed and repay at your convenience.

Merchandise Loan Facility

Our Merchandise Loans provide short-term financing for the purchase of inventory or goods for resale—helping you maintain optimal stock levels without straining your cash reserves. This facility is especially beneficial for businesses with seasonal demand or frequent inventory turnover.

Pre-Shipment Export Finance

Zemen Bank’s Pre-Shipment Facility supports exporters by providing working capital for the production, packaging, and transportation of goods prior to shipment. The facility is typically secured against a confirmed export order or letter of credit.
