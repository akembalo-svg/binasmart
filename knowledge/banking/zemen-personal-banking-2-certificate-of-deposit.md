## Certificate of Deposit Products

Certificate of Deposit (CDs) is an excellent tool for managing your money when you are able to set aside funds for a period of time. With a CD, you maximize your interest rate and security. Zemen offers CDs for many terms, some with a minimum investment of ETB 1,000,000.

## What are the benefits of CD's?

CDs typically pay higher interest rates than many other savings products.

The uniqueness of Zemen’s CD is we pay Upfront Interest immediately.

At Zemen, Interest can be credited to the CD or to a Zemen checking or savings accounts.

A Zemen CD can be used as collateral for a Zemen loan with a discounted rate
