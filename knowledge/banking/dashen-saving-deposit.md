# Saving Deposit

### Saving Account / Saving Deposit

It is a regular saving account which allows customers to deposit money, keep it safe, and withdraw funds, all the while earning interest.

- Safe and bears impressive interest Rate compounded every month on the minimum balance of the month

- Passbook may or may not be issued depending on the customer’s interest

- No Minimum initial deposit

- Limitless deposit and withdrawal

- Free, convenient and limitless intra-bank transfer of funds (only local)+

- 25 birr Initial Deposit with no minimum and maximum deposit limit

- Can be withdrawn with Cash, ATM, POS, Account to Account transfer, Debit Card, Standing instruction and internet and mobile transaction

Eligibility Requirements
For Retail

- Has to be of legal age [ > = 18 years]

For Artificial Persons [Partnership, Assn., Ço-ops., PLCs, Share Companies, etc.]

- Renewed trade license, commercial registration and certificate of registration

- Tin registration certificate

- Authenticated Memorandum and Article of association, Partnership Agreement

- The minutes of the board of directors held to discuss the opening and operation of the account (For Share companies)

- A letter of request to open the account and operate

- Valid and renewed ID card
