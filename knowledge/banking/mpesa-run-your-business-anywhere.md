## Run your business anywhere, anytime

Manage your business from anywhere, anytime with the M‑PESA Business App. It allows you to monitor performance and make payments on the go, with no limitations on time or location.

- Get full visibility of your business health performance with a simple graphical visualization of money-in and money-out trends.

- Solve payment collection problems with our transacting capability that enables businesses to initiate fund withdrawals, make payments to suppliers' mobile or business numbers, refunds or salary payments.

- Access account-related services such as exporting full statements, initiating payment reversals, and configuring users with our do-it-yourself services.

- Promote growth and support digital transformation with the Business Tools Marketplace (MiniApp), which enables third-party providers to publish digital business tools such as bookkeeping, ordering, invoicing, payroll, and bulk SMS.

- Receive payments faster and error-free with QR for Business that SME merchants can generate within the app.
