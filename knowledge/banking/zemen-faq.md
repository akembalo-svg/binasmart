## Frequently Asked Questions

## General Information

What are Zemen Bank's working hours?

Our branches are open Monday through Saturday from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM and for specific branches and services; please refer to our Branch Locator on the website.

How can I contact Zemen Bank for support?

You can reach us through our customer service call center 6500, email us at customerservice@zemenbank.com, or visit any of our branches. You can also connect with us via our social media channels.

Where is the headquarters of Zemen Bank located?

Our headquarters is located at Mexico- Senga tera Ras Abebe Aregay, Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. You can find detailed directions and other branch locations on our Branch Locator page.

## Account Services

What types of accounts can I open with Zemen Bank?

We offer a variety of accounts, including Savings Accounts, Current Accounts, Fixed Deposit Accounts, and Foreign Currency Accounts. Each type of account has its own benefits and features tailored to meet your financial needs

How do I open an account with Zemen Bank?

To open an account, please visit any of our branches with a valid ID, passport-size photo, and the minimum initial deposit. You can also begin the process online through our website and complete it at a branch.

Can I open an account online?

Yes, you can start the account opening process online by filling out the necessary forms on our website. Once you submit your application, you will be contacted by a Zemen Bank representative to finalize the process at a branch.

## Digital Banking

How can I register for Zemen Bank's Internet Banking?

To register for Internet Banking, visit our website and click on the “Internet Banking” link. By entering your 16 digit bank account or by entering your ATM card details. In both cases, you will receive one time password to the already registered mobile number associated with your account. You will be requested to input your preferred username and password. Follow the instructions to sign up. You will need your account details and a valid email address. For assistance, visit any branch or call 6500.

What services are available through Internet Banking?

With Zemen Bank’s Internet Banking, you can check account balances, view transaction history, transfer funds, pay bills, and more. The platform is designed for ease of use, ensuring you can manage your finances anytime, anywhere.

Does Zemen Bank offer Mobile Banking?

Yes, our mobile banking service allows you to access your accounts, transfer funds, pay bills, and more directly from your smartphone. Download the Zemen Bank Mobile App from the App Store or Google Play or use *844# and follow the registration instructions.

What are the interest rates for loans at Zemen Bank?

Interest rates vary depending on the type of loan, loan amount, and your financial profile. Please visit our website or contact a branch for detailed information on current rates.

## Cards

What types of cards does Zemen Bank offer?

We offer Debit Cards, Credit Cards, and Prepaid Cards. Each card type has different features and benefits designed to meet your spending and financial needs.

How can I apply for a Zemen Bank card?

To apply for a card, visit any of our branches with a valid ID and the required documents. You can also start the application process online via our website.

What should I do if I lose my Zemen Bank card?

If your card is lost or stolen, please report it immediately by calling our call center at 6500 or visit the nearest branch. You can also deactivate your card using internet banking. We will block the card to prevent unauthorized transactions and guide you through the process of getting a replacement.

## Security and Fraud Prevention

How does Zemen Bank ensure the security of my accounts?

We use advanced security measures, including encryption, two-factor authentication, and regular monitoring, to protect your accounts. We also encourage customers to follow best practices for online security, such as not sharing passwords and regularly updating login details.

What should I do if I suspect fraudulent activity on my account?

If you suspect any fraudulent activity on your account, please contact our call center 6500 or the nearest branch immediately. We will assist you in securing your account and investigating the issue.

How can I protect myself from online banking fraud?

Always keep your login credentials secure, avoid using public Wi-Fi for banking transactions, and regularly monitor your account for any suspicious activities. Zemen Bank will never ask for your password or PIN via email or phone.
