# Bank the Shariah-compliant way with CBE Noor!

CBE NOOR is a dedicated interest-free banking service of Commercial Bank of Ethiopia established to provide Shariah-compliant financial products. It operates in alignment with Islamic finance principles, which prohibit interest (Riba) and emphasize fairness, transparency, and risk-sharing. CBE NOOR caters to individuals, businesses, and diaspora communities seeking ethical and interest-free banking options.
