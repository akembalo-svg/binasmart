What is Dashen Mobile Plus?

- Dashen Mobile Plus is an Omni channel payment and commerce platform provided by Dashen Bank to its esteemed customers.

- The channel enables the customer to stay on top of their account 24 x 7 without being limited by time and geographic location.

How can I get registered on Dashen Mobile Plus?

- Customers can visit any of the nearest Dashen Branches/Agents for registration.

- Customers without even visiting branches can easily self register on Dashen Mobile Plus various channels i.e. Mobile App, Internet Banking and USSD
