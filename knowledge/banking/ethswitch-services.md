### Services

### Ethiopay - Domestic Card Scheme

Ethswitch is the owner of Ethiopay-Ethiopia’s domestic Card Scheme. Ethiopay is developed for all banks in Ethiopia to issue and acquire payment cards.

### Electronic Retail Payment Switching and Clearing

We provide all Financial Service Providers (FSPs) interoperability with their payment systems and help them serve their customers with convenience. Be it ATM, POS, Mobile or Internet banking

### Hosting

Etswitch provides FSPs with option of enjoying the full benefits of owning such systems & technologies without the costly investments. Currently, there are seven banks hosted to Ethswitch.

### Central Card Printing, Personalization and Issuance

Ethswitch operates state of the art card printing, personalization as well as PIN mailer technologies that are capable of producing large number of cards per day, enough to serve every bank in Ethiopia

### Dispute Management and Fraud Monitoring

Ethswitch, being the National Payment Switch, provides dispute management & fraud monitoring services for all interoperability transactions

### Advisory & Training

Ethswitch has the responsibility of providing its customers and partners with market insights and strategic advisory services courtesy of its vast market data. To this end it has established an external training & advisory wing.
