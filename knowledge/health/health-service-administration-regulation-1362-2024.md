# Health Service Administration and Regulation Proclamation No. 1362/2024 — የጤና አገልግሎት አስተዳደርና ቁጥጥር አዋጅ ቁጥር 1362/2017

Federal Negarit Gazette 31st Year No. 45, Addis Ababa, 20 May 2025. From the Ministry of Justice legal database (justice.gov.et), listed as in force on 2026-09-14. Its Art. 57 repeals Proclamation No. 661/2002 E.C. (661/2009, Food, Medicine and Health Care Administration and Control) with respect to the matters it covers. English column only: the Amharic column of the published PDF uses a font without a character map and extracts as symbols. This is a regulatory statute; it contains no treatment advice or dosages.

## What this law covers (in brief)

The national rules for health services: patients' consent and rights, duties of health institutions (including issuing medical certificates and handling grievances), vaccination, emergency care, the referral system and referral abroad, mental health services, donation and transplantation, registration and licensing of health professionals and institutions, health financing, and complaints.

## Key articles

- Art. 3 — applies throughout the country.
- Art. 4 — every patient has the right to consent-based health service; details of patients' rights and obligations are to be set by Council of Ministers regulation.
- Art. 5 — every health institution must respect patients' rights, give clients up-to-date information and counselling, meet the required standards, give a medical certificate to a patient, set up a grievance handling system, and report professional misconduct to the regulator.
- Art. 7 — parents and guardians must have children vaccinated with the vaccines given for children; everyone must take the vaccines the Ministry designates as mandatory nationwide to prevent epidemics.
- Art. 9 — medical services are given only with the consent of the patient or a close relative.
- Art. 10 — every health institution must provide emergency and life-saving medical services.
- Art. 11 — an institution must refer a patient whose problem is beyond its capacity, with full information, after basic life-saving support, and must accept patients who come through the referral system; treatment abroad requires medical board evidence and permission when the problem is beyond the country's capacity.
- Art. 19 — every health institution provides mental health services at its level and refers after emergency care; a person with mental illness may get mental health services without going through the referral system.
- Art. 21 — anyone may donate blood, cells, tissues or organs voluntarily with consent, only to an institution established for that purpose, and may withdraw consent before transplantation.
- Art. 34 — health professionals must pass the competency assessment to be registered and licensed. Art. 38 — every health institution must be registered and hold a certificate of competency.
- Art. 49 — the Ministry sets up a fee-based health service system; health care costs that are not covered by health insurance and cannot be paid out of pocket are to be covered by the government or another body.
- Art. 54 — each health institution sets up an ethics committee to hear patients' appeals; a person whose rights under this law are denied may complain to the appropriate body within 60 consecutive days; the body decides within 15 working days; a final administrative decision may be appealed to a regular court within 60 consecutive days.

### Article 5. Duties of a Health Institution

Any health institution shall have the following duties:

1/ TO respect the rights of patient stipulated on this proclamation and other laws enacted subsequent to this proclamation;

2/ To provide the client with an appropriate and up-to-date information about the service and health education and counseling he provides;

3/ To establish patient handover system during health professionals’ rotation based on the rotation system of the health institution;

4/ To ensure that the services it provides fulfills the standard or quality set by the relevant body empowered by law;

5/ To give medical certificate for a patient;

6/ To establish a grievance handling system;

7/ To provide information about the service provided in the health institution and other relevant information upon request by the client or an authorized body;

8/ To facilitate, supervise and control the health professional of the institution to adhere to professional ethics;

9/ To notify the regulatory body in the event *  of professional misconduct in the institution;

10/ To do all the necessary preventive measures to protect patients and employees from communicable diseases;

11/ To respond in the event of natural and man-made disasters and make health services accessible;

12/ To continue to providing care in the event of man-made and natural disasters until the necessary measures have been taken to ensure the safety of the patient;

13/ To allow health science students to practice when selected for practical education; clearly inform the patient that students may practice on him, and supervise the practice of students, control activities that affect the practical education;

14/ TO take joint responsibility with the traqining institution for any damage sustained on patient for lack of supervision during practical training;
