# ለውጭ አገር ዜጎች የሥራ ፈቃድ — Work permit for foreign nationals

## በአጭሩ

- ማንኛውም የውጭ አገር ዜጋ በኢትዮጵያ ተቀጥሮ መሥራት የሚችለው **ከሥራና ክህሎት ሚኒስቴር** የሥራ ፈቃድ ሲኖረው ብቻ ነው (አዋጅ 1156/2011 አንቀጽ 176(1))።
- **የጸና ጊዜ:** ፈቃዱ ለአንድ የሥራ መደብ **እስከ ሦስት ዓመት** ይሰጣል፤ **በየዓመቱ ይታደሳል**። ሚኒስቴሩ የሦስት ዓመቱን ገደብ ሊያሻሽል ይችላል (አንቀጽ 176(2)፤ መመሪያ 44/2013 አንቀጽ 12(1))።
- ፈቃዱን የሚጠይቀው **አሠሪው** ነው። ፈቃዱ የሚያገለግለው ለዚያው አሠሪና ለዚያው የሥራ መደብ ብቻ ነው። ሥራው በኢትዮጵያዊያን ሊሸፈን የማይችል መሆን አለበት (መመሪያ 44/2013 አንቀጽ 6)።
- የውጭ አገር ዜጋው ወደ አገር ከገባ ከ**90 የሥራ ቀን** በኋላ የሚቀርብ አዲስ ፈቃድ ጥያቄ ተቀባይነት የለውም (አንቀጽ 12(4))።
- **ለአዲስ ፈቃድ የሚያስፈልጉ (አንቀጽ 15):** የአሠሪው ማመልከቻ፤ የሠራተኛው የግል ታሪክ ቅጽ፤ የተረጋገጠ የትምህርትና የሥራ ልምድ ማስረጃ (ኦሪጅናልና ኮፒ)፤ ቢያንስ **3 ወር** የሚቀረው ፓስፖርት፤ **4 ጉርድ ፎቶ**፤ ጊዜው ያላለፈ ቪዛ (BV፣ WV፣ GV ወይም NV)፤ ከሚመለከተው መሥሪያ ቤት የድጋፍ ደብዳቤ (በተለይ ለጤናና ለትምህርት ዘርፍ)፤ እንደአግባቡ የቅጥር ውል፤ የአገልግሎት ክፍያ ደረሰኝ።
- **በተጨማሪ:** የግል ድርጅት የንግድ ወይም የኢንቨስትመንት ፈቃድ ያቀርባል። መንግሥታዊ ያልሆነ ድርጅት ወይም የሃይማኖት ተቋም የምዝገባ ሰርተፍኬቱን ያቀርባል። የቪዛ ነፃ ስምምነት ካለ ቪዛ አያስፈልግም።
- **ዕድሳት (አንቀጽ 16):** ፈቃዱ ከማለቁ በፊት ባለው **አንድ ወር** ውስጥ ይቀርባል። የሚያስፈልጉት፦ ማመልከቻ፣ ፓስፖርት፣ የሥራ ፈቃድ ደብተር፣ **የመኖሪያ ፈቃድ**፣ የድጋፍ ደብዳቤ፣ የቅጥር ውል፣ ለኢትዮጵያዊ ተተኪዎች የተደረገ የዕውቀት ሽግግር ሪፖርት እና የክፍያ ደረሰኝ ናቸው።
- **ዘግይቶ ማሳደስ:** ፈቃዱ ካበቃ በኋላ ባሉት 3 ወራት ውስጥ ማሳደስ ይቻላል። በተጨማሪ ቅጣት ይከፈላል፦ በመጀመሪያው ወር የዕድሳት ክፍያውን 50%፣ በሁለተኛው 75%፣ በሦስተኛው 100% (አንቀጽ 16(5))።
- **ክፍያ (ደንብ 394/2009፣ ታኅሣሥ 2009 ዓ.ም.):** አዲስ ፈቃድ **2,000 ብር**፣ ዕድሳት **1,500 ብር**፣ ምትክ **1,200 ብር**።
- **ኦንላይን:** የሥራና ክህሎት ሚኒስቴር "Expatriate Work permit" አገልግሎትን https://ewp.lmis.gov.et/ ላይ ያመለክታል። ስልክ፦ +251 116 671792፤ ኢሜይል፦ info@Mols.gov.et።
- በኢትዮጵያ ኢንቨስትመንት ኮሚሽን ፈቃድ ያገኙ ባለሀብቶች ሠራተኞች ጉዳይ በኮሚሽኑ የአንድ ማዕከል አገልግሎት ይታያል (መመሪያ 772/2013)።

## In short (English)

- A foreigner may work in Ethiopia only with a work permit from the **Ministry of Labour and Skills** (Labour Proclamation 1156/2019, Art. 176(1)).
- **Validity:** issued for one specific job for **up to three years**, **renewed every year**. The Ministry may change the three-year limit (Art. 176(2); Directive 44/2013, Art. 12(1)). The Ministry may cancel the permit if the foreigner is not needed for the work (Art. 176(3)).
- The **employer** applies. The permit is valid only with that employer and for that position, for work that Ethiopians cannot fill (Directive 44/2013, Art. 6). New applications made more than **90 working days** after the foreigner entered the country are not accepted (Art. 12(4)).
- **New permit documents (Art. 15):** the employer's application; the foreigner's personal history form; authenticated education and work-experience documents (original and copy); a passport valid for at least **3 months**; **four passport photos**; a valid BV, WV, GV or NV visa as appropriate; a support letter from the relevant government office (especially for the health and education sectors); the employment contract where applicable; and the fee receipt. Private firms also submit their trade or investment licence. NGOs and religious bodies submit their registration certificate. No visa is needed where a visa-waiver agreement applies.
- **Renewal (Art. 16):** apply in the month before expiry with the application, passport, work permit booklet, **residence permit**, support letter, contract, a report on knowledge transfer to Ethiopian understudies, and the fee receipt. **Late renewal** is possible within three months of expiry, with a penalty of 50%, 75% or 100% of the renewal fee for the first, second or third month (Art. 16(5)).
- **Fees (Council of Ministers Regulation 394/2016, 12 December 2016):** new permit **Birr 2,000**; renewal **Birr 1,500**; replacement **Birr 1,200**.
- **Online:** MoLS lists its "Expatriate Work permit" service at https://ewp.lmis.gov.et/. MoLS phone: +251 116 671792; email: info@Mols.gov.et.
- Investors licensed by the Ethiopian Investment Commission handle their expatriate permits through the Commission's one-stop service (Directive 772/2021).

## Official text

**Labour Proclamation No. 1156/2019, Art. 176 — Employment of Foreign Nationals** (Federal Negarit Gazette No. 89, 5 September 2019; https://justice.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/አዋጅ-ቁጥር-1156-2011.pdf)
> 1/ any foreigner may only be employed in any type of work in Ethiopia where he possesses a work permit given to him by the Ministry.
> 2/ a work permit shall be given for an employment in a specific type of work for three years and shall be renewed every year; provided, however, that the Ministry may vary the three years limit as required.
> 3/ Where the Ministry ascertains that the foreigner is not required for the work, the work permit may be cancelled.
> 4/ The Ministry may, in accordance with the law, charge service fees for the issuance, renewal or replacement of work permit.

**አንቀጽ ፻፸፮(፪) (የአማርኛ ጽሑፍ)**
> የሥራ ፈቃድ የሚሰጠው በአንድ የሥራ መስክ ለማገልገል እስከ ሦስት ዓመት ሲሆን በየዓመቱ ይታደሳል፡፡ ሆኖም ሚኒስቴሩ የሦስት ዓመቱን የጊዜ ገደብ እንደአስፈላጊነቱ ሊያሻሽለው ይችላል፡፡

**Directive No. 44/2013 E.C. — ለውጭ አገር ዜጎች የሥራ ፈቃድ የሚሰጥበትን ሁኔታ ለመወሰን ተሻሽሎ የወጣ መመሪያ** (Ministry of Labour and Social Affairs, ጥቅምት 30፥ 2013; PDF https://justice.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/Revised-Guidelines-for-Determining-the-Conditions-for-Granting-Work-Permits-to-Foreign-Citizens-Directive-No.-44-2013.pdf)

> አንቀጽ 15(1)፦ ... ሀ/ በቀጣሪ ድርጅት የሚቀርብ የሥራ ፈቃድ ጥያቄ ማመልከቻ፣ ለ/ የተቀጣሪው የውጭ አገር ዜጋ የግል ታሪክ የተሞላበት ቅጽ፣ ሐ/ አግባብ ባለው አካል የተረጋገጠ የውጭ አገር ዜጋው የትምህርት ማስረጃ እንዲሁም የሥራ ልምድ ማስረጃ /ኦሪጅናልና ኮፒ/፣ መ/ የውጭ አገር ዜጋው ከሦስት ወራት ያላነሰ ጊዜ ያለው የጉዞ ሕጋዊ ፓስፖርት፣ ሠ/ የፓስፖርት መጠን ያላቸው የውጭ አገር ዜጋው አራት ጉርድ ፎቶግራፎች፣ ... ቀ/ በሕግ የተወሰነ የአገልግሎት ክፍያ የተከፈለበት ደረሰኝ፣
> አንቀጽ 16(5)፦ አሠሪው የውጭ አገር ዜጋውን የሥራ ፈቃድ በወቅቱ ሳያሳድስ የቀረ እንደሆነ ፈቃዱ ፀንቶ የሚቆይበት ጊዜ ካበቃበት በሚቀጥሉት ሦስት ወራት ውስጥ ለመጀመሪያው ወር የዕድሳት ክፍያውን ሃምሣ በመቶ (50%)፣ ለሁለተኛው ወር ሰባ አምስት በመቶ (75%) እና ለሦስተኛው ወር መቶ በመቶ (100%) ቅጣት በተጨማሪ በመክፈል የሥራ ፈቃዱን ሊያሳድስ ይችላል፡፡

**Council of Ministers Regulation No. 394/2016 — Schedule of Fees** (Federal Negarit Gazette 23rd Year No. 6, 12 December 2016; PDF https://justice.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/ደንብ-ቁጥር-394-2009.pdf)
> 1. Issuance of Work Permit for Foreigners — For New License 2000 — For Renewal of License 1500 — For Replacement of License 1200 (Amount of Fee, Birr)

This regulation repealed the earlier fee Regulation No. 282/2013 (Art. 4).

**Ethiopian Investment Commission Directive No. 772/2021** (https://justice.gov.et/am/directives/a-directive-regulating-the-issuance-of-work-permit-to-expats-employed-in-investments-and-the-implementation-of-knowledge-and-skill-transfer-from-expats-to-ethiopians-directive-no-772-2021/) sets work-permit and knowledge-transfer rules for expatriates in licensed investments. For example, the number of expatriates generally may not exceed 10% of the Ethiopian employees, and the Commission may allow more in some phases.

## What is not published

- **Current fee:** no official fee schedule newer than Regulation 394/2016 was found. Confirm the amount with the Ministry of Labour and Skills before paying.
- **Newer directive:** Directive 44/2013 was issued under the old Labour Proclamation 377/2003. No newer MoLS work-permit directive was found on justice.gov.et. Ask MoLS whether a new directive applies.
- **Online steps:** the portal ewp.lmis.gov.et showed no public instructions. Ask MoLS (+251 116 671792).
- **Processing time:** not published.
- **Residence permit and work visa:** these come from the Immigration and Citizenship Service, not MoLS, and are not covered here.
