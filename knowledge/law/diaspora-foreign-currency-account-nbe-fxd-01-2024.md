# የዳያስፖራ የውጭ ምንዛሪ ሒሳብ — Diaspora foreign currency (FCY) account rules (National Bank of Ethiopia)

## በአጭሩ (Amharic summary)

- በውጭ የሚኖሩ ኢትዮጵያውያንና **ትውልደ ኢትዮጵያውያን የውጭ ዜጎች** (በአዋጅ 270/1994 መታወቂያ ያላቸው) በኢትዮጵያ በማንኛውም ፈቃድ ባለው ባንክ **የውጭ ምንዛሪ ሒሳብ** መክፈት ይችላሉ (የብሔራዊ ባንክ መመሪያ FXD/01/2024፣ አንቀጽ 14)።
- "በውጭ የሚኖር ኢትዮጵያዊ" ማለት ከ1 ዓመት በላይ በውጭ የኖረና የሠራ ኢትዮጵያዊ፣ ወይም ከ1 ዓመት በላይ ለሥራ ለመሄድ በሂደት ላይ ያለና የተረጋገጠ ሰነድ ያለው ነው።
- የሚያስፈልገው፦ በትክክል የተሞላ የማመልከቻ ቅጽ (በዲጂታል መንገድም ይቻላል) እና የጸና ፓስፖርት ወይም መታወቂያ። ከአንድ በላይ ባንክ መክፈት ይቻላል፤ በጋራ ሒሳብም ይቻላል።
- ቀድሞ የነበረው **የ100 ዶላር ዝቅተኛ መክፈቻ መጠን ተነስቷል** (FXD/04/2026፣ ከየካቲት 5 ቀን 2018 ዓ.ም. / ፌብሩዋሪ 12 ቀን 2026 ጀምሮ)።
- የሒሳብ ዓይነቶች፦ ተንቀሳቃሽ (current)፣ ቁጠባ እና የጊዜ ገደብ ተቀማጭ። ገንዘቡ በዶላር፣ በፓውንድ ስተርሊንግ፣ በዩሮ ወይም ብሔራዊ ባንክ በሚቀበላቸው ሌሎች ምንዛሪዎች ይያዛል፤ ዩዋን፣ የካናዳ ዶላር፣ የሳዑዲ ሪያል፣ የን፣ የአውስትራሊያ ዶላርና ድርሃምም ተቀብለው ወደ ሒሳቡ ምንዛሪ ይቀየራሉ።
- ሒሳቡ ገቢ የሚደረግበት፦ ከውጭ በባንክ የሚላክ ገንዘብ፣ በዓለም አቀፍ ሐዋላ አገልግሎት የሚመጣ፣ በሀገር ውስጥ ካለ ሌላ የውጭ ምንዛሪ ሒሳብ፣ የውጭ ገንዘብ ኖቶች (ጥሬ ገንዘብ)፣ ከውጭ የመጣ ቼክ፣ በዓለም አቀፍ ካርዶች።
- **ከ10,000 ዶላር በላይ ጥሬ ገንዘብ ሲያስገቡ ይጠየቅ የነበረው የጉምሩክ ዲክላራሲዮን ተነስቷል** (FXD/04/2026)።
- አጠቃቀም፦ ወደ ብር መቀየር፤ በደረሰኝ የውጭ አገልግሎት ክፍያ መፈጸም (ለራስ፣ ለትዳር ጓደኛና ለልጆች — ዝምድና የሚያሳይ ሰነድ ያስፈልጋል)፤ በፎሬክስ ቢሮ ደንብ መሠረት ጥሬ የውጭ ገንዘብ ማውጣት፤ ዓለም አቀፍ ካርድ ያለ ቪዛና ትኬት ማግኘት (FXD/04/2026)።
- **ትውልደ ኢትዮጵያውያን የውጭ ዜጎች** ያስገቡትን የመጀመሪያ ገንዘብና የተገኘውን ወለድ ያህል ወደ ውጭ ማስተላለፍ ይችላሉ።
- ሒሳቡ በሀገር ውስጥ የብር ብድር ለማግኘት እንደ ዋስትና ሊያገለግል ይችላል (በባንኩ የብድር ፖሊሲ)።
- ለጉዞ፦ የውጭ ምንዛሪ ሒሳብ ያለው ግለሰብ ተጓዥ እስከ 10,000 ዶላር በጥሬ ወይም በካርድ፣ በተጨማሪም የሒሳቡን እስከ 20% በካርድ መውሰድ ይችላል (FXD/03/2025)።
- የኢትዮጵያ ዳያስፖራ አገልግሎት "የዳያስፖራ አካውንት" አገልግሎት ይሰጣል፤ አድራሻ፦ ብሉም ታወር 5ኛና 6ኛ ፎቅ፣ አዲስ አበባ፤ ስልክ **011 554 4600**፤ ኢሜይል diaspora.service@mfa.gov.et።

## In short (English)

- Non-resident Ethiopians and **non-resident foreign nationals of Ethiopian origin** (holders of the ID under Proclamation 270/2002) may open a **foreign currency account** at any authorised bank (NBE Directive FXD/01/2024, Art. 14).
- "Non-resident Ethiopian": an Ethiopian living and working abroad for more than one year, or leaving to work abroad for more than one year with authenticated documents.
- Needed: a completed application (digital accepted) and a valid passport or ID. Accounts may be opened at more than one bank and jointly.
- The former **USD 100 minimum opening amount was removed** by FXD/04/2026 (in force 12 February 2026).
- Account types: current, savings, time deposit. Currencies: USD, GBP, EUR and others accepted by NBE; CNY, CAD, SAR, JPY, AUD and AED deposits are converted to the account currency.
- Credits: transfers from abroad through banks; international remittance services; other FCY accounts in Ethiopia; foreign cash notes; cheques from abroad; international cards.
- **The customs declaration for cash above USD 10,000 was removed** for deposits (FXD/04/2026).
- Uses: convert to Birr; pay foreign services with invoices (for yourself, spouse and children, with proof of relationship); withdraw foreign cash under forex bureau rules; international cards without visa/ticket (FXD/04/2026).
- **Foreign nationals of Ethiopian origin** may transfer abroad up to their initial balance plus interest.
- The account can serve as collateral for a Birr loan, per the bank's credit policy.
- Travel: an FCY-account holder may take up to USD 10,000 cash or by card plus up to 20% of the balance by debit card (FXD/03/2025).
- Ethiopian Diaspora Service offers a "Foreign Currency (Diaspora Account) Service": Bloom Tower 5th & 6th floor, Addis Ababa; **011 554 4600**; diaspora.service@mfa.gov.et.

## Official text

**FXD/01/2024** (https://nbe.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/FXD012024-FOREIGN-EXCHANGE-1-1.pdf)
- 2.61: "'Non-resident Ethiopians' means: a) All Ethiopian nationals who have already been living and working abroad for more than one year b) Business entities owned by non-resident Ethiopians and located outside the Ethiopian territory for more than one year c) Ethiopian nationals in due process of leaving abroad for work for more than one year and who can produce authenticated documents to this effect."
- 2.62: "'Non-resident Foreign Nationals of Ethiopian Origin' means: (1) A non-resident and holder of a valid identification card, obtained pursuant to Proclamation No. 270/2002 or amendment thereto ..."
- 14.1: "The following individuals and/or entities may open a foreign currency account in any Authorized Bank in Ethiopia: 1. Residents of Ethiopia ... 2. Non-Resident Ethiopian Individuals; and 3. Persons of Ethiopian Origin, i.e. Non-Resident Foreign Nationals of Ethiopian Origin."
- 14.2.1: "1. Completion of an Application forms properly filled and signed by the account holder; digital submissions with sufficient identification verification procedures shall be acceptable. 2. For individuals, valid passport and/or identification cards. ... 4. Minimum account required for opening an FCY account shall be USD 100 ... 5. Two or more depositors may together open a single account." 14.2.2: "... may be opened at more than one bank."
- 14.3: "... current accounts, savings accounts, or time deposit accounts."
- 14.4: "i. The US dollar ii. Pound Sterling iii. Euro iv. Other tradeable currencies acceptable by NBE"; 14.4.2: "Chinese Yuan, Canadian Dollar, Saudi Riyal, Japanese Yen, Australian Dollar and UAE Dirham; these other currencies shall be converted to the currency used for account opening at the spot exchange rate."
- 14.5.1: credited by "1. Transfers from accounts abroad effected through the banking system; 2. Foreign currency transferred through International Remittance Service Providers ...; ... 4. A transfer from any foreign currency account within Ethiopia. 5. Foreign currency cash notes ... 6. Check deposits originated from abroad ... 7. Receipt through international payment cards ..."
- 14.6.1: "1. Make conversions into a Birr account or into Birr cash notes ... 2. Make foreign service payments based on the presentation of underlying invoices; 3. Withdraw foreign currency cash notes in line with the rules governing forex bureaus. 4. Top up debit cards upon presentation of valid travel document ... 5. Persons of Ethiopian origin (Foreign Nationals) who have deposited funds in their FCY Accounts are entitled to effect transfers abroad as long as this amount does not exceed the initial balance plus any interest received on the deposited amount."
- 14.7.1: "The deposit account can serve as collateral to get credit in local currency form domestic Authorized Banks in line with the opening authorized bank's credit policy."

**FXD/03/2025** (in force May 20, 2025; https://nbe.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/FXD32025-AMENDEMENT-TO-FOREIGN-EXCHANGE-DIRECTIVE-No.-FXD012024.pdf): "For personal traveller FCY Account holder, up to USD 10,000 ... may be taken in cash notes or via a debit card. In addition, the account holder can take up to 20% of his outstanding FCY Account balance via a debit card."

**FXD/04/2026** (in force 12 February 2026; https://nbe.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/DIRECTIVE-NO.-FXD042026.pdf)
- 3.3: "banks are allowed to issue internationally recognized cards for all foreign currency account holders and load foreign currency as per the account holder's instruction, without visa and ticket requirements".
- 3.6: "the minimum amount of foreign currency required for FX account opening is hereby removed".
- 3.7: "the requirements for cash notes declaration for an authorized dealer to accept/purchase or deposit an amount exceeding USD 10,000 or equivalent in any other currency is removed".
- 3.8: "An FX account holder may use funds for his/herself, spouse, and children foreign service payments abroad upon presentation of valid documents to confirm relationship with the account holder and underlying invoices".
- 3.13: "For amounts exceeding USD 10,000 ... the customs declaration requirement for foreign currency needed for FX exchange at forex bureaus, depositing to FX account, and border entry is removed".

FXD/05/2026 (in force 25 May 2026) changes only trade/letter-of-credit rules.

**Ethiopian Diaspora Service** (https://www.ethiopiandiasporaservice.gov.et/services): lists "Foreign Currency (Diaspora Account) Service"; contact "Bloom Tower @5th & 6th floor, Addis Ababa, Ethiopia; 011 554 4600; diaspora.service@mfa.gov.et".

## What is not published

- Interest rates, fees and each bank's own opening procedure — ask the bank.
- Whether a non-resident can open the account fully online from abroad — the directive accepts digital submissions with ID verification; ask your bank.
- Details of the Diaspora Service's account service page — ask the Ethiopian Diaspora Service.
