# Federal Income Tax (Amendment) Proclamation No. 1395/2025 — the salary tax brackets — የፌዴራል የገቢ ግብር ማሻሻያ አዋጅ ቁጥር 1395/2017

Federal Negarit Gazette 31st Year No. 64, Addis Ababa, 1 September 2025. Amends the Federal Income Tax Proclamation No. 979/2016. From the Ministry of Justice legal database (justice.gov.et; its page labels the number "1395/2024", the Gazette itself prints 1395/2025). This file holds the English column. The Amharic column extracts with shifted letters (for example "ሊይ" for "ላይ"); it is reproduced, with the letters repaired and the rate tables checked against the English, in the companion file income-tax-amendment-1395-2025-am. The base Proclamation 979/2016 is not in this library: the MoJ copy is Amharic-only and its digits extract incorrectly.

## What this amendment covers (in brief)

It rewrites parts of the income tax law from July 2025: new monthly rates for tax on salaries (employment income), new rates for rental income and business income of individuals, taxpayer categories (Category A threshold Birr 2,000,000 annual turnover), digital content creators, an alternative minimum tax, and withholding changes.

## Key provisions

- Art. 2(6) — Article 11 of 979/2016 is replaced. Monthly employment income tax rates:
  - Birr 0 – 2,000: 0%
  - Birr 2,001 – 4,000: 15%
  - Birr 4,001 – 7,000: 20%
  - Birr 7,001 – 10,000: 25%
  - Birr 10,001 – 14,000: 30%
  - over Birr 14,000: 35%
- Art. 2(8) — Article 14(2) replaced. Annual rental income tax rates for individuals: Birr 0–24,000 0%; 24,001–48,000 15%; 48,001–84,000 20%; 84,001–120,000 25%; 120,001–168,000 30%; over 168,000 35%.
- Art. 2(10) — Article 19(2) replaced. Annual business income tax rates for individuals (except Category "B"): the same bands as rental income (0% up to Birr 24,000 … 35% over Birr 168,000).
- Art. 2(2) — Article 3 replaced: Category "A" taxpayers are bodies and any other person with annual turnover of Birr 2,000,000 or more.
- Art. 2(11) — new articles on income from digital content creation and an alternative minimum tax on businesses.
- Art. 3 — the old provisions continue to apply to the tax year before the one in which this amendment enters into force.
- Art. 4 — effective dates: the alternative minimum tax on income collected from 8 July 2025; Schedule "D" income tax and the withholding in renumbered Art. 97 from 7 August 2025; all other provisions (including the salary brackets) on income derived from 8 July 2025.

The rate table is the one printed in the Gazette. How the tax is calculated in practice (deduction amounts per band, pension contributions, exemptions under other articles of 979/2016) is not restated here; BinaSmart's income tax calculator page applies these brackets.

### Article 3. Transitory Provision

The provisions of the income tax proclamation before this amendment and the Turnover Tax Proclamation repealed shall continue to apply for the tax year proceeding the tax year in which this Proclamation enters into force.

### Article 4. Effective Date

This Proclamation shall come into force on the following dates:

1/ The Alternative Minimum tax shall be applicable on income tax to be collected as of July 8, 2025;

2/ The tax to be collected on schedule “D‟ income and the withholding tax collected on transactions as provided under renumbered Article 97 shall become effective as of August 7,2025;

3/ Subject to the provisions of Sub- Articles (1) and (2) of this Article all other provisions of this amendment shall be applicable on income derived as of July 8, 2025.
