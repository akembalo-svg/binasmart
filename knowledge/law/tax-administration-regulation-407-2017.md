# Council of Ministers Federal Tax Administration Regulation No. 407/2017 — የሚኒስትሮች ምክር ቤት የፌዴራል ታክስ አስተዳደር ደንብ ቁጥር 407/2009 (read by OCR — check figures against the original)

## What this regulation covers (in brief)

The documents needed to register for tax, biometric information, tax representatives of non-residents, the ceiling on remission of tax debt for hardship, how declarations are filed and tax is paid, the Tax Appeal Commission's hearings, and who may be licensed as a tax agent.

## Key articles

- Art. 4 — an individual applying for registration gives one of: a current residence identity card or a certified copy of the personal-information page of a current Ethiopian or foreign passport; a certified copy of a current Ethiopian driving licence; a certified copy of a current foreign driving licence with photograph; or a certified copy of the birth certificate. The Authority may ask for other evidence. A company attaches its certificate of incorporation or registration; a partnership its partnership deed; any other body its certificate of registration or founding document.
- Art. 5 — for registration the Authority may require biometric information (facial, fingerprint, voice, iris or retina recognition) to identify the individual and counter identity theft or fraud.
- Art. 6 — an agent who is a non-resident's permanent establishment in Ethiopia is that non-resident's tax representative.
- Art. 7 — the most tax debt and late payment interest the Minister may remit for serious hardship is Birr 10,000,000.
- Art. 8 — declarations and other documents are filed, and tax paid, electronically where the Authority has directed it, otherwise by personal delivery or post to a tax office.
- Arts. 9–17 — the Tax Appeal Commission may take evidence on oath and proceed without an absent party; an appellant may withdraw an appeal; an appeal dismissed for non-appearance or non-compliance may be reinstated on application within 30 days; the parties may settle and the Commission decide on the agreed terms.
- Art. 18 — a tax agent needs at least a first degree in taxation, accounting, business, law, economics, management or a similar field and at least 2 years' full-time experience in the previous 5 years in Ethiopia as a tax agent under supervision or as a tax officer; a bankrupt, a person found guilty of professional misconduct, a person convicted of an offence of dishonesty, or one with a poor tax compliance record is not fit and proper.
