# የላሊበላ ውቅር አብያተ ክርስቲያናት የመግቢያ ክፍያ — Lalibela Rock-Hewn Churches Entrance Fee

## በአጭሩ (Amharic summary)

- የላሊበላ ውቅር አብያተ ክርስቲያናት የመግቢያ ክፍያ መጠን በኦፊሴላዊ ምንጭ ላይ አልተገኘም (እስከ 2026-09-17 ድረስ)። የተፈለገው በቱሪዝም ሚኒስቴር ድረ-ገጾች፣ በፍትሕ ሚኒስቴር የክፍያ ደንቦች ዝርዝርና በመንግሥት ሚዲያ ነው።
- በበይነመረብ የሚገኙት የዋጋ መረጃዎች ከጉዞ መድረኮች፣ ከብሎጎችና ከሆቴሎች የመጡ ናቸው። ስላልተረጋገጡ እዚህ አልተጻፉም።
- የቱሪዝም ሚኒስቴር የመዳረሻ ድረ-ገጽ (Visit Ethiopia) እንደሚለው፣ አስጎብኚ ማግኘት በጣም ይመከራል፤ አስጎብኚ የሚገኘው ከሰሜናዊው የአብያተ ክርስቲያናት ቡድን ውጪ ባለው የትኬት መሸጫ ቢሮ ነው።
- ትክክለኛውን ዋጋ ለማወቅ በላሊበላ የሚገኘውን የትኬት ቢሮ ይጠይቁ ወይም የቱሪዝም ሚኒስቴርን ያነጋግሩ፦ ስልክ +251 935 203 138፤ ኢሜይል info@mot.gov.et።
- "የላሊበላ ውቅር አብያተ ክርስቲያናት ዓለም ዓቀፍ ቅርሶች የተከለሉ ስፍራዎች መሰየሚያ የሚኒስትሮች ምክር ቤት ደንብ" ቁጥር 344/2007 (344/2015) በሥራ ላይ ነው። ይህ ደንብ የቅርሱን ክልል የሚሰይም ሲሆን ስለ መግቢያ ክፍያ መኖሩን አላረጋገጥንም።

## In short (English)

- As of 2026-09-17, we found no official source for the Lalibela churches' entrance fee. We checked the Ministry of Tourism websites, the Ministry of Justice list of fee regulations and state media.
- The prices you see online come from travel forums, blogs and hotels. We could not verify them, so they are not given here.
- The Ministry of Tourism's destination site (Visit Ethiopia) says a guide is strongly recommended and can be hired at the ticket office outside the Northern church cluster.
- For the current price, ask at the Lalibela ticket office or contact the Ministry of Tourism: Tel +251 935 203 138, email info@mot.gov.et.
- Council of Ministers Regulation No. 344/2015, which designates the Lalibela World Heritage reserved area, is in force. We did not confirm whether it says anything about entrance fees.

## Official text

**Visit Ethiopia — Lalibela** — https://www.visitethiopia.et/space/lalibela-1 (Ministry of Tourism destination site; contact listed as info@mot.gov.et)

- "A guide is strongly recommended and can be obtained at the ticket office outside the Northern church cluster"
- The page gives no entrance fee or opening hours.

**Ministry of Justice law database** — https://justice.gov.et/en/law/monolithic-churches-of-lalibela-world-heritage-reserved-area-designation-regulation-regulation-no-344-2015/

- "Monolithic Churches of Lalibela World Heritage Reserved Area Designation Council of Ministers Regulation (Council of Ministers Regulation No. 344/2015)"; "Date: August 28, 2015"; "In Force".
- "This Regulation is issued by the Council of Ministers pursuant to Article 5 of the Definition of Powers and Duties of the Executive Organs of the Federal Democratic Republic of Ethiopia Proclamation No.691/2010 and Article 42 (1) of the Research and Conservation of Cultural Heritage Proclamation No 209/2000."
