# ያገለገለ መኪና ማስገባት፦ "አዲስ" እና "ያገለገለ" ተሽከርካሪ በኤክሳይዝ ሕግ — Importing a Used Car: New vs Used Vehicle under the Excise Tax Law (Proclamation 1287/2023)

## በአጭሩ (Amharic summary)
- ከውጭ በሚገባ መኪና ላይ የሚከፈለው የኤክሳይዝ ታክስ መኪናው "አዲስ" ወይም "ያገለገለ" በመሆኑ ይለያያል። ልዩነቱን የሚወስነው የኤክሳይዝ ታክስ (ማሻሻያ) አዋጅ ቁጥር 1287/2015 ነው። አዋጁ ከሚያዝያ 2015 ዓ.ም (29 April 2023) ጀምሮ ጸንቷል።
- ሙሉ በሙሉ ተገጣጥሞ የሚገባ መኪና (ታሪፍ ርዕስ 87.02፣ 87.03፣ 87.04፣ 87.05፣ 87.11) "አዲስ" የሚባለው ሁለት ነገሮች ሲሟሉ ነው። የመጀመሪያው፣ የተመረተበትን ዓመት ጨምሮ በ3 የጎርጎሮሳውያን ዓመት ውስጥ ኢትዮጵያ የጉምሩክ መግቢያ በር መድረስ አለበት። ሁለተኛው፣ እስከ መግቢያ በሩ የተነዳው ርቀት ከ4,000 ኪሎ ሜትር መብለጥ የለበትም።
- በእነዚህ ርዕሶች ያልተመደቡ ሌሎች ተሽከርካሪዎች የተመረቱበትን ዓመት ጨምሮ በ4 ዓመት ውስጥ ከደረሱ "አዲስ" ይባላሉ።
- በሀገር ውስጥ እንዲገጣጠሙ ተበትነው (SKD ወይም CKD) የሚገቡ ተሽከርካሪዎችም "አዲስ" ናቸው።
- ከዚህ ውጭ ያለ ሁሉ "ያገለገለ ተሽከርካሪ" ነው። ዕድሜው የሚቆጠረው ከተመረተበት የጎርጎሮሳውያን ዓመት ጀምሮ ኢትዮጵያ የጉምሩክ መግቢያ በር እስከደረሰበት ዓመት ድረስ ነው።
- ምሳሌ፦ በ2023 (እ.ኤ.አ.) የተመረተ መኪና በ2025 መግቢያ በር ቢደርስና ከ4,000 ኪ.ሜ በታች ቢነዳ፣ 2023፣ 2024 እና 2025 ተቆጥረው በ3 ዓመት ውስጥ ስለሆነ "አዲስ" ይሆናል። ከ4,000 ኪ.ሜ በላይ ከተነዳ ግን "ያገለገለ" ይሆናል።
- የኤክሳይዝ ታክሱ መጣኔ በአዋጁ "ሠንጠረዥ 1" ውስጥ ነው። ታክሱ የሚሰላው ግዴታው በተፈጠረበት ጊዜ በሥራ ላይ ባለው መጣኔ ነው። ይህ ሠንጠረዥ ግን በፍትሕ ሚኒስቴር ቅጂ ውስጥ ስላልተካተተ የዕድሜ ደረጃዎቹንና የመቶኛ መጣኔዎቹን እዚህ መስጠት አልተቻለም።
- የጉምሩክ ቀረጥ፣ ሱር ታክስ፣ የኤሌክትሪክ መኪና የቀረጥ መጣኔ እና በቤንዚን/ናፍጣ የሚሠሩ መኪናዎችን ማስገባት መከልከሉን የሚገልጽ ኦፊሴላዊ ሰነድ አልተገኘም። ከኢትዮጵያ ጉምሩክ ኮሚሽን ወይም ከትራንስፖርትና ሎጂስቲክስ ሚኒስቴር ያረጋግጡ።
- የገቢዎች ሚኒስቴር እንዳብራራው፣ የኤሌክትሪክ ተሽከርካሪዎች ከውጭ ሲገቡም ሆነ በሀገር ውስጥ ሲሸጡ ከተጨማሪ እሴት ታክስ (ቫት) ነጻ አይደሉም (ቀኑ ያልተገለጸ ምላሽ)።
- ከቀረጥ ነጻ የገባ መኪናን ነጻ መብት ለሌለው ሰው መሸጥ የሚቻለው ቀሪውን የጉምሩክ ቀረጥና ታክስ በመክፈል ነው (የገቢዎች ሚኒስቴር)።
- የመኪና ባለቤት ሞቶ መኪናው በኑዛዜ ወይም በውርስ ሲተላለፍ ኤክሳይዝ ታክስ አይከፈልም (አዋጅ 1186/2012 አንቀጽ 33(2))።

## In short (English)
- The excise tax on an imported car depends on whether it counts as "new" or "used". Excise Tax (Amendment) Proclamation No. 1287/2023, in force from 29 April 2023, sets that line.
- A fully built car under tariff headings 87.02, 87.03, 87.04, 87.05 or 87.11 counts as **new** if two conditions are met. First, it must reach the Ethiopian customs entry point within **three Gregorian calendar years**, counting the year it was made. Second, it must have been driven no more than **4,000 km** by the time it arrives.
- Other vehicles, not under those headings, count as new if they arrive within **four** Gregorian calendar years, counting the year of manufacture.
- Vehicles imported as SKD or CKD kits for local assembly also count as new.
- Every other vehicle is **used**. Its age is counted from the Gregorian year it was made to the Gregorian year it reaches the customs entry point.
- Example: a car made in 2023 that reaches the border in 2025 with less than 4,000 km counts 2023, 2024 and 2025. That is within three years, so it is "new". The same car with more than 4,000 km is "used".
- The excise rates are in the First Schedule, and the rate in force when the tax liability arises applies. The Ministry of Justice copy of the proclamation does not include that schedule, so the age bands and percentages cannot be given here.
- No official document was found for customs duty or surtax rates on cars, duty rates for electric vehicles, or the ban on importing petrol and diesel cars. Check with the Ethiopian Customs Commission or the Ministry of Transport and Logistics.
- The Ministry of Revenue says electric vehicles are **not** exempt from VAT, whether imported or sold locally (the answer is undated).
- A car imported duty-free can be sold to someone without a duty-free right only after the remaining customs duty and taxes are paid (Ministry of Revenue).
- No excise tax is due when a car passes to another person by will or inheritance after the owner's death (Proclamation 1186/2020, Art. 33(2)).

## Official text
**Excise Tax (Amendment) Proclamation No. 1287/2023**, Federal Negarit Gazette 29th Year No. 39, 15 September 2023. PDF: https://justice.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/አዋጅ-ቁጥር-1287-2015.pdf

- Preamble: "WHEREAS, it has become necessary to lower the exaggerated excise tax rate imposed on imported new vehicles and some other items; WHEREAS, it is found not proper to grant persons and organizations with special tax and duty privileges to also import goods on which excise tax is imposed because of the damage they inflict on the environment free of excise tax".
- Art. 2(1), new Art. 2(24) of Proclamation 1186/2020: "“New Vehicle” means a vehicle that has not been used over what is stated in this Sub-Article since its manufacturing year and includes the following:
  a) imported in semi-knocked Down (SKD) or Complete Knocked Down (CKD) form to be assembled by local industry,
  b) a Complete Built Up (CBU) vehicle of tariff heading 87.02, 87.03, 87.04, 87.05 and 87.11 which arrives at the Ethiopian customs territory (entry point) within a time period of three Gregorian calendar years including the manufacturing calendar year of the vehicle and when the number of kilometers driven abroad at the time it arrives at the Ethiopian custom territory (entry point) does not exceed 4,000 kilometers,
  c) if the vehicle is not covered under Pharagraph (b) of this Sub-Article it arrives at Ethiopian custom territory (entry point) within a time period of four Gregorian calendar years including the manufacturing calendar year of the vehicle."
- Amharic, Art. 2(24)(ለ) (in part): "...በኢትዮጵያ የጉምሩክ ክልል (መግቢያ በር) በደረሰበት ጊዜ የተመረተበትን የዘመን መቁጠሪያ ዓመት ጨምሮ በጎርጎሮሳውያን ዘመን መቁጠሪያ ዓመት ዕድሜው ከሦስት ዓመት የማያልፍ እና ከውጭ አገር እስከ ኢትዮጵያ የጉምሩክ ክልል (መግቢያ በር) ድረስ የተነዳው ርቀት ከ4ሺ ኪሎ ሜትር ያልበለጠ".
- Art. 2(1), new Art. 2(25): "“Used Vehicle” means a vehicle not covered under Sub-Article (24) of this Article and the age is counted beginning the manufacturing Gregorian calendar year to the Gregorian calendar year of its arrival at Ethiopian customs territory (entry point)."
- Art. 2(2), new Art. 5(2): "Excise Tax shall be charged at the rate specified in the first Schedule attached herewith for the excisable goods and services in force at the time the liability arises for Excise Tax as determined under Article 9 of this Proclamation."
- Art. 3: "This Proclamation shall enter into force on the date of its adoption by the House of Peoples Representatives on the 29 day of April 2023."

**Excise Tax Proclamation No. 1186/2020** (Federal Negarit Gazette No. 25, 17 March 2020; https://justice.gov.et/am/law/the-excise-tax-proclamation/), Art. 33(2): "in the case of a motor vehicle, excise tax shall not be payable where in the case of a natural person, that person dies and the ownership of the motor vehicle is transferred by way of bequest to or inheritance by another person." Art. 44(1) repealed the old Excise Tax Proclamation No. 307/2002.

**Ministry of Revenue FAQ** (mor.gov.et, undated):
- "የኤሌክትሪክ ተሸከርካሪዎች ከውጭ ሲገቡም ሆነ በአገር ውስጥ ግብይት ሲፈፀምባቸው ከተጨማሪ እሴት ታክስ ነፃ አይደሉም።"
- On selling a duty-free vehicle: "በተሸከርካሪው ላይ የሚፈለገውን ቀሪ የጉምሩክ ቀረጥና ታክስ በመክፈል መሸጥ ይቻላል።"

## What is not published
- The age bands and excise percentages for used cars, from the First Schedule of 1287/2023, were not in the official copy found. Ask the Ethiopian Customs Commission or a licensed customs broker for the current rates before you buy.
- Customs duty and surtax rates on cars, and any special duty rate for electric vehicles, were not found in an official source. Ask the Customs Commission.
- No official text or start date was found for the ban on importing petrol and diesel cars. Ask the Ministry of Transport and Logistics or the Customs Commission whether a fuel car can be cleared.
- The Customs Commission's online "Vehicle Verification" and e-SAD services are listed on eservices.gov.et, but their fees were not found.
