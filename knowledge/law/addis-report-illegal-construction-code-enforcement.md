- የግንባታ ፈቃድ ወይም ቁጥጥርን በሚመለከት ቅሬታ በኢትዮጵያ ሕንፃ አዋጅ 1356/2017 አንቀጽ 50 መሠረት ለይግባኝ ሰሚ ቦርድ ይቀርባል።

## In short (English)

- To report illegal construction in Addis Ababa, call the Addis Ababa City Administration Code Enforcement Authority on 0115621721 or short code 9995.
- Office: Piassa, the area commonly called "Eri Bekentu", EMA Tower, 7th–13th floors. Open Monday–Friday 8:30am–5:30pm.
- Alternatively use the Mayor's Office "Informer" service: free call 9977 (24/7); direct lines 011-1-71-02-81, 011-1-71-09-93, 011-1-71-04-77, 011-1-71-05-56; or online at smart.addismayor.gov.et/inform/public/en. Report types include "Issues Related to Land Encroachment" and "Issues Related to Green Areas".
- Rewards for reports are handled under the city's Incentive Regulation No. 121/2013 and Implementation Guideline No. 122/2014.
- "Illegal construction" covers building through land grabbing or illegal expansion of a holding, or building on legal land without a permit, with any material; it includes changes of size, material or design and refurbishment (Reg. 150/2015, Art. 2(16)).
- A code enforcement officer who receives a report must check it and, if confirmed, act (Art. 22(12)).
- Construction inputs used for illegal construction through land grabbing are confiscated (Art. 12(1)). Illegal buildings can be demolished, and land seizure cases are sent to the justice body for prosecution. Fine amounts are in the Regulation's schedule but may have been amended — ask the Authority.
- If you were fined and disagree: complain to the woreda office where the offence happened within 2 working days after the fine's due date; the woreda decides within 5 working days; then sub-city, then the Authority, then court (Art. 18).
- Complaints about building permits and supervision go to the Appeals Board under Building Proclamation 1356/2024, Art. 50.

### Regulation No. 150/2015 E.C. (Addis Negari Gazeta 31st year No. 55, 25 Feb 2023; bilingual)
PDF: https://www.aajs.gov.et/uploads/Publication/150-2015-2024-09-25-66f40331b4972.pdf

**Art. 2(16) (EN).** "“Illegal Construction” means any construction on the surface of land or underneath using wood, plastics, canvas, bricks, metal, or any other material through illegal land seizure or illegal expansion of land possession or without getting a construction permit on a legal land title; and it includes the change in size, material, or design as well as refurbishment;"

**አንቀጽ 2(16) (AM).** "“ሕገ-ወጥ ግንባታ’’ ማለት በሕገወጥ መንገድ መሬትን በመያዝ ወይም ሕገወጥ የመሬት ይዞታ በማስፋፋት በተያዘ ቦታ ወይም በሕጋዊ ይዞታ ላይ ያለ ግንባታ ፈቃድ በእንጨት፣ በፕላስቲክ፣ በሸራ፣ በብሎኬት፣ በብረት ወይም በማናቸዉም ቁሳቁስ ከላይኛዉ የመሬት ገጽታ በላይም ሆነ በታች የተገነባ ማንኛውም ግንባታ ሆኖ የመጠን፣ የቁስም ሆነ የዲዛይን ለዉጥ ያደረገ ሲሆን እድሳትንም ያካተተ ነው፤"

**Art. 12(1) (EN).** "Goods obtained from illegal street vending or construction inputs that are used for illegal construction through land grabbing by violating this Regulation shall be confiscated;"

**Art. 22(12) (EN).** "Shall have the obligation to prevent, control, and take the appropriate measure against illegal activities based on this regulation when he encounters or receive reports of illegal constructions, chat chewing, or shisha smoking activities after examining and confirming the accuracy of the thereof; ..."

**አንቀጽ 22(12) (AM).** "ሕገወጥ ግንባታ፣ ጫት ማስቃም፣ ሽሻ ማስጨስ መፈፀሙን ሲመለከት ወይም ጥቆማ ሲደርሰው እና አጣርቶ ትክክለኛነቱን ሲያረጋግጥ በዚህ ደንብ መሠረት ሕገወጥ ድርጊቱን የመከላከል፣ የመቆጣጠርና ተገቢውን እርምጃ የመውሰድ ግዴታ አለበት፤"

**Art. 18. Complaint Submittal (EN, for persons who were fined).** "1. Any person, to whom an administrative penalty has been imposed by violating this regulation, could lodge his complaint to the woreda office where the offense was committed within two working days after the due date for the payment of the fine; 2. The woreda office shall ... make decisions within 5 (five) working days; ... 4. ... not satisfied with the final decision of the woreda office can submit his complaint to the sub-city office; 5. ... sub-city office can submit his complaint to the authority; 6. ... the authority can submit his complaint to the relevant court."

**Schedule 1 "Illegal Land Seizure and Illegal Construction", Item 1 (EN).** For holding land without permission, or land plus illegal construction: "Prevent, stop, issue warning and organize evidences and send it to the relevant justice body to initiate a prosecution". Item 3 (construction on legally held land without a permit) includes: "Demolish the construction and maintain the previous status quo or return to the legal system".

The Code Enforcement Authority states that Regulation 150/2015 replaced Regulation 54/2005.
