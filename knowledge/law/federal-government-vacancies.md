# የፌዴራል መንግሥት ክፍት የሥራ ቦታዎች — Federal Government Vacancies

**ማሳሰቢያ፦** የፌዴራል መንግሥት ሠራተኞች አዋጅ ቁጥር 1064/2010 በአዋጅ ቁጥር 1353/2017 አንቀጽ 159(2) ተሽሯል። የሲቪል ሰርቪስ ጉዳይ አሁን የሚመራው በኢፌዲሪ ሲቪል ሰርቪስ ኮሚሽን ነው።

**Note:** Federal Civil Servants Proclamation No. 1064/2017 was repealed by Proclamation No. 1353/2025, Art. 159(2). The federal civil service body is now the FDRE Civil Service Commission.

## In short (English)

- Under Proclamation 1353/2025 Art. 28, every federal government office must report each vacant position to the Ethiopian Competency and Management Institute.
- The Institute advertises every vacancy through accessible public media and invites both new applicants and existing civil servants to register.
- The Institute checks that applicants have the required qualifications and a Competency Certificate, compares them, and carries out the hiring (Art. 28(3)).
- The Institute was created by Proclamation 1353/2025 Art. 20. It trains civil servants and leaders and provides competency assessment and certification. Its powers are to be set by a Council of Ministers regulation.
- The FDRE Civil Service Commission is an autonomous federal body (Proclamation 1263/2021 Art. 42). Its website is fcsc.ecsc.gov.et.
- The Commission's "Vacancies" page is https://fcsc.ecsc.gov.et/am/vacancies. On 17 September 2026 it showed no listings.
- Commission contacts: Tel +251 11 5 54 96 66 /67/68, Fax +251115549707, P.O. Box 1080, email ethiocs@gmail.com, Facebook "Civil service commission".
- No single official online job portal for federal jobs was found. Private job-listing sites are not official.
- Whether the Institute has actually started running recruitment is not published. If you see an advert, confirm it directly with the hiring office.

## Official text

### Proclamation No. 1353/2025 — Art. 28 (Vacancy Announcement and Recruitment Procedures)
Source: https://justice.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/አዋጅ-ቁጥር-1353-2017.pdf (law page: https://justice.gov.et/en/law/federal-civil-servants-proclamation-4/)

> "28. Vacancy Announcement and Recruitment Procedures 1/ Any Government office shall report every vacant position to the institute. 2/ The institute shall advertise every vacant position of any Government office through accessible public media to invite existing employee and a new entrant for registration. 3/ Notwithstanding provision of this proclamation Article 24 which stipulate eligibility for employment and Article 26 wich state about the requirement of pre-employment training respectively, the institute is required to verify permanent employee and new entrant applicants that meet the required qualification and hold Competency Certificate and subsequently compute to undertake employment."

> "፳፰. ማስታወቂያ ስለማውጣትና ስለቅጥር አፈጻጸም ፩/ ማንኛውም የመንግሥት መሥሪያ ቤት ክፍት የሥራ መደብ ሲኖረው ለኢንስቲትዩቱ ያሳውቃል፡፡ ፪/ ኢንስቲትዩቱ የማንኛውንም መንግሥት መሥሪያ ቤት ክፍት ሥራ መደብ ተደራሽነት ባለው መገናኛ ብዙኀን ማስታወቂያ በማውጣት አዲስ አመልካች እና ነባር መንግሥት ሰራተኛ እንዲመዘገብ ማድረግ አለበት፡፡ ፫/ በዚህ አዋጅ አንቀጽ ፳፬ መሠረት ለመቀጠር የሚያስችሉ ሁኔታ እና በአንቀጽ ፳፮ ስለዝግጁነት ስልጠና የተደነገገው እንደተጠበቀ ሆኖ የነባር ሠራተኛ ወይም የዝግጁነት ሰልጣኝ የሥራ ቅጥር አመልካች ተፈላጊ ችሎታ ማሟላታቸውን እና የብቃት የምስክር ማረጋገጫ ወረቀት ያላቸው መሆኑን በማረጋገጥ እና በማወዳደር ኢንስቲትዩቱ ቅጥር መፈጸም አለበት፡፡"

### Proclamation No. 1353/2025 — Art. 20 (Establishment of the Institute)
Amharic heading: "፳. የኢትዮጵያ ብቃትና ሥራ አመራር ኢንስቲትዩት"

> "20. Establishment of Ethiopian Competency and Management Institute 1/ The Ethiopian Competency and Management Institute /hereinafter the ''Institute''/ is hereby established by this proclamation. This Institute provides training for the civil servants and leaders shall be created by short term technical and behavioural competency modular training, supplementary education, research and studies, consultancy service and Competency Assessment and Certificate. 2/ The Powers, Duties and Organization of the Institute shall be determined by the Council of Ministers Regulation."
