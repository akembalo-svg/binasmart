# በአዲስ አበባ የከተማ አስተዳደር ቅሬታና ጥቆማ ማቅረቢያ መስመሮች — Addis Ababa City Complaint and Reporting Lines

## በአጭሩ (Amharic summary)

- በአዲስ አበባ ከተማ አስተዳደር ተቋማት አገልግሎት ላይ ቅሬታ ለማቅረብ የከንቲባ ጽ/ቤት ስማርት ፖርታል የቅሬታ ቅጽን ይጠቀሙ፦ smart.addismayor.gov.et/complaint/en/all
- ቅጹ ሁለት ዓይነት ቅሬታ ይቀበላል፦ "የመልካም አስተዳደር ቅሬታ" (Good governance complain) እና "መደበኛ ቅሬታ" (Formal complaint)። ቅሬታው በዘርፍ፣ በወረዳ፣ በክፍለ ከተማ ወይም በማዕከል ደረጃ ይታያል።
- በቅጹ ላይ ከሚመረጡት ዘርፎች መካከል የግንባታ ፈቃድና ቁጥጥር ባለሥልጣን፣ የደንብ ማስከበር ጽ/ቤት፣ የመሬት አስተዳደር ጽ/ቤት እና የአዲስ አበባ ፍርድ ቤቶች ይገኙበታል።
- ቅሬታ ቅጹ ላይ ስልክ ቁጥር አልተገለጸም።
- ሕገ-ወጥ ድርጊትን (ለምሳሌ የመሬት ወረራ፣ የአረንጓዴ ቦታ፣ የመንግሥት ቤት ጉዳዮች) ለማሳወቅ፦ የ"Informer" አገልግሎት ነፃ ስልክ 9977 (24 ሰዓት)፤ ቀጥታ ስልኮች 011-1-71-02-81፣ 011-1-71-09-93፣ 011-1-71-04-77፣ 011-1-71-05-56፤ ድረ-ገጽ smart.addismayor.gov.et/inform/public/en።
- ይህ አገልግሎት ጉዳዮችን አይዳኝም፣ ፍርድ ቤትንም አይተካም። በፍርድ ቤት እየታዩ ያሉ፣ በሕግ የታገዱ ወይም በፀረ-ሙስና ኮሚሽን እየተያዙ ያሉ ጉዳዮችን አይቀበልም።
- ሕገ-ወጥ ግንባታ፣ የጎዳና ላይ ንግድና ተመሳሳይ ደንብ ጥሰቶች፦ የደንብ ማስከበር ባለሥልጣን ስልክ 0115621721 ወይም 9995።
- የአዲስ አበባ ከተማ ፍትሕ ቢሮ፦ ስልክ +251115585011።
- ለሁሉም የከተማ ጉዳዮች የሚያገለግል አንድ አጠቃላይ የቅሬታ ስልክ በይፋ አልተገኘም፤ የሕዝብ እምባ ጠባቂ ተቋም ስልክም በይፋዊ ገጽ አልተረጋገጠም።

## In short (English)

- To complain about an Addis Ababa city office, use the Mayor's Office Smart portal complaint form: smart.addismayor.gov.et/complaint/en/all
- It takes two types: "Good governance complain" and "Formal complaint". Complaints are seen by the sector, woreda, sub-city or central level.
- Sectors on the form include the Construction Permit and Control Authority, Office of Law Enforcement, Land Administration Office and Addis Ababa Courts.
- The complaint page shows no phone number.
- To report illegal acts (e.g. land encroachment, green areas, government houses): Informer free call 9977 (24/7); direct lines 011-1-71-02-81, 011-1-71-09-93, 011-1-71-04-77, 011-1-71-05-56; online at smart.addismayor.gov.et/inform/public/en.
- The Informer directorate does not decide cases or replace courts, and does not take matters under legal injunction, before a court, or already with the anti-corruption commission.
- Illegal construction, street vending and other code violations: Code Enforcement Authority 0115621721 or 9995.
- Addis Ababa City Justice Bureau: +251115585011.
- No single general city complaints hotline, and no Ombudsman phone, was found on an official page.

## Official text

### Smart portal complaint form (verbatim)
Source: https://smart.addismayor.gov.et/complaint/en/all
- Complaint types: "Good governance complain", "Formal complaint".
- Routing: "Seen by Sector / Seen by Woreda / Seen by Subcity / Seen by Central".
- The page refers to "Order No. 143/2015" ("Cases where the standard complaint procedure does not apply to Order No. 143/2015").
- Sectors listed include "Construction Permit and Control Authority", "Office of Law Enforcement", "Land Administration Office", "Addis Ababa Courts".

### Informer — reporting illegal acts (verbatim)
Source: https://smart.addismayor.gov.et/inform/public/en
- "Free Call Number 9977"
- "Free Call Number (24/7 available)"
- "Direct Call Number 011-1-71-02-81 / 011-1-71-09-93 / 011-1-71-04-77 / 011-1-71-05-56" (tel: +251111710281, +251111710993, +251111710477, +251111710556)

- Operator: "Addis Ababa City Administration Information Receiving and Refining Directorate".
- "The Directorate does not adjudicate cases or replace court or legal proceedings."
- It handles reports "that are not under legal injunction, are not sub judice (under court review), and are not already being handled by the anti-corruption commission."
