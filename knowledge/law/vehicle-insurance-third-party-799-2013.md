## What this law covers (in brief)

Compulsory third-party motor insurance: every vehicle on the road must be insured against death, injury and property damage it causes to others; what the policy excludes; insurance certificates and stickers; compensation limits; emergency medical treatment for accident victims; and the fund that pays when the vehicle was uninsured or not traced.

## Key articles

- Art. 3 — no one may drive or use a vehicle on a road without valid third-party insurance for it.
- Art. 7 — the policy does not cover, among others, death, injury or property damage of the insured person or his family, injury to the insured's employees in the course of employment, damage to the insured vehicle itself, and goods carried for hire.
- Arts. 9–14 — certificate of insurance, its validity, the insurance sticker, and replacements.
- Art. 15 — the insured must be covered for liability from collision, fire or explosion, objects falling from the vehicle, and damage from the vehicle's movement.
- Art. 16 — as printed in 2013: compensation not less than Birr 5,000 and not more than Birr 40,000 for the death of one person, up to Birr 40,000 for bodily injury of one person, up to Birr 100,000 for property damage (the OCR reads "100,090", almost certainly 100,000); medical expenses count within these amounts. IMPORTANT: Regulation No. 554/2024 (Art. 4) replaced the compensation amounts with a "Schedule B", and the premium tariff with a "Schedule A". The schedules are not in the published PDF used here, so the current amounts are not in this file — ask the insurer or the Road Safety and Insurance Fund Service.
- Art. 18 — the insurer must pay a court judgment against the insured for covered liability.
- Arts. 19–23 — the Fund: when the vehicle was uninsured or not traced, it provides emergency medical treatment to the injured and compensation to injured persons and to the families of those killed, up to the Art. 16 limits, and recovers the cost from the person responsible.
- Emergency treatment (Part Six, miscellaneous provisions) — any person injured in a vehicle accident is entitled to emergency medical treatment costing up to Birr 2,000 (as printed in 2013), whether a third party or not; every medical institution must give emergency treatment to an accident victim who comes to it and may claim the cost under a Ministry directive.
- Art. 30 — penalty: violating the proclamation or its regulations: fine of Birr 3,000 to 5,000 or imprisonment of one to two years (as printed in 2013).
