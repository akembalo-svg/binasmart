# የአካል ጉዳተኝነት መታወቂያ በአዲስ አበባ — Disability ID Card in Addis Ababa

## በአጭሩ (Amharic summary)

- የሴቶችና ማህበራዊ ጉዳይ ሚኒስቴር (ሴ.ማ.ጉ.ሚ) ለአካል ጉዳተኞች መታወቂያ ይሰጣል። ይህን የሚገልጸው የገንዘብ ሚኒስቴር መመሪያ ቁጥር 894/2014 ነው።
- በመመሪያው መሠረት መታወቂያው የባለመብቱን ስም፣ አድራሻ እና ፎቶግራፍ ያሳያል። በሕክምና ተቋም የተረጋገጠውን የአካል ጉዳት ዓይነትና መጠንም ያሳያል።
- መመሪያው ይህን መታወቂያ የሚጠቅሰው ከቀረጥና ታክስ ነፃ ለሚገቡ የአካል ጉዳተኞች ተሽከርካሪዎች ነው። መታወቂያው ሁልጊዜ በተሽከርካሪው ውስጥ መኖር አለበት፤ የሚመለከተው አካል ሲጠይቅም መታየት አለበት።
- በአዲስ አበባ ጉዳዩን የሚከታተለው የአዲስ አበባ ከተማ አስተዳደር ሴቶች፣ ሕፃናትና ማህበራዊ ጉዳይ ቢሮ ነው። በቢሮው ውስጥ "የአካል ጉዳተኞች፣ አረጋውያን እና ቤተሰብ ጉዳዮች ማስተባበሪያና መከታተያ ዳይሬክቶሬት" አለ። ዳይሬክተሯ ወ/ሮ ህይወት በቀለ ናቸው።
- ቢሮው የሚገኘው ፒያሳ (ደጃች ውቤ) ነው። ስልክ፦ +251 118-72-32-97 ወይም +251 118-58-15-52። ኢሜይል፦ bowcsa@aawcsa.gov.et
- ዳይሬክቶሬቱ በድረ-ገጹ ላይ የዘረዘራቸው ተግባራት የአካል ጉዳተኞችና የአረጋውያን ማህበራትን መደገፍ፣ የሰው ሠራሽ አካልና የአካል ድጋፍ አገልግሎት ማመቻቸት እና የተሃድሶ አገልግሎት መስጠት ናቸው። የመታወቂያ አገልግሎት በዝርዝሩ ውስጥ የለም።
- በአዲስ አበባ መታወቂያውን ለማውጣት የሚያስፈልጉ ሰነዶች፣ ክፍያ፣ የአሰራር ቅደም ተከተልና የሚፈጀው ጊዜ በይፋ አልተገለጸም። ከመሄድዎ በፊት ቢሮውን በስልክ ይጠይቁ።
- ከቀረጥ ነፃ ተሽከርካሪ ለማስገባት የቀድሞው መመሪያ 230/2013 እነዚህን ሰነዶች ይጠይቃል፦ የነዋሪነት መታወቂያ፣ በጤና ሚኒስቴር እውቅና ካለው ሆስፒታል የሐኪሞች ቦርድ የሰጠው የሕክምና ማስረጃ፣ እና የአካል ጉዳተኝነት ማረጋገጫ የድጋፍ ደብዳቤ። ደብዳቤውን ቀድሞ የሠራተኛና ማህበራዊ ጉዳይ ሚኒስቴር ይሰጥ ነበር፤ ይህ ሥልጣን አሁን ወደ ሴ.ማ.ጉ.ሚ ተላልፏል።
- የአካል ጉዳተኞች የሥራ ስምሪት መብት በአዋጅ ቁጥር 568/2000 ተደንግጓል። ይህ አዋጅ ስለ መታወቂያ የሚናገረው ነገር የለም።

## In short (English)

- The Ministry of Women and Social Affairs (MoWSA) issues an ID card for persons with disabilities. This is stated in Ministry of Finance Directive No. 894/2014 E.C.
- Under the directive, the ID shows the holder's name, address and photograph. It also shows the type and degree of the disability as certified by a health institution.
- The directive mentions this ID in the rules on duty-free vehicles for persons with disabilities. The ID must be kept in the vehicle at all times and shown when the concerned body asks for it.
- In Addis Ababa, the responsible office is the Addis Ababa Women, Children and Social Affairs Bureau. Inside it is the "Persons with Disabilities, Elderly and Family Affairs Coordination and Monitoring Directorate".
- The bureau is in Piassa (Dejach Wube). Phone: +251 118-72-32-97 or +251 118-58-15-52. Email: bowcsa@aawcsa.gov.et
- The directorate's website lists its work as supporting associations of persons with disabilities and older people, arranging prosthetic and orthotic services, and providing rehabilitation services. An ID card service is not on that list.
- The required documents, fee, steps and processing time for the disability ID in Addis Ababa are not published. Call the bureau before you go.
- For importing a duty-free vehicle, the earlier Directive 230/2013 asks for: a residence ID; a medical certificate from a board of doctors at a hospital recognised by the Ministry of Health; and a support letter confirming the disability. That letter used to come from the Ministry of Labour and Social Affairs. This power now belongs to MoWSA.
- The employment rights of persons with disabilities are set out in Proclamation No. 568/2008. That law says nothing about an ID card.

Unofficial translation: "An identification card issued by the Ministry of Women and Social Affairs, showing the beneficiary's name, address, photograph and the type and degree of disability certified by a health institution, must be kept in the vehicle at all times and shown when requested by the concerned body."

Effective date: "ይህ መመሪያ ከግንቦት 26 ቀን 2014 ዓ.ም. ጀምሮ የፀና ይሆናል" (in force from 26 Ginbot 2014 E.C., about 3 June 2022). The new Art. 5(2) of the same directive also requires confirmation "በሐኪሞች ቦርድ እና በሴቶች እና ማህበራዊ ጉዳይ ሚኒስቴር" (by a board of doctors and MoWSA) that the person cannot drive.

### MoF Directive No. 230/2013 E.C. — Art. 6 (preconditions for a duty-free vehicle)

Sub-articles 1–3 require: (1) Ethiopian nationality and a residence ID from the local administration; (2) a medical certificate, issued by a board of doctors at a hospital in Ethiopia recognised by the Ministry of Health, confirming the disability and its type; (3) a disability confirmation support letter from the Ministry of Labour and Social Affairs.

### Proclamation No. 568/2008 (Right to Employment of Persons with Disability) — Art. 4(1), 5(3)
Source: https://justice.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/አዋጅ-ቁጥር-568-2000.pdf

> "4(1) Unless the nature of the work dictates otherwise, a person with disability having the necessary qualification and scored more to that of other candidates shall have the right with out any discrimination: a) to occupy a vacant post in any office or undertaking through recruitment, promotion, placement or transfer procedures; or b) to participate in a training programme to be conducted either locally or abroad."

> "5(3) When a disabled person is not in a position to exercise his equal right of employment opportunity, as a result of absence of a reasonable accommodation, such an act shall be regarded as discrimination."
