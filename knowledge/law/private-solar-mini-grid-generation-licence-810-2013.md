# የግል ኩባንያ የፀሐይ ኃይል ማመንጨትና መሸጥ (ኦፍ-ግሪድ፣ ሚኒ-ግሪድ፣ IPP ፈቃድ) — Can a Private Company Generate and Sell Solar Power? (Off-grid, Mini-grid and IPP Licences)

## በአጭሩ (Amharic summary)

- አዎ፣ የግል ሰው ወይም ኩባንያ ኤሌክትሪክ አመንጭቶ መሸጥ ይችላል። ነገር ግን ለንግድ ዓላማ ኤሌክትሪክ ለማመንጨት፣ ለማስተላለፍ፣ ለማከፋፈልና ለመሸጥ በመጀመሪያ ፈቃድ ማውጣት ግዴታ ነው (አዋጅ 810/2006 አንቀጽ 6(1))።
- ለራስ ጥቅም ብቻ (ለንግድ ያልሆነ) ኤሌክትሪክ የሚያመነጭ ሰው ፈቃድ ሳይሆን አስቀድሞ ለባለሥልጣኑ ማሳወቅ አለበት። የአካባቢ ጥበቃና የደህንነት መስፈርቶችን ማሟላቱን የሚያሳዩ ሰነዶችንም ያቀርባል (አንቀጽ 6(2))።
- በአሁኑ ጊዜ በኢነርጂ ዘርፍ የንግድ ፈቃድና የሙያ ሰርተፊኬት የሚሰጠው የነዳጅና ኢነርጂ ባለሥልጣን ነው (ደንብ 521/2014 አንቀጽ 5(3))። ዋና መሥሪያ ቤቱ አዲስ አበባ ነው።
- የፈቃድ ማመልከቻ በባለሥልጣኑ ቅጽ ይቀርባል። አብረው የሚቀርቡት፦ የአመልካቹ ማንነትና አድራሻ፣ የገንዘብ አቅም ማስረጃ፣ የቴክኒክ ብቃትና ልምድ እንዲሁም የማመልከቻ መመርመሪያ ክፍያ ደረሰኝ ናቸው (ደንብ 447/2011 አንቀጽ 3(1))።
- የማመንጨት ፈቃድ ማመልከቻ የኃይል ምንጩን፣ የፕሮጀክቱን ቦታ ካርታ፣ ጠቅላላ የማመንጨት አቅሙን፣ ከብሔራዊ ግሪድ ጋር የሚገናኝ ወይም ኦፍ-ግሪድ መሆኑን፣ ቢያንስ የቅድመ-አዋጭነት ጥናትን እና የፋይናንስ ምንጩን መያዝ አለበት (አንቀጽ 7(1))።
- ፈቃድ ከመሰጠቱ በፊት ማመልከቻው በአመልካቹ ወጪ በጋዜጣ ወይም በሌላ መገናኛ ብዙኃን ይታወጃል። ጉዳት ይደርስብኛል የሚል ሰው በ30 ቀናት ውስጥ ተቃውሞ ማቅረብ ይችላል (አንቀጽ 5)።
- ለብሔራዊ ግሪድ በጅምላ የሚሸጥ የግል አምራች (IPP) የኃይል ግዢ ስምምነት (PPA) ያስፈልገዋል። የኃይል ግዢ ስምምነቶችን የሚያጸድቀው ባለሥልጣኑ ነው (ደንብ 447 አንቀጽ 2(22)፣ 7(2)፤ ደንብ 521 አንቀጽ 5(12))።
- የፀሐይ፣ የንፋስና የባዮማስ ማመንጫ ፈቃድ እስከ 20 ዓመት ያገለግላል፤ እስከ 10 ዓመት ሊታደስ ይችላል። ለእድሳት ፈቃዱ ከማለቁ አንድ ዓመት በፊት ማመልከት ያስፈልጋል (ደንብ 447 አንቀጽ 12)።
- የፀሐይ ፈቃድ ያገኘ ሰው ፈቃዱ በተሰጠ በ3 ዓመት ውስጥ ማመንጨት መጀመር አለበት። ካልጀመረ ፈቃዱ ሊሰረዝ ይችላል (አዋጅ 1085/2010)።
- ኤሌክትሪክን ወደ ውጭ መላክና ከውጭ ማስገባት ለመንግሥት ብቻ የተከለለ ነው (አዋጅ 1085/2010)።
- ለኦፍ-ግሪድ አነስተኛ ማመንጫና ስርጭት ፈቃድ ባለሥልጣኑ የተለየ መመሪያ ያወጣል (ደንብ 447 አንቀጽ 3(8))። የኦፍ-ግሪድ ታሪፍ መመሪያም በባለሥልጣኑ ይወጣል (ደንብ 591/2018)።
- ብሔራዊ ግሪድ ወደ ኦፍ-ግሪድ ኔትወርክዎ ቢደርስ፣ ግሪዱ በስምምነትና ተመጣጣኝ ካሳ በመክፈል ሊያዋህደው ይችላል። በካሳ ላይ ካልተስማሙ ባለሥልጣኑ በግልግል ይወስናል (ደንብ 447 አንቀጽ 81)።
- የፈቃድ ክፍያ መጠንና የሚኒ-ግሪድ መመሪያው ሙሉ ጽሑፍ በይፋ አልተገኘም። የነዳጅና ኢነርጂ ባለሥልጣንን ይጠይቁ።

## In short (English)

- Yes, a private person or company may generate and sell electricity, but a licence is required first to generate, transmit, or distribute and sell electricity for commercial purposes (Proclamation 810/2013 Art. 6(1)).
- For non-commercial (own-use) generation, no licence is needed, but you must notify the Authority in advance and show that environmental and safety conditions are met (Art. 6(2)).
- Energy-sector business licences and professional certificates are now issued by the Petroleum and Energy Authority (PEA), head office in Addis Ababa (Regulation 521/2022 Art. 5(3), Art. 3).
- Apply on the Authority's form with: identity and address, proof of financial capacity, technical competency and experience, and the receipt for the application examination fee (Regulation 447/2019 Art. 3(1)).
- A generation-licence application states the energy source, a site map, total capacity, whether the plant connects to the grid or runs off-grid, at least a prefeasibility study, and planned finance (Art. 7(1)).
- Before a licence is issued, the application is published in a newspaper or other media at the applicant's expense, and objections may be filed within 30 days (Art. 5).
- An independent power producer (IPP) sells bulk power to the national grid under a power purchase agreement (PPA). The Authority approves PPAs (Reg. 447 Art. 2(22), 7(2); Reg. 521 Art. 5(12)).
- Solar, wind and biomass generation licences last up to 20 years and can be renewed for up to 10 years. Apply for renewal one year before expiry (Reg. 447 Art. 12).
- A solar licensee must start generating within 3 years of the licence being issued. The licence can be revoked if generation does not start (Proclamation 1085/2018).
- Only the government may export or import electricity (1085/2018).
- The Authority issues a separate directive for off-grid small-scale generation, distribution and sale licences (Reg. 447 Art. 3(8)), and also issues the directives that set off-grid tariffs (Reg. 591/2026).
- If the national grid reaches an off-grid network, the grid licensee may take it over by agreement and pay reasonable compensation. The Authority arbitrates disputes over compensation (Reg. 447 Art. 81).
- Licence fees and the full Mini-Grid Directive were not found in official sources. Ask the Petroleum and Energy Authority.

## Official text

**Energy Proclamation No. 810/2013, Art. 6 (English column read by OCR)** — https://justice.gov.et/en/law/energy-proclamation/
> 1/ No person may generate, transmit, distribute and sale, import or export electricity for commercial purposes or engage in energy efficiency and conservation consulting, energy service contracting, energy audit or in other related activities without having a license in accordance with this Proclamation and the regulation to be issued hereunder.
> 2/ Any person desiring to generate, transmit or distribute electricity for non-commercial purposes shall notify the Authority in advance and produce documents evidencing that he has fulfilled environmental protection and safety conditions.

**Art. 7:**
> Any person shall be issued with a license where the Authority is satisfied that he meets the requirements of the license specified in this Proclamation, regulation to be issued hereunder and the investment and other relevant laws, qualifies to carry on trade under the Commercial Code, and has the financial resources, technical competence, professional skill and experience required to fulfill the license obligations.

**Energy (Amendment) Proclamation No. 1085/2018, Art. 2(3), replacing Art. 10(3) of 810/2013 (original spelling kept)** — https://justice.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/አዋጅ-ቁጥር-1085-2010.pdf
> b/ Any person who obtained license on solar, wind, biomass, coal, natural gas and others license to engage in the generation, electricity shall commence electricity generation, in accordance with the conditions of the licence within three years of the issuance of a license, and accordingly report to the Authority about its commencement.
> Electricity export to or import is exlusively resereved for the government.

Art. 2(2) (new Art. 8(6)): the licence "shall, without any condition, be revoked if not commenced operation within two years or failed to comply with sub-article (a) and (b) of Article 10".
