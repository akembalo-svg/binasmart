# ዳያስፖራ ቤት መግዛት ይችላል? — Can diaspora and foreigners buy a house or condominium in Ethiopia (foreign currency rules)

## በአጭሩ (Amharic summary)

- **በውጭ የሚኖር ኢትዮጵያዊ ዜጋ** እንደማንኛውም ኢትዮጵያዊ ቤት የመግዛት መብት አለው፤ የውጭ ዜጎችን የሚመለከተው አዋጅ 1388/2017 በእርሱ ላይ አይሠራም።
- **ትውልደ ኢትዮጵያዊ የውጭ ዜጋ** (በአዋጅ 270/1994 መታወቂያ ያለው)፦ የውጭ ዜጎች የማይንቀሳቀስ ንብረት እንዳይዙ የሚከለክሉት የፍትሐ ብሔር ሕግ አንቀጽ 390–393 **አይመለከቱትም**፤ ቪዛ ወይም የመኖሪያ ፈቃድ አያስፈልገውም፤ እንደ ሀገር ውስጥ ባለሀብት ይቆጠራል። አዋጅ 1388/2017 "የውጭ ዜጋ" ከሚለው ትርጉም ትውልደ ኢትዮጵያውያንን አውጥቷቸዋል፤ ልዩ መብታቸው እንደተጠበቀ ነው። (ምርጫ የመምረጥና በመከላከያ፣ በደኅንነትና በውጭ ጉዳይ በቋሚነት የመቀጠር መብት የላቸውም።)
- **ሌሎች የውጭ ዜጎች** (አዋጅ 1388/2017፣ ነጋሪት ጋዜጣ መስከረም 22 ቀን 2018 ዓ.ም. / ኦክቶበር 2 ቀን 2025)፦ በአንድ ቤት ቢያንስ **150,000 የአሜሪካ ዶላር** (የሙሉ ሊዝ ዋጋን ጨምሮ) ሲያወጡ የመኖሪያ ቤት ባለቤት መሆን ይችላሉ፤ በአንድ ጊዜ **አንድ ቤት** ብቻ (ሚኒስቴሩ በመመሪያ ሊለውጠው ይችላል)።
- ቤቱ መሆን ያለበት **በሊዝ በጨረታ በተሰጠ መሬት** ላይ ነው። የሊዝ፣ የቤት ግዢ፣ የግንባታ ፈቃድና የመጀመሪያ ዓመት ክፍያዎች **በውጭ ምንዛሪ** ይከፈላሉ፤ የሊዝ ዋጋ **በአንድ ጊዜ** ሙሉ በሙሉ ይከፈላል። ከሀገር ውስጥ ባንክ ብድር ወይም ከሀገር ውስጥ በተሰበሰበ ገንዘብ መግዛት አይፈቀድም።
- የውጭ ዜጋ **በመንግሥት ድጎማ ለዜጎች በተገነባ ኮንዶሚኒየም** ውስጥ ቤት መያዝ **አይችልም**፤ በመንግሥት–የግል አጋርነት ወይም ለትርፍ ለገበያ በቀረቡ የመንግሥት ፕሮጀክቶች ግን ይፈቀዳል።
- ፈቃድ የሚሰጠው **የከተማና መሠረተ ልማት ሚኒስቴር** ነው፤ ማመልከቻ በአካል ወይም በኦንላይን ይቀርባል፤ ውሳኔው በ**30 የሥራ ቀናት** ይሰጣል። መስፈርቶች፦ ማንነትና ዜግነትን የሚያሳይ ሰነድ፣ የገንዘብ አቅም ማስረጃ፣ የወንጀል ሪከርድ አለመኖር፣ የደኅንነት ገደብ አለመኖር።
- ቤት ያለው የውጭ ዜጋ ለራሱና ለቤተሰቡ የመኖሪያ ፈቃድ ወይም **እስከ 5 ዓመት የሚያገለግል ተደጋጋሚ መግቢያ ቪዛ** ያገኛል፤ ከኪራይ ወይም ከሽያጭ ያገኘውን ገቢ በብሔራዊ ባንክ መመሪያ መሠረት ወደ ውጭ ማስተላለፍ ይችላል። ቤቱን ለንግድ ሥራ መጠቀም አይችልም (ለመኖሪያ ማከራየት ግን ይቻላል)።
- **የ40/60 የቤት ልማት ፕሮግራም**፦ የኢትዮጵያ ዳያስፖራ አገልግሎት ለተመዘገቡ ዳያስፖራዎች ዶላርን ወደ ብር በመቀየር የቁጠባ ክፍያ የመፈጸም አገልግሎት ይሰጣል። የሚያስፈልጉ፦ በፕሮግራሙ መመዝገብ፣ የውጭ ምንዛሪው ምንጭ ማስረጃ፣ የጸና መታወቂያ፣ የክፍያ መርሐ ግብር።
- አሁን ኮንዶሚኒየም በቀጥታ በውጭ ምንዛሪ የሚገዛበት ኦፊሴላዊ ፕሮግራም በዚህ ምንጭ **አልተገኘም**።
- የዳያስፖራ አገልግሎት፦ ብሉም ታወር 5ኛና 6ኛ ፎቅ፣ አዲስ አበባ፤ ስልክ 011 554 4600፤ diaspora.service@mfa.gov.et።

## In short (English)

- **Ethiopian citizens living abroad** can buy homes like any Ethiopian; Proclamation 1388/2025 for foreigners does not apply to them.
- **Foreign nationals of Ethiopian origin** (ID under Proclamation 270/2002): Civil Code Articles 390–393 (restrictions on foreigners owning immovables) do **not** apply; no visa or residence permit needed; treated as domestic investors. Proclamation 1388/2025 excludes them from "foreign national" and keeps their special rights. (No right to vote or to regular employment in defence, security or foreign affairs.)
- **Other foreigners** (Proclamation 1388/2025, Gazette of 2 Oct 2025): may own a home with at least **USD 150,000 per house** (including the full lease price); **one house at a time** unless the Ministry changes this by directive.
- The home must be on **leasehold land allocated by public bid**. Lease, purchase, construction-permit and first-year government fees are paid **in foreign currency**; the lease price is paid **in one instalment**. No financing from domestic banks or domestically raised capital.
- Foreigners may **not** own a unit in **government-subsidised condominiums for citizens**; exceptions: PPP schemes and government projects built for profit and sold on the market.
- Permit: **Ministry of Urban and Infrastructure Development**, in person or online; decision within **30 working days**. Requirements: identity/nationality documents, proof of funds, no criminal record, no security restriction.
- Owners get a residence permit or a **multiple-entry visa of up to five years** for themselves and family, and may transfer rental/sale income abroad under NBE rules. No commercial use (renting as a residence is allowed).
- **40/60 housing programme**: the Ethiopian Diaspora Service offers a savings-payment (dollar-to-birr conversion) service for registered diaspora; needs programme registration, proof of FX source, valid ID and the payment schedule.
- No official current scheme for buying a condominium directly in foreign currency was found.
- Ethiopian Diaspora Service: Bloom Tower 5th & 6th floor, Addis Ababa; 011 554 4600; diaspora.service@mfa.gov.et.

## Official text

**Proclamation No. 1388/2025**, Federal Negarit Gazette No. 67, 2 October 2025 (PDF: https://justice.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/አዋጅ-ቁጥር-1388-2017-1.pdf)
- Art. 2(1): "'Foreign National' means any natural person or legal entity that does not possess an Ethiopian Nationality or holds the Nationality of another country, excluding a Foreign National of Ethiopian origin;" Art. 2(7): "'Ministry' means the Ministry of Urban and Infrastructure Development."
- Art. 3(2): "The special right and procedure established under the applicable law in respect of foreigners of Ethiopian origin shall remain applicable."
- Art. 4(2): "... a foreign national may acquire ownership of a residential house only on land provided through a lease and allocated under a public bidding process."
- Art. 6(1): "The minimum amount of money allocated for the construction of a house or the purchase of a house, including the full lease price, ... shall not be less than one hundred fifty thousand United States Dollars (USD 150,000.00) per residential house." Art. 6(2): "Any foreign national shall have one residential house at a time, however, the Ministry ... may modify this number by a Directive."
- Art. 7(1): "a) submission of valid legal documents that disclose the name, nationality, and other identifying particulars of the applicant; b) proof of sufficient financial capacity ...; c) having no criminal record; d) be free any restriction arising from considerations of national peace, security, public order ...; e) obtain authorization from the Ministry."
- Art. 8(1): "... it is prohibited to use the residential house for commercial purposes." Art. 8(2): "a) A residence permit ...; or b) The right to obtain a multiple-entry visa valid for up to five years ...; c) The right to transfer the rental or sale income legally obtained from the residential house abroad in foreign currency ... in accordance with the Directives and procedures issued by the National Bank of Ethiopia."
- Art. 9(2): "b) paying the lease, house purchase, construction permit, or first-year related government service fees required to own a house only in the equivalent foreign currency acceptable in Ethiopia; c) if one acquires land on lease to own a residential house, one must pay the entire lease price in single installment."
- Art. 10(2)–(4): "No foreign citizen may own a dwelling in a condominium building constructed for citizens with direct government subsidy." "The restriction ... shall not apply to residential houses built by a Federal or Regional government through: a) public-private partnership scheme ...; or b) Residential projects implemented for profit and availed to the market." "No foreign national shall be permitted to acquire ownership of a residential house through financing obtained from domestic financial institutions, nor by mobilizing or collecting capital from sources within the Country."
- Art. 13(2): application "either in person or through the online system established by the Ministry". Art. 14(1): decision "within thirty (30) working days from the date of submission."

**Proclamation No. 270/2002** (https://justice.gov.et/en/law/providing-foreign-nationals-of-ethiopian-origin-with-certain-rights-to-be-exercised-in-their-country-of-origin-proclamation/), Art. 5: "1) he shall not be required to have an entry visa or residence permit to live in Ethiopia; 2) ... he shall have the right to be employed in Ethiopia without a work permit; ... 4) without prejudice to Article 40(3) of the Constitution, the provisions of Articles 390-393 of the Civil Code shall not apply to persons of Ethiopian origin holding the Identification Card; 5) he shall have the right to be considered as domestic investor ..." Art. 6: "1) Shall have no right to vote or be elected ... 2) Shall have no right to be employed on a regular basis in the National Defence, Security, Foreign Affairs and other similar political establishments."

**Ethiopian Diaspora Service** (https://www.ethiopiandiasporaservice.gov.et/services/explore?service=housing-development): "The 40/60 Housing Development Program allows diaspora members to participate in housing schemes by converting foreign currency to birr for payments. Requirements: Registration in the housing program; Proof of foreign currency source; Valid identification documents; Housing program payment schedule."
