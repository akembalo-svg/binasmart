# ከወንጀል ነፃ ምስክር ወረቀት (የፖሊስ ክሊራንስ) — Police Clearance / No-Criminal-Record Certificate

## በአጭሩ (Amharic summary)

- "ከወንጀል ነፃ ምስክር ወረቀት" (የፖሊስ ክሊራንስ) የሚሰጠው በኢትዮጵያ ፌደራል ፖሊስ ነው። በድረ-ገጹ ላይ ይህ አገልግሎት በ"የፎረንሲክ ምርመራ አገልግሎቶች" ሥር ተዘርዝሯል።
- ፌደራል ፖሊስ ምስክር ወረቀቱ ለ3 ጉዳዮች እንደሚሰጥ ይገልጻል፦ ለሥራ ቅጥር፣ ለውጭ ሀገር ጉዞ እና ለውጭ ሀገር ትምህርት። ስለዚህ ለውጭ ሀገር የሚጠየቀውም ይህ ምስክር ወረቀት ነው።
- በዚሁ የአገልግሎት ክፍል ሥር "የወንጀል ተጠርጣሪዎች አሻራ ምርመራ" የሚል አገልግሎትም ተዘርዝሯል።
- የፌደራል ፖሊስ ዋና መሥሪያ ቤት አድራሻ፦ ሜክሲኮ አደባባይ፣ አዲስ አበባ። ስልክ፦ +251 118 886 228። ኢሜይል፦ ethiopiafederalpolice@gmail.com (በድረ-ገጹ ግርጌ የተጻፈ)።
- ፌደራል ፖሊስ ለሚሰጣቸው አገልግሎቶች የሚከፈለው ክፍያ የሚወሰነው በሚኒስትሮች ምክር ቤት ደንብ ቁጥር 512/2014 (512/2022) ሠንጠረዥ ነው (አንቀጽ 2)። ፍትሕ ሚኒስቴር ባወጣው PDF ላይ ግን ሠንጠረዡ ባዶ ስለሆነ የክፍያው መጠን በይፋ አልተገኘም።
- የሚያስፈልጉ ሰነዶች፣ ምስክር ወረቀቱ የሚደርስበት ጊዜ እና የኦንላይን ማመልከቻ መኖሩ በፌደራል ፖሊስ ድረ-ገጽ ላይ አልተገለጸም። ከመሄድዎ በፊት በ+251 118 886 228 ደውለው ይጠይቁ።

- ማሳሰቢያ፦ "ክሊራንስ በቀላሉ እናወጣለን" የሚሉ የግል ድረ-ገጾች ኦፊሴላዊ አይደሉም። ክፍያና መስፈርት ከፌደራል ፖሊስ ብቻ ያረጋግጡ።

## In short (English)

- The "no criminal record" certificate (ከወንጀል ነፃ ምስክር ወረቀት, police clearance) is issued by the Ethiopian Federal Police. Its website lists it under "forensic investigation services".
- The Federal Police lists three uses: for employment, for travel abroad and for study abroad. This is the certificate people need for use abroad.
- The same service group also lists fingerprint examination of criminal suspects.
- Federal Police headquarters: Mexico Square (ሜክሲኮ አደባባይ), Addis Ababa. Phone +251 118 886 228. Email ethiopiafederalpolice@gmail.com (from the website footer).
- Fees for Federal Police services are set in the schedule of Council of Ministers Regulation No. 512/2022 (Art. 2). The schedule page in the Ministry of Justice PDF is blank, so the fee amount was not found.
- The Federal Police website does not publish the required documents, the processing time or whether you can apply online. Call +251 118 886 228 before you go.
- No official source describes how to apply from abroad through an embassy. Ask your nearest Ethiopian embassy or the Federal Police.
- Private websites that offer Ethiopian police clearance are not official. Check fees and requirements with the Federal Police only.

## Reported change, May 2026 (overseas workers)

Meseret Media (14 May 2026, https://www.meseretmedia.org/p/ec3) reported a Federal Police statement: from ግንቦት 06 ቀን 2018 ዓ.ም. (14 May 2026) people going abroad for work no longer have to travel to the forensic investigation directorate in Addis Ababa. They get the service (fingerprints / police clearance) through centres prepared by the Ministry of Labour and Skills, via the licensed agencies that send them, near where they live. The report followed its investigation of queues of up to 5,000 people a day and brokers charging up to 10,000 birr. The statement itself was not found on federalpolice.gov.et (on 30 Sep 2026 the site showed a browser certificate warning). Worker's cost: Proclamation 1389/2025 Art. 19(2); refunded if not deployed through the agency's or employer's fault, 19(4).

## What is not published

- **Fee:** The amount is in the schedule of Regulation 512/2022, but that page is blank in the official PDF. Ask the Federal Police how much it costs.
- **Documents:** The official list of documents (ID, passport, photos, fingerprints, request letter) is not published. Ask the office before you go.
- **Processing time and office hours:** not published.
- **Online application:** No official online application was found on federalpolice.gov.et.
- **Applying from abroad (embassy route) and legalisation for use abroad:** not published by the Federal Police. Ask the embassy, or ask the Ministry of Foreign Affairs about authentication.
- **Regional certificates:** It is not published whether regional police commissions also issue this certificate. Ask your regional police.
