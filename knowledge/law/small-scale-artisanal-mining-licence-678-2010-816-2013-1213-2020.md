# የአነስተኛና ባሕላዊ ማዕድን ማምረት ፈቃድ — Small-scale, special small-scale and artisanal mining licences

## በአጭሩ (Amharic summary)

- ሕጉ የማዕድን ሥራዎች አዋጅ ቁጥር 678/2002 (678/2010) ሲሆን በአዋጅ 816/2006 (816/2013) እና 1213/2012 (1213/2020) ተሻሽሏል፤ ዝርዝሩ በሚኒስትሮች ምክር ቤት ደንብ ቁጥር 423/2010 (423/2018) ተቀምጧል።
- ፈቃድ ሰጪው የማዕድን ሚኒስቴር ወይም የክልሉ የማዕድን ዘርፍ ኃላፊ አካል ነው። **ባሕላዊ (artisanal) የማዕድን ፈቃድ የሚሰጠው በክልሉ ነው**፤ ክልሎች ለሀገር ውስጥ ባለሀብቶች ለኢንዱስትሪ ማዕድናት አነስተኛ ፈቃድ እና ለኮንስትራክሽን ማዕድናት አነስተኛና ከፍተኛ ፈቃድ ይሰጣሉ።
- **ባሕላዊ ማዕድን ማምረት**፦ በግለሰቦች ወይም በጥቃቅንና አነስተኛ ኢንተርፕራይዞች የሚካሄድ፣ በአብዛኛው በእጅ የሚሠራ፣ ተቀጣሪ ሠራተኛ የሌለበት ሥራ ነው። የገንዘብ ወይም የቴክኒክ ብቃት አይጠየቅም፤ የውጭ ዜጋ ግን አይፈቀድለትም። ፈቃዱ ቢበዛ ለ2 ዓመት ይሰጣል፤ አይታደስም። ቦታው በአንድ ብሎክ ቢበዛ 5,000 ካሬ ሜትር ነው (በአንድ ጊዜ ከሁለት ፈቃድና ከ10,000 ካሬ ሜትር አይበልጥም)።
- ለባሕላዊ ፈቃድ ማመልከቻ፦ ሙሉ ስም፣ ዜግነት፣ ጾታ፣ አድራሻና ዕድሜ፤ የማዕድኑ ቦታ፤ ኢንተርፕራይዝ ከሆነ የምዝገባ ማስረጃ።
- **ልዩ አነስተኛ (special small-scale)**፦ የከበሩ ድንጋዮች ወይም የወርቅ፣ ብር፣ ፕላቲኒየም፣ ታንታለም ደለል (placer) ሥራ፤ ቀደም ሲል ባሕላዊ ፈቃድ ለነበራቸውና ማሽን ለመጠቀም አቅም ላላቸው። ፈቃዱ ቢበዛ 10 ዓመት፣ እድሳት እያንዳንዱ ቢበዛ 5 ዓመት። ማመልከቻው የአካባቢና የተፈጥሮ ሀብት ተጽዕኖ ግምገማ ሰነድ፣ የመልሶ ማቋቋሚያ ወጪ፣ የሥራ ፕሮግራምና የገንዘብ አቅም እንዲሁም የቀድሞውን ባሕላዊ ፈቃድ ዋናና ቅጂ ይይዛል።
- **አነስተኛ (small-scale) ፈቃድ**፦ የሚሰጠው የሥራ ፕሮግራሙ ሲጸድቅ፣ የገንዘብና የቴክኒክ አቅም ሲኖር፣ የአካባቢ ተጽዕኖ ግምገማ ሲጸድቅ ነው። ማመልከቻው የአዋጭነት ጥናት (feasibility study)፣ የአካባቢና ማኅበራዊ ተጽዕኖ ግምገማ ሪፖርት እና የልማት ፕሮግራም ይይዛል። ፈቃዱ ቢበዛ 10 ዓመት፣ እድሳት ቢበዛ 5 ዓመት፤ ሥራ በ1 ዓመት ውስጥ መጀመር አለበት። እድሳት ከማብቂያው 180 ቀን በፊት ይጠየቃል።
- **ወርቅ ወይም ብር** የሚያመርቱ ባሕላዊና ልዩ አነስተኛ ፈቃድ ያላቸው ምርታቸውን **ለኢትዮጵያ ብሔራዊ ባንክ** መሸጥ አለባቸው፤ ምርቱን በየወሩ (በ10 ቀን ውስጥ) ሪፖርት ማድረግና በየሩብ ዓመቱ (በ30 ቀን ውስጥ) መሸጥ አለባቸው።
- የፌደራል ፈቃድ ክፍያ (ደንብ 423/2010)፦ አነስተኛ ፈቃድ ለኢንዱስትሪና ኮንስትራክሽን ማዕድን **4,207 ብር**፤ ለከበሩ፣ በከፊል የከበሩ፣ ብረታብረትና ሌሎች ማዕድናት **7,520 ብር** (በብሎክ)። የማመልከቻ ምዝገባ 401 ብር። በክልል ለሚሰጡ ፈቃዶች ክፍያው በክልሉ ሕግ ይወሰናል።
- የውጭ ዜጋ ለአሸዋ፣ ጠጠር (aggregate) ወዘተ የኮንስትራክሽን ማዕድን ፈቃድ አያገኝም፤ የውጭ ባለሀብት የደለል (placer) ማዕድን ሥራ ሊሰማራ አይችልም (ቢያንስ 25% ድርሻ ካለው የሀገር ውስጥ ባለሀብት ጋር የሚደረግ ከፍተኛ ደረጃ ደለል ሥራ ካልሆነ በስተቀር)።
- ባሕላዊና ልዩ አነስተኛ ፈቃድ የሚተላለፈው በውርስ ብቻ ነው።

## In short (English)

- Law: Mining Operations Proclamation 678/2010 as amended by 816/2013 and 1213/2020; details in Council of Ministers Regulation 423/2018.
- Licensing authority: the Ministry or a regional state organ in charge of mining. Regions issue artisanal licences, small-scale licences for industrial minerals and small/large licences for construction minerals to domestic investors.
- **Artisanal**: individuals or small and micro enterprises, mostly manual, no employed workers; no financial/technical competence required; not for non-citizens. Valid up to 2 years, not renewable. Max 5,000 m² per block; at most two licences / 10,000 m² at a time. Application: name, nationality, sex, address, age; location of the mineral; enterprise registration proof if an enterprise.
- **Special small-scale**: gemstones or placer gold, silver, platinum, tantalum, by former artisanal licence holders able to use machinery. Up to 10 years, renewals up to 5 years. Application: environmental and natural-resource impact document, restoration budget, work programme and financial position, original and copy of the previous artisanal licence.
- **Small-scale**: granted if the work programme and EIA are approved and the applicant has financial and technical ability. Application includes a feasibility study, ESIA reports and development programme. Up to 10 years, renewals up to 5 years; start mining within 1 year; apply for renewal 180 days before expiry.
- Artisanal and special small-scale **gold/silver must be sold to the National Bank of Ethiopia**; report production monthly (within 10 days) and sell quarterly (within 30 days).
- Federal licence fee per block (Reg. 423/2018): small-scale industrial/construction Birr 4,207; small-scale precious/semi-precious/metallic/other Birr 7,520; registration of a mining licence application Birr 401. Fees for regionally issued licences are set by regional law.
- Non-citizens cannot get licences for construction minerals such as sand and aggregate; foreign investors cannot do placer mining except large-scale placer with a domestic partner holding at least 25%.
- Artisanal and special small-scale licences can be transferred only by inheritance.

## Official text

**Proclamation 678/2010** (https://justice.gov.et/en/law/mining-operations-proclamation/)
- Art. 2(16): "'licensing authority' means the Ministry of Mines and Energy or a state organ in charge of the mining sector as appropriate;"
- Art. 2(35): "'small scale mining' means any mining operation of which the annual run-off mine ore does not exceed: a) regarding gold, platinum, silver and other precious and semiprecious minerals: (1) 100,000m3 for placer operation; (2) 75,000 tons for primary deposit mining; b) regarding metallic minerals such as iron, lead, copper and nickel: (1) 150,000 tons for open pit mining; (2) 75,000 tons for under ground mining operation;"
- Art. 11(2): "No person is required to possess financial resources, technical and professional competence in order to carry on artisanal mining operation."
- Art. 28(1): "The Licensing Authority shall grant an exclusive small scale mining license to an applicant where: a) the proposed work program is approved; b) the applicant has access to financial resources and technical ability ...; c) the environmental impact assessment has been approved; and d) the applicant is not in contravention of any obligations of the exploration license, if any."
- Art. 29(1)–(2): "A small scale mining license shall be valid for the period specified in the license; provided however, that such period shall not exceed 10 years." / "... may be renewed for a period not exceeding five years each ..."
- Art. 30: holder has "the rights to market and sell minerals produced" and shall "commence mining operations within one year from the date on which the license becomes effective".
- Art. 52(1): "The licensing authority of a state shall have the powers and duties to: a) issue artisanal mining license; b) issue to domestic investors: ... (2) small scale mining licenses for industrial minerals and small and large scale mining licenses for construction minerals;"
- Art. 69: "The amount and manner of payment of such fees shall be determined by regulation and the laws of states as appropriate."

**Proclamation 816/2013** (https://justice.gov.et/en/law/a-proclamation-to-amend-the-mining-operations-proclamation/)
- New Art. 2(2): "'artisanal mining' means a mining operation carried out by individuals or small and micro enterprises which is mostly of manual nature and does not involve the engagement of employed workers;"
- New Art. 2(44): "'special small scale mining' means mining operation of gemstones or placer resources of gold, silver, platinum or tantalum of which the annual run-of-mine does not exceed the limit stated in paragraph (a) of sub-article (35) ... carried out by individuals or small and micro-enterprises who were the holders of artisanal mining license and have sufficient financial capacity to employ modern machineries and equipment in such operation."
- Artisanal licence: "3/ An artisanal mining license shall be valid for the period specified in the license, provided however, that such period may not exceed two years. 4/ Artisanal mining license shall not be renewed."
- Transfer: "artisanal or special small scale mining licenses may only be transferred in the case of inheritance."
- Sale: "the holder of artisanal and special small scale mining license that has engaged in gold or silver mining shall sell the minerals produced to the National Bank of Ethiopia."
- Duties: "report to the Licensing Authority the quantity and type of mineral mined each month within 10 days from the end of such month and sell the minerals mined every financial quarter within 30 days from the end of each financial quarter ..."

**Proclamation 1213/2020** (https://justice.gov.et/en/law/mining-operations-amendment-proclamation/), new Art. 11(3)(d)–(e): no licence to "a non-citizen of Ethiopia, construction minerals excluding those used as raw material and dimension stones such as sand, aggregate, selected materials and others" nor, "except a foreign investor who would like to engage in large scale placer mining in partnership with a domestic investor holding a minimum of 25% share, a foreign investor who would like to engage in any other placer mining operation."

**Regulation 423/2018** (https://justice.gov.et/en/law/mining-operations-council-of-ministers-regulation-council-of-ministers-regulation-no-423-2018/)
- Art. 7 (special small scale application): "b) environmental and natural resource impact assessment document ...; c) intended amount of expenditure for the environmental and natural resource impact restoration work ...; d) information with regard to the intended work program ... and his financial position ...; e) the original and a copy of his previous artisanal mining license."
- Art. 8(1): "a) his full name, nationality, sex, address and age; b) location of the minerals for which the license is requested; c) other relevant documents determined by the directive of the Licensing Authority;"
- Art. 14(2)–(3): "d) 5,000 m2 for artisanal mining license; e) 100,000 m2 for the small scale industrial and construction minerals mining license; ... g) one square kilo meter for small scale and large scale mining license for precious, semi-precious, metallic and other minerals." "An artisanal mining licensee shall not be granted with more than two licenses and an area exceeding 10,000 m2 at a time."
- Art. 18(1): renewal "shall be made 180 days prior to the expiry of the term of the license".
- Art. 46(1): "d) small scale industrial and construction mining license ... Birr 4,207 ... f) precious, semi-precious, metallic and other minirals of small scale mining license ... Birr 7,520". Art. 46(3): fees for licences granted by regions "shall be determined by the laws of the Regional States." Art. 47(2)(a): "registration of application for mining works license ... Birr 401".
