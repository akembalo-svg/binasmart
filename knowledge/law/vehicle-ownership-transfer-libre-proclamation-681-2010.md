# የተሽከርካሪ ሊብሬ ስም ማዛወር — Transferring Vehicle Ownership (Libre / Title Certificate Book) under Proclamation 681/2010

## በአጭሩ (Amharic summary)
- "ሊብሬ" በሕጉ "የባለቤትነት ማረጋገጫ ደብተር" (title certificate book) ይባላል። ተሽከርካሪ በሽያጭ፣ በስጦታ ወይም በሌላ መንገድ የተላለፈለት ሰው **በ6 ወር ውስጥ** ሊብሬው በራሱ ስም እንዲሰጠው ለሚመለከተው አካል ማመልከት አለበት (አዋጅ 681/2002 አንቀጽ 6(3))።
- **ማመልከቻው የሚያስፈልገው (አንቀጽ 6(4))፦** የቀድሞው ሊብሬ (የባለቤትነት ማረጋገጫ ደብተር)፣ እና ዝውውሩ ሕጋዊ መሆኑን የሚያሳይ፣ በሚመለከተው አካል የተሰጠ ወይም የተረጋገጠ ሰነድ (ለምሳሌ የተረጋገጠ የሽያጭ ወይም የስጦታ ውል)።
- በ6 ወር ውስጥ ሊብሬውን በስሙ ያላዛወረ ሰው **700 ብር** ይቀጣል (አንቀጽ 48(1)(ሐ)፣ በOCR የተነበበ)።
- በአንድ ተሽከርካሪ ላይ በተላላፊዎች መካከል ክርክር ቢነሳ ቅድሚያ የሚሰጠው ሊብሬው ቀድሞ ለወጣለት ነው።
- ዓመታዊ ምርመራውን (ቦሎ) ያሳለፈ ተሽከርካሪ ለእያንዳንዱ 15 ቀን መዘግየት 100 ብር ይቀጣል (አንቀጽ 48(1)(ሀ))። ስም ከማዛወርዎ በፊት ቦሎው ወቅታዊ መሆኑን ያረጋግጡ።
- የገቢዎች ሚኒስቴር የታክስ ክሊራንስ መመሪያ 180/2015 ለተሽከርካሪ ዓመታዊ ምዝገባና ምርመራ፣ ለሰሌዳ ለውጥ እና ግብር የሚከፈልበትን የካፒታል ሀብት ለማስተላለፍ የታክስ ክሊራንስ እንደሚጠየቅ ይዘረዝራል። ንግድ ያለዎት ሻጭ ከሆኑ ይህንን ያስቡበት።
- **የፌዴራል ክፍያ (ደንብ 206/2003፣ በፌዴራል ለሚመዘገቡ ተሽከርካሪዎች)፦** ሊብሬ ለሞተር ሳይክል 8 ብር፣ ለሌሎች ተሽከርካሪዎች 15 ብር፤ የዝርዝር መረጃ ለውጥ በእያንዳንዱ 5 ብር፤ ምትክ ሊብሬ ከመጀመሪያው ክፍያ በተጨማሪ 5 ብር። እነዚህ የ2003 ዓ.ም መጠኖች ናቸው፤ በአዲስ አበባ የሚከፈለው የአሁኑ ክፍያ አልተገኘም።
- **የት፦** በፌዴራል ደረጃ የትራንስፖርትና ሎጂስቲክስ ሚኒስቴር "የባለቤትነት ማረጋገጫ ዝውውር" አገልግሎትን በeservices.gov.et ላይ ያቀርባል። በአዲስ አበባ የተመዘገቡ መኪኖች ጉዳይ የሚስተናገደው በአዲስ አበባ አሽከርካሪና ተሽከርካሪ ፈቃድና ቁጥጥር ባለሥልጣን ነው። በሸገር ከተማ ሊብሬ የሚሰጠው በ eservice.shaggarcity.et በኩል ነው።
- በአዲስ አበባ ያለውን ዝርዝር ሂደት፣ የሰነድ ዝርዝርና ክፍያ በቅርንጫፍ ጽ/ቤቱ ይጠይቁ። የሽያጭ ውሉ እንዴት እንደሚረጋገጥ ለማወቅ የሰነዶች ማረጋገጫና ምዝገባ አገልግሎትን ይጠይቁ።

## In short (English)
- The "libre" is the vehicle's title certificate book. Anyone who receives a vehicle by sale, donation or otherwise must apply **within six months** to have the title certificate book re-issued in their own name (Proclamation 681/2010, Art. 6(3)).
- **The application needs (Art. 6(4)):** the former title certificate book, and a document issued or authenticated by the concerned body proving the transfer is legal (for example, an authenticated sale or gift contract).
- Failing to transfer the title certificate book into your name within six months carries a fine of **Birr 700** (Art. 48(1)(c), read by OCR).
- If transferees dispute ownership of a vehicle, the person whose title certificate book was issued first has priority.
- A vehicle presented late for annual inspection is fined Birr 100 for each fifteen days of delay (Art. 48(1)(a)). Check that the inspection is current before transferring.
- The Ministry of Revenue's tax clearance Directive 180/2015 E.C. lists annual vehicle registration and inspection, plate changes, and the transfer of a taxable capital asset among the services that need a tax clearance. This matters if the seller is a business.
- **Federal fees (Regulation 206/2011, for vehicles registered by the federal authority):** title certificate book Birr 8 for a motorcycle and Birr 15 for other vehicles; change of particulars Birr 5 per item; replacement book, the original amount plus Birr 5. These are 2011 amounts, and current Addis Ababa fees were not found.
- **Where:** at federal level, the Ministry of Transport and Logistics lists "Application for Transfer of ownership certificate" on eservices.gov.et. Vehicles registered in Addis Ababa are handled by the Addis Ababa Driver and Vehicle Licensing and Control Authority. Sheger City issues libres through eservice.shaggarcity.et.
- Ask the Addis Ababa authority's branch office for the current steps, document list and fees. Ask the Documents Authentication and Registration Service how to authenticate the sale contract.

## Official text
**Vehicles Identification, Inspection and Registration Proclamation No. 681/2010**, Federal Negarit Gazeta No. 46, 11 August 2010. Scanned PDF: https://www.motl.gov.et/sites/default/files/resource/የተሽ_መለያ፣መ_መመዝገቢያ_አዋጅ_ቁ_681_2002_0.pdf (English column read by OCR; one word is illegible).
- Art. 6 (Application for Registration and Title Certificate Book), sub-art. 3: "Any person to whom a vehicle is transferred by way of sale, donation, [illegible] or otherwise, shall apply to the appropriate organ within six months for the re-issue of the title certificate book in his own name."
- Art. 6(4): "The application to be submitted pursuant to sub-article (3) of this Article shall be accompanied with the former title certificate book issued with respect to the vehicle and with a document issued or authenticated by the concerned body to attest the legality of the transfer."
- Art. 7(1): the appropriate organ, once satisfied that the application is genuine and the vehicle is technically fit, "upon payment of the prescribed fees", enters the particulars in the official register, "a) the name of the owner and his address; b) chassis number ...; c) motor number ...".
- On evidence: "In the event of dispute between transferees of ownership with respect to a vehicle, precedence of evidence shall be determined on the basis of the order of the date of issuance of the title certificate book."
- Art. 48(1)(a): a person who "presents his vehicle for inspection after the expiry of the regular annual inspection period shall be punished with a fine of Birr 100 for each fifteen days of delay". Art. 48(1)(c) (OCR, partly garbled): "... [fails] to transfer the vehicle's title certificate book in his own name within six months shall be punished with fine of birr 700 (seven hundred birr)".

Art. 3: "This Regulation shall be applicable with respect to vehicles to be registered by the Federal Transport Authority." Schedule, item 1, "Title Certificate Book" ("የባለንብረት መታወቂያ ደብተር"):

**eservices.gov.et** (Ministry of Innovation and Technology portal, as captured 2026-09-14): the Ministry of Transport and Logistics lists "Application for Transfer of ownership certificate", described as "the official change of vehicle ownership from one individual or entity to another", with application on eservices.gov.et. Sheger City Administration lists "Issuance of Table No. and Libre (Ownership Book)" at https://eservice.shaggarcity.et/.

**Ministry of Revenue Directive 180/2015 E.C.** (tax clearance), Art. 4 lists services that need a tax clearance, including "ለተሽከርካሪዎች ወይም የኮንስትራክሽን ማሽነሪ ዓመታዊ ምዝገባና ምርመራ", "የሰሌዳ ለውጥ ለማድረግ" and "ግብር የሚከፈልበት የካፒታል ሃብት ለማስተላለፍ".

## What is not published
- The current step-by-step process, document checklist, fees and branch offices of the Addis Ababa Driver and Vehicle Licensing and Control Authority were not found online. Ask the authority's branch office where the car is registered.
- Whether the Addis Ababa authority requires the sale contract to be authenticated by the Documents Authentication and Registration Service (DARS), and what DARS charges for a vehicle sale contract, is not stated in these sources. Ask DARS or the authority.
- Any stamp duty, valuation or tax due on selling a private car was not found in these sources. Ask the Ministry of Revenue or the Addis Ababa Revenue Bureau.
- The 2011 federal fees may since have been changed. Confirm the amount at the office.
