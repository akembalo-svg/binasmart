# የስፖርት ክለብ ምዝገባ — Registering a Sports Club

## በአጭሩ (Amharic summary)

- በፌዴራል ደረጃ የሚንቀሳቀሱ የስፖርት ማኅበራትን የሚመዘግበውና የሚደግፈው **የባህልና ስፖርት ሚኒስቴር** ነው (አዋጅ 1263/2021 አንቀጽ 37(1)(n))።
- ሚኒስቴሩ የስፖርት ማኅበራት አመሠራረትን የሚመለከት መመሪያ ያወጣል፣ ማኅበራቱ ከዓላማቸው ጋር የሚጣጣም ገቢ ማስገኛ ሥራ እንዲሠሩም ይፈቅዳል።
- ቀድሞ የስፖርት ኮሚሽን የነበሩት ሥልጣኖች ወደ ባህልና ስፖርት ሚኒስቴር ተዛውረዋል።
- "የስፖርት ማኅበር" ማለት የስፖርት ክለብ፣ የኢትዮጵያ ኦሊምፒክ ኮሚቴ ወይም ማንኛውም የስፖርት ፌዴሬሽን ነው (በቀድሞው አዋጅ 12/1995 ትርጉም)።
- ሕጉ ሚኒስቴሩ የሚመዘግበው በፌዴራል ደረጃ የሚንቀሳቀሱትን ብቻ እንደሆነ ያመለክታል። በአንድ ክልል ወይም ከተማ ውስጥ ብቻ የሚንቀሳቀስ ክለብ በክልሉ ወይም በከተማው የስፖርት አካል ሊመዘገብ እንደሚችል ይገመታል፤ ሕጉ ግን ይህን በቀጥታ አይገልጽም።
- በአዲስ አበባ የሚመለከተው አካል የአዲስ አበባ ከተማ አስተዳደር ወጣቶችና ስፖርት ቢሮ ነው (addisyouthandsport.gov.et)፤ ድረ-ገጹ ግን የክለብ ምዝገባ ሂደት አያሳይም።
- የሚያስፈልጉ ሰነዶች፣ ክፍያ እና የሚኒስቴሩ መመሪያ በይፋ አልተገኙም። ከመመዝገብዎ በፊት ሚኒስቴሩን ወይም የከተማዎን/የክልልዎን ስፖርት ቢሮ በቀጥታ ይጠይቁ።

## In short (English)

- The **Ministry of Culture and Sport** registers and supports sports associations operating at the **federal** level (Proc. 1263/2021, Art. 37(1)(n)).
- The Ministry issues directives on establishing sports associations and can authorise them to run income-generating activities consistent with their objectives.
- Powers once held by the Sport Commission now sit with the Ministry of Culture and Sport (Art. 37(2)).
- "Sports association" historically meant any sport club, the Ethiopian Olympic Committee or any sports federation (Proc. 12/1995, Art. 2(3)).
- The law implies the Ministry registers only federal-level associations; a club active in one region or city would register with the regional or city sport body — but the law does not say this word for word.
- In Addis Ababa, the relevant body is the Addis Ababa City Administration Youth and Sports Bureau (addisyouthandsport.gov.et); its website does not publish a club registration procedure.
- The required documents, fees and the Ministry's directive were not found officially. Ask the Ministry or your city/regional sport bureau before applying.

## Official text

**Proclamation No. 1263/2021, Art. 37** — https://justice.gov.et/en/law/definition-of-powers-and-duties-of-the-executive-organs-proclamation/
Art. 37(1)(n): "issue Directives governing the establishment of sports associations; register and provide necessary support to sports associations operating at the federal level; authorize sports associations to engage in income generating activities which are consistent with their objectives with a view to meet their financial needs;"

Art. 37(2): "The powers and duties entrusted to the Sport Commission and Ministry of Culture and Tourism with respect to culture and language under other laws that are currently in force are hereby vested in the Ministry of Culture and Sport established by this Proclamation."

**Sports Commission Establishment Proclamation No. 12/1995 (historical)** — https://justice.gov.et/en/law/sports-commission-establishment-proclamations/
Art. 2(3): "'Sports association' means any sport club formed in accordance with the provisions of this Proclamation, the Ethiopian Olympic Committee or any sports federation;"
Art. 6(2): "to issue directives concerning the establishment and determination of the standards of sports associations; issue permits to sports associations formed at the national level; coordinate and supervise the programmes and international contacts of such sports associations;"

## What is not published

- The Ministry of Culture and Sport's directive on establishing sports associations (its number, text, required documents and fees).
- Any Addis Ababa procedure for registering a sports club; the Youth and Sports Bureau's only listed directive (No. 107/2014) is about youth centres, not clubs.
- Whether Art. 37 of Proclamation 1263/2021 has been amended since.
- Whether a club can instead register as a civil society organisation under Proclamation 1113/2019 was not checked; ask the relevant authority.
