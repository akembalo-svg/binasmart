# የሕፃናት ጥቃትን ሪፖርት ማድረግ — How to Report Child Abuse in Ethiopia

## በአጭሩ (Amharic summary)

- አንድ ሕፃን አሁን አደጋ ላይ ከሆነ በአዲስ አበባ ወደ ፖሊስ የአደጋ ጊዜ ቁጥር 991 ይደውሉ። ይህ ቁጥር የተገኘው በኢትዮጵያ የፈረንሳይ ኤምባሲ የአደጋ ጊዜ ቁጥሮች ገጽ ላይ ነው።
- በዚሁ ገጽ መሠረት 991 ላይ 1ን በመጫን ወደ ፌዴራል ፖሊስ፣ 2ን በመጫን ወደ አዲስ አበባ ፖሊስ ይገናኛሉ። የአዲስ አበባ ፖሊስ ቀጥታ መስመር +251 (0)11 111 01 11 ነው። የፌዴራል ፖሊስ ቀጥታ መስመር +251 (0)11 552 63 03 ነው።
- በዚሁ ገጽ መሠረት ለአደጋ ጊዜ እርዳታ (አደጋ፣ ጥቃት) የአዲስ አበባ ፖሊስ ይመከራል፤ ወንጀልን ሪፖርት ለማድረግ ደግሞ የፌዴራል ፖሊስ ይመከራል።
- 7711 ከክፍያ ነፃ የሆነ የጾታዊና ጾታን መሠረት ያደረገ ጥቃት ብሔራዊ የስልክ መስመር ነው። የሚያስተዳድረው የኢትዮጵያ ሴት ሕግ ባለሙያዎች ማህበር ከኢትዮ ቴሌኮም ጋር በመተባበር ነው። ይህን የገለጸው UNFPA በ2021 (እ.ኤ.አ.) ነው።
- 7711 ምክርና ወደ ሌሎች አገልግሎቶች ሪፈራል ይሰጣል። አገልግሎቱ በአማርኛ፣ በአፋን ኦሮሞ እና በትግርኛ ነው። የሕፃናት ጉዳዮችን ይቀበል እንደሆነ ምንጩ አይገልጽም።
- በአዲስ አበባ ለሕፃናት ጥበቃ ኃላፊነት ያለበት የአዲስ አበባ ከተማ አስተዳደር ሴቶች፣ ሕፃናትና ማህበራዊ ጉዳይ ቢሮ ነው። ቢሮው ፒያሳ (ደጃች ውቤ) ይገኛል። ስልክ፦ +251 118-72-32-97 ወይም +251 118-58-15-52። ኢሜይል፦ bowcsa@aawcsa.gov.et
- በቢሮው ውስጥ "የሕፃናት መብትና ደህንነት ማስጠበቅ ዳይሬክቶሬት" አለ። ዳይሬክቶሬቱ የሀገር ውስጥ ጉዲፈቻ፣ ሕፃናትን መልሶ የማቀላቀል፣ የአደራ ቤተሰብ እና የማህበረሰብ አቀፍ ድጋፍ አገልግሎቶችን ይዘረዝራል። የጥቃት ሪፖርት ማድረጊያ ስልክ ግን አልዘረዘረም።
- በፌዴራል ደረጃ የሴቶችና ማህበራዊ ጉዳይ ሚኒስቴር በሕግ የሕፃናትን መብትና ደህንነት ለማስጠበቅ ባለድርሻ አካላትን ያስተባብራል። ሚኒስቴሩ አራት ኪሎ፣ ከቱሪስት ሆቴል ጀርባ ይገኛል። ስልክ፦ +251-11-862 5869። ኢሜይል፦ info@mowsa.gov.et
- ሚኒስቴሩ ጥቃት ለደረሰባቸው ሴቶችና ሕፃናት የተቀናጀ የጤና፣ የሥነ-ልቦና፣ የሕግ እና የተሃድሶ አገልግሎት የሚሰጡ ማዕከላት እንዲቋቋሙ የመደገፍ ኃላፊነት አለበት።
- በይፋ የታወቀ ብሔራዊ የሕፃናት ጥቃት ሪፖርት ማድረጊያ የመንግሥት ስልክ መስመር አልተገኘም።

## In short (English)

- If a child is in danger right now in Addis Ababa, call the police emergency number 991. This number comes from the French Embassy in Ethiopia's emergency numbers page.
- According to the same page, on 991 you press 1 for the Federal Police or 2 for the Addis Ababa Police. The Addis Ababa Police direct line is +251 (0)11 111 01 11. The Federal Police direct line is +251 (0)11 552 63 03.
- The same page says to use the Addis Ababa Police for emergency help (accidents, assaults) and the Federal Police for reporting crime.
- 7711 is a toll-free national hotline for sexual and gender-based violence. It is run by the Ethiopian Women Lawyers Association (EWLA) in partnership with Ethio Telecom, according to UNFPA (2021).
- 7711 offers counselling and referrals to other services, in Amharic, Afan Oromo and Tigrigna. The source does not say whether it takes child cases.
- In Addis Ababa, the office responsible for child protection is the Addis Ababa Women, Children and Social Affairs Bureau. It is in Piassa (Dejach Wube). Phone: +251 118-72-32-97 or +251 118-58-15-52. Email: bowcsa@aawcsa.gov.et
- The bureau has a Child Rights and Protection Directorate. It lists domestic adoption, family reintegration, foster care and community-based support. It does not list a reporting phone line.
- At federal level, the Ministry of Women and Social Affairs (MoWSA) is required by law to coordinate all stakeholders to protect children's rights and well-being. It is at 4 Kilo, behind Tourist Hotel. Phone: +251-11-862 5869. Email: info@mowsa.gov.et
- MoWSA must also support the setting up of centres that give women and children who are victims of violence holistic health, psychological, legal and rehabilitation services.
- No official national government child abuse hotline was found.

## Official text

### Police emergency — Embassy of France in Ethiopia, "Who to contact in an emergency?" (updated 24 Feb 2026)
Source: https://et.diplomatie.gouv.fr/en/en/node/7

> "Police – Addis Ababa 📞 991 Press 1 to reach the Federal Police (direct line: +251 (0)11 552 63 03) Press 2 to reach the Addis Ababa Police (direct line: +251 (0)11 111 01 11) The choice depends on the situation: Addis Ababa Police: emergency assistance (accidents, assaults) Federal Police: reporting (crime, terrorism)"


The Federal Police website (https://www.federalpolice.gov.et/en/federal/police/home) has an "Emergency" menu, but no number could be read from it.

### 7711 hotline — UNFPA Ethiopia, 26 March 2021
Source: https://ethiopia.unfpa.org/en/news/unfpa-supported-hotline-sexual-and-gender-based-violence-launched

> "A toll-free national hotline for sexual and gender-based violence which was established through the partnership of the Ethiopian Women Lawyers Association (EWLA) and Ethio Telecom was launched today."

> "The initiative is expected to provide vital support, information and advice to survivors of sexual and gender-based violence through offering counselling and referrals to existing appropriate services across the country. The national hotline has a toll free number of 7711 and the services will be given in the Amharic, Afan Oromo and Tigrigna languages."

The launch notice names the "Ministry of Women, Children and Youth"; its powers now belong to the Ministry of Women and Social Affairs (Proclamation 1263/2021 Art. 36(2)).

### Proclamation No. 1263/2021 — Art. 36(1) (Ministry of Women and Social Affairs)
Source: https://justice.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/አዋጅ-ቁጥር-1263-2014.pdf

> "l) design strategies to prevent harmful practices and violence as well as measures to be taken; implement the same in collaboration with the concerned organs;"

> "m) provide support for the establishment of centers for the provision of holistic health, psychological, legal and rehabilitation services for women and children who are victims of violence; and follow up the implementation of the same;"

> "p) coordinate all stakeholders to protect the rights and well-being of children;" (the PDF's two columns overlap at this point; wording restored from the jumbled extraction)

MoWSA contacts: https://www.mowsa.gov.et/?page_id=147&lang=en — "4kilo, Addis Ababa, Behind Tourist Hotel", Tel "+251-11-862 5869", info@mowsa.gov.et
