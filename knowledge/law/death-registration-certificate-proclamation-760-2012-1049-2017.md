# የሞት ምዝገባና የሞት ምስክር ወረቀት — Registering a death and getting a death certificate

## በአጭሩ
- ሞት ከተከሰተበት ቀን ጀምሮ በ30 ቀን ውስጥ መመዝገብ አለበት። ከዘገየ የመዘግየቱን ምክንያት የሚያስረዳ ማስረጃ ያስፈልጋል (አዋጅ 760/2004 አንቀጽ 18)።
- ሞቱን የሚያሳውቀው ከሟቹ ጋር አብሮ ይኖር የነበረ ሰው ነው። እንደዚህ ያለ ሰው ከሌለ ዘመድ፣ ቅርብ ጎረቤት ወይም ሞቱን የሚያውቅ ማንኛውም ሰው ያሳውቃል (አንቀጽ 39)።
- አሳዋቂው በአካል ቀርቦ በመዝገቡ ላይ የሰፈረውን መረጃ በፊርማ (ወይም በአሻራ) ያረጋግጣል (አንቀጽ 17፣ 22፣ 23)።
- ሞቱ በጤና ተቋም ከሆነ ተቋሙ የሞት ማሳወቂያ ወረቀት አዘጋጅቶ ለአሳዋቂው ይሰጣል። ሞቱ ከጤና ተቋም ውጭ ከሆነ በአካባቢው ያለ የጤና ተቋም ወይም የጤና ባለሙያ ማሳወቂያ አዘጋጅቶ ለምዝገባ ጽ/ቤቱ ይልካል (አንቀጽ 41 በአዋጅ 1049/2009 እንደተሻሻለ)።
- ስለዚህ ይዘው የሚሄዱት፦ የሞት ማሳወቂያ ወረቀት (ካለ)፣ የሟቹ መረጃ (ሙሉ ስም፣ ፆታ፣ ዕድሜ፣ ሥራ፣ አድራሻ፣ ዜግነት፣ ሃይማኖት)፣ የሞቱ ቀን፣ ቦታና ምክንያት (አንቀጽ 38)። ሌሎች የሚጠየቁ ሰነዶችን (ለምሳሌ መታወቂያ) ጽ/ቤቱን ይጠይቁ።
- ምዝገባው የሚፈጸመው ለሟቹ መደበኛ መኖሪያ ቅርብ በሆነው የምዝገባ ጽ/ቤት ነው። ማንነቱ ወይም አድራሻው ካልታወቀ አስከሬኑ በተገኘበት ቦታ አቅራቢያ ይመዘገባል (አንቀጽ 43)።
- በአዳሪ ትምህርት ቤት ወይም በሌላ የጋራ መኖሪያ ቢሞት የተቋሙ ኃላፊ፣ በእስር ቤት ቢሞት የእስር ቤቱ ኃላፊ በ5 ቀን ውስጥ ያሳውቃል። ማንነቱ ያልታወቀ ሰው በአደጋ ቢሞት ፖሊስ በ3 ቀን ውስጥ ያሳውቃል (አንቀጽ 39፣ 41)።
- ሰው ጠፍቶ ፍርድ ቤት የመጥፋት ወይም የሞት ውሳኔ ከሰጠ የውሳኔው ቅጂ ለሟቹ የመጨረሻ መኖሪያ ቅርብ በሆነው ጽ/ቤት ለምዝገባ ይቀርባል (አንቀጽ 44)።
- የሞት ምስክር ወረቀቱ ለአሳዋቂው ይሰጣል። ክፍያ በደንብ ይወሰናል። የጠፋ ወይም የተበላሸ ምስክር ወረቀት በክፍያ ይተካል (አንቀጽ 47)።
- በአዲስ አበባ፦ የአዲስ አበባ የሲቪል ምዝገባና የነዋሪነት አገልግሎት ኤጀንሲ (aacrrsa.gov.et)፣ ስልክ 7533።

## In short (English)
- Register a death within 30 days. After that, bring evidence explaining the delay (Proc. 760/2012 Art. 18).
- The declarant is someone who lived with the deceased. If there is no such person, a relative, a close neighbour or anyone who knows of the death declares it (Art. 39).
- The declarant must appear in person and confirm the entry by signature or fingerprint (Arts 17, 22, 23).
- If the death happened in a health facility, the facility gives the declarant a death notification paper. If it happened elsewhere, the local health facility or health professional prepares the notification and sends it to the registration office (Art. 41 as amended by 1049/2017).
- What to bring: the death notification paper, if any; the deceased's details (full name, sex, age, occupation, residence, citizenship, religion); and the date, place and cause of death (Art. 38). Ask the office what ID or other documents it wants.
- Register at the office nearest the deceased's principal residence. If the identity or residence is unknown, register near where the body was found (Art. 43).
- Deaths in boarding schools or other shared residences are declared by the person in charge, and deaths in prison by the head of the prison, within 5 days. Police declare an accidental death of an unidentified person within 3 days (Arts 39, 41).
- If a court declares a person absent or dead, present a copy of the judgment to the office nearest that person's last residence (Art. 44).
- The death certificate is given to the declarant. The fee is set by regulation, and a lost or damaged certificate can be replaced for a fee (Art. 47).
- In Addis Ababa: Addis Ababa Civil Registration and Residency Service Agency (aacrrsa.gov.et), phone 7533.

## Official text
**Proclamation No. 760/2012** — https://justice.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/አዋጅ-ቁጥር-760-2004.pdf (MoJ status: "Amended"), amended by **Proclamation No. 1049/2017** (Federal Negarit Gazette 23rd Year No. 74, 7 August 2017).
- Art. 18(1): "Any vital event, other than birth, shall be registered within 30 days following the date of its occurrence unless there is sufficient cause for delay." (3): late registration requires the declarant to "produce evidence to justify the delay."
- Art. 38: "The records of death shall contain … 1/ the full name, title, sex, age, occupation, principal residence, citizenship, ethnic origin and religion of the deceased; 2/ date, place and cause of the death and a reference to evidence of the death, if any; …"
- Art. 39: "1/ Any person who used to live with the deceased shall declare the death for registration. 2/ In the absence of persons used to live with the deceased, his relatives by consanguinity or affinity, close neighbors or any person who knows his death shall declare the death for registration. 3/ Any police which has received a report on the death of a person by accidental or unknown cause and whose identity is not known shall declare the death for registration within three days following the date of receipt of the report."
- Art. 40: the death of a soldier on active duty is declared by the unit's commanding officer to the officer named by the Ministry of National Defense.
- Art. 41(1)–(2): deaths in "a boarding school or in any other common dwelling place" or in prison are declared by the person in charge or the head of the prison "within five days."
- Art. 41(3)–(4) as replaced by Proc. 1049/2017: "3/ Where a death occurs in a health institution or a corpse is brought to it for examination, the institution shall forthwith prepare a notification paper of death containing the name, sex and date of birth of the deceased, the date, place and cause of death and other related information and give the notification paper to the person having the responsibility of declaring the death. 4/ Where death occurs out of a health institution, the health institution or health professional found at the lowest level of administrative hierarchy shall forthwith prepare a notification paper … and give such notification paper to the nearest administrative office found at death place."
- Art. 42: when several people die in a disaster, a missing person's death is registered on evidence from the body that investigated it.
- Art. 43: "1/ The place of registration of death shall be the nearest administrative office to the principal residence of the deceased. 2/ Where the identity or principal residence of the deceased is unknown, … the nearest administrative office to the place where his corpse was found."
- Art. 44(1): whoever obtains "a judicial declaration on the absence or death of a person shall present the copy of the judgment, for registration of death, to the officer of civil status of the nearest administrative office to the last principal residence…"
- Art. 47(4): "A certificate of registration of death shall be given to the person who has declared the death." (6): a lost or damaged certificate is replaced "upon payment of the prescribed service fee." (5) as amended: fees by Council of Ministers regulation; "Regional States may issue their own service fee laws."
- Proc. 1049/2017 new Art. 7: Ethiopian missions are the registration office for Ethiopians living abroad. New Art. 68: you can get service from any registration office that keeps copies of the record. Amended Art. 67(2): a lost certificate issued under older laws is replaced from the old register.

**UNHCR Ethiopia** — https://help.unhcr.org/ethiopia/services/documentation/birth-and-death-certificates/ — for refugees, RRS registers vital events in camps, zonal offices and its Addis Ababa offices, and certificates are free of charge.

## What is not published
- The Addis Ababa office checklist (for example, whether the deceased's kebele ID must be returned, and how many witnesses are needed without a health-facility notification). Ask your woreda civil registration office or call 7533.
- The death certificate fee in Addis Ababa and the regions. Ask the office.
- Registering a death that happened abroad: the law assigns this to Ethiopian missions. Ask the embassy.
