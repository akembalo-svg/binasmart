# ለአረጋውያን የመንግሥት ድጋፍ በኢትዮጵያ — Government Support for Older People in Ethiopia

## በአጭሩ (Amharic summary)

- በፌዴራል ደረጃ የአረጋውያን ጉዳይ ኃላፊ የሴቶችና ማህበራዊ ጉዳይ ሚኒስቴር ነው (አዋጅ ቁጥር 1263/2014 አንቀጽ 36)። ሚኒስቴሩ በአስቸጋሪ ሁኔታ ላይ ላሉ የኅብረተሰብ ክፍሎች፣ በተለይም ለአረጋውያንና ለአካል ጉዳተኞች፣ አስፈላጊውን አገልግሎት የመስጠት ኃላፊነት አለበት።
- ኢትዮጵያ የአፍሪካ አረጋውያን መብቶች ፕሮቶኮልን በአዋጅ ቁጥር 1182/2012 አጽድቃለች። አዋጁ አስፈጻሚ አድርጎ የሰየመው የሠራተኛና ማህበራዊ ጉዳይ ሚኒስቴርን ነው፤ ይህ ሥልጣን አሁን ወደ ሴቶችና ማህበራዊ ጉዳይ ሚኒስቴር ተላልፏል።
- ኢትዮጵያ ማኅበራዊና ኢኮኖሚያዊ መብቶችን የምትፈጽመው ባላት የኢኮኖሚ አቅም መጠንና ደረጃ በደረጃ መሆኑን በዚሁ አዋጅ አስታውቃለች።
- ለአረጋውያን ብቻ የወጣ አዋጅ የለም። የኢትዮጵያ ሰብዓዊ መብቶች ኮሚሽን እንደሚለው መዋጮ የማይጠይቅ በሕግ የተደነገገ የማኅበራዊ ዋስትና ሥርዓት የለም።
- በአዲስ አበባ የአዲስ አበባ ከተማ አስተዳደር ሴቶች፣ ሕፃናትና ማህበራዊ ጉዳይ ቢሮ የማህበራዊ ሴፍቲኔትና ኑሮ ማሻሻያ ዳይሬክቶሬት ለቋሚ ቀጥታ ድጋፍ ተጠቃሚዎች ትክክለኛና ወቅቱን የጠበቀ ክፍያ እንዲያገኙ ያደርጋል። ለቋሚና ለጊዜያዊ ቀጥታ ድጋፍ ተጠቃሚዎችም የኬዝ ማኔጅመንት አገልግሎት ይሰጣል።
- በአዲስ አበባ የማህበራዊ ጥበቃ ማስተባበሪያና መከታተያ ዳይሬክቶሬት ከፍተኛ የምግብ ችግር ያለባቸውን ሕፃናት፣ አረጋውያን፣ አካል ጉዳተኞችና የአእምሮ ሕሙማን ይለያል፣ ይመዘግባል፤ ወደ ምገባ ኤጀንሲ ያስተላልፋል። ተጠቃሚዎቹ በቀን አንድ ጊዜ ጥራቱን የጠበቀ ምግብ እንዲያገኙ ይደረጋል።
- ይኸው ዳይሬክቶሬት ችግረኞችን ከማህበረሰብ ክብካቤ ጥምረቶች ጋር ያገናኛል፤ እድሮችን ይመዘግባል፤ ቀባሪ ላጡ ሰዎች የቀብር አገልግሎት ይሰጣል።
- የቢሮው የአካል ጉዳተኞች፣ አረጋውያንና ቤተሰብ ጉዳዮች ዳይሬክቶሬት የአረጋውያን ማህበራትን ይደግፋል።
- ቢሮው ፒያሳ (ደጃች ውቤ) ይገኛል። ስልክ፦ +251 118-72-32-97 ወይም +251 118-58-15-52። ኢሜይል፦ bowcsa@aawcsa.gov.et

- የመንግሥት ሠራተኞች የጡረታ መውጫ ዕድሜ 60 ዓመት ነው (አዋጅ ቁጥር 1267/2014)።

## In short (English)

- At federal level, older people's affairs sit with the Ministry of Women and Social Affairs (Proclamation No. 1263/2021, Art. 36). The Ministry must provide the necessary services to people in difficult circumstances, particularly older people and persons with disabilities.
- Ethiopia ratified the AU Protocol on the Rights of Older Persons by Proclamation No. 1182/2020. That law named the Ministry of Labour and Social Affairs as the implementing body; this power has since passed to the Ministry of Women and Social Affairs.
- In the same law, Ethiopia declared that it will realise the Protocol's social and economic rights according to its available economic resources and step by step.
- There is no proclamation just for older people. The Ethiopian Human Rights Commission says there is no statutory non-contributory social security system.
- In Addis Ababa, the Social Safety Net and Livelihood Improvement Directorate of the Addis Ababa Women, Children and Social Affairs Bureau makes sure people on permanent direct support are paid correctly and on time. It also gives case management to people on permanent and temporary direct support.
- In Addis Ababa, the bureau's Social Protection Coordination and Monitoring Directorate identifies and registers children, older people, persons with disabilities and people with mental illness who face severe food shortage, and refers them to the feeding agency. They then get one quality meal a day.
- The same directorate links people in need with community care coalitions, registers iddirs, and arranges burial for people with no one to bury them.
- The bureau's Persons with Disabilities, Elderly and Family Affairs Directorate supports associations of older people.
- The bureau is in Piassa (Dejach Wube). Phone: +251 118-72-32-97 or +251 118-58-15-52. Email: bowcsa@aawcsa.gov.et
- According to the ILO (2021), the Urban Productive Safety Net Project (UPSNP) gives cash transfers to poor urban residents in 11 major cities, through public works or direct support. In the rural PSNP, households with older or disabled members and no able-bodied adults get direct transfers every month all year.
- The retirement age for public servants is 60 (Proclamation No. 1267/2022).
- Support amounts, eligibility rules, age limits and how to apply are not published. Ask the bureau or your woreda office.

## Official text

### Proclamation No. 1263/2021 — Art. 36 (Ministry of Women and Social Affairs), extracts
Source: https://justice.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/አዋጅ-ቁጥር-1263-2014.pdf

> "f) work, in collaboration with concerned organs, to make social protection and social security systems accessible to citizens step by step by expanding the same;"

> "g) work, in collaboration with concerned organs, to prevent social and economic problems and provide the necessary services to segments of the society under difficult circumstances particularly to the elderly and persons with disabilities;"

> "2/ The powers and duties entrusted to the Ministry of Labor and Social Affairs other than those concerning labor affairs and the powers and duties entrusted to the Ministry of Women, Children and Youth under other laws that are currently in force are hereby vested in the Ministry of Women and social affairs established by this Proclamation."

### Proclamation No. 1182/2020 — AU Older Persons Protocol Ratification (Negarit Gazette 26th Year No. 23, 13 March 2020)
Source: https://justice.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/አዋጅ-ቁጥር-1182-2012.pdf ; law page: https://justice.gov.et/en/law/protocol-to-the-african-charter-on-human-and-peoples-rights-on-the-rights-of-older-persons-in-africa-ratification-proclamation/

### ILO, "Mapping of the National Social Protection System in Ethiopia" (2021)
Source: https://www.ilo.org/media/381431/download

> "The UPSNP provides cash transfers to urban poor living below the poverty line in 11 major cities in exchange for participation in PW or as direct support."

> "The most vulnerable households, with elderly or disabled members and lacking any able-bodied adults, or female-headed households with high dependency ratios, receive direct transfers each month throughout the year." (This passage describes the rural PSNP. The report predates the 2021 reorganisation, so its "MoLSA" is now MoWSA or the Ministry of Labor and Skills.)

### Ethiopian Human Rights Commission — policy brief on older persons' social protection (19 Aug 2024)
Source: https://ehrc.org/policy-brief-strategies-to-strengthen-the-social-protection-system-to-enhance-the-human-rights-of-older-persons-in-ethiopia/

> "in 2017/18, there were only 317,189 old-age pensioners in Ethiopia." (citing HelpAge International 2019)

> "practically most in-need older persons in Ethiopia are not covered by any social protection intervention."

The brief notes that the final draft of the National Social Protection Policy proposed a non-contributory universal social pension for people over 70, but this was not included in the policy adopted in November 2014.

## What is not published

- How much direct support pays, and who qualifies (age, income, family situation) in Addis Ababa.
- How to apply for direct support or the feeding programme, and which office takes the application.
- An official government copy of the National Social Protection Policy (November 2014).
- Any universal pension for older people: none exists in law.
- A proclamation specifically on the rights of older people.
