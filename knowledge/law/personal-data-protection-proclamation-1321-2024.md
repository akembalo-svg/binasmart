# የግል ዳታ ጥበቃ አዋጅ ቁጥር 1321/2016 — Personal Data Protection Proclamation No. 1321/2024

## በአጭሩ (Amharic summary)

- ኢትዮጵያ የመጀመሪያውን የግል ዳታ ጥበቃ ሕግ፣ አዋጅ ቁጥር 1321/2016 (1321/2024)፣ በፌዴራል ነጋሪት ጋዜጣ ሐምሌ 17/2016 ዓ.ም (24 ጁላይ 2024) አውጥታለች። አዋጁ በታተመበት ቀን ሥራ ላይ ውሏል።
- **ተቆጣጣሪው፦** የኢትዮጵያ ኮሙኒኬሽን ባለሥልጣን (ECA) ነው። ECA የዳታ ተቆጣጣሪዎችንና አቀናባሪዎችን ይመዘግባል፣ ይቆጣጠራል፣ ቅሬታ ይመረምራል፣ መመሪያ ያወጣል። አድራሻ በአዲስ አበባ፦ ቦሌ ክ/ከተማ፣ ወረዳ 03፣ የቤት ቁጥር 2483፣ ሮቤል ፕላዛ።
- **የእርስዎ መብቶች፦** ስለሚሰበሰበው መረጃ የማወቅ፤ መረጃዎን በነፃና ያለ አላስፈላጊ መዘግየት የማግኘት፤ የማስተካከል፤ የማሰረዝ፤ የመቃወም (ለንግድ ማስታወቂያም ጭምር)፤ አቀነባበሩን የማስገደብ፤ በራስ-ሰር (automated) ውሳኔ ብቻ ያለመወሰን፤ መረጃዎን በማሽን በሚነበብ ቅርጸት ወስዶ ወደ ሌላ ተቋም የማዛወር (አንቀጽ 24–32)።
- **ቅሬታ፦** መብትዎ ከተጣሰ ለECA በጽሑፍ ቅሬታ ማቅረብ ይችላሉ። በECA ውሳኔ ካልተስማሙ በ60 ቀናት ውስጥ ለፌዴራል ከፍተኛ ፍርድ ቤት ይግባኝ ማለት ይችላሉ (አንቀጽ 58)።
- **የተቋማት ግዴታዎች፦**
  - ዳታ ከማቀናበራቸው በፊት በECA መመዝገብ። የምዝገባ ሰርተፊኬቱ ለ2 ዓመት ያገለግላል፣ በየ2 ዓመቱ ይታደሳል (አንቀጽ 33፣ 35)።
  - የመንግሥት ተቋማት እና ሰዎችን በሰፊው በመደበኛነት የሚከታተሉ ተቋማት የዳታ ጥበቃ ኃላፊ (DPO) መመደብ (አንቀጽ 40)።
  - የዳታ ጥሰት ሲያጋጥም በ72 ሰዓት ውስጥ ለECA ማሳወቅ (አንቀጽ 43)።
  - ሕጋዊ መሠረት (ለምሳሌ ፈቃድ፣ ውል፣ ሕጋዊ ግዴታ) ሲኖር ብቻ ማቀናበር (አንቀጽ 7)። ለስሱ ዳታ፣ ለሕፃናት ዳታ እና ከሀገር ውጭ ለሚተላለፍ ዳታ ተጨማሪ ጥብቅ ደንቦች አሉ።
- **ቅጣቶች (አንቀጽ 64)፦**
  - ጥሰትን አለማሳወቅ ወይም ሕገ-ወጥ ማቀናበር፦ ከ1–3 ዓመት ቀላል እስራት ወይም ከ60,000–100,000 ብር መቀጮ ወይም ሁለቱም
  - የማሰረዝ ወይም የመቃወም መብትን አለማክበር፦ ከ3–5 ዓመት እስራት ወይም ከ100,000–200,000 ብር ወይም ሁለቱም
  - የግል ዳታ መሸጥ፣ ማንነቱ የተደበቀ ዳታን መልሶ መለየት ወይም ከሀገር ውጭ በሕገ-ወጥ ማስተላለፍ፦ ከ5–10 ዓመት ጽኑ እስራት ወይም ከ200,000–600,000 ብር ወይም ሁለቱም
  - ተቋም ሲፈጽመው፣ ከባድ ጉዳት ሲያደርስ፣ ስሱ ዳታ ወይም የሕፃናት ዳታ ሲሆን፦ ካለፈው የሂሳብ ዓመት ዓለም አቀፍ ጠቅላላ ገቢ እስከ 4% መቀጮ።
- የአስተዳደራዊ ቅጣቶች ዝርዝር በደንብ እንደሚወሰን አዋጁ ይገልጻል (አንቀጽ 60)፤ ደንቡ በይፋ አልተገኘም።

## In short (English)

- Ethiopia's first data protection law, Proclamation No. 1321/2024 (Amharic numbering 1321/2016), was published in the Federal Negarit Gazette on 24 July 2024 and took effect that day.
- **Regulator:** the Ethiopian Communications Authority (ECA). It registers and supervises data controllers and processors, investigates complaints and issues directives. Address: Bole Sub-city, Woreda 03, House No. 2483, Robel Plaza, Addis Ababa.
- **Your rights:** to be informed; to get access to your data free of charge and without excessive delay; to have it corrected or erased; to object, including to direct marketing; to restrict processing; not to be subject to purely automated decisions; and to receive your data in a machine-readable format and move it to another provider (Arts. 24–32).
- **Complaints:** if your rights are violated, complain in writing to the ECA. You can appeal the ECA's decision to the Federal High Court within 60 days (Art. 58).
- **What organisations must do:**
  - register with the ECA before processing personal data. The registration certificate lasts 2 years and must be renewed every 2 years (Arts. 33, 35).
  - appoint a Data Protection Officer if they are a government body, or if their core work involves regular, systematic, large-scale monitoring of people (Art. 40).
  - report a personal data breach to the ECA within 72 hours (Art. 43).
  - process data only on a lawful basis, such as consent, a contract or a legal obligation (Art. 7). Stricter rules apply to sensitive data, children's data and transfers abroad.
- **Penalties (Art. 64):**
  - failing to report a breach, or processing unlawfully: 1–3 years' simple imprisonment, a fine of 60,000–100,000 birr, or both
  - ignoring the right to erasure or objection: 3–5 years' imprisonment, a fine of 100,000–200,000 birr, or both
  - selling personal data, re-identifying de-identified data, or transferring data abroad unlawfully: 5–10 years' rigorous imprisonment, a fine of 200,000–600,000 birr, or both
  - where the offence is committed by an institution, causes serious damage, or involves sensitive data or a child's data: a fine of up to 4% of total worldwide turnover in the previous financial year.
- The Proclamation says the details of administrative fines will be set by regulation (Art. 60). That regulation was not found.

## Official text

**Proclamation No. 1321/2024 — PDF: https://justice.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/seasiisia-seusiNse¡-1321-2016.pdf** (English text as printed in the Gazette)

> **Art. 2(36)** "“Authority” means the Ethiopian Communications Authority established as per the Communications Proclamation No. 1148/2019;"

> **Art. 7(2)** "The processing of personal data shall be regarded as lawful when:- a) The data subject has given his consent; b) Processing is necessary and is related to the fulfillment of a contract with the data subject …; c) The processing is necessary for compliance with a legal obligation to which the data controller is subject; d) The processing is necessary to protect vitally important interests of the data subject, including life and health; …"

> **Art. 25(1)** "A data subject shall have a right to obtain, on request, at reasonable intervals, free of charge, and without excessive delay: a) Confirmation of the processing of personal data relating to him; b) The communication in an intelligible form of the data processed; c) All available information on their origin; …"

> **Art. 28(1)** "A data subject shall have on request, free of charge and without excessive delay, the right to erasure of personal data where: a) The data are no longer necessary in relation to the purpose for which they were collected or otherwise processed; b) The data subject withdraws consent on which the processing is based and where there is no other legal ground for the processing; …"

> **Art. 29(1)** "The data subject shall have the right to object in writing at any time to the processing of personal data concerning him unless the data controller demonstrates in a written format compelling legitimate grounds for the processing …"

> **Art. 32(1)** "A data subject has the right to receive personal data concerning him, which the data subject has provided to a data controller or data processor, in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format."

> **Art. 33(1)** "In order to process personal data the data controller or the data processor shall be registered with the Authority."

> **Art. 35(2)** "The Authority shall issue a certificate of Registration which is valid for a period of two years; the certificate shall be renewed every two years."

> **Art. 40(1)** "A data controller or data processor shall designate or appoint a data protection officer … where: a) The processing is carried out by a goverment body, except for courts acting in their judicial capacity; b) The core activities of the data controller or data processor consist of processing operations which, by virtue of their nature, scope or purposes, require regular and systematic monitoring of data subjects on a large scale; or …"

> **Art. 43(1)** "Where there is a personal data breach, the data controller shall within 72 hours after having become aware of it, notify the personal data breach to the Authority."

> **Art. 58(1)** "Data subjects shall have the right to submit a complaint in writing to the Authority to have remedy for violation of his rights under this Proclamation." **(3)** "The decision of the Authority may be appealed to the Federal High Court within sixty days of the date the decision was rendered."

> **Art. 60(2)** "When the offence has been committed a) by an institution, b) in relation to sensitive data, or c) Against the personal data of a minor, the offence shall be punishable by a fine up to four per cent of its total worldwide turn over of the preceding financial year. Any gain made through this act will go to the government." **(3)** "… the details of administrative offences and fines shall be governed by regulation."

> **Art. 64(1)** "Any person who a) does not to notify personal data breach b) does not implement technical and organizational measures when a breach is committed; or c) Processes personal data in contravention of the provisions of this Proclamation; Shall be punished with simple imprisonment from one to three years or fined from 60,000 – 100,000 birr or both."
> **(2)** "Any person who against the rights of the data subject a) fails to erase personal data; b) fails to respect the right to object processing; c) restricts processing; or d) Does not respect the right against automated decisions shall be punished with imprisonment for a period starting from three years to five years or fined from 100,000 – 200,000 birr or both."
> **(3)** "Any person a) Re-identifies personal data which has been de-identified; or b) Processes Re-identified personal data …; c) sells or offers to sell personal data; or d) Transfers of personal data outside Ethiopia in violation of this Proclamation; shall be punished with serious imprisonment from five years to ten years or fined from 200,000 - 600,000 birr or both."
> **(4)** "… if the offence a) has been committed by an institution; or b) has caused any damage and as a result became a serious offence; c) has been committed in relation to sensitive personal data; or d) has been committed in relation the personal data of a minor, the offence shall be punishable with a fine up to four per cent of its total worldwide turnover of the preceding financial year."

> **Art. 70** "This proclamation shall enter into force on the date of publication in the Negarit Gazeta."

**Ethiopian Communications Authority — https://www.eca.et/about/**
> "Under the Personal Data Protection Proclamation No. 1321/2024, the Ethiopian Communications Authority (ECA) also serves as the national authority responsible for regulating and enforcing personal data protection in Ethiopia." — "ECA registers and supervises data controllers and processors, investigates complaints and violations, conducts compliance monitoring, and issues guidelines and directives to support responsible data processing."

## What is not published

- The ECA directive on registering data controllers and processors, including the documents needed and any fee. Ask the ECA.
- The Council of Ministers regulation that sets administrative fines.
- A dedicated ECA phone number, email or online form for data-protection complaints. The ECA "About" page gives only the street address above.
- Whether registration is already being enforced, and from what date. Ask the ECA.
