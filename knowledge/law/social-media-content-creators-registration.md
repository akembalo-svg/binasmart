# የዩቲዩብና የማኅበራዊ ሚዲያ ይዘት ፈጣሪዎች ምዝገባ — Do Content Creators Need to Register?

## በአጭሩ (Amharic summary)

- እስከ 17 September 2026 ድረስ ለዩቲዩብ፣ ቲክቶክ ወይም ፌስቡክ ይዘት ፈጣሪዎች ምዝገባን **ግዴታ የሚያደርግ ሕግ ወይም መመሪያ አልተገኘም**።
- የመገናኛ ብዙኃን አዋጅ 1238/2021 "ኦንላይን ሚዲያ" የሚለው ዋና ሥራው ዜና ወይም ፕሮግራም ማዘጋጀትና ማሰራጨት የሆነና በኤዲቶሪያል ኃላፊነት የሚሠራ ድርጅትን ነው። አዋጁም ቢሆን ምዝገባን "ሊጠይቁ ይችላሉ" ይላል።
- የባለሥልጣኑ መመሪያ ቁጥር 902/2014 የሚከተሉትን ከ"ኦንላይን ሚዲያ" ትርጉም ውጭ ያደርጋል፦ ማኅበራዊ ሚዲያን (ኦንላይን ሚዲያው ሙሉ በሙሉ በእሱ ካልተሰራጨ በቀር)፣ የግለሰብ ጦማሮችን (ብሎግ)፣ በግለሰቦች መካከል የሚደረግ የመረጃ ልውውጥን እና ሌሎችን።
- ስለዚህ የግል ይዘት ፈጣሪ (ዋና ሥራው ዜና ወይም ፕሮግራም ያልሆነ) ከሕጉ ትርጉም ውጭ ነው።
- ሙሉ በሙሉ በዩቲዩብ ወይም በፌስቡክ የሚሠራ የዜና ወይም የፕሮግራም ተቋም ግን በትርጉሙ ውስጥ ይገባል፤ ቢሆንም ሕጉ "ሊጠይቅ ይችላል" ነው የሚለው።
- በዜና ዘገባ (ethiopiatoday.net፣ 12 July 2026) መሠረት የኢትዮጵያ መገናኛ ብዙኃን ባለሥልጣን ዋና ዳይሬክተር ሃይማኖት ዘለቀ አሁን ባለው ሕግ ምዝገባ በፈቃደኝነት እንደሆነና ግዴታ እንዳልሆነ ገልጸዋል።
- ባለሥልጣኑ ሁሉንም ዲጂታል ሚዲያ በምዝገባ ሥርዓት ውስጥ ለማስገባት አዋጁን ለማሻሻል ማቀዱን ተናግሯል፤ ይህ ማሻሻያ እስካሁን አልጸደቀም።
- መመዝገብ ከፈለጉ ሂደቱ በ"የበይነ-መረብ መገናኛ ብዙኃን ምዝገባ" ገጽ ተገልጿል።
- በዲጂታል ይዘት ገቢ ላይ ስለሚጣል ግብር ረቂቅ እንዳለ ተዘግቧል፤ የጸደቀ ሕግ ወይም የግብር መጣኔ አልተገኘም።

## In short (English)

- As of 17 September 2026, **no law or directive makes registration mandatory** for YouTube, TikTok or Facebook content creators.
- Proclamation 1238/2021 defines "online media" as an organization whose principal business is producing and disseminating news or programmes under editorial responsibility, and says Ethiopians "may request" registration.
- EMA Directive 902/2014 E.C. excludes from "online media": social media (unless an online media outlet is distributed entirely through it), individual blogs, exchanges between individuals, online books, and more.
- A personal creator whose main business is not news or programme production therefore falls outside the definition.
- A news or programme outlet that runs entirely on YouTube or Facebook falls inside the definition — but even then the law says "may request".
- According to news reports (ethiopiatoday.net, 12 July 2026), EMA Director General Haymanot Zeleke said that under current law people "'may apply' for certification but are not legally obligated to do so."
- The EMA has said it wants to amend the proclamation so all media join the registration system; no such amendment had been enacted as of 17 September 2026.
- Some press articles claim individuals "must now be registered"; that conflicts with the Proclamation and the EMA's own July 2026 statement.
- A Ministry of Finance draft to tax digital content income has been reported, but no enacted text or rate was found.

## Official text

**Media Proclamation No. 1238/2021, Art. 2(4)** — https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/legislation/details/21659
"'Online media' means an internet-based information dissemination service by an organization whose principal business involves the collection, production, processing and dissemination of news or programs or news and programs, through online images, audio, video and websites or through a combination of the aforementioned means, in accordance with the editorial responsibility of a media service provider;"

Art. 27(1): "Ethiopian citizens either privately or through juridical person may request for an online media registration after fulfilling the following conditions:"

**EMA Internet Media Directive No. 902/2014 E.C., Art. 2(1) — exclusions from "online media"** — https://justice.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/Internet-Media-Directive-No.-902-2014.pdf
> ሀ) በበይነ-መረብ የሚሰራጭ መፅሀፍን፤
> ለ) የበይነ-መረብ መገናኛ ብዙሃኑ ሙሉ በሙሉ የሚሰራጭበት እስካልሆነ ድረስ ማኅበራዊ መገናኛ ብዙሃንን፤
> ሐ) የበይነ-መረብ ግለሰብ ጦማሮችን፤
> መ) የበይነ-መረብ ጋዜጣና መፅሔት አካል ያልሆነ ፎቶ፣ ሥዕልና ካርቱንን፤
> ሠ) በግለሰቦች መካከል የሚደረግ የበይነ-መረብ መረጃ ልውውጥን፤ እና፣
> ረ) የበይነ-መረብ መገናኛ ብዙሃን አገልግሎት ለመስጠት ባልተቋቋሙ ተቋማት የሚሰራጭ የድረ-ገፅ ይዘት፡፡

Unofficial English: (a) books published online; (b) social media, unless the online media outlet is distributed entirely through it; (c) individual online blogs; (d) photos, drawings and cartoons not part of an online newspaper or magazine; (e) online exchanges between individuals; (f) website content of bodies not set up to provide online media services.

## What is not published

- No EMA directive specifically for content creators or influencers was found.
- No enacted amendment making digital-media registration mandatory was found as of 17 September 2026; if one is passed, this page will be out of date.
- No enacted tax rule or rate for income from digital content was found.
