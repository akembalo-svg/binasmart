## In short (English)

- In Addis Ababa, report illegal dumping to the Addis Ababa City Administration Code Enforcement Authority on the free line **9995** (also 0115621721).
- Code Enforcement Authority email: Codeenforcement05@gmail.com; office: Piassa, the area known as "Eri Bekentu", EMA Tower, floors 7–13.
- Pollution complaints: Addis Ababa Environmental Protection Authority, tel. 011-8-33-41-30, info@addisnviroment.gov.et (spelled this way on the site), Gurd Shola, 200 m from the Athletics Federation, Monday–Friday 8:30am–5:30pm.
- Regulation 150/2023, Penalty Schedule 2 — taking solid waste out of a compound and dumping it in an unauthorized place: household Birr 500; industrial Birr 10,000; health institution Birr 15,000; other organizations Birr 2,000 — plus removing the waste.
- Littering cigarette butts, tissues, plastic bags, chat leftovers, tickets etc. on roads or drains: Birr 200, plus cleaning the place.
- Dumping waste from a car: Birr 1,000; driver or passenger throwing waste out of a window or door: Birr 200.
- Sand, soil, grass, leaves or construction debris in an unauthorized place without a permit: Birr 1,000. Removing or damaging roadside litter bins: Birr 20,000.
- Refusing to pay means court and double the fine; repeat offenders pay double.
- **Caution:** later city regulations (No. 167 and No. 180) may have changed these amounts. The Authority itself reported fines of 30,000–50,000 Birr on businesses and a hospital in Arada sub-city. Check the current amount on 9995.
- Nationwide (Proclamation 1383/2025): no dumping outside designated areas and no burning waste in cities; households must keep 20 m around their home clean, businesses 50 m.

**Federal Proclamation No. 1383/2025** — https://www.epa.gov.et/images/PDF/Proc.No%201383-2025%20Solid%20Waste%20Management%20and%20Disposal.pdf
Art. 9(1): "No person shall dispose of solid waste outside the designated area."
Art. 9(2): "No person shall dispose of solid waste by burning it in public or private spaces in cities."
Art. 10(1): "Each person shall clean its surroundings and prevent littering within a radius of twenty meters from its household." Art. 10(2): commercial and service institutions, "within a radius of fifty meters from its premises."
