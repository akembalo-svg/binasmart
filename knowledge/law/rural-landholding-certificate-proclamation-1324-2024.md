# የገጠር መሬት ይዞታ ማረጋገጫ ደብተር — Rural Landholding Certificate for Farmers

**ማሳሰቢያ፦** የቀድሞው የፌዴራል የገጠር መሬት አስተዳደርና አጠቃቀም አዋጅ ቁጥር 456/1997 (456/2005) ተሽሯል። አሁን የሚሠራው የገጠር መሬት አስተዳደርና አጠቃቀም አዋጅ ቁጥር 1324/2016 (1324/2024) ነው። አዋጁ ነሐሴ 8 ቀን 2016 (14 August 2024) በነጋሪት ጋዜጣ ታትሞ ጸንቷል (አንቀጽ 67(1)፣ 68)። ከዚህ በፊት የተሰጡ የይዞታ ማረጋገጫ ደብተሮች አሁንም ያገለግላሉ (አንቀጽ 65(2))።

**Note:** Proclamation No. 456/2005 is repealed and replaced by the Rural Land Administration and Use Proclamation No. 1324/2024. It took effect on publication, 14 August 2024 (Arts. 67(1), 68). Certificates issued before then remain valid (Art. 65(2)).

## በአጭሩ (Amharic summary)

- የግል፣ የወል እና የመንግሥት ይዞታዎች በዘመናዊ መሣሪያ ተለክተው ይመዘገባሉ። የተመዘገበ መሬት ባለይዞታ ወሰኑንና ስፋቱን የሚያሳይ የይዞታ ማረጋገጫ ደብተር የማግኘት መብት አለው (አንቀጽ 22)።
- ለእያንዳንዱ ማሳ (parcel) የተለየ ደብተር ይሰጣል (አንቀጽ 51(1))።
- መሬቱ የባልና ሚስት ወይም የጋራ ከሆነ ደብተሩ በሁሉም የጋራ ባለይዞታዎች ስም ይዘጋጃል (አንቀጽ 22(3))። የወል መሬት ደብተር በተጠቃሚው ማኅበረሰብ ስም ይሰጣል (አንቀጽ 24(3))።
- የት ይሄዳሉ? ምዝገባና ልኬት የሚያካሂዱት ክልሎች በክልል፣ በዞን፣ በልዩ ወረዳ፣ በከተማና በወረዳ ደረጃ የሚያቋቁሟቸው የገጠር መሬት ምዝገባና ቅየሳ ክፍሎች ናቸው (አንቀጽ 42)። በወረዳዎ ያለውን የገጠር መሬት አስተዳደር ጽ/ቤት ይጠይቁ።
- ምዝገባው አስገዳጅና ሥርዓት ያለው፣ በማሳ ላይ የተመሠረተና ተሳትፎ ያለበት ነው (አንቀጽ 43፣ 46)። በምዝገባ ወቅት ወኪል ከቀረበ የውክልና ሥልጣን ማቅረብ አለበት (አንቀጽ 47(3))።
- ክፍያ፦ ለመጀመሪያ ምዝገባና ልኬት ምንም ክፍያ አይጠየቅም። መረጃ ሲሻሻል (ለምሳሌ ባለይዞታ ሲቀየር) የአገልግሎት ክፍያ ይከፈላል። ማሳ ለማቀናጀት በሚደረግ ልውውጥ ግን ክፍያ አይጠየቅም (አንቀጽ 58)። የክፍያው መጠን በክልል ሕግ ይወሰናል።
- ቅሬታ፦ መረጃው ወደ መዝገብ ከመግባቱ በፊት ቅሬታ ማቅረብ ይቻላል። የጊዜ ገደቡን ክልሉ ይወስናል፤ ግን ከ6 ወር ማነስ የለበትም (አንቀጽ 48)። ጊዜው ካለፈ በኋላ "አላወቅሁም" ብሎ መቃወም አይቻልም።
- የደብተሩ ዋጋ፦ ደብተሩ ባለይዞታነትን የሚያረጋግጥ የመጨረሻ ማስረጃ ነው። ሊቃወሙት የሚቻለው ምዝገባው ሥልጣን በሌለው አካል ከተካሄደ ወይም በሕገ-ወጥ ሰነድ ከተመሠረተ ብቻ ነው (አንቀጽ 52)።
- ባለይዞታ ሲቀየር፣ ማሳ ሲከፈል ወይም ሲዋሃድ መረጃው መሻሻል አለበት (አንቀጽ 53(1))።
- ቅጣት፦ ሐሰተኛ ደብተር ማቅረብ ከ10,000 እስከ 25,000 ብር ወይም ከ1 እስከ 3 ዓመት ጽኑ እስራት ወይም ሁለቱንም ያስቀጣል። መሬት መሸጥ ወይም መግዛት ደግሞ ከ100,000 እስከ 200,000 ብር ወይም ከ1 እስከ 5 ዓመት ጽኑ እስራት ወይም ሁለቱንም ያስቀጣል (አንቀጽ 62)።
- የፌዴራል ብሔራዊ የገጠር መሬት መረጃ ሥርዓትን የሚያደራጀው የግብርና ሚኒስቴር ነው (አንቀጽ 54)።

## In short (English)

- Private, communal and state holdings are surveyed with modern instruments and registered. The holder of registered land has the right to a holding certificate showing the plot's boundaries and size (Art. 22).
- A separate certificate is issued for each parcel (Art. 51(1)).
- Land held by spouses or other joint holders is certified in all their names (Art. 22(3)). Communal land is certified in the name of the user community (Art. 24(3)).
- Where to go: regions set up rural land registration and surveying departments at regional, zone, special woreda, city and woreda level (Art. 42). Ask your woreda rural land administration office.
- Registration is compulsory, systematic, parcel-based and participatory (Arts. 43, 46). A representative who comes on your behalf must show a power of attorney (Art. 47(3)).
- Fees: first-time registration and surveying are free. Updating the record, for example after a change of holder, costs a service fee. Land exchanges made to consolidate plots are free (Art. 58). Regional law sets the fee amounts.
- Objections: you can object before the information is entered in the register. Your region sets the deadline, which must be at least 6 months (Art. 48). After it passes, you cannot object on the grounds that you did not know about the registration.
- Legal effect: the certificate is the ultimate evidence of who holds the land. It can only be challenged if an unauthorized body did the registration or it was based on an unlawful document (Art. 52).
- The record must be updated when the holder changes or a parcel is divided or merged (Art. 53(1)).
- Penalties: submitting a forged certificate carries a fine of Birr 10,000–25,000, 1–3 years' rigorous imprisonment, or both. Selling or buying land carries a fine of Birr 100,000–200,000, 1–5 years' rigorous imprisonment, or both (Art. 62).
- The Ministry of Agriculture runs the national rural land information system (Art. 54).

## Official text

**Rural Land Administration and Use Proclamation No. 1324/2024** — Federal Negarit Gazette, 30th Year No. 58, 14 August 2024. PDF: https://justice.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/seasiisia-seusiNse¡-1324-2016.pdf (via https://justice.gov.et/en/law/rural-land-administration-and-use-proclamation/)

- Art. 2(27): "“Ministry” means the Ministry of Agriculture;"
- Art. 22: "1/ Private, communal, and state holdings shall be surveyed using modern surveying instruments and registered. 2/ Holders of lands surveyed and registered under Sub-Article (1) of this Article shall have the right to get a holding certificate for their parcels showing boundaries and land size. 3/ The holding certificate shall be prepared in the name of all joint holders where the land belongs to spouses or other joint holders."
- Art. 42(1): "Rural land registration and surveying departments shall be established by regional governments under their Rural Land Administration and Use Institutions."
- Art. 43: "1/ The rural land adjudication system shall be compulsory and systematic. 2/ The registration system shall be parcel based."
- Art. 48(2)–(3): "The time limit to be determined as per Sub- Article (1) of this Article may not be less than six month." / "After the lapse of the time limit … every land holder is presumed to be aware of the registered information. objection based on lack of knowledge of registration is not acceptable."
- Art. 51: "1/ Rural landholders shall be issued with a landholding certificate for each of their parcels. 2/ The landholding certificates may carry secret codes."
- Art. 52(2): "Landholding certificate is the ultimate evidence that proves a landholder. Contrary evidence may not be admitted to disprove the landholding certificate."
- Art. 52(3): "Objections over rights accrued under Sub-Articles (1) and (2) of this Article may only be made where: a) The registration was conducted by an unauthorized organ; or b) The registration was made based on an unlawful document."
- Art. 58: "1/ No payment shall be requested for initial registration and surveying. 2/ Service fees shall be paid for updating landholding data. 3/ Service fees may not be paid in cases of land exchange which has the effect of land consolidation."
- Art. 62(5): "A landholder who submits a forged landholding certificate, a professional who hides evidence or gives false evidence shall be fined with payment of 10,000 to 25,000 Birr or punished with 1 to 3 years rigorous imprisonment or both."
- Art. 62(6): "Anyone who sells or buys land shall be fined with payment of 100,000 to 200,000 Birr or punished with 1 to 5 years of rigorous imprisonment or both."
- Art. 65(2): "Landholding certificates, which were issued before the coming in to force of this Proclamation shall be valid."
- Art. 67(1): "The Federal Rural Land Administration and Use Proclamation No 456/2005 is hereby repealed."

## What is not published

- Fee amounts for updating a certificate, and the exact objection deadline in your region.
- How long it takes to receive the certificate.
- A national online portal where farmers can check their parcel. None was found.
