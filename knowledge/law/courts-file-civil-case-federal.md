## In short (English)

- A civil case starts with a written statement of claim. It must state the court's name and place, the parties' names, residence and address for service, the facts of the cause of action (when and where it arose), the facts showing the court has jurisdiction, and the exact relief claimed (CPC Arts. 222, 224).
- For a money claim, the precise amount must be stated (Art. 226(1)).
- Attach a certified list of witnesses (full name and address) and of documents, the original and a copy of documents you hold, or a declaration that you have none; file enough copies for every defendant (Art. 223).
- The registrar rejects a claim that is not in the Art. 222 form, lacks the Art. 223 annexes, or is not verified per Art. 92 (Art. 229).
- The court fee is paid before the file is opened and numbered, by cash, bank, card or electronic payment (Fee Regulation 1/2024, Art. 5).
- Non-monetary claims: Birr 1,000 at the Federal First Instance Court, Birr 1,500 at the Federal High Court, Birr 2,000 at the Federal Supreme Court (Art. 8). Money claims are charged by the percentage table attached to the Regulation (Art. 7); ask the registry for the amount.
- Other fees (FSC page): summons Birr 50 per witness; certified copies Birr 5 per page; setting aside an ex parte judgment Birr 500; intervention Birr 1,000.
- Exempt from fees: alimony and child custody applications, persons allowed to sue as paupers, criminal cases, environmental cases and the other items in Art. 21.
- Refunds (Art. 20): withdrawal before adjournment/summons, 5% deducted; dismissal for no cause of action or no jurisdiction, 10% deducted; case interrupted before trial, 15% deducted; no refund if withdrawn after trial has begun.
- E-filing: per the FSC FAQ, "only lawyers" can currently file electronically. Self-represented parties should go to the court registry.
- Federal First Instance Court benches in Addis Ababa: Lideta, Addis Ketema, Yeka, Arada, Kolfe Keraniyo, Bole, Kirkos, Menagesha, Akaki, Nefas Silk Lafto; plus Dire Dawa. Phones below.
- Federal Supreme Court: +251-11-1-56-55-71, short code 992; hours Mon–Fri 8:30–12:30 and 1:30–5:30.

## Official text

### Civil Procedure Code 1965 (English scan, verbatim; OCR typos marked [sic])
Located via https://www.lawethiopia.com/images/codes/Civil%20Procedure%20Code%20(English).pdf (no government copy found).

**Art. 222(1).** "Every statement of claim shall contain: (a) the name and place of the court in which the action is brought; (b) the title of the action; (c) the name, description, place of residence and address for service of the plaintiff and defendant; (d) where the plaintiff or defendant is a person under disability, a statement to that effect; (e) where the plaintiff is suing in a representative capacity, a statement showing the capacity in which he is suing; (f) the facts constituting the cause of action, and when and where it arose; (g) the facts showing that the court has jurisdiction; (h) the facts showing that the defendant is or claims to be interested in the subject-matter and is liable to be called upon to answer the claim; (i) where appropriate, a statement of the value of the subject-mater [sic] of the action."

**Art. 223(1)-(2).** "(1) The plaintiff shall attach to the statement of claim: (a) a list, which he shall certify to be complete, of the witnesses to be called at the hearing, with their full name and address and the purpose for which they are to be called, and of the documents on which he relies, specifying in whose possession or power such documents are; (b) the original and a copy of any document in his possession upon which he sues; (c) where he has no witnesses or documents to produce, a declaration to that effect. (2) A sufficient number of copies of the statement of claim and list, documents or declaration annexed thereto shall be filed for the purpose of service on all the defendants named therein."

**Art. 224(1).** "The statement of claim shall state specifically the relief which the plaintiff claims either simply or in the alternative ..."

**Art. 226(1).** "Where the plaintiff seeks the recovery of money the precise amount claimed shall be indicated in the statement of claim."

**Art. 229.** "The statement of claim shall be rejected by the registrar where: (a) it is not in the form provided for by Art. 222; (b) it is not accompanied by the annexes provided for by Art. 223; or (c) it is not verified in the manner provided for by Art. 92."

### Federal Courts Court Fee Regulation No. 1/2024 (English column, verbatim)
Source: https://justice.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/ደንብ-ቁጥር-1-2017.pdf (Federal Negarit Gazette 31st Year No. 41, 7 May 2025)

**Art. 5.** "1/ Unless otherwise exempted by this Regulation and other Laws, any party who file a Suit with the Court shall pay the court fee. ... 3/ Court fee may be paid by cash, bank, credit card, debit card or electronic payment method as the court deems convenient. 4/ The court fee shall be paid before the Suit File is opened and Numbered."

**Art. 7.** "... the court fee should be calculated based on the percentage stated in table attached to this Regulation."

**Art. 8.** "1/ If the application is submitted to the Federal First Instance Court, Birr 1,000 (One Thousand) shall be paid" — Federal High Court Birr 1,500; Federal Supreme Court Birr 2,000; Birr 500 for approval of a contract between spouses about their property under the Family Code; Birr 100 for leave to appeal or cassation out of time.

**Art. 20. Refunding of Court Fee.** "1/ If the plaintiff decides to withdraw the case before an adjournment is given or before the summons is issued, 5% (Five Percent) of the court fee paid shall be deducted ... 2/ If the Court ... dismisses the case on the grounds that the plaintiff has no reason to bring the suit or the Court lacks jurisdiction ..., 10% (Ten Percent) ... shall be deducted ... 3/ If the case is interrupted before Trial, 15% (Fifteen Percent) of the Court Fee paid shall be deducted ... 6/ The total court fee shall not be refunded if the plaintiff withdraws the suit after the trial has begun."

**Art. 21. Matters Exempted from Court Fee.** "1/ Applications regarding alimony and child custody; 2/ Suits for the enforcement of the collective and basic human rights of women, children, elderly persons, persons with disabilities, matters related to vulnerable groups of the society, public interest suits and suits of Habeas Corpus brought by Civil society organizations; 3/ Cases related to Environmental protection; 4/ A person allowed to bring the Suit as a Pauper in accordance with the law; 5/ Criminal Cases; 6/ An application submitted by an individual Employee for judicial review of a decision rendered by an Administrative Agency."

**Art. 23(1).** "The Court Fee rule under legal notice No. 177/1952 is hereby repealed by this Regulation."
