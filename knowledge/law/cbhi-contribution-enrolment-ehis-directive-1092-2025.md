# የማኅበረሰብ አቀፍ የጤና መድን (ማአጤመ) — CBHI contribution and enrolment

## በአጭሩ

- **ማን ይመዘገባል:** የማኅበረሰብ አቀፍ የጤና መድን መደበኛ ባልሆነ ዘርፍ ለሚተዳደሩ ቤተሰቦች ነው፤ ለምሳሌ አርሶ አደሮች፣ ነጋዴዎችና የቀን ሠራተኞች። የሚመዘገበው አባወራው/እማወራው ወይም ነጠላው ሰው ነው።
- **የሚያስፈልግ ማስረጃ (ደንብ 535/2015 አንቀጽ 7):** ከሚከተሉት አንዱ፦ የቀበሌ መታወቂያ ወይም ፓስፖርት፣ መንጃ ፈቃድ፣ ወይም ነዋሪነትን የሚያረጋግጥ ከመንግሥት አስተዳደር የተሰጠ ደብዳቤ። ምንም ሰነድ የሌለው ሰው በቀበሌው ቋሚ ነዋሪ በሆኑ **3 ምስክሮች** ሊመዘገብ ይችላል።
- **የምዝገባ ጊዜ:** ምዝገባና ዕድሳት **በዓመት አንድ ጊዜ ለሁለት ተከታታይ ወራት** ብቻ ይካሄዳል። ጊዜው በ**መስከረም 1 እና በየካቲት 30** መካከል ሲሆን ትክክለኛውን ወር ክልሉ ይወስናል። ከዚህ ጊዜ ውጭ ምዝገባ የለም (አዋጅ 1273/2014 አንቀጽ 14፤ ደንብ 535 አንቀጽ 8)። አባልነት ለአንድ ዓመት ያገለግላል፤ በየዓመቱ ይታደሳል።
- **መዋጮው ስንት ነው:** የብር መጠኑ በሕጉ ውስጥ **አልተጻፈም**። መጠኑን የማአጤመ ብሔራዊ ምክር ቤት በኢትዮጵያ ጤና መድን አገልግሎት ጥናት መሠረት በ**የመክፈል አቅም እርከን** ይወስናል (መመሪያ 1092 አንቀጽ 6(1))። በከተማና በገጠር ሊለያይ ይችላል (አዋጅ አንቀጽ 15(6))። ትክክለኛውን መጠን **የወረዳዎን የጤና መድን ጽ/ቤት** ይጠይቁ።
- ለተጨማሪ ቤተሰብ አባል የሚከፈለው ከመሠረታዊ ቤተሰብ መዋጮ **ከአንድ አምስተኛ ያላነሰ** ነው (ደንብ 535 አንቀጽ 9)። መንግሥት ከጠቅላላ ዓመታዊ መዋጮው **25%** አጠቃላይ ድጎማ ይሸፍናል (አዋጅ አንቀጽ 15(4))።
- መዋጮው **በምዝገባ ወቅት በአንድ ጊዜ** ይከፈላል። ከዚህ በተጨማሪ **የመመዝገቢያ ክፍያ** አለ፤ መጠኑን ክልሎች ይወስናሉ። ለክፍያው ደረሰኝ ይሰጣል (መመሪያ 1092 አንቀጽ 6)።
- **መክፈል የማይችሉ (ድሆች):** በወረዳ አስተዳደር ተለይተው ይመዘገባሉ። መዋጮአቸውን ክልሉ/ወረዳው በ**የተናጠል ድጎማ** ሙሉ በሙሉ ይሸፍናል። የፎቶ ወጪያቸውንም ወረዳው ይሸፍናል (አዋጅ አንቀጽ 2(11) እና 15(5)፤ መመሪያ 1092 አንቀጽ 3(3)፣ 4(3)፣ 6(7))።
- **መታወቂያ:** መዋጮ ከከፈሉ በኋላ በ6 ወር ውስጥ የተነሳ የእርስዎንና የቤተሰብዎን ፎቶ ያቀርባሉ። ከ2 ዓመት በታች ለሆኑ ልጆች ፎቶ አያስፈልግም። መታወቂያው በ**15 ቀን** ይሰጣል፤ ለ**5 ዓመት** ያገለግላል። ከዘገየ የመዋጮ ደረሰኝዎን አሳይተው መታከም ይችላሉ (መመሪያ 1092 አንቀጽ 4)።
- **ኢትዮጵያ ጤና መድን አገልግሎት (ኢጤመአ):** አድራሻ በአዲስ አበባ፣ ንፋስ ስልክ ላፍቶ ክ/ከተማ ወረዳ 7፣ ጎተራ ፔፕሲ ፋብሪካ አካባቢ። ስልክ፦ 011-5-576721/23፣ 011-5-576758/36። የመረጃ ዴስክ በድረ-ገጹ "9060" ተብሎ ተገልጿል። ኢሜይል፦ ehis@ehis.gov.et። የሥራ ሰዓት፦ ሰኞ–ዓርብ 8:30–5:00 (ድረ-ገጹ እንደጻፈው)።

## In short (English)

- **Who:** CBHI is for households outside formal employment, such as farmers, traders and daily workers. The household head, or a single person, registers.
- **Proof of residence (Regulation 535/2023, Art. 7):** one of a kebele ID or passport, a driving licence, or a letter from the government administration. People with no documents can register on the testimony of **three permanent residents** of the kebele.
- **When:** registration and renewal happen **once a year, for two consecutive months** between **Meskerem 1 and Yekatit 30**. Each region sets its own window, and there is no enrolment outside it (Proclamation 1273/2022, Art. 14; Regulation 535, Art. 8). Membership lasts one year and must be renewed.
- **How much:** the law gives **no Birr figure**. The national CBHI council sets contribution tiers by **ability to pay**, based on an EHIS study (Directive 1092, Art. 6(1)). Amounts may differ between urban and rural areas (Proclamation, Art. 15(6)). **Ask your woreda health insurance office** for the current amount.
- Each extra family member pays at least **one fifth** of the basic family contribution (Regulation 535, Art. 9). The federal government adds a **25%** general subsidy (Proclamation, Art. 15(4)).
- You pay the contribution **in one payment when you register**, plus a **registration fee** set by the region, and you get a receipt (Directive 1092, Art. 6).
- **People who cannot pay (indigents):** the woreda identifies them. A targeted regional/woreda subsidy must cover their full contribution, and the woreda also pays for their photos (Proclamation, Arts. 2(11) and 15(5); Directive 1092, Arts. 3(3), 4(3), 6(7)). Indigents are also exempt from any co-payment (Regulation 535, Art. 15).
- **Membership ID:** after paying, submit photos of yourself and your family taken within the last six months (not needed for children under two). The card is issued within **15 days** and is valid for **5 years**. If it is late, you can get treatment by showing your contribution receipt (Directive 1092, Art. 4).
- **EHIS contacts (ehis.gov.et):** Gotera around Pepsi Factory, Woreda 7, Nifas Silk Lafto Sub-city, Addis Ababa. Tel. (+251) 011-5-576721/23 and 011-5-576758/36. The site shows "Information Desk (+251) 9060". Email ehis@ehis.gov.et. Open Mon–Fri 8:30am–5:00pm.

## Official text

**Proclamation No. 1273/2022 (Community Based Health Insurance)** — https://justice.gov.et/en/law/community-based-health-insurance-proclamation/
> Art. 14: 1/ Registration will be valid for one year and must be renewed annually. 2/ Member registration or renewal will be carried out once a year for two consecutive months only. 3/ The respective regional implementing body shall determine the registration and renewal period.
> Art. 15: 4/ The amount of the General Subsidy ... shall be 25 Percent of the total annual contribution. 5/ The annual contribution of indigents shall be covered through targeted subsidy. 6/ Community Based Health Insurance contribution amount may vary with income level and between urban and rural settings by considering the community's ability to pay and the cost of health services.

**Council of Ministers Regulation No. 535/2023**
> Art. 7(1): Membership registration shall be made by the household head or a single individual's residence upon receipt of one of the following proof of residency: a) kebele identification card or a passport; b) driving license; c) a letter from the Government Administration proving residence. (2) ... persons without any documents can be registered based on the testimonies of three individuals that have permanent residence in the Kebele ...
> Art. 8(1): The two-month-long Community-Based Health Insurance enrollment and renewal period ... shall range from Meskerem 1 to Yekatit 30.

**EHIS Directive No. 1092/2017 E.C. (የማህበረሰብ አቀፍ የጤና መድኅን መመሪያ)** — PDF https://justice.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/1092-የማህበረሰብ-አቀፍ-የጤና-መድኅን-መመሪያ-1092-2017.pdf

> አንቀጽ 6(3)፦ የአባልነት መዋጮ በአንድ ጊዜ ክፍያ የሚከፈል ሆኖ በምዝገባ ወቅት መከፈል ይኖርበታል።
> አንቀጽ 6(5)፦ ... የሚከፈለው የመመዝገቢያ ክፍያ መጠን በክልሎች ይወሰናል።

## What is not published

- **Annual contribution in Birr** (any year or region): not found in any official source. Ask your woreda or kebele CBHI office, or call EHIS.
- **Registration fee amount:** set by each region and not published centrally.
- **Exact enrolment month for your region:** set by the regional implementing body. Ask the woreda office.
- **Online enrolment:** none found. Enrolment is done through the kebele or woreda.
- The **"9060"** information-desk number appears on ehis.gov.et without further explanation. If it does not connect, use the 011 numbers above.
