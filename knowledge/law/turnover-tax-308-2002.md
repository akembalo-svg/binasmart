# Turnover Tax Proclamation No. 308/2002 — የተርን ኦቨር ታክስ አዋጅ ቁጥር 308/1995

Federal Negarit Gazeta No. 21, Addis Ababa, 31 December 2002; in force from 31 December 2002 (Art. 41). Listed as in force by the Ministry of Revenue on mor.gov.et, together with the Turnover Tax (Amendment) Proclamation No. 611/2008 (25 December 2008). The English column below comes from the Ministry's old scanned PDF and still carries the scanner's misreadings (for example "Short Tide", "BiIT" for Birr); where a figure matters, check it against the Gazette. The Amharic column is in a legacy font and is not reproduced. Proclamation 611/2008 is not reproduced either: its PDF on mor.gov.et extracts as mixed garbage; readable parts show that it renamed the tax authority, allowed a turnover tax return to be filed and paid electronically, and replaced Article 20 so that administrative penalties may be waived under directives, without waiving interest.

Much of the procedure in this 2002 law (filing, assessment, collection, penalties, appeals) is now governed for every federal tax by the Federal Tax Administration Proclamation No. 983/2016, whose definition of "tax law" includes the Turnover Tax Proclamation (Art. 2(36)(f)). The accounting periods in Art. 10 refer to the taxpayer categories of the repealed Income Tax Proclamation 286/2002.

## What this law covers (in brief)

A tax on the gross receipts of sellers of goods and services who are not registered for VAT, collected from the buyer.

## Key articles

- Art. 3 — turnover tax is payable on goods supplied and services rendered by persons not registered for value added tax, unless exempted.
- Art. 4 — rates: 2% on goods sold locally; for services rendered locally, 2% for contractors, grain mills, tractors and combine-harvesters, and 10% for other services.
- Art. 5 — the base is gross receipts, without deducting expenses.
- Art. 6 — the seller collects the tax from the buyer and pays it to the tax authority, and is principally accountable for it.
- Art. 7 — exempt: the sale or transfer of a dwelling used for at least two years, and the lease of a dwelling; financial services; national or foreign currency and securities; religious services by religious organisations; prescription drugs and medical services; education and pre-school child care; humanitarian aid; electricity, kerosene and water; transport; permits and licence fees; goods and services of workshops where more than 60% of employees are disabled; books. The Minister may exempt others by directive.
- Art. 10 — a turnover tax return is filed, and the tax paid, within one month after the end of each accounting period: monthly for category "A" taxpayers not required to register for VAT, every three months for category "B" taxpayers who keep records, and the fiscal year for category "C" taxpayers (categories of the repealed Income Tax Proclamation 286/2002).
- Art. 17 — a change of name, address, place of business or principal activity is notified within 21 days.
