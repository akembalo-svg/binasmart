# የቡና ላኪነት ፈቃድ መስፈርቶች — Requirements for a Coffee Export Licence

## በአጭሩ (Amharic summary)

- የቡና ግብይትና ጥራት ቁጥጥርን የሚመራው የቡና ግብይትና ጥራት ቁጥጥር አዋጅ ቁጥር 1051/2009 (1051/2017) ነው። የሚያስፈጽመው የኢትዮጵያ ቡናና ሻይ ባለስልጣን (ECTA) ነው። አዋጁን የሚያሻሽል አዲስ አዋጅ አላገኘንም።
- ቡና ላኪ መሆን የሚችለው ከሚመለከተው የመንግሥት አካል የብቃት ማረጋገጫ ምስክር ወረቀትና የንግድ ፈቃድ የያዘ ሰው ነው (አንቀጽ 10(1))።
- የብቃት ማረጋገጫውን የሚሰጠው፣ የሚከታተለውና የሚያግደው ወይም የሚሰርዘው ባለስልጣኑ ነው። ይህን የሚያደርገው በደንብና በመመሪያ በሚወጡ መስፈርቶች መሠረት ነው (አንቀጽ 18(2)፣ (7))።
- ከራሱ እርሻ ወይም ከአባላቱ የሰበሰበውን ቡና የሚልክ "አምራችና ላኪ" (የኅብረት ሥራ ማኅበራትን ጨምሮ) ቡና ላኪ መሆን ይችላል። እሱም የብቃት ማረጋገጫና የቡና ላኪነት ንግድ ፈቃድ ያስፈልገዋል (አንቀጽ 2(15))።
- ከላኪ የሚጠበቁ ግዴታዎች (አንቀጽ 10)፦
  - ቡናውን በሀገሪቱ የጥራት ደረጃ ማዘጋጀት፤
  - የቡናውን ስም፣ ባህሪና የመገኛ ቦታ መጠበቅ፤
  - የሽያጭ ውሉን በ24 ሰዓት ውስጥ በኢትዮጵያ ብሔራዊ ባንክ ማስመዝገብና በ3 የሥራ ቀናት ውስጥ ለባለስልጣኑ ማሳወቅ፤
  - ቡናውን ከቀጣዩ ምርት በፊት መላክ፤
  - ውሉን በጊዜው መፈጸም።
- ላኪ በጥራት ምርመራ ማዕከል ደረጃ የተሰጠውን የወጪ ቡና ያለ ባለስልጣኑ ልዩ ፈቃድ ለሀገር ውስጥ ገበያ ማቅረብ አይችልም (አንቀጽ 10(8))።

- ማሳሰቢያ፦ በ2017 ዓ.ም (2025) ባለስልጣኑ የላኪዎችን መነሻ ካፒታልና ሌሎች መስፈርቶች የሚያሻሽል መመሪያ ማውጣቱ በግል ሚዲያ ተዘግቧል። ኦፊሴላዊ ጽሑፉን ማግኘት ስላልቻልን አሃዞቹን አላካተትንም። ወቅታዊውን መስፈርት ከኢትዮጵያ ቡናና ሻይ ባለስልጣን በቀጥታ ይጠይቁ።
- የክፍያ መጠን፣ የሚፈጀው ጊዜ እና የባለስልጣኑ ስልክ በይፋ ሊገኙ አልቻሉም።

## In short (English)

- Coffee trade and quality are governed by the Coffee Marketing and Quality Control Proclamation No. 1051/2017, which the Ethiopian Coffee and Tea Authority (ECTA) enforces. We found no amending proclamation.
- To export coffee, you need a valid certificate of competence and a trade licence from the appropriate government body (Art. 10(1)).
- ECTA issues, monitors, suspends and revokes certificates of competence. It does so under criteria set in regulations and directives (Art. 18(2), (7)).
- A "coffee producer and exporter" may export coffee from its own farm or out-growers, and cooperatives may export their members' coffee. They also need a certificate of competence and a coffee export business licence (Art. 2(15)).
- An exporter's duties (Art. 10):
  - process export coffee to the national quality standard;
  - keep the coffee's name, characteristics and place of origin;
  - register the sales contract with the National Bank of Ethiopia within 24 hours and notify ECTA within 3 working days;
  - export the coffee before the next harvest;
  - perform the contract on time.
- An exporter may not sell graded export coffee on the domestic market without ECTA's special permission (Art. 10(8)).
- ECTA carries out quality inspection of export coffee and issues its certificate, release letter and country-of-origin certificate (Art. 18(3)).
- Note: private media reported that in 2025 ECTA issued an amendment directive raising exporters' minimum capital and other requirements. We could not obtain the official text, so the figures are not included here. Ask ECTA directly for the current requirements.
- Fees, processing time and an ECTA phone number were not found in official sources.

## Official text

**Coffee Marketing and Quality Control Proclamation No. 1051/2017** — Federal Negarit Gazette, 23rd Year No. 43, 5 June 2017. Copy: https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/eth183197.pdf

- Art. 2(15): "“coffee producer and exporter” means a person who has obtained certificate of competence and coffee export business license from appropriate government organ either by collecting from his own farm or out growers in accordance with law, and coffee cooperatives collected from its members and exports coffee in compliance with the export quality and standards;"
- Art. 2(16): "“coffee exporter” means a person who, upon being licensed to trade coffee by the appropriate government organ, and upon purchasing coffee from the transaction integrated lawful line or the Ethiopian Commodity Exchange, processed and exports coffee in compliance with the export quality and standards;"
- Art. 10 — Obligations of Coffee Exporters. "Any coffee exporter shall:
  1/ hold valid competence certificate and trade license from appropriate government organ;
  2/ process export coffee in accordance with the country’s quality standard, for export;
  3/ maintain the name, characteristics and place of origin of export coffee, except in cases of mixing processes, registered under special permission of the Authority;
  4/ register the contract within 24 hours, at the National Bank of Ethiopia, and notify the same to the Authority within three working days;
  5/ export before the next harvest, coffee collected from any alternative transactions or his own farm or if it is co-operative association collected from the members farm, without wastages and keeping quality;
  6/ sell coffee by product leftover from export coffee in the auction centers or in the Ethiopia Commodity Exchange;
  7/ without prejudice to the extension of a contract permitted by the National Bank of Ethiopia upon showing of sufficient reasons, perform the contract on the due date;
  8/ not submit export coffee graded by the coffee quality inspection and grading center, for domestic sale in the Ethiopian Commodity Exchange or in other alternative transaction center, unless it is specially allowed by the Authority."
- Art. 18 — Power of the Authority: "2/ issue certificate of competence to coffee transaction actors follow up and inspect same pursuant to criteria’s issued in regulation and directives; 3/ grant certificate, conduct quality inspection, issue letter of release, and issue production country certification for export coffee; … 7/ suspend or revoke certificate of competence of coffee transaction actor, who is found distorting the transaction process or who lacks competence, pursuant to regulations and directives …"
