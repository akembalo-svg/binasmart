# የግል ድርጅት ሠራተኞች ጡረታ — Private employees' pension (Proclamation 1268/2022)

**Current law:** Proclamation **1268/2022** replaced the old Proclamation 715/2011 and its amendment 908/2015 (Art. 62). Do not rely on 715/2011 any more.

## በአጭሩ

- የግል ድርጅት ሠራተኞች ጡረታ አሁን የሚተዳደረው በ**አዋጅ ቁጥር 1268/2014** ነው። ይህ አዋጅ የቀድሞውን አዋጅ 715/2003 እና ማሻሻያውን 908/2007 ሽሯል (አንቀጽ 62)።
- **የጡረታ መውጫ ዕድሜ 60 ዓመት** ነው። ዕድሜው የሚቆጠረው ሠራተኛው ለመጀመሪያ ጊዜ ሲቀጠር ባስመዘገበው የልደት ዘመን ነው (አንቀጽ 18(1))። በኋላ ዕድሜን ለመቀየር የሚቀርብ ማስረጃ ተቀባይነት የለውም (አንቀጽ 18(2))። ለአደገኛ ሥራዎች ወይም ልዩ ሙያዎች የሚኒስትሮች ምክር ቤት ዝቅ ያለ ወይም ከፍ ያለ ዕድሜ ሊወስን ይችላል።
- **መዋጮ:** ከሠራተኛው ደመወዝ ላይ ተመስርቶ **አሠሪው 11%**፣ **ሠራተኛው 7%** ይከፍላሉ (አንቀጽ 10)።
- አሠሪው የሠራተኛውን ድርሻ ከደመወዙ ቆርጦ ከራሱ ድርሻ ጋር **በየወሩ** ይከፍላል። ክፍያው ደመወዝ ከተከፈለበት ወር መጨረሻ ቀን ጀምሮ በ30 ቀን ውስጥ መፈጸም አለበት (አንቀጽ 12)። አሠሪው ሳይቆርጥ ቢቀር ራሱ ይከፍላል። ካዘገየ ወለድና በወር 5% ቅጣት ይከፍላል።
- መዋጮው የሚሰበሰበው በፌዴራል ደረጃ በገቢዎች ሚኒስቴር፣ በክልል ደግሞ በገቢዎች ቢሮ ነው (አንቀጽ 11)።
- **የጡረታ አበል:** ቢያንስ **10 ዓመት** ያገለገለ ሠራተኛ የጡረታ ዕድሜ ሲደርስ ዕድሜ ልክ አበል ያገኛል (አንቀጽ 19(1))። አበሉ ከመጨረሻዎቹ ሦስት ዓመታት አማካይ ደመወዝ **30%** ሲሆን ከ10 ዓመት በላይ ለሚቆጠር እያንዳንዱ ዓመት **1.25%** ይጨመራል። ከ70% መብለጥ አይችልም (አንቀጽ 20)።
- **ቢያንስ 25 ዓመት** አገልግሎ በራሱ ፈቃድ የለቀቀ ሠራተኛ፣ የጡረታ ዕድሜ ከመድረሱ **አምስት ዓመት ቀደም ብሎ** አበል መቀበል ይጀምራል (አንቀጽ 19(2))። በዲሲፕሊን ምክንያት የተሰናበተ ግን ይህን መብት አያገኝም (አንቀጽ 19(3))።
- ከ10 ዓመት በታች አገልግሎ የጡረታ ዕድሜ የደረሰ ሠራተኛ **ዳረጎት** ያገኛል። መጠኑ የ1.25 ወር ደመወዝ ተባዝቶ በአገልግሎት ዓመታት ቁጥር ነው (አንቀጽ 21–22)።
- **መዋጮዎን ለማረጋገጥ:** እያንዳንዱ ሠራተኛ የማኅበራዊ ዋስትና መለያ ቁጥር ይሰጠዋል። የግብር ከፋይ መለያ ቁጥር (TIN) ወይም ብሔራዊ መታወቂያ ቁጥርም እንደ መለያ ቁጥር ሊያገለግል ይችላል (አንቀጽ 5)። ይህን ቁጥርና መታወቂያዎን ይዘው **የግል ድርጅት ሠራተኞች ማኅበራዊ ዋስትና አስተዳደርን** ይጠይቁ። ኢሜይል፦ poessa.comm@poessa.gov.et። ለሕዝብ ክፍት የሆነ የኦንላይን መዋጮ ማረጋገጫ አልተገኘም።
- አዋጁ **የቤት ሠራተኞችን**፣ የዓለም አቀፍ መንግሥታዊ ድርጅቶችንና የዲፕሎማቲክ ሚሲዮኖችን ሠራተኞች አይሸፍንም (አንቀጽ 3(4))። የሃይማኖትና የፖለቲካ ድርጅቶች ሠራተኞች እንዲሁም መደበኛ ባልሆነ ዘርፍ የሚሠሩ ሰዎች ፈቃደኛ ከሆኑ ሊሸፈኑ ይችላሉ (አንቀጽ 3(3))።

## In short (English)

- Law in force: **Proclamation 1268/2022**. It repealed 715/2011 and its amendment 908/2015 (Art. 62).
- **Retirement age: 60**, counted from the date of birth registered when the employee was first hired (Art. 18(1)). Later evidence to change that age is not accepted (Art. 18(2)). The Council of Ministers may set a higher age for some professions, or a lower one for hazardous jobs (Art. 18(3)–(4)).
- **Contributions**, based on salary: **employer 11%, employee 7%** (Art. 10). The employer deducts the employee's share and pays both shares **monthly**, within 30 days after the end of the salary month (Art. 12(1)–(2)). If the employer fails to deduct, it pays the employee's share itself. Late payment carries interest plus a 5% monthly penalty, capped at the amount owed (Art. 12(3)–(4)). The Ministry of Revenue and regional revenue bureaus collect the contributions (Art. 11).
- **Pension:** you need at least 10 years of service to get a pension for life at retirement age (Art. 19(1)). The amount is 30% of your average salary over the last three years, plus 1.25% for each year of service beyond 10, up to a maximum of 70% (Art. 20).
- With at least **25 years** of service, an employee who resigns gets a pension starting **five years before** retirement age (Art. 19(2)). This does not apply to dismissal on disciplinary grounds (Art. 19(3)).
- Less than 10 years of service → a one-off **gratuity** of 1.25 months' salary × years of service (Arts. 21–22).
- **Checking your contributions:** each employee gets a social security ID number. Your TIN or national ID number can also serve as that number (Art. 5). Take the number and your ID to the **Private Organization Employees' Social Security Administration (POESSA)**, or email poessa.comm@poessa.gov.et. We found no public online contribution statement.
- The law does **not** cover domestic workers, or staff of international governmental organisations and diplomatic missions (Art. 3(4)). Employees of religious and political organisations and informal-sector workers can join if they choose (Art. 3(3)).

## Official text

Source: Proclamation No. 1268/2022, Federal Negarit Gazette 28th Year No. 16, 18 March 2022. PDF: https://justice.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/አዋጅ-ቁጥር-1268-2014.pdf

**Article 10. Contribution to the Private Organization Employees' Service Pension Fund**
> The contributions payable to the Private Organizations employees' service Pension Fund shall, based on the salary of the employee of the private organization, be: 1/ by the employer, 11%; 2/ by the employee, 7%.

**አንቀጽ ፲ (የአማርኛ ጽሑፍ)**
> ለግል ድርጅት ሠራተኞች አገልግሎት ጡረታ ፈንድ በሠራተኛው ደመወዝ ላይ ተመስርቶ የሚደረገው መዋጮ እንደሚከተለው ይሆናል፡- ፩/ በግል ድርጅቱ ፲፩ በመቶ፤ ፪/ በግል ድርጅት ሠራተኛው ፯ በመቶ፡፡

**Article 12. Payment of Pension Contributions**
> 1/ Every private organization shall deduct contributions of its employees from their salaries and pay the amount, together with its own contributions to the Pension Fund monthly.
> 2/ The contributions referred to in Sub-Article (1) of this Article shall be paid to the Pension Fund within 30 days from the last day of the month in which payment of salary shall be effected.
> 3/ Where the private organization fails to deduct contributions of its employees from their salaries, it shall be liable for payment of same.

**Article 18. Retirement Age**
> 1/ The retirement age of an employee shall be 60 years based on the date of birth registered when he was employed for the first time.

**አንቀጽ ፲፰**
> ፩/ የጡረታ መውጫ ዕድሜ የግል ድርጅት ሠራተኛው ለመጀመሪያ ጊዜ ሲቀጠር በቅድሚያ የመዘገበውን የልደት ዘመን መሠረት በማድረግ ፷ ዓመት ይሆናል፡፡

**Article 19. Retirement Pension**
> 1/ An employee of a private organization who has served for at least 10 years, if terminate his contract of employment, he shall receive retirement benefit for life up on attaining retirement age.
> 2/ An employee who has completed at least 25 years of service and separates from the service by voluntary resignation or for any other causes other than those provided for in this Proclamation shall receive retirement pension for life beginning with five years prior to retirement age.

**Article 20. Amount of Retirement Pension**
> 1/ The retirement pension due to any employee shall be 30% of his average salary of the last three years preceding retirement and shall be increased by 1.25% for each year of service beyond 10 years.
> 4/ The retirement pension ... may not exceed 70% of the average salary calculated for pension benefit entitlement of the employee.

**Article 5. Social Security Registration Identification Number**
> 1/ Any private organization or an employee shall, upon submission of complete data for registration, be assigned with social security identification number; provided, however, that the tax identification number or national identification number given by authorized government body if any, shall also be taken as social security identification number as the case may be;

**Article 62. Repealed laws**
> Proclamation No 715/2011 and Amendment Proclamation No. 908/2015 here by repealed

**POESSA contact (website http://www.poessa.gov.et/, 2026-09-17):** የግል ድርጅት ሠራተኞች ማህበራዊ ዋስትና አስተዳደር — email poessa.comm@poessa.gov.et. The "Guidelines and Forms" page offers the employee registration form (ቅጽ 1) and the benefit claim form (ቅጽ 2).

## What is not published

- **Online contribution check:** none found for the public. Ask your employer's HR office for payment slips, or go to a POESSA office with your ID number.
- **POESSA phone number and branch addresses:** not listed on its website. Ask POESSA by email.
- **Pension Implementation Directive No. 1085/2025** is listed on justice.gov.et, but its details are not summarised here. Ask POESSA about claim documents.
- This page is not about the public servants' pension. That is Proclamation 1267/2022, handled by the Public Servants Social Security Administration.
