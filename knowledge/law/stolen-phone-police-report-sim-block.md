# ስልክ ሲሰረቅ ወይም ሲጠፋ ምን ማድረግ አለብኝ? — What to Do When Your Phone Is Stolen or Lost

## በአጭሩ (Amharic summary)

- **1. ሲም ካርዱን ወዲያውኑ ያዘጉ።** ኢትዮ ቴሌኮም እንደሚለው፣ ሞባይል ስልክዎ ወይም ሲም ካርድዎ ከጠፋ ወዲያውኑ ወደ 994 ደውለው ሲም ካርድዎን ማዘጋት አለብዎት። 994 የኢትዮ ቴሌኮም የጥሪ ማዕከል ነው (ድረ-ገጹ 251-994፣ +251-980 እና 994@ethionet.et የሚሉ አድራሻዎችንም ይዘረዝራል)። ወደ 994 የጽሑፍ መልዕክትም መላክ ይቻላል።
- ሲሙ በቴሌብር ወይም በባንክ አገልግሎት ላይ የተገናኘ ከሆነ ሲሙን በፍጥነት ማዘጋቱ ገንዘብዎን ለመጠበቅ ይረዳል።
- **2. ለፖሊስ ያመልክቱ።** በአዲስ አበባ ወደ **991** (ነፃ ስልክ) ይደውሉ ወይም በአቅራቢያዎ ወዳለ ፖሊስ ጣቢያ፣ ፖሊስ መምሪያ ወይም ፖሊስ ኮሚሽን በአካል ሄደው ያመልክቱ (ኢዜአ፣ ጥር 3/2011)።
- ለፖሊስ ሲያመለክቱ የስልኩን ዓይነትና ሞዴል፣ የIMEI ቁጥሩን (ካለዎት)፣ የተሰረቀበትን ቀን፣ ሰዓትና ቦታ ይዘው ይሂዱ። (ይህ ጠቃሚ ምክር ነው፤ በይፋ የተዘረዘረ መስፈርት አልተገኘም።)
- የአዲስ አበባ ፖሊስ ድረ-ገጽ "ኢ-ፖሊስ" ክፍል ውስጥ "የጠፉ እቃዎች" ገጽ አለ። ሲታይ ግን ምንም ማስታወቂያ አልነበረውም።
- **3. ሲሙን በተመሳሳይ ቁጥር ያስመልሱ።** ኢትዮ ቴሌኮም የተበላሸ ሲም በ85 ብር ክፍያ በተመሳሳይ ቁጥር እንደሚተካ ይገልጻል (ቀን አልተገለጸም)። ለጠፋ ሲም የሚያስፈልጉ ሰነዶችና ክፍያ በይፋ አልተገለጹም፤ መታወቂያዎን ይዘው ወደ ኢትዮ ቴሌኮም አገልግሎት ማዕከል ሄደው ይጠይቁ።
- **የሳፋሪኮም ደንበኛ ከሆኑ** የሳፋሪኮም ኢትዮጵያን የደንበኞች አገልግሎት ያነጋግሩ። ሲም የማዘጊያ አሠራሩ በዚህ ምርምር አልተገኘም።
- **ስልኩን በIMEI ማዘጋት፦** የተሰረቀ ቀፎን በIMEI የማዘጋት ይፋዊ አሠራር (ከኢትዮ ቴሌኮም፣ ከሳፋሪኮም ወይም ከኢትዮጵያ ኮሚዩኒኬሽን ባለሥልጣን) አልተገኘም። ፖሊስ ሪፖርት ካደረጉ በኋላ 994 ላይ ደውለው ይጠይቁ።
- በማኅበራዊ ሚዲያ "ስልክዎን በIMEI እናገኝልዎታለን" የሚሉ ግለሰቦችን አይመኑ፤ ይፋዊ አይደሉም።

## In short (English)

- **1. Block the SIM at once.** Ethio telecom says that if your mobile phone or SIM card is lost, you should immediately call its call centre at 994 to deactivate the SIM. The website also lists 251-994, +251-980 and 994@ethionet.et. You can also text 994.
- If the SIM is linked to telebirr or mobile banking, blocking it quickly helps protect your money.
- **2. Report to the police.** In Addis Ababa, call the free line **991** or go in person to a nearby police station, police department or the Police Commission (ENA, Tir 3, 2011 E.C.).
- Bring the phone's make and model, the IMEI number if you have it, and the date, time and place of the theft. (This is a practical tip, not an official list of requirements.)
- The Addis Ababa Police website has a "lost items" page in its e-police section, but it had no listings when checked.
- **3. Get a replacement SIM with the same number.** Ethio telecom says a damaged SIM is replaced with the same number for a fee of 85 birr (no date given). The documents and fee for replacing a lost SIM are not published. Take your ID to an Ethio telecom service centre and ask.
- **Safaricom customers:** contact Safaricom Ethiopia customer care. Its SIM-blocking procedure was not found in this research.
- **Blocking the handset by IMEI:** No official procedure was found from Ethio telecom, Safaricom or the Ethiopian Communications Authority. After you report to the police, call 994 and ask.
- Do not trust people on social media who offer to "track your phone by IMEI". They are not official.

## Official text

**Ethio telecom — FAQ** (https://www.ethiotelecom.et/faq/), verbatim:

> "What should I do if my mobile phone or SIM Card is lost? Immediately contact Ethio call center at 994 to deactivate your SIM card."

> "What should I do if my SIM is damaged? Damaged SIM's will be replaced with same mobile number after paying replacement fee of Birr 85."

> "How I can get Ethio telecom contact center online? For 24/7 live agent online support click the social media links at the bottom of this website and select 'Connect to live agent' menu by following the steps. You can also send your queries at 994 via text message."

Contact block on the same page: "251-994 or +251-980 — 994 — 994@ethionet.et"

**Ethiopian News Agency (state news)**, https://www.ena.et/web/amh/w/am_32638, dateline "አዲስ አበባ ጥር 3/2011", verbatim:

> "በየአካባቢው በሚገኙ ፖሊስ ጣቢያዎች ፣ ፖሊስ መምሪያዎች እንዲሁም ፖሊስ ኮሚሽን ድረስ በአካል በመቅረብ እንዲሁም በነፃ የስልክ መስመር 991 ወይም 01-11-11-01-11 ጥቆማ መስጠት እንደሚቻል"

**Addis Ababa Police Commission** — https://addispolice.gov.et/emergency lists "991 Addis Ababa Police Commission"; the e-police "lost items" page (https://addispolice.gov.et/epolicing/lostitems) showed "No articles to display" on 2026-09-17.

**Ethiopian Federal Police** — the crime investigation department's services include "የወንጀል ጥቆማዎችንና አቤቱታዎችን መቀበል" (receiving crime tips and complaints) and, among organised crimes, "ከባድ ስርቆት" (aggravated theft) (https://www.federalpolice.gov.et/am/federal/police/crime/investigation). Federal Police phone: +251 118 886 228.

## What is not published

- **IMEI blocking of stolen handsets:** No official procedure, form or portal was found. Ask Ethio telecom (994) or Safaricom Ethiopia.
- **Police report or letter for the phone company:** It is not published whether Ethio telecom needs a police letter to block or replace the SIM, or what the police report costs. Ask the police station and the service centre.
- **Lost-SIM replacement:** The fee and documents are not published; only the damaged-SIM fee (85 birr, undated) is. Ask at the service centre.
- **Safaricom Ethiopia:** The steps for lost or stolen phones could not be read from its website. Ask Safaricom customer care.
- **Outside Addis Ababa:** Police contact numbers for the regions were not found. Go to the nearest police station.
