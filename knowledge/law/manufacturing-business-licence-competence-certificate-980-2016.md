# ለአነስተኛ ማምረቻ ንግድ ፈቃድና የብቃት ማረጋገጫ — Business licence and certificate of competence for a small manufacturing workshop

## በአጭሩ (Amharic summary)

- ማንኛውም ሰው **የጸና የንግድ ፈቃድ ሳይኖረው** የንግድ ሥራ (ማምረትን ጨምሮ) መሥራት አይችልም። ሕጉ የንግድ ምዝገባና የንግድ ፈቃድ አዋጅ ቁጥር 980/2008 (980/2016) ሲሆን በአዋጅ 1150/2011 ተሻሽሏል።
- ፈቃድ ሰጪው **የንግድ ሚኒስቴር ወይም የክልል የንግድ ቢሮ** ነው፤ "ክልል" የሚለው **አዲስ አበባንና ድሬዳዋን** ያካትታል። በክልል ደረጃ የሚሰጡ የንግድ ዘርፎች በክልሉ/በከተማው ቢሮ ይሰጣሉ።
- ለአንዳንድ የንግድ ዘርፎች ፈቃድ ከመሰጠቱ በፊት **የብቃት ማረጋገጫ ምስክር ወረቀት** ያስፈልጋል። ይህን የሚሰጠው የሚመለከተው የዘርፉ መንግሥት መሥሪያ ቤት ነው። የትኞቹ ዘርፎች እንደሚያስፈልጋቸው በመመሪያ ይወሰናል።
- የብቃት ማረጋገጫ ያገኛችሁበት አድራሻ ወይም መስፈርት ከተቀየረ አዲስ ምስክር ወረቀት ማውጣት አለባችሁ።
- ማመልከቻ የሚቀርበው በተዘጋጀው ፎርም ሲሆን በሚኒስትሮች ምክር ቤት ደንብ የተዘረዘሩ ሰነዶች ይያያዛሉ። መስፈርቱ ከተሟላ የአገልግሎት ክፍያ ተከፍሎ ፈቃድ ይሰጣል፤ ውድቅ ከሆነ ምክንያቱ በጽሑፍ ይገለጻል።
- የማምረቻ ፈቃድ ያለው ምርቱን በማምረቻው ቦታ ወይም በተመዘገበበት አድራሻ **በጅምላ** ለመሸጥ ተጨማሪ ፈቃድ አያስፈልገውም። **በችርቻሮ መሸጥ ግን አይፈቀድም** (በደንብ የተፈቀዱ ምርቶች ካልሆኑ በስተቀር)።
- ለተመሳሳይ ሥራ ቅርንጫፍ ለመክፈት ተጨማሪ ፈቃድ አያስፈልግም።
- በሕጉ "ማምረት" ማለት በኢንዱስትሪ በመጠቀም የሚካሄድ ማቀናበር፣ መለወጥ፣ መገጣጠም ወይም ማዘጋጀትን ያካትታል።
- የትኞቹ የማምረቻ ዘርፎች የብቃት ማረጋገጫ እንደሚጠይቁ፣ ዝርዝር ሰነዶችና ክፍያዎች በዚህ ምንጭ አልተገኙም — በአዲስ አበባ የክፍለ ከተማችሁን የንግድ ጽ/ቤት ወይም የኢንዱስትሪ ቢሮን ጠይቁ።

## In short (English)

- No one may operate a business, including manufacturing, **without a valid business licence** (Proclamation 980/2016, amended by 1150/2019).
- Licensing authority: **Ministry of Trade or the regional trade bureau**; "region" includes **Addis Ababa and Dire Dawa**. Regional-category businesses are licensed by the regional/city bureau.
- For some business categories a **certificate of competence** from the relevant sector office is a prerequisite for the licence; the categories are set by directive.
- If your address or the criteria change, the competence certificate must be replaced with a new one.
- Apply on the official form with the documents listed in the Council of Ministers regulation; the licence is issued after the service fee is paid; a refusal is explained in writing.
- A licensed manufacturer may **wholesale** its own products at the factory or registered address without another licence, but **may not sell at retail** except products allowed by regulation.
- No extra licence is needed for branches doing the same business.
- "Manufacturing" includes formulation, alteration, assembling or processing carried out using industry.
- Not found: which manufacturing categories need a competence certificate, the document checklist and fees — ask the sub-city trade office or the industry bureau in Addis Ababa.

## Official text

Proclamation No. 980/2016, Federal Negarit Gazette No. 101, 5 August 2016 — https://justice.gov.et/en/law/commercial-registration-and-business-licensing-proclamation-3/ (amended by Proclamation 1150/2019: https://justice.gov.et/en/law/commercial-registration-and-business-licensing-amendment-proclamation/)

- Art. 2(13): "'industry' means being any commercial activity, includes the manufacturing of goods and inputs used to produce goods using motor-power-driven equipments or other equipments, engineering services ..."
- Art. 2(14): "'manufacturing' includes any formulation, alteration, assembling or processing or agricultural development or mine exploration and development or operation activity carried out by using industry;"
- Art. 2(30): "'certificate of competence' means a certificate issued by the concerned sectoral government offices to a certain relevant commercial activities in accordance with directives issued by the Ministry upon verifying that the required competence is fulfilled ..."
- Art. 2(44): "'relevant authority' means the Ministry of Trade or Regional organs administering commercial activities;" Art. 2(46): "'region' means any of those regions ... and includes the Addis Ababa and Dire Dawa Cities Administrations;"
- Art. 4(7): "The Ministry shall issue licenses for those business categories to which licenses are issued at federal level; and regional organs administering commercial activities shall issue licenses for business categories to which licenses are issued at regional level."
- Art. 22: "1/ No person shall engage in a business activity without having a valid business license. 2/ ... shall not be required to obtain additional business license for branches he open for the same type of business activity. 3/ A business person licensed to engage in manufacturing business shall not be required to obtain a business license to wholesale his products only at the same address of his manufacturing facility or at such other address of his registration. 4/ A business person licensed to engage in a manufacturing business shall not be permitted to sell his products on retail; provided, however, that types of products in respect of which retail sales is permitted shall be determined by a regulations ..."
- Art. 23: "Any person desiring to engage in a commercial activity shall submit to the appropriate authority an application for business license by completing application form prepared for this purpose accompanied with important documents prescribed in the regulations issued by the Council of Ministers."
- Art. 24(1)–(2): the authority "shall issue a business license to the applicant upon payment of appropriate service fee by ascertaining that the requirements set are fulfilled ..."; otherwise it "shall reject the application and notify the applicant in writing the reasons for rejecting the application."
- Art. 42: "1/ The relevant sector offices shall issue directive on criteria for issuance of certificate of competence for types of business activities that need competence certificate ...; and issue the certificate competence. ... 3/ Where there is change in address or criteria corresponding to the certificate of competence issued, the previously issued certificate of competence shall be changed by new certificate of competence ... 4/ Certificate of competence shall be obtained as prerequisite to obtain a business license for business license determined by directive to have certificate of competence. 5/ ... the Ministry shall determine, in consultation with other sector offices, the business sectors which do not require certificate of competence as prerequisite for issuance of business license."

## What is not published

- The list of manufacturing activities that need a certificate of competence, and the competence criteria (premises, machinery, staff) — ask the Ministry of Industry or the Addis Ababa industry bureau.
- Licence and competence certificate fees and the document checklist — ask the sub-city trade office.
- Which products a manufacturer may sell at retail — set by a Council of Ministers regulation not collected here.
