# የፌዴራል መንግሥት ሠራተኞች አዋጅ 1353/2017 — Federal Civil Servants Proclamation 1353/2025: Leave, Working Hours and Probation

**ማሳሰቢያ፦** የቀድሞው የፌዴራል መንግሥት ሠራተኞች አዋጅ ቁጥር 1064/2010 በአዲሱ አዋጅ ቁጥር 1353/2017 አንቀጽ 159(2) ተሽሯል። ከዚህ በታች ያሉት ሁሉ ከአዋጅ 1353/2017 ናቸው።

**Note:** the old Federal Civil Servants Proclamation No. 1064/2017 was repealed by Proclamation No. 1353/2025, Art. 159(2). Everything below is from Proclamation 1353/2025, which came into force on publication in the Federal Negarit Gazette (Art. 160; gazette dated 9 January 2025).

## በአጭሩ (Amharic summary)

- **የዓመት ዕረፍት፦** በመጀመሪያው የአገልግሎት ዓመት 20 የሥራ ቀናት። ለእያንዳንዱ ተጨማሪ ዓመት አንድ የሥራ ቀን ይጨመራል፤ ከ30 የሥራ ቀናት ግን አይበልጥም (አንቀጽ 61)።
- አዲስ ተቀጣሪ 11 ወር ሳያገለግል የዓመት ዕረፍት አያገኝም (አንቀጽ 60(2))። በፌዴራል፣ በከተማ አስተዳደር ወይም በክልል መሥሪያ ቤት የቀድሞ አገልግሎት ይታሰባል (አንቀጽ 61(3))።
- ዕረፍት ሲወስዱ የወር ደመወዝዎን በቅድሚያ መውሰድ ይችላሉ (አንቀጽ 62(2))።

- መካከለኛ ወይም ከዚያ በላይ ውጤት ያመጣ ሠራተኛ የቋሚነት ደብዳቤ ይሰጠዋል (አንቀጽ 34(1))።

## In short (English)

- **Annual leave:** 20 working days in the first year of service. One working day is added for each extra year, up to a maximum of 30 working days (Art. 61).
- A new employee gets no annual leave before 11 months of service (Art. 60(2)). Earlier service in federal, city administration or regional government offices counts (Art. 61(3)).
- You can take your monthly salary in advance when you go on annual leave (Art. 62(2)).
- If work demands it, the head of the office can postpone your leave for up to two budget years; it must then be given in the third year. Only the first year of postponed leave can be paid out in cash (Art. 63).
- Annual leave is normally not paid out in cash. When your employment ends, unused leave days are paid (Art. 60(3), 64(1)).
- **Maternity leave:** 30 consecutive days before the expected delivery date plus 90 consecutive days after delivery, 120 days in total, with pay (Art. 65(3)). Paid leave for pregnancy check-ups on a doctor's advice (Art. 65(1)).
- **Miscarriage:** at 3 to 6 months of pregnancy, 30 consecutive days of paid leave according to the Amharic text of Art. 65(9); the English text only says "leave with pay". If a pregnancy of at least 6 months ends before prenatal leave starts, 60 days of post-delivery leave (Art. 65(7)). Both need a medical certificate.
- **Paternity leave:** 10 working days with pay when your wife gives birth (Art. 65(10)).
- **Working hours:** at least 39 and at most 48 hours a week (Art. 56). Office start and end times are set by a Council of Ministers regulation (Art. 57). Overtime earns compensatory leave or overtime pay, whichever the civil servant prefers (Art. 58).
- **Probation:** 6 months. If the performance result is below average, it can be extended by 3 more months (Art. 33(3)). If the result is still below average after the extension, the employee is dismissed (Art. 33(4)).
- A probationer with an average or higher result gets a letter of permanent appointment (Art. 34(1)).

### Art. 56–58 — Working hours and overtime
> "56. Regular Working Hours — The Council of Ministers wherever necessary may decide in the future, for the present time the regular working hours of civil servants shall be not less than 39 hours and greater than 48 hours per week."

> "፶፮. መደበኛ የሥራ ሰዓት — አስፈላጊ ሆኖ በተገኘ ጊዜ የሚኒስትሮች ምክር ቤት በሚያወጣው ደንብ የመወሰን ሥልጣኑ እንደተጠበቀ ሆኖ የመንግስት ሠራተኞች መደበኛ የሥራ ሰዓት በሣምንት ሠላሳ ዘጠኝ ሰዓት ሳያንስ እና አርባ ስምንት ሰዓት ሳይበልጥ ይሆናል።"

> "57. Office Hours — The time when the office hours of civil servants begins and ends shall be determined by Regulation of the Council of Ministers."

> "58. Overtime Work 1/ Any Civil Servant who has worked overtime is entitled to compensatory leave or overtime pay based on his preference. 2/ The Commission shall issue Directive on the conditions of overtime work, amount of payment and compensatory leave."

### Art. 60–64 — Annual leave
> "60 ... 2/ Any newly appointed Civil Servant shall not be entitled to annual leave before serving for eleven months. 3/ There shall be no payment in lieu of annual leave; provided, however, that payment may be made for unused annual leave due to termination of appointment."

> "61. Duration of Annual Leave 1/ A civil servant shall be entitled to annual leave of 20 working days for his first year of service. 2/ A civil servant having a service of more than a year shall be entitled to additional leave of one working day for every additional year of service; provided, however, that the duration of annual leave shall not exceed 30 working days. 3/ Previous service rendered in any federal, city Government administration and Regional Government offices shall be considered for the application of sub-Article (2) of this Article."

> "፷፩(፩) አንድ ዓመት ያገለገለ የመንግሥት ሠራተኛ ሀያ የሥራ ቀናት የዓመት ዕረፍት ፈቃድ ያገኛል፡፡ (፪) ከአንድ ዓመት በላይ ያገለገለ ሠራተኛ ለእያንዳንዱ ተጨማሪ ዓመት አንድ የሥራ ቀን እየታከለበት የዓመት ዕረፍት ፈቃድ ያገኛል፣ ሆኖም የሚሰጠው የአንድ ዓመት ዕረፍት ፈቃድ ከሠላሳ የሥራ ቀኖች መብለጥ የለበትም፡፡"

> "62 ... 2/ A Civil Servant shall be entitled to advance payment of his monthly salary at the time of taking his annual leave."

> "63. Postponement of Annual Leave 1/ ... the head of a government institution may authorize the postponement of annual leave for two budget years, where the government office, due to compelling reasons, is unable to grant a Civil Servant his annual leave within the same budget year; provided however, that the accumulated leave shall be granted to the civil servant in the third budget year. 2/ ... a Civil Servant whose annual leave is postponed for two years ... may claim payment, and the Government institution shall make the payment for the first year of the accumulated annual leave from a budget allocated for such purpose. 3/ Where payment is made to a civil servant in lieu of his accumulated leave; his daily salary shall be calculated by dividing his monthly salary by 30 days."

> "64. Unused Annual Leave 1/ Where the appointment of a civil servant is terminated; payment shall be made to the Civil Servant for the number of working days of unused annual leaves."

### Art. 65 — Maternity, miscarriage and paternity leave
> "65 ... 1/ A pregnant civil servant shall be entitled to: - a) paid leave for medical examination in accordance with a doctor's recommendation; b) Paid leave before delivery if recommended by a doctor. 2/ The leave referred to in Sub-Article (1) of this Article shall not be considered as sick leave. 3/ A pregnant Civil Servant shall be entitled to period of 30 consecutive days of prenatal leave preceding the presumed date of her confinement and a period of 90 consecutive days after her confinement, in total 120 days of maternity leave with pay. 4/ If the pregnant civil servant delivers before the completion of the prenatal leave which is granted under Sub-Article (3) of this Article, the unused prenatal leave shall be granted after her confinement."

> "7/ Any Civil Servant who encounters a miscarriage of not less than six month's pregnancy prior to her prenatal leave shall be entitled to 60 days post confinement maternity leave if the miscarriage is confirmed by medical certificate. 8/ If a Civil Servant on prenatal leave encounters a miscarriage of pregnancy, her prenatal leave shall terminate and she shall be entitled to the 90 days post confinement maternity leave referred to in Sub-Article (3) of this Article. 9/ Any Civil Servant who encounters a miscarriage of three to six month's pregnancy shall be granted leave with pay if the miscarriage is confirmed by medical certificate. 10/ Any Civil Servant shall be entitled a paternity leave with pay for 10 working days at the time of his wife's delivery."

> "፷፭(፱) ከሦስት እስከ ስድስት ወር ባለው የእርግዝና ጊዜ ውስጥ የፅንስ መቋረጥ ያጋጠማት የመንግሥት ሠራተኛ ደመወዝ የሚከፈልበት ሠላሳ ተከታታይ ቀን ፈቃድ ይሰጣታል፡፡"

Note: for a miscarriage at 3–6 months, the Amharic text gives 30 consecutive days; the English text states no number. The Amharic 65(9) also does not mention a medical certificate.
