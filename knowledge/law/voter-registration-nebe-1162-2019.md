# የመራጮች ምዝገባ — How Voter Registration Works

**ማሳሰቢያ (2025 ማሻሻያ):** በአዋጅ 1394/2025 መሠረት የዕድሜ መስፈርቱ ተቀይሯል፤ አሁን **በምርጫው ዕለት** 18 ዓመት የሚሞላው ሰው መመዝገብ ይችላል (ቀድሞ "በምዝገባው ዕለት" ነበር)። የNEBE መመሪያ 06/2021 አሁንም የቀድሞውን አነጋገር ይዟል።

**Note (2025 amendment):** Proclamation 1394/2025 changed the age rule — you must be 18 **on voting day** (previously "on the day of registration"). NEBE Directive 06/2021 still uses the old wording and is outdated on this point.

## በአጭሩ (Amharic summary)

- መመዝገብ የሚችለው፦ ኢትዮጵያዊ ዜጋ፣ በምርጫው ዕለት 18 ዓመትና ከዚያ በላይ የሆነ፣ በምርጫ ክልሉ ቢያንስ 6 ወር የኖረ።
- መመዝገብ የማይችሉት፦ በአእምሮ ሕመም ምክንያት መወሰን እንደማይችሉ የተረጋገጠላቸው እና የመምረጥ መብታቸው በፍርድ ቤት የተገደበ ሰዎች።
- የምዝገባ ቀናትን የሚወስነው የኢትዮጵያ ብሔራዊ ምርጫ ቦርድ ነው፤ መጀመሪያውን ከ15 ቀን በፊት ያሳውቃል።
- ምዝገባ የሚካሄደው በምርጫ ጣቢያ ብቻ ነው፤ ከቤት ወደ ቤት መመዝገብ የተከለከለ ነው (በአርብቶ አደር አካባቢዎች በቀር)። ከ2025 ጀምሮ ቦርዱ በቴክኖሎጂ አማራጮች ምዝገባ ማካሄድ ይችላል።
- ምዝገባ በመንግሥት የሥራ ሰዓት፣ በቦርዱ ሰሌዳ መሠረት ቅዳሜና እሁድንም ጨምሮ ይካሄዳል።
- ሰነድ፦ የቀበሌ መታወቂያ ወይም ፓስፖርት (ጊዜው ያለፈበትም ቢሆን ያገለግላል)።
- መታወቂያ ከሌለ፦ ፎቶ ያለበት መንጃ ፈቃድ፣ የመኖሪያ መታወቂያ፣ የውትድርና ስንብት ሰነድ፣ የተማሪ ወይም የሠራተኛ መታወቂያ።
- ምንም ሰነድ ከሌለ፦ መዝጋቢዎቹ ካወቁዎት፣ ወይም በቀበሌው ለረጅም ጊዜ መኖራቸው የተረጋገጠ ሦስት ሰዎች ከመሰከሩ መመዝገብ ይቻላል።
- ከተመዘገቡ በኋላ የመራጭነት ካርድ ይሰጥዎታል።
- ለ7ኛው ጠቅላላ ምርጫ ምዝገባ በ7 March 2026 ተጀምሮ ለአንድ ወር መካሄዱን ኢዜአ ዘግቧል፤ በዲጂታል ("ምርጫዬ" መተግበሪያ) እና በእጅ ተካሂዷል።
- ለወታደሮች፣ ለመንግሥት ሠራተኞች፣ ለከፍተኛ ትምህርት ተማሪዎች፣ ለተፈናቃዮችና ለታራሚዎች ልዩ ምርጫ ጣቢያ ሊቋቋም ይችላል።

## In short (English)

- Who can register: an Ethiopian citizen, 18 or older **on voting day**, who has lived in the constituency for at least 6 months.
- Not eligible: people proven unable to make decisions due to mental disorder, and people whose voting rights a court has restricted.
- NEBE (National Election Board of Ethiopia) sets the registration dates and announces the start 15 days in advance.
- Registration happens only at polling stations — no door-to-door registration, except in pastoralist areas. Since 2025 NEBE may also use technological alternatives.
- Registration runs during government working hours, including Saturdays and Sundays per NEBE's timetable.
- Documents: Kebele ID card or passport — even if expired.
- Without those: a recent-photo ID such as a driving licence, residence ID, military discharge document, student ID or employee ID.
- With no document: officers who know you can register you, or three long-time Kebele residents can testify.
- You receive a voter registration card.
- For the 7th general election, ENA reported registration started on 7 March 2026 and ran for one month, digitally (the "Mirchaye" app) and manually.
- NEBE may set special polling stations for military in camps, civil servants, higher-education students, displaced people and prisoners who keep voting rights.

## Official text

**Proclamation No. 1162/2019, Art. 18(1) (original)** — https://nebe.org.et/sites/default/files/Ethiopian-Electoral-Proclamtion-No-1162.pdf
"Any person may be registered as a voter, who: a) is an Ethiopian citizen; b) is 18 years old and above on the day of registration; and c) has been residing within the constituency for at least 6 months."
> ፩/ ማንኛውም ሰው:- ሀ) ኢትዮጵያዊ ዜጋ ከሆነ፤ ለ) በምዝገባው ዕለት ዕድሜው ፲፰ ዓመትና ከዚያ በላይ ከሆነ፤ ሐ) በምርጫ ክልሉ ቢያንስ ስድስት ወር የኖረ ከሆነ፣ በመራጭነት ሊመዘገብ ይችላል፡፡

**Proclamation No. 1394/2025, Art. 2(5) — replaces Art. 18(1)(b)** — https://nebe.org.et/sites/default/files/Proclamation%201394%20A%20proclamation%20to%20amend%20the%20Ethiopian%20Electoral%2C%20Political%20Parties%20Registrati.pdf
"(b) is 18 years old and above on the day of voting;"

Art. 18(3), not eligible: "a) A person who is proved to be incapable of making decisions due to mental disorder as confirmed by the relevant authority or sufficient evidence; b) A person whose voting rights have been restricted by the decision of a court of law based on law or relevant law."

Art. 19(1): "Nationwide voter registration for general elections shall commence and conclude on dates fixed by the Board. The Board shall, before fixing the dates for registration, consult political parties contesting in the elections." Art. 19(2): the start date is announced "15 days prior to the commencement of the registration."

Art. 20(1): "Voter registration shall be conducted only at the polling stations established to register voters. It is prohibited to conduct registration outside the polling station, by going door-to-door or moving around."
New Art. 20(2) (Proc. 1394/2025): "Without prejudice to the prohibitions provided under Sub-Article (1) of this Article, the Board may decide voter registration to be conducted through technological alternatives."

Art. 21(1): "A voter may be registered in a polling station where he resides by producing a Kebele identification card or a passport that proves his identity."
> ተመዝጋቢው በሚኖርበት የምርጫ ጣቢያ ማንነቱን የሚገልፅ የቀበሌ መታወቂያ ካርድ ወይም ፓስፖርት በመያዝ ለመመዝገብ ይችላል፡፡

Art. 21(2): "The Kebele identification card or passport, even if expired, may serve for the purpose of registration."
Art. 21(3): "evidence of identity bearing a recent photograph of the voter, such as a driving license, residence identification card, military discharge document or student identity card".
Art. 21(5): undocumented voters can be registered "based on the testimonies of three individuals, whose long residence in the Kebele ... has been proven".

**NEBE Directive No. 06/2021** — https://nebe.org.et/sites/default/files/7Directive%20no.%2006.2021%20on%20Conducting%20Voters%20Registration-Final%20for%20publishing%20WAA.pdf
Art. 9(5): "Voter registration shall be conducted on government working hours, except for public holidays ... including on Saturdays and Sundays according to the Board's timetable."
Art. 20(1)(b): "if the identification card has not been renewed for the past five years, she shall be required to provide another evidence".

**ENA, 7 March 2026** — https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_8440638 — registration started 7 March 2026 and "will run for one month", through digital and manual systems.

## What is not published

- Whether the Fayda national digital ID can be used to register is not stated in the law or directive we found; ask NEBE.
- No procedure for Ethiopians living abroad to register or vote has been published (the law only asks NEBE to study one).
- Dates for voter registration for any future election have not been announced in our sources.
