# የአደጋ ጊዜ ስልክ ቁጥሮች፦ አምቡላንስ 907፣ ፖሊስ 991፣ እሳት 939 — Emergency Numbers in Addis Ababa: Ambulance 907, Police 991, Fire 939

## በአጭሩ (Amharic summary)

- **አምቡላንስ (የአደጋ ጊዜ ሕክምና) — 907** (የኢትዮጵያ ቀይ መስቀል ማኅበር አምቡላንስ)። **እሳትና አደጋ — 939** (የአዲስ አበባ እሳትና አደጋ ሥጋት ሥራ አመራር ኮሚሽን)። **ፖሊስ — 991**።
- **991** — የአዲስ አበባ ፖሊስ ኮሚሽን የነፃ ስልክ መስመር ነው። በአዲስ አበባ ወንጀል ሲፈጸም፣ አጠራጣሪ ነገር ሲያዩ ወይም የፖሊስ እርዳታ ሲፈልጉ ይደውሉ።
- የአዲስ አበባ ፖሊስ ኮሚሽን ድረ-ገጽ "ያግኙን" ገጽ ላይ ያለው ስልክ +251 111 11 02 11 ነው። ኢዜአ (ጥር 3/2011) ደግሞ ጥቆማ በ991 ወይም በ"01-11-11-01-11" መስጠት እንደሚቻል ዘግቧል። ሁለቱ ቁጥሮች ይለያያሉ፤ የትኛው አሁን እንደሚሠራ አልተረጋገጠም። በአቅራቢያዎ ወዳለ ፖሊስ ጣቢያ፣ ፖሊስ መምሪያ ወይም ፖሊስ ኮሚሽን በአካል መሄድም ይቻላል።
- **915** — ፌደራል ፖሊስ፤ **916** — የፌደራል ፖሊስ ኮሚሽን (የአዲስ አበባ ፖሊስ ድረ-ገጽ ሰንጠረዥ)።
- የኢትዮጵያ ፌደራል ፖሊስ ዋና መሥሪያ ቤት፦ ሜክሲኮ አደባባይ፣ አዲስ አበባ፤ ስልክ +251 118 886 228።
- ሌሎች አጫጭር ቁጥሮች (ከዚሁ ሰንጠረዥ)፦ **912** — እሳት አደጋ፤ **907** — ቀይ መስቀል፤ **905** — የኤሌክትሪክ አገልግሎት፤ **906** — ውሃና ፍሳሽ፤ **994** — የኢትዮ ቴሌኮም የጥሪ ማዕከል።
- ማሳሰቢያ፦ የአዲስ አበባ ፖሊስ ሰንጠረዥ የ2017 (እ.ኤ.አ.) ነው፤ አንዳንድ ቁጥሮች ተቀይረው ሊሆን ይችላል። 991 ግን በኢዜአ ዘገባም ተረጋግጧል።
- ከአዲስ አበባ ውጭ ያሉ የክልል ፖሊስ ቁጥሮች በዚህ ምንጭ አልተገኙም።

## In short (English)

- **Ambulance (medical emergency): 907** (Ethiopian Red Cross Society ambulance; listed as "Ambulance (ERCS – Red Cross) 907" on the French Embassy in Ethiopia emergency numbers page and as "907 Red Cross" in the Addis Ababa Police table). **Fire and disaster in Addis Ababa: 939** (the city fire and disaster commission's own number; the older police table says 912). **Police: 991.**
- **991** is the Addis Ababa Police Commission's free line. Call it in Addis Ababa to report a crime or something suspicious, or to get police help.
- The Addis Ababa Police Commission contact page gives +251 111 11 02 11. An ENA report (Tir 3, 2011 E.C.) says tips can go to 991 or "01-11-11-01-11". These two numbers are different, and it was not confirmed which one works now. You can also report in person at a local police station, a police department or the Police Commission.
- **915** is listed for the Federal Police and **916** for the Federal Police Commission (Addis Ababa Police website table).
- Ethiopian Federal Police headquarters: Mexico Square, Addis Ababa; phone +251 118 886 228.
- Other short codes from the same table: **912** fire; **907** Red Cross; **905** electricity (listed as "EEPCO"; the Ethiopian Electric Utility website also gives 905 as its call centre); **906** water and sewerage; **994** Ethio telecom call centre.
- Caution: the Addis Ababa Police table dates from 2017, so some codes may have changed. 991 is also confirmed by ENA.
- Police numbers for the regions outside Addis Ababa were not found in these sources.

## What is not published

- **Current national list:** The Federal Police does not publish a current list of emergency short codes. The Addis Ababa Police table is from 2017. If a number does not work, call 991 in Addis Ababa or go to the nearest police station.
- **Regional police numbers** (Oromia, Amhara, Sidama and others): not found here. Ask the regional police commission.
- **Ambulance number:** not in these police sources. Check with the health bureau.
- **Which landline is current:** The contact page and the ENA report give different landlines. Ask the Commission which one works.
- **Opening hours of 991:** The ENA report calls it a free line, but no official source says whether it works 24 hours.
