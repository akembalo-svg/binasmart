# በቴሌኮም አገልግሎት ላይ ቅሬታ ማቅረብ — How to file a complaint against a telecom operator (Ethiopian Communications Authority)

## በአጭሩ (Amharic summary)

- ፈቃድ ባለው የቴሌኮም አገልግሎት ሰጪ (ኦፕሬተር) ላይ ቅሬታ ማቅረብ ትችላላችሁ፦ የአገልግሎት ጥራት፣ አገልግሎት በጊዜ አለመጀመር፣ የተቋረጠ አገልግሎት በጊዜ አለመመለስ፣ የክፍያ ቢል ስሕተት እና የግል መረጃ ጥሰት ከሚቀርቡ ቅሬታዎች መካከል ናቸው።
- **መጀመሪያ ቅሬታውን ለራሱ ለኦፕሬተሩ (ለአገልግሎት ሰጪው ድርጅት) በመደበኛነት ማቅረብ አለባችሁ።** ኦፕሬተሩ ውሳኔ ሳይሰጥ የኢትዮጵያ ኮሙኒኬሽን ባለሥልጣን (ECA) ቅሬታውን አይመለከተውም።
- ኦፕሬተሩ ቅሬታውን በ**10 የሥራ ቀናት** ውስጥ መፍታት አለበት።
- በኦፕሬተሩ ውሳኔ ካልረካችሁ፣ ወይም ኦፕሬተሩ በ10 የሥራ ቀናት ውስጥ ካልመለሰ፣ ውሳኔው ከተሰጠበት ቀን ጀምሮ በ**30 የሥራ ቀናት** ውስጥ ለECA ቅሬታ ማቅረብ ትችላላችሁ።
- ለECA የሚቀርበው ቅሬታ የሚከተሉትን መያዝ አለበት፦ የቅሬታ አቅራቢው ሙሉ ስም፣ አድራሻና ስልክ፤ የኦፕሬተሩ ስም፣ አድራሻና ስልክ፤ የቅሬታው ዝርዝርና ደጋፊ ሰነዶች ቅጂ፤ የደረሰባችሁ ጉዳት፤ የምትጠይቁት መፍትሔ (የገንዘብ መጠን ካለ ጨምሮ)።
- ቅሬታውን በጽሑፍ ወይም በኤሌክትሮኒክ መንገድ፣ በECA ድረ ገጽ ላይ ባሉ ፎርሞች ማቅረብ ይቻላል። ECA ቅሬታው እንደደረሰው በ5 የሥራ ቀናት ውስጥ ለሁለቱም ወገኖች ያሳውቃል።
- ማመልከቻው ያልተሟላ ከሆነ ECA ከማስቀረቱ በፊት ለማስተካከል 10 የሥራ ቀናት ይሰጣል። ጉዳዩ በፍርድ ቤት ላይ ከሆነ ወይም ስለ ሚዲያ/ይዘት ከሆነ ECA አይቀበለውም።
- ያልተጠቀማችሁበት ወይም ያልተመዘገባችሁበት አገልግሎት ሊከፈልባችሁ አይገባም። በጥያቄ ዝርዝር የቢል መግለጫ (የጥሪዎች፣ የዳታ አጠቃቀም) የማግኘት መብት አላችሁ፤ ኦፕሬተሩ የቢል መዝገብ ቢያንስ ለ12 ወራት መያዝ አለበት።
- የECA አድራሻ፦ ቦሌ ክፍለ ከተማ፣ ወረዳ 03፣ የቤት ቁ. 2483፣ ሮቤል ፕላዛ፣ አዲስ አበባ።

## In short (English)

- You can complain about quality of service, delay in connecting or restoring service, billing, and privacy violations by a licensed telecom operator.
- **First file a formal complaint with the operator itself.** ECA will not review a complaint until the operator has given a decision.
- The operator has a **10-working-day** complaint resolution period.
- If you are not satisfied, or the operator did not decide within 10 working days, file with ECA **within 30 working days** of the operator's decision.
- Your complaint to ECA must include: your full name, address and phone; the operator's name, address and phone; a statement of the complaint with copies of supporting documents; the harm suffered; the remedy sought, including any amount claimed.
- File in writing or electronically using the forms on ECA's website. ECA notifies both parties of receipt within 5 working days; incomplete claims get 10 working days to be fixed. ECA declines matters already in court and disputes about content.
- You must not be billed for services you did not use or subscribe to; you can request an itemised bill; operators keep billing records at least 12 months.
- ECA office: Bole Sub-city, Woreda 03, House No. 2483, Robel Plaza, Addis Ababa.

## Official text

Source: ECA, Consumer Affairs — https://eca.et/consumer-affairs/ (fetched 2026-09-17). The consumer directive is listed by ECA as "Telecommunications Consumer Rights and Protection Directive No. 832/2021" (https://www.eca.et/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/2022-03-24T06-44-15.927ZTelecommunications-Consumer-Rights-and-Protection-Directive-No.-832-2021-English.pdf).

**Complaint Handling**
> "Consumers shall have the right to submit Complaints about the service(s) a Licensee provides them, including, but not limited to, quality of service, delay in service provision, delay in service restoration, billing, and privacy violations."

> "A Consumer's Complaint shall not be reviewed by the Authority unless the Consumer has first filed a formal Complaint with the relevant Licensee and obtained a decision from the Licensee. However, a Consumer may file a Complaint with the Authority within thirty (30) working days from date of the decision of the Licensee, where the Consumer is not satisfied by the Licensee's decision, or the Licensee has failed to consider the Complaint within the prescribed ten (10) working day Complaint resolution period."

> "The process by which the Authority adjudicates Complaints and the remedies it may impose shall be pursuant to the Authority's Dispute Resolution Directive No. 796/2021."

> "Consumers can lodge their complaints with ECA in accordance with the following procedures: The full name, address and telephone number of the complainant. The name, address and telephone number of the Service provider/Operator against whom the complaint is made. A statement of the nature of complaint together with copies of any document in support or in relation to the complaint. The nature of dissatisfaction or the alleged harm that the complainant has suffered as a result of the alleged action. The remedy or relief sought including any amount claimed. Any other relevant information."

**Dispute Resolution**
> "The party who desires the resolution of a Dispute by the Authority ... may initiate proceedings by: Filing its claim for resolution with the Authority in written or electronic submission using forms available on the Authority's website; and, Serving the other party to the Dispute ... with a copy of the claim."

> "The Authority shall, within five (5) working days of receipt of the claim, notify both Claimant and Respondent of receipt of the claim."

> "The Authority shall decline to accept a Request for Dispute Resolution where: ... The Request for Dispute Resolution arises from a Dispute with a Licensee is not made within thirty (30) working days of a 'Notification of Resolution' provided by a Licensee ...; The Complaint ... is the subject of ongoing legal proceedings before a court of law ...; The Request ... relates to the content of a Communications Service ..."

> "The Authority shall not decline to resolve for the claim for the reason of incompleteness without giving an opportunity to the person filing the claim to rectify the irregularities within ten (10) working days."

> "The Authority's Decision may be reviewed pursuant to the provisions in Article 38 of the Proclamation."

**Billing rights (Rights and Obligations)**
> "O) A Consumer shall not be billed for a service not used or not subscribed for."
> "U) Billing records shall be kept by a Licensee for no less than twelve (12) months."
> "W) Methods on how to lodge a Complaint with a Licensee's top management if a dispute relating to a bill is not resolved after an initial attempt shall be clearly indicated to the Consumer."
