# የምግብ ምርት በኢትዮጵያ ምግብና መድኃኒት ባለስልጣን ማስመዝገብ — Registering a Food Product with EFDA

## በአጭሩ (Amharic summary)

- ምግብ የሚቆጣጠረው የኢትዮጵያ ምግብና መድኃኒት ባለስልጣን (EFDA) ነው። የሚመራበት ሕግ የምግብና መድኃኒት አስተዳደር አዋጅ ቁጥር 1112/2011 (1112/2019) ነው። ይህን አዋጅ የሚያሻሽል አዲስ አዋጅ አላገኘንም።
- በምግብ ንግድ ከመሰማራትዎ በፊት በባለስልጣኑ ወይም በክልል ጤና ተቆጣጣሪ መመዝገብ አለብዎት (አንቀጽ 6(1))። በአዋጁ "ክልል" የሚለው አዲስ አበባንና ድሬዳዋንም ይጨምራል።
- የታሸገ ምግብ ለሕዝብ ለማቅረብ ከባለስልጣኑ ወይም ከክልል ጤና ተቆጣጣሪ ፈቃድ ያስፈልጋል (አንቀጽ 6(2))። ምዝገባው በተወሰነው ጊዜ መታደስ አለበት (አንቀጽ 6(3))።
- ኢፌዲኤ ነሐሴ 2 ቀን 2011 (8 August 2019) ባወጣው ማስታወቂያ መሠረት፦ ማንኛውም የምግብ ምርት ሳይመዘገብና የገበያ ፈቃድ ሳያገኝ መሸጥ፣ ማስተዋወቅ ወይም ይዘቱን፣ ዓይነቱንና ሂደቱን መቀየር አይቻልም።
- ምዝገባው የሚካሄደው በ"የምግብ ምዝገባ መመሪያ ቁጥር 42/2011" መሠረት ነው።
- አሠራሩ በማስታወቂያው መሠረት፦ (1) አምራቹ ወይም አስመጪው መጀመሪያ ተጠሪ ሰው (Focal Person) ለባለስልጣኑ ያሳውቃል፤ (2) በመመሪያው የተጠየቁትን ሰነዶች ያያይዛል፤ (3) ምርቱን በመስመር ላይ www.eris.efda.gov.et (eRIS) ላይ ያስመዘግባል።
- ማሟያ ምግብ (food supplement) ሳይመዘገብ ወደ ሀገር ማስገባትም መሸጥም አይቻልም (አንቀጽ 13(1))።
- ምግብ ማስመጣት የሚቻለው የደህንነት ደረጃውን ሲያሟላና ባለስልጣኑ ፈቃድ ሲሰጥ ብቻ ነው (አንቀጽ 10(1))።
- የታሸገ ምግብ አምራች የምግቡን ዓይነት፣ ይዘት ወይም የአመራረት ሂደት ከቀየረ ለባለስልጣኑ ማሳወቅ አለበት (አንቀጽ 9(3))።
- የሚያስፈልጉ ሰነዶች ሙሉ ዝርዝር፣ ክፍያውና የሚፈጀው ጊዜ በይፋ ሊገኝ አልቻለም። ኢፌዲኤን ይጠይቁ፦ ስልክ +251 11 552 4122፤ አድራሻ፦ አፍሪካ ጎዳና፣ ወሎ ሰፈር አካባቢ፣ ቂርቆስ ክፍለ ከተማ፣ በአዲስ አበባ፤ የሥራ ሰዓት ከሰኞ እስከ ዓርብ 8:30–5:30 (በፈረንጅ አቆጣጠር 8:30 AM–5:30 PM)።

## In short (English)

- Food is regulated by the Ethiopian Food and Drug Authority (EFDA) under the Food and Medicine Administration Proclamation No. 1112/2019. We found no amending proclamation.
- Before you start a food business, you must register with EFDA or with the regional health regulator (Art. 6(1)). In this law, "region" also covers the Addis Ababa and Dire Dawa city administrations.
- A food business needs permission from EFDA or the regional health regulator before it sells pre-packed food to the public (Art. 6(2)). The registration must be renewed within the period the regulator sets (Art. 6(3)).
- EFDA announcement of 8 August 2019: no food product may be sold or advertised without EFDA registration and a market authorization. Its content, type or production process may not be changed without that registration either.
- Registration follows Food Registration Directive No. 42/2011 E.C.
- According to the announcement, the steps are: (1) the manufacturer or importer first names a focal person to EFDA; (2) attaches the documents the directive requires; (3) registers the product online at www.eris.efda.gov.et (eRIS).
- Food supplements may not be imported or sold without registration (Art. 13(1)).
- Food may be imported only if it meets the safety standard and EFDA has issued a permit (Art. 10(1)).
- A packaged-food manufacturer must tell EFDA if it changes the food's type, content or production process (Art. 9(3)).
- We could not verify the full document list, the fees or the processing time from an official source. Ask EFDA: Tel +251 11 552 4122. Address: Africa Avenue, near Wolosefer, Kirkos sub-city, Addis Ababa. Hours: Mon–Fri, 8:30 AM–5:30 PM.

## Official text

**Food and Medicine Administration Proclamation No. 1112/2019** (Federal Negarit Gazette, 25th Year No. 39, 28 February 2019). Source: https://www.efda.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/Food-and-Medicine-Administration-Proclamation-1112.pdf

- Art. 2(58): "“region” means any state referred to under Article 47 of the Constitution of the Federal Democratic Republic of Ethiopia and includes the Addis Ababa and Dire Dawa City Administrations;"
- Art. 2(51): "“product registration” means a recognition granted to regulated product in accordance with set requirements;"
- Art. 6 — Registration of food and food trade establishment:
  - "1/ Every person shall be registered by the executive organ or regional health regulator before commencing a food trade activity."
  - ፮(፩)፦ "ማንኛውም ሰው በምግብ ንግድ ሥራ ላይ ከመሰማራቱ በፊት በአስፈጻሚ አካሉ ወይም በክልል ጤና ተቆጣጣሪ መመዝገብ አለበት፡፡"
  - "2/ Unless authorized by the executive organ or regional health regulator, no food trade establishment may provide a pre-packed food for use by the public."
  - "3/ Registration of food and food trade establishment shall be renewed within the time frame required by the executive organ or regional health regulator."
- Art. 9(3): "Every packaged food manufacturer shall report to the executive organ if it introduces change in the type, content, and manufacturing process of the food it produces."
- Art. 10(1): "Food may be imported only when it complies with applicable safety standard, and a permit is granted by the executive organ."
- Art. 13(1): "Food supplement may not be imported or placed on the market without registration."
