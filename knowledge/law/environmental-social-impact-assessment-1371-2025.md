# የአካባቢና ማኅበራዊ ተጽዕኖ ግምገማ መቼ ያስፈልጋል? — When Is an Environmental and Social Impact Assessment Required?

**ማሳሰቢያ:** የቀድሞው የአካባቢ ተጽዕኖ ግምገማ አዋጅ ቁጥር 299/2002 በአዲሱ አዋጅ ቁጥር 1371/2025 አንቀጽ 24(1) ተሽሯል። (የፍትሕ ሚኒስቴር ድረ-ገጽ 299/2002ን አሁንም "በሥራ ላይ ያለ" ብሎ ያሳያል፤ ይህ ያልተሻሻለ መረጃ ነው።)

**Note:** Proclamation No. 299/2002 (EIA) has been replaced — Art. 24(1) of Proclamation No. 1371/2025 repeals it. (The Ministry of Justice website still marks 299/2002 as "In Force"; that listing is out of date. Some unofficial sources call the new law "1317/2025"; the Gazette text says 1371/2025.)

## በአጭሩ (Amharic summary)

## In short (English)

- The law is the Environmental and Social Impact Assessment Proclamation No. 1371/2025, published and in force on 11 April 2025. It replaces Proclamation 299/2002.
- Which projects need an ESIA is set by a Directive issued under the Proclamation (Art. 6). That directive was not found on official sites as of 17 September 2026.
- The only official category list found is the EPA's 2020 ESIA guideline (written under the old law, not binding): e.g. dams, mining, quarrying, fuel filling stations, hospitals and major urban projects (multi-storey buildings, terminals, markets) need a full EIA; any project in an environmentally sensitive area is treated as full-EIA.
- Garages, carpentry, brick making, rural roads and similar are in the "preliminary study" schedule.
- Who decides: the federal EPA if the project is licensed, executed or supervised federally or likely has trans-regional impact; otherwise the Regional Environmental Agency (Addis Ababa and Dire Dawa count as regions).
- You may not start a project that needs an ESIA without authorization. Licensing agencies must check for environmental and social clearance before issuing or renewing investment, trade or operating licences.
- The proponent pays for the assessment and must hold public consultations.
- The authority evaluates the report within 45 working days (extendable) and approves, approves with conditions, or refuses. The report is put out for public comment for 15 working days.
- Proponents review the project yearly and update the management plan every three years; the authority audits at least once every two years.
- Grievances go to the head of the Authority or Regional Agency; a decision is due within 30 days.
- Fines: operating without authorization 500,000–10,000,000 Birr; false information 100,000–500,000 Birr; failing to keep records or meet conditions 100,000–300,000 Birr; negligent manager of a company 50,000–150,000 Birr.
- Existing projects that skipped assessment must prepare a management plan within one year of the Proclamation coming into force.

## Official text

**Proclamation No. 1371/2025 (English)** — https://www.epa.gov.et/images/PDF/Proc%20No.1371-2025-Environmental%20and%20Social%20Impact%20Assessment.pdf

Art. 4(1): "without authorization from the Authority or from the relevant Regional Environmental Agency, no person shall commence implementation of any project that requires environmental and social impact assessment as determined in a Directive issued pursuant to this Proclamation."

Art. 4(2): "when the Authority or the relevant Regional Environmental Agency assumes that the possible impacts of the project are insignificant, it may decide not to require the concerned proponent to conduct an environmental and social impact assessment."

Art. 4(3): "any licensing agency shall, prior to issuing and renewing of an investment permit or a trade or an operating license for any project, ensure having the environmental and social clearance from the authority or the relevant regional environmental agency."

Art. 6(1): "any project which falls in any category listed in any Directive issued pursuant to this Proclamation shall be subject to environmental and social impact assessment."

Art. 8(3): "the cost of undertaking an environmental and social impact assessment and preparing an environmental and social impact study report shall be borne by the proponent."

Art. 10(2): "the Authority or the competent Regional Authority by taking into account any public comments and expert opinions shall evaluate an environmental and social impact study report within 45 working days and: a) approve the project without conditions ... b) approve the project and issues authorization with conditions ... c) refuse implementation of the project ..."

Art. 15(1): "the Authority shall be responsible ... when the project is subject to licensing, execution or supervision by a Federal government or when it is likely to produce trans-regional impact."
Art. 15(2): "the Regional Environmental Agency in each region shall be responsible ... if the project is not subject to licensing, execution and supervision by a federal agency and if it is unlikely to produce trans-regional impact."

Art. 16(4): "... shall disseminate the environmental and social impact assessment report through public notice and accept comments for 15 working days."

Art. 17(1): "... shall audit the implementation of the project at least once in two years."

Art. 20(1): "any proponent or public instrument initiator who implements a project or public instruments without obtaining the required environmental and social assessment or strategic environment and social assessment authorization ... shall be punishable with a fine not less than 500,000 Birr and not exceeding 10,000,000 Birr."
Art. 20(2): "any person who presents a false statement or information ... fine not less than 100,000 Birr and not exceeding 500,000 Birr."
Art. 20(3): "any person who fails to keep records or to full fill the condition of authorization ... fine not less than 100,000 Birr and not exceeding 300, 000 Birr."

Art. 21: an existing project that did not go through assessment "shall prepare environmental and social management plan within one year beginning from the date of the coming into force of this Proclamation".

Art. 24(1) repeals EIA Proclamation No. 299/2002. Art. 25: "This Proclamation shall come into force upon publication in the Federal Negarit Gazetteee."

**EPA ESIA Guideline With Respect To IRM, August 2020 (guideline, not a directive)** — https://www.epa.gov.et/images/licencing/National%20ESIA-IRM%20guideline.pdf

"Schedule 1: Projects which may have adverse and significant environmental impacts thus requiring a full Environmental Impact Assessment
Schedule 2: Projects whose type, scale or other relevant characteristics have potential to cause some significant environmental impacts but are not likely to warrant a full EIA study
Schedule 3: Projects which would have no impact and do not require an EIA"

Schedule 1 examples: "Construction of dams, man-made lakes, and artificial enlargement of lakes with surface areas of 200 hectares or more."; "major urban projects (multi-storey building, motor terminals, markets etc)"; "storage facilities for petroleum, petrochemical and other chemical products (i.e. filling stations)"; "construction of Municipal Solid Waste landfill facility"; "Hospitals, Health centers"; "27. All projects in environmentally sensitive areas should be treated as equivalent to Schedule 1 activities irrespective of the nature of the project."

Schedule 2 examples: "Brick making", "Garages", "Carpentry", "Tanneries", "artisanal and small scale mining", "Rural road", "Sewerage system". Schedule 3 examples: "Educational facilities (small scale)", "Medical centre (small scale)", "Telecommunication", "Small scale Research". (The guideline lists tanneries and hospitals in both Schedule 1 and 2.)

## What is not published

- The directive under Proclamation 1371/2025 that officially lists which projects need an ESIA has not been published on the EPA website. Ask the EPA or your regional environmental office which category your project falls in.
- The official Amharic text of Proclamation 1371/2025 is only available as a scanned-font PDF; we could not copy it.
- EPA service fees for ESIA review exist (Regulation No. 579/2025), but the published table does not make clear whether amounts are in birr or dollars. Confirm the fee directly with the EPA.
