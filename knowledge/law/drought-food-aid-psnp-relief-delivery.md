# የድርቅ ጊዜ የምግብ ድጋፍና የምርታማ ሴፍቲኔት (PSNP) አሰጣጥ — How Drought and Food Aid Is Delivered (Targeting, PSNP and Relief)

## በአጭሩ (Amharic summary)

- በገጠር ምግብ ዋስትና ለሌላቸው ቤተሰቦች የሚሰጠው ዋናው መደበኛ ድጋፍ የምርታማ ሴፍቲኔት ፕሮግራም (PSNP) ነው። ፕሮግራሙን በዋናነት የሚያስፈጽመው የግብርና ሚኒስቴር ነው።
- የገንዘብ ሚኒስቴር ሀብት ያሰባስባል። የሴቶችና ማኅበራዊ ጉዳይ ሚኒስቴር መሥራት ለማይችሉ ቀጥተኛ ድጋፍ ተጠቃሚዎች ድጋፍ ይሰጣል። የኢትዮጵያ አደጋ ሥጋት ሥራ አመራር ኮሚሽን ደግሞ የአደጋ ሥጋት አመራሩን ያስተባብራል።
- **ተጠቃሚዎች እንዴት ይመረጣሉ፦** በመጀመሪያ እጅግ ድሃ የሆኑ ወረዳዎች ይለያሉ። ከዚያ ቀበሌዎችና የተጠቃሚ ኮታ በበጀት መጠን ይደለደላሉ። የመጨረሻው የተጠቃሚዎች ዝርዝር በማኅበረሰቡ ተገምግሞ ይጸድቃል (ማኅበረሰብ ተኮር አመራረጥ)።
- በየክልሉና በየወረዳው ያሉ የምግብ ዋስትና ዴስኮች ከቀበሌ መዋቅሮች ጋር በመሆን ተጠቃሚዎችን ይመርጣሉ፣ የሕዝብ ሥራዎችንም ያቅዳሉ።
- **የድጋፍ ዓይነቶች (PSNP 6)፦** የመሥራት አቅም ያላቸው ቤተሰቦች (6 ሚሊዮን ሰዎች) በሕዝብ ሥራ ተሳትፈው ክፍያ ያገኛሉ። የመሥራት አቅም የሌላቸው ወይም ውስን የሆኑ (2 ሚሊዮን ሰዎች) የፍጆታ ገንዘብ ያገኛሉ። በድርቅና በሌሎች አደጋዎች ለተጎዱ ደግሞ ጊዜያዊ የገንዘብ ድጋፍ ይሰጣል።
- PSNP 6 በገጠር ወደ 8 ሚሊዮን (ወደ 2 ሚሊዮን ቤተሰብ) ተጠቃሚዎችን ይሸፍናል። ሽፋኑ ከ11 ወደ 13 ክልሎች (ቤኒሻንጉል ጉሙዝና ጋምቤላ ተጨምረው) ይሰፋል። ዓለም ባንክ ለዚህ 200 ሚሊዮን ዶላር ብድር አጽድቋል (ማርች 2026)።
- **ቅሬታ ካለዎት፦** በቀበሌ የይግባኝ ኮሚቴ ማቅረብ ይችላሉ። የPSNP ነጻ የስልክ መስመርና የጥሪ ማዕከል በሥራ ላይ እንዲውል ታቅዷል፤ ቁጥሩ ግን በይፋ አልተገኘም።
- **የአደጋ ጊዜ (ሪሊፍ) ድጋፍ፦** ኮሚሽኑ ለድንገተኛና ለተራዘመ አደጋ የሚያስፈልጉ የምግብና ምግብ ነክ ያልሆኑ ቁሳቁሶችን ያከማቻል። አደጋው ከክልሎች አቅም በላይ ሲሆን ከክምችቱ አውጥቶ በማጓጓዝ ድጋፍ ይሰጣል (አዋጅ 1386/2017 አንቀጽ 9(5)፣(24))።
- በአደጋ ተጋላጭነት ላይ የተመሠረተ አመራረጥ መንግሥት፣ ማኅበረሰቡና የሰብዓዊ ድርጅቶች በሙሉ በሚሳተፉበት ሁኔታ መካሄድ አለበት (አንቀጽ 9(29))። ክልሎችም የሰብዓዊ ድጋፍ ልየታና ስርጭት በግልጽነትና በተጠያቂነት እንዲፈጸም ማረጋገጥ አለባቸው (አንቀጽ 21(6))።
- ኮሚሽኑ የክልል የድጋፍ ድልድል ዕቅዶችንና የስርጭት መረጃዎችን (ለተፈናቃዮች፣ ለሪሊፍ፣ ለPSNP) በ edrmc.gov.et ላይ ያወጣል።
- በአሁኑ ጊዜ በአንድ ሰው የሚሰጠው የምግብ መጠን (ራሽን) እና የገንዘብ ክፍያ መጠን በይፋ አልተገኘም። የወረዳዎን የምግብ ዋስትና ዴስክ ወይም የግብርና ጽ/ቤት ይጠይቁ።

## In short (English)

- The main regular support for food-insecure rural households is the Productive Safety Net Program (PSNP). The Ministry of Agriculture (MoA) is the lead implementing agency.
- The Ministry of Finance mobilises resources. The Ministry of Women and Social Affairs supports Direct Support (people who cannot work). The Ethiopian Disaster Risk Management Commission (EDRMC) coordinates disaster risk management.
- **Targeting:** the poorest woredas are chosen first using small-area estimates. Kebeles and caseloads are then set by quotas within the budget, and the community validates the final beneficiary list. This is community-based targeting with geographic prioritisation.
- Regional and Woreda Food Security Desks handle targeting and public-works planning, working with kebele structures.
- **Types of support (PSNP 6):** 6 million people from households that can work earn wages on public works. 2 million people with no or limited labour capacity get cash to cover consumption. People hit by climate and other shocks get temporary cash.
- PSNP 6 covers about 8 million rural beneficiaries (about 2 million households) and expands from 11 to 13 regions by adding Benishangul-Gumuz and Gambella. The World Bank approved a US$200 million credit (March 2026).
- **Complaints:** use the Kebele Appeals Committee. A dedicated call centre and toll-free hotline are planned, but no number was published.
- **Emergency relief:** EDRMC stores food and non-food items for sudden-onset and prolonged disasters. When a disaster is beyond a region's capacity, it releases and transports supplies from its reserve (Proclamation 1386/2025 Art. 9(5), (24)).
- Vulnerability-based targeting must involve the government, the community and humanitarian operators (Art. 9(29)). Regions must make sure aid is identified and distributed transparently and accountably (Art. 21(6)).
- EDRMC publishes regional allocation plans and dispatch data (IDPs, relief, PSNP) on edrmc.gov.et.
- Current ration sizes and cash transfer amounts were not found. Ask your woreda food security desk or agriculture office.

## Official text

**World Bank, PSNP 6 Project Appraisal Document (Report PADHI01234, 8 February 2026)** — https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099021126194012306/pdf/BOSIB-a7e3e155-9f89-43d5-b7ff-bc4c5b0a5e12.pdf
> The MoA is the lead implementing agency, focusing on planning, operations, and multisectoral coordination. The Ministry of Finance (MoF) is responsible for resource mobilization and fiduciary management and oversight. The Ministry of Women and Social Affairs (MoWSA) supports implementation of Direct Support (vulnerable beneficiaries who cannot work) ... The Ethiopia Disaster Risk Management Commission (EDRMC) coordinates disaster risk management.

> Within each region, the PSNP will operate in rural woredas and will prioritize extreme poor areas by using small-area estimations for selection of woredas. Choice of kebeles and caseload allocation will be based on quotas within the fiscal space, and a final list of beneficiaries will be confirmed through community validation.

> (b) 6 million beneficiaries from households with labor capacity will benefit from wages for working on public works sites; and (c) 2 million beneficiaries with no or limited labor capacity will be supported with cash for consumption smoothing. Additionally, a varied number of vulnerable individuals affected by climate and other shocks will be provided with temporary cash ...

> At the local level, Regional and Woreda Food Security Desks will be responsible for implementation, including tasks like targeting, planning public works, and working closely with kebele structures.

> PSNP 6 will continue to use the Kebele Appeals Committee structures and modernize the project’s grievance mechanism by fully operationalizing the dedicated call center and toll-free hotline ...

World Bank press release, 3 March 2026 (US$200 million IDA credit for PSNP 6): https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2026/03/03/ethiopia-a-new-phase-of-support-for-safety-nets-will-foster-resilience-and-create-pathways-to-decent-jobs

**Ethiopian Disaster Risk Management Proclamation No. 1386/2025** — https://justice.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/አዋጅ-ቁጥር-1386-2017-1.pdf
> Art. 9(5) When it is beyond the capacity of regions or when there is a call for support that is approved by the Commission, the Commission may provide support by withdrawing from its reserve and transporting; and when it is deemed necessary and decided by the government, it may withdraw from the strategic reserve on a replenishment basis and use it for the required humanitarian support;
> Art. 9(24) Reserve and administer food and non-food items necessary for humanitarian assistance in the event of sudden-onset and prolonged disasters;
> Art. 9(29) Ensures that vulnerability based targeting shall be conducted with the full participation of all stakeholders namely the government, the community, and the humanitarian operators;
> Art. 21(6) Ensure the identification, distribution and provision humanitarian support activities are conducted based on the principles transparency and accountability;
