# የሲቪል ማኅበረሰብ ድርጅት (በጎ አድራጎት/NGO) ምዝገባ — Registering a charity or NGO (local and foreign) with ACSO, and fees

## በአጭሩ (Amharic summary)

- በኢትዮጵያ የሲቪል ማኅበረሰብ ድርጅት (በጎ አድራጎት፣ NGO፣ ማኅበር) የሚመዘገበው **በሲቪል ማኅበረሰብ ድርጅቶች ባለሥልጣን (ACSO)** ነው፤ ሕጉ አዋጅ ቁጥር 1113/2011 (1113/2019) ነው።
- **የሀገር በቀል ድርጅት ማመልከቻ** በመሥራቾቹ ተፈርሞ የሚከተሉትን ይይዛል፦ የመሥራቾች ስም፣ አድራሻና ዜግነት ያለበት የምሥረታ ስብሰባ ቃለ ጉባዔ፤ የመሥራቾች መታወቂያ ወይም ፓስፖርት ቅጂ፤ የድርጅቱ ስምና አርማ (ካለ)፤ ዓላማና የሚሠራበት ዘርፍ፤ የሚሠራበት ክልል፤ በመሥራቾች የጸደቀ መተዳደሪያ ደንብ፤ የድርጅቱ አድራሻ።
- **የውጭ ድርጅት** ከላይ ካሉት በተጨማሪ፦ ከመጣበት ሀገር የተሰጠና የተረጋገጠ (authenticated) የምዝገባ ምስክር ወረቀት፤ በኢትዮጵያ ለመሥራት የወሰነበት የተረጋገጠ ውሳኔ፤ ለሀገር ተወካዩ የተሰጠ የተረጋገጠ ውክልና፤ ከሀገሩ ኤምባሲ (ከሌለ ከሀገሩ ሥልጣን ያለው አካል ወይም ከኢትዮጵያ ውጭ ጉዳይ ሚኒስቴር) የድጋፍ ደብዳቤ፤ ቢያንስ **የ2 ዓመት የሥራ ዕቅድ**።
- ACSO ድረ ገጽ ላይ የማመልከቻ ፎርሞች፣ የሞዴል መተዳደሪያ ደንቦችና የቃለ ጉባዔ ናሙናዎች ይገኛሉ።
- ACSO መስፈርቱ ከተሟላ **ለሀገር በቀል በ30 ቀን፣ ለውጭ ድርጅት በ45 ቀን** ውስጥ ምስክር ወረቀት መስጠት አለበት። ካልሰጠ በ30 ቀን ውስጥ ለቦርዱ ቅሬታ ይቀርባል፤ ቦርዱ በ60 ቀን ይወስናል፤ ከዚያ በ30 ቀን ውስጥ ለፌደራል ከፍተኛ ፍርድ ቤት ይግባኝ ይቻላል።
- ማመልከቻው ካልተሟላ ACSO በጽሑፍ ያሳውቃል፤ አሟልቶ ለማቅረብ **30 ቀን** ይሰጣል።
- **የምዝገባ ክፍያ (ደንብ 514/2014፣ ከግንቦት 2014 ዓ.ም. ጀምሮ)**፦ የሀገር በቀል ድርጅት ምዝገባ **1,000 ብር**፤ የውጭ ድርጅት ምዝገባ **500 የአሜሪካ ዶላር**፤ ፈጣን ምዝገባ ለሀገር በቀል **2,000 ብር**፣ ለውጭ **1,000 ዶላር**፤ በጊዜው ዳግም ምዝገባ ያላደረገ ሀገር በቀል **3,000 ብር**፣ ውጭ **1,000 ዶላር**።
- ሌሎች ክፍያዎች፦ የአርማና ምልክት ምዝገባ 200 ብር፤ የስም/መተዳደሪያ ደንብ ለውጥ 200 ብር፤ የበጎ አድራጎት ኮሚቴ ማጽደቅ 300 ብር፤ የሕልውና ማረጋገጫ ደብዳቤ 500 ብር፤ የድጋፍ ደብዳቤ በገጽ 10 ብር።
- ለሕዝብ ወይም ለሦስተኛ ወገን ጥቅም የተቋቋመ ድርጅት **የአስተዳደር ወጪው ከጠቅላላ ገቢው 20% መብለጥ የለበትም**።
- የACSO አድራሻ፦ ቸርችል ጎዳና፣ ቴዎድሮስ አደባባይ፣ አዲስ አበባ፤ ስልክ **011 558 2378**፣ **011 558 2379**፤ ፋክስ 011 558 1825፤ ኢሜይል info@acso.gov.et፤ ፖ.ሳ.ቁ. 14340።

## In short (English)

- Civil society organizations (charities, NGOs, associations) register with the **Authority for Civil Society Organizations (ACSO)** under Proclamation 1113/2019.
- **Local organization** application, signed by the founders: minutes of the formative meeting with founders' names, addresses and citizenship; copies of founders' ID or passport; name and logo (if any); objectives and sector; region of operation; rules approved by founders; address.
- **Foreign organization**, in addition: authenticated certificate of registration from the home country; authenticated resolution to operate in Ethiopia; authenticated delegation of the country representative; recommendation letter from its embassy (or home-country authority, or Ethiopia's Ministry of Foreign Affairs); a work plan for at least **two years**.
- Forms, model by-laws and model minutes are downloadable on acso.gov.et.
- ACSO must issue the certificate within **30 days (local) / 45 days (foreign)**. If it does not, complain to the Board within 30 days; the Board decides within 60 days; appeal to the Federal High Court within 30 days.
- Incomplete applications: ACSO replies in writing and allows **30 days** to complete.
- **Fees (Regulation 514/2022, from 23 May 2022)**: local registration **Birr 1,000**; foreign registration **USD 500**; fast registration **Birr 2,000** (local) / **USD 1,000** (foreign); late re-registration **Birr 3,000** (local) / **USD 1,000** (foreign).
- Other fees: emblem/symbol registration Birr 200; name/rules amendment Birr 200; charitable committee approval Birr 300; letter confirming existence Birr 500; support letters Birr 10 per page.
- Organizations for the public/third-party benefit: **administrative costs may not exceed 20%** of total income.
- ACSO: Churchill Avenue, Tewodros Square, Addis Ababa; Tel **(011) 558 2378**, **(011) 558 2379**; Fax (011) 558 1825; info@acso.gov.et; P.O. Box 14340.

## Official text

**ACSO — How to apply** (https://acso.gov.et/en/how-to-apply): "Before registering your organization, you must make sure that all the documents and items listed below are completed along with the registration form(s)." ... "The applicant shall pay a registration fee to be determined by the relevant Regulation."

**Proclamation 1113/2019** (https://justice.gov.et/en/law/organizations-of-civil-societies-proclamation/)
- Art. 57(2): "The Agency shall, upon application and after ensuring the fulfillment of the requirements stipulated under this Proclamation, register the applicant and issue a registration certificate within 30 days from the date of application for Local Organizations and within 45 days for a foreign organization."
- Art. 57(3)–(4), (6): complaint to the Board "within 30 days from the expiry of such period"; the Board decides "within a period of 60 (sixty) days"; appeal "before the Federal High Court within 30 days from receiving the Board's decision."
- Art. 58(1): "An application for registration by Local Organization shall be signed by the founders and contain the following particulars: a) The minutes of the formative meeting indicating the names, addresses and citizenship of the founders; b) Copy of the identity card or passport of the founders; c) The name of the organization and its logo, if it has one; d) The objectives of the organization and its intended sector of operation; e) The Region where it intends to operate; f) The Rules of the organization approved by the founders; g) The Organization's address."
- Art. 58(2): "a) Duly authenticated certificate of registration showing its establishment from its country of origin; b) Duly authenticated resolution of its competent organ to operate in Ethiopia; c) Duly authenticated power of deligation of the country representative; Letter of recommendation from the embassy in which the charity is incorporated or in the absence of such by a competent authority in the country of Origin from Ministry of Foreign affairs of Federal Democratic Republic of Ethiopia and; d) A Work plan for a minimum period of two years."
- Art. 58(4): "The applicant shall pay a registration fee to be determined by the relevant Regulation."
- Art. 59(1): refusal where requirements are unmet after request, aims are unlawful or against public morals, the name/symbol resembles another's, or documents are forged. Art. 59(3): "the Agency must provide a written response permitting the applicant to fulfill such requirements and resubmit its application within 30 days."
- Art. 20(1): "Board-led Organization means formed by two or more founders, its Board being the supreme organ." Art. 20(2): "The Board shall have a minimum of five and a maximum of thirteen members."
- Art. 63(2): "The Administrative cost of an Organization established for the benefit of the general public or that of third Parties may not exceed twenty percent of its total income."

**Regulation 514/2022**, Federal Negarit Gazette No. 36, 23 May 2022 (https://justice.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/ደንብ-ቁጥር-514-2014.pdf) — "Service Fee Schedule for Various Services Provided by the Authority for Civil Society Organizations":
- "1 Registration of Local Organisations — 1000.00 [Birr]"; "2 Registration of Foreign Organisations — 500.00 [USD]"; "3 Late Reregistration Fee of Local Organisations — 3,000.00 [Birr]"; "4 Late Reregistration Fee of Foreign Organisations — 1000.00 [USD]"; "5 Support or similar letters per Page — 10.00"; "6 Registration of Emblem and Symbol — 200.00"; "7 Registration and Amendment of Name and Rules — 200.00"; "8 Opposition on Name and Symbols — 100.00"; "9 Clients Guideline — 100.00".
- Amharic schedule rows 10–14: "የበጎ አድራጎት ኮሚቴ ማፅደቅ ፫፻ [300 ብር]"; "የሰነድ ትክክለኛ ቅጂዎች ወይም ኮፒ በገፅ ፲ [10 ብር]"; "የሕልውና ማረጋጋጫ ደብዳቤ /ማስረጃ/ ፭፻ [500 ብር]"; "ፈጣን ምዝገባ ለሀገር በቀል ድርጅቶች ፪ሺ [2,000 ብር]"; "ፈጣን ምዝገባ ለውጭ ድርጅቶች ፩ሺ [1,000 ዶላር]".
- Art. 3: "This Regulation shall enter into force on the date of its publication in the Federal Negarit Gazette."
