# የስታርታፕ ስያሜ (ምዝገባ) በአዋጅ ቁጥር 1396/2017 — Startup Designation under the Ethiopian Startup Proclamation No. 1396/2025

## በአጭሩ (Amharic summary)

- የኢትዮጵያ ስታርታፕ አዋጅ ቁጥር 1396/2017 (1396/2025) በፌዴራል ነጋሪት ጋዜጣ ነሐሴ 27/2017 ዓ.ም (2 ሴፕቴምበር 2025) ታትሞ በዚያው ቀን ሥራ ላይ ውሏል። አስፈጻሚው የኢኖቬሽንና ቴክኖሎጂ ሚኒስቴር (MInT) ነው።
- **ስያሜ ግዴታ አይደለም።** ያለ ስያሜ እንደ ስታርታፕ መሥራት ይቻላል። በአዋጁ የተሰጡትን ማበረታቻዎች (ለምሳሌ የግብር ነፃነት፣ የብድር ዋስትና) ለማግኘት ግን ስያሜ ቅድመ ሁኔታ ነው (አንቀጽ 6)።
- **ማን ማመልከት ይችላል (አንቀጽ 7)፦** በአዋጁ ትርጓሜ መሠረት "ስታርታፕ" የሆነ። ያልተመዘገበ ግለሰብ ማመልከት ይችላል፤ ለዚህ የምርቱ/አገልግሎቱ ባለቤት መሆኑን (በፊርማ ቃለ መሐላም ቢሆን) ማሳየት፣ ቢያንስ 25% ካፒታሉ በመሥራቹ የተያዘ መሆኑ እና ፐብሊክ ኩባንያ አለመሆኑ ያስፈልጋል። ሕጋዊ ፈቃድ ያለውና ከተቋቋመ ከ5 ዓመት ያልበለጠ ድርጅትም ማመልከት ይችላል።
- **እንዴት ማመልከት (አንቀጽ 9)፦** በዲጂታል ስታርታፕ ፖርታል ቅጽ ሞልተው ሰነዶችን ያያይዛሉ። MInT የሚያስተዳድረው ፖርታል StartupET ነው፦ https://startupethiopia.et/ (ምዝገባ፦ https://startupethiopia.et/auth/sign-up)። ኢሜይል፦ registration.startup@mint.gov.et ወይም info@startupethiopia.et።
- የብሔራዊ ስያሜ ኮሚቴው ለቃለ መጠይቅ ወይም ለምርት ማሳያ ሊጠራዎት ወይም በአካል ሊጎበኝዎት ይችላል። ማመልከቻው በ30 የሥራ ቀናት ውስጥ ምላሽ ያገኛል። ውድቅ ከሆነ ምክንያቱ በጽሑፍ ይገለጽልዎታል።
- **የምስክር ወረቀቱ** የስታርታፑን ስም፣ የመሥራቾችን ስም፣ የዕድገት ደረጃ እና ዘርፉን ይይዛል። **ለ2 ዓመት ያገለግላል**። ዕድሳቱ ከማብቃቱ **30 የሥራ ቀናት በፊት** በፖርታሉ መቅረብ አለበት። በአጠቃላይ ከ**8 ዓመት** በላይ አይጸናም (አንቀጽ 13)።
- **ግዴታዎች (አንቀጽ 12)፦** ሕግ ማክበር፣ ሲጠየቁ መረጃ መስጠት፣ ስለተቀበሉት ማበረታቻ ወቅታዊ ሪፖርት ማቅረብ፣ የድርጅት ለውጥ ሲኖር በ10 የሥራ ቀናት ውስጥ ለሚኒስቴሩ ማሳወቅ፣ ትክክለኛ የሂሳብ መዝገብ መያዝ።
- ግዴታን አለመወጣት ወይም የንግድ ፈቃድ አለማደስ ስያሜውን ያሳግዳል። በሐሰተኛ ሰነድ የተገኘ ወይም ያልታደሰ ስያሜ ይሰረዛል (አንቀጽ 14–15)።
- **የግብር ማበረታቻ (አንቀጽ 30)፦** ስያሜው እስኪያበቃ ድረስ፣ በገንዘብ ሚኒስቴር መመሪያ መሠረት፣ ከገቢ ግብር፣ ከትርፍ ድርሻ ግብር እና ከዊዝሆልዲንግ ግብር ነፃ ይሆናል። ማመልከቻውን MInT አረጋግጦ ለገንዘብ ሚኒስቴር ያቀርባል።
- የማመልከቻ ክፍያ እና ዝርዝር የሰነድ ዝርዝር በMInT መመሪያ ይወሰናል፤ በይፋ አልተገኘም። በStartupET ገጽ ላይ የሚታዩ የ"$" ዋጋዎች ይፋዊ ክፍያ አይደሉም።

## In short (English)

- The Ethiopian Startup Proclamation No. 1396/2025 (Amharic numbering 1396/2017) was published in the Federal Negarit Gazette on 2 September 2025 and took effect that day. The Ministry of Innovation and Technology (MInT) implements it.
- **Designation is optional.** You can operate as a startup without it, but you need it to get the Proclamation's incentives, such as tax exemptions and credit guarantees (Art. 6).
- **Who can apply (Art. 7):** anyone who meets the Proclamation's definition of a startup. An unregistered individual can apply if they own the product, process or service (a signed affidavit is accepted as proof), the founder holds at least 25% of the capital, and the business is not a public company. A licensed organisation established no more than 5 years ago may also apply.
- **How to apply (Art. 9):** fill in the form on the Digital Startup Portal and attach the documents. MInT runs this portal as StartupET: https://startupethiopia.et/ (sign up at https://startupethiopia.et/auth/sign-up). Email registration.startup@mint.gov.et or info@startupethiopia.et.
- The National Designation Committee may call you for an interview or product presentation, or visit you in person. You get a response within 30 working days. If you are rejected, you are told the reasons in writing.
- **The certificate** shows the startup's name, the founders' names, its growth stage and its sector. It is **valid for 2 years**. Apply to renew **30 working days before it expires**, through the portal. No designation can last more than **8 years** in total (Art. 13).
- **Obligations (Art. 12):** follow the law, give information when asked, report regularly on incentives received, tell the Ministry about corporate changes within 10 working days, and keep proper books of account.
- Failing your obligations or not renewing your business licence can lead to suspension. A designation obtained with false documents, or not renewed, is revoked (Arts. 14–15).
- **Tax incentives (Art. 30):** until the designation expires, and following a Ministry of Finance directive, a designated startup is exempt from income tax, tax on dividends and withholding tax. MInT verifies the application and sends it to the Ministry of Finance for approval.
- The application fee and the exact document list are set by a MInT directive, which was not found. The "$" prices on some StartupET pages are not official fees.

## Official text

**Startup Proclamation No. 1396/2025 — https://justice.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/አዋጅ-ቁጥር-1396-2017-1.pdf** (English text as printed in the Gazette)

> **Art. 6(1)** "Designation for Startup or startup Ecosystem Builders shall not be mandatory to operate as a Startup or startup Ecosystem Builder." **(2)** "Notwithstanding Sub-Article (1) of this Article, being designated as a Startup or startup Ecosystem Builder is a prerequisite to be entitled for the incentives and privileges provided under this Proclamation."

> **Art. 7(2)** "An individual who is not registered under the law and meets the definition of a startup as set out in this Proclamation may be designated as a startup: a) Be the owner of the product, process, or service it provides or intends to provide to the market or be able to demonstrate in its application that it is the owner by a signed affidavit; b) Have at least Twenty Five percent of its capital held by the founder; and c) Not be a Public Company." **(3)** "… an organization that has a valid license and has been established for no more than Five years may submit an application and obtain a designation."

> **Art. 8(1)** "The designation certificate of the startup must meet the following requirement: a) Name of the Startup; b) Founder/s' name; c) Growth stage of the Startup; and d) Industry or sector of the economy that it is engaged in."

> **Art. 9(1)** "The application for Designation of a Startup shall be done through the Digital Startup Portal by filling out the form prepared for this purpose and providing the attached documents. The details will be determined by the Directive issued by the Ministry." **(2)** "… if the application for Designation is rejected, the Ministry shall notify the applicant in writing by stating the reason for rejection within Thirty working days." **(3)** "Where relevant, the Designation Committee may call the applicant for an interview or practical presentation … or may conduct an in-person observation." **(4)** "An application for a Startup Designation filed with the Designation Committee shall be responded within Thirty working days."

> **Art. 12(1)** "A Designated Startup shall: … d) Provide periodical report to the Ministry on incentives received …; e) Inform the Ministry of any change in its corporate or legal structure, composition, or business activity within Ten working days …; g) Maintain proper book of accounts …"

> **Art. 13** "1/ A Startup Designation shall be valid for Two years from the date of issue. 2/ An application for renewal of a Startup Designation shall be submitted Thirty working days prior to the end of the Two years for which the Designation was valid. 3/ The designated startup completes the renewal form and attaches other documents as stipulated in the Directives issued by the Ministry, submitting them on the Digital Startup Platform. 4/ A Startup Designation given under this Proclamation shall not in any way stay valid for more than Eight years."

> **Art. 30(2)** "… a designated startup before the expiration of the designation, in accordance with the Directive issued by the Ministry of Finance: a) From Any income tax; b) From income tax on dividend distributed to shareholders; and c) From withholding tax; It will be exempt." **(5)** "The application for eligibility to tax exemption shall be verified by the Ministry and shall be submitted for approval to the Ministry of Finance."

> **Art. 44** "This Proclamation shall enter into force on the date of its publication in the Federal Negarit Gazette."

- The Council of Ministers regulation on the credit guarantee fund and the regulatory sandbox.
