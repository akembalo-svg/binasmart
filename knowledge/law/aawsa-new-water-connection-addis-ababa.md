## In short (English)

- In Addis Ababa, new water connections are handled by the Addis Ababa Water and Sewerage Authority (AAWSA) **branch office** for your area (its water customer service unit). AAWSA has 8 branches.
- **Documents:** house ownership map (ካርታ) or title booklet (original and photocopy); renewed ID (original and photocopy); a letter of authorisation if a representative applies.
- For a woreda or rental-housing-agency house, bring a cooperation letter from the woreda or the agency. For a government or public organisation, bring an authorisation letter from the organisation.
- If there is no public main line nearby and you want to connect from an existing customer's line, bring that customer's letter of consent and a copy of their water bill.
- You must pay the service charge. Under Regulation 149/2023, the charge for a new connection, modification or relocation is **40% of the material cost**, added on top of the materials.
- **Customer deposit:** 1,720 birr for a 1/2-inch meter; 3,600 birr for 3/4-inch; 5,050 birr for 1-inch. AAWSA pays no interest on deposits (Reg. 149, Art. 9).
- **Monthly meter rent (5th tariff year, from 8 July 2026):** 1/2-inch 20 birr; 3/4-inch 27 birr; 1-inch 45 birr.
- AAWSA must tell you its decision **within 2 days** of your application. If it refuses, it must give detailed reasons. Service follows the order of registration (Art. 7).
- AAWSA's service standard for a new connection says "12 m, 14 hours".
- The regulation says the required documents are set by AAWSA directives, so check with the branch before you go. Office hours: Monday–Thursday 8:30–12:30 local time (2:30–6:30 Ethiopian time), Friday 8:30–11:30 (2:30–5:30), and afternoons 1:30–5:30 (7:30–11:30).
- **Branch phones (one each):** Megenagna 0116674035; Gurd Shola 0116675508; Arada 0111260810; Akaki 0114716362; Addis Ketema 0112736004; Nefas Silk 0114700920; Gulele 0111260742; Mekanisa 0113691338. Toll-free: **906**.

## What is not published

- **Current AAWSA directive on documents:** Regulation 149 leaves the document list to an AAWSA directive, which was not found online. The list above is from AAWSA's undated service page. Ask your branch.
- **Material cost estimate:** The 40% charge is added to the material cost, but typical material costs are not published. The branch prepares the estimate.
- **Meaning of "12 m / 14 hours":** The standard does not explain whether this means 12 metres of pipe within 14 working hours. Ask the branch.
- **Which branch covers your woreda:** The contact page lists the woredas each branch covers. If unsure, call 906.
- **Online application:** No online application for a new water connection was found.
