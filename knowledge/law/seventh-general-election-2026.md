# 7ኛው አገራዊ ጠቅላላ ምርጫ — Ethiopia's 7th General Election

## በአጭሩ (Amharic summary)

- የኢትዮጵያ ብሔራዊ ምርጫ ቦርድ 7ኛው ጠቅላላ ምርጫ **ሰኞ 1 June 2026** (እ.ኤ.አ.) እንደሚካሄድ በ9 December 2025 አስታውቋል (ኢዜአ እንደዘገበው፤ ማስታወቂያውን የሰጡት የቦርዱ ሰብሳቢ ሜላትወርቅ ኃይሉ ናቸው)።
- ምርጫው ተካሂዷል፤ ስለዚህ "ቀጣዩ ምርጫ" አይደለም። ኢዜአ በ2 June 2026 እንደዘገበው ድምጽ መስጠት 1 June ጠዋት 6:00 a.m. (በፈረንጆች ሰዓት አቆጣጠር) ጀምሮ በመላ አገሪቱ በስኬት ተጠናቋል።
- ኢዜአ ከ54 ሚሊዮን በላይ የተመዘገቡ መራጮች እንደነበሩ ዘግቧል። በ2 June ቆጠራ በሲዳማ፣ ጋምቤላ፣ አማራና ሶማሌ ክልሎች ገና በሂደት ላይ ነበር።
- ኢዜአ (7 March 2026) እንደዘገበው ከ47 ፓርቲዎች 10,934 ዕጩዎች እና 73 የግል ዕጩዎች ተወዳድረዋል።
- የመራጮች ምዝገባ በ7 March 2026 ተጀምሮ ለአንድ ወር ተካሂዷል።
- የመጨረሻ የተረጋገጠ ውጤት፣ አዲሱ ምክር ቤት የሚመሠረትበት ቀን እና የቀጣዩ (8ኛው) ምርጫ ቀን በእኛ ምንጮች ውስጥ የሉም። ለዚህ የምርጫ ቦርድን (nebe.org.et) ይመልከቱ።

## In short (English)

- NEBE announced on 9 December 2025 that the 7th General Election would be held on **Monday, 1 June 2026** (reported by ENA; announced by NEBE Chairperson Melatwork Hailu).
- The election has taken place. ENA reported on 2 June 2026 that voting, which began at 6:00 a.m. on 1 June, concluded successfully across the country.
- ENA mentioned more than 54 million registered voters; on 2 June counting was still ongoing in Sidama, Gambella, Amhara and Somali.
- ENA (7 March 2026) reported 10,934 candidates from 47 parties plus 73 independent candidates.
- Voter registration began on 7 March 2026 and ran for one month.
- Final certified results, the date the new parliament forms, and the date of the next (8th) general election are **not in our sources**. Check NEBE (nebe.org.et).

## Official text

**ENA, 9 December 2025** — https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_7883413
"The National Election Board of Ethiopia (NEBE) announced today that the 7th General Election will be conducted on June 1, 2026."

**ENA, 7 March 2026** — https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_8440638
"Scheduled for June 1, 2026, the election comes at a time when expectations for credible and transparent democratic practices are increasing."

**ENA, 2 June 2026** — https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_8974343
