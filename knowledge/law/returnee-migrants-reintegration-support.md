# ከውጭ አገር ለሚመለሱ ዜጎች የሚደረግ ድጋፍ — Support for Ethiopians Returning from Abroad

## በአጭሩ (Amharic summary)

- **የሕግ ለውጥ፦** የቀድሞው የውጭ አገር ሥራ ስምሪት አዋጅ ቁጥር 923/2008 እና ማሻሻያው (1246/2013) ተሽረዋል። የተተኩት ከነሐሴ 8 ቀን 2017 ዓ.ም (ኦገስት 14 ቀን 2025) ጀምሮ በሥራ ላይ በዋለው አዋጅ ቁጥር 1389/2017 እንደሆነ ተዘግቧል። የአዲሱ አዋጅ ሙሉ ጽሑፍ በይፋ ምንጭ አልተገኘም።
- ከውጭ የሚመለሱ ዜጎችን በሥራ ዕድል የሚደግፈው የሥራና ክህሎት ሚኒስቴር ነው። በ2018 በጀት ዓመት የመጀመሪያዎቹ 11 ወራት ከ28,000 በላይ ተመላሽ ስደተኞች የሥራ ዕድል አግኝተዋል (ፋና፣ ነሐሴ 14 ቀን 2018 ዓ.ም / ኦገስት 20 ቀን 2026)።
- የድጋፉ ዓላማ ተመላሾች ሕይወታቸውን መልሰው እንዲገነቡና በሥራ ራሳቸውን እንዲያቋቁሙ የሥራ ዕድልና ምቹ የሥራ ሁኔታ መፍጠር ነው።
- የስደተኞችና ተመላሾች አገልግሎት (ስተአ) ተልዕኮ ኢትዮጵያውያን ተመላሾችን መልሶ ማቋቋምን ያካትታል። ስልክ፦ +251111551111፤ ኢሜይል፦ info@rrs.gov.et።
- በአደጋ ሥጋት ሥራ አመራር አዋጅ 1386/2017 መሠረት የሥራና ክህሎት ሚኒስቴር ለተፈናቀሉና በመጠለያ ለሚኖሩ ዜጎች የክህሎት ስልጠና፣ የብድርና የቤት አቅርቦት ማመቻቸት እና የሥራ ዕድል የመፍጠር ኃላፊነት አለበት (አንቀጽ 20(19))።
- የሴቶችና ማኅበራዊ ጉዳይ ሚኒስቴር በአደጋ ለተጎዱ ሴቶች፣ ወጣቶች፣ ሕፃናት፣ አካል ጉዳተኞችና አረጋውያን እንክብካቤና ጥበቃ የሚሰጥ ዋና ተቋም ነው (አንቀጽ 20(13))።
- ተመላሾች የት እንደሚመዘገቡ፣ ምን ዓይነት የገንዘብ፣ የመጠለያ ወይም የሥነ-ልቦና ድጋፍ እንደሚያገኙ እና የስልክ መስመሩ በይፋ አልተገኘም። በአካባቢዎ ያለውን የሥራና ክህሎት ቢሮ ወይም ስተአን ይጠይቁ።

## In short (English)

- **Law change:** Overseas Employment Proclamation 923/2016 and its amendment 1246/2021 have been repealed. They were reportedly replaced by Proclamation 1389/2025, in force from 14 August 2025. The full text of the new law was not found from an official source.
- The Ministry of Labor and Skills (MoLS) supports returnees with jobs. More than 28,000 returning migrants got job opportunities in the first 11 months of the 2025/26 fiscal year (Fana, 20 August 2026).
- The aim is to create jobs and a good working environment so that returnees can rebuild their lives and settle through work.
- The Refugees and Returnees Service (RRS) includes "reintegrating Ethiopian returnees" in its mission. Phone +251111551111, email info@rrs.gov.et.
- Under Disaster Risk Management Proclamation 1386/2025, MoLS must provide skills training, help with loans and housing, and create jobs for displaced and camp-dwelling citizens (Art. 20(19)).
- The Ministry of Women and Social Affairs leads care and protection for women, youth, children, people with disabilities and older people affected by disaster (Art. 20(13)).
- No official source says where returnees register, what cash, shelter or psychosocial help they get, or which hotline to call. Ask your local labour and skills office or RRS.

## Official text

**Fana Media Corporation, 20 August 2026, "Ethiopia Expands Jobs, Reintegration Support for Returning Migrants"** — https://www.fanamc.com/english/ethiopia-expands-jobs-reintegration-support-for-returning-migrants/
> Ethiopia created job opportunities for more than 28,000 returning migrants during the first 11 months of the just-ended 2025/26 fiscal year

The report names the Ministry of Labor and Skills as the provider. It gives the aim as "Creating employment opportunities and building a conducive work environment that enables them to rebuild their lives and prosper".

**Refugees and Returnees Service, mission** — https://rrs.et/history-of-rrs/
> Ensuring durable solutions to refugees by recognizing, protecting and helping them benefit socially and economically along with their hosts; and reintegrating Ethiopian returnees.

**Ethiopian Disaster Risk Management Proclamation No. 1386/2025, Art. 20** — https://justice.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/አዋጅ-ቁጥር-1386-2017-1.pdf
> 19/ Ministry of Labor and Skills;- shall act as a lead institution in reducing disaster risks and vulnerability by providing livelihoods and employment opportunities for citizens living in extreme food insecurity and unemployment, providing training, facilitating loans and housing, encouraging innovation, and providing skills training to displaced and camp-dwelling citizens to be productive.
> 13/ Ministry of Women and Social Affairs:- shall act as lead sector institution for providing care and protection to women, youth, children, people with disabilities, the elderly, and other sectors of the society who have been affected by disaster.

**Repeal of 923/2016 (reported, not from the gazette):** a legal-database summary of Proclamation 1389/2025 states: "This Proclamation repeals the previous Overseas Employment Proclamation No. 923/2016 and its amendment." It gives the entry into force as 14 August 2025 (https://ethiodata.et/ethiopias-overseas-employment-proclamation-no-1389-2025/).
