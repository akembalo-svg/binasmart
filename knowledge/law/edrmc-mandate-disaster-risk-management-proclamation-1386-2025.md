# የኢትዮጵያ አደጋ ሥጋት ሥራ አመራር ኮሚሽን ሥልጣንና ተግባር (አዋጅ 1386/2017) — Mandate of the Ethiopian Disaster Risk Management Commission (Proclamation 1386/2025)

## በአጭሩ (Amharic summary)

- **ወቅታዊ ሕግ፦** ኮሚሽኑ አሁን የሚተዳደረው በኢትዮጵያ አደጋ ሥጋት ሥራ አመራር አዋጅ ቁጥር 1386/2017 ነው። አዋጁ ሐምሌ 21 ቀን 2017 ዓ.ም (ጁላይ 28 ቀን 2025) በነጋሪት ጋዜጣ ታትሞ በዚያው ቀን ሥራ ላይ ውሏል።
- ይህ አዋጅ የአስፈጻሚ አካላት አዋጅ 1263/2014 አንቀጽ 70ን፣ የስትራቴጂክ ምግብ ክምችት ኤጀንሲ ማቋቋሚያ ደንብ ቁጥር 284/2013 (እ.ኤ.አ.) እና የብሔራዊ አደጋ ሥጋት ሥራ አመራር ኮሚሽን ማቋቋሚያ ደንብ ቁጥር 363/2015 (እ.ኤ.አ.) ሽሯል (አንቀጽ 38)።
- ኮሚሽኑ ራሱን የቻለ የፌዴራል መንግሥት አስፈጻሚ አካል ሲሆን ተጠሪነቱ ለጠቅላይ ሚኒስትሩ ነው። ዋና መሥሪያ ቤቱ አዲስ አበባ ነው (አንቀጽ 5፣ 8)።
- ዋና ዋና ተግባራቱ፦ ብሔራዊ የአደጋ ሥጋት ዕቅድ ማዘጋጀት፣ አጠቃላይ የቅድመ ማስጠንቀቂያ ሥርዓት መዘርጋት እና የፌዴራል የቅድመ ማስጠንቀቂያና የአደጋ ጊዜ ማስተባበሪያ ማዕከልን መምራት ናቸው (አንቀጽ 9)።
- የምግብና ምግብ ነክ ያልሆኑ የሰብዓዊ ድጋፍ ክምችቶችን፣ የስትራቴጂክ ክምችት አገልግሎትን እና የአደጋ ሥጋት ምላሽ ፈንድን ያስተዳድራል። የሰብዓዊ ድጋፍ ማጓጓዣ ሥርዓትን እንዲሁም የእሳትና የሌሎች ድንገተኛ አደጋዎች አገልግሎቶችን ያስተባብራል (አንቀጽ 9(24)–(28))።
- በአደጋ ሥጋት ሥራ አመራር ለሚሠሩ መንግሥታዊ ያልሆኑ ድርጅቶች የሙያ ፈቃድ ይሰጣል፣ ይቆጣጠራቸዋል (አንቀጽ 9(21))።
- ለሕዝቡ ስለአደጋ ሥጋት ግንዛቤ ይፈጥራል፤ የጥንቃቄ መልእክቶችንም በየጊዜው ያሰራጫል (አንቀጽ 9(18))።
- ኮሚሽኑ በጠቅላይ ሚኒስትሩ የሚሾም ኮሚሽነር ይመራዋል። በአደረጃጀቱ ውስጥ የኢትዮጵያ አደጋ ሥጋት ሥራ አመራር ምክር ቤትም አለ። ምክር ቤቱ የፌዴራል ጣልቃ ገብነት የሚያስፈልገው አደጋ መከሰቱን ወይም ማብቃቱን ያውጃል። ድንገተኛ አደጋ ሲከሰትና ምክር ቤቱ መሰብሰብ ካልቻለ ጠቅላይ ሚኒስትሩ ሊያውጅ ይችላል (አንቀጽ 6፣ 14፣ 23)።
- ክልሎች የራሳቸውን የአደጋ ሥጋት ሕግና ስትራቴጂ ያወጣሉ፣ የቅድመ ማስጠንቀቂያ ሥርዓትና የምግብ ክምችት ያቋቁማሉ። ከአደጋ ወሰኑ በታች ያለውን አደጋ በራሳቸው አቅም ይመልሳሉ (አንቀጽ 21፣ 22)።
- በአዋጁ የኢትዮጵያ የአደጋ ሥጋት ምላሽ ፈንድ ተቋቁሟል (አንቀጽ 27)።

## In short (English)

- **Current law:** EDRMC is now governed by Ethiopian Disaster Risk Management Proclamation No. 1386/2025, published in the Negarit Gazette on 28 July 2025 and in force from that day.
- It repealed Art. 70 of Executive Organs Proclamation 1263/2021, Strategic Food Reserve Agency Regulation 284/2013 and National Disaster Risk Management Commission Establishment Regulation 363/2015 (Art. 38).
- The Commission is an autonomous federal executive body. It reports to the Prime Minister and has its head office in Addis Ababa (Art. 5, 8).
- Core duties: preparing the national disaster risk management plan, building a comprehensive early-warning system, and running the Federal Early Warning and Emergency Coordination Centre (Art. 9).
- It manages humanitarian food and non-food reserves, the Strategic Reserve Stock Service and the Disaster Risk Response Reserve Fund. It also coordinates the humanitarian aid transport system and fire and other emergency services (Art. 9(24)–(28)).
- It licenses and supervises NGOs working in disaster risk management (Art. 9(21)).
- It raises public awareness and regularly sends out precautionary messages (Art. 9(18)).
- A Commissioner appointed by the Prime Minister leads the Commission. The Commission's structure also includes the Ethiopian Disaster Risk Management Council, which declares when a disaster needing federal intervention has occurred or ended. The Prime Minister may declare a rapid-onset emergency if the Council cannot meet (Art. 6, 14, 23).
- Regions make their own DRM laws and strategies, set up early-warning systems and food reserves, and handle disasters below the threshold themselves (Art. 21, 22).
- The proclamation creates the Ethiopian Disaster Risk Response Fund (Art. 27).

## Official text

**Ethiopian Disaster Risk Management Proclamation No. 1386/2025** — https://justice.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/አዋጅ-ቁጥር-1386-2017-1.pdf (listed on justice.gov.et as "ETHIOPIAN DISASTER RISK MANAGEMENT PROCLAMATION No. 1386/2025")

> Art. 5 1/ The Commission is hereby established as an autonomous Federal Government Executive body. 2/ The Commission shall be accountable to the Prime Minister.

> Art. 9 The Commission shall have the following powers and duties:
> 2/ Prepare and present to the Government annual national disaster risk management plan by collecting and coordinating from regions and lead sector institutions, and implement when approved;
> 5/ When it is beyond the capacity of regions or when there is a call for support that is approved by the Commission, the Commission may provide support by withdrawing from its reserve and transporting; ...
> 9/ On the basis of the disaster risk profile, develop and implement comprehensive early warning system and ensure it is accessible, responsive and disaster-ready;
> 12/ Lead and Administer the tasks of the Federal Early Warning and Emergency Coordination Centre through the support of modern technology, and provide assistance as required for establishing similar centers at regional levels;
> 18/ In collaboration with the Federal and Regional sector institutions and the media, creat awareness about disaster risks and occurrences; disseminate information and precautionary messages to the public in a regular basis;
> 21/ Coordinate, supervise, issue professional license to non-governmental organizations working in areas of disaster risk management, and ensure their capacity;
> 24/ Reserve and administer food and non-food items necessary for humanitarian assistance in the event of sudden-onset and prolonged disasters;
> 25/ Administer and coordinate the Disaster Risk Response Reserve Fund;
> 26/ Administer and coordinate the Strategic Reserve Stock Service;
> 27/ Establish administer and coordinate the humanitarian aid transport system;
> 28/ Administer and coordinate the fire and other emergency disasters services;
> 31/ Following the announcement of the declaration, the Commission shall engage in allocating, mobilizing and coordinating resources;

> Art. 6 The Commission shall have: 1/ The Council; 2/ A Commissioner appointed by the Prime Minister, and Deputy Commissioners as may be necessary; and 3/ The necessary staff.

> Art. 14(2) Declare the occurrence or removal of disasters that necessitate Federal Government intervention based on their scale of impact and complexity;

> Art. 23 1/ Declaration of emergency disaster shall be made by the Council upon submission by the Commission and when the disaster is beyond the capacity of the region and cannot be addressed by the regular preparedness level. 2/ Notwithstanding the above, the Prime Minister may declare the state of emergency disaster where there is rapid onset disaster and the Council is not in a position to convene.

> Art. 38 Repealed and Inapplicable Laws: 1/ Article 70 of the Definition of Powers and Duties of the Executive Organs Proclamation No. 1263/2021 is hereby repealed by this Proclamation. 2/ Strategic Food Reserve Agency Establishment Council of Ministers Regulation No. 284/2013 is hereby repealed by this Proclamation. 3/ National Disaster Risk Management Commission Establishment Council of Ministers Regulation No. 363/2015 is hereby repealed by this Proclamation.

> Art. 40 This Proclamation shall enter into force as of the day of its publication on the Federal Negarit Gazette.
