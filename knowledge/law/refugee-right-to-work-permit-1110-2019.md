# የስደተኞች የመሥራት መብትና የሥራ ፈቃድ አወጣጥ — Refugees' Right to Work and How to Get a Work Permit

## በአጭሩ (Amharic summary)

- እውቅና ያገኙ ስደተኞችና ጥገኝነት ጠያቂዎች ለውጭ አገር ዜጎች ከሚሰጠው በጣም ተመራጭ አያያዝ ጋር እኩል በሆነ ሁኔታ በደመወዝ ሥራ የመቀጠር መብት አላቸው (አዋጅ 1110/2011 አንቀጽ 26(1))።
- በግብርና፣ በኢንዱስትሪ፣ በጥቃቅንና አነስተኛ ድርጅት፣ በእደ-ጥበብና በንግድ በግልም ሆነ በቡድን መሰማራት ይችላሉ (አንቀጽ 26(2))።
- መንግሥትና ዓለም አቀፉ ማኅበረሰብ ለስደተኞችና ለኢትዮጵያውያን በጋራ በቀረጹት ፕሮጀክት የሚሠሩ ስደተኞች ከኢትዮጵያውያን ጋር እኩል ይስተናገዳሉ። ለእነዚህ ፕሮጀክቶች ለተመረጡ ስደተኞች በየ5 ዓመቱ የሚታደስ የመኖሪያ ፈቃድ ሊሰጥ ይችላል (አንቀጽ 26(4)፣(6))።
- ከኢትዮጵያዊ ጋር የተጋቡ ወይም ኢትዮጵያዊ ዜግነት ያለው ልጅ ያላቸው ስደተኞች የውጭ ዜጎችን ቅጥር የሚገድቡ ሕጎች አይመለከቷቸውም (አንቀጽ 26(9))።
- ስደተኞች በብሔራዊ መከላከያ፣ በደህንነት፣ በውጭ ጉዳይና በመሳሰሉ የፖለቲካ ተቋማት መቀጠር አይችሉም (አንቀጽ 26(10))።
- የሥራ ፈቃድ የሚሰጠው የሥራና ክህሎት ሚኒስቴር ወይም እሱ የሚወክለው አካል ነው (የ2016 ዓ.ም (2024) ረቂቅ መመሪያ አንቀጽ 6)። ከላይ የተጠቀሱት ከኢትዮጵያዊ ጋር የተጋቡ ወይም ኢትዮጵያዊ ልጅ ያላቸው እንዲሁም በጋራ ፕሮጀክት የተመረጡ ስደተኞች የሥራ ፈቃድ አያስፈልጋቸውም።
- ስደተኛው ራሱ ሲያመለክት በረቂቁ መሠረት የሚያስፈልጉት፦ የተሞላ የግል ታሪክ ቅጽ፣ ስደተኞችና ተመላሾች አገልግሎት (ስተአ) የሰጠው መታወቂያ ሰነድ እና ከ6 ወር ያልበለጠ 4 ፓስፖርት መጠን ፎቶ ናቸው። ለአዲስ የሥራ ፈቃድ ክፍያ አይጠየቅም (ረቂቅ አንቀጽ 9)።
- ቀጣሪው ሲያመለክት በረቂቁ መሠረት የሚያስፈልጉት፦ የማመልከቻ ቅጽ፣ የሠራተኛው የግል ታሪክ ቅጽ፣ 4 ፎቶ፣ ሙያው የሚጠይቅ ከሆነ የብቃት ማረጋገጫ እና የቅጥር ደብዳቤ ወይም ውል ናቸው። ነጋዴ ከሆነ የንግድ ፈቃድ ወይም የኢንቨስትመንት ፈቃድ፣ ግብረ ሰናይ ድርጅት ከሆነ ደግሞ የምዝገባ ሰርተፊኬት ይጨምራል (ረቂቅ አንቀጽ 8)።
- ስደተኞች በስተአ በተሰጣቸው መታወቂያ የባንክ ሂሳብ መክፈት ይችላሉ (አዋጅ 1110 አንቀጽ 33)።
- **ማሳሰቢያ፦** የ2016 መመሪያው ረቂቅ ነው፤ መጽደቁ አልተረጋገጠም። በሥራ ላይ ያለውን አሠራር ከስተአ (ስልክ +251111551111) ወይም ከሥራና ክህሎት ሚኒስቴር ያረጋግጡ።
- ዓለም ባንክ (ኦክቶበር 2025) እንደዘገበው ከ16,400 በላይ የመኖሪያ ፈቃድ፣ 6,600 የሥራ ፈቃድ እና 1,200 የንግድ ፈቃድ ለስደተኞች ተሰጥቷል።

## In short (English)

- Recognized refugees and asylum-seekers may take wage-earning jobs on the same terms as the most favourable treatment given to foreign nationals (Proclamation 1110/2019 Art. 26(1)).
- They may work, alone or in groups, in agriculture, industry, small and micro enterprise, handicrafts and commerce (Art. 26(2)).
- Refugees in joint government and international projects that benefit both refugees and Ethiopians are treated the same as Ethiopians. Refugees selected for these projects may get a residence permit that is renewed every five years (Art. 26(4), (6)).
- Refugees married to an Ethiopian, or with at least one child who holds Ethiopian nationality, are exempt from the labour-market restrictions that apply to foreigners (Art. 26(9)).
- Refugees cannot be employed in National Defense, Security, Foreign Affairs or similar political bodies (Art. 26(10)).
- Work permits are issued by the Ministry of Labor and Skills (MoLS) or a body it authorizes (2024 draft directive, Art. 6). Refugees married to Ethiopians or with an Ethiopian child, and refugees selected for joint projects, do not need a work permit.
- A refugee applying in person needs, under the draft: a completed personal history form, the identification document issued by RRS, and 4 passport photos not older than six months. No fee is charged for a new work permit (draft Art. 9).
- An employer applying needs, under the draft: the application form, the employee's personal history form, 4 photos, a competency certificate if the job requires one, and an offer letter or employment contract. A trader adds a business licence or investment permit, and an NGO adds its registration certificate (draft Art. 8).
- Refugees can open a bank account using their RRS-issued ID (Proclamation 1110 Art. 33).
- **Caution:** the 2024 directive is a draft and its adoption is not confirmed. Confirm current practice with RRS (+251111551111) or MoLS.
- The World Bank (Oct 2025) reported more than 16,400 residence permits, 6,600 work permits and 1,200 business licences issued to refugees.

## Official text

**Refugees Proclamation No. 1110/2019, Art. 26 "The Right to Work"** (Federal Negarit Gazette No. 38, 27 Feb 2019) — https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/eth216792.pdf ; https://justice.gov.et/en/law/refugees-proclamation-proclamation/
> 1/ Recognized refugees and asylum-seekers shall have the right to engage in wage earning employment in the same circumstance as the most favorable treatment accorded to foreign nationals pursuant to relevant laws.
> 2/ Every recognized refugee and asylum-seeker shall have the right to engage, individually or in group, in agriculture, industry, small and micro enterprise, handicrafts and commerce, in the same circumstance as the most favorable treatment accorded to foreign nationals pursuant to relevant laws.
> 3/ Every recognized refugee who has academic credentials authenticated by the competent government authority, and who desires to practice his profession, may be accorded the most favorable treatment as accorded to foreign nationals in areas permitted to foreign nationals.
> 6/ The Agency may issue a residence permit, subject to renewal every five year, to any recognized refugee selected to engage in the projects indicated under sub-Articles (4) and (5) of this Article.
> 9/ Restrictive measures imposed, by applicable laws, on employment of foreign nationals for the protection of the national labor market shall not be applicable to recognized refugee or asylum seeker who is married to Ethiopian national or has one or more child in possession of Ethiopian nationality.
> 10/ Notwithstanding to the provisions of this Article, any recognized refugee or asylum-seeker shall have no right to be employed in the National Defense, Security, Foreign Affairs and other similar political establishments.

**Art. 33:** "Every recognized refugee or asylum-seeker has the right to open a personal bank account, deposit, transfer or withdraw money and obtain other banking services in accordance with relevant Ethiopian financial laws using identification document issued by the Agency."

(The "Agency" in 1110/2019 is now called the Refugees and Returnees Service, RRS, according to rrs.et.)

**RRS draft Right-to-Work Directive, "Directive No. ____/2024" (DRAFT)** — https://rrs.et/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/27.06.2024_R2W-DD_English-Version_Final-Draft-update.pdf
> Art. 6(2) Any recognized refugee or asylum seeker may engage in employment in sectors not restricted by the Proclamation upon obtaining a work permit from the Ministry of Labor and Skills or any organ authorized by it.
> Art. 9(1) An application for a new work permit by the recognized refugee or asylum seeker shall fulfill the following requirements: a) A duly completed personal history form b) An Identification Document issued by the Services c) 4 passport size, not older than six months, photographs
> Art. 9(3) No fee shall be imposed on a recognized refugee or asylum seeker to obtain a new work permit

**Earlier Directive No. 02/2019 (as described by ReDSS, June 2025, not an official text)** — https://www.regionaldss.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/ReDSS-Ethiopia-Policy-Brief-Refugee-Right-to-Work.pdf: it sets out "three avenues ... i) joint projects; ii) wage earning employment; and iii) self-employment". Work permits are issued "if the work for which the work permit is sought cannot/may not be covered by Ethiopians", and the refugee agency writes a support letter.

## What is not published

- Which MoLS office handles refugee work permits, how long processing takes, and renewal fees: not found. Ask MoLS or RRS.
- Whether a support letter from RRS is still required: the requirement is described only in the ReDSS brief on the 2019 directive. Ask RRS.
