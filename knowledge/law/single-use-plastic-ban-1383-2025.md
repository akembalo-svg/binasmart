# የፕላስቲክ ከረጢትና የአንድ ጊዜ ፕላስቲክ እገዳ — Ban on Plastic Bags and Single-Use Plastic

**ማሳሰቢያ:** የቀድሞው የደረቅ ቆሻሻ አስተዳደር አዋጅ ቁጥር 513/2007 በአዲሱ አዋጅ ቁጥር 1383/2025 አንቀጽ 27(1) ተሽሯል። የቀድሞው ሕግ አንዳንድ ስስ ከረጢቶችን ይፈቅድ ነበር፤ ይህ አሁን አይሠራም።

**Note:** Solid Waste Management Proclamation No. 513/2007 is replaced — Art. 27(1) of Proclamation No. 1383/2025 repeals it. The old rule that allowed some thin or biodegradable bags no longer applies.

## In short (English)

- Yes. Proclamation No. 1383/2025 (in force on publication, 31 July 2025) bans single-use plastic.
- Producing, placing on the market, importing, storing, selling or using any single-use plastic is prohibited (Art. 13(1)).
- Stock made or imported before the law got a six-month grace period only (Art. 13(2)–(3)).
- According to news reports (Fana, Xinhua), enforcement started on 31 January 2026; no EPA notice confirming this was found.
- The EPA may allow some single-use plastics under special conditions by directive, but this exception never covers plastic bags (Art. 14(6)).
- Individuals using or intending to use a plastic bag: fine of 2,000–5,000 Birr.
- Making, importing, distributing, selling, storing or possessing plastic bags for business: 50,000–200,000 Birr fine plus up to five years' rigorous imprisonment.
- If an institution commits the offence, the fine is three times higher.
- Plastic producers must ensure their products are collected for reuse or recycling and cover the cost (Art. 5(1)).
- In Addis Ababa, city Regulation 150/2023 fines littering plastic bags (and other small waste) on roads Birr 200; report violations to the Code Enforcement Authority free line 9995.

## Official text

**Proclamation No. 1383/2025 (English)** — https://www.epa.gov.et/images/PDF/Proc.No%201383-2025%20Solid%20Waste%20Management%20and%20Disposal.pdf

Art. 2(10): "'Plastic Bag' means a low-density, non-biodegradable and a plastic used to contain other products;"
Art. 2(11): "'Single-Use Plastic' means a plastic product that is not reused or recycled and used only once and is disposed of directly once used;"

Art. 13 Single-use plastic
"1/ The production, placing into the market, importation, storage, sale, or use of any single-use plastic is prohibited.
2/ The provision in Sub-Article (1) of this Article shall not apply to single-use plastic manufactured domestically, imported into the country, or in the process of being imported before the coming into force of this proclamation.
3/ The special condition outlined in Sub-Article (2) of this Article shall be valid only for six months from the effective date of this Proclamation.
4/ The Authority or the Regional environmental protection office shall regulate the enforcement of the provisions of this Proclamation related to single-use plastic and take appropriate corrective measures in the event of violations."

Art. 14(1): "Notwithstanding with Article 13 of this Proclamation, the authority shall issue a directive to regulate the production, import, or use of single-use plastics under special conditions."
Art. 14(5): "Any importer or manufacturer engaged in the production of single-use plastic before the entry into force of this Proclamation shall comply with the requirements outlined in the proclamation within six months."
Art. 14(6): "The exceptions outlined under this Article do not apply to plastic bag."

Art. 25 Criminal liability
"1/ Any individual who:
a) Uses or intends to use a plastic bag shall be subject to a fine of no less than two thousand birr and no more than five thousand birr;
b) Manufactures, imports, distributes, sells, stores, or possesses plastic bags for commercial purposes shall be subject to a fine of no less than fifty thousand birr and no more than two hundred thousand birr, along with a term of rigorous imprisonment not exceeding five years.
2/ Notwithstanding the provisions of Sub-Article (1) of this Article, if the offence is committed by an institution, the penalty to be imposed shall be three times the amount prescribed therein."

Art. 28: "This Proclamation shall come into force up on Publication in the Federal Negarit Gazeta. Done at Addis Ababa, this 31st Day of July, 2025"
