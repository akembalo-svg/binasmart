## In short (English)

- **For a burst line, a water cut or a complaint,** call the Addis Ababa Water and Sewerage Authority (AAWSA) toll-free on **906**. Head-office lines: 0116187641/42/43 (Megenagna, next to the Revenues and Customs Authority). Email info@aawsa.gov.et; fax 0116623924.
- **For water that smells or looks unusual,** call 0911862727. AAWSA says that if muddy water comes out of the tap, you can let it run for 2–3 minutes until it clears and then use it.
- You can complain in writing, verbally, by fax, email or phone. If you get no answer, go to the complaint-hearing committee at the head office or your branch. If it is still not resolved, you can go to the Ombudsman, the Anti-Corruption Commission, the courts or the media.
- **Paying the bill (Commercial Bank of Ethiopia):** using your customer account number, pay at any CBE branch, through CBE Birr (AAWSA account number **878787**), at CBE agents, by standing order from your CBE savings account, or through CBE internet banking. The FAQ gives the payment window as "from the 26th to the 18th" of the month (as published).
- **Water tariff in Addis Ababa (Reg. 149/2023, 5th year: 8 July 2026 – 7 July 2027, birr per m³):** 1–5: 22.17; 6–14: 38.80; 15–23: 72.07; 24–32: 110.87; 33–41: 133.04; 42–50: 155.22; above 50: 177.39. The rates are the same for domestic and non-domestic customers.
- Domestic bills are progressive: each block is charged at its own rate. Non-domestic customers pay their whole consumption at the rate of the block it falls in. Public fountains always pay the first-block rate (22.17 birr).
- **Sewerage tariff (5th year):** domestic 7.30 birr/m³ for 1–5 m³ and 12.20 birr/m³ above 5 m³; non-domestic 17.10 birr/m³.
- Every bill is based on at least 3 m³. If the meter reads 0 for 6 months in a row, AAWSA may remove the meter.
- Monthly meter rent (5th year): 1/2-inch 20 birr; 3/4-inch 27 birr.
- Other fees on the AAWSA website (undated): reconnection after paying debts 56 birr; name/address change 12 birr; leak inspection after a "bill too high" complaint 45 birr for a house up to one storey and 284 birr for a building above two storeys.
- Caution: the regulation says the tariff "may be adjusted when necessary" (Art. 5(3)). Check the rate on your bill with your branch.
