# ጠበቃ ፈቃድ እንዳለው ማረጋገጥ — Checking Whether an Advocate Is Licensed (Federal)

## በአጭሩ (Amharic summary)

- ማንኛውም ሰው ፈቃድ ሳይኖረው የጥብቅና አገልግሎት መስጠት አይችልም (አዋጅ 1249/2013 አንቀጽ 4(1))።
- ፈቃድ የማያስፈልጋቸው፦ የራሱን ጉዳይ የሚከራከር ሰው፤ ያለ ክፍያ የትዳር ጓደኛውን፣ ወላጁን፣ ልጁን፣ አያቱን፣ እህቱን፣ ወንድሙን ወዘተ የሚወክል ሰው፤ ከሥራው ጋር በተያያዘ ዐቃቤ ሕግ (አንቀጽ 5)።
- የፌደራል የጥብቅና ፈቃድ ሦስት ዓይነት ነው፦ የፌደራል የመጀመሪያ ደረጃ ፍርድ ቤት ፈቃድ፣ የሁሉም ፌደራል ፍርድ ቤቶች ፈቃድ እና የፌደራል ልዩ ፈቃድ (አንቀጽ 11)። ጠበቃዎ ፈቃዱ ጉዳይዎ ለሚታይበት ፍርድ ቤት የሚያገለግል መሆኑን ይጠይቁ።
- የፈቃድ ያላቸው ጠበቆችና የሕግ ድርጅቶች መዝገብ የሚይዘውና መረጃውን ለሕዝብ ተደራሽ የማድረግ ግዴታ ያለበት ቀድሞ ዐቃቤ ሕግ፣ አሁን ፍትሕ ሚኒስቴር ነው (አንቀጽ 17)።
- ለሕዝብ ክፍት የሆነ የኦንላይን የጠበቃ ፍለጋ አገልግሎት በመንግሥት ድረ-ገጾች ላይ አልተገኘም። ስለዚህ ጠበቃው ፈቃዱን እንዲያሳይዎ ይጠይቁ፤ ጥርጣሬ ካለ ፍትሕ ሚኒስቴርን በቀጥታ ይጠይቁ።
- ፈቃዱ በየዓመቱ፣ የጸናበት ጊዜ ካለቀ በአንድ ወር ውስጥ መታደስ አለበት። ካልታደሰ በቅጣት በሚቀጥለው አንድ ወር ውስጥ ሊታደስ ይችላል። ያላሳደሰ ጠበቃ በዲሲፕሊን ይጠየቃል (አንቀጽ 19)። ስለዚህ የፈቃዱን የጸናበት ቀን ይመልከቱ።
- ለእድሳት የሚያስፈልጉት፦ የግብር ክሊራንስ፣ የግዴታ ሥልጠና ማስረጃ፣ የማኅበር መዋጮ ማስረጃ፣ ከ70 ዓመት በላይ ለሆኑ የጤና ማስረጃ እና የነፃ ጥብቅና ግዴታን የተወጣበት ማስረጃ (አንቀጽ 20)።
- ከሁለት ዓመት በላይ ከጥብቅና ሥራ የራቀ ጠበቃ ፈቃዱን መልሶ የሚያገኘው የመግቢያ ፈተና ካለፈ ብቻ ነው (አንቀጽ 21(3))።
- የፌደራል ጠቅላይ ፍርድ ቤት የኤሌክትሮኒክ ክስ ማቅረቢያ ሥርዓት የጠበቆችን የፈቃድ ቁጥር ያረጋግጣል፤ ይህ ግን ለጠበቆች ምዝገባ ብቻ ነው፣ ለሕዝብ ፍለጋ አይደለም።
- ፍትሕ ሚኒስቴር፦ ስልክ +251 115 51 50 99፣ ባምቢስ ፊት ለፊት፣ አዲስ አበባ፤ ሰኞ–አርብ 8:30–5:30።

## In short (English)

- No one may provide advocacy services without a licence (Proclamation 1249/2021, Art. 4(1)).
- No licence is needed for: a person handling his own case; a person representing, without charge, close family members listed in the law; a public prosecutor in his work (Art. 5).
- There are three federal licence types: Federal First Instance Court, All Federal Courts, and Federal Special advocacy licence (Art. 11). Ask whether your advocate's licence covers the court hearing your case.
- The register of licensed advocates and law firms is kept by the Attorney General — whose functions now sit with the Ministry of Justice — which must make the information accessible to the public "in different ways" (Art. 17).
- No public online advocate search was found on government sites. Ask the advocate to show the licence, and check with the Ministry of Justice if in doubt.
- Licences are renewed every year within a month of the last validity date; late renewal is possible within the following month with a fine; an advocate who does not renew faces disciplinary charges (Art. 19). Check the validity date on the licence.
- Renewal requires: tax clearance, proof of mandatory training, proof of Association membership contribution, a health certificate for advocates over 70, and proof of pro bono service (Art. 20).
- An advocate away from practice for more than two years gets the licence back only by passing the entrance exam (Art. 21(3)).
- The Federal Supreme Court's e-filing system verifies lawyers' licence numbers, but only as lawyer registration, not as a public search.
- Ministry of Justice: +251 115 51 50 99, in front of Bambis, Addis Ababa; Mon–Fri 8:30 AM–5:30 PM.

## Official text

### Proclamation No. 1249/2021 (English column, verbatim)
Federal Negarit Gazette 27th Year No. 42, 5 August 2021. PDF: https://justice.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/አዋጅ-ቁጥር-1249-2013.pdf

**Art. 4(1).** "No person shall provide advocacy services without having a license;"

**Art. 5 (no licence required).** "1/ Any person handling his own case; 2/ A person who represents, without charge, his spouse, parent, child, grandparent, sister, brother, the parent of his spouse, a person to whom he is the designated tutor or guardian; 3/ A Public prosecutor on cases related to his job;" (sub-arts. 4-5 cover certain employees or officials of organisations.)

**Art. 11. Types of Advocacy License.** "1/ The Federal First Instance Court Advocacy License; 2/ All Federal Courts Advocacy License; and 3/ The Federal Special Advocacy License."

**Art. 17. Registration.** "1/ The Attorney General shall maintain a state-of-the-art [word missing in the gazette] that contains full information about register of advocates and law firms that have been granted advocacy licenses; 2/ The particulars required in the registration process shall be determined by a Directive to be issued by the Attorney General; 3/ The Attorney General shall notify the Association, every three months, the list of newly licensed and registered advocates and law firms pursuant to Sub-Article (1) of this Article; 4/ The Attorney General shall make accessible the information, about licensed advocates and law firms, to the society in different ways."

**Art. 19. Renewal of License.** "1/ An advocacy license shall be renewed every year within a month of its last validity date; 2/ Any advocate or firm that fails to renew his or its license within the period provided in Sub-Article (1) of this Article, upon payment of fine the amount of which shall be determined by a Directive to be issued by the Attorney General, may renew his or its license within the following one month; ... 4/ Any advocate or law firm that has not renewed his or its license according to this Article, shall be charged for disciplinary misconduct."

**Art. 20. Documents Necessary for Renewal of License.** "1/ Tax clearance certificate; 2/ With the exception of the advocate whose mandatory training was rescheduled pursuant to Article 30 Sub-Article (4) of this Proclamation, evidence showing that the advocates completed the mandatory training prescribed by this Proclamation; 3/ Evidence given by the Association which certifies that the advocate has discharged his duty of paying membership contribution; 4/ Evidence from a health institution, for those advocates above 70 years old, which certifies that the advocate is fit and in good health condition to provide advocacy service; and 5/ Evidence showing that the Advocate discharged his duty of providing pro bono service expected of him in a year."

**Art. 21(1), (3).** An advocate must return the licence "a) when he permanently engaged in jobs other than advocacy service; b) when he is unable to render advocacy service; c) when he decides to terminate providing advocacy service." "... an advocate who dissociated himself from advocacy service for more than two years can get back his license only when he passes the entrance exam set for his class of license."

**Who is the "Attorney General" now:** the Ministry of Justice article on free legal aid (https://justice.gov.et/en/article/free-legal-aid-service/) states that the MoJ, set up under Proclamation 1263/2014, took over the Attorney General's powers.
