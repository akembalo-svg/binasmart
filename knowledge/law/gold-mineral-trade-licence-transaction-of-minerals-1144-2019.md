# የወርቅና የማዕድን ግብይት ፈቃድ — Gold and mineral trading (supplier, jewellery, trade, export) licences

## በአጭሩ (Amharic summary)

- ማዕድን (ወርቅን ጨምሮ) ለመግዛትና ለመሸጥ በማዕድን ግብይት አዋጅ ቁጥር 1144/2011 (1144/2019) መሠረት **ፈቃድ** ያስፈልጋል። የንግድ ፈቃድ ከመጠየቃችሁ በፊት **የብቃት ማረጋገጫ ምስክር ወረቀት** ማቅረብ አለባችሁ።
- የብቃት ማረጋገጫ የሚሰጠው የማዕድን ሚኒስቴር ወይም የክልሉ የማዕድን ዘርፍ ኃላፊ አካል ነው፤ የንግድ ፈቃዱ የሚሰጠው የንግድ ሚኒስቴር ወይም የክልሉ አግባብ ያለው አካል ነው።
- የፈቃድ ዓይነቶች፦ የማዕድን አቅራቢ (supplier)፣ የማዕድን ሥራ/ጌጣጌጥ (crafting — አንጥረኛ፣ ድንጋይ ቆራጭ/lapidary፣ ማጣመር)፣ ማጣሪያ (refining)፣ ማቅለጫ (smelting)፣ የማዕድን ንግድ (trade) እና የውጭ ንግድ (export)።
- **የወርቅ/ብር አቅራቢ** ከሕጋዊ አምራቾች ገዝቶ **ለብሔራዊ ባንክ ወይም ሥልጣን ለተሰጠው አካል በ30 ቀን ውስጥ** መሸጥ አለበት፤ ግዢው ፈቃዱ በተሰጠበት ክልል ውስጥ ብቻ ነው፤ ወርቁን በቅርብ ወዳለው ባንክ ያጓጉዛል፤ ሕጋዊ ደረሰኝ ይጠቀማል፤ በየ3 ወሩ ለክልሉ ሪፖርት ያደርጋል።
- **የወርቅ አንጥረኛ (smithery)** ጥሬ ወርቅ ወይም ብር መግዛት የሚችለው ከብሔራዊ ባንክ ወይም ሥልጣን ካለው አካል ብቻ ነው፤ ጥሬውን ለሌላ ሰው ማስተላለፍ አይችልም።
- **የማዕድን ንግድ ፈቃድ** ከማዕድን የተሠሩ ጌጣጌጦችን በጅምላና በችርቻሮ ለመሸጥ ያስችላል።
- ለአቅራቢ ፈቃድ የሚቀርቡ፦ ስም፣ የሥራ አድራሻና ዜግነት፤ የማዕድኑ ዓይነት፤ የሚገዛበትና የሚሸጥበት ቦታ፤ ቋሚ መጋዘንና የሥራ ቦታ ማስረጃ፤ የንግድ ዕቅድ (business plan)፤ የካፒታል ማስረጃ። ለንግድ ፈቃድ በተጨማሪ ለደንበኞች የማዕድኑን ጥራትና መጠን የሚያሳውቁበት ሥርዓትና የሥራ ቦታ ማስረጃ ያስፈልጋል። የጌጣጌጥ ሥራ ፈቃድ የአካባቢ ተጽዕኖ ግምገማ ጥናት ይጠይቃል።
- ፈቃዱም የብቃት ማረጋገጫውም የሚያገለግሉት **ለ1 ዓመት** ነው፤ ለማደስ የዓመቱን **የግብር ክሊራንስ** ማቅረብ ያስፈልጋል።
- **የውጭ ባለሀብት** የአቅራቢ ፈቃድ ወይም የወርቅና ብር ማቅለጫ ፈቃድ፣ እንዲሁም የንግድ ወይም የውጭ ንግድ የብቃት ማረጋገጫ አያገኝም። የማዕድን ማምረት ፈቃድ ያለው ሰው የአቅራቢ ፈቃድ አይሰጠውም።
- ፈቃድ ከተጠየቀ በኋላ ውድቅ ከሆነ ምክንያቱ በ10 ቀን (ለብቃት ማረጋገጫ በ5 ቀን) በጽሑፍ ይገለጻል። ፈቃዱ የተሰረዘበት ሰው ለ2 ዓመት ሌላ ፈቃድ አያገኝም።
- ክፍያዎች በፈቃድ ሰጪው አካልና በደንብ ይወሰናሉ፤ መጠኑ በዚህ ምንጭ አልተገኘም።

## In short (English)

- Buying and selling minerals, including gold, needs a **licence** under Transaction of Minerals Proclamation 1144/2019; a trade-licence applicant must first hold a **certificate of competence**.
- Certificates of competence: Ministry of Mines or the regional mining authority. Licences: Ministry of Trade (and Industry) or the relevant regional authority.
- Licence types: mineral supplier, crafting (smithery, lapidary, combining), refining, smelting, trade, exporting.
- **Gold/silver supplier**: buys from legal miners and must sell to the National Bank of Ethiopia or an authorised entity **within 30 days**; buys only in the region where licensed; transports gold to the nearest bank; uses legal invoices; reports every 3 months.
- **Goldsmiths** may buy raw gold/silver only from the National Bank or an authorised entity and may not pass it on in raw form.
- **Mineral trade licence**: bulk and retail sale of ornaments made from minerals.
- Supplier application: name, work address, nationality; mineral type; buying/selling location; proof of permanent store and workplace; business plan; proof of capital. Trade licence adds a system for informing consumers about quality and quantity, and proof of workplace. Crafting adds an environmental impact assessment.
- Licences and certificates are valid **one year**; renewal requires a **tax clearance** for the budget year.
- **Foreign investors** cannot get supplier or gold/silver smelting licences, nor trade or export certificates of competence. Mining licence holders cannot get a supplier licence.
- Refusals are notified in writing within 10 days (5 days for certificates). A cancelled licensee cannot hold another licence for 2 years.
- Fee amounts: set by the licensing authority/regulation; not found.

## Official text

Proclamation No. 1144/2019, Federal Negarit Gazette No. 86, 15 August 2019 — https://justice.gov.et/en/law/transaction-of-minerals-ratification-proclamation/

- Art. 2(24): "'License Issuing Authority' means as the case may be the Ministry of Trade and Industry or any relevant regional authority." Art. 2(25): "'Certificate of Competence Issuing Authority' means as the case may be the Ministry or any regional authority responsible for managing the mineral sector;" Art. 2(26): "'Ministry' means the Ministry of Mines and Petroleum;" Art. 2(28): "'Bank' means the National Bank of Ethiopia;"
- Art. 5: "1. Any person may carry out transactions of minerals upon obtaining license pursuant to this proclamation and other relevant laws. 2. Any person requested for trade license of mineral transaction shall present certificate of competence granted by the certificate of competence issuing. Authority."
- Art. 6: "1. Mineral supplier license; 2. Mineral crafting license; 3. Mineral refining license; 4. Mineral smelting license 5. Mineral Trade license. 6. Mineral Exporting license."
- Art. 7(1)(c): "When it is gold or silver, the supplier license grants the holders of the license the right to purchase mineral from legal miners, hold for sale, transport or sale locally to the bank or to an authorized entity within 30 days."
- Art. 8(2): "Smithery license grants the holder of the license the right to purchase, hold, transport and smithering gold or silver as promulgated by the law and sale the product locally or abroad. However, the holder of this license can not purchase, make jewelries and sale raw minerals."
- Art. 11(1): "Mineral transaction license grants the right to purchase in bulk and retail locally ornaments made from metallic, precious or semi-precious minerals."
- Art. 13(2): "a/ ... mineral supplier license or gold and silver smelting license shall not be issued to foreign investor. b/ No person, whose license has been cancelled pursuant to this proclamation, may hold another license for two years following such cancellation. c/ A supplier license shall not be issued to holder of mining license."
- Art. 14(1): supplier application: "a/ Name, occupational address and nationality of the applicant; b/ The type of mineral ...; c/ The location where the applicant purchases and sale the mineral; d/ An evidence showing the permanent store and work place ...; e/ Business plan; f/ An evidence showing the capital required ..." Art. 14(2)(c): crafting — "An environmental impact assessment study ..." Art. 14(5): transaction licence — "b/ System as to how he notifies the consumers on the quality and quantity of the mineral he sells; c/ Evidence that shows the availability of work place ..."
- Art. 15(3): refusal notified "in writing within 10 days."
- Art. 16: "1. Any license shall be valid for one year from the date of issuance. ... 4. Any licensee who wishes to renew his license shall produce tax clearance certificate for the budget year from tax office."
- Art. 17(2): supplier shall "a/ Supply gold and silver to the bank or legally authorized entity every budget year an amount to be determined by an agreement with the licensing authority ... f/ Purchase mineral only within the region where his license is issued. g/ Transport gold or silver to the nearest bank or legally authorized entity ... h/ Use legal invoices ... i/ Provide report of purchased or sold minerals every three months to the regional licensing authority ..."
- Art. 17(3): crafter shall "a/ Purchase raw gold or silver only from bank or legally authorized entity; b/ Not to transfer the gold or silver he purchased to any other person in its raw form."
- Art. 22(2)(a): "Mineral transaction or exporter certificate of competence shall not be issued to foreign investor."
- Art. 24(3): certificate refusal notified "in writing within 5 days." Art. 25(1): "Any certificate of competence shall be valid for one year from the date of issuance." Art. 25(4): renewal requires "tax clearance certificate for the budget year".
- Art. 27: certificate fees "shall be determined in accordance with regulation ...; however previous fee payment procedures shall remain applicable until the Regulation becomes effective."

Proclamation 816/2013 (amending 678/2010): "the holder of artisanal and special small scale mining license that has engaged in gold or silver mining shall sell the minerals produced to the National Bank of Ethiopia."
