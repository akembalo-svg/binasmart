# የ12ኛ ክፍል ብሔራዊ ፈተና ውጤት እንዴት እንደሚታይ — How to Check the Grade 12 National Exam (ESSLCE) Result

## በአጭሩ (Amharic summary)

- የ12ኛ ክፍል ብሔራዊ ፈተናን (ESSLCE) የሚያዘጋጀውና ውጤቱን የሚያወጣው የትምህርት ምዘናና ፈተናዎች አገልግሎት (EAES) ነው።
- EAES ያሳወቃቸው 3 ይፋዊ የውጤት ማያ መንገዶች፦
  1. ድረ-ገጽ፦ https://result.eaes.et/
  2. ቴሌግራም ቦት፦ https://t.me/EAESbot
  3. አጭር የጽሑፍ መልዕክት (SMS)፦ 6284
- በድረ-ገጹ ውጤት ለማየት የመፈተኛ ቁጥርዎን (Admission Number) እና የመጀመሪያ ስምዎን ያስገቡ።
- ውጤቱ በመገናኛ ብዙኃን ይፋ እንደተደረገ፣ ውጤትዎንና ጊዜያዊ ሰርተፊኬትዎን (temporary certificate) በEAES ድረ-ገጽ ማግኘት ይችላሉ።
- ዋናው (ኦሪጅናል) ሰርተፊኬት የሚሰጠው በተፈተኑበት ትምህርት ቤት ርዕሰ መምህር በኩል ነው።
- ትክክለኛ የውጤት መረጃ የሚገኘው ከEAES ይፋዊ ገጾች ብቻ ነው። ሌሎች "የውጤት ማያ" ድረ-ገጾችና ቦቶች ይፋዊ አይደሉም።
- የ2018 ዓ.ም ውጤት (EAES፣ ነሐሴ 16/2018 ዓ.ም = 22 ኦገስት 2026)፦ ከተፈተኑ 550,210 ተማሪዎች መካከል 70,544 (12.8%) 50% እና ከዚያ በላይ አምጥተዋል። በ2017 ዓ.ም ያለፉት 8.9% ነበሩ።

## In short (English)

- The Educational Assessment and Examinations Service (EAES) runs the Grade 12 national exam (ESSLCE) and releases its results.
- EAES lists 3 official ways to get your result:
  1. Website: https://result.eaes.et/
  2. Telegram bot: https://t.me/EAESbot
  3. SMS: short code 6284
- On the website, enter your Admission Number and your first name.
- Once the results are announced in the media, you can get your result and a temporary certificate from the EAES website.
- The original certificate is handed out through the head of the school where you sat the exam.
- Only EAES's own channels give reliable results. Other "result checker" websites and bots are not official.
- 2018 E.C. result (EAES, 22 August 2026): 70,544 of the 550,210 students who sat the exam (12.8%) scored 50% or more. In 2017 E.C. the pass rate was 8.9%.
- If you failed in an earlier year and want to sit again as a private candidate, register at https://register.eaes.et/Online. There is a registration fee.
- EAES head office: King George VI Street, 5 Kilo, Addis Ababa. Phone +251 11 123 2890, +251 11 123 2884 or +251 11 154 4477. Email info@eaes.et. P.O. Box 30747.

## What is not published

- EAES does not say what to type in the SMS to 6284 (for example, only the admission number) or what the SMS costs. Follow the instructions EAES gives on release day, or ask EAES.
- There is no official date for the next (2019 E.C.) result release yet. Follow EAES's official pages.
- The private-candidate registration fee and the list of required documents are not shown on the public page. They appear in the registration form.
