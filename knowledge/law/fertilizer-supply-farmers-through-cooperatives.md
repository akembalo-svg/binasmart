# ማዳበሪያ ለአርሶ አደሮች እንዴት ይቀርባል — How Fertilizer Reaches Farmers in Ethiopia

## በአጭሩ (Amharic summary)

- ማዳበሪያ በመንግሥት ተገዝቶ ወደ ሀገር ይገባል። ፋና ነሐሴ 27 ቀን 2016 (2 September 2024) እንደዘገበው፣ መንግሥት ለ2017 (2024/25) የምርት ዘመን 25 ሚሊዮን ኩንታል ማዳበሪያ ለመግዛት 1.3 ቢሊዮን የአሜሪካ ዶላር አጽድቋል። ይህም 13 ሚሊዮን ኩንታል ዩሪያና 12 ሚሊዮን ኩንታል ዳፕ (DAP) ነው።
- በዚሁ ዘገባ መሠረት ማዳበሪያው ለአርሶ አደሮች የሚከፋፈለው በመሠረታዊ (የመጀመሪያ ደረጃ) የኅብረት ሥራ ማኅበራት በኩል ነው።
- ስለዚህ አርሶ አደር ማዳበሪያ ለማግኘት በቀበሌው ያለውን መሠረታዊ የኅብረት ሥራ ማኅበር ወይም የወረዳውን ግብርና ጽ/ቤት ይጠይቃል።
- ፋና እንደዘገበው ለ2016 (2023/24) የምርት ዘመን ከተገዛው 20 ሚሊዮን ኩንታል ውስጥ 18.8 ሚሊዮን ኩንታሉ ወደ ሀገር ገብቷል።
- የማዳበሪያ ዋጋ፣ የክፍያ ወይም የብድር ሁኔታ እና ለመግዛት ምን ሰነድ እንደሚያስፈልግ በይፋ የታተመ አላገኘንም። ዋጋው በየዓመቱና በየአካባቢው ሊለያይ ስለሚችል ከኅብረት ሥራ ማኅበሩ ወይም ከወረዳ ግብርና ጽ/ቤት ያረጋግጡ።
- የቀድሞው የማዳበሪያ ማምረትና ንግድ አዋጅ ቁጥር 137/1991 (137/1998) በፍትሕ ሚኒስቴር የሕግ ቋት ላይ "የተሻረ" ተብሎ ተመዝግቧል። የተካውን ሕግ ማግኘት አልቻልንም።

## In short (English)

- The government buys fertilizer and imports it. Fana reported on 2 September 2024 that the government had approved USD 1.3 billion to buy 25 million quintals of fertilizer for the 2024/25 growing season: 13 million quintals of urea and 12 million quintals of DAP.
- According to the same report, the fertilizer is distributed to farmers through basic (primary) cooperatives.
- So a farmer who needs fertilizer should ask the primary cooperative in their kebele or the woreda agriculture office.
- Fana also reported that 18.8 million of the 20 million quintals bought for the 2023/24 season had arrived in the country.
- We found no official published information on fertilizer prices, payment or credit terms, or the documents needed to buy. Prices can differ by year and area, so check with your cooperative or woreda agriculture office.
- The Ministry of Justice law database lists the old Fertilizer Manufacturing and Trade Proclamation No. 137/1998 as "Repealed". We could not identify the law that replaced it.

## Official text

**Fana Media Corporation, 2 September 2024** — https://www.fanamc.com/english/govt-approves-us1-3-for-25m-quintals-of-soil-fertilizer-procurement/

- "the government has approved 1.3 billion US dollars for the procurement of 25 million quintals of soil fertilizer, which includes 13 million quintals of Urea and 12 million quintals of DAP"
- "25 million quintals of soil fertilizer will be purchased for the 2024/25 growing season"
- "out of the 20 million quintals of soil fertilizer procured for the 2023/24 cultivation season, 18.8 million quintals have been successfully transported into the country"
- "These fertilizers have been and are currently being distributed to farmers through basic cooperatives for their use in the cultivation season"

**Ministry of Justice law database** — https://justice.gov.et/en/law/fertilizer-manufacturing-and-trade-proclamation/

- "Law ID: Proclamation No. 137/1998", "Date: November 24, 1998", status: "Repealed".

- The official figures and allocation for the current (2026/27) season.
- The law that replaced Proclamation 137/1998 on fertilizer trade and licensing.
