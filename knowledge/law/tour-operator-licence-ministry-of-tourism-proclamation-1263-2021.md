# የአስጎብኚ ድርጅት (ቱር ኦፕሬተር) ፈቃድ — Tour Operator Licence in Ethiopia

## በአጭሩ (Amharic summary)

- የቱሪዝም ሚኒስቴር ሥልጣንና ተግባር የተደነገገው በአስፈጻሚ አካላት ሥልጣንና ተግባር መወሰኛ አዋጅ ቁጥር 1263/2014 (1263/2021) አንቀጽ 24 ነው። የፍትሕ ሚኒስቴር ይህ አዋጅ በሥራ ላይ መሆኑን ያሳያል።
- የቱሪስት አገልግሎት ሰጪዎችን ደረጃ የሚወስነውና አፈጻጸሙን የሚቆጣጠረው የቱሪዝም ሚኒስቴር ነው (አንቀጽ 24(1)(m))።
- ፈቃድ የሚሰጠው ማን ነው? ሚኒስቴሩ ፈቃድ የሚሰጠው፦ (1) ከአንድ ክልል በላይ አገልግሎት ለሚሰጡ ባለሀብቶች፣ (2) ሕግ ሲፈቅድ በውጭ ባለሀብቶች ለሚካሄዱ የቱሪስት አገልግሎቶች ነው (አንቀጽ 24(1)(n))። ሥልጣኑ በግልጽ ለሌላ አካል ከተሰጠ ግን ሚኒስቴሩ አይሰጥም።
- ስለዚህ በአንድ ክልል ወይም በአዲስ አበባ ብቻ ለሚሠራ የአገር ውስጥ አስጎብኚ ድርጅት ፈቃድ የሚሰጠው አካል በዚህ አዋጅ አልተገለጸም። የክልልዎን (በአዲስ አበባ የከተማ አስተዳደሩን ባህል፣ ኪነ-ጥበብና ቱሪዝም ቢሮ) ወይም ሚኒስቴሩን ይጠይቁ።
- የኢትዮጵያ ኢ-ሰርቪስ ፖርታል (www.eservices.gov.et) "Tour operator Certification" የሚባል አገልግሎት ይዘረዝራል። በፖርታሉ ላይ ተመዝግቦ ማመልከት ይቻል እንደሆነ ይመልከቱ። የፖርታሉ የቴክኒክ ድጋፍ ስልክ 888 ነው።
- የቱሪዝም ሚኒስቴር አድራሻ፦ ስልክ +251 935 203 138፤ ኢሜይል info@mot.gov.et፤ ቦሌ ጃፓን መብራቱ ጋር ወደ ሰሜን አቅጣጫ 100 ሜትር ገባ ብሎ (በአዲስ አበባ)።
- የሚያስፈልጉ ሰነዶች (ለምሳሌ ተሽከርካሪ፣ ቢሮ፣ ካፒታል፣ ባለሙያ)፣ ክፍያና የሚፈጀው ጊዜ በይፋ ሊገኙ አልቻሉም።
- ተዛማጅ ሕጎች በፍትሕ ሚኒስቴር የሕግ ቋት ላይ አሉ፦ የቱሪዝም የሥነ-ምግባር ደንብ መመሪያ ቁጥር 472/2020፤ የአዲስ አበባ ከተማ አስተዳደር መመሪያ ቁጥር 95/2014 (በእንግሊዝኛ ርዕሱ "Cultural and Tourism Service Providers Professional Qualification and Standards Guide")። ጽሑፋቸውን አላነበብንም።

## In short (English)

- The Ministry of Tourism's powers are set in Article 24 of the Definition of Powers and Duties of the Executive Organs Proclamation No. 1263/2021. The Ministry of Justice lists this proclamation as in force.
- The Ministry of Tourism sets the standards for tourist service providers and supervises how they are applied (Art. 24(1)(m)).
- Who licenses? The Ministry licenses (1) investors who provide services in more than one region and (2), where the law says so, tourist services run by foreign investors (Art. 24(1)(n)). This does not apply where the law clearly gives that power to another body.
- The proclamation does not say who licenses a domestic tour operator working in only one region or only in Addis Ababa. Ask your regional tourism bureau (in Addis Ababa, the city's Culture, Arts and Tourism Bureau) or the Ministry.
- The Ethiopian eServices portal (www.eservices.gov.et) lists a service called "Tour operator Certification". Check whether you can register and apply there. The portal's technical support line is 888.
- Ministry of Tourism contacts: Tel +251 935 203 138, email info@mot.gov.et. Office: about 100 m north of the Bole Japan traffic light, Addis Ababa.
- We found no official information on the documents required (for example vehicle, office, capital or staff), the fees or the processing time.
- Related laws in the Ministry of Justice database, which we did not read: Tourism Code of Ethics Directive No. 472/2020, and Addis Ababa City Administration Cultural and Tourism Service Providers Professional Qualification and Standards Guide Directive No. 95/2014.

## Official text

**Proclamation No. 1263/2021** — Federal Negarit Gazette, 28th Year No. 4, 25 January 2022. PDF: https://justice.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/አዋጅ-ቁጥር-1263-2014.pdf (MoJ page shows "In Force")

- Art. 24(1): "The Ministry of Tourism shall have the following powers and duties:"
  - "m) determine the standard of tourist service providers and supervise its implementation;"
  - "n) issue, unless such power is clearly given to other organ, license to investors who provide service in more than one region or, where the law so provides, to tourist service providers to be conducted by foreign investors; supervise the same;"
  - "p) coordinate government and private sector engaged in tourism sector; prepare and implement a framework to work in partnership with regional tourism organs; create a joint platform in connection with tourism to coordinate multifaceted efforts in order to ensure quality tourism service and safety of tourists;"
- Art. 24(2): "The powers and duties entrusted to Ministry of Culture and Tourism and Tourism Ethiopia under other laws that are currently in force with respect to Tourism are hereby vested in the Ministry of Tourism established by this Proclamation."
