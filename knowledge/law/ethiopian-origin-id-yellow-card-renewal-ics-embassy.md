## In short (English)
- The Ethiopian Origin ID (yellow card) is issued to foreign nationals of Ethiopian origin. The Immigration and Citizenship Service (ICS) issues and renews it.
- In Ethiopia, apply on the ICS portal www.immigration.gov.et (it also handles Origin ID cards) or at the nearest immigration office.
- Abroad, you can apply through the Ethiopian embassy or consulate in your country of residence (ICS FAQ).
- ICS documents: completed application form; an ordinary passport valid for more than 6 months; a copy of your entry visa and entry stamp (for an e-visa, the printout); if your visa was extended, a copy of the latest extension; one recent passport photo.
- Proof of Ethiopian origin: a machine-readable Ethiopian passport or an authenticated birth certificate, or a copy of an Ethiopian parent's kebele ID or passport.
- If you changed your name, bring a certified court decision. If it is not in Amharic or English, a legal entity must translate it.
- Fee (ICS page, viewed Sept 2026): USD 300 regular, USD 400 urgent.
- A daily penalty applies if you do not renew on time. ICS does not publish the amount.
- The Origin ID cannot be issued by proxy.
- ICS says most applications take about 5 to 10 business days.
- The Brussels embassy pages say the card is valid for five years. A card not renewed within a year after it expires is treated as returned. Embassies list their own fees, so check with yours before applying.
- ICS toll-free line: 8133. Email: info@ics.gov.et.

## Official text
**ICS — Ethiopian Origin ID** — https://ics.gov.et/services/ethiopian-origin-id/ (viewed 17 Sept 2026)
> "The Ethiopian Origin ID is issued to individuals of Ethiopian descent who hold foreign citizenship. This ID grants various benefits, including visa-free entry into Ethiopia, the right to own property, and easier access to residency and employment opportunities."

Requirements (verbatim): "Properly filled out application form. An ordinary passport valid for more than 6 months. Copy of entry visa (If it's e-visa, visa print out) and date stamp. If the tourist visa has been extended before, a copy of the last extended visa. Machine readable Ethiopian passport or Authenticated birth certificate. If your mother or father is an Ethiopian citizen, you must provide a copy of their Kebele ID or passport. … One recent passport-sized photograph. … In case of name change, a certified legal court decision must be submitted; if the language is not Amharic or English, it must be translated by a legal entity. In cases where family documents are unavailable due to death, submission of a death certificate is mandatory."

> "N.B: A mandatory service fee applies: 300 USD for regular and 400 USD for urgent service. A daily penalty applies if you don't renew your ID on time. It is not possible to issue Ethiopian origin ID by proxy."

FAQ on the same page: "Generally, it takes about 5 to 10 business days for most applications, but some may take longer." "Yes, many services can be applied for from outside Ethiopia. You will need to submit your application through the Ethiopian Embassy or Consulate in your country of residence." You can check your status online with your application reference number.

**ICS ePassport portal** — https://www.immigration.gov.et/ — "apply for and manage your passports, Ethiopian Origin ID cards, visas, and work permits."

**Embassy of Ethiopia, Brussels** (undated pages)
- https://ethiopiaembassy.eu/consular-and-visa-services/ethiopian-origin-id-card/ — "Ethiopian Origin ID cards are valid for five years. If not renewed in time (in a year), it will be considered as if it were returned." Renewal: existing card, a copy of a valid passport, a completed application form, and a service fee. Cards are collected in person at the embassy's consular section.
- https://ethiopianembassy.be/?page_id=28 — renewal by post: two application forms; your existing card with two copies; proof of Ethiopian origin; two copies of a passport valid for more than 6 months; five recent colour photos; fingerprints (not needed if already given for an electronic card); a return envelope with tracking. The page also lists a police report for a lost card, and asks you to apply 60 days before travel.
- The two Brussels pages show different fees (USD 40 and USD 200). The Canada embassy (ethioembassy.ca) says a new website for new and renewal applications is coming. Ask your own embassy for its current fee.

**Law:** Proclamation No. 270/2002, Providing Foreign Nationals of Ethiopian Origin with Certain Rights to be Exercised in their Country of Origin. Listed on the Ministry of Justice site: https://justice.gov.et/en/law/providing-foreign-nationals-of-ethiopian-origin-with-certain-rights-to-be-exercised-in-their-country-of-origin-proclamation/ (text not copied here).

## What is not published
- The amount of the daily late-renewal penalty. Ask ICS (8133).
- A separate ICS checklist for renewal (the ICS page gives one list for the card). Bring your current card as well, and ask the office.
- Current embassy fees. The embassy pages are undated and do not match each other or the ICS fee. Ask your embassy.
- Fee for replacing a lost card at ICS in Ethiopia. Ask ICS.
