# ከውጭ አገር የሚላክ ውክልና — Power of attorney from abroad

## በአጭሩ
- ከኢትዮጵያ ውጭ የሚኖሩ ሰዎች በኢትዮጵያ ለሚገኝ ወኪላቸው ውክልና የሚሰጡት በውጭ ጉዳይ ሚኒስቴር የ"Digital MoFA" ኦንላይን አገልግሎት (digitalmofa.com ወይም የሞባይል መተግበሪያው) ነው።
- በዋሽንግተን ያለው የኢትዮጵያ ኤምባሲ ከሚያዝያ 2 ቀን 2026 (እ.ኤ.አ.) ጀምሮ ውክልናና የሰነድ ማረጋገጥ በአካል መስጠቱን አቁሟል፤ አገልግሎቱ በDigital MoFA ብቻ ይሰጣል። በሌሎች አገሮች ያለውን አሠራር ኤምባሲዎን ይጠይቁ።
- ደረጃዎቹ፦ (1) የሚኖሩበትን አገር ይምረጡ፤ (2) የወካዩን (የእርስዎን) መረጃ ይሙሉ፤ (3) የወኪሉን መረጃና የሚሰጡትን ሥልጣን ይጨምሩ፤ (4) የሚጀምርበትንና የሚያበቃበትን ቀን ይጨምሩ፣ ገምግመው ይፈርሙ፤ (5) ደጋፊ ሰነዶችን ይጫኑ፤ (6) የተረጋገጠውን ውክልና ይቀበሉ።
- ተቀባይነት ያላቸው መታወቂያዎች፦ ፓስፖርት፣ መንጃ ፈቃድ፣ የመኖሪያ ፈቃድ ካርድ፣ የስቴት መታወቂያ፣ የትውልደ ኢትዮጵያዊ መታወቂያ (ቢጫ ካርድ)፣ የውትድርና መታወቂያ።
- ኢትዮጵያውያን የኢትዮጵያ ፓስፖርት ወይም የትውልደ ኢትዮጵያዊ መታወቂያ ያስፈልጋቸዋል። ኢትዮጵያዊ ያልሆኑ ሰዎች ሁለት የመኖሪያ አገር መታወቂያዎች (ለምሳሌ መንጃ ፈቃድ፣ ግሪን ካርድ ወይም የውትድርና መታወቂያ) ማቅረብ ይችላሉ።
- ሂደቱ ከ24 እስከ 48 ሰዓት ይወስዳል (Digital MoFA)።
- ወኪሉ የተረጋገጠውን ውክልና (E-POA) ኦሊ ታወር ሕንፃ ውስጥ ካለው የውጭ ጉዳይ ሚኒስቴር ቢሮ የማመልከቻ መለያ ቁጥሩንና የሚሠራ መታወቂያ ይዞ ይረከባል።
- በአሜሪካ የተዘጋጀ ሰነድ ወደ Digital MoFA ከመጫኑ በፊት በአሜሪካ የውጭ ጉዳይ መሥሪያ ቤት (U.S. Department of State) መረጋገጥ አለበት (የዋሽንግተን ኤምባሲ)።
- በአዲስ አበባ ለቀበሌ/ነዋሪነት አገልግሎት (ለምሳሌ የ"ያላገባ" ማስረጃ) የሚቀርብ ከውጭ አገር የተላከ ውክልና በውጭ ጉዳይ ሚኒስቴርና በሰነዶች ማረጋገጫና ምዝገባ አገልግሎት (DARS) ተመዝግቦ የተረጋገጠ መሆን አለበት (መመሪያ 145/2015 አንቀጽ 26(5))።
- የDigital MoFA ክፍያ በገጹ ላይ አልተገለጸም።

## In short (English)
- People living outside Ethiopia give a power of attorney to an agent in Ethiopia through the Ministry of Foreign Affairs "Digital MoFA" online service (digitalmofa.com or its mobile app).
- The Ethiopian Embassy in Washington stopped in-person power-of-attorney and document-authentication services from 2 April 2026. These are now done only through Digital MoFA. Ask your own embassy what applies in your country.
- Steps: (1) select your country; (2) enter your details as the principal; (3) add the agent's details and the powers you give; (4) add the start and end dates, review and sign; (5) upload supporting documents; (6) receive the certified power of attorney.
- Accepted IDs: passport, driver's licence, resident permit card, state ID, Ethiopian Origin ID (yellow card), military ID.
- Ethiopians need an Ethiopian passport or an Origin ID. Non-Ethiopians may use two IDs from their country of residence, such as a driving licence, green card or military ID.
- Processing takes 24 to 48 hours (Digital MoFA).
- The agent collects the E-POA from the Ministry of Foreign Affairs office in the Oli Tower building, bringing the reference number and a valid ID.
- A document from the United States must first be authenticated by the U.S. Department of State before you upload it to Digital MoFA (Washington embassy).
- In Addis Ababa, a power of attorney made abroad and used for kebele/residency services (for example, an unmarried certificate) must be authenticated and registered by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and DARS (Directive 145/2015 Art. 26(5)).
- Digital MoFA does not show its fees.

## Official text
**Digital MoFA — Power of Attorney** — https://digitalmofa.com/power-of-attorney/ (Ministry of Foreign Affairs; viewed 17 Sept 2026)
- Steps: "Select your country" → "Fill up the Details of Principal who is granting the Power of Attorney" → "Add Details of Agent and Powers who do you wish to act on your behalf" → "Add Effective and Termination Date, Review, and Sign" → "Upload Supportive Documents" → "Receive Power of Attorney Certification".
- Accepted IDs: "Passport", "Driver License", "Resident Permit Card", "State ID", "Ethiopian Origin ID (Yellow Card)", "Military ID". Non-Ethiopians may use "two residence IDs like a driving license, green card or military ID."
- Processing: "24 up to 48 hrs".
- Collection: the agent receives the E-POA "From the ministry of foreign affairs inside the building of OLI tower"; "The agent needs the reference number and valid id".
- Apps: iOS https://apps.apple.com/us/app/digital-mofa/id1597040733 ; Android https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.viditure.mofa

**Embassy of Ethiopia, Washington DC** — https://ethiopianembassy.org/authentication-legalization/
> "Effective April 2, 2026, consular services previously provided in person, including: Power of Attorney [and] Document Authentication are no longer available for walk-in or physical processing at the Embassy." "These services are now rendered exclusively through the online system via the Digital MoFA application." "…any document originating from the United States must first be authenticated by the U.S. Department of State, Office of Authentications, before submission through the Digital MoFA system."

Older Washington page (https://ethiopianembassy.org/power-of-attorney/, undated, apparently replaced by the notice above) listed USD 62 per document for Ethiopians and Origin ID holders and USD 95 for others, sent by tracked mail.

**Proclamation No. 922/2015, Art. 22**: detailed procedures for documents "sent from other countries to Ethiopia shall be regulated by regulation to be issued by the Council of Ministers." Art. 9(b): a power of attorney has no legal effect unless authenticated and registered.

**Addis Ababa Directive No. 145/2015, Art. 26(5)**: "አመልካቾች ከኢትዮጲያ ውጪ የሚኖሩ ኢትዮጲያውያን እና ትውልደ ኢትዮጲያውያን ከሆኑና ጥያቄዉ በዉጭ አገር ዉክልና የቀረበ ሲሆን በውጭ ጉዳይ ሚኒስቴር እና በሰነዶች ማረጋገጫ እና ምዝገባ አገልግሎት ተመዝግቦ የተረጋገጠ መሆን አለበት፤" Art. 2(28) recognises powers of attorney given by an Ethiopian "ቆንጽላ ጽ/ቤት ወይም ኤምባሲ".

## What is not published
- Digital MoFA fees. Check the app at payment time or ask the embassy.
- Whether a Digital MoFA E-POA must also be registered at DARS before every use in Ethiopia. The Addis Ababa directive requires MoFA and DARS for residency services. For other uses, ask DARS or the office that will receive it.
- Procedures at embassies other than Washington. Ask your embassy.
