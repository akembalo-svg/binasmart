# Taxes on goods imported into Ethiopia

The Ethiopian Customs Commission (ECC) collects these taxes in this order. Duty, excise, VAT and sur-tax compound (each base includes the ones before it); the social welfare levy and withholding are on the CIF value only.

1. **Customs duty.** A percentage of the CIF value. The rate comes from the customs tariff book (revised 6 August 2021, about 8,000 lines, HS-based).
   - Six bands: 0%, 5%, 10%, 20%, 30% and 35%.
   - First-Schedule goods (raw materials, semi-finished goods, producer goods, public-use items): 0–10%.
   - Second-Schedule goods (consumer or finished goods for personal or non-productive use): 20–35%.
   - COMESA-origin goods can get a preferential rate. (USDA FAS ET2021-0002.)
2. **Excise tax.** Only on excisable goods (Excise Tax Proclamation 1186/2020 as amended by 1287/2023). The base is customs value + customs duty (1186/2020 Art. 7). Rates: see the excise file (5%–500% by item).
3. **VAT.** 15% of CIF + customs duty + excise (VAT Proclamation 1341/2024).
4. **Sur-tax.** 10% of **CIF + customs duty + excise + VAT** (Council of Ministers Regulation No. 133/2007). Some goods are exempt (for example fertilisers and petroleum, per USDA), as are goods imported by organisations exempt from customs duty.
5. **Social welfare levy** (Regulation No. 519/2022, in force 22 August 2022):
   - Levied on all goods in the customs tariff except those exempted under Art. 6.
   - Used for education, training, health and other social services.
   - The rate is 3%, "in addition to any other customs duties and taxes".
   - The base is the **CIF value** only, and the levy is not a base for any other tax.
   - Art. 6 exempts:
     - (1) persons and organisations with diplomatic privileges;
     - (2) **goods that are subject to the sur-tax under Regulation 133/2007**;
     - (3) goods exempted by a Ministry of Finance directive for social or economic reasons.
   - In practice an import pays **either** the 10% sur-tax **or** the 3% social welfare levy, not both.
   - Collected under Customs Proclamation 859/2014 (as amended).
6. **Withholding tax.** 3% of the CIF value on goods imported for commercial use. It is an advance payment credited against the importer's income tax for the year (USDA FAS; Income Tax Proclamation 979/2016).

## በአማርኛ (ለመልስ)
በገቢ ዕቃ ላይ በቅደም ተከተል የሚጣሉ ታክሶች፦
1. **የጉምሩክ ቀረጥ** — ከCIF ዋጋ 0%፣ 5%፣ 10%፣ 20%፣ 30% ወይም 35%፦
   - ጥሬ ዕቃና የካፒታል ዕቃ 0–10%
   - ለግል ጥቅም የሚውል የተጠናቀቀ ዕቃ 20–35%
2. **ኤክሳይዝ** — ለተወሰኑ ዕቃዎች ብቻ፤ በCIF + ቀረጥ ላይ፣ ምጣኔው እንደ ዕቃው (አዋጅ 1186/2012)።
3. **ተ.እ.ታ** — 15% በCIF + ቀረጥ + ኤክሳይዝ ላይ።
4. **ሰርታክስ** — 10% በCIF + ቀረጥ + ኤክሳይዝ + ተ.እ.ታ ላይ (ደንብ 133/2007)።
5. **የማኅበራዊ ደኅንነት ቀረጥ (Social Welfare Levy)** — 3% በCIF ዋጋ ላይ ብቻ (ደንብ ቁጥር 519/2014፣ 22 August 2022)። ሰርታክስ በሚከፈልበት ዕቃ ላይ አይጣልም፤ ስለዚህ አንድ ዕቃ ሰርታክስ ወይም ይህን ቀረጥ እንጂ ሁለቱንም አይከፍልም።
6. **ተቀናሽ ግብር (withholding)** — ለንግድ ለሚገባ ዕቃ ከCIF 3%፤ በዓመቱ የገቢ ግብር ላይ ይታሰባል።

ትክክለኛውን መጠን በECC የጉምሩክ ታክስ ካልኩሌተር ያረጋግጡ።
