# ለአገር ውስጥ ባለሀብቶች የተከለሉ የኢንቨስትመንት መስኮች — Investment Areas Reserved for Domestic Investors (Regulation 474/2020, Directive 1082/2025)

## በአጭሩ (Amharic summary)
- በኢንቨስትመንት አዋጅ 1180/2012 መሠረት ለመንግሥት፣ ለአገር ውስጥ ባለሀብቶች ወይም ከአገር ውስጥ ባለሀብት ጋር በጋራ ብቻ የተከለሉ መስኮች በደንብ ቁጥር 474/2012 ተዘርዝረዋል። ያልተዘረዘሩት ሁሉ ለውጭ ባለሀብቶች ክፍት ናቸው። ዝርዝሩን የኢትዮጵያ ኢንቨስትመንት ቦርድ ማሻሻል ይችላል።
- **ለአገር ውስጥ ባለሀብቶች ብቻ የተከለሉት (ደንብ 474፣ አንቀጽ 4)** 32 መስኮች ናቸው። ከነዚህ ውስጥ፦ ባንክ፣ መድን እና አነስተኛ ብድር (በሚመለከተው ሕግ መሠረት)፤ በብሔራዊ ግሪድ የኤሌክትሪክ ማስተላለፍና ማከፋፈል፤ የመጀመሪያና መካከለኛ ደረጃ የጤና አገልግሎት፤ የጅምላና የችርቻሮ ንግድ፤ የገቢ ንግድ፤ የቡና፣ ጫት፣ የቅባት እህል፣ ጥራጥሬ፣ ማዕድን፣ ቆዳና ሌጦ፣ የደን ውጤቶችና የቁም እንስሳት ወጪ ንግድ።
- ሌሎቹም፦ ከደረጃ 1 በታች የሆነ ግንባታና ቁፋሮ፤ ኮከብ ያልተሰጣቸው ሆቴሎች፣ ሎጆች፣ ሞቴሎች፣ እንግዳ ማረፊያዎች፣ ምግብ ቤቶች፣ ካፌዎችና ቡና ቤቶች፤ የጉዞ ወኪልና አስጎብኚ ድርጅት፤ አብዛኛው የትራንስፖርት አገልግሎት፤ ዳቦና ኬክ፣ ወፍጮ፣ ፀጉር ቤትና የውበት ሳሎን፣ ልብስ ስፌት፤ ጥገና፤ ሚዲያ፤ የጉምሩክ አስተላላፊነት፤ ብሎኬትና ጡብ፤ ሎተሪና ስፖርት ውርርድ፤ የጥበቃ አገልግሎት፤ ደላላነት፤ የጥብቅና አገልግሎት፤ የግል ሥራና ሠራተኛ አገናኝ ኤጀንሲ።
- **ከመንግሥት ጋር በጋራ ብቻ (አንቀጽ 3)**፦ የጦር መሣሪያና ፈንጂ ማምረት፤ የኤሌክትሪክ ኃይል ገቢና ወጪ ንግድ፤ ዓለም አቀፍ የአየር ትራንስፖርት፤ የፈጣን አውቶቡስ (BRT)፤ የፖስታ አገልግሎት (ከኩሪየር በስተቀር)።
- **የውጭ ባለሀብት ከአገር ውስጥ ባለሀብት ጋር በጋራ ብቻ (አንቀጽ 5)** የሚገባባቸው መስኮች፦ የጭነት አስተላላፊነትና የመርከብ ወኪል፤ የአገር ውስጥ አየር ትራንስፖርት፤ ከ45 በላይ ወንበር ባላቸው አውቶቡሶች የአገር አቋራጭ ትራንስፖርት፤ ከፍተኛ የመጫን አቅም ያለው የከተማ ትራንስፖርት፤ ማስታወቂያ፤ ኦዲዮቪዥዋል፤ የሂሳብ አያያዝና ኦዲት። በነዚህ መስኮች የውጭ ባለሀብቱ ድርሻ ከ49% መብለጥ አይችልም።
- **የ2017 ዓ.ም ለውጥ (የኢንቨስትመንት ቦርድ መመሪያ 1082/2017)**፦ ቀደም ሲል ለአገር ውስጥ ባለሀብቶች ብቻ የተከለሉት የወጪ፣ የገቢ፣ የጅምላና የችርቻሮ ንግድ መስኮች በቅድመ ሁኔታ ለውጭ ባለሀብቶች ተከፍተዋል።
- ወጪ ንግድ፦ የውጭ ባለሀብት ጥሬ ቡና፣ የቅባት እህል፣ ጫት፣ ጥራጥሬ፣ ቆዳና ሌጦ፣ የደን ውጤቶች፣ የዶሮና የቁም እንስሳት ወጪ ንግድ መሥራት ይችላል። ለቡና፣ ለቅባት እህል፣ ለጫትና ለጥራጥሬ ግን የታማኝነትና የአቅም ማጣሪያ (due diligence) ሪፖርት ማቅረብ አለበት።
- ገቢ ንግድ፦ ከማዳበሪያና ከነዳጅ በስተቀር ሁሉም ተከፍቷል (የማጣሪያ ሪፖርት ያስፈልጋል)። ጅምላ ንግድ፦ ከማዳበሪያ በስተቀር ሁሉም ተከፍቷል (የማጣሪያ ሪፖርት ያስፈልጋል)።
- ችርቻሮ ንግድ፦ ተከፍቷል። ፈቃድ የሚሰጠው ግን ቢያንስ 2,500,000 የአሜሪካ ዶላር የተከፈለ ካፒታል (በጥሬ ገንዘብና በንብረት) ሲኖር፣ እንዲሁም ኮሚሽኑ በሚመክረው አካል የተዘጋጀ የማጣሪያ ሪፖርት ሲቀርብ ነው። ለታዋቂ ነጠላ-ብራንድ ችርቻሮ ቦርዱ በአነስተኛ ካፒታል በየጉዳዩ ሊወስን ይችላል።
- በነዚህ መስኮች ማመልከቻ የሚቀበለውና የኢንቨስትመንት ፈቃድ የሚሰጠው የኢትዮጵያ ኢንቨስትመንት ኮሚሽን ነው። የንግድ ፈቃድ የሚሰጠው የንግድና ቀጣናዊ ትስስር ሚኒስቴር ነው።
- መመሪያ 1082/2017 የቀድሞውን መመሪያ 1001/2016 ሽሮታል።

## In short (English)
- Under Investment Proclamation 1180/2020, Regulation 474/2020 lists the areas reserved for the Government, for domestic investors, or for joint investment with domestic investors. Every area not on those lists is open to foreign investors, and the Ethiopian Investment Board may revise the lists.
- **32 areas are reserved exclusively for domestic investors (Art. 4).** They include banking, insurance and microfinance (subject to the relevant law); electricity transmission and distribution through the national grid; primary and middle-level health services; wholesale, retail and import trade; and exports of raw coffee, khat, oilseeds, pulses, minerals, hides and skins, natural forest products and livestock.
- The list continues: construction and drilling below Grade I; hotels, lodges, motels, guesthouses, restaurants, cafés and bars that are not star-designated; travel agencies and tour operation; most transport services; bakeries, grinding mills, barbershops, beauty salons and tailoring; repair services; media; customs clearance; brick and block making; lottery and sports betting; security services; brokerage; legal services; and private employment agencies.
- **Only jointly with the Government (Art. 3):** weapons and explosives; import and export of electricity; international air transport; bus rapid transit; and postal services other than courier.
- **Foreign investors only jointly with a domestic investor (Art. 5):** freight forwarding and shipping agency; domestic air transport; cross-country buses with more than 45 seats; large-capacity urban transport; advertising; audiovisual production; and accounting and auditing. In these areas the foreign share may not exceed 49%.
- **2025 opening (Investment Board Directive 1082/2025):** export, import, wholesale and retail trade that had been reserved for domestic investors are now open to foreign investors, subject to conditions.
- Export: foreign investors may export raw coffee, oilseeds, khat, pulses, hides and skins, forest products, poultry and livestock. For coffee, oilseeds, khat and pulses they must present a due-diligence report on their integrity and capacity.
- Import: all reserved import trade is open except fertilizer and petroleum, with a due-diligence report. Wholesale: all reserved wholesale is open except fertilizer, with a due-diligence report.
- Retail: open. A permit is issued only with at least **USD 2,500,000** paid-up capital (cash and assets) and a due-diligence report from an agency recommended by the Commission. The Board may decide case by case on reputable single-brand retail with less capital.
- The Ethiopian Investment Commission takes the applications and issues the investment permits. The Ministry of Trade and Regional Integration issues the business licences.
- Directive 1082/2025 repealed the earlier Directive 1001/2024.

## Official text
**Investment Proclamation No. 1180/2020** (Federal Negarit Gazette No. 28, 2 April 2020), Art. 6: "2/ Areas of investment reserved for joint investment with the Government, for domestic investors, and for joint investment with domestic investors shall be specified by Regulation. 3/ Except those reserved in accordance with Sub-article (2) of this Article, all areas of investment shall be open to foreign investors. 4/ The Board may revise the list of investment areas specified as per Sub-articles (2) and (3) of this Article as it deems it necessary."

**Investment Regulation No. 474/2020** (Federal Negarit Gazette No. 78, 2 September 2020; PDF https://justice.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/ደንብ-ቁጥር-474-2012.pdf):
- Art. 3: "Any investor may invest in the following investment areas only jointly with the Government: 1/ Manufacturing of weapons, ammunition and explosives used as weapons or to make weapons; 2/ Import and export of electrical energy; 3/ International air transport services; 4/ Bus rapid transit; and 5/ Postal services excluding courier services."
- Art. 4: "The following investment areas are exclusively reserved for domestic investors:" 1/ banking, insurance and microfinance, excluding capital goods finance; 2/ electricity transmission and distribution through the integrated national grid; 3/ primary and middle-level health services; 4/ "Wholesale trade, petroleum products, wholesale of own products produced in Ethiopia; excluding wholesale of electronic commerce" (as printed); 5/ "Retail trade excluding retail of electronic commerce as provided under appropriate law, of own products produced in Ethiopia" (as printed); 6/ "Import trade, excluding liquefied petroleum gas and bitumen"; 7/ "Export trade of raw coffee, khat, oil seeds, pulses, minerals, hides and skins, products of natural forest, chicken, and livestock including pack animals bought on the market"; 8/ "Construction and drilling services below Grade I"; 9/ hotels, lodges, resorts, motels, guesthouses and pensions, excluding star-designated ones; 10/ restaurants, tearooms, coffee shops, bars, nightclubs and catering, excluding star-designated national cuisine restaurants; 11/ travel agency, ticket sales and trade auxiliary services; 12/ tour operation; 13/ operating lease of equipment, machinery and vehicles (except industry-specific heavy equipment and specialized vehicles); 14/ "Transport services, excluding" railway, cable-car, cold-chain, freight over 25 tonnes, and transport reserved for joint investment; 15/ indigenous traditional medicines; 16/ bakery products and pastries for the domestic market; 17/ grinding mills; 18/ barbershops and beauty salons, smithery, and tailoring except by garment factories; 19/ maintenance and repair, including aircraft MRO, but excluding heavy industry machinery and medical equipment; 20/ aircraft ground handling; 21/ saw milling, timber and semi-finished wood products; 22/ media services; 23/ customs clearance; 24/ brick and block manufacturing; 25/ quarrying; 26/ lottery and sports betting; 27/ laundry, except on an industrial scale; 28/ translation and secretarial services; 29/ security services; 30/ brokerage; 31/ attorney and legal consultancy; 32/ private employment agencies, except for seafarers and similar highly skilled professionals.
- Art. 5(1): foreign investors only jointly with a domestic investor: "a) Freight forwarding and shipping agency services; b) Domestic air transport service; c) Cross-country public transport service using buses with a seating capacity of more than 45 passengers; d) Urban mass transport service with large carrying capacity; e) Advertisement and promotion services; f) Audiovisual services; motion picture and video recording, production and distribution; and g) Accounting and Auditing services." 5(2): the foreign investor "shall not hold more than 49% of the share capital".
- Art. 6: "Investment areas not listed under Articles 3, 4 and 5 herein are open to foreign investors."

**Ethiopian Investment Board Directive No. 1082/2025** (PDF via https://justice.gov.et/am/directives/ethiopian-investment-board-directive-to-regulate-foreign-investors-participation-in-restricted-export-import-wholesale-and-retail-trade-investments/):
- Art. 5(1): "any foreign investor can engage in the export trade investment of raw coffee, oilseeds, khat, pulses, hides and skins, forest products, and poultry and livestock bought on the market." 5(2): for raw coffee, oilseeds, khat and pulses, "when it presents a comprehensive due diligence report prepared by itself or a recognized national or international verification agency, detailing the investor’s integrity and capacity."
- Art. 6(1): "except for fertilizer and petroleum import trade, any foreign investor can engage in all import trade investments reserved for domestic investors under the Regulation."
- Art. 7(1): "except for the wholesale of fertilizers, any foreign investor can engage in wholesale trade investment of all sectors reserved for domestic investors under the Regulation."
- Art. 8(2): a retail investor gets a permit when "a) the investor raises a paid-up capital of at least USD Two Million Five Hundred Thousand (2,500,000) in cash and assets; and b) the investor presents a comprehensive due diligence report prepared by a recognized national or international verification agency recommended by the Commission". 8(4): the Board may decide case by case on "reputable single brand retail trade operating on a smaller capital."
- Art. 11(1): Directive No. 1001/2024 "is hereby repealed." Art. 12: in force on registration with the Ministry of Justice and upload to the Commission's website.

## What is not published
- The date Directive 1082/2025 took effect is not printed in the text. Ask the Ethiopian Investment Commission.
- The Board may revise the reserved lists (Proclamation 1180, Art. 6(4)). Apart from Directive 1082/2025, no other revision was found. Banking, insurance and other sectors governed by their own laws may follow different rules, so ask the regulator concerned (for banks, the National Bank of Ethiopia).
- Which verification agencies the Commission recommends for due-diligence reports is not listed. Ask the Commission.
