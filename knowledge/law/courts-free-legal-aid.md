# ነፃ የሕግ ድጋፍ አገልግሎት — Free Legal Aid in Ethiopia

## በአጭሩ (Amharic summary)

- ነፃ የሕግ ድጋፍ ማለት በነፃ ወይም በጣም አነስተኛ ዋጋ የሚሰጥ የሕግ አገልግሎት ነው፦ ምክር፣ ለፍርድ ቤት የሚቀርቡ ጽሑፎችን ማዘጋጀት፣ ወክሎ መከራከር፣ ማስማማት ወዘተ።
- አገልግሎቱን የሚሰጡት፦ ፍትሕ ሚኒስቴር፣ ጠበቆችና የጥብቅና ድርጅቶች፣ የዩኒቨርሲቲ የሕግ ትምህርት ቤቶች እና እንደ ኢትዮጵያ ሴት የሕግ ባለሙያዎች ማኅበር ያሉ ማኅበራት ናቸው።
- ፍትሕ ሚኒስቴር በፌደራል ፍርድ ቤቶች የፍትሐብሔር ክስ ለማቅረብ የገንዘብ አቅም የሌላቸውን ዜጎች፣ በተለይም ሴቶችን፣ ሕፃናትን፣ አካል ጉዳተኞችንና አረጋውያንን ወክሎ ይከራከራል።
- የወንጀል ክስ ለቀረበበትና ጠበቃ መቅጠር ለማይችል ሰው፦ በፌደራል ጠቅላይ ፍርድ ቤት ሥር ያለው የተከላካይ ጠበቆች ጽሕፈት ቤት በፍርድ ቤቱ ትዕዛዝ ነፃ ውክልና ይሰጣል። ጉዳዩ በሚታይበት ችሎት ተከላካይ ጠበቃ እንዲቆምልዎ ይጠይቁ።
- ነፃ ጠበቃ ለማግኘት፦ የገንዘብ አቅም እንደሌለዎት የሚያሳይ፣ በምስክሮች የተረጋገጠ ማስረጃ ከሚኖሩበት ቀበሌ ማቅረብ አለብዎት።
- ቀደም ሲል በፍርድ ቤት ውሳኔ የተሰጠበትን ጉዳይ ይዘው መቅረብ አይችሉም።
- ነፃ ጠበቃ ቢመደብልዎትም የዳኝነት ክፍያ፣ የቴምብር ቀረጥና የፎቶ ኮፒ ወጪዎችን እርስዎ ይከፍላሉ። ሆኖም ፍርድ ቤቱ በድሃ ደንብ እንዲከራከሩ ከፈቀደልዎ ከዳኝነት ክፍያ ነፃ ይሆናሉ (ደንብ 1/2017 አንቀጽ 21(4))።
- በግል የሚሠራ ጠበቃ በዓመት "ከሦስት ላልበለጡ" ጉዳዮች ነፃ የጥብቅና አገልግሎት የመስጠት ግዴታ አለበት (አዋጅ 1249/2013 አንቀጽ 31(1))። ጉዳዮቹን ለጠበቆች የሚመድበው ፍትሕ ሚኒስቴር ነው።
- በተመደበልዎ ጠበቃ ላይ ችግር ካለ ለፍትሕ ሚኒስቴር ያመልክቱ።
- ፍትሕ ሚኒስቴር፦ ስልክ +251 115 51 50 99 (011 551 5099)፣ ባምቢስ ፊት ለፊት፣ አዲስ አበባ፤ ሰኞ–አርብ 8:30–5:30።
- ዩኒቨርሲቲዎች (አዲስ አበባ፣ አርባ ምንጭ፣ አሶሳ፣ ደባርቅ፣ ጋምቤላ፣ ጅግጅጋ፣ መቀሌ፣ ሰመራ) ነፃ የሕግ ድጋፍ ማዕከላት አሏቸው፤ በአብዛኛው ምክርና ክስ/መልስ መጻፍ ይሰጣሉ።
- የሰብዓዊ መብት ጥሰትን ለኢትዮጵያ ሰብዓዊ መብቶች ኮሚሽን በነፃ ስልክ 7307 ማሳወቅ ይቻላል (ኮሚሽኑ የጠበቃ አገልግሎት አይሰጥም)።

## In short (English)

- Free legal aid means legal services given free or at very low cost: advice, drafting court documents, representation, conciliation and similar.
- Providers named by the MoJ: the Ministry of Justice, advocates and law firms, university law schools, and associations such as the Ethiopian Women Lawyers Association.
- The MoJ represents citizens who cannot afford to bring civil suits in the federal courts, especially women, children, persons with disabilities and the elderly.
- Criminal cases: the Federal Supreme Court's Public Defenders' Office gives free representation to indigent accused persons on the order of the court. Ask the bench hearing your case for a public defender.
- To get a free lawyer you must bring witnessed proof from your kebele that you cannot afford one.
- A case already decided by a court will not be taken.
- Even with a free lawyer, you pay court fees, stamp duty and photocopy costs — unless the court allows you to sue as a pauper, which exempts you from court fees (Fee Regulation 1/2024, Art. 21(4)).
- Private advocates must give pro bono service "for not more than three cases in a year" (Proclamation 1249/2021, Art. 31(1)); the MoJ assigns these cases.
- Report problems with your assigned lawyer to the MoJ.
- Ministry of Justice: +251 115 51 50 99 (011 551 5099), in front of Bambis, Addis Ababa; Mon–Fri 8:30 AM–5:30 PM; info@moj.gov.et.
- University legal aid centres exist at Addis Ababa, Arba Minch, Assosa, Debark, Gambella, Jigjiga, Mekelle and Samara universities; they mainly advise and draft pleadings and cannot appear in court.
- Human-rights violations can be reported to the Ethiopian Human Rights Commission, toll-free 7307 (the EHRC is not described as a legal-aid provider).

### Federal Supreme Court — Public Defenders' Office (English, verbatim)
Source: https://www.fsc.gov.et/Court-Services/Legal-Support/Public-Defenders-Office
- "The Public Defenders’ Office organized under the Federal Supreme Court is mandated to provide free legal representation & services to indigent persons, who can’t afford to have representation as per the order of the bench/court."
- Services: "Counseling Services / Legal Representation in Court / Assist in the Preparation of Court Documents" (described for criminal defendants: bail, trial, appeal and cassation).
- No separate phone is shown; use the Federal Supreme Court line +251-11-1-56-55-71 or short code 992.

### Proclamation No. 1249/2021 (English, verbatim)
Source: https://justice.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/አዋጅ-ቁጥር-1249-2013.pdf

**Art. 31(1).** "Every advocate who practices privately or works in a law firm either as a partner or as an employee has the obligation to provide pro bono advocacy service, for not more than three cases in a year, based on the simplicity or the complexity of each case. And the service is provided to the following persons: a) to those persons who cannot afford to pay for the advocacy service; b) to charity organizations, civic societies and mass institutions; c) to those persons whom courts request the provision of pro bono advocacy service; d) to committees and organizations which work on the advancement, promotion and development of law and improvement of the justice system."

**Art. 32(1).** "The Attorney General shall, without passing the limit each advocate should render pro bono service in a given year, identify and assign pro bono cases to advocates;" (These Attorney General functions now sit with the Ministry of Justice.)

### Court Fee Regulation No. 1/2024, Art. 21(4) (English, verbatim)

## What is not published
