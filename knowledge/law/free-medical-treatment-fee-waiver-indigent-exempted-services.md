# ነጻ ሕክምና — Free medical treatment and fee waivers

## በአጭሩ

- **መሠረታዊ ሕግ:** በአዋጅ 1362/2017 መሠረት የጤና አገልግሎት በክፍያ ይሰጣል። ነገር ግን **በጤና መድን ያልተሸፈነና ከኪስ መክፈል የማይቻል** የሕክምና ወጪ በመንግሥት ወይም በሌላ አካል ይሸፈናል (አንቀጽ 49(4)–(5))።
- **የአስቸኳይ ሕክምና:** ማንኛውም የጤና ተቋም የአስቸኳይና ሕይወት አድን ሕክምና የመስጠት ግዴታ አለበት (አንቀጽ 10(1))።
- **ለሁሉም ሰው ነጻ የሆኑ አገልግሎቶች:** በፌዴራልና በዩኒቨርሲቲ ሆስፒታሎች "ከክፍያ ነጻ አገልግሎቶች" ገቢ ሳይታይ ለሁሉም በነጻ ይሰጣሉ፤ ምክንያቱም የሕዝብ ጤና ጉዳዮች ናቸው (መመሪያ 920/2014 አንቀጽ 3(7))። ተጠቃሚዎቹ የምርመራ (ካርድ) ክፍያ አይከፍሉም (አንቀጽ 7(4))።
- በዚሁ መመሪያ መሠረት **የምግብ እጥረት (malnutrition)** እና **የሥጋ ደዌ (leprosy)** ሕክምና በተመላላሽም ሆነ በተኝቶ ሕክምና ነጻ ነው። በዘመቻ ወይም በሌላ አካል የተሸፈኑ አገልግሎቶችም ያለክፍያ ይሰጣሉ (አንቀጽ 14)። ሌላ ተጓዳኝ ሕመም ካለ ግን ለዚያ አገልግሎት ይከፈላል (አንቀጽ 14(3))።
- **ተመሳሳይ ሕመም ክትትል:** በአንድ ወር ውስጥ ለክትትል ተመልሰው ሲመጡ የምርመራ ክፍያ እንደገና አይከፍሉም (አንቀጽ 7(3))።
- **መክፈል የማይችሉ ሰዎች (ድሆች) — የጤና መድን:** ወረዳው መክፈል የማይችሉ ቤተሰቦችን ይለያል (አዋጅ 1273 አንቀጽ 2(11))። እነዚህ ቤተሰቦች በማኅበረሰብ አቀፍ ጤና መድን ይመዘገባሉ፤ ዓመታዊ መዋጮአቸውን ክልሉ/ወረዳው **ሙሉ በሙሉ** ይከፍላል (መመሪያ 1092 አንቀጽ 3(3)፣ 6(7))። የፎቶ ወጪያቸውንም ወረዳው ይሸፍናል (አንቀጽ 4(3))።
- የድሆች ተጠቃሚዎች **ከተጋሪ ክፍያ (co-payment) ነጻ** ናቸው (ደንብ 535 አንቀጽ 15(2))።
- **ምን ማድረግ አለብዎት:** ① ወደ ቀበሌ/ወረዳ አስተዳደር ሄደው መክፈል የማይችሉ መሆንዎን ያስለዩ። ② በማኅበረሰብ አቀፍ ጤና መድን በምዝገባ ጊዜ ይመዝገቡ (በመስከረም 1 እና በየካቲት 30 መካከል፣ ክልሉ በሚወስነው 2 ወር)። ③ የአባልነት መታወቂያዎን ይዘው **በሪፈራል ሥርዓቱ** ከጤና ጣቢያ ይጀምሩ።
- ቤት አልባ ለሆኑ ወይም በአምልኮ ቦታዎች አካባቢ ለሚኖሩ ሰዎች የክልሉ የሴቶችና ማኅበራዊ ጉዳይ ቢሮ ሰነድ ያዘጋጃል፣ መዋጮ ይከፍላል፣ ያስመዘግባል (ደንብ 535 አንቀጽ 7(3))።
- በሆስፒታል ቅሬታ ካለዎት፦ ማንኛውም የጤና ተቋም የቅሬታ ማስተናገጃ ሥርዓት ሊኖረው ይገባል (አዋጅ 1362 አንቀጽ 5(6))።

## In short (English)

- **Basic rule:** under Proclamation 1362/2024, health services are fee-based. Care costs that are **not covered by health insurance and cannot be paid out of pocket** are to be covered by the government or another body (Art. 49(4)–(5)).
- **Emergencies:** every health institution must provide emergency and life-saving care (Art. 10(1)).
- **Services free for everyone:** at federal and university hospitals, "exempted health services" are free for all, whatever their income, because they are public-health services (Directive 920/2022, Art. 3(7)). No consultation fee is charged for them (Art. 7(4)).
- The same directive makes **malnutrition** and **leprosy** care free for outpatients and inpatients. Services paid for by a campaign or another party are also free (Art. 14). Treatment for another illness at the same visit is charged (Art. 14(3)).
- **Revisit:** a follow-up visit within one month needs no new examination fee (Art. 7(3)).
- **People who cannot pay (indigents) — via CBHI:** the woreda identifies households that cannot afford the contribution (Proclamation 1273/2022, Art. 2(11)). They are enrolled in community-based health insurance, and the regional/woreda targeted subsidy covers their **full** annual contribution (Directive 1092, Arts. 3(3), 6(7)). The woreda also pays for their ID photos (Art. 4(3)). Indigents are **exempt from co-payment** (Regulation 535/2023, Art. 15(2)).
- **Steps:** ① go to your kebele/woreda administration and ask to be identified as unable to pay; ② enrol in CBHI during your region's two-month window (between Meskerem 1 and Yekatit 30); ③ use your membership card and follow the **referral system**, starting at the health centre.
- For homeless people and people living in and around places of worship, the regional women and social affairs bureau (or the body in charge) prepares documents, pays the fee and registers them (Regulation 535, Art. 7(3)).
- Complaints: every health institution must have a grievance handling system (Proclamation 1362, Art. 5(6)).

## Official text

**Proclamation No. 1362/2024 (Health Service Administration and Regulation)** — https://justice.gov.et/en/law/health-service-administration-and-regulation-proclamation-no-1362-2024/
> Art. 10: [every health institution shall] 1/ provide emergency and life-saving medical services;
> Art. 49: The Ministry shall: ... 4/ Establish a system where all health services are provided for a fee; 5/ Without prejudice to Sub-Article (4) of this Article, the expenses of health care services which are not covered by health insurance and cannot be paid out of pocket shall be covered by the government or by any other body as the case may be;

**Directive No. 920/2022 — Federal and University Hospitals User Fee Implementation Directive** (Ministry of Finance, 19 July 2022; PDF https://justice.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/Federal-and-University-Hospitals-User-Fee-Implementation-Directive-No.-920-2022.pdf)
> Art. 3(7): "Exempted Health Services" means health services provided free of charge to all people irrespective of level of income by reason of them being public health nature that widely affect the general public and improving the health seeking behavior of the society.
> Art. 7(3): If the service user comes to the hospital for a revisit consultation within one month, he or she can receive the service without having to pay another examination fee. (4) ... exempted health services beneficiaries will not be charged consultation fee.
> Art. 14: 1/ In addition to the list of exempted health service in the regulation, malnutrition shall be provided free of charge in outpatient and inpatient department. 2/ ... leprosy case shall also be provided free of charge. 3/ ... if the patient come to the health facility for co-morbidity service, users shall be required to pay the fee of the respective service ... 4/ Service costs covered by the campaign and other party; the service shall be provided without any charge.

**Proclamation No. 1273/2022 (CBHI)**
> Art. 2(11): "Indigent" means household head or a person identified by the Woreda administration as being unable to afford annual membership contribution according to the income amount criteria;
> Art. 15(5): The annual contribution of indigents shall be covered through targeted subsidy.

**Regulation No. 535/2023**
> Art. 15(2): Beneficiaries who adhere to the referral system, patients on medical appointments, emergency patients, and indigents are exempted from co-payment.

**EHIS Directive No. 1092/2017 E.C.**
> አንቀጽ 4(3)፦ መክፈል የማይችሉ የህብረተሰብ ክፍሎች የፎቶግራፍ መነሻ ወጪ የወረዳው አስተዳደር ይሸፍናል።
> አንቀጽ 6(7)፦ ... ክልሉ የሚመድበው የተናጠል ድጎማ በጀት ... መክፈል የማይችሉ የህብረተሰብ ክፍሎችን መዋጮ ሙሉ በሙሉ የሚሸፍን መሆን ይኖርበታል።

## What is not published

- **Full list of exempted (free) services:** Directive 920 points to a fee regulation whose number is left blank, and that regulation was not found. Ask the hospital's finance or social-work office which services are free.
- **An individual "fee waiver" letter** outside CBHI (the procedure, the office that issues it, the income criteria): not found in official sources. Ask your woreda administration or the hospital social worker.
- **Addis Ababa city hospitals and health centres:** their fee and exemption rules were not found (the Addis Ababa Health Bureau site was unreachable). Ask the Addis Ababa Health Bureau or the facility.
- **Income criteria for "indigent":** set per region and not published centrally.
