# በአዲስ አበባ የግል ቤት ወይም ቦታ (ከጋራ መኖሪያ ቤት ውጭ) ካርታ በሽያጭ ለገዢ ማዛወር — Transferring an ordinary house or land title deed to a buyer in Addis Ababa (not condominiums)

This page covers ordinary houses and plots (ካርታ / ይዞታ ማረጋገጫ ደብተር). Condominium units follow Addis Ababa Directive 66/2013 and are covered on a separate page (addis-condominium-title-name-transfer-directive-66-2013).

## በአጭሩ

- **ሕጉ ተቀይሯል፦** የከተማ መሬት ይዞታ ምዝገባ አዋጅ ቁጥር 818/2006 ተሽሯል። በምትኩ የከተማ መሬት ይዞታ እና መሬት ነክ ንብረት ምዝገባ አዋጅ ቁጥር 1381/2017 ሐምሌ 11 ቀን 2017 ዓ.ም. (July 18, 2025) በነጋሪት ጋዜጣ ታትሞ ሥራ ላይ ውሏል (አንቀጽ 61፣ 62)። ከአዋጁ በፊት የተጀመሩ ጉዳዮች በቀድሞው ሕግ ይቀጥላሉ (አንቀጽ 60)።
- **1ኛ ደረጃ – የሽያጭ ውል በሰነዶች ማረጋገጫና ምዝገባ አገልግሎት (DARS)፦** በአዲስ አበባ ሰነድ የሚያረጋግጠው የፌዴራሉ ተቋም ነው (አዋጅ 922/2008 አንቀጽ 4)። የቤት ሽያጭ ውል በተቋሙ ፊት በሁለት ምስክሮች ይፈረማል (አንቀጽ 17)።
- ከማረጋገጡ በፊት ኖተሪው ሻጩ ካርታ እንዳለው፣ ንብረቱ በብድር መያዣ ወይም በፍርድ ቤት እግድ ሥር እንዳልሆነ እና ፈራሚው ሥልጣን እንዳለው ያረጋግጣል (አንቀጽ 15)። የቴምብር ቀረጥ መከፈሉንም ያረጋግጣል (አንቀጽ 34)።
- **የቴምብር ቀረጥ፦** ለንብረት ባለቤትነት ምዝገባ ከንብረቱ ዋጋ 2% ነው (አዋጅ 110/1990 ሠንጠረዥ)። ሌላ ስምምነት ከሌለ የሚከፍለው ገዢው ነው (አንቀጽ 6(5))። ዋጋው ሁለቱ ወገኖች የተስማሙበት ሆኖ በግብር ባለሥልጣኑ ተቀባይነት ማግኘት አለበት (አንቀጽ 5(6))።
- **ዋጋ አሳንሶ መጻፍ ወንጀል ነው፦** የፍትሕ ሚኒስቴር ታኅሣሥ 20 ቀን 2015 ዓ.ም. ባወጣው ዜና መሠረት፣ ቤቶችን በ7.7 እና በ17 ሚሊዮን ብር ሸጣ በውሉ ላይ 1 ሚሊዮን ብር ብላ የጻፈች ሰው በታክስ አስተዳደር አዋጅ ተከሳለች።
- **የካፒታል ሀብት ማስተላለፍ ግብር (ሻጩ)፦** ከሐምሌ 1 ቀን 2017 ዓ.ም. (July 8, 2025) ጀምሮ መጣኔው 15% ነው። ጥቅሙ የሚሰላው፦ የተላለፈበት ዋጋ − (የተገዛበት ዋጋ + 30% የዋጋ ንረት ማስተካከያ) (አዋጅ 1395/2017 አንቀጽ 2(23))።
- ቤቱ ከመሸጡ በፊት ለ2 ዓመት ሙሉ በሙሉ ለመኖሪያነት ያገለገለ ከሆነ ግብር የሚከፈልበት ሀብት አይደለም። ግንባታው ያልተጠናቀቀ ወይም ለንግድ ያገለገለ ቤት ግን 15% ይከፍላል (መመሪያ 210/2013 አንቀጽ 10)።
- በአዲስ አበባ ግብሩን የሚሰበስበው የአዲስ አበባ ከተማ አስተዳደር የገቢዎች ባለሥልጣን ሊሆን ይችላል (መመሪያ 210/2013 አንቀጽ 3(7))። ግብሩ በቀጥታ ለባለሥልጣኑ ይከፈላል (አንቀጽ 15)። በውርስ ሲተላለፍ ይህ ግብር አይከፈልም (አንቀጽ 14)። በስጦታ ሲተላለፍ ግብሩን የሚከፍለው ስጦታ ተቀባዩ ነው (አንቀጽ 13)።
- **የታክስ ክሊራንስ፦** ግብር የሚከፈልበትን የካፒታል ሀብት ለማስተላለፍ የታክስ ክሊራንስ ይሰጣል፤ ከመሰጠቱ በፊትም ማጣራት ይደረጋል (መመሪያ 180/2015 አንቀጽ 4(7)፣ 8(1))።
- **የሊዝ ግዴታ፦** በነባር (ከሊዝ በፊት በተያዘ) ይዞታ ላይ ያለ ንብረት በውርስ ካልሆነ በሌላ መንገድ ሲተላለፍ፣ ገዢው ይዞታውን የሚይዘው በሊዝ ነው (አዋጅ 721/2004 አንቀጽ 6(3))። የሊዝ ይዞታ ሲተላለፍ የሊዝ ውሉ ግዴታዎች በሙሉ ወደ ገዢው ይተላለፋሉ (አንቀጽ 24(8))።
- **2ኛ ደረጃ – በመሬት ይዞታ መዝጋቢ ተቋም ማስመዝገብ፦** በሽያጭ የተገኘ መብት መመዝገብ አለበት (አዋጅ 1381 አንቀጽ 33(2))። ገዢው መብቱን ያገኘበትን ሰነድ ያቀርባል (አንቀጽ 38(2))። ማመልከቻው በአካል ወይም በኢንተርኔት ሊቀርብ ይችላል (አንቀጽ 32(3))። ቀድሞ የተመዘገበ መብት ቅድሚያ ስላለው ቶሎ ማስመዝገብ ይጠቅማል (አንቀጽ 46)።
- የአዲስ አበባ ተቋም ዝርዝር የሰነድ ዝርዝር፣ ክፍያና የሚፈጅበት ጊዜ በይፋ አልተገኘም። ቢሮውን ይጠይቁ።

## In short (English)

- **The law changed:** Urban Landholding Registration Proclamation 818/2014 was repealed by Proclamation 1381/2025. The new law was published on 18 July 2025 and took effect on publication (Arts. 61–62). Work that started before it continues under the old law (Art. 60).
- **Step 1 – the sale contract at DARS:** in Addis Ababa, documents are authenticated by the federal authentication and registration institution (Proc. 922/2015 Art. 4). A contract transferring immovable property by sale is signed before the institution with **two witnesses** (Art. 17).
- Before authenticating, the notary checks three things: the seller holds a title certificate, the property is not mortgaged, pledged or under a court attachment, and the signatory has authority (Art. 15). The notary also checks that stamp duty has been paid (Art. 34).
- **Stamp duty:** registering title to property costs **2% of its value** (Proc. 110/1998 Schedule, item 13). Unless the parties agree otherwise, the **buyer** pays (Art. 6(5)). The value is the one the parties agreed, but the tax authority must accept it (Art. 5(6)).
- **Under-declaring the price is prosecuted.** In a news item dated ታኅሣሥ 20, 2015 E.C., the Ministry of Justice reported charges against a seller. She wrote Birr 1,000,000 in the contracts for houses actually sold for Birr 7.7 million and Birr 17 million.
- **Capital gains tax (seller):** on income from 8 July 2025, the rate is **15%**. The gain is the transfer price minus (cost + a 30% inflation adjustment) (Proc. 1395/2025 Art. 2(23), Amharic text).
- A building held and used wholly as a private residence for **2 years** before the sale is not a taxable asset (same article). A building that is unfinished, or that was licensed and used for business, is taxed at 15% (Directive 210/2013 Art. 10).
- In Addis Ababa, the tax authority may be the city's Revenue Authority (Directive 210/2013 Art. 3(7)). The tax is paid directly to the authority (Art. 15). The authority accepts the price in the DARS contract only if it matches current market prices (Art. 8(5)).
- Inheritance is exempt (Art. 14). For a gift, the recipient pays (Art. 13). If the sale contract is cancelled, you can ask for a refund within one year of paying the tax (Art. 17).
- **Tax clearance:** a clearance certificate is issued for transferring a taxable capital asset, after the tax office has checked the case (MoR Directive 180/2015 Arts. 4(7), 8(1)).
- **Lease:** when property on an old (pre-lease) holding is transferred other than by inheritance, the new holder holds the land under **lease** (Proc. 721/2011 Art. 6(3)).
- A leaseholder can transfer the leasehold only up to the lease amount already paid (Art. 24(1)). All obligations in the lease contract pass to the buyer (Art. 24(8)). If annual lease payments fall three years behind, the property can be seized and sold (Art. 20(6)).
- **Step 2 – registration at the land registration institution:** rights created by sale must be registered (Proc. 1381/2025 Art. 33(2)). The buyer submits the documents through which the right was acquired (Art. 38(2)). Applications can be made in person or online (Art. 32(3)).
- The certificate is issued in the new holder's name (Art. 36). If two people buy from the same seller, whoever registers first has priority (Art. 46).
- Fees follow a cost-sharing principle and a federal formula (Art. 57). The actual Addis Ababa fee amounts were not found.

## Official text

**Proclamation No. 1381/2025** (Federal Negarit Gazette 31st Year No. 56, 18 July 2025) — https://justice.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/አዋጅ-ቁጥር-1381-2017-1.pdf
- Art. 61(1): "The Urban Landholding Registration Proclamation No. 818/2014 are hereby repealed." Amharic: "…አዋጅ ቁጥር ፰፻፲፰/፪ሺ፮ በዚህ አዋጅ ተሽሯል፡፡"
- Art. 33(2): "All rights, restrictions, and responsibilities created through total or partial sale, donation, inheritance, assignment of share, contribution in kind or other act or event as permitted by this proclamation and regulations and directives to be issued hereunder shall be registered."
- Art. 32(3): "The land holder can submit applications to request certificate of registration in person or using the Internet."
- Art. 38(2): "Any person who has acquired a right … shall submit the documents based on which the transferee acquired the right to be registered while applying for registration."

**Proclamation No. 922/2015** — https://justice.gov.et/en/law/authentication-and-registration-of-documents-proclamation/
- Art. 15: before authenticating a transfer of titled property the notary shall ascertain that "1/ the transferor of the property has title certificate for the property in accordance with the relevant law; 2/ the property is not mortgaged or pledged or is not attached by a court order; 3/ the person who has signed or is about to sign the document has legal or contractual right or authority to sign the document."
- Art. 17(1): "…the following documents shall be signed before the relevant authentication and registration institution by two witnesses: a) contracts of transfer of ownership of immovable properties by selling or donation;" Amharic: "ሀ) የማይንቀሳቀሱ ንብረቶችን ባለቤትነት በሽያጭ ወይም በስጦታ ለማስተላለፍ የሚደረጉ ውሎች፤"
- Art. 34(1): "The notary shall make sure the payment of stump duty pursuant to the relevant law before authenticating and registering a document."

**Stamp Duty Proclamation No. 110/1998** — https://justice.gov.et/en/law/stamp-duty-proclamation/
- Schedule item 13: "Register title to property — on value — 2%".
- Art. 6(5): "The transferee shall, unless otherwise agreed, be liable for the payment of stamp duty chargeable on documents transferring title to property."

**Income Tax (Amendment) Proclamation No. 1395/2025**, Art. 2(23) (new Art. 61) — https://justice.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/አዋጅ-ቁጥር-1395-2017.pdf
- Amharic: "(፪) … መጣኔ 15% (አስራ አምስት በመቶ) ይሆናል። (፫) … ሐ - (ሀ + ለ) = ግብር የሚከፈልበት ጥቅም … ሀ - ሀብቱ የተፈራበት/የተገዛበት ዋጋ ለ- የዋጋ ንረት ማስተካከያ 30% (ሠላሳ በመቶ) ሐ - ሀብቱ የተላለፈበት ዋጋ (፯) … “ግብር የሚከፈልበት ሃብት” ማለት አክሲዮን፣ ቦንድ እና ቤት ሲሆን፤ ቤትን በሚመለከት ቤቱ ከመተላለፉ በፊት ለ፪ (ሁለት) አመታት ሙሉ በሙሉ ለመኖሪያነት ያገለገለ ህንጻን አይጨምርም።"
- English: "(7) … 'taxable asset' means shares, bonds and buildings but with respect to buildings does not include a building held for and wholly used as a private residence for 2 (Two) years prior to the disposal of the asset." In the English column's formula, the letters are swapped (A is the consideration and C is the cost). The Amharic text is quoted above.

**Income Tax Regulation No. 410/2017**, Art. 55 (OCR): for capital gain tax, "the transferor is treated as having disposed of the asset and the transferee is treated as having acquired the asset at the time the contract of sale, exchange or gift is registered by an entity empowered to exercise the function of the notary."

**Ministry of Finance Directive No. 210/2013** (effective ነሐሴ 1, 2011 E.C.; amended by 267/2013) — https://justice.gov.et/am/directives/implementation-of-tax-on-gains-from-the-transfer-of-capital-assets-directive-no-210-2013/ (the source uses old letter forms, corrected here)
- Art. 10(1): "ግንባታው ያልተጠናቀቀ ወይም ሁለት አመት በመኖሪያነት ያላገለገለ የማይንቀሳቀስ ሀብት የተላለፈ እንደሆነ በማስተላለፍ ከሚገኝ ጥቅም ላይ በአዋጁ መሰረት 15 በመቶ ግብር ይከፈልበታል፡፡"
- Its Art. 5 (15% immovable, 30% shares/bonds) predates the single 15% rate of Proclamation 1395/2025.

**Urban Lands Lease Holding Proclamation No. 721/2011** — https://justice.gov.et/en/law/urban-lands-lease-holding-proclamation/
- Art. 6(3): "…where a property attached on an old possession is transferred to a third party through any modality other than inheritance, the person to whome the property is transferred becomes the possessor through lease holding."
- Art. 24(8): "The transfer of the leasehold right … shall unconditionally transfer all contractual obligations assumed by the lessee to the third party to whom the leasehold right is transferred."

## What is not published

- The Addis Ababa land registration office's list of documents, steps, service fee and processing time for a sale transfer was not found (its website could not be reached on 2026-09-17). Ask the sub-city land office or the city's land registration agency.
- DARS fees for a house sale contract were not found. The fee schedule of Regulation 543/2024 is not readable in the published PDF. Ask DARS.
- No Addis Ababa directive on transferring ordinary (non-condominium) houses by sale was found in the Ministry of Justice database.
- The law does not say what amount the 30% inflation adjustment is taken from, or how the city values property for stamp duty. Ask the Addis Ababa Revenue Bureau.
- Whether sellers of a private home need a tax clearance, and which documents they must bring, was not found. Ask the revenue office.
- Requirements for a lease-arrears clearance, and whether an old-possession holding has to be converted to lease before transfer in Addis Ababa, were not found. Ask the land office.
