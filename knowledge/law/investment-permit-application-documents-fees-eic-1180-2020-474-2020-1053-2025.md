# የኢንቨስትመንት ፈቃድ እንዴት ይወጣል — How to Get an Investment Permit: Who Needs One, Documents, Capital and Fees (1180/2020, 474/2020, 1053/2025)

## በአጭሩ (Amharic summary)
- **ፈቃድ የሚያስፈልጋቸው (አዋጅ 1180/2012 አንቀጽ 10)፦** የውጭ ባለሀብቶች፤ በጋራ የሚያለሙ የአገር ውስጥና የውጭ ባለሀብቶች፤ እንደ አገር ውስጥ ባለሀብት የሚቆጠሩ የተወሰኑ ሰዎች፤ ማበረታቻ (ለምሳሌ ከቀረጥ ነጻ ዕቃ ማስገባት) የሚፈልጉ የአገር ውስጥ ባለሀብቶች፤ እንዲሁም ለማስፋፊያ ወይም ለማሻሻያ ማበረታቻ የሚፈልጉ ባለሀብቶች።
- ማበረታቻ በሌለበት መስክ (ወይም ማበረታቻውን ትቶ) የሚያለማ፣ እንደ አገር ውስጥ ባለሀብት የሚቆጠር ትውልደ ኢትዮጵያዊ የውጭ ዜጋ ያለ ኢንቨስትመንት ፈቃድ ማልማት ይችላል። አንድ ሰው የአገር ውስጥና የውጭ ባለሀብት ፈቃድ በአንድ ጊዜ መያዝ አይችልም።
- **የት ይቀርባል፦** የውጭ ባለሀብት ለኢትዮጵያ ኢንቨስትመንት ኮሚሽን ያቀርባል። የአገር ውስጥ ባለሀብት ለሚመለከተው የኢንቨስትመንት አካል ያቀርባል። ማመልከቻው በወረቀት ወይም በኦንላይን ቅጽ ይሞላል (ደንብ 474/2012 አንቀጽ 7 እና 8)።
- **የአገር ውስጥ ባለሀብት ሰነዶች፦** በወኪል ከሆነ የውክልና ሰነድ፤ ለግል ነጋዴ መታወቂያ (ወይም የአገር ውስጥ ባለሀብትነትን የሚያሳይ ሰነድ) እና 2 ፓስፖርት መጠን ፎቶ፤ ለድርጅት መመሥረቻ ጽሑፍና መተዳደሪያ ደንብ እንዲሁም የንግድ ምዝገባ ምስክር ወረቀት። አዲስ ድርጅት ከሆነ ረቂቅ መመሥረቻና መተዳደሪያ ደንብ፣ የእያንዳንዱ ባለአክሲዮን መታወቂያ እና የሥራ አስኪያጁ 2 ፎቶ ያስፈልጋል። ለኅብረት ሥራ ማኅበር መተዳደሪያ ደንቡ ይቀርባል።
- **የውጭ ባለሀብት ሰነዶች፦** የውክልና ሰነድ (በወኪል ከሆነ)፤ የፓስፖርት መረጃ ገጽ ቅጂና 2 ፎቶ፤ ለሚቋቋም ድርጅት ረቂቅ መመሥረቻና መተዳደሪያ ደንብ፣ የባለአክሲዮኖች ፓስፖርት ቅጂ እና የሥራ አስኪያጁ 2 ፎቶ።
- ባለአክሲዮኑ ድርጅት ከሆነ የድርጅቱ መመሥረቻ ሰነዶች፣ የንግድ ምዝገባው እና በኢትዮጵያ ለማልማት የወሰነበት ቃለ-ጉባኤ ይቀርባሉ። ለቅርንጫፍ የእናት ድርጅቱ ሰነዶች፣ የሥራ አስኪያጁ ሹመትና ፓስፖርት፣ እንዲሁም ቅርንጫፍ ለመክፈት የተወሰነበት ቃለ-ጉባኤ ይቀርባሉ። ኮሚሽኑ ከጠየቀ የፋይናንስ አቅም ማስረጃም ያስፈልጋል።

## In short (English)
- **Who needs a permit (Proclamation 1180/2020, Art. 10):** foreign investors; domestic and foreign investors investing jointly; certain persons treated as domestic investors; domestic investors who want incentives (such as duty-free imports); and investors who want incentives for an expansion or upgrade.
- A foreign national of Ethiopian origin treated as a domestic investor may invest without a permit in areas that carry no incentives, or by giving up the incentives. No one may hold domestic and foreign investor permits at the same time.
- **Where to apply:** foreign investors apply to the Ethiopian Investment Commission (EIC), and domestic investors to the appropriate investment organ. Applications use a paper-based or online form (Regulation 474/2020, Arts. 7–8).
- **Documents for domestic investors:** an authorization document if an agent applies. A sole proprietor gives an ID card (or a document showing domestic-investor status) and two passport-size photos. A company gives its memorandum and articles of association and its commercial registration certificate. A new company gives draft memorandum and articles, each shareholder's ID, and two photos of the general manager. A cooperative gives its articles of association.
- **Documents for foreign investors:** an agent's authorization (if an agent applies), the passport bio-page, and two photos. A company to be formed in Ethiopia gives draft memorandum and articles, each shareholder's passport bio-page, and two photos of the general manager.
- A corporate shareholder must give its constitutive documents, commercial registration, and the board resolution to invest in Ethiopia. A branch must give the parent company's documents, the manager's appointment and passport, and the parent's resolution to open a branch. The EIC may also ask for proof of financial standing.
- Documents from abroad must be authenticated by the authorized foreign and Ethiopian bodies.
- **Minimum capital for foreign investors:** USD 200,000 per project, or USD 150,000 jointly with a domestic investor. For architectural, engineering, technical consultancy, testing and publishing work it is USD 100,000 alone or USD 50,000 jointly. Imported capital must be registered within one year.
- **EIC fees (Directive 1053/2025, April 2025, One Stop Shop, in USD):** new investment permit 50; expansion or upgrading permit 50; renewal 50; amendment 25; substitute permit 25; cancellation 25.
- Other fees: company name registration 50; commercial registration 50; capital registration 50; business licence (new or expansion) 100; business licence renewal 75; work permit 100; work permit renewal 80; notarized copies 25 each; customs duty-free permission 20 per request. Fees in the Dire Dawa Free Trade Zone are higher (for example, a new business licence costs USD 1,000).
- **Renewal:** the permit is renewed every year until the investor starts marketing products or services. Apply within one month after the year ends. No renewal is needed once a business licence is issued.
- The permit is revoked if the project has not started within two years of issue, though it can be renewed for sufficient cause.
- The EIC's website (investethiopia.gov.et) describes its forms and steps, but the "600 birr" fee quoted there predates Directive 1053/2025.

## Official text
**Investment Proclamation No. 1180/2020** (Federal Negarit Gazette No. 28, 2 April 2020; https://justice.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/አዋጅ-ቁጥር-1180-2012.pdf):
- Art. 9(1): "Any foreign investor ... shall be required to allocate a minimum capital of USD 200,000.00 (two hundred thousand) for a single investment project." 9(2): USD 150,000 jointly with a domestic investor. 9(3): architectural or engineering works, related technical consultancy, technical testing and analysis, or publishing: "a) USD 100,000.00 ... if the investment is made on his own; b) USD 50,000.00 ... if the investment is made jointly with a domestic investor." 9(5): capital must be registered "with in one year".
- Art. 10(1): "The following investors shall be required to obtain investment permits: a) Foreign investors; b) Domestic and foreign investors investing jointly; c) Investors investing as domestic investors pursuant to Article 2 Sub-article (5) paragraphs (f), (g), (h) and (i) ...; [d]) Domestic investors who, investing in areas eligible for incentives, seek to be beneficiaries of such incentives; and e) An investor seeking to expand or upgrade an existing investment ... [and] seeks to be beneficiary of such incentives." 10(4): "No investor may, at any time, hold domestic and foreign investor permits simultaneously."
- Art. 11(1): "An investment permit shall be renewed annually until the investor commences marketing his products or services. There shall be no need for renewal of investment permit after issuance of business license." 11(2): renewal "within one month after the end of a period of one year". 11(3): revoked if the investor "fails to commence implementation of his project within two years of being issued the permit".

**Investment Regulation No. 474/2020** (https://justice.gov.et/am/law/investment-regulation-council-of-ministers-regulation-no-474-2019/):
- Art. 7: "any domestic investor must fill out a paper-based or online application form and submit same to the appropriate investment organ accompanied by the following documents" (agent authorization; sole proprietor's ID and "two recent passport photo size photographs"; company documents; public enterprise or PPP company documents; cooperative's articles).
- Art. 8(1): "any foreign investor must fill out a paper-based or online application form and submit same to the Commission" with the documents listed above, including "f) Document evidencing the financial position or profile of the investor, when deemed appropriate by the Commission." 8(2): "Documents ... originating from outside of Ethiopia must be authenticated by foreign and domestic bodies authorized to authenticate documents."

## What is not published
- The current official processing time for an investment permit was not found. The EIC FAQ says a minimum of 5 days, but that page predates the 2025 fee directive. Ask the EIC.
- The address of the EIC's online application system was not found in these laws. Ask the EIC or check investethiopia.gov.et.
- Whether fees are payable in birr at the exchange rate is not stated. Ask the EIC.
- Fees charged by regional investment bureaus to domestic investors are not covered by Directive 1053/2025. Ask your regional bureau.
