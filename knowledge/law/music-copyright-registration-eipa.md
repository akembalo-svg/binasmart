# የሙዚቃ የቅጂ መብት ምዝገባ — Registering Copyright for Music

## በአጭሩ (Amharic summary)

- የቅጂ መብት ጥበቃ **ያለ ምዝገባ በራሱ ይገኛል**፤ ሥራው ወጥ (original) እና በሆነ መልኩ የተቀረጸ/የተመዘገበ (fixed) ሲሆን ከተፈጠረበት ጊዜ ጀምሮ ይጠበቃል (አዋጅ 410/2004 አንቀጽ 6(1))።
- ምዝገባ በፈቃደኝነት ነው፤ አለመመዝገብ ጥበቃውን አያስቀርም። ነገር ግን የምዝገባ ምስክር ወረቀቱ የባለቤትነት የመጀመሪያ ደረጃ ማስረጃ (prima facie evidence) ሆኖ ያገለግላል።
- ምዝገባውን የሚያከናውነው የኢትዮጵያ አእምሯዊ ንብረት ባለሥልጣን (EIPA) ነው። ሙዚቃ፣ የድምጽ ቅጂ (sound recording) እና ኦዲዮቪዥዋል ሥራዎች ሊመዘገቡ ይችላሉ።
- የሚያስፈልጉ፦ የማመልከቻ ቅጽ መሙላት፣ ቃለ-መሐላ መፈጸም፣ የሥራውን ናሙና (በሲዲ፣ ቴፕ ወዘተ)፣ የመታወቂያ ወይም ፓስፖርት ቅጂ፣ የአገልግሎት ክፍያ ደረሰኝ፤ እንደ አስፈላጊነቱ የውክልና ሰነድ፣ የሕጋዊ ሰውነት ማስረጃ ወይም የመብት ማስተላለፊያ ማስረጃ።
- ማመልከቻው በአማርኛ ወይም በእንግሊዝኛ፣ በፖስታ ወይም በድረ-ገጽ አድራሻ ሊቀርብ ይችላል፤ አንድ ማመልከቻ ለአንድ ሥራ ብቻ ነው።
- EIPA የሚያየው ፎርማሊቲውን ብቻ ነው፤ ጉድለት ካለ በአንድ ወር ውስጥ መስተካከል አለበት፣ ካልሆነ ማመልከቻው እንደተተወ ይቆጠራል። ሐሰተኛ ቃለ-መሐላ የወንጀል ተጠያቂነት ያስከትላል።
- ምስክር ወረቀቱ ለ5 ዓመት ያገለግላል፤ ጊዜው ካለፈ በ6 ወር ውስጥ ለተከታታይ 5 ዓመታት ማደስ ይቻላል።
- ክፍያ (ግለሰብ / ድርጅት)፦ የምዝገባ ማመልከቻ 250 / 315 ብር፤ የማመልከቻ እርማት 250 / 315 ብር፤ እድሳት 250 / 315 ብር፤ የመብት ማስተላለፍ ምዝገባ 300 / 375 ብር፤ የተመዘገቡ ሥራዎች መረጃ ማግኘት 100 / 125 ብር።
- ሙዚቃን ለንግድ የሚጠቀም ማንኛውም ሰው ለሚመለከተው የጋራ አስተዳደር ማኅበር የሮያሊቲ ክፍያ የመክፈል ግዴታ አለበት (አዋጅ 872/2014)።
- የመብት ባለቤቶች በባለሥልጣኑ እውቅና ለትርፍ ያልተቋቋመ የጋራ አስተዳደር ማኅበር ማቋቋም ይችላሉ። የሮያሊቲ አሰባሰብና ክፍፍል ይግባኝ በአእምሯዊ ንብረት ልዩ ፍርድ ሰጪ (IP Tribunal) ይታያል።

## In short (English)

- Copyright protection is **automatic, without any formality**, once a work is original and fixed (Proc. 410/2004, Art. 6(1)).
- Registration is voluntary; not registering does not remove protection. A registration certificate serves as prima facie evidence of ownership.
- Registration is done by the Ethiopian Intellectual Property Authority (EIPA). Music, sound recordings and audiovisual works can be registered.
- You need: the application form, a sworn affidavit, a sample of the work (CD, tape, etc.), ID or passport copy, the fee receipt, and where relevant a power of attorney, proof of legal personality or proof of assignment.
- Apply in Amharic or English, by post or through EIPA's website address; one application per work.
- EIPA checks form only; defects must be fixed within one month or the application is treated as abandoned. A false affidavit is a criminal matter.
- The certificate is valid for five years; renew within six months after expiry for further five-year periods.
- Fees (individual / firm): application 250 / 315 Birr; correction 250 / 315; renewal 250 / 315; registration of assignment 300 / 375; access to information on registered works 100 / 125.
- Anyone who uses protected works commercially must pay royalties to the relevant collective management society (Proc. 872/2014).
- Right holders may form a non-profit collective management society recognised by EIPA; royalty disputes can be appealed to the IP Tribunal.

## Official text

**Proclamation No. 410/2004, Art. 6(1)** — https://eipa.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/proc-no-410-2004-copyright-and-neighboring-rights-protection.pdf
"The author of a work shall, irrespective of the quality of the work and the purpose for which the work may have been created, be entitled to protection, for his work without any formality and - upon creation where it is: a) Original; and b) Fixed."

**Regulation No. 305/2014** — https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/legislation/details/21135 ; Amharic: https://eipa.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/CR-registration-Regulation-305-2006-Amharic-Only.pdf
Art. 3(1): "The owner of copy right or neighboring right or any person to whom such right is assigned may register his right by applying to the Office in accordance with this Regulation."
Art. 4(2): registrable classes include "artistic works such as stage, music and audio visual works"; Art. 4(5) adds "sound recordings".
Art. 10: "The Office may not undertake substantive examination ... provided, however, that the applicant shall ascertain through affidavit that he is the owner."
Art. 12(2): the certificate "shall serve as a prima facie evidence of ownership".
Art. 12(3): "Notwithstanding the provisions of sub-article (2) of this Article, failure to register any work entitling copyright and neighboring rights pursuant to this Regulation may not affect the protection of copyright and neighboring rights under the Proclamation."
አንቀጽ 12(3) (Amharic, letter forms corrected): "የዚህ አንቀጽ ንዑስ አንቀጽ (2) ድንጋጌ ቢኖርም የቅጅና ተዛማጅ መብት የሚያስገኝ ሥራ በዚህ ደንብ መሠረት አለመመዝገቡ በአዋጁ መሠረት የሚደረግ የቅጅና ተዛማጅ መብቶች ጥበቃን አያስቀርም፡፡"
Art. 13(1): the certificate is "valid for five years from the date of its issuance".

**EIPA registration-procedure page** — https://eipa.gov.et/registration-procedure/
"Registration of works Entitling Copyright and Neighboring Rights is exercised up on the request of the right owner. Registration, however, is not a precondition to get copyright protection."
"Filling application form / Swearing an affidavit / Providing the sample of the work (on a CD, Tape, etc) / Evidence of payment of service fee / The original work and related documents such as identification card ... relevant evidences of assignments etc"

**Fees** — Regulation 305/2014 Schedule; https://eipa.gov.et/c-fees-and-payments/
| Service | Individual (Birr) | Firm (Birr) |
|---|---|---|
| Application for registration | 250 | 315 |
| Correction of application | 250 | 315 |
| Renewal of registration | 250 | 315 |
| Registration of assignment of rights | 300 | 375 |
| Access to information on registered works | 100 | 125 |

**Proclamation No. 872/2014** — https://eipa.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/proclamation-no-872-2014-copyright-and-neighbouring-rights-protection-1.pdf
Art. 38(1): "Any person who uses any works protected under this Proclamation for commercial purpose shall have the obligation to pay royalty to the relevant collective management society."
Art. 32: "Right holders of works protected under this Proclamation may form collective management society to jointly administer their rights." "A collective management society shall be formed based on the recognition granted by the office." It "shall be formed for non-profit purpose."
