# የጠፋ ወይም የተሰረቀ ፓስፖርት መተካት — Replacing a lost or stolen Ethiopian passport

## በአጭሩ
- ፓስፖርትዎ ከጠፋ ወይም ከተሰረቀ ምትክ ፓስፖርት የሚሰጠው የኢሚግሬሽንና ዜግነት አገልግሎት (ICS) ነው።
- የሚያስፈልጉ ሰነዶች፦ (1) ፓስፖርቱ ከጠፋበት አካባቢ የተሰጠ የፖሊስ ማስረጃ፣ (2) የታደሰ የቀበሌ መታወቂያ፣ (3) የጠፋው ፓስፖርት መረጃ (ቁጥሩ፣ የተሰጠበትና የሚያበቃበት ቀን)።
- የፓስፖርቱን ቁጥር ካላወቁ በአቅራቢያዎ ወዳለው የኢሚግሬሽን ቢሮ በአካል ሄደው መረጃውን ማግኘት ይችላሉ።
- ማመልከቻው በመስመር ላይ በ www.immigration.gov.et ብቻ ይቀርባል። ከጥር 2026 (እ.ኤ.አ.) ጀምሮ ይህ ድረ-ገጽ በአዲስ አበባን ጨምሮ በ23ቱም ቅርንጫፍ ቢሮዎች ይሠራል።
- ከተመዘገቡ በኋላ ክፍያውን በ3 ሰዓት ውስጥ ይከፍላሉ፤ የቀጠሮ ማረጋገጫውን አትመው በቀጠሮው ቀን በአካል ቀርበው አሻራና ፎቶ ይሰጣሉ።
- ፓስፖርቱ የሚደርስበትን ቀን ከ 7876 በሚላክ የጽሑፍ መልእክት (SMS) ያውቃሉ፤ ያመለከቱበት ቢሮ ሄደው ይረከባሉ።
- በውክልና ማመልከት አይቻልም፤ በአካል መቅረብ ግዴታ ነው።
- ክፍያ (የሚኒስትሮች ምክር ቤት ደንብ ቁጥር 550/2024 እ.ኤ.አ.፣ ICS የካቲት 2025 እ.ኤ.አ. ያወጣው ሠንጠረዥ)፦ የጠፋ ፓስፖርት በመደበኛ 13,000 ብር፤ በአስቸኳይ በ5 ቀን 28,000 ብር፤ በአስቸኳይ በ2 ቀን 33,000 ብር።
- የጠፋ ፓስፖርት ከመረጃ እርማት ጋር፦ መደበኛ 20,500 ብር፤ 5 ቀን 35,500 ብር፤ 2 ቀን 40,500 ብር።
- አስቸኳዩ አገልግሎት (2 ወይም 5 ቀን) በቀጠሮ ቀን ብቻ በጎተራ በሚገኘው ዋናው የICS ቢሮ እና በመሶብ የአንድ ማዕከል አገልግሎት ይሰጣል (ICS፣ የካቲት 4 ቀን 2026 እ.ኤ.አ.)።
- የማመልከቻ ሁኔታን passport.ics.gov.et ላይ ማየት ይቻላል። ነፃ የስልክ መስመር፦ 8133፤ ኢሜይል፦ info@ics.gov.et።

## In short (English)
- Lost or stolen passports are replaced by the Immigration and Citizenship Service (ICS).
- Documents: (1) a police report from the area where the passport was lost; (2) a valid, renewed kebele ID; (3) details of the lost passport (number, issue date, expiry date).
- If you do not know the passport details, go in person to the nearest immigration office to get them.
- Apply online only at www.immigration.gov.et. Since January 2026 this portal is used by all 23 branch offices, Addis Ababa included.
- Pay within 3 hours of registering online, print the appointment confirmation, and go in person on the appointment day for fingerprints and a photo.
- An SMS from 7876 tells you the collection date. You collect the passport at the office where you applied.
- No applications by power of attorney. You must appear in person.
- Fees (Regulation 550/2024, ICS table posted Feb 2025): lost passport ETB 13,000 normal, ETB 28,000 urgent 5-day, ETB 33,000 urgent 2-day.
- Lost passport with a data correction: ETB 20,500 normal, ETB 35,500 5-day, ETB 40,500 2-day.
- Urgent 2-day and 5-day service is given only on the appointment date, at the ICS head office at Gotera and at Mesob one-stop service centres (ICS notice, 4 Feb 2026).
- Check your application's status at passport.ics.gov.et. Toll-free line: 8133. Email: info@ics.gov.et.

## Official text
**ICS: "How to Obtain a Replacement Passport (Lost Passport)"** (posted 26 February 2025) — https://ics.gov.et/information/how-to-obtain-a-replacement-passport-lost-passport/
- Required documents: "A police report from the area where your passport was lost"; "A valid and renewed Kebele ID (local administrative identification)"; information from the lost passport (number, issue date, expiry date). If this information is not available, visit the nearest Immigration Office in person to get it.
- "Complete the payment using your preferred payment method within 3 hours of online registration." Then print the appointment confirmation.
- "You will receive a text message (SMS) from 7876 with your passport delivery date."
- "Applications can not be processed through power of attorney." You must appear in person to give your photo or more information. If 7876 contacts you asking for photos or more documents, bring the police report and the appointment confirmation.
- Note: this 2025 page names www.ethiopianpassportservices.gov.et as the portal. The later ICS notices below name www.immigration.gov.et.

## What is not published
- The normal (non-urgent) processing time for a replacement passport. Ask ICS (8133).
- Whether a stolen passport needs anything more than a loss report. ICS only says "police report from the area where your passport was lost". Ask the police station and ICS.
- Replacing a lost passport from abroad: the ICS page covers applications in Ethiopia only. Ask your nearest Ethiopian embassy or consulate for its procedure and fee.
- Whether the old ethiopianpassportservices.gov.et address still works. Use www.immigration.gov.et as in the 2026 notices.
