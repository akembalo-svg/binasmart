# የጋብቻ ምዝገባና የጋብቻ ምስክር ወረቀት — Marriage registration and marriage certificate

## በአጭሩ
- ማንኛውም ጋብቻ (በፍትሐ ብሔር፣ በሃይማኖት ወይም በባህል የተፈጸመ) በክብር መዝገብ ሹም (የወሳኝ ኩነት ምዝገባ ጽ/ቤት) መመዝገብ አለበት (የቤተሰብ ሕግ አንቀጽ 28)።
- ሁለቱም ተጋቢዎች ቢያንስ 18 ዓመት የሞላቸው መሆን አለባቸው (የቤተሰብ ሕግ አንቀጽ 7)።
- በፍትሐ ብሔር (በክብር መዝገብ ሹም ፊት) ለመጋባት ሠርጉ ከመፈጸሙ ቢያንስ አንድ ወር በፊት ለክብር መዝገብ ሹሙ ማሳወቅ ያስፈልጋል (አንቀጽ 23)።
- ጋብቻው የሚፈጸመው ከአንዱ ተጋቢ ወይም ከወላጃቸው ወይም ከቅርብ ዘመዳቸው ቢያንስ ለ6 ወር ያለማቋረጥ በሚኖርበት ቦታ ነው (አንቀጽ 22)።
- በሠርጉ ቀን ለእያንዳንዱ ተጋቢ ሁለት ሁለት ምስክሮች (በአጠቃላይ 4) ያስፈልጋሉ፤ ተጋቢዎቹና ምስክሮቹ በመሐላ ያረጋግጣሉ፣ በመዝገቡ ላይ ይፈርማሉ (አንቀጽ 25)።
- በሃይማኖት ወይም በባህል ከተጋቡ የሃይማኖት ተቋሙ ወይም ሽማግሌዎች የሚሰጡትን የጋብቻ ማስረጃ ለምዝገባ ያቀርባሉ። ማስረጃው የተጋቢዎቹን ስም፣ ዕድሜና አድራሻ፣ የጋብቻውን ቀንና ቦታ እንዲሁም የምስክሮቹን ስምና አድራሻ ይይዛል (አዋጅ 760/2004 አንቀጽ 31–33)።
- ምዝገባው ከጋብቻው ቀን ጀምሮ በ30 ቀን ውስጥ መፈጸም አለበት፤ ከዘገየ የመዘግየቱን ምክንያት በማስረጃ ማቅረብ ያስፈልጋል (አንቀጽ 18)።
- ምዝገባ የሚፈጸመው ተጋቢዎቹ በመረጡት ቦታ፣ ከሁለቱ አንዱ በሚኖርበት ቦታ ወይም የወላጅ ወይም የቅርብ ዘመድ መኖሪያ ባለበት ቦታ ነው (አንቀጽ 32)።
- የጋብቻ ምስክር ወረቀቱ በሁለት ቅጂ ተዘጋጅቶ ለእያንዳንዱ ተጋቢ ይሰጣል፤ ክፍያው በደንብ ይወሰናል፤ ክልሎች የራሳቸውን የክፍያ ሕግ ሊያወጡ ይችላሉ (አንቀጽ 47)።
- በአዲስ አበባ "ያላገባ" ማስረጃ የሚሰጠው በወረዳ ጽ/ቤት ነው፤ የታደሰ መታወቂያ ወይም ፓስፖርት ቅጂና ከ6 ወር ወዲህ የተነሳ 4x4 ፎቶ ይጠየቃል፤ ማስረጃው ለ6 ወር ያገለግላል (መመሪያ 145/2015 አንቀጽ 26–27)።
- የአዲስ አበባ የሲቪል ምዝገባና የነዋሪነት አገልግሎት ኤጀንሲ ስልክ፦ 7533።

## In short (English)
- Every marriage (civil, religious or customary) must be registered by an officer of civil status at the vital events office (Family Code Art. 28).
- Both spouses must be at least 18 (Family Code Art. 7).
- For a civil marriage, tell the officer of civil status at least one month before the ceremony (Art. 23).
- The ceremony takes place where one spouse, or a parent or close relative of one spouse, has lived continuously for at least 6 months (Art. 22).
- On the day you need two witnesses for each spouse (four in all). Spouses and witnesses declare under oath and sign the register (Art. 25).
- For a religious or customary marriage, bring the written evidence the religious institution or elders give you. It shows the couple's names, ages and residences, the date and place of the marriage, and the witnesses' names and residences (Proc. 760/2012 Arts 31–33).
- Register within 30 days of the marriage. If you register late, bring evidence explaining the delay (Art. 18).
- You can register where you both choose, where either spouse used to live, or where a parent or close relative lives (Art. 32).
- The certificate is made in two copies, one for each spouse. The fee is set by regulation, and regions may set their own fee laws (Art. 47 as amended).
- In Addis Ababa, the woreda office issues the "unmarried" (single-status) certificate. You need a copy of a renewed ID or passport and a 4x4 photo taken in the last 6 months. The certificate is valid for 6 months (Directive 145/2015 Arts 26–27).
- Addis Ababa Civil Registration and Residency Service Agency: phone 7533.

## Official text
**Proclamation No. 760/2012** (Federal Negarit Gazeta No. 58, 22 August 2012) — PDF: https://justice.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/አዋጅ-ቁጥር-760-2004.pdf. The MoJ lists it as "Amended"; the amendment is Proclamation No. 1049/2017 (Federal Negarit Gazette 23rd Year No. 74, 7 August 2017).
- Art. 17(3): "A declarant shall appear in person before an officer of civil status to have a vital event registered."
- Art. 18: "1/ Any vital event, other than birth, shall be registered within 30 days following the date of its occurrence unless there is sufficient cause for delay. … 3/ Where the registration of a vital event is not effected within the period specified … the declarant shall produce evidence to justify the delay."
- Art. 30 (records of marriage): "1/ the couples' full name, date and place of birth, principal residence, citizenship, ethnic origin and religion; 2/ the date, place and form of the marriage; 3/ the couples' signature; 4/ the evidence or the names and principal residences of witnesses of the marriage, as the case may be; …"
- Art. 31: "1/ Where marriage is celebrated before an officer of civil status, the officer of civil status who observed the marriage ceremony shall immediately register the marriage. 2/ Where marriage is celebrated by religious or customary ceremony, the couples shall present to an officer of civil status, for registration, the evidence of the marriage referred to in Article 33."
- Art. 32: "The place of registration of marriage shall be the place where the couples have jointly decided, the place where either of the couples had used to reside or the principal residence of the parents or close relative of either of the couples."
- Art. 33: the religious institution or elders "shall immediately provide the couples with evidence of the marriage containing the following: 1/ the names, ages and principal residences of the couples; 2/ the date and place of the marriage; and 3/ the names and principal residences of the witnesses."
- Art. 47(2): "A certificate of registration of marriage shall be prepared in two copies and be given to each spouse." Art. 47(6): a lost or damaged certificate is replaced on payment of the fee.
- Proc. 1049/2017 replaced Art. 47(5): other authorised bodies issue certificates "upon payment of service fee prescribed by regulation to be issued by council of Ministries; and Regional States may issue their own service fee laws." It also made Ethiopian missions the administrative office for Ethiopians abroad (new Art. 7), and a new Art. 68 lets you get service from any registration office that keeps copies of your record.

**Revised Family Code No. 213/2000** — https://justice.gov.et/en/law/amended-family-law-proclamation/
- Art. 7(1): "Neither a man nor a woman who has not attained the full age of eighteen years shall conclude marriage."
- Art. 22: civil marriage takes place before the officer of civil status of the place where one spouse, or an ascendant or close relative of one spouse, has lived "continuously … for not less than six months prior to the date of the marriage."
- Art. 23: "The future spouses shall inform the officer of civil status of their intention to conclude marriage not less than one month prior to its celebration."
- Art. 19(1): an objection to the marriage must be made in writing "within fifteen days from the notification of the marriage."
- Art. 25: "1) Marriage shall be concluded publicly in the presence of the future spouses and two witnesses for each of the future spouses. 2) The future spouses and the witnesses shall declare, under oath, that the essential conditions of marriage are not violated. … 5) Each of the spouses and their witnesses shall sign in the register of civil status."

**Addis Ababa Directive No. 145/2015** (unmarried certificate), Art. 26: applicants give a copy of a renewed ID, passport, driving licence, travel document or refugee ID; may appear in person or through a legal representative; bring a 4x4 or passport-size photo taken in the last six months. A representative needs an authenticated power of attorney that expressly covers this. For Ethiopians and people of Ethiopian origin living abroad, a power of attorney made abroad must be authenticated and registered by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and DARS. Art. 27(2): the certificate "የሚያገለግለው ለስድስት ወር ብቻ ሲሆን" (is valid for six months only). Art. 27(7): for people living abroad, it covers only their marital status while in Ethiopia.

**UNHCR Ethiopia** (for refugees only) — https://help.unhcr.org/ethiopia/services/documentation/marriage-and-divorce/ — RRS registers refugee marriages free of charge. It asks for two witnesses for each spouse, with valid IDs, proof of refugee status, proof of non-married status, and two 3x4 cm photos.

## What is not published
- The Addis Ababa agency's own checklist for citizens (photos, ID copies, single-status proof, medical papers). Its service pages could not be opened. Ask your woreda civil registration office or call 7533.
- The current fee for a marriage certificate in Addis Ababa or in the regions. Ask the office.
- Registering a marriage for Ethiopians abroad: the law makes Ethiopian missions the registration office. Ask your embassy for its requirements.
