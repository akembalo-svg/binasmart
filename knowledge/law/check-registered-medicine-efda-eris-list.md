## In short (English)

- No medicine may be imported, made, stored, distributed, sold or used in Ethiopia unless the Ethiopian Food and Drug Authority (EFDA) has registered it and granted a marketing authorization (Proclamation 1112/2019, Art. 20(1)).
- To see the list of registered medicines, go to www.efda.gov.et and choose "Public Information" → "Products and Institutions" → "Registered Medicines". The link opens https://www.eris.efda.gov.et/products.
- The list of licensed medicine manufacturers is at https://www.eris.efda.gov.et/report/manufacturers.
- The same section also has "Summary of product characteristics", "Patient information leaflets" and the "Ethiopian Public Assessment Report".
- EFDA is legally required to tell the public which foods and medicines are registered (Art. 4(8)).
- If you can't find a medicine on the list, or you think a medicine is suspicious, contact EFDA: Tel +251 11 552 4122. Address: Africa Avenue, near Wolosefer, Kirkos sub-city, Addis Ababa.
- EFDA also has a Telegram channel: t.me/ethiopianfoodanddrugauthority.

## Official text

**Proclamation No. 1112/2019** — https://www.efda.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/Food-and-Medicine-Administration-Proclamation-1112.pdf

- Art. 4(8): "prepare and, as necessary, revise list of essential medicines, notify registered foods and medicines to the public; issue national medicine formulary, classify medicines into different categories, revise the classification whenever necessary;"
- Art. 20(1): "Any medicine and medical device shall not be manufactured, imported, exported, stored, distributed, transported, sold, hold, used, or transfer to any other person without registration and marketing authorization."
- Art. 20(2): "The executive organ shall register and grant marketing authorization … after it assesses the quality, safety and efficacy of the medicine…"

**EFDA "Products and Institutions" page** — https://www.efda.gov.et/public-information/

- Links: "Registered Medicines" → https://www.eris.efda.gov.et/products ; "Licensed Medicine Manufacturers" → https://www.eris.efda.gov.et/report/manufacturers ; "iLicense User Manual v2".
- The site menu under "Public Information" also lists "Approved Medicine Product Information", "Ethiopian Public Assessment Report", "Summary of product characteristics", "Patient information leaflets", "Appointed Inspectors" and "Measure Taken".

**EFDA Powers and Duties** — https://www.efda.gov.et/about-efda/power-and-duties/

- "Notify and create awareness, as appropriate, to the public and stakeholders about registered or authorized or illicit products, establishments, administrative measures taken and the Authority`s regulatory functions;"
- Contact: "Africa Avenue, near Wolosefer, Kirkos sub city, 02/03 kebelle"; "Tel: +25111-5-524122".
