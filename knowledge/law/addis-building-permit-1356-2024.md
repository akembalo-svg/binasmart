# በአዲስ አበባ የግንባታ ፈቃድ ማውጣት — Getting a Building Permit in Addis Ababa

**ማሳሰቢያ፦** የቀድሞው የኢትዮጵያ ሕንፃ አዋጅ ቁጥር 624/2001 (624/2009) ተሽሯል። የተካው የኢትዮጵያ ሕንፃ አዋጅ ቁጥር 1356/2017 (1356/2024) ነው (አንቀጽ 53(1))። የአዲስ አበባ መመሪያ ቁጥር 127/2014 የወጣው በቀድሞው አዋጅ መሠረት ስለሆነ ከአዲሱ አዋጅ ጋር የሚቃረን ክፍል ተፈጻሚ አይሆንም (አንቀጽ 53(2))፤ ለወቅታዊ አሠራር ቢሮውን ይጠይቁ።

**Note:** Building Proclamation No. 624/2009 is repealed and replaced by the Ethiopian Building Proclamation No. 1356/2024 (Art. 53(1)). Addis Ababa Directive 127/2014 was issued under the old proclamation; parts inconsistent with 1356 do not apply (Art. 53(2)). Confirm current practice with the office.

## In short (English)

- In Addis Ababa, anyone who builds new, upgrades, expands or renovates a building must first get a building permit (Proc. 1356, Art. 7(1)).
- Step one is planning consent (plan agreement). Under Directive 127/2014: apply on Form 003, attach a copy of the holding certificate or lease contract; the consent is valid for 12 months only.
- Then fill in permit application Form 001, attach the plans, and apply in person or via website (no official portal address was found).
- Only the owner, a legal agent, or the consultant/professional who prepared the design may file; the permit is handed only to the owner or legal agent.
- Main attachments: proof of land-holding rights, planning-consent information, architectural/structural/sanitary/electrical/fire designs by category, soil test where required, floor-area or cost estimate, professional and consultant licence copies, TIN, insurance cover, and a debt-and-encumbrance clearance letter not older than 4 months. The full list must be posted on the notice board at the service office.
- Designs: in the working language or English, 3 copies, A3 to A1.
- Plan review time (Directive 127): category "ሀ"/A (non-real-estate) max 5 working days; "ለ"/B max 7; "ሐ"/C and real-estate B max 21.
- Permit validity (Proc. 1356, Art. 12): expires after 6 months if work has not started; if started but unfinished: A 2 years, B 3, C 4, D 5; extensions capped at A 2 years 6 months, B 4 years, C 5 years, D 6 years.
- Fees: a design review fee and an inspection fee exist; amounts are set by regulation (Art. 24) and were not found — ask the office.
- Appeals go to the Appeals Board, then to the ordinary court (Art. 50).
- Old Building Regulation 243/2003 and Directive 5/2003 stay in force for two years (Art. 52).
- Complaints about the permit office can be filed on the Mayor's Office Smart portal, sector "Construction Permit and Control Authority".

## Official text

### Ethiopian Building Proclamation No. 1356/2024 (English column, verbatim, translation errors kept)
Page: https://justice.gov.et/en/law/ethiopian-building-proclamation-no-1356-2024/ — PDF: https://justice.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/አዋጅ-ቁጥር-1356-2017-1.pdf (Federal Negarit Gazette 31st Year No. 39, 7 May 2025; listed "In Force")

**Art. 7(1).** "Any person must first obtain the appropriate building permit to carry out a new construction or to upgrade or expand or renovate an existing building."

**Art. 8.** "1/ Any person to get permission for new construction: a) First, the relevant party must submit a plan agreement. b) The person who wants to get a construction permit must submit and approve the design of the building to be built. ... 3/ In writing, the applicant should submit a construction permit application based on the planning agreement provided to the institution; ... 5/ An application to build a building must include a design and report according to the category of the building. The content of the application list depends on the Regulations and Directives."

**Art. 12.** "1/ If the construction work does not begin, the validity of the construction permit shall expire within six months from the date of issuance ... 2/ If the work is started but the work is not completed, the validity ... expires from the date of issuance of a) 2 years for category “A” buildings b) 3 years for category “B” buildings c) for category "C" buildings, 4 years d) for category "D" buildings, 5 years 3/ ... may be extended ... However, the duration of the construction permit cannot exceed: i) 2 years and 6 month for category “A” ... ii) 4 years for category “B” ... iii) ... "C" ... 5 years. iv) ... "D" ... 6 years."

**Art. 2(12).** "Category 'D' Building" means any building of more than twelve stories above the ground or five or more floors above the ground ... It is also any public utility or institution-related building or workshop, factory and industrial building."

**Art. 24. Payment.** "1/ Design review or approval fee shall be paid upon request for design and other documents. 2/ An inspection fee based on the category of the building must be paid for an inspection visit. The amount ... shall be determined by a Regulation issued for the implementation of this Proclamation."

**Art. 50.** "1/ An Appeals Board shall be established ... who shall investigate and resolve complaints related to construction permit and supervision activities. ... 3/ The Board shall have the power to hear and decide appeals against decisions made by the Building Construction Licensing and Supervision Institution. The party who is not satisfied with by the decision may submit the matter to the competent ordinary court."

**Art. 52.** "Building Regulation No.243/2003 and Building Directive No.5/2003 issued before the date of entry into force of this Proclamation shall remain in force for two years until amended."

**Art. 53(1).** "Building Proclamation No.624/2001 was repealed in accordance with this Proclamation."
