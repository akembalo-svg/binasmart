# Civil and Family Registration Proclamation No. 1370/2025 — key articles

- **Art. 4 — Registration organ:** the Immigration and Citizenship Service (ICS) is the registration organ, with administrative structures from the federal level down. Officers of civil status register the events.
- **Addis Ababa** (from the 760 notes in this library, not from this proclamation): registration is done at the offices of the Addis Ababa Civil Registration and Residency Service Agency (aacrrsa.gov.et, 7533).
- **Art. 6 — Abroad:** Ethiopian missions are civil registration offices for Ethiopians living abroad.
- **Art. 18 — Period of registration** ("unless there is a sufficient reason for delay"):
  - birth: within **90 days** of its occurrence
  - death: within **50 days**
  - any other vital event (marriage, divorce, adoption, acknowledgment): within **30 days**
- **Art. 49 — Payment for certificate:**
  - (1) a person who registers within the Art. 18 time limit is issued the certificate **free of charge**;
  - (2) if registration is late, the certificate is issued on payment of a service fee set by a Council of Ministers regulation.
- **Art. 58 — Family registration:** the Service registers, coordinates and administers family registration data. Every Ethiopian and permanent foreign resident registers household family members within 90 days of the start of registration in the area, or of a new member joining.
- **Art. 74 — Repeal:** Vital Events Registration and National ID Proclamation No. 760/2012 (as amended) is repealed. Earlier registrations and certificates stay valid unless contrary to this law.
