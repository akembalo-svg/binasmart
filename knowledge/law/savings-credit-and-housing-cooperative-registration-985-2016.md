# የቁጠባና ብድር ወይም የቤቶች ኅብረት ሥራ ማኅበር ማቋቋምና ማስመዝገብ — How to form and register a savings and credit (SACCO) or housing cooperative

## በአጭሩ (Amharic summary)

- ሕጉ የኅብረት ሥራ ማኅበራት አዋጅ ቁጥር 985/2009 (985/2016) ነው። የቁጠባና ብድር ማኅበርም ሆነ የቤቶች ኅብረት ሥራ ማኅበር በዚሁ ሕግ ይቋቋማሉ።
- **ተመዝጋቢው አካል**፦ የፌደራል የኅብረት ሥራ ተቆጣጣሪ አካል ወይም በክልል (በአዲስ አበባ የከተማው) የኅብረት ሥራ ተቆጣጣሪ አካል ነው። በፌደራል ደረጃ የሚቋቋም መሠረታዊ ማኅበር ከሁለትና ከዚያ በላይ ክልሎች አባላት ሊኖሩት ይገባል።
- **አባላት**፦ በአንድ አካባቢ የሚኖሩ፣ የሚሠሩ ወይም በአንድ ሙያ የተሰማሩ ግለሰቦች፤ ቢያንስ **50 አባላት**። ተቆጣጣሪው አካል እንደ ሥራው ዓይነት በመመሪያ ዝቅተኛውን ቁጥር ሊቀንስ ይችላል፤ ግን **ከ10 በታች አይሆንም**።
- **አባል ለመሆን**፦ 18 ዓመት የሞላው፣ የአክሲዮን (ዕጣ) እና የመመዝገቢያ ክፍያ መክፈል የሚችል፣ የማኅበሩን ዓላማና መተዳደሪያ ደንብ የተቀበለ።
- **ለምዝገባ የሚቀርቡ ሰነዶች**፦ (1) የመሥራቾች ስብሰባ ቃለ ጉባዔ፤ (2) መተዳደሪያ ደንብ በ3 ቅጂ፤ (3) የሥራ አመራር ኮሚቴ አባላት ስም፣ አድራሻና ፊርማ፤ (4) የቁጥጥር ኮሚቴ አባላት ስም፣ አድራሻና ፊርማ፤ (5) አባላት የአባልነት መስፈርት ማሟላታቸውን የሚያሳይ ዝርዝር፤ (6) የ3–5 ዓመት የሥራ ዕቅድ፤ (7) የመነሻ ካፒታልና በባንክ የተቀመጠውን ገንዘብ የሚያሳይ ሰነድ፤ (8) የሥራ ቦታ መግለጫ፤ (9) በመመሪያ የሚጠየቁ ሌሎች።
- ስሙ መጨረሻ ላይ **"ኃላፊነቱ የተወሰነ የኅብረት ሥራ ማኅበር"** የሚል ሐረግ መያዝ አለበት።
- ሰነዱ ከተሟላ ተቆጣጣሪው አካል በ**5 ተከታታይ የሥራ ቀናት** ውስጥ ምዝገባ ምስክር ወረቀት ይሰጣል፤ ውድቅ ካደረገ በ5 ቀን ውስጥ በጽሑፍ ምክንያቱን ያሳውቃል። ቅሬታ ካለ በ**60 ተከታታይ ቀናት** ውስጥ ለክልል ፍርድ ቤት (ፌደራል ከሆነ ለፌደራል ከፍተኛ ፍርድ ቤት) ማቅረብ ይቻላል። ከ(1)–(7) ያሉት ከተሟሉ እስከ 1 ዓመት የሚያገለግል ጊዜያዊ ምስክር ወረቀት ሊሰጥ ይችላል።
- የተመዘገበ ማኅበር **ተጨማሪ የንግድ ፈቃድ ሳያስፈልገው** በመተዳደሪያ ደንቡ ባለው ሥራ ይሰማራል፤ ሕጋዊ ሰውነት ያገኛል፤ ኃላፊነቱም በንብረቱ ልክ የተወሰነ ነው።
- **የቁጠባና ብድር ማኅበር** ከአባላት ቁጠባ ይሰበስባል፣ ብድር ይሰጣል፣ የብድር-ሕይወት መድን አገልግሎት ይሰጣል። ብድር የሚሰጠው **ለአባላት ወይም ለኅብረት ሥራ ማኅበራት ብቻ** ነው፤ የወለድ መጠኑ በጠቅላላ ጉባዔ ልዩ ውሳኔ በመተዳደሪያ ደንቡ ይወሰናል።
- ከተጣራ ትርፍ **30%** ለመጠባበቂያ ፈንድ ይያዛል (መጠባበቂያው የካፒታሉ 30% እስኪደርስ)፤ ቀሪው 70% በጠቅላላ ጉባዔ ይከፋፈላል። ማኅበሩ ከገቢ ግብር ነፃ ሲሆን አባላት በሚያገኙት ትርፍ ድርሻ ግብር ይከፍላሉ።
- **የቤቶች ኅብረት ሥራ ማኅበር በአዲስ አበባ**፦ ምዝገባው በዚሁ አዋጅ ነው፤ ነገር ግን የመሬት አሰጣጥ፣ የቁጠባ መጠንና የከተማው ተጨማሪ መስፈርቶች በዚህ ምንጭ አልተገኙም — የክፍለ ከተማችሁን የኅብረት ሥራ ጽ/ቤት ጠይቁ።

## In short (English)

- Law: Cooperative Societies Proclamation 985/2016; it covers both SACCOs and housing cooperatives.
- Registrar: the federal cooperative authority or the regional (in Addis Ababa, city) cooperative body. A federal-level primary cooperative needs members from two or more regions.
- Members: individuals who live, work or share a profession in an area; at least **50**; a directive may set a lower minimum, never **below 10**.
- Membership: age 18+, able to pay the share and registration fee, accepts the objectives and by-laws.
- Registration documents: founders' meeting minutes; by-laws in 3 copies; names, addresses and signatures of management and control committee members; proof members meet requirements; 3–5-year action plan; proof of initial capital deposited in a bank; description of the place of operation; other items set by directive.
- The name must end with "Cooperative Society with Limited Liability".
- Certificate within **5 consecutive working days** if complete; written reasons within 5 days if refused; court petition within **60 consecutive days**. A temporary certificate (up to 1 year) may be issued once items (a)–(h) are met.
- A registered society needs **no separate business licence** for its by-law activities; it is a legal person with limited liability.
- **SACCOs** collect members' savings, give credit and loan-life insurance; loans only to **members or cooperatives**; interest rate fixed in the by-laws by special resolution.
- **30%** of net profit goes to the reserve fund until it reaches 30% of capital; the general assembly decides the other 70%. The society is exempt from income tax; members pay tax on dividends.
- **Housing cooperatives in Addis Ababa**: registered under this law, but land allocation, savings amounts and city-specific conditions were not found — ask your sub-city cooperative office.

## Official text

Proclamation No. 985/2016, Federal Negarit Gazette 23rd Year No. 7 — https://justice.gov.et/en/law/cooperative-societies-proclamation-2/

- Art. 2(7): "'saving and credit cooperative society' means a society established to provide saving, credit and loan-life-insurance services to its members;"
- Art. 2(20): "'appropriate authority' means the Federal Cooperative Agency, or an organ established at regional levels to execute the cooperative societies proclamation, lead and regulate the cooperative sector;"
- Art. 7: "2/ The primary cooperative society shall be established: a) by individuals who live or work or engaged in specific profession within a given area; and b) by number of members not less than fifty. 3/ ... the appropriate authority may specify in the directive the minimum number of members ...; provided, however, that the minimum number of members shall not be less than 10. 4/ Primary cooperative society established at federal level shall consist members from two or more regional states."
- Art. 9(2): "The phrase 'Cooperative Society with Limited Liability' shall appear in the end of the name of any cooperative society."
- Art. 10(2): "a) minutes of the founders meeting; b) the by-laws of the cooperative society in three copies; c) names, address and signature of the members of the management committee ...; d) name, address and signature of the members of the control committee ...; e) a detailed description which proves that the registered members ... have met the requirements for membership ...; ... g) three up to five years action plan of the society; h) documents showing the amount of initial capital of the society and the capital that has been collected and deposited in a bank account ...; i) the description of the place at which the cooperative society operates; j) other particulars that may be specified in the regulations or directives ..."
- Art. 10(3): "The appropriate authority shall register the cooperative society and issue a certificate of registration within five consecutive working days ..." Art. 10(4): written explanation "within five consecutive working days". Art. 10(5): petition "within 60 consecutive days". Art. 10(7): "... without the need to secure additional business license." Art. 10(8): "temporary certificate which may serve not more than a year".
- Art. 11: "1/ ... shall have juridical personality from the date of its registration. 2/ ... shall not be liable beyond its total asset; and it has limited liability."
- Art. 21(9): "saving and credit cooperate society collect member saving, provide credit, and also provide micro credit life insurance service for members loan or saving ..."
- Art. 24(2): "a) has attained the age of 18; b) is able to pay the share capital and registration fee required by the cooperative society; c) has accepted the objectives of the cooperative society and its by-laws ..."
- Art. 43: the society shall "be exempted from income tax; provided, however, that the members shall pay income tax on their dividends ..."
- Art. 45(1): "Any cooperative society shall deduct 30% of the net profit and allocate for the reserve fund. The amount allocated for the reserve fund shall continue to be deducted until it reaches 30% of the capital ..."
- Art. 48: "1/ A cooperative society shall not extend loan other than to its members or a cooperative society established under this Proclamation. 2/ Any cooperative society for the money lends, shall prescribe the interest rate in its by-laws based on the special resolution of the General Assembly."

## What is not published

- The current name and address of the federal cooperative regulator (the law says "Federal Cooperative Agency") — ask the cooperative office.
- Any directive lowering the 50-member minimum for SACCOs or housing cooperatives, registration fees and model by-laws — ask the cooperative office.
- Addis Ababa rules for housing cooperatives (land, savings, eligibility such as not owning a house) — ask the Addis Ababa cooperative office or sub-city office.
