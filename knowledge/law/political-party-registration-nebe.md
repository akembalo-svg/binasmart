# የፖለቲካ ፓርቲ ምዝገባ — Registering a Political Party

**ማሳሰቢያ (2025 ማሻሻያ):** አዋጅ 1394/2025 ለአገር አቀፍ ፓርቲ የሚያስፈልገውን የክልሎች ስርጭት ቀይሯል። እንዲሁም ከአንቀጽ 96 በኋላ ያሉ አንቀጾች (97–159) ወደ 98–160 ተሸጋግረዋል።

**Note (2025 amendment):** Proclamation 1394/2025 changed the regional-spread rule for nationwide parties and renumbered Arts. 97–159 as 98–160.

## በአጭሩ (Amharic summary)

- ማንኛውም የፖለቲካ ፓርቲ በኢትዮጵያ መንቀሳቀስ የሚችለው በኢትዮጵያ ብሔራዊ ምርጫ ቦርድ ተመዝግቦ የሕጋዊ ሰውነት ምስክር ወረቀት ሲያገኝ ብቻ ነው።
- **አገር አቀፍ ፓርቲ** (አሁን ያለው ሕግ)፦ ቢያንስ 10,000 መሥራች አባላት፤ ከነዚህ ከ40% ያልበለጡት የአንድ ክልል ነዋሪዎች፤ ቀሪዎቹ ቢያንስ በሌሎች **ስድስት** ክልሎች ቋሚ ነዋሪዎች፤ ከእያንዳንዱ ከስድስቱ ክልሎች ቢያንስ 10% መሥራቾች።
- **ክልላዊ ፓርቲ**፦ ከመሥራች አባላቱ ከ60% በላይ የክልሉ ቋሚ ነዋሪዎች መሆን አለባቸው። የሚያስፈልገው የመሥራች አባላት ቁጥር በአዋጁ የእንግሊዝኛና የአማርኛ ቅጂዎች መካከል ስለሚለያይ ከምርጫ ቦርድ ያረጋግጡ።
- መሥራቾች ቢያንስ 18 ዓመት የሞላቸው፣ የመምረጥ መብታቸው ያልተገፈፈ፣ ከሙሉ ስማቸው ጎን የፈረሙና የመኖሪያ ማስረጃ የሰጡ መሆን አለባቸው። ዝርዝሩን የሚያረጋግጥ የቃለ-መሐላ ሰነድ ይያያዛል።
- **ጊዜያዊ ምዝገባ**፦ የጽሑፍ ጥያቄ፣ ለአገር አቀፍ ቢያንስ 200፣ ለክልላዊ ቢያንስ 100 ሰዎች የፈረሙበት ቃለ-ጉባኤ፣ ጊዜያዊ ስም፣ ሕግ ለማክበር ማረጋገጫ እና የቦርዱን የአገልግሎት ክፍያ የከፈሉበት ደረሰኝ።
- ጊዜያዊ ምስክር ወረቀቱ ለ3 ወር ያገለግላል፤ በቂ ምክንያት ካለ ለተጨማሪ 3 ወር ሊራዘም ይችላል።
- **ሙሉ ምዝገባ**፦ መሥራች ጠቅላላ ጉባኤ ካካሄደ በኋላ በመሪው የተፈረመ ማመልከቻ፣ የመመሥረቻ ጽሑፍ፣ የፖለቲካ ፕሮግራም፣ መተዳደሪያ ደንብ እና የመሪዎች ስምና አድራሻ ከፈቃዳቸው ጋር፤ ሰነዶቹ በዋና ቅጂና በኤሌክትሮኒክ ቅጂ ይቀርባሉ።
- በማጭበርበር ለመመዝገብ የሞከረ ፓርቲ ለቀጣይ 5 ዓመታት እንዳይመዘገብ ይታገዳል።
- ቦርዱ ፓርቲን እስከ 5 ዓመት ማገድ ይችላል፤ ይግባኝ በ30 ቀናት ውስጥ ለፌዴራል ከፍተኛ ፍርድ ቤት ይቀርባል። የመሥራች አባላት ቁጥር ከዝቅተኛው በታች ወርዶ በ2 ወር ውስጥ ካልተስተካከለ ምዝገባው ሊሰረዝ ይችላል።
- በምርጫ ወቅት የሚቀርብ የምዝገባ ጥያቄ የመራጮች ምዝገባ ከመጀመሩ 90 ቀን በፊት ከቀረበ ከዕጩዎች ምዝገባ በፊት ይታያል።

## In short (English)

- A party may operate only after NEBE registers it and issues a certificate of legal personality (Art. 66(1)).
- **Nationwide party** (current rule): at least 10,000 founding members; no more than 40% from one region; the rest permanent residents of at least **six** other regions; at least 10% of founders from each of those six regions.
- **Regional party:** more than 60% of founders must be permanent residents of that region. The required number of founders differs between the English and Amharic texts of the law — check the figure with NEBE.
- Founders must be 18+, not deprived of voting rights, sign next to their full name and give proof of residence; a sworn statement confirming founders' details is attached.
- **Provisional registration:** written request; meeting minutes with 200 signatures (nationwide) or 100 (regional); provisional name; commitment to obey the election law; receipt for NEBE's service fee.
- The provisional certificate lasts three months, extendable by three more with good reason.
- **Full registration:** after the founding general assembly, an application signed by the leader, with memorandum of association, political programme, by-laws, and leaders' names, addresses and signed consent — original and electronic copies.
- Fraudulent registration bars the party for the next five years.
- NEBE can suspend a party for up to five years (appeal to the Federal High Court within 30 days); a party whose founders fall below the minimum and does not fix it within two months of notice can be deregistered.
- Applications made during an election period are handled before candidate registration if submitted 90 days before voter registration starts.

## Official text

**Proclamation No. 1162/2019** — https://nebe.org.et/sites/default/files/Ethiopian-Electoral-Proclamtion-No-1162.pdf
**Proclamation No. 1394/2025** — https://nebe.org.et/sites/default/files/Proclamation%201394%20A%20proclamation%20to%20amend%20the%20Ethiopian%20Electoral%2C%20Political%20Parties%20Registrati.pdf

Art. 64(1) (original English): "a) it has at least 10,000 founding members; b) not more than 40 Percent of its founding members are residents of a single region; ..."
Amharic: "ሀ) ፲ሺ መሥራች አባላት ሲኖሩት፤ ለ) ከመስራች አባላቱ ውስጥ ከ፵ በመቶ የማይበልጡት የአንድ ክልል መደበኛ ነዋሪዎች ሲሆኑ፤ ..."

Proc. 1394/2025, Art. 2(16), replacing Art. 64(1)(c)–(d):
"(c) Subject to Sub-Article (1) pharagraph (b) of this Article, the remaining founding members are permanent residents of at least six other regions, and"
"(d) The number of members registered as founders from the six regions as laid down in Sub-Article (1) (c) of this article, shall constitute at least ten percent (10%) of the total founding members from each regions."

Art. 64(5): fraudulent registration → "the party shall be barred from registering as a political party for the next five years".

Art. 65(1)(b) (regional party): "more than sixty percent (60%) of the founding members are permanent residents of the same regional state."
Art. 65(8): requests are dealt with before candidate registration if "submitted 90 days prior to commencement of voter registration".

Art. 66(1): "Any political party can operate as a Political Party in Ethiopia only upon registration by the Board and receipt of a certificate of legal personality".
Art. 66(4): "a written request for registration; meeting minutes with 200 signatures for a Nationwide Party or 100 signatures for a Regional Party; the provisional name of the party to be established; a confirmation to abide by the election law and other relevant laws; and receipt showing the payment of the Board's service fee."
Amharic: "...ለአገር አቀፍ ፓርቲነት ቢያንስ ሁለት መቶ፣ ለክልል ፓርቲነት ቢያንስ አንድ መቶ አመልካቾች የፈረሙበት የስብሰባ ቃለ ጉባኤ..."
Art. 66(5): the provisional certificate "is valid for only three months", extendable "for additional three months".

New Art. 67(1) (Proc. 1394/2025): "Any political party, after conducting its founding general assembly shall submit a registration application signed by its leader."
New Art. 67(3): "The Original and Electronics copies of the documents listed under Sub-Article (2) of this Article shall be submitted with the registration application."

## What is not published

- The amount of NEBE's service fee for provisional registration.
