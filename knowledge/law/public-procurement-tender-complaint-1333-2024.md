# በፌደራል መንግሥት ጨረታ ላይ ቅሬታና ይግባኝ ማቅረብ — Complaining About a Federal Tender

**ማሳሰቢያ፦** የቀድሞው አዋጅ ቁጥር 649/2009 እና በእሱ ሥር የነበሩት የቅሬታ አንቀጾች ተሽረዋል፤ የተካው አዋጅ ቁጥር 1333/2024 ነው (አንቀጽ 88(1)፣ ከመስከረም 9 ቀን 2024 ጀምሮ)። **Note:** Proclamation 649/2009 and its complaint articles are repealed and replaced by Proclamation No. 1333/2024 (Art. 88(1), 9 September 2024). ## In short (English)

- A bidder who has suffered, or is likely to suffer, damage from a public body's act or omission may complain; it is heard only if lodged before the contract is signed (Proclamation 1333/2024, Art. 73).
- Step 1: complain to the head of the public body within 5 working days of when you knew or should have known (Art. 74(2)). Late complaints are not accepted (Directive Art. 154(1)).
- The head must suspend the process and give a written decision within 7 working days, publishing a summary on the electronic system (Art. 74(3)).
- Step 2: if there is no decision or you are not satisfied, appeal to the Board within 5 working days of when the decision was or should have been communicated (Art. 75(1)).
- Complaints about bid-document clarifications/modifications before opening go directly to the Board within 5 days (Art. 75(2)). For public bodies run by a governing board, appeals may go directly to the Board (Directive Art. 153(4)).
- The public body answers the Board within 5 working days; the Board decides within 15 working days, extendable by up to 10 working days (Art. 76).
- The Board may dismiss the appeal, prohibit unlawful action, order the body to proceed lawfully, or annul the unlawful act in whole or part (Art. 77(1)); it publishes decisions on the electronic system (Art. 78).
- Step 3: appeal to the Federal High Court on questions of law within 15 working days of the Board's decision (Art. 81(1)). You must exhaust these remedies before going to an ordinary court (Art. 81(2)).
- No contract may be signed before the complaint and appeal periods expire (Art. 58(5)).
- No appeal lies on: choice of procurement method, rejection of bids under Art. 26, selection of candidates for restricted tender or request for quotation, bid criteria after opening, and disputes arising after contract signature (Art. 80(1)). Improper practice can still be reported to the head of the body, the Authority or another competent body (Art. 80(3)).

## Official text

### Proclamation No. 1333/2024 (English, verbatim)
Source: https://justice.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/seasiisia-seusiNse¡-1333-2016.pdf

**Art. 58(5).** "The Public Body may not sign any contract prior to giving notification of the result of the bid evaluation to all bidders and before the expiration of the period specified under Article 74 and 75 of this Proclamation for lodging complaint or appeal."

**Art. 67(1).** "There is here by re-established aBoard (hereinafter referred to as the "Board") as a quasi-judicial and impartial body which investigates and decides on appeals lodged in regard to the conduct of public procurement and property disposal." Art. 67(2): "The Board shall be accountable to the Minister."

**Art. 73.** "1/ Any candidate or bidder is entitled to submit a complaint or an appeal within the time limit stipulated herein, to the head of the Public Body or to the Board, as the case may be, against an act or an omission of the Public Body ... and can show that he has sustained or is likely to sustain damage as a result of such action or inaction of the Public Body. 2/ Complaint or appeal lodged pursuant to Sub-Article (1) of this Article will be heard if it is lodged before the contract had been signed with the successful bidder."

**Art. 74.** "1/ Any complaint against an act or an omission by a Public Body in regard to a conduct of public procurement or sale of disposable property shall, in the first instance, be submitted to the head of the Public Body. 2/ Any candidate or bidder shall submit his complaint within 5 (five) working days next following the date on which he knew or should have known of the circumstance or decision giving rise to the complaint. 3/ Unless the complaint is resolved by mutual agreement, the head of the Public Body shall suspend the procurement or property disposal proceedings and shall, within 7 (seven) working days after the submission of the complaint, issue a written decision on the complaint and cause the summary of the decision to be disclosed using the electronic system."

**Art. 75.** "1/ If the head of the Public Body does not give decision on the complaint within the time limit stated in Sub-Article (3) of Article 74, or if the complainant is not satisfied with the decision of the head of the Public Body, the complainant is entitled to lodge appeal to the Board within 5 (five) working days next following the date on which the decision was or should have been communicated to the complainant by the Public Body. 2/ ... any candidate not satisfied with the response of the Public Body to inquiries of candidates concerning any modification on the content of the bid document made by the Public Body or clarification thereon before the opening of the bid may lodge appeal directly to the Board within 5 (five) days from the date on which he received or should have received such response."

**Art. 76.** "3/ ... the Public Body ... shall ... submit its statement of response on the appeal to the Board together with documentary evidence supporting the statement within 5 (five) working days from the date on which it received the notification. ... 5/ The Board shall give decision on the appeal within 15 (fifteen) working days following the date on which it received or from the last day on which it should have received the statement of response ... 6/ ... the Board may ... extend the period for deciding the appeal for a period not exceeding 10 (ten) working days ..."

**Art. 77(1).** "a) dismiss the appeal ... b) prohibit the Public Body from acting unlawfully ... c) order the Public Body to proceed in conformity with the law other than giving decision of award or conclusion of contract, d) annul in whole or in part, the unlawful act or decision of the Public Body."

**Art. 81.** "1/ An appeal may be lodged to the Federal High Court on questions of law by any party aggrieved by the decision of the Board within 15 (fifteen) working days next following the date of the decision. 2/ ... any party dissatisfied with the process of Public Procurement and Property disposal, shall not bring his case to adjudication by regular court of law without first exhausting the administrative and quasi-judicial remedies available to it under this Proclamation. 3/ The decision of the Board shall be effective during the course of the appeal unless the court of appeal orders a stay of execution ..."

### Directive No. 1073/2025 (English, verbatim)
Source: https://www.ppa.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Directive_No_1073_2025_Federal_Government_Public_Procurement_Directive.pdf

**Art. 153(4).** "... for Public Bodies managed by a governing board ... appeals ... may be submitted directly to the appeal review board without first being presented to the Head of the Public Body."

**Art. 154(1).** "A bidder or candidate bidder who claims to have suffered harm or injustice during the procurement process shall submit complaints to the Head of the Public Body that issued the tender within five (5) consecutive working days from the date he became aware, or should have become aware, of the final decision that forms the basis of the complaint. Complaints submitted after this period will not be accepted."

**Art. 155(1).** "If the Public Body verifies that the complaint was submitted within the timeframe specified in this Directive, it shall suspend the tender process until a decision on the complaint is issued."

**Art. 156(4).** "If the Head of the Public Body fails to issue a decision within the stipulated timeframe, the appellant may submit an appeal to the board within five (5) working days after the expiration of the seven-day deadline."
