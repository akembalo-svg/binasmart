## በአጭሩ (Amharic summary)

- የስደተኞችና ተመላሾች አገልግሎት (ስተአ) በስደተኞችና በጥገኝነት ጠያቂዎች ጉዳይ ላይ መንግሥትን የሚወክል ዋና ተቋም ነው። ቀደም ሲል "የስደተኞችና ከስደት ተመላሾች ጉዳይ ኤጀንሲ" ይባል ነበር። ስሙ የተቀየረው በአዋጅ ቁጥር 1263/2014 ዓ.ም ነው።
- ዋና ዋና ሥራዎቹ፦ የስደተኛነት ሁኔታን መወሰን፣ የስደተኛ ካምፖችን ማቋቋምና ማስተባበር፣ ለስደተኞች አካላዊ ጥበቃ መስጠት፣ መሠረታዊ አገልግሎቶችን ማቅረብና ማስተባበር፣ አጋር ድርጅቶችን ማመቻቸት እና ሁኔታው ሲፈቅድ ስደተኞችን ወደ አገራቸው መመለስ ናቸው።

## In short (English)

- The Refugees and Returnees Service (RRS) is the government's lead agency for all matters concerning refugees and asylum-seekers. It was formerly the Agency for Refugee and Returnee Affairs (ARRA) and was renamed under Proclamation No. 1263/2021 (1263/2014 E.C.).
- Its main work: deciding refugee status, setting up and coordinating camps, physical protection, providing and coordinating basic services, supporting NGO partners, and organizing repatriation when conditions allow.
- Its mission also covers reintegrating Ethiopian returnees.
- Documents RRS issues to refugees: passing permit, proof of registration, ID card, and birth, death, marriage and divorce certificates.
- Asylum seekers start by registering their application with RRS at the Gambela, Asosa, Melkadida, Jigjiga or Metema branches or at the Addis Ababa head office. RRS must decide within six months (Proclamation 1110/2019 Art. 16(1)).
- A rejected applicant can appeal to the Refugee Appeal Board and, if needed, to the Federal Supreme Court.
- **Head office contact (Addis Ababa):** phone +251111551111, fax +251111552008, P.O. Box 84, email info@rrs.gov.et, website rrs.et (has an online contact form).
- Hours: Monday–Friday, 9:00 AM–5:00 PM.
- Branch offices: Gambella, Melkadida, Assosa, Jigjiga and Semera, plus the Alem-Wach and Moyale refugee sites.
- RRS reports 1.1 million registered refugees and asylum-seekers in Ethiopia (2025).

**RRS, Our Mandates** — https://rrs.et/mandates/
> The key mandate of RRS is to support and maintain the physical safety of refugees as well as ensure refugees live in dignity until durable solutions are found to their plights.
> Serve as a key government agency and representative on all matters of refugees and asylum-seekers. Conduct refugee status determination exercises and deciding on refugee status. Establish refugee camps and managing the overall coordination of camp activities. Provide physical protection and maintain the well-being of all people of concern. Provide and coordinate basic and social service delivery to refugees. Coordinate country-level refugee assistance programs. Assist and facilitate NGO partners and other stakeholder interventions in the discharge of their activities. Facilitate and undertake repatriation movements when the causes of refugee displacement are solved.

**RRS, History** — https://rrs.et/history-of-rrs/
> ... the Agency changed its nomenclature to “Refugees and Returnees Service” based on Proclamation No. 1263/2014 under the National Security and Intelligence Services.

**RRS, Protection** — https://rrs.et/registration-and-protection/
> Refugee documentations are issued by RRS, these documents are namely such as passing permit, Proof of Registration, Identity Cards and Vital Event Documents which includes, birth, death, marriage and divorce certificates.
> The RSD process begins when an asylum seeker registers their application for asylum with RRS. RRS can be approached at their branch offices in Gambela, Asosa, Melkedida ,Jigjiga, Metema and Addis Ababa (Head office).
> Applicants whose application are rejected have an opportunity to appeal with to the Refugee Appeal Board and if required to the Federal Supreme Court.
