# ለአርሶ አደሮችና ማኅበረሰቦች የመስኖ ልማት ድጋፍ — How Farmers and Communities Get Irrigation Development Support

## በአጭሩ (Amharic summary)

- በሕግ የተቀመጠው ዋናው መንገድ የመስኖ ውሃ ተጠቃሚዎች ማኅበር ማቋቋም ነው (አዋጅ ቁጥር 841/2014 እ.ኤ.አ.)። ማኅበሩ የመስኖና የፍሳሽ ሥርዓቱን ያስተዳድራል፣ ውሃውን ለአባላቱ በፍትሐዊነት ያከፋፍላል፣ አባላቱንም በመስኖ ዘዴዎች ያሠለጥናል (አንቀጽ 4)።
- ቢያንስ 5 የመስኖ ውሃ ተጠቃሚዎች ማኅበር ማቋቋም ይችላሉ። የአገልግሎት ክልሉን የሚለይ ጊዜያዊ አስተባባሪ ቡድን እና ከ12 የማይበልጡ አባላት ያሉት መሥራች ኮሚቴ ያደራጃሉ (አንቀጽ 10(1))።
- ማኅበሩ በአባላቱ ፍላጎት ሊቋቋም ይችላል። በፌዴራል በጀት በተገነባ ዘመናዊ መስኖ ላይ ደግሞ ተቆጣጣሪው አካል ሊያቋቁመው ይችላል (አንቀጽ 7)።
- የአባልነት መሠረቱ በመሬት ይዞታ ሥርዓት መሬት ይዞ ከባህላዊ ወይም ከዘመናዊ የመስኖ ሥርዓት ውሃ የሚጠቀም መሆን ነው (አንቀጽ 9(1))።
- ባህላዊ መስኖ የሚያስተዳድር ማኅበር በውሃ ሀብት አስተዳደር አዋጅ ቁጥር 197/1992 መሠረት የውሃ አጠቃቀም ፈቃድ ጠይቆ የማግኘት መብት አለው (አንቀጽ 9(2))።
- በፌዴራል በጀት ለተገነባ መስኖ ማኅበራትን የሚያደራጅና የሚመዘግብ እንዲሁም ስልጠናና የቴክኒክ ድጋፍ የሚሰጥ "ተቆጣጣሪ አካል" ሚኒስቴሩ ነው። በክልል በጀት ለተገነባ መስኖ ደግሞ ክልሉ የሚሰይመው አካል ነው (አንቀጽ 2(16))።
- ማኅበሩ በአገልግሎት ክልሉ ውስጥ በመንግሥት ገንዘብ የተገነባ ወይም የታደሰ የዘመናዊ መስኖ ክፍል እንዲተላለፍለት ለመሠረተ ልማት አካሉ ማመልከት ይችላል። ዝውውሩ በወጪ መመለስ መርህ ላይ ተመሥርቶ በስምምነት ይፈጸማል።
- ድርቅ በሚያጠቃቸው ቆላማ፣ አርብቶ አደርና ከፊል አርብቶ አደር አካባቢዎች ልማት ላይ ያሉ የአደጋ ሥጋቶችን በዋናነት የሚመራው የመስኖና ቆላማ አካባቢ ሚኒስቴር ነው (አዋጅ 1386/2017 አንቀጽ 20(16))።
- የድጋፍ ፕሮግራሞች ዝርዝር፣ የማመልከቻ ቅጽ፣ የብድር ወይም የድጎማ መጠን በይፋ አልተገኘም። የወረዳዎን ግብርና ጽ/ቤት ወይም የክልልዎን የመስኖ ቢሮ ይጠይቁ።

## In short (English)

- The route set out in law is to form an Irrigation Water Users' Association (Proclamation 841/2014). The association runs the irrigation and drainage system, shares water fairly among members and trains members in irrigation techniques (Art. 4).
- At least 5 irrigation water users can form an association. They set up a provisional initiative team to define the service area and a founders committee of no more than 12 members (Art. 10(1)).
- An association can be formed by its members, or by the supervising body on modern systems built with federal money (Art. 7).
- Members are landholders who irrigate their land from a traditional or modern irrigation system (Art. 9(1)).
- An association that manages a traditional system can apply for a water use permit under Water Resources Management Proclamation 197/2000 (Art. 9(2)).
- The "supervising body" organises and registers associations and gives them training and technical help. It is the Ministry for schemes built with federal money and a body chosen by the regional state for schemes built with regional money (Art. 2(16)).
- An association can apply to the infrastructure body to take over part of a government-built modern scheme in its service area. The transfer is made by agreement, based on cost recovery.
- The Ministry of Irrigation and Lowland leads on disaster risks tied to developing drought-prone lowland, pastoral and semi-pastoral areas (Proclamation 1386/2025 Art. 20(16)).
- No official list of support programmes, application form, or loan or subsidy amounts was found. Ask your woreda agriculture office or your regional irrigation bureau.

## Official text

**Irrigation Water Users' Associations Proclamation No. 841/2014 (English, FAOLEX copy)** — https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/eth183180.pdf

> Art. 2(16) “supervising body” means the Ministry, regarding irrigation infrastructures constructed with federal government budget, and for irrigation infrastructures constructed with state government budget, a body designated by the state government as being responsible for organizing and registering associations, providing training and other technical assistance to associations or undertaking other activities specified in this Proclamation;

> Art. 3 This Proclamation shall be applicable to irrigation water user' associations, which are formed on irrigation infrastructures constructed by federal government throughout Ethiopia.

> Art. 4 An association formed pursuant to this Proclamation shall have the objectives to: 1/ manage an irrigation and drainage system wholly or partly in its service area and provide water equitably to its members for agricultural purpose; ... 3/ maintain, rejuvenate and improve the irrigation and drainage system within its service area and to undertake construction and reconstruction works as may be necessary; ... 6/ procure, substitute, maintain and operate irrigation equipments; 7/ train its members in irrigation techniques

> Art. 9(2) An association formed to manage a traditional irrigation and drainage system pursuant to sub-article (1) of this Article shall be entitled to apply for and to obtain a water use permit in accordance with the Ethiopian Water Resources Management Proclamation No. 197/2000.

> Art. 10(1) Irrigation water users not less than five may form association; to this effect they shall organize provisional initiative team to identify the service area of the proposed association and also founders committee which may not exceed twelve potential members of the association.

> Transfer of Modern Irrigation and Drainage Systems, sub-art. 2: An association may apply to the appropriate infrastructure body to transfer to itself, for indefinite period, part of a modern irrigation and drainage system constructed or rehabilitated by government finance, if such infrastructure lies within its service area.

**Ethiopian Disaster Risk Management Proclamation No. 1386/2025, Art. 20(16)** — https://justice.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/አዋጅ-ቁጥር-1386-2017-1.pdf
> Ministry of Irrigation and Lowland:- shall act as the lead sector institution for addressing disaster risks related to the development of drought-prone lowland, pastoral, and semi-pastoral areas

## What is not published

- Contacts for the Ministry of Irrigation and Lowland, its support programmes (small-scale irrigation, pumps, credit) and how to apply: not found, because the website would not load. Ask the ministry or your regional bureau.
- Whether Proclamation 841/2014 has been amended: no amendment was found. The law says "the Ministry" without naming which ministry holds that role today. Ask the office.
- Fees or water charges: the law lets each association's general assembly set members' fees and fines; no national amount was found.
- Irrigation loan projects (IFAD, World Bank) appear in ratification laws, but their eligibility rules are not published as citizen guidance.
