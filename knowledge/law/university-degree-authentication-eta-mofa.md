# የዩኒቨርሲቲ ዲግሪ ለውጭ ሀገር ማረጋገጥ — Authenticating an Ethiopian University Degree for Use Abroad

## በአጭሩ (Amharic summary)

- **ቅደም ተከተል፦** (1) ዲግሪውና ትራንስክሪፕቱ በተቋሙ ሬጅስትራርና ኃላፊ ይፈረማሉ፣ ይታተማሉ። (2) በኢፌዲሪ የትምህርትና ሥልጠና ባለሥልጣን (ETA) ይረጋገጣሉ። (3) ከዚያ በውጭ ጉዳይ ሚኒስቴር (MoFA) ይረጋገጣሉ (legalization)። ማሳሰቢያ፦ የMoFA ገጽ (ኤፕሪል 2025) የETA/HERQA ማረጋገጫን በስም የሚጠቅሰው ለግል ተቋማት ዲግሪ ነው። ለመንግሥት ዩኒቨርሲቲ ዲግሪ የሬጅስትራር ማረጋገጫ ይጠቅሳል። ስለዚህ ከመሄድዎ በፊት MoFAን ይጠይቁ።
- የETA ኦፊሴላዊ ድረ-ገጽ https://neta.gov.et/ ነው (eta.gov.et የቴክኖሎጂ ባለሥልጣን ስለሆነ አያምታታዎት)። ማመልከቻው ኦንላይን በ https://neta.gov.et/requestverification ይቀርባል።
- ማመልከቻው የፋይዳ (Fayda) ብሔራዊ መታወቂያ ቁጥር (FIN) እና በስልክ የሚላክ OTP ይጠይቃል። ሰነዶቹን ከፊትና ከኋላ ስካን አድርገው ይጭናሉ።
- **የሚያስፈልጉ ሰነዶች (ETA)፦**
  - ከጀርባው የኮሌጁ ኃላፊና ሬጅስትራር ፊርማና ማኅተም ያለበት ኦሪጅናል ዲግሪ
  - በተመሳሳይ የተፈረመና የታተመ ኦሪጅናል ትራንስክሪፕት (Student Copy)
  - ለዲግሪው መግቢያ የተጠቀሙበት ኦሪጅናል የ12ኛ ክፍል ሰርተፊኬት (በፈተናዎች አገልግሎት/EAES የተረጋገጠ) ወይም ዲፕሎማ (በክልሉ የቴክኒክና ሙያ ተቋም የተረጋገጠ እና የደረጃ 4 COC ያለው)
  - መግቢያው የውጭ ሀገር ማስረጃ ከሆነ ከETA የተገኘ የአቻ ግመታ ደብዳቤ
  - ስም ከቀየሩ የፍርድ ቤት ውሳኔ
  - ማስተርስ ለማረጋገጥ መጀመሪያ የመጀመሪያ ዲግሪዎ መረጋገጥ አለበት።
- **ክፍያ፦** በመጨረሻ በሚሰጥዎ የክፍያ ሊንክ በቴሌብር (telebirr) ወይም በሲቢኢ ብር (CBE-Birr) ብቻ ይከፈላል። በመሶብ የአንድ ማዕከል አገልግሎት ከተጠቀሙ ተጨማሪ 100 ብር ይከፍላሉ። የETA የማረጋገጫ ክፍያ መጠን በገጹ ላይ አልተገለጸም።
- የቀጠሮ ቀን በኢሜይል ወይም በSMS ሲደርስዎ ራስዎ ወይም ሕጋዊ ውክልና ያለው ተወካይ ሁሉንም ኦሪጅናል ሰነዶች ይዞ መቅረብ አለበት። ያልተሟላ ማመልከቻ አይስተናገድም።
- ETA በአዲስ አበባ ሽሮ ሜዳ፣ ከአሜሪካ ኤምባሲ ፊት ለፊት ይገኛል። ነፃ የስልክ ጥሪ፦ 9799። ስልክ፦ +251 111 23 22 30 ወይም +251 111 23 61 30። ኢሜይል፦ Info@eta.et። የሥራ ሰዓት፦ ሰኞ–ዓርብ 8:30–17:00፣ ቅዳሜ 8:30–12:30። የማመልከቻዎን ሁኔታ በ https://neta.gov.et/check-status ይከታተሉ።
- የ12ኛ ክፍል ሰርተፊኬትዎን EAES አረጋግጦ በ https://services.eaes.et/ በኩል ኦንላይን ወደ ETA እንዲልክልዎ ማመልከት ይችላሉ። ቢሮ መሄድ አያስፈልግም።
- በአዲስ አበባ ዩኒቨርሲቲ የተሰጡ ሰነዶች በዩኒቨርሲቲው ሬጅስትራር፣ በክልል ዩኒቨርሲቲዎች የተሰጡ ደግሞ በሰጠው ተቋም ሬጅስትራር መረጋገጥ አለባቸው (የMoFA መስፈርት)።

## In short (English)

- **Order:** (1) The degree and transcript are signed and sealed by the institution's registrar and head. (2) The Education and Training Authority (ETA) authenticates them. (3) The Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MoFA) then legalizes them. Note: the April 2025 MoFA page names the ETA/HERQA step only for degrees from private institutions. For public-university documents it names registrar certification. Confirm with MoFA before you go.
- ETA's official portal is https://neta.gov.et/ (eta.gov.et is a different body, the Technology Authority). Apply online at https://neta.gov.et/requestverification.
- The form asks for your Fayda national ID number (FIN) and a one-time code (OTP) sent to your phone. Upload each document scanned front and back.
- **Documents ETA requires:**
  - the original degree, signed and stamped on the back by the college head and registrar
  - the original transcript (student copy), signed and stamped the same way
  - the original document you used to enter the degree: a Grade 12 certificate authenticated by the examinations agency (EAES), or a diploma authenticated by the regional TVET agency or zone/city labour and skills office, plus a Level 4 COC
  - an ETA equivalence letter if you entered with a foreign qualification
  - a court decision if you changed your name
  - for a master's degree, your first degree must be authenticated first.
- **Payment:** pay only through the payment link given at the end of the application, by telebirr or CBE-Birr. Applying through a Messob One-Stop Center costs an extra 100 birr. The ETA page does not show the authentication fee itself.
- When you get your appointment date by email or SMS, you or a representative with a legal power of attorney must bring all the original documents. Incomplete applications are not processed.
- ETA is at Shiro Meda, opposite the US Embassy, Addis Ababa. Toll-free 9799. Phone +251 111 23 22 30 or +251 111 23 61 30. Email Info@eta.et. Open Mon–Fri 8:30–17:00 and Sat 8:30–12:30. Track your application at https://neta.gov.et/check-status.
- EAES can authenticate your Grade 12 certificate and send it to ETA online through https://services.eaes.et/, with no office visit needed.
- MoFA's rules: documents from Addis Ababa University are certified by the University Registrar, and documents from regional universities by the issuing institution's registrar.

## What is not published

- **The ETA authentication fee.** It is shown only during payment on the portal.
- **MoFA's legalization fee, office address, hours and online application steps.** The MoFA website (mfa.gov.et) could not be opened on 17 September 2026. Ask MoFA or the Ethiopian embassy where you will use the document.
- Whether MoFA now requires ETA authentication for public-university degrees as well. The archived MoFA page names only registrar certification for those degrees.
- Whether the destination country also needs an embassy stamp or an apostille. Ask the embassy of that country.
- The ETA page says only "a few working days" for processing. No exact number of days is given.
