# አከራይ ተከራይን ከማስወጣቱ በፊት የሚሰጠው ማስጠንቀቂያ — Landlord's notice period before eviction (Rent Control Proclamation 1320/2024)

## በአጭሩ

- የመኖሪያ ቤት ኪራይ ውል ቢያንስ **ሁለት ዓመት** ነው (አንቀጽ 6(1))። ውሉ ሳያልቅ አከራዩ በዚህ አዋጅ ከተፈቀደው ውጭ ተከራይን ማስወጣት ወይም ኪራይ መጨመር **አይችልም** (አንቀጽ 6(7))።
- **የ6 ወር ማስጠንቀቂያ:** ቤቱ በውርስ፣ በሽያጭ፣ በዕዳ ወይም በሌላ ሕጋዊ ምክንያት ለሌላ ሰው ከተላለፈ፣ ውሉ ሊቋረጥ የሚችለው ቤቱ ከተላለፈበት ጊዜ ጀምሮ ለተከራዩ **የ6 ወር ማስጠንቀቂያ** ሲሰጥ ብቻ ነው (አንቀጽ 6(3))።
- ቤቱ **በስጦታ** ከተላለፈ ግን ተከራዩ የውል ዘመኑ እስኪያልቅ ድረስ በቤቱ የመቆየት መብት አለው (አንቀጽ 6(4))። አዲሱ ባለቤት ከፈቀደ ተከራዩ መቀጠል ይችላል (አንቀጽ 6(5))።
- **ተከራዩ መልቀቅ ከፈለገ** የ**ሁለት ወር** የቅድሚያ ማስታወቂያ ይሰጣል። ውሉ በሁለቱም ስምምነትም ሊቋረጥ ይችላል (አንቀጽ 15)።
- **ያለማስጠንቀቂያ መቋረጥ (አንቀጽ 16):** ተከራዩ ኪራይን ለመጀመሪያ ጊዜ ከመክፈያ ቀኑ **15 ቀን** ካሳለፈ፣ ለሁለተኛ ጊዜ **7 ቀን** ካሳለፈ፣ ቤቱን ያለፈቃድ ለንግድ ከተጠቀመ፣ የአካባቢውን ሰላም በተደጋጋሚ ካወከ፣ ቤቱን ለወንጀል ከተጠቀመ፣ በቤቱ ላይ ጉልህ ጉዳት ካደረሰ ወይም በሌላ ተመሳሳይ ምክንያት አከራዩ ያለማስጠንቀቂያ ውሉን ሊያቋርጥ ይችላል።
- ከላይ በተጠቀሱት ምክንያቶች ውሉ ከተቋረጠ፣ ለአዲሱ ተከራይ የሚከራየው ዋጋ የቀድሞው ውል መነሻ ዋጋ ነው (አንቀጽ 17 እና 6(6))።
- **ቅሬታ:** ክርክር በጽሑፍ ለተቆጣጣሪው አካል ይቀርባል። የተመዘገበ የኪራይ ውልና የባንክ ወይም የኤሌክትሮኒክ ክፍያ ደረሰኝ ያስፈልጋል (አንቀጽ 21)። ማመልከቻው ጉዳዩ ከተፈጠረ በ**30 ቀን** ውስጥ ይቀርባል፤ ውሳኔው በ**30 የሥራ ቀን** ይሰጣል (አንቀጽ 22)።
- በውሳኔው ያልተስማማ ሰው ቤቱ ባለበት አካባቢ ላለው **የቅሬታ ሰሚ ኮሚቴ** በ**15 የሥራ ቀን** ውስጥ ያመለክታል (አንቀጽ 23)። ከዚያም ለፍርድ ቤት ይግባኝ ይላል። ፍርድ ቤቱ በ30 ቀን ይወስናል፤ ውሳኔው የመጨረሻ ነው (አንቀጽ 26)።
- ስለ አዋጁ ጥሰት ጥቆማ ጉዳዩ ከታወቀ በ30 ቀን ውስጥ በማስረጃ በጽሑፍ ይቀርባል። አስተዳደራዊ ቅጣቱ ከዚያ ቤት የሦስት ወር ኪራይ መብለጥ አይችልም (አንቀጽ 20)።

## In short (English)

- A residential rent contract lasts at least **two years** (Art. 6(1)). Evicting the tenant or raising the rent before the term ends, except as this law allows, is prohibited (Art. 6(7)).
- **Six months' notice:** if the house passes to someone else by inheritance, sale, sale for a debt or any other means, the contract can be ended only by giving the tenant **six months' notice** (Art. 6(3)).
- If the house is **donated**, the tenant may stay until the contract ends; the new owner takes over the landlord's rights and duties (Art. 6(4)). The tenant may also stay on if the new owner agrees (Art. 6(5)).
- **A tenant who wants to leave** gives **two months' notice**. The parties can also end the contract by agreement (Art. 15).
- **No notice needed (Art. 16):** the landlord may end the contract without notice if the tenant is late with rent by **15 days** the first time or **7 days** the second time; uses the house for business without consent; disturbs the neighbourhood; commits crimes there or uses the house for crime; causes substantial damage; or for any other similar cause.
- After such a termination, the rent for the next tenant is the baseline price of the old contract (Arts. 17 and 6(6)).
- **Disputes:** apply in writing to the regulatory body within **30 days**, with the registered contract and a bank or electronic payment receipt. It decides within **30 working days** (Arts. 21–22). The next step is the local **grievance hearing committee**, within **15 working days** (Art. 23). After that you can appeal to the court, which decides within 30 days; its decision is final (Art. 26).
- Anyone can report a violation in writing, with evidence, within 30 days of learning of it. Administrative penalties cannot exceed three months' rent for that house (Art. 20).

**Article 6. Term of Contract of Rent (English column)**
> 1/ Subject to the provisions of Article 15 and 16 of this Proclamation, a contract rent shall have a minimum duration of two years.
> 3/ A rental contract may be terminated by providing the tenant with a six month notice when the ownership of the rental property is transferred to third parties through inheritance, sold to satisfy a debt, or by any other means.
> 4/ Notwithstanding the provision of Sub- Article (3) above, the tenant shall retain the right to see through the full duration of the rental contract when the ownership of the rental property is transferred to third parties through donation. The donee shall have the rights and obligations of the donor.
> 7/ Evicting the tenant or rent increase prior to the expiry of the duration of the contract of rent other than in accordance with this proclamation shall be prohibited.

**አንቀጽ 6 (የአማርኛ ጽሑፍ)**
> ፫/ የአንድ ቤት ባለቤትነት በውርስ፣ በሽያጭ፣ በዕዳ ወይም በሌላ ማናቸውም ህጋዊ ምክንያት ለሌላ ወገን ከተላለፈ የመኖሪያ ቤት ኪራይ ውሉ ሊቋረጥ የሚችለው ቤቱ ከተላለፈበት ጊዜ ጀምሮ የ፮ ወር ማስጠንቀቂያ ለተከራዩ በመስጠት ነው።
> ፯/ የቤት ኪራይ ውል ዘመን ሳይጠናቀቅ ተከራይን ከቤት ማስወጣት ወይም በዚህ አዋጅ ከተፈቀደው ሁኔታ ውጭ የዋጋ ጭማሪ ማድረግ አይቻልም፡፡

**Article 15. Termination by Agreement or Notice**
> A contract of rent may be terminated through mutual agreement of the parties, by the tenant's decision to vacate with a two-month notice, or in accordance with Article 6(3) of this Proclamation.

**አንቀጽ 15**
> ማንኛውም የመኖሪያ ቤት ኪራይ ውል የኪራይ ዘመኑ ሳይጠናቀቅ በአከራዩና ተከራዩ ስምምነት፣ ተከራይ ቤቱን መልቀቅ ሲፈልግ የሁለት ወር የቅድሚያ ማስታወቂያ በመስጠት ወይም በዚህ አዋጅ አንቀጽ ፮ (፫) አግባብ ማስጠንቀቂያ በመስጠት ሊቋረጥ ይችላል፡፡

**Article 16. Termination Without Notice**
> 1/ A contract of rent may be terminated by the Owner without notice when the tenant;
> a) Failed to pay the rent, for the first time, 15 days from the date of payment indicated in the contract
> b) Notwithstanding to paragaraph (a) of this sub-article, faild to pay the rent for the second time, with in 7 days of the date specified in the contract,
> c) Uses the rental property for business without the consent of the owner
> d) Disrupts the peace and tranquillity of the neighbourhood.
> e) Engaged in illegal activities or use the rental property for the commission of crime
> f) Inflicts substantial damage on the property, whether intentionally or due to negligence, or
> g) Any other similar cause .

**Articles 22, 23 and 26 (complaints)**
> 22(1) An application to be brought under Article 21 of this Proclamation shall be submitted in writing to the Regulatory Body within 30 days from the occurrence of the event that gave rise to the complaint. (2) The Regulatory Body, to which the application is submitted, shall investigate the complaint and render a decision within 30 working days.
> 23. Any person aggrieved by the decision under Article 22(2) above shall make an application to the Grievance Hearing Committee ... in the locality where the residential house is situated, within 15 working days.
> 26(2) The Court where the appeal is presented ... shall render a decision within 30 days from the date of the application. (3) The decision of the court shall be final.

## What is not published

- The law does not say which office is the "regulatory body" in each town or sub-city. Ask your woreda or sub-city housing/rent office. In Addis Ababa, ask the city administration.
- No directive on complaint grounds or penalty amounts (promised in Art. 20(2)) was found. Ask the regulatory body.
- The law does not define "any other similar cause" in Art. 16(1)(g). A disputed case goes to the regulatory body, then the committee, then the court.
