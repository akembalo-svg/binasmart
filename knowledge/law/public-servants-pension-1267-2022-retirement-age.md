# የመንግሥት ሠራተኞች የጡረታ መውጫ ዕድሜ — Retirement Age of Public Servants

**ማሳሰቢያ፦** የቀድሞው የመንግሥት ሠራተኞች የጡረታ አዋጅ ቁጥር 714/2003 (በአዋጅ ቁጥር 907/2007 እንደተሻሻለ) በአዋጅ ቁጥር 1267/2014 አንቀጽ 63(1)(ሀ) ተሽሯል። አሁን የሚሠራው አዋጅ 1267/2014 ነው።

**Note:** the old Public Servants' Pension Proclamation No. 714/2011 (as amended by Proclamation No. 907/2015) was repealed and replaced by Proclamation No. 1267/2022, Art. 63(1)(a). The law in force is 1267/2022 (gazette dated 18 March 2022).

- ከመከላከያና ከፖሊስ አባላት ውጭ ላሉ የመንግሥት ሠራተኞች የጡረታ መውጫ ዕድሜ 60 ዓመት ነው (አንቀጽ 18(1)(ሐ))።
- ዕድሜው የሚታሰበው ሠራተኛው ለመጀመሪያ ጊዜ ሲቀጠር ባስመዘገበው የልደት ዘመን ነው (አንቀጽ 18(1))።
- የመከላከያ ሠራዊት አባላት የጡረታ ዕድሜ በሠራዊቱ መተዳደሪያ ሕግ፣ የፖሊስ አባላት ደግሞ በፖሊስ መተዳደሪያ ሕግ ይወሰናል (አንቀጽ 18(1)(ሀ)፣(ለ))።
- የሚኒስትሮች ምክር ቤት አስተዳደሩ በሚያቀርበው ጥናት መሠረት ለልዩ ሙያዎች ከ60 በላይ፣ ለከባድ ወይም ለጤናና ለሕይወት አስጊ ሥራዎች ደግሞ ከ60 በታች የጡረታ ዕድሜ ሊወስን ይችላል (አንቀጽ 18(2)፣(3))።
- ቢያንስ 10 ዓመት አገልግሎ የጡረታ ዕድሜ ላይ ሲደርስ የሚወጣ ሠራተኛ ዕድሜ ልክ የጡረታ አበል ያገኛል (አንቀጽ 19(1))።
- ቢያንስ 10 ዓመት አገልግሎ በራሱ ፈቃድ ወይም በሌላ ምክንያት የለቀቀ ሠራተኛ የጡረታ ዕድሜ ሲደርስ ዕድሜ ልክ አበል ያገኛል (አንቀጽ 19(2))።
- ቢያንስ 25 ዓመት አገልግሎ የለቀቀ ሠራተኛ ከጡረታ ዕድሜው 5 ዓመት ቀደም ብሎ ጀምሮ ዕድሜ ልክ አበል ያገኛል (አንቀጽ 19(3))። ይህ በዲሲፕሊን ምክንያት ለተሰናበተ ሠራተኛ አይሠራም (አንቀጽ 19(4))።
- አንድ ሙሉ የሥራ ዘመን ያገለገለ እና ሲለቅ 50 ዓመት የሞላው ከፍተኛ የመንግሥት ባለሥልጣን ወይም የምክር ቤት አባል ዕድሜ ልክ አበል ያገኛል (አንቀጽ 19(5))።

- የጡረታ ጉዳይን የሚያስፈጽመው የመንግሥት ሠራተኞች ማኅበራዊ ዋስትና አስተዳደር (PSSSA) ነው።

## In short (English)

- The retirement age is 60 for public servants other than members of the defence force and the police (Art. 18(1)(c)).
- Age is counted from the date of birth the employee registered when first employed (Art. 18(1)).
- For the defence force and the police, the retirement age is set by their own administration laws (Art. 18(1)(a), (b)).
- Based on studies from the Administration, the Council of Ministers may set a higher age for professions that deserve special consideration, and a lower age for hazardous or health- and life-threatening jobs (Art. 18(2), (3)).
- A public servant with at least 10 years of service who retires at retirement age gets a pension for life (Art. 19(1)).
- A public servant with at least 10 years of service who resigns or leaves for another reason gets a pension for life once they reach retirement age (Art. 19(2)).
- A public servant with at least 25 years of service who leaves gets a pension for life starting 5 years before retirement age (Art. 19(3)). This does not apply to someone dismissed for disciplinary reasons (Art. 19(4)).
- A senior government official or member of parliament who served at least one full term and is 50 or older on leaving office gets a pension for life (Art. 19(5)).
- A public servant who leaves because the office closed, because of staff reduction or privatisation, and who has at least 25 years of service and is at least 50 years old, gets a pension for life on the decision of the Council of Ministers (Art. 19(7)).
- Pensions are handled by the Public Servants Social Security Administration (PSSSA).

### Art. 18 — Retirement Age
> "18. Retirement Age 1/ The retirement age of a public servant shall, based on the date of birth registered when he was employed for the first time, be: a) for a member of the defense force, the age determined by the law regulating to the administration of the defense force; b) for a member of the police, the age determined by the law regulating the administration of the police; c) for other public servant, 60 years. 2/ On the basis of studies submitted to it by the Administration, the Council of Ministers may determine retirement age higher than the age provided under Sub-Article (1) of this Article with respect to professions that may deserve special consideration; 3/ On the basis of studies submitted to it by the Administration, the Council of Ministers may determine retirement age lesser than the age provided under Sub-Article (1) of this Article for public servants working on hazardous jobs or on jobs involving risks to health and life."

> "፲፰. የጡረታ መውጫ ዕድሜ ፩/ የጡረታ መውጫ ዕድሜ የመንግሥት ሠራተኛው ለመጀመሪያ ጊዜ ሲቀጠር በቅድሚያ የመዘገበውን የልደት ዘመን መሠረት በማድረግ እንደሚከተለው ይሆናል፡ ሀ) ለመከላከያ ሠራዊት አባላት በሠራዊቱ መተዳደሪያ ሕግ በሚወሰን ዕድሜ፤ ለ) ለፖሊስ አባላት በፖሊስ መተዳደሪያ ሕግ በሚወሰን ዕድሜ፤ ሐ) ለሌሎች የመንግሥት ሠራተኞች ፷ ዓመት፡፡"

### Art. 19 — Retirement Pension (extracts)
> "1/ A public servant who has completed at least 10 years of service and retires upon attaining retirement age shall receive retirement pension for life;"

> "2/ A public servant who has completed at least 10 years and separates from the service by voluntary resignation or for any other cause other than those provided for in this Proclamation, commencing from the date of July 08, 2015, shall receive retirement pension for life upon attaining retirement age pursuant to Sub-Article (1) of Article 18 of this Proclamation;"

> "3/ A public servant who has completed at least 25 years and separates from the service by voluntary resignation or for any other cause other than those provided for in this Proclamation, shall receive retirement pension for life beginning with five years prior to retirement age;"

> "5/ Notwithstanding the provisions of Sub-Article (1) of this Article, a senior government official or a member of parliament, who has served at least one full term and has attained the age of 50 years when he leaves office shall receive retirement pension for life;"

Sub-articles 4 and 7 are jumbled in the text extraction. In summary: 19(4) says sub-article 3 does not apply to a public servant who leaves on disciplinary grounds (without affecting their pension at retirement age); 19(7) gives a life pension, on a Council of Ministers decision, to a public servant with at least 25 years of service and aged at least 50 who leaves because the office ceased to exist, because of workforce reduction or privatisation.
