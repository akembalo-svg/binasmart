# የሆቴል ኮከብ ደረጃ አሰጣጥ — How Hotel Star Rating Is Done in Ethiopia

## በአጭሩ (Amharic summary)

- የሆቴሎችና ተዛማጅ የቱሪዝም መሠረተ ልማቶች የኮከብ ደረጃ የሚሰጠው በቱሪዝም ሚኒስቴር ነው። ሚኒስቴሩ ሥራቸውንም ይቆጣጠራል (የሚኒስቴሩ ድረ-ገጽ፣ "Mandates")።
- በሕግ፦ የቱሪስት አገልግሎት ሰጪዎችን ደረጃ የሚወስነውና አፈጻጸሙን የሚቆጣጠረው የቱሪዝም ሚኒስቴር ነው (አዋጅ 1263/2014 አንቀጽ 24(1)(m))። የቱሪዝም መስህቦችን፣ ምርቶችንና አገልግሎቶችን መለያ (label) የሚቀርጸውም ሚኒስቴሩ ነው (አንቀጽ 24(1)(l))።
- ቀድሞ ለባህልና ቱሪዝም ሚኒስቴርና ለቱሪዝም ኢትዮጵያ በሌሎች ሕጎች የተሰጡ የቱሪዝም ሥልጣኖች አሁን ወደ ቱሪዝም ሚኒስቴር ተላልፈዋል (አንቀጽ 24(2))።
- ሆቴልዎን ለማስመዘን ወይም ስለ አንድ ሆቴል ደረጃ ለማጣራት የቱሪዝም ሚኒስቴርን ያነጋግሩ፦ ስልክ +251 935 203 138፤ ኢሜይል info@mot.gov.et፤ ቦሌ ጃፓን መብራቱ ጋር ወደ ሰሜን አቅጣጫ 100 ሜትር ገባ ብሎ (በአዲስ አበባ)።
- የምዘና መስፈርቶች፣ የማመልከቻ ሂደት፣ ክፍያ፣ ደረጃው ለስንት ዓመት እንደሚጸና እና በደረጃ የተመዘኑ ሆቴሎች ዝርዝር በይፋ ሊገኙ አልቻሉም።
- በግል ሚዲያ የወጡ የምዘና ውጤቶችና የጊዜ ገደቦች በኦፊሴላዊ ምንጭ ስላልተረጋገጡ እዚህ አልተካተቱም።

## In short (English)

- The Ministry of Tourism awards star ratings to hotels and related tourism facilities and regulates their operation (Ministry website, "Mandates").
- In law, the Ministry of Tourism sets the standards for tourist service providers and supervises how they are applied (Proclamation 1263/2021, Art. 24(1)(m)). It also designs labels for the country's tourism attractions, products and services (Art. 24(1)(l)).
- Tourism powers that other laws gave to the former Ministry of Culture and Tourism and to Tourism Ethiopia now belong to the Ministry of Tourism (Art. 24(2)).
- To have your hotel rated, or to check a hotel's rating, contact the Ministry of Tourism: Tel +251 935 203 138, email info@mot.gov.et. Office: about 100 m north of the Bole Japan traffic light, Addis Ababa.
- We found no official information on the rating criteria, application steps, fees, how long a rating lasts, or a list of rated hotels.
- Rating results and re-rating periods reported by private media could not be confirmed from an official source, so they are not included.

## Official text

**Ministry of Tourism, About us** — https://mot.gov.et/about-us/

- "The Ministry is mandated with the following missions." … "Undertake star ratings of hotels and relevant tourism infrastructures and regulate their operation."
- "የቱሪዝም ሚኒስቴር አድራሻ +251 9 35 20 31 38 info@mot.gov.et የቦሌ ጃፓን መብራቱ ጋር ወደ ሰሜን አቅጣጫ 100 ሜትር ገባ ብሎ"

**Proclamation No. 1263/2021** — Federal Negarit Gazette, 28th Year No. 4, 25 January 2022. PDF: https://justice.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/አዋጅ-ቁጥር-1263-2014.pdf

- Art. 24(1)(l): "design tourism labels for identification of the country’s tourism attraction, production and service and improve them from time to time and cause the implementation of the same;"
- Art. 24(1)(m): "determine the standard of tourist service providers and supervise its implementation;"
- Art. 24(2): "The powers and duties entrusted to Ministry of Culture and Tourism and Tourism Ethiopia under other laws that are currently in force with respect to Tourism are hereby vested in the Ministry of Tourism established by this Proclamation."

## What is not published

- The hotel star-rating directive or standard, including its criteria and scoring. Ask the Ministry of Tourism.
- How to apply, the fee, and how long it takes.
- How long a star rating is valid and when hotels are re-rated.
- An official, current list of star-rated hotels, in Addis Ababa or nationally.
- Whether regional or Addis Ababa tourism bureaus rate smaller hotels and lodges.
