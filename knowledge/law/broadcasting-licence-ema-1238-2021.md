# የብሮድካስት ፈቃድ — Broadcasting Licences and the Ethiopian Media Authority

## በአጭሩ (Amharic summary)

- በኢትዮጵያ የሬዲዮና የቴሌቪዥን ብሮድካስት አገልግሎትን የሚቆጣጠረውና ፈቃድ የሚሰጠው፣ የሚያድሰው፣ የሚያግደውና የሚሰርዘው **የኢትዮጵያ መገናኛ ብዙኃን ባለሥልጣን** ነው (አዋጅ 1238/2021 አንቀጽ 6(1))።
- ማንም ሰው ከባለሥልጣኑ ፈቃድ ሳያገኝ ወይም በታገደ/በተሰረዘ ፈቃድ የብሮድካስት አገልግሎት መስጠት አይችልም (አንቀጽ 30)።
- የፈቃድ ዓይነቶች አራት ናቸው፦ የሕዝብ አገልግሎት፣ ልዩ የሕዝብ አገልግሎት፣ የንግድ እና የማኅበረሰብ ብሮድካስት ፈቃድ (አንቀጽ 31)።
- ፈቃዱ በማሰራጫ ዘዴ ይከፈላል፦ በምድር (አናሎግ/ዲጂታል)፣ በሳተላይት፣ በኬብል እና በኢንተርኔት፣ በነጻ፣ በደንበኝነት ወይም በፍላጎት (on demand) (አንቀጽ 29)።
- የንግድ የሬዲዮ ሞገድ ፈቃድ የሚሰጠው አመልካቾችን በውድድር በመጋበዝ ነው (አንቀጽ 33)።
- የፈቃድ ዘመን፦ በሬዲዮ ሞገድ የሚሠራ ቴሌቪዥን ለመጀመሪያ ጊዜ 10 ዓመት፤ ሬዲዮ፦ ብሔራዊ 10 ዓመት፣ በአንድ ክልል 8 ዓመት፣ በአካባቢ ደረጃ 6 ዓመት፣ ማኅበረሰብ 10 ዓመት፣ የአጭር ጊዜ ማኅበረሰብ 1 ዓመት (አንቀጽ 42)።
- በ2025 ማሻሻያ (አዋጅ 1374/2025) መሠረት ባለፈቃዱ የፈቃድ ክፍያና ዓመታዊ የማደሻ ክፍያ ለመገናኛ ብዙኃን ባለሥልጣን፣ ዓመታዊ የሞገድ (spectrum) ክፍያ ደግሞ ለኢትዮጵያ ኮሙኒኬሽን ባለሥልጣን ይከፍላል።
- የክፍያ መጠኖቹ በመንግሥት ይወሰናሉ፤ መጠኑ በይፋ አልተገኘም።
- ማሻሻያው ቀጥታ ሥርጭትን ጨምሮ ማንኛውም ፕሮግራም ከሕግ ጋር የሚጣጣም መሆኑን የማረጋገጥ ኃላፊነት በብሮድካስተሩ ላይ ጥሏል።
- ከውጭ ፈቃድ አግኝተው ወደ ኢትዮጵያ የሚያሰራጩ ጣቢያዎችም፣ ከይዘታቸው ከ50% በላይ ኢትዮጵያን የሚመለከት ከሆነ፣ በአገር ውስጥ ቋንቋ አገልግሎት ከሰጡና ከአገር ውስጥ ማስታወቂያ ገቢ ካገኙ በአዋጁ ይሸፈናሉ።

## In short (English)

- The **Ethiopian Media Authority (EMA)** sets licensing conditions and issues, renews, suspends and revokes broadcasting licences (Proc. 1238/2021, Art. 6(1)).
- Broadcasting without an EMA licence, or with a suspended or revoked one, is prohibited (Art. 30).
- Four licence types: public service, special public service, commercial, and community broadcasting (Art. 31).
- Licences are also classed by means of transmission: terrestrial analogue or digital, satellite, cable and internet — free, subscription or on-demand (Art. 29).
- Commercial radio-wave licences are awarded through a competitive invitation to applicants (Art. 33).
- Validity: TV using radio waves, first licence 10 years; radio — national 10 years, regional 8, local 6, community 10, short-term community 1 year (Art. 42).
- Since the 2025 amendment (Proc. 1374/2025), licensees pay the licence fee and annual renewal fee to the EMA and an annual spectrum fee to the Ethiopian Communications Authority.
- Fee amounts are set by the government and were not found.
- The broadcaster is responsible for ensuring every programme, including live transmissions, complies with the law.
- Foreign-licensed broadcasters targeting Ethiopia are covered if more than 50% of content focuses on Ethiopia, they serve audiences in local languages, and they earn advertising or sponsorship revenue in Ethiopia (Art. 3(3)).
- EMA directives also exist for religious (No. 899/2014), community (No. 924/2014) and public (No. 926/2014) radio and TV.

## Official text

**Media Proclamation No. 1238/2021 (English)** — https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/legislation/details/21659

Art. 6(1): the Authority shall "determine the licensing condition of broadcasting services; and issue, renew, suspend and revoke broadcasting license;"

Art. 30: "No person may undertake broadcasting service by using means of broadcasting service transmission without obtaining a broadcasting license from the Authority or by using a suspended or revoked license."

Art. 31(1): "a) public service broadcasting license, b) special public service broadcasting license, c) commercial broadcasting service license, and d) community broadcasting service license."

Art. 29 (licence classes): terrestrial analogue radio; terrestrial analogue television; terrestrial analogue free; terrestrial analogue subscription; terrestrial digital radio; terrestrial digital television; terrestrial digital free; terrestrial digital subscription; satellite free; satellite subscription; satellite on demand; cable subscription; cable on demand; internet free; internet subscription; internet on demand; "any other class of license prescribed by the Authority."

Art. 42(1): "A television broadcasting service license that uses radio wave granted for the first time shall be valid for 10 years."
Art. 42(2), radio: "a) where the transmission is at the national level, for 10 years; b) where the transmission is limited to a regional state, for 8 years; c) where the transmission is limited to local level, for 6 years; d) where the transmission is community broadcasting service, for 10 years; and e) for short-term community broadcasting service 1 year."

Art. 3(3): covers foreign-licensed broadcasters where "a) more than 50 percent of its content focuses on Ethiopia; b) providing services to Ethiopian audiences in local languages; and, c) whose revenue derived from advertising and sponsorship within the country."

**Media (Amendment) Proclamation No. 1374/2025, Art. 2(7) — new Art. 46(1)** — https://justice.gov.et/en/law/media-amendment-proclamation-no-1374-2025/
"Any broadcasting Service licensee shall pay license fee and annual license renewal fee to the Ethiopian Media Authority; for his allocated spectrum, he shall pay annual spectrum fee to the Ethiopian Communications Authority."

Art. 2(8) (new Art. 55(1)(f)): the broadcaster ensures any programme, "including live transmissions, complies with the law before or during its transmission".

(Original Art. 46 set late payment at "5 percent ... for the delay of each month", capped at 50%, with annual and renewal fees due "within 60 days after the end of the fiscal year"; check with the EMA whether this still applies after the amendment.)
