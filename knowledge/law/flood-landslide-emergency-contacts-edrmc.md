# ከጎርፍ ወይም ከመሬት መንሸራተት አደጋ በኋላ ማንን ማግኘት ይቻላል — Who to Contact After a Flood or Landslide

## በአጭሩ (Amharic summary)

- **በአዲስ አበባ** ለእሳትና ለሌሎች አደጋዎች (ጎርፍን ጨምሮ) የአዲስ አበባ ከተማ አስተዳደር የእሳትና አደጋ ሥጋት ሥራ አመራር ኮሚሽንን በ**939** ይደውሉ። ኮሚሽኑ በድረ-ገጹ በሚያሰራጨው የጎርፍ ማስጠንቀቂያ "በአፋጣኝ ወደ ከፍታ ቦታዎች ይውጡ … ለአደጋ ጊዜ በ939 ይደውሉ" ይላል።
- **በአዲስ አበባ** የፖሊስ የአደጋ ጊዜ ስልክ 991 ነው (የአዲስ አበባ ፖሊስ ኮሚሽን ሰንጠረዥ)።
- የኢትዮጵያ አደጋ ሥጋት ሥራ አመራር ኮሚሽን ዋና መሥሪያ ቤት፦ አዲስ አበባ፣ ንፋስ ስልክ ላፍቶ ክፍለ ከተማ፣ ከንፋስ ስልክ የሙያ ትምህርት ቤት (Nifas Silk Vocational School) አጠገብ፤ ስልክ +251-115516407፤ ፋክስ +251-115514788፤ ፖ.ሳ.ቁ. 5686። በድረ-ገጹ (edrmc.gov.et/contact) "Early Warning and Response" የሚለውን ርዕስ መርጠው መልእክት መላክ ይቻላል።
- በአዋጁ መሠረት አደጋው ከክልሉ የአደጋ ወሰን በታች ከሆነ ክልሉ በራሱ አቅም ምላሽ ይሰጣል። ከአቅሙ በላይ ከሆነ በቀጣዩ ከፍተኛ የክልል አካል፣ ከዚያም በፌዴራል መንግሥት ምላሽ ይሰጣል (አዋጅ 1386/2017 አንቀጽ 22)። ስለዚህ መጀመሪያ የቀበሌ/ወረዳ አስተዳደርዎን እና የክልልዎን አደጋ ሥጋት ሥራ አመራር ቢሮ ያነጋግሩ።
- ለጎርፍና ለግድብ ሙላት ምላሽ ዋናው ተቋም የውሃና ኢነርጂ ሚኒስቴር ነው። እንደ መሬት መንሸራተትና የመሬት መንቀጥቀጥ ያሉ የጂኦሎጂ አደጋዎችን የሚለየው የማዕድን ሚኒስቴር ነው። የጠፉ ሰዎችን መፈለግና ሕይወት ማዳን የመከላከያ ሚኒስቴር፣ የማዳን ሥራና የፀጥታ ቁጥጥር ደግሞ የፌዴራል ፖሊስ ኃላፊነት ነው (አንቀጽ 20)።
- ለተጎጂዎች ጊዜያዊ መጠለያ ማስገንባትን የከተማና መሠረተ ልማት ሚኒስቴር ያስተባብራል። በመጠለያ ላሉት የጤና አገልግሎትና የሥነ-ልቦና ድጋፍ የጤና ሚኒስቴር፣ የውሃ አቅርቦትን ደግሞ የውሃና ኢነርጂ ሚኒስቴር ይሰጣሉ (አንቀጽ 20)።
- ተጎጂዎች በአገሪቱ አቅም መጠን ሰብዓዊ ድጋፍ የማግኘት መብት አላቸው (አንቀጽ 25(4))።
- ተጎጂዎች ከአደገኛ ቦታ እንዲለቁ የሚሰጠውን ትእዛዝ የማክበርና ስለአደጋው የሚያውቁትን መረጃ ወዲያውኑ ለሚመለከተው የመንግሥት አካል የመስጠት ግዴታ አለባቸው (አንቀጽ 26)።
- ኮሚሽኑ የቅድመ ማስጠንቀቂያ መረጃዎችን (ሳምንታዊ የአማርኛ ሁኔታ ሪፖርት፣ የጎርፍና የድርቅ ማንቂያዎች) በ edrmc.gov.et ላይ ያወጣል።
- ብሔራዊ ነጻ የአደጋ ጥሪ መስመር እና የክልል ቢሮዎች ስልክ በይፋ አልተገኙም።

## In short (English)

- **In Addis Ababa**, call **939**, the Addis Ababa City Fire and Disaster Risk Management Commission, for fire and other emergencies, including floods. The commission's own flood alert says: "Evacuate to higher ground immediately. Stay safe and contact 939 for emergencies."
- **In Addis Ababa**, the police emergency number is 991 (Addis Ababa Police Commission table).
- Ethiopian Disaster Risk Management Commission (EDRMC) head office: Nifas Silk Lafto Sub City, Addis Ababa, beside Nifas Silk Vocational School; phone +251-115516407; fax +251-115514788; P.O. Box 5686. The online form at edrmc.gov.et/contact has an "Early Warning and Response" category.
- Under the law, a region handles a disaster below the threshold with its own capacity. Anything beyond that goes to the next higher regional authority and then to the federal government (Proclamation 1386/2025 Art. 22). So contact your kebele or woreda administration and your regional disaster risk management office first.
- Lead bodies (Art. 20): Ministry of Water and Energy for floods and dam overflows; Ministry of Mines for geological hazards such as landslides and earthquakes; Ministry of Defense for searching for missing people and saving lives; Federal Police for rescue and security.
- The Ministry of Urban and Infrastructure coordinates temporary shelters. In shelters, the Ministry of Health provides health services and psychological support, and the Ministry of Water and Energy provides water (Art. 20).
- Victims have the right to humanitarian assistance to the extent of the country's capacity (Art. 25(4)).
- Victims must obey evacuation orders and must report what they know about a disaster to the relevant government body right away (Art. 26).
- EDRMC publishes early-warning material (a weekly Amharic situation update, flood and drought alerts) on edrmc.gov.et.
- No national toll-free disaster hotline and no regional office numbers were found.

Amharic version on the same site: "ለአደጋ ጊዜ በ939 ይደውሉ። - FDRMC"

**Ethiopian Disaster Risk Management Proclamation No. 1386/2025** (Federal Negarit Gazette No. 57, 28 July 2025) — https://justice.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/አዋጅ-ቁጥር-1386-2017-1.pdf
> Art. 20(3) Ministry of Water and Energy: shall act as a lead institution in responding to floods and other water supply dangers, as well as dam overflows and associated disasters; ...
> Art. 20(6) Ministry of Mines shall act as a lead sector institution in identifying geological disasters such as earthquakes, volcanoes, landslides, and related disasters ...
> Art. 20(7) Ministry of Defense shall act lead sector institution in searching for disappeared people, saving their lives and securing orders in the event of a severe manmade and natural disaster.
> Art. 20(21) Federal Police: - In times of emergency, it carries out various forensic investigations, rescue operations, and early arrival and control in disaster areas, ensuring security operations, preventing further damage, and ensuring that humanitarian aid reaches the beneficiaries properly.
> Art. 22(3) In situations where the disaster is below the threshold ..., Regions shall have the responsibility to use their own capacity to prevent, prepare, respond and rehabilitate the disaster.
> Art. 22(4) The next Higher Regional Authority and the Federal Government, respectively, shall respond to a disaster which is beyond the threshold ...
> Art. 25(4) When affected by a disaster, receive humanitarian assistance to the extent of the capacity of the country for specific period in accordance with the law and until rehabilitated;
> Art. 26(1) Comply with instructions and commands of competent authority to evacuate from hazardous area or not to enter a certain area;
> Art. 26(5) Immediately provide information within their knowledge to concerned government body regarding a disaster;

## What is not published

- A national toll-free disaster or early-warning hotline: not found.
- Phone numbers for regional disaster risk management offices or for the Federal Early Warning and Emergency Coordination Centre: not found. Ask your woreda administration.
- Emergency numbers outside Addis Ababa: the 939 and 991 lines above were confirmed only for Addis Ababa. An older Addis police table lists 912 for fire, which differs from 939.
- The disaster "threshold" regulation (Art. 22(1)) was not found, so it is unclear when the federal government steps in.
