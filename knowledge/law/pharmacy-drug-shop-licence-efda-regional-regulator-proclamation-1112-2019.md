# ፋርማሲ ወይም መድኃኒት መደብር ለመክፈት ፈቃድ — Licence to Open a Pharmacy or Drug Shop

## በአጭሩ (Amharic summary)

- ማንኛውም የመድኃኒት ተቋም ሥራ ከመጀመሩ በፊት መመዝገብና የብቃት ማረጋገጫ ምስክር ወረቀት ማግኘት አለበት። ፈቃዱን የሚሰጠው እንደአግባቡ ኢፌዲኤ (EFDA) ወይም የክልል ጤና ተቆጣጣሪ ነው (አዋጅ 1112/2011 አንቀጽ 23(1))።
- ማን ይሰጣል? ኢፌዲኤ የብቃት ማረጋገጫ የሚሰጠው ለአስመጪዎች፣ ላኪዎች፣ ከአንድ ክልል በላይ ለሚሠሩ አምራቾችና ለጅምላ አከፋፋዮች ነው (የኢፌዲኤ ሥልጣንና ተግባር ገጽ፣ ደንብ ቁጥር 531/2023)። በዚህ ዝርዝር ውስጥ ችርቻሮ ፋርማሲና መድኃኒት መደብር አልተጠቀሱም፤ በአዲስ አበባ ደግሞ የከተማው ባለስልጣን ለፋርማሲዩቲካል ተቋማት የብቃት ማረጋገጫ ይሰጣል። ስለዚህ ለችርቻሮ ፋርማሲ በመጀመሪያ የክልልዎን ጤና ተቆጣጣሪ ይጠይቁ። (ለችርቻሮ ማን እንደሚሰጥ በግልጽ የሚናገር ሕግ አላገኘንም።)
- "ክልል" የሚለው በዚህ አዋጅ አዲስ አበባንና ድሬዳዋን ይጨምራል (አንቀጽ 2(58))።
- በአዲስ አበባ፦ የአዲስ አበባ ምግብና መድኃኒት ባለስልጣን "ለፋርማሲዩቲካል ተቋማት የብቃት ማረጋገጫ መስጠት" የሚል አገልግሎት አለው (aafda.gov.et)። ስልክ፦ 0118284038 / 8864፤ ኢሜይል፦ Proflisence@gmail.com።
- የብቃት ማረጋገጫ ምስክር ወረቀቱን በምንም መንገድ ለሌላ ሰው ማስተላለፍ የተከለከለ ነው (አንቀጽ 23(2))።
- ተቋሙ የሚቀጥረው ባለሙያ በሙያው የተመዘገበና ብቁ መሆኑን ማረጋገጥ አለበት (አንቀጽ 24(1))።
- በችርቻሮ መድኃኒት ተቋማት በየደረጃቸው ሊያዙ የሚችሉ መድኃኒቶችን ዝርዝር የሚያወጣው ኢፌዲኤ ነው።
- ኢፌዲኤ ግንቦት 14 ቀን 2017 (22 May 2025) የብሔራዊ ፋርማሲ ደረጃዎችን ለማስፈጸም ለክልል ተቆጣጣሪዎች ስልጠና መጀመሩን አስታውቋል። የ12 ክልል ተቆጣጣሪ አካላት ኢንስፔክተሮች ተመሳሳይ ስልጠና ወስደዋል።
- ኢፌዲኤ ለአስመጪ፣ ላኪ፣ ጅምላ አከፋፋይና አምራች የብቃት ማረጋገጫ ማመልከቻ በመስመር ላይ የሚቀበለው "i-License" በሚባል ሥርዓት ነው።
- የቦታ ስፋት፣ የባለሙያ ብዛትና የሥራ ልምድ፣ ክፍያና የሚፈጀው ጊዜ በይፋ ሊገኙ አልቻሉም። የክልልዎን ጤና ተቆጣጣሪ ወይም በአዲስ አበባ የአዲስ አበባ ምግብና መድኃኒት ባለስልጣንን ይጠይቁ።

## In short (English)

- Every medicine establishment must be registered and hold a certificate of competence before it starts trading. Depending on the case, the certificate comes from EFDA or from the regional health regulator (Proclamation 1112/2019, Art. 23(1)).
- Who issues what: EFDA issues certificates of competency to importers, exporters, manufacturers working across regions and wholesalers (EFDA powers page, Regulation No. 531/2023). Retail pharmacies and drug shops are not on that list, and in Addis Ababa the city authority certifies pharmaceutical institutions. So for a retail pharmacy, ask your regional health regulator first. (We found no text that explicitly names the licensing body for retail outlets.)
- In this law, "region" also covers the Addis Ababa and Dire Dawa city administrations (Art. 2(58)).
- In Addis Ababa, the Addis Ababa Food and Drug Authority (aafda.gov.et) offers a service called "Providing certification to pharmaceutical institutions". Phone: 0118284038 / 8864. Email: Proflisence@gmail.com.
- A certificate of competence may not be transferred to anyone else in any way (Art. 23(2)).
- The establishment must check that any professional it hires is registered and competent (Art. 24(1)).
- EFDA decides which medicines each level of retail medicine outlet may stock.
- On 22 May 2025, EFDA started training regional regulators to apply the national pharmacy standards. Inspectors from 12 regional regulatory bodies had already done similar training.
- EFDA's online system "i-License" takes certificate-of-competency applications from importers, exporters, wholesalers and manufacturers.
- We found no official figures for room size, number of staff, required experience, fees or processing time. Ask your regional health regulator, or in Addis Ababa, the Addis Ababa Food and Drug Authority.

## Official text

**Proclamation No. 1112/2019** — https://www.efda.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/Food-and-Medicine-Administration-Proclamation-1112.pdf

- Art. 2(59): "“regional health regulator” means a regional government body which is empowered to administer this proclamation and other laws issued to implement this proclamation at regional government level;"
- Art. 23(1): "No medicine or medical device institution may engage in medicine or medical device trade unless it is registered and licensed, as appropriate, by the executive organ or regional health regulator."
  - ፳፫(፩)፦ "ማንኛውም የመድኃኒት ወይም የህክምና መሣሪያ ተቋም በመድኃኒት ወይም የህክምና መሣሪያ ንግድ ሥራ ላይ ከመሰማራቱ በፊት እንደአግባቡ በአስፈጻሚ አካሉ ወይም በክልል ጤና ተቆጣጣሪው መመዝገብ እና የብቃት ማረጋገጫ ምስክር ወረቀት ማግኘት አለበት፡፡"
- Art. 23(2): "It shall be prohibited, in any manner, to transfer a medicine and medical device institution certificate of competence to a third party."
- Art. 24(1): "Every medicine and medical device institution shall hire a health professional upon ensuring that the professional is duly registered and competent to perform the task."

**EFDA Powers and Duties** — https://www.efda.gov.et/about-efda/power-and-duties/

- "Pursuant to the Ethiopian Food and Medicines Authority Regulation No. 531/2023, EFDA discharges the following functions:-"
- "Issue certificate of competency or special regulatory license to importer, exporter, for cross regional purpose manufacturer or wholesaler of regulated products, quality control service provider and bioequivalence centers;"
- "Issue list or categorize medicines to be possessed by retail medicine establishment in their respective level;"
- "Provide, to the possible extent, support to Regional health regulatory and work in collaboration;"

**EFDA e-service** — https://www.efda.gov.et/e-service/

- "i-License is application that allows importers, exporters, wholesalers and Manufacturers to apply for certificate of competency."

- Whether pharmacy applications in Addis Ababa can be made online.
