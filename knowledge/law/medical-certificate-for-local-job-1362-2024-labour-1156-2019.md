# ለሀገር ውስጥ ሥራ የሕክምና ማስረጃ — Medical certificate for a local job

For a medical check-up before working **abroad**, see the separate page on Proclamation 1389/2025 (overseas employment medical examination). This page is about jobs inside Ethiopia.

## በአጭሩ

- **የት ይገኛል:** ማንኛውም የጤና ተቋም ለታካሚ **የሕክምና ማስረጃ የመስጠት ግዴታ** አለበት (አዋጅ 1362/2017 አንቀጽ 5(5))። ስለዚህ ፈቃድ ካለው የመንግሥት ወይም የግል ጤና ጣቢያ ወይም ሆስፒታል ማግኘት ይቻላል። በሕግ የተመዘገበና የብቃት ማረጋገጫ ያለው ተቋም መሆኑን ያረጋግጡ (አንቀጽ 38)።
- **ማን ይከፍላል:** የጤና ምርመራው **በሕግ ወይም አግባብ ባለው ባለሥልጣን** ሲጠየቅ ወጪውን **አሠሪው** ይሸፍናል (አዋጅ 1156/2011 አንቀጽ 12(6))።
- ሠራተኛው በሕግ ወይም አሠሪው በበቂ ምክንያት ሲጠይቀው የጤና ምርመራን መቃወም አይችልም። **የኤች.አይ.ቪ/ኤድስ ምርመራ ግን አይገደድም** (አንቀጽ 14(2)(ሠ))።
- አሠሪ ሠራተኞችን በኤች.አይ.ቪ/ኤድስ ወይም በአካል ጉዳት ምክንያት ማግለል የተከለከለ ነው (አንቀጽ 14(1)(ረ))።
- **አሠራር (ተለምዶ):** መታወቂያዎን እና ከአሠሪው ምን ዓይነት ምርመራ እንደሚፈለግ የሚገልጽ ደብዳቤ ካለ ይዘው ይሂዱ። ካርድ ያውጡ፣ ሐኪም ይመርምርዎ፣ የሚታዘዙትን ምርመራዎች ያድርጉ፣ ከዚያም ማስረጃው በተቋሙ ማኅተም ይሰጥዎታል። (ትክክለኛውን የሰነድ ዝርዝር ተቋሙን ይጠይቁ።)
- **ክፍያ:** በፌዴራልና በዩኒቨርሲቲ ሆስፒታሎች የካርድና የምርመራ ክፍያ በሐኪሙ ደረጃ ይለያያል። በአንድ ወር ውስጥ ለክትትል ሲመለሱ እንደገና አይከፍሉም (መመሪያ 920/2014 አንቀጽ 7(2)–(3))። የላቦራቶሪና የራጅ ምርመራዎች በየአገልግሎቱ ተመን ይከፈላሉ (አንቀጽ 10)።
- **በአዲስ አበባ የመንግሥት ሆስፒታሎች የሕክምና ማስረጃ ክፍያ በይፋ የታተመ አልተገኘም።** ከመሄድዎ በፊት ሆስፒታሉን ወይም ጤና ጣቢያውን ይጠይቁ።
- የሲቪል ሰርቪስ ወይም የአሠሪው ደንብ የተወሰነ ሆስፒታል (ለምሳሌ የሐኪሞች ቦርድ) ሊጠይቅ ይችላል። አሠሪዎን ይጠይቁ።

## In short (English)

- **Where:** every health institution must **give a medical certificate** to a patient (Proclamation 1362/2024, Art. 5(5)). Any licensed public or private health centre or hospital can issue one. Make sure the facility is registered and holds a certificate of competency (Art. 38).
- **Who pays:** when a medical examination is **required by law or by the appropriate authority**, the **employer** covers the cost (Labour Proclamation 1156/2019, Art. 12(6)).
- A worker may not refuse a medical examination required by law or by the employer for good cause, **except an HIV/AIDS test** (Art. 14(2)(e)).
- Employers may not discriminate against workers on grounds including HIV/AIDS status or disability (Art. 14(1)(f)).
- **Typical steps:** bring your ID and, if you have one, the employer's letter saying what check-up is needed. Get a card, see the doctor, do the ordered tests, and collect the stamped certificate. Ask the facility for its exact document list.
- **Fees:** at federal and university hospitals, the consultation fee depends on the level of the professional, and a revisit within one month is free of a new examination fee (Directive 920/2022, Art. 7(2)–(3)). Laboratory and radiology tests are charged per service (Art. 10).
- **No official published fee for a medical certificate at Addis Ababa public hospitals was found.** Ask the hospital or health centre before you go.
- Your employer's rules may require a certificate from a particular hospital or a medical board. Ask your employer.

## Official text

**Proclamation No. 1362/2024 — Art. 5 (duties of a health institution)** — https://justice.gov.et/en/law/health-service-administration-and-regulation-proclamation-no-1362-2024/
> Any health institution shall have the following duties: ... 5/ To give medical certificate for a patient; 6/ To establish a grievance handling system; 7/ To provide information about the service provided in the health institution and other relevant information upon request by the client or an authorized body;

**Labour Proclamation No. 1156/2019** (Federal Negarit Gazette No. 89, 5 September 2019; PDF https://justice.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/አዋጅ-ቁጥር-1156-2011.pdf)
> Art. 12 (Obligations of an Employer) 6/ To cover the cost of medical examination of the worker whenever such medical examination is required by law or the appropriate authority;
> Art. 14 (Prohibited Acts) 2/ It shall be unlawful for a worker to: ... e) Except for HIV/AIDS test, refuse to submit himself for medical examination when required by law or by the employer for good cause;
> Art. 14(1) [It shall be unlawful for an employer to:] ... f) Discriminate between workers on the basis of Nation, sex, religion, political outlook, HIV/AIDS disablement or disablement or any other grounds;

**Directive No. 920/2022 — Federal and University Hospitals User Fee Implementation** (Ministry of Finance; PDF https://justice.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/Federal-and-University-Hospitals-User-Fee-Implementation-Directive-No.-920-2022.pdf)
> Art. 7(2): ... consultation fee shall vary according to the level of the health professional providing the service and shall be made in accordance with the fee set out in the regulation ...
> Art. 7(3): If the service user comes to the hospital for a revisit consultation within one month, he or she can receive the service without having to pay another examination fee.
> Art. 10: For each of the services listed in the radiology and laboratory department, each patient will be required to pay in accordance with the regulation of each service fee

## What is not published

- **Fee for a medical certificate or pre-employment check-up** at Addis Ababa city hospitals, health centres or federal hospitals: not found. Ask the facility's card room or finance office.
- **Which tests an employer may require** for ordinary local jobs: no general official list. Ask your employer (for food handlers and similar jobs, ask the health regulatory office).
- **Addis Ababa Health Bureau** procedures: its website could not be reached. Ask the bureau or your sub-city health office.
