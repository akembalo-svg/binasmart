## In short (English)
- Driving without a fastened seat belt, or without making sure passengers have fastened theirs, is a **Third Category Offence** under Schedule "B" of Regulation 557/2024 (item 10).
- A first offence carries a fine of **Birr 1,500** and **3 demerit points** (Schedule B, Part 2(A)). For comparison, a First Category offence is Birr 500 and 1 point, and a Second Category offence is Birr 1,000 and 2 points.
- The seat-belt duty covers the driver and every passenger in the front and back seats. It does not apply on motorcycles. A passenger who cannot wear a belt for medical reasons must show a medical certificate. A child under seven must sit in a child safety seat fixed to the back seat (Art. 7).
- For repeat offences, the penalty depends on the total points recorded: 2–4 points Birr 600; 5–7 Birr 850; 8–10 Birr 1,600; 11–13 Birr 2,000.
- **Suspension starts at 14 points.** At 14–16 points the licence is suspended for six months, at 17–20 points for one year, and at 21 points or more for one year and six months. The suspension is lifted only after rehabilitation training.
- Recorded points are kept for **one year only** (Art. 95(9)).
- Before reaching the suspension threshold, a driver can take remedial training to have **4 points** reduced or cancelled (Art. 96(2)).
- The fine must be paid within ten working days. After the tenth day, 5% interest is added for each day, and the fine can still be paid for up to six months. If it is not paid in that time, the licence is suspended for six months (Art. 95(6), (10), (11)).
- To complain about a fine, go first to the traffic-control office that issued the charge letter, within two working days. If you are not satisfied with its decision, complain to the appropriate body within five working days (Art. 95(5)).
- In Addis Ababa, the regulation is enforced by the Addis Ababa Traffic Management Authority. Its website gives 696 (free line) and 0116672386 (checked 2026-09-17).

## Official text
Road Transport Traffic Control Council of Ministers Regulation No. 557/2024, Federal Negarit Gazette 30th Year No. 57, 19 August 2024. It repeals Regulation No. 208/2011 as amended by No. 395/2017 (Art. 98).

**Schedule "B", 1. C. Third Category Offences** (gazette page 15904; OCR of the scanned copy at https://aatma.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Regulation-No.-557-2016.pdf):
> 10 | Drive a vehicle without buckling up /fastening seatbelt/ or without ensuring that the passenger in the vehicle bucked up /fastened seatbelt/

Amharic column: "የደህንነት ቀበቶ ሳያስር ወይም ተሳፋሪዎች የደህንነት ቀበቶ ማስራቸውን ሳያረጋግጥ ያሽከረከረ".

**Schedule "B", 2. A. Offence Committed for the First Time** (page 15906):

| Category of the offence | Demerit points | Fine |
|---|---|---|
| First Category Offences | 1 | 500.00 Birr |
| Second Category Offences | 2 | 1000.00 Birr |
| Third Category Offences | 3 | 1500.00 Birr |
| Special Category | 8–21 | a range of monetary fines |
| Other violations | 0 | 100–20000 Birr |
| Offences by Pedestrians | 0 | 100–150 Birr |

**Schedule "B", 2. B. For an Offence Committed for the Second Time and above** (page 15907):

| Demerit points registered | Measures and fines |
|---|---|
| 2–4 | 600 Birr |
| 5–7 | 850 Birr |
| 8–10 | 1600 Birr |
| 11–13 | 2000 Birr |
| 14–16 | "Suspended for six months and the suspension could be raised only after taking the rehabilitation training." |
| 17–20 | "Suspended for one year and the suspension could be raised only after taking the rehabilitation training." |
| 21 and more | "Suspended for one year and six months and the suspension could be raised only after taking the rehabilitation training." |

**Schedule "B", 2. C. Late payment** (page 15908): paying before the eleventh day costs only the fine. A person who pays after 11 days "will be fined with 5% interest added for each day from the eleventh day". The Amharic column also records 1 extra point for late payment.

**Articles** (text layer: https://justice.gov.et/en/law/road-transport-traffic-control-council-of-ministers-regulation/):
- Art. 7(1): "Any driver may not drive any vehicle other than motorcycle on road without buckling up safety belt." 7(2): the driver "shall ascertain that the said passenger has buckled-up safety belt whether seated in the front or back seat." 7(3): a medical certificate is required for a passenger who cannot wear a belt. 7(4): a child under seven years must be in a child safety seat fixed to the back seat.
- Art. 95(2): "A traffic controller shall request and take the driving [licence] from a vehicle driver who violates the provision of this regulation and give appropriate charge letter which commands to pay fines."
- Art. 95(5)(a): a complaint "within two working days to the office of the traffic controller who provided him with a letter of charge"; 95(5)(b): if dissatisfied, a complaint "to the appropriate body within five working days".
- Art. 95(9): "the point of offences recorded as a penalty shall be kept for one year only."
- Art. 95(10): "pay only the fined amount up to the tenth (10th) day, and after the tenth day, 5 % interest will be calculated daily and can pay the fine for six months and take the driving licence." 95(11): "The driving licence of the driver shall be suspended for six months if he fails to pay the fine in accordance to sub article 10".
- Art. 96(1): a driver suspended under the point system "could get the lifting of the suspension by taking rehabilitation training." 96(2): "the driver can get reduce or cancel 4 points from the amount of penalty by taking remedial training to reduce the penalty point before reaching the suspension threshold."

## What is not published
- No official date was found for when Addis Ababa began enforcing these fines. Ask the Addis Ababa Traffic Management Authority.
- Where rehabilitation or remedial training is given, and what it costs, is not stated in the regulation. Ask the Road Safety and Insurance Fund Service or the licensing office.
- The schedule was read by OCR from a scan. If a figure matters for a dispute, check it against the printed gazette at the traffic office.
