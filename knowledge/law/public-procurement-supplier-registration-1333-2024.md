# ለፌደራል የመንግሥት ግዥ አቅራቢ ሆኖ መመዝገብ (e-GP) — Registering as a Supplier for Federal Public Procurement (e-GP)

**ማሳሰቢያ፦** የቀድሞው የፌደራል መንግሥት ግዥና ንብረት አስተዳደር አዋጅ ቁጥር 649/2009 ተሽሯል፤ የተካው አዋጅ ቁጥር 1333/2024 ነው (አንቀጽ 88(1)፣ ከመስከረም 9 ቀን 2024 ጀምሮ)። ተቆጣጣሪው ተቋም አሁን "የመንግሥት ግዥና ንብረት ባለሥልጣን" (ppa.gov.et) ይባላል።

**Note:** Proclamation 649/2009 is repealed and replaced by Proclamation No. 1333/2024 (Art. 88(1); gazette of 9 September 2024). The regulator is now the Federal Public Procurement and Property Authority (ppa.gov.et); older texts say "Agency".

## በአጭሩ (Amharic summary)

- በፌደራል መንግሥት የኤሌክትሮኒክ ግዥ ለመሳተፍ የሚፈልግ ነጋዴ በኤሌክትሮኒክ ሥርዓቱ (e-GP) መመዝገብ፣ መረጃውን ወቅታዊ ማድረግ እና እንደአስፈላጊነቱ ምዝገባውን ማደስ አለበት (መመሪያ 1073/2017 አንቀጽ 24(3))።
- የe-GP ፖርታል፦ https://egp.gov.et/ (የሥራ ገጽ https://production.egp.gov.et/egp/)።
- ጨረታዎች የሚቀርቡት በኤሌክትሮኒክ ሥርዓቱ በኩል ነው፤ በሥርዓቱ የቀረቡ ሰነዶች ሕጋዊ አስገዳጅነት አላቸው (አዋጅ 1333 አንቀጽ 53(1) እና 27(5))።
- በጨረታ ለመሳተፍ የሚያስፈልጉ ሰነዶች (መመሪያ አንቀጽ 39(1))፦ የሚሠራ የተጨማሪ እሴት ታክስ (VAT) ምዝገባ ምስክር ወረቀት (ግዥው ከVAT ነፃ ካልሆነ)፤ የንግድ ምዝገባ ምስክር ወረቀትና የሚሠራ የንግድ ፈቃድ፤ ለኤክሳይዝ ታክስ ለሚገዙ ዕቃዎች የኤክሳይዝ ምዝገባ፤ የጨረታ ማስከበሪያ፤ የተሞላ የጨረታ ማቅረቢያ ቅጽ፤ የፀረ-ሽርክና (anti-collusion) ማረጋገጫ፤ የሚሠራ የግብር ክሊራንስ፤ የብቃት ማረጋገጫ ሰነዶች (ለምሳሌ የአምራች/አከፋፋይ ፍቃድ ደብዳቤ)።
- የVAT ምዝገባ የሌለው አቅራቢ እስከ 500,000 ብር ለሚደርስ የአንድ ጊዜ ግዥ እንዲሳተፍ መሥሪያ ቤቱ ሊፈቅድ ይችላል (አንቀጽ 39(2))።
- ሕጋዊ ሰውነት ያላቸው ድርጅቶች ትክክለኛ ባለቤቶቻቸውን (beneficial owners) ማሳወቅ አለባቸው፤ ይህም ቢያንስ 10% ድርሻ ያላቸውን ይጨምራል (አንቀጽ 39(3)–(4))።
- "የአገር ውስጥ አቅራቢ" ማለት በኢትዮጵያ ሕግ ተመዝግቦ የሚሠራ፣ የሚሠራ የንግድ ፈቃድ፣ የግብር ከፋይ መለያ ቁጥር (TIN) እና የንግድ ምዝገባ ምስክር ወረቀት ያለው ተጫራች ነው፤ የባለአክሲዮኖቹ ዜግነት አይመለከትም (አንቀጽ 54(6))። በሀገር አቀፍ ግልጽ ጨረታ የሚሳተፉት የአገር ውስጥ አቅራቢዎች ብቻ ናቸው (አንቀጽ 54(5))።
- ባለሥልጣኑ የተመዘገቡ አቅራቢዎችንና ከግዥ የታገዱ አቅራቢዎችን ዝርዝር በe-GP ላይ ያወጣል (አንቀጽ 37(2))።
- የምዝገባ ክፍያ ካለ በይፋ አልተገለጸም።

## In short (English)

- Businesses wanting to take part in federal electronic procurement must register on the e-GP system, keep their information current and renew registration as needed (Directive 1073/2025, Art. 24(3)).
- e-GP portal: https://egp.gov.et/ (application at https://production.egp.gov.et/egp/).
- Bids are submitted through the electronic system, and documents submitted there have binding legal effect (Proclamation 1333/2024, Arts. 53(1), 27(5)).
- Documents needed (Directive Art. 39(1)): valid VAT registration (unless the procurement is VAT-exempt); trade registration certificate and valid trade licence; excise registration where relevant; bid security; completed bid submission form; anti-collusion declaration; valid tax clearance certificate; competency documents (e.g. manufacturer/distributor authorisation, consortium agreement).
- Public bodies may let non-VAT-registered suppliers into a one-time procurement of up to ETB 500,000 (Art. 39(2)).
- Legal persons must disclose beneficial owners, including anyone holding at least 10% of shares (Art. 39(3)–(4)).
- A "domestic supplier" is a bidder registered and operating under Ethiopian law with a valid business licence, TIN and commercial registration certificate, regardless of shareholders' nationality (Art. 54(6)). National open tenders are for domestic suppliers only unless otherwise provided (Art. 54(5)).
- The Authority publishes lists of registered and debarred suppliers on e-GP (Art. 37(2)).
- No registration fee is published.

## Official text

### Proclamation No. 1333/2024 (English, verbatim)
Source: https://justice.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/seasiisia-seusiNse¡-1333-2016.pdf (listed at https://justice.gov.et/en/law/the-federal-public-procurement-and-property-administration-proclamation/; Federal Negarit Gazette 30th Year No. 66, 9 September 2024)

**Art. 12(9)** (Authority's duties): "Introduce and administer an efficient system of registration for candidates who are interested in participating in public procurement or sale of disposable property,"

**Art. 27(1).** "The Ministry shall establish electronic system to achieve economy, efficiency, transparency and modernization in the execution of public procurement and property administration, and it shall direct Public Bodies to implement electronic procurement ..."

**Art. 27(5).** "... documents submitted or communications made using the electronic system established pursuant to Sub-Article (1) of this Article has a binding legal effect."

**Art. 53(1).** "Bid proposals shall be submitted by using the electronic system before the expiry of the deadline for submission of bids ..."

Art. 88(1) repeals Proclamation 649/2009.

### Directive No. 1073/2025 (English, verbatim)
Source: https://www.ppa.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Directive_No_1073_2025_Federal_Government_Public_Procurement_Directive.pdf

**Art. 24(3)** (p. 39): "The business community interested in participating in government electronic procurement processes must register on the electronic system, ensure their information remains current, and renew their registration as necessary."

**Art. 37(2).** "The Public Procurement and Property Authority shall publish, through the electronic government procurement system, a list of registered suppliers and suppliers debarred from participating in public procurement."

**Art. 39(1) Pre-Qualification Criteria** (pp. 53–54): "a) Valid value-added tax (VAT) registration certificate, unless the procurement is exempt from VAT, b) Trade registration certificate and valid trade license issued by the appropriate government authority, c) For procurements subject to excise tax, a valid excise tax registration certificate, d) Bid security in accordance with the bid document, e) Properly completed bid submission form, f) Properly completed anti-collusion declaration, g) Valid tax clearance certificate [...], h) Documents verifying competency, including manufacturer or distributor authorization letters or consortium formation agreements, where applicable."

**Art. 39(2).** "... public bodies may allow suppliers not registered for value added tax to participate in a one-time procurement of up to ETB 500,000 (Five Hundred Thousand Birr)."

**Art. 39(4).** "beneficial ownership shall include: a) Individuals who directly or indirectly own at least 10% of the shares ..."

**Art. 54(6).** "a "domestic supplier" means a bidder registered and operating under Ethiopian law, possessing a valid business license, Tax Identification Number (TIN), and commercial registration certificate, irrespective of the nationality of its shareholders or owners."
