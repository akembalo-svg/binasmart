# የከተማ ቦታ የሊዝ ክፍያ ጊዜ — Urban land lease payment period

**Law in force:** Urban Lands Lease Holding Proclamation **No. 721/2011** (listed "In Force" on justice.gov.et, checked 2026-09-17). No newer federal lease proclamation was found. The lease **term** (up to 99 years for housing; 60/70/90 years in Addis Ababa depending on use) is a different thing from the **payment period** covered here.

## በአጭሩ

- **የመክፈያ ጊዜ:** የሊዝ ተጠቃሚ የሊዝ ዋጋውን የሚከፍልበት ጊዜ፣ ኢንቨስትመንቱ ወጪውን የሚመልስበትን ጊዜ ታሳቢ በማድረግ ይወሰናል (አዋጅ 721/2004 አንቀጽ 20(1))።
- **ቅድመ ክፍያ:** መጠኑን ክልሉ ወይም የከተማ አስተዳደሩ ይወስናል፤ ከጠቅላላ የሊዝ ዋጋው **ከ10% ማነስ የለበትም** (አንቀጽ 20(2))።
- **ቀሪው ክፍያ:** ቅድመ ክፍያው ከተከፈለ በኋላ ቀሪው በመክፈያ ዘመኑ **በእኩል ዓመታዊ ክፍያ** ይከፈላል (አንቀጽ 20(3))። በቀሪው ገንዘብ ላይ **በኢትዮጵያ ንግድ ባንክ የብድር ወለድ ተመን** ወለድ ይከፈላል (አንቀጽ 20(4))።
- **የችሮታ ጊዜ:** ቅድመ ክፍያ ከተፈጸመ በኋላ ዓመታዊ ክፍያ ከመጀመሩ በፊት ከክፍያ ነጻ የሚሆኑበት ጊዜ ነው (አንቀጽ 2(12))።
- **ማዘግየት:** ዓመታዊ ክፍያውን በወቅቱ ካልከፈሉ፣ ኢትዮጵያ ንግድ ባንክ ባዘገዩ ተበዳሪዎች ላይ በሚጥለው ተመን ቅጣት ይከፍላሉ (አንቀጽ 20(5))። **የ3 ዓመት ውዝፍ** ካለ አግባብ ያለው አካል ንብረቱን ይዞ በመሸጥ ዕዳውን ይሰበስባል (አንቀጽ 20(6))።
- ለባለበጀት የመንግሥት መሥሪያ ቤትና ለሃይማኖት ተቋም በምደባ የተሰጠ ቦታ ላይ እነዚህ የክፍያ ድንጋጌዎች ተፈጻሚ አይሆኑም (አንቀጽ 20(7))።
- **በአዲስ አበባ (መመሪያ 91/2004 አንቀጽ 72)** — የችሮታ ጊዜ / ቅድመ ክፍያ / የክፍያ ማጠናቀቂያ ጊዜ፦
  - **መኖሪያ ቤት:** 2 ዓመት / በጨረታ ያቀረቡት ወይም 10% / **40 ዓመት**
  - ኢንዱስትሪ፦ 4 ዓመት / 10% / 40 ዓመት
  - ሆስፒታል እና ዩኒቨርሲቲ፦ 3 ዓመት / 10% / 40 ዓመት
  - ባለ 4 ኮከብና ከዚያ በላይ ሆቴል፦ 3 ዓመት / 15% / 40 ዓመት
  - ግዙፍ ሪል ስቴት፦ 3 ዓመት / 20% / 40 ዓመት
  - ሌሎች፦ 2 ዓመት / 20% / **60 ዓመት**
  - ለዝቅተኛ ገቢ ማኅበረሰብ ክፍል የሚመደብ መኖሪያ፦ እስከ 99 ዓመት
- **በአዲስ አበባ ቀድሞ ለሚከፍሉ ቅናሽ (አንቀጽ 71):** ሙሉ ዋጋውን ውል በተፈረመ በ1 ዓመት ውስጥ ከከፈሉ 3%፣ በ2 ዓመት 2.5%፣ በ3 ዓመት 2%፣ በ4 ዓመት 1.5%፣ በ5 ዓመት 1% ከጠቅላላ የሊዝ ዋጋው (ወለድ ሳይጨምር) ይቀነሳል።
- **በአዲስ አበባ ውዝፍ (አንቀጽ 73):** በየዓመቱ የጽሑፍ ማስጠንቀቂያ ይሰጣል። የሦስት ዓመት ውዝፍ ካለ ከአራተኛው ዓመት ጀምሮ ጽ/ቤቱ ቦታውንና በላዩ ያለውን ንብረት በጨረታ ይሸጣል።

## In short (English)

- **Payment period:** a leaseholder may be given a payment period that takes into account the payback period of the investment (Proclamation 721/2011, Art. 20(1)).
- **Down payment:** set by the region or city administration, but **not less than 10%** of the total lease amount (Art. 20(2)).
- **Balance:** paid in **equal annual instalments** over the payment term (Art. 20(3)), with interest at the **Commercial Bank of Ethiopia's lending rate** (Art. 20(4)).
- **Grace period:** the time after the down payment during which no annual instalment is due (Art. 2(12)).
- **Late payment:** a penalty at the rate CBE charges defaulting borrowers (Art. 20(5)). With **three years of arrears**, the authority may seize and sell the lessee's property to collect the debt (Art. 20(6)).
- These payment rules do not apply to land allotted to budgetary government bodies or religious institutions (Art. 20(7)).
- **Addis Ababa (Directive 91/2004 E.C., Art. 72)** — grace period / down payment / payment completion period:
  - **Residential house:** 2 years / the percentage offered in the bid, or 10% / **40 years**
  - Industry: 4 years / 10% / 40 years
  - Hospital and university: 3 years / 10% / 40 years
  - Hotels of four stars and above: 3 years / 15% / 40 years
  - Large real estate: 3 years / 20% / 40 years
  - Others: 2 years / 20% / **60 years**
  - Housing allotted for low-income groups: up to 99 years
- **Addis Ababa early-payment discount (Art. 71):** paying the full lease price within 1 year of the contract earns 3% off; within 2 years 2.5%; 3 years 2%; 4 years 1.5%; 5 years 1% (on the total lease price, excluding interest).
- **Addis Ababa arrears (Art. 73):** written warnings are given each year. After three years of arrears, from the fourth year the office sells the leased land and the property on it by auction.

## Official text

**Proclamation No. 721/2011** (Federal Negarit Gazeta No. 4, 28 November 2011; PDF https://justice.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/አዋጅ-ቁጥር-721-2004.pdf)

> Art. 2(12) "grace period" means a time frame that a lessee is relieved from payment after effecting the advance lease payment and before the commencement of the annual lease payment;

> **20. Period of Payment**
> 1/ A person permitted urban land lease holding may be given a period of lease payment taking into account the payback period of the investment.
> 2/ The amount of down payment, to be determined in accordance with the prevailing factors of the region or the city administration, may not be less than 10% of the total lease amount of the urban land.
> 3/ The remaining balance of the lease amount shall be paid on the basis of equal annual installments during the payment term.
> 4/ Interest shall be paid on the remaining balance as per the prevailing interest rate on loans offered by the Commercial Bank of Ethiopia. The appropriate body shall have the responsibility to follow up the current loan interest rate and to update the applicable interest rate accordingly.
> 5/ Failure to pay the annual payment in time as originally scheduled shall result in penalty fee equivalent to the rate of penalty fee imposed by the Commercial Bank of Ethiopia on defaulting debtors.
> 6/ Where a lessee has failed to make payments within the specified time limit and accumulated arrears for three years, the appropriate body shall have the power to seize and sale the property of the lessee to collect the arrears.
> 7/ The provisions from sub-article (1) to (5) of this Article may not be applicable to urban land holding granted by allotment to a budgetary government entity or a religious institution ...

## What is not published

- **Whether Directive 91/2004 is still applied in Addis Ababa** or has been replaced: not confirmed. Ask the Addis Ababa Land Development and Administration Bureau.
- **Current CBE lending and penalty rates** used for lease interest: not published with the law. Ask the land office or CBE.
- **Payment periods in other regions:** set by regional lease regulations, which are not covered here.
- **Old (pre-lease, "permit") holdings:** Proclamation 721/2011 has separate transition rules, which are not covered here.
