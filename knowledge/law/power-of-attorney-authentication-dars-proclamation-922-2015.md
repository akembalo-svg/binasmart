# ውክልና ማረጋገጥና ማስመዝገብ በDARS — Authenticating a power of attorney at DARS

- ውክልና (የውክልና ሥልጣን) እና የውክልና መሻሪያ በሰነዶች ማረጋገጫና ምዝገባ አገልግሎት (DARS) ወይም በክልል ተቋማት ካልተረጋገጡና ካልተመዘገቡ ሕጋዊ ውጤት የላቸውም (አዋጅ 922/2008 አንቀጽ 9)።
- DARS ውክልና፣ ሽያጭ፣ ስጦታ፣ ብድር፣ የመመሥረቻ ጽሑፍና ልዩ ልዩ ውሎችን ያረጋግጣል (dars.gov.et)።
- አገልግሎቱን በDARS ቅርንጫፍ ቢሮዎች፣ በኦንላይን ፖርታል (dars.gov.et) እና በDARS የሞባይል መተግበሪያ (App Store/Play Store) ማግኘት ይቻላል።
- ወካዩ (ሥልጣን የሚሰጠው ሰው) በአረጋጋጩ ፊት ይፈርማል። አረጋጋጩ የፈራሚው ስምና አድራሻ በሰነዱ ላይ ካለው ጋር አንድ መሆኑን በማስረጃ ያረጋግጣል (አንቀጽ 10)። ስለዚህ ማንነትዎን የሚያሳይ መታወቂያ ይዘው ይቅረቡ።
- አረጋጋጩ የፈራሚውን የመፈረም ችሎታና ሥልጣን ያጣራል፤ ጥርጣሬ ካለ ምርመራ ያደርጋል (አንቀጽ 14)። የሰነዱ ይዘት ሕገ-ወጥ ወይም ከሥነ-ምግባር ውጭ መሆን የለበትም (አንቀጽ 13)።

- ከመረጋገጡ በፊት የቴምብር ቀረጥ መከፈሉ ይረጋገጣል። የአገልግሎት ክፍያው በሚኒስትሮች ምክር ቤት ደንብ ቁጥር 543/2016 ይወሰናል። የክፍያ ሠንጠረዡ በታተመው ደንብ ውስጥ ስለሌለ መጠኑን DARSን ይጠይቁ (አንቀጽ 34)።
- የተረጋገጠና የተመዘገበ ውክልና በውስጡ ላለው ነገር በቂ ማስረጃ ነው። በፍርድ ቤት ሊቃወሙት የሚቻለው ፍርድ ቤቱ በበቂ ምክንያት ሲፈቅድ ብቻ ነው (አንቀጽ 23)።
- በአዲስ አበባ፦ የልደታ ቅርንጫፍ 10 ደንበኞች ከሐምሌ 1 ጀምሮ የውክልና አገልግሎትን በአዲሱ ኦንላይን ሥርዓት (በሙከራ) እንዲጠይቁ DARS አሳውቋል።
- ውክልናው ለነዋሪነት (የቀበሌ) አገልግሎት በአዲስ አበባ ሲቀርብ ተወካዩ የሚያስፈጽመውን ጉዳይ በግልጽ የሚጠቅስ መሆን አለበት (መመሪያ 145/2015 አንቀጽ 2(28)፣ 28፣ 29(6))።
- ከውጭ አገር ለሚላክ ውክልና "ከውጭ አገር የሚላክ ውክልና" የሚለውን ገጽ ይመልከቱ።

## In short (English)
- A power of attorney, and its revocation, has no legal effect unless DARS (or a regional authentication office) authenticates and registers it (Proc. 922/2015 Art. 9).
- DARS authenticates powers of attorney, sales, gifts, loans, memoranda of association and other contracts (dars.gov.et).
- You can get the service at DARS branches, through the online portal linked from dars.gov.et, or with the DARS mobile app (App Store / Play Store).
- The principal signs in front of the notary. The notary checks with evidence that the signer's name and address match the document (Art. 10), so bring identification.
- The notary checks that the signer has the capacity and authority to sign, and investigates if in doubt (Art. 14). The content must not be illegal or immoral (Art. 13).
- If you bring a document you have already signed, the notary checks that the signature or seal is genuine (Art. 11).
- The notary makes sure stamp duty has been paid first. The service fee is set by Council of Ministers Regulation 543/2024, but the published regulation does not include its fee schedule. Ask DARS for the amount (Art. 34).
- An authenticated and registered power of attorney is conclusive evidence of its contents. It can be challenged in court only with the court's permission, for good cause (Art. 23).
- In Addis Ababa, DARS announced that customers of Lideta branch 10 should request power-of-attorney service through its new online system (a pilot) from Hamle 1.
- A power of attorney used for Addis Ababa residency (kebele) services must expressly name the matter the agent will handle (Directive 145/2015 Arts 2(28), 28, 29(6)).
- For a power of attorney made abroad, see the page on powers of attorney from abroad.

- Online portal links on the page: https://dars.gov.et:5102/landing and https://www.dars.gov.et:8086/Account/Login.

**Proclamation No. 922/2015** (Federal Negarit Gazette 22nd Year No. 39, 15 February 2016) — https://justice.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/አዋጅ-ቁጥር-922-2008.pdf — MoJ status: "In Force", no amending law listed (checked 17 Sept 2026).
- Art. 2(1): "document" includes a "document of power of attorney or revocation".
- Art. 9: documents that have no legal effect unless authenticated and registered include "(b) power of attorney or revocation of power of attorney".
- Art. 10(1): "Where a new document is submitted for authentication, the notary shall authenticate the document after having ascertained by means of evidence that the name and address of the person who is about to sign the document is the same as the name and address on the document."
- Art. 11(1): "Where a document which is already signed is submitted for authentication, the notary shall authenticate such document by ascertaining that the signature on the document is that of the person who has signed it or that the seal on the document is genuine."
- Art. 13(1): "A notary shall, before authenticating and registering a document, ascertain that its contents are not illegal or immoral."
- Art. 14(1)–(2): "The notary shall, before authenticating a document, make sure that the person who has signed or is about to sign the document has the right or authority to sign it. Where the capacity of a person … is doubtful …, the notary shall make the necessary investigation in appropriate way."
- Art. 22: detailed procedures for documents "sent from Ethiopia to other countries and; those sent from other countries to Ethiopia shall be regulated by regulation to be issued by the Council of Ministers."
- Art. 23: "1/ … a document authenticated and registered in accordance with this Proclamation shall be conclusive evidence of its contents. 2/ Authenticated and registered documents may, during proceeding, be challenged only with the permission of a court for good cause."
- Art. 34: "1/ The notary shall make sure the payment of stump duty pursuant to the relevant law before authenticating and registering a document. … 3/ The amount of service fee shall be determined by regulation to be issued by the Council of Ministers."

**Council of Ministers Regulation No. 543/2024** — https://justice.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/ደንብ-ቁጥር-543-2016.pdf — Art. 2: fees "as prescribed in the Schedule attached here to". Art. 4 repeals Regulation No. 451/2019. The published 2-page PDF does not contain the schedule.

**Addis Ababa Directive No. 145/2015**, Art. 2(28): ውክልና is a general or special power of attorney given by a competent Ethiopian body, a consulate or embassy, or a court, "ተወካይ … ጉዳዩን እንዲያሰፈጽምለት የሚያስችል ስልጣንን ለተወካይ በግልጽ የተሰጠበት". Art. 29(6): a residence release letter can be handled by an agent only under a special power of attorney that expressly covers it.
