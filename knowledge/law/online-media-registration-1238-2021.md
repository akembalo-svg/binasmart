# የበይነ-መረብ መገናኛ ብዙኃን ምዝገባ — Registering an Online Media Outlet

## በአጭሩ (Amharic summary)

- የበይነ-መረብ (ኦንላይን) መገናኛ ብዙኃን **ፈቃድ አይወስዱም፤ ይመዘገባሉ**። ምዝገባውን የሚያከናውነው የኢትዮጵያ መገናኛ ብዙኃን ባለሥልጣን ነው።
- "ኦንላይን ሚዲያ" ማለት ዋና ሥራው ዜና ወይም ፕሮግራም መሰብሰብ፣ ማዘጋጀትና ማሰራጨት የሆነ፣ በኤዲቶሪያል ኃላፊነት የሚሠራ ድርጅት ነው (አዋጅ 1238/2021 አንቀጽ 2(4))።
- ኢትዮጵያዊ ዜጎች በግል ወይም በሕጋዊ ሰውነት ምዝገባ ሊጠይቁ ይችላሉ።
- የሚያስፈልጉ መረጃዎችና ሰነዶች፦ የባለቤቱ ወይም የድርጅቱ ስምና አድራሻ፣ ስልክና ኢሜይል፣ የዶሜይን ስምና የባለቤትነት ማረጋገጫ፣ የኢንተርኔት አገልግሎት ሰጪ፣ የግብር ከፋይ መለያ ቁጥር (TIN)፣ የዋና አዘጋጁ ስምና አድራሻ፣ የንግድ ፈቃድ ወይም ምዝገባ፣ የመሥራቹ፣ የባለቤቱና የዋና አዘጋጁ መታወቂያ ወይም ፓስፖርት ቅጂ።
- ግለሰብ አመልካቾች 2 ባለቀለም የፓስፖርት መጠን ፎቶ ያቀርባሉ።
- ባለሥልጣኑ በ30 ቀናት ውስጥ የምዝገባ ምስክር ወረቀት መስጠት አለበት። ሙሉ ማመልከቻ ቀርቦ በ30 ቀናት ውስጥ ካልተሰጠ ሕጋዊ እውቅና እንዳገኘ ይቆጠራል፤ ሥራ መጀመርም ይቻላል።
- ምዝገባ ሊከለከል የሚችለው አስፈላጊ መረጃ ካልቀረበ ወይም ስሙ ቀድሞ ከተያዘ ብቻ ነው። ቅሬታ ለዋና ዳይሬክተሩ፣ ከዚያ ለቦርዱ፣ ከዚያ ለፌዴራል ከፍተኛ ፍርድ ቤት ይቀርባል።
- ምስክር ወረቀቱ በየዓመቱ ይታደሳል፤ ከተሰጠ በኋላ ለንግድ ሚኒስቴር ወይም ለክልል ንግድ ቢሮ ቀርቦ መመዝገብ አለበት።
- ፈቃድ ያለው ሬዲዮ፣ ቴሌቪዥን ወይም ጋዜጣ የራሱን ይዘት ብቻ በኦንላይን ካሰራጨ እንደገና መመዝገብ አያስፈልገውም። የመንግሥትና መንግሥታዊ ያልሆኑ ተቋማት ለራሳቸው ተልዕኮ የሚያሰራጩት ይዘትም ምዝገባ አይፈልግም።
- የማመልከቻና የምስክር ወረቀት ክፍያ በአገልግሎት ክፍያ ደንብ ይወሰናል፤ መጠኑ በይፋ አልተገኘም።

## In short (English)

- Online media outlets are **registered, not licensed**, by the Ethiopian Media Authority (EMA).
- "Online media" means an organization whose principal business is collecting, producing and disseminating news or programmes online under editorial responsibility (Proc. 1238/2021, Art. 2(4)).
- Ethiopian citizens, privately or through a company, may request registration (Art. 27(1)).
- You provide: owner/company name and address, office phone and email, domain name and proof of ownership, internet service provider details, TIN, Editor-in-Chief's name and address, trade licence or registration, and ID/passport copies of the founder, owner and editor-in-chief. Individuals add two passport-size colour photos (Directive 902, Art. 9(1)).
- The EMA must decide within 30 days. If a complete application gets no certificate in 30 days, recognition is presumed and you may start operating.
- Registration can be refused only for missing information or a name already taken. Appeals: EMA Director General → Board → Federal High Court.
- The certificate is renewed every year and must then be registered with the Ministry of Trade or regional trade bureau.
- Licensed radio, TV or print outlets that only republish their own content online, and public or non-government bodies publishing for their own mandate, do not need to register.
- Application and certificate fees are set by a service-fee regulation; the amount was not found.
- The 2025 amendment (Proc. 1374/2025) mainly changed EMA governance and appeals, not online-media registration.

## Official text

**Media Proclamation No. 1238/2021 (English)** — https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/legislation/details/21659

Art. 2(4): "'Online media' means an internet-based information dissemination service by an organization whose principal business involves the collection, production, processing and dissemination of news or programs or news and programs, through online images, audio, video and websites or through a combination of the aforementioned means, in accordance with the editorial responsibility of a media service provider;"

Art. 6(3): the Authority shall "give legal recognition to a periodical or news service activity or an online media the dissemination of which covers more than one regional state by registering and issuing certificate of registration;"

Art. 27(1): "Ethiopian citizens either privately or through juridical person may request for an online media registration after fulfilling the following conditions:" (name and address of the entity; ownership conditions; domain name and proof of ownership; office phone; email; ISP details; nationality condition; taxpayer identification number; name and address of the Editor-in-Chief; trade licence or main registration).

Art. 27(2): "The Authority has to complete the registration and issue certificate of registration within 30 days of request ... Legal recognition is presumed if certificate of registration is not issued after 30 days of the application."

Art. 27(3): "Online media can start operations after 30 days if a certificate of registration has not been issued while the Authority has obtained the application meeting all the required conditions."

Art. 27(4): "Registration may only be refused within 30 days only if the applicant does not provide the necessary information ..."

Art. 61 (obligations, excerpt): online media must avoid obscene language and content inciting hostility or violence, and "clearly indicate contents originating from third party affiliates, special interest groups, and advertisers".

**EMA Internet Media Directive No. 902/2014 E.C. (Amharic)** — https://justice.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/Internet-Media-Directive-No.-902-2014.pdf

Art. 5(5): "... በበይነ-መረብ መገናኛ ብዙሃን የሚያሠራጭ ከሆነ በዚህ መመሪያ መሠረት የመመዝገብ ግዴታ አይኖርበትም፡፡"
Art. 9(1): individual applicants add "2 ጉርድ የፓስፖርት መጠን ባለቀለም ፎቶግራፍ".
Art. 10(3): fees are paid "በአገልግሎት ክፍያ ደንብ የሚተመነውን".
Art. 10(5): "አመልካቹ ከባለሥልጣኑ የተሰጠውን የምዝገባ ምስክር ወረቀት ለንግድ ሚኒስቴር ወይም ለክልል ንግድ ቢሮ በማቅረብ ማስመዝገብ አለበት፡፡"
Art. 12(1): the certificate "በየአመቱ ይታደሳል".
