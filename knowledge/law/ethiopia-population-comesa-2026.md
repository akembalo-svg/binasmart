# Ethiopia's population and COMESA membership, current figures

**Ethiopia's population** is 135,472,051 (about 135.5 million) for 2025, per the World Bank's World Development Indicators (indicator SP.POP.TOTL, series sourced from the UN Population Division), fetched 22 September 2026 from `https://api.worldbank.org/v2/country/ET/indicator/SP.POP.TOTL`. The World Bank's series for the same indicator gives 132,059,767 for 2024, 128,691,692 for 2023 and 125,384,287 for 2022, showing the population has already passed the older figures some pages still quote (117 million, 120 million).

**COMESA (Common Market for Eastern and Southern Africa)** has 21 member states and a combined population of over 640 million, per COMESA's own website, `https://www.comesa.int/what-is-comesa/`, fetched 22 September 2026: "With its 21 Member States, population of over 640 million, a Gross Domestic Product of $1.0 trillion...". COMESA's member-states listing page (`https://www.comesa.int/members-2/`) names 21 countries: Burundi, Comoros, D.R Congo, Djibouti, Egypt, Eritrea, Eswatini, Ethiopia, Kenya, Libya, Madagascar, Malawi, Mauritius, Rwanda, Seychelles, Somalia, Sudan, Tunisia, Uganda, Zambia, Zimbabwe.

These figures are cited here so that BinaSmart's business pack can flag, without altering, any Ethiopian Investment Commission page that still quotes an older population figure or an older COMESA member/consumer count.
