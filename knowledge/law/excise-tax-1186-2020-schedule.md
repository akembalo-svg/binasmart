# Excise Tax Proclamation No. 1186/2020 — rates (First Schedule, 2020 text)

**Scope.** Excise tax is charged on the excisable goods listed in the First Schedule (19 groups; rates from 5% to 500%). It is **not** only for luxury goods: it also covers everyday goods like fuel, sugar, salt, water and soft drinks, and goods harmful to health.

**Imports (Art. 7).** For imported goods the taxable value is the customs value of the goods **plus the customs duty** payable. The importer pays at the time of importation, collected under the customs law as if it were customs duty (Art. 37(3)-(4)).

**Local manufacture.** Returns and payment are due monthly, by the 30th day of the following month (Arts. 36-37).

## First Schedule (2020) — excise rate on value, by group
1. **Fats and oils**
   - Edible fats/oils with 40g+ saturated fat per 100g (15.01–15.15): 30%
   - Hydrogenated fats (15.16): 40%
   - Margarine (15.17): 50%
2. **Sugar and sugar confectionery**
   - Any sugar, solid or liquid, excluding molasses and maple (17.01, 1702.2000): 20%
   - Chewing gum and sugar confectionery (1704): 30%
3. **Chocolate** and food preparations containing cocoa (18.06, not infant food): 30%
4. **Powder soft drinks** (2106.9040): 25%
5. **Beverages**
   - Plain bottled or sparkling water, factory-canned, no sugar or flavour (22.01): 10%
   - Sweetened or flavoured waters and other soft drinks, excluding juices (22.02): 25%
   - Non-alcoholic beer: 25%
   - Malt beer: 40% or Birr 11 per litre, whichever is higher
   - Beer made exclusively from barley grown and malted in Ethiopia: 35% or Birr 9 per litre
   - Beer with at least 75% local raw material: 30% or Birr 8 per litre
   - Other fermented beverages (cider, mead, etc.) and ready-to-drink mixes up to 7%: 40% or Birr 11 per litre
   - Grape wine (22.04): 40%
   - Wine with at least 75% local raw material: 30%
   - Spirits (whisky, gin, vodka, rum, liqueurs) and denatured alcohol: 80%
   - Pure alcohol (2207.2000): 60%
6. **Tobacco**
   - Tobacco leaf: 20%
   - Cigarettes: 30% + Birr 8 per pack of 20
   - Cigars, pipe tobacco, snuff and other tobacco products: 30% + Birr 250 per kg
7. **Salt** (25.01): 25%
8. **Fuel** — petrol, benzene, gasoline, jet fuel (27.10): 30%
9. **Perfumes, toilet waters, cosmetics and beauty products** (except sunscreen and shampoo): 100%
10. **Fireworks**: 100%
11. **Plastic shopping bags**: Birr 40 per kg
12. **Rubber tyres** (40.11–40.13): 5%
13. **Textiles**
    - Industrial textile fabrics, blankets, bedsheets, towels, etc. (not mosquito nets or abugedi): 8%
    - Carpets: 30%
    - Garments: 8%
14. **Artificial flowers**, foliage and fruit: 10%
15. **Human hair and wigs**: the rate cell is blank in the gazette copy read — not confirmed.
16. **Asbestos and asbestos products**: 20%
17. **Pearls, precious stones, precious metals and imitation jewellery** (71.01–71.17): 20%
18. **Video recorders/players, TV and digital cameras, radio receivers, televisions** (85.21, 85.25, 85.27, 85.28): 10%
19. **Motor vehicles, tractors, motorcycles, trailers** (87.01–87.16), plus gambling machines and playing cards (20%) and shisha/smoking pipes (20%)
    - Vehicle rates depend on engine size, age and form (CKD/SKD/CBU); in 2020 they ran from 5% to 500%.
    - ⚠️ 1287/2023 changed the vehicle rates and definitions. A "new vehicle" is now CBU arriving within 3 calendar years of manufacture with at most 4,000 km, plus SKD/CKD kits; "used" is anything else.
    - Do NOT quote a vehicle excise rate from this file. Point to the ECC calculator or the used-car guide.
