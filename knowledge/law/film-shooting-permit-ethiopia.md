# በኢትዮጵያ ፊልም ለመቅረጽ የሚያስፈልግ ፈቃድ — Film Shooting Permits in Ethiopia

## በአጭሩ (Amharic summary)

- በይፋ የተገኘው ሕግ **የውጭ ዜጎች** በኢትዮጵያ ፊልም ሲቀርጹ የሚመለከት ብቻ ነው። ኢትዮጵያውያን ፊልም ለመሥራት ወይም ፊልም ለማሳየት ፈቃድ እንደሚያስፈልግ የሚገልጽ ይፋዊ ሕግ አልተገኘም።
- የውጭ ዜጎችን የፊልም ቀረጻ የሚመዘግበውና ፈቃድ የሚሰጠው የኢትዮጵያ መገናኛ ብዙኃን ባለሥልጣን ነው (መመሪያ ቁጥር 900/2014)። (ደንብ 66/1992 ስሙን "የማስታወቂያና ባህል ሚኒስቴር" ብሎ ይጠራል፤ ይህ ሚኒስቴር አሁን የለም።)
- የውጭ ዜጋ የትረካ ወይም ዘጋቢ ፊልም ለመቅረጽ ኢትዮጵያ ከመግባቱ ቢያንስ 15 ቀን በፊት በአቅራቢያው ለሚገኝ የኢትዮጵያ ኤምባሲ ወይም ቆንስላ የፐብሊክ ዲፕሎማሲ ክፍል ማመልከት አለበት።
- ኤምባሲው ማመልከቻውን በ5 ቀን ውስጥ በውጭ ጉዳይ ሚኒስቴር በኩል ይልካል፤ ሚኒስቴሩ በ2 የሥራ ቀናት ውስጥ ለባለሥልጣኑ ያስተላልፋል።
- ባለሥልጣኑ ቪዛውን አረጋግጦ ክፍያ ከተቀበለ በኋላ በአንድ ቀን ውስጥ ምስክር ወረቀት ወይም ፈቃድ ይሰጣል።
- ማመልከቻው የፊልሙን ዓላማና ርዕስ፣ የቀረጻ ጊዜ፣ ትክክለኛ ቦታ፣ ጠቅላላ የምርት ወጪና በኢትዮጵያ የሚወጣውን ድርሻ ይገልጻል። ይያያዛሉ፦ ስክሪፕትና ማጠቃለያ (ለትረካ ፊልም) ወይም ማጠቃለያ (ለዘጋቢ ፊልም)፣ ፊልሙ ታዋቂ ኢትዮጵያዊን የሚያሳይ ከሆነ "ተቃውሞ የለኝም" የሚል ሰነድ፣ የጋራ ፕሮዳክሽን ስምምነት ካለ፣ የሠራተኞች ዜግነት፣ ፓስፖርትና አድራሻ።
- የፈቃድ ክፍያ (ደንብ 66/1992 አንቀጽ 12)፦ ለመጀመሪያው 100,000 ብር የምርት ወጪ 1,000 ብር፤ ከ100,000 ብር በላይ ላለው ተጨማሪ ወጪ 0.2%። የቀረጻ ቦታ ክፍያ ለብቻው ይከፈላል።
- በአውሮፕላን ለመቅረጽ የበረራ ፈቃድ ከኢትዮጵያ ሲቪል አቪዬሽን ባለሥልጣን በ10 ቀን ውስጥ ይገኛል።
- የባህልና ስፖርት ሚኒስቴር የፊልም ኢንዱስትሪን የማበረታታት ሚና አለው፤ ፊልም የመፍቀድ ሥልጣን ግን በአዋጁ አልተሰጠውም።

## In short (English)

- The official rules found cover only **foreigners** filming in Ethiopia. No official rule was found requiring Ethiopians to get a permit to produce a film, or covering film screening.
- The Ethiopian Media Authority (EMA) registers and licenses foreign filming (Directive 900/2014 E.C.). Regulation 66/2000 names the "Ministry of Information and Culture", which no longer exists.
- A foreign national making a narrative or documentary film must apply at least 15 days before arriving, to the public diplomacy unit of the nearest Ethiopian embassy or consulate.
- The embassy forwards it within 5 days via the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, which sends it to the EMA within 2 working days.
- After checking the visa and collecting the fee, the EMA issues the certificate or permit within one day.
- The application states the film's purpose and title, shooting period, exact location, and total production cost with the share spent in Ethiopia; attach the script and synopsis (feature) or synopsis (documentary), a no-objection document if an Ethiopian personality is portrayed, any co-production agreement, and crew nationality, passport details and addresses.
- Permit fee (Reg. 66/2000, Art. 12): Birr 1,000 for the first Birr 100,000 of production cost, plus 0.2% of the cost above Birr 100,000. Location fees are separate. Directive 900 applies this same formula.
- Aerial filming needs a flight permit from the Ethiopian Civil Aviation Authority, issued within 10 days.
- The Ministry of Culture and Sport promotes the film industry but has no permit-issuing power under Proclamation 1263/2021.

## Official text

**Council of Ministers Regulation No. 66/2000** — https://justice.gov.et/en/law/film-shooting-permit-council-of-ministers-regulation-council-of-ministers-regulation-no-66-2000/
Art. 5: "A foreigner wishing to shoot a feature or documentary film in Ethiopia shall obtain a permit from the Ministry."
Art. 6(1): apply "not later than ten (10) days prior to the day on which he is due to commence filming."
Art. 7(1): the permit is issued "within 3 days" once requirements are met and the fee is paid.
Art. 8: flight permit from the Ethiopian Civil Aviation Authority "within 10 days".
Art. 9: the applicant submits "two English VHS Video cassette copies of the film produced" and signs an undertaking.
Art. 12(1) Permit Fee: "(a) Birr, 1,000.00 (one thousand birr) for the first Birr, 100,000.00 (one hundred thousand birr) of the production cost; and (b) 0.2% on the additional cost of production over and above Birr, 100,000.00."

(Note: Regulation 66/2000 says apply 10 days before filming and a 3-day issue time; the newer EMA Directive 900/2014 E.C. says apply at least 15 days before arrival, with a one-day issue time once the file reaches the EMA.)

**EMA Directive No. 900/2014 E.C. (Amharic)** — https://justice.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Foreign-Media-News-Agencies-and-Foreign-Citizens-Film-Recording-Registration-and-Licensing-Directive-No.-900-2014.pdf
Art. 13(1): "... ቢያንስ ከ15 ቀን በፊት በአቅራቢያው ለሚገኝ የኢፌዲሪ ኤምባሲ ወይም ቆንስላ ጄኔራል ጽሕፈት ቤት የፐብሊክ ዲፕሎማሲ ሥራ ክፍል ... ማቅረብ ወይም መላክ አለበት፡፡"
Art. 19(2): "... የምዝገባ ወይም የፈቃድ ክፍያ በማስከፈል፣ ጥያቄው በቀረበለት በአንድ ቀን ጊዜ ውስጥ የምዝገባ የምስክር ወረቀት ወይም ፈቃድ ይሰጣል"
Art. 20(2): the fee follows "የሚኒስትሮች ምክር ቤት ደንብ ቁጥር 66/1992 አንቀጽ 12" or a future amended fee law.

**Proclamation No. 1263/2021, Art. 37(1)(d)** — https://justice.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/አዋጅ-ቁጥር-1263-2014.pdf
The Ministry of Culture and Sport shall "create conducive condition for the development of the country's film industry and theatrical arts".

## What is not published

- Any permit requirement for Ethiopian citizens or companies producing a film.
- Any federal or Addis Ababa rule on film screening, cinema licensing or age classification.
- Any "Ethiopian Film Authority" or official film-commission law; private film-commission websites are not official sources.
- Location fee amounts (the regulation says they are paid separately but does not list them).
