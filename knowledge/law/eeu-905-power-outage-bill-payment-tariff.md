## In short (English)

- **For a power outage or fault,** call the Ethiopian Electric Utility (EEU) call centre on **905**. The website's "Hot line" setting shows 905 and 904.
- Other channels: the emergency repair phone of your nearest service centre, suggestion boxes, any EEU office in person, or www.eeu.gov.et.
- Planned maintenance outages are announced in advance in the "Power Interruptions" section of eeu.gov.et (the latest notice was published in September 2026).
- **To report corruption or ethics violations,** use https://eeuethics.et; your identity is kept confidential. EEU also has a Telegram bot for this. Email: contact@ethiopianelectricutility.gov.et.
- **Paying from anywhere (notice of July 2026):** telebirr (app or *127#), CBE Birr (app or *889#), Awash Birr Pro, M-PESA (Safaricom), Coopay-Ebirr (Oromia Bank), and the mobile banking of all banks.
- Postpaid customers must pay on the same schedule they used before. Late payment brings a penalty and extra costs.
- Prepaid customers must top up at least once every 3 months. When you top up with telebirr, an SMS link to the receipt is sent to you.
- **What the bill includes:** energy charge, service charge, VAT 15%, regulator levy 0.5% and a TV tax of 10 birr. Residential customers who use less than 200 kWh a month do not pay VAT and get a subsidy on their energy charge (notice of July 2026).
- **Residential tariff for Tir 1 to Megabit 30, 2018 E.C. (birr per kWh):** 0–50: 0.8377; 51–100: 2.0324; 101–200: 3.4549; 201–300: 5.2177; 301–400: 5.4057; 401–500: 5.5354; above 500: 5.5017 (as published by EEU). General (commercial) tariff: 5.4958.
- Service charge for the same period: residential postpaid 11.66 birr up to 50 kWh and 48.65 birr above 50 kWh; prepaid 4.70 birr up to 50 kWh and 16.92 birr above 50 kWh.
- The tariff has been rising every quarter. No rate table for Miyazia 2018 E.C. or later was found on the website. Call 905 for the current rates.
