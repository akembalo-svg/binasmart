# በፌደራል መንግሥት ጨረታ ለመሳተፍ የሚያስፈልጉ መስፈርቶችና የጨረታ ማስከበሪያ — Requirements to Bid on a Federal Tender and Bid Security

**ማሳሰቢያ፦** አዋጅ ቁጥር 649/2009 ተሽሯል፤ የተካው አዋጅ ቁጥር 1333/2024 ነው (አንቀጽ 88(1))። **Note:** Proclamation 649/2009 is repealed and replaced by Proclamation No. 1333/2024 (Art. 88(1)).

## በአጭሩ (Amharic summary)

- በፌደራል መንግሥት ግዥ ለመሳተፍ ተጫራቹ፦ ውል የመዋዋል ችሎታ ያለው፣ ያልከሰረ ወይም በመፍረስ ላይ ያልሆነ፣ የጥቅም ግጭት የሌለበት፣ ከግዥ የታገደበት ጊዜ ያበቃ፣ የታደሰ የንግድ ፈቃድ ያለውና የግብር ግዴታውን የተወጣ እንዲሁም የባንክ ሂሳብ ያለው መሆን አለበት (አዋጅ 1333 አንቀጽ 24(1))።
- የመሥሪያ ቤቱ የቀድሞ ሠራተኛ ከሆኑ ከለቀቁ ቢያንስ አንድ ዓመት ማለፍ አለበት (አንቀጽ 24(1)(ሰ))።
- የውጭ ድርጅት በኢትዮጵያ የንግድ እንቅስቃሴ ከሌለው፣ በተቋቋመበት አገር የተሰጠውን ፈቃድና ምዝገባ በኢትዮጵያ ኤምባሲ አረጋግጦ ያቀርባል።
- በሽርክና፣ በጋራ ማኅበር ወይም በኮንሶርቲየም መሳተፍ ይቻላል (አንቀጽ 24(2))። ሐሰተኛ ሰነድ ያቀረበ ተጫራች ከውድድሩ ይሰረዛል (አንቀጽ 24(7))።
- የሚያስፈልጉ ሰነዶች (መመሪያ 1073 አንቀጽ 39(1))፦ VAT ምዝገባ፣ የንግድ ምዝገባና የሚሠራ የንግድ ፈቃድ፣ (ካስፈለገ) የኤክሳይዝ ምዝገባ፣ የጨረታ ማስከበሪያ፣ የጨረታ ማቅረቢያ ቅጽ፣ የፀረ-ሽርክና ማረጋገጫ፣ የግብር ክሊራንስ እና የብቃት ሰነዶች። ከጨረታ ማስከበሪያ ውጪ ያሉ ሕጋዊ ሰነዶች ጨረታው ከመዘጋቱ በፊት የተሰጡ ከሆኑ በቅድመ ግምገማ ወቅት ማቅረብ ሊፈቀድ ይችላል (አንቀጽ 39(7))።
- የጨረታ ማስከበሪያ መጠን ከግዥው ጠቅላላ ግምት ከ0.5% ያላነሰና ከ2% ያልበለጠ ነው፤ በምንም ሁኔታ ከ2,000,000 ብር አይበልጥም። መጠኑ በጨረታ ማስታወቂያውና በሰነዱ ላይ ይገለጻል (መመሪያ አንቀጽ 116(3))።
- የማስከበሪያው ዓይነት በተጫራቹ ምርጫ፦ በባንክ የተረጋገጠ የክፍያ ትዕዛዝ (CPO)፣ ቅድመ ሁኔታ የሌለው የባንክ ዋስትና ወይም በባንክ የተረጋገጠ ሌተር ኦፍ ክሬዲት (አንቀጽ 116(5))።
- ማስከበሪያው የጨረታ ዋጋው ጸንቶ ከሚቆይበት የመጨረሻ ቀን በኋላ ቢያንስ ለ28 ቀናት ጸንቶ መቆየት አለበት (አንቀጽ 116(6))። የውጭ ተጫራቾች በአገር ውስጥ ባንክ የተረጋገጠ የውጭ ባንክ ዋስትና ያቀርባሉ።
- ለአማካሪ አገልግሎትና ለዋጋ ማቅረቢያ (price quotation) ግዥ በአብዛኛው ማስከበሪያ አይጠየቅም፤ ለኤሌክትሮኒክ ጨረታዎችና ለአንዳንድ ግዥዎች በማስከበሪያ ፋንታ የተፈረመ የማስከበሪያ መተማመኛ ቅጽ ሊጠየቅ ይችላል (አንቀጽ 116(1)–(2))።
- ማስከበሪያው የሚወረሰው፦ ጨረታው ከተከፈተ በኋላ ሰነዱን ቢያነሱ፣ አሸንፈው ውል ለመፈረም ወይም የውል ማስከበሪያ ለማቅረብ እምቢ ቢሉ፣ የሒሳብ እርማትን ባይቀበሉ ወይም ማጭበርበር/መመሳጠር ከተረጋገጠ (አዋጅ አንቀጽ 52(3))።
- ጨረታ የሚቀርበው በኤሌክትሮኒክ ሥርዓት ብቻ ሲሆን ከማቅረቢያ ጊዜ በኋላ የቀረበ ጨረታ ውድቅ ይሆናል (አንቀጽ 53)።

## In short (English)

- To bid, a candidate must: have capacity to contract; not be insolvent, bankrupt or being wound up; have no conflict of interest; have finished any debarment period; hold a renewed trade licence and have paid taxes; and have a bank account (Proclamation 1333/2024, Art. 24(1)).
- Ex-employees of the procuring body must have left at least one year earlier (Art. 24(1)(g)).
- A foreign company with no business in Ethiopia submits its home licence and registration authenticated by the Ethiopian Embassy.
- Joint ventures, partnerships and consortia may bid (Art. 24(2)); bidders who submit false documents are disqualified (Art. 24(7)).
- Documents (Directive 1073/2025, Art. 39(1)): VAT registration, trade registration and valid trade licence, excise registration if relevant, bid security, bid submission form, anti-collusion declaration, tax clearance and competency documents. Missing legal documents (not bid security) may be allowed at preliminary evaluation if issued before bid closing (Art. 39(7)).
- Bid security: no less than 0.5% and no more than 2% of the estimated cost, never above ETB 2,000,000, stated in the invitation and bid documents (Directive Art. 116(3)).
- Form, at the bidder's choice: certified bank payment order (CPO), unconditional bank guarantee, or bank-confirmed letter of credit (Art. 116(5)).
- It must stay valid at least 28 calendar days beyond the bid validity date (Art. 116(6)). Foreign bidders use foreign-bank instruments certified by a domestic bank (Art. 116(7)).
- Consultancy and price-quotation procurements normally need no bid security; for electronic tenders and some other cases a signed bid security declaration may replace it (Art. 116(1)–(2)).
- Bid security can be forfeited if the bidder withdraws after opening, the winner refuses to sign or give performance security, the bidder rejects arithmetic correction, or fraud/collusion is proved (Proclamation Art. 52(3)).
- Bids are submitted only through the electronic system; late bids are rejected (Art. 53).

**Art. 24(1) (EN, verbatim, excerpts).** "In order to participate in public procurement and sale of disposable property, candidates shall qualify by meeting the following criteria as appropriate, and such other criteria as the Public Body considers appropriate under the circumstances: ... c) that the candidate does not have conflict of interest ... d) that the candidate has the capacity to enter into a contract, e) that the candidate is not insolvent, in receivership, bankrupt or being wound up, or that his business activities have not been suspended ... f) that the period for which he was suspended from participating in public procurement or sale of disposable property must have been over, g) ... in the case of a candidate being an ex-employee of the Public Body executing the procurement ... at least one year mast have lapsed since he left the Public Body, h) the candidate who has renewed trade license and fulfilled his obligation to pay taxes in accordance with the Ethiopian tax laws or a foreign company having no business operation in Ethiopia and with business license and certificate of registration authenticated by Embassy of Ethiopia in a country where the company has been licensed or registered, and i) unless it is a foreign company that does not have business in Ethiopia, the candidate has a bank account."

**Art. 52 Bid Security (EN, verbatim).** "1/ Public Bodies shall include statement in the bid documents that bid proposals shall be accompanied by bid security or otherwise fill out the bid security form. Public Bodies shall ensure the genuineness of the bid security. 2/ The amount of bid security ... shall be prescribed in the Directive to be issued hereunder. 3/ A bid security furnished by any bidder may be forfeited for one of the following reasons: a) where the bidder withdraws his bid proposal after the bids were opened and within the validity period of the bid, or b) where the successful bidder refuses to sign the contract or fails to take delivery of the disposable property or to furnish performance security after notification of award, c) where the bidder refuses to accept arithmetic correction made in accordance with this Proclamation and the Directive issued hereunder, d) where the bidder commits fraudulent or deceitful act, collides with other bidders or conceals information and where such act or omission is proved by a competent Authority."

### Directive No. 1073/2025 (English, verbatim)
Source: https://www.ppa.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Directive_No_1073_2025_Federal_Government_Public_Procurement_Directive.pdf

**Art. 39(7).** "if bidders fail to submit required legal documents (excluding bid security) at the time of bid submission, they may be permitted to submit them during the preliminary evaluation stage, provided that the documents were issued before bid closing ..."

**Art. 54(5).** "only domestic suppliers shall be allowed to participate in national open tenders" (unless otherwise provided).

**Art. 116 Bid Security** (pp. 149–150):
"1. Except for procurements conducted through consultancy services and the price quotation method, Public Bodies shall clearly specify and include the bid security requirement in the bidding documents for all procurements conducted using open bidding, limited bidding, or the two-stage bidding method. ...
2. ... for electronic tenders, and for other procurement methods, the Public Body may, based on the urgency or nature of the procurement, require bidders to submit a signed bid security declaration form in lieu of a formal bid security.
3. The Public Body shall determine the bid security amount to be no less than 0.5% and no more than 2% of the total estimated cost of the procurement, and shall indicate this amount clearly in both the invitation to bid and the bidding documents. In any case, the bid security amount shall not exceed Ethiopian Birr 2,000,000 (Two Million Birr).
5. ... The bid security may be provided in one of the following forms, at the discretion of the bidder: a certified bank payment order, an unconditional bank guarantee, or a bank-confirmed letter of credit issued by a recognized financial institution.
6. The bid security submitted by bidders shall remain valid for a minimum of twenty-eight (28) calendar days beyond the last date of validity of the bid price.
7. Foreign bidders shall submit bid security instruments issued by foreign banks that are certified by a domestic bank operating in Ethiopia. ..."

## What is not published

- The price of bid documents — it is set in each tender notice, not in the law; check the notice or ask the procuring body.
- The exact bid security amount for a given tender — it is stated in that tender's invitation and bid documents.
