If you are planning to travel within a week (including the current date), you can submit a request for the flight date change. If you want to travel on the same date as the original travel date but with a different flight, please contact us through our global support numbers.

## Rules and Regulations for Flight Change

- Complete Information:
Please ensure you provide full and accurate details when completing the flight change form to avoid delays.
- Price Changes:
Please note that prices are subject to change at any time.
- No-Show Fee:
If a flight date change is requested on or after the flight date, an additional No-Show fee will apply. A No-Show occurs when a passenger either fails to use their booking or does not cancel the reservation at least 24 hours before the scheduled departure.
- Direct Flight Change for Bookings on Mobile App or Website:

If you made your booking via the Ethiopian Airlines mobile app or official website, you can directly process your flight change by clicking here.

- Non-Reversible Request:
Once you submit the request, it cannot be reversed. Make sure all details are correct before proceeding.
- SOTO Update:
For tickets purchased outside of Ethiopia that include an ADD stopover, if a change takes place after the outbound segment is used and the traveller wants to change their return date, fare differences, taxes, and penalties can be paid in ETB for those holding valid Ethiopian residency documents.
