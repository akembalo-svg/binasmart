# Fully Refundable booking

Travel plans can change, but your peace of mind shouldn’t. With Ethiopian’s Refundable Booking service, you no longer have to worry about losing your money when life takes an unexpected turn. For a small additional fee, you can protect your ticket and enjoy complete flexibility.
If you’re unable to travel due to unforeseen circumstances, you’ll be eligible for a 100% refund on your flight. It’s a simple, smart way to safeguard your travel plans and book with confidence. Fly stress-free and let us take care of the unexpected.

## HOW DOES IT WORK?

- Book your Ethiopian airlines flight online
- Select refundable ticket when you reach payment option
- Complete your booking and receive your purchase documents for Refund protection
- If you have already purchased the service and wants to apply for refund, click here to apply. https://form.refundable.me/
- Submit all the required documents or evidence to show you are unable to attend your flight for unforeseen situations.
- Refund shall be verified by our partner Protect international SA and be refunded in the average of 4hours if all the application requirements are met

Terms and ConditionsOpen the terms and conditions of your Refund Protection .

1. How do I apply for refund for my flight ticket?

If you are not able to attend your flight and want to apply for refund due to the conditions mentioned in the Terms & Condition, you can only apply online Here.

2. Am I entitled for full ticket refund?

You can get a full refund for your flight ticket if you have purchased a Refundable Booking service while booking on our website and the reason for your cancellation is covered by the service's Terms & Condition

3. Do I get refund even if my journey includes other airline other than Ethiopian airlines?

Yes, you will be eligible for full refund as long as you purchase the service on the Ethiopian Airlines website and meet the terms and conditions.

4. How can I follow up my refund application?

You will receive an email notification with your application status to the email address you provided. You can also request a reminder email if needed.

5. Can I apply for refund if I haven’t purchased this refundable booking?

If you have not purchased a Refundable Booking service, your refund will be subject to the regular ticket refund conditions. Therefore, you need to contact your nearest Ethiopian Airlines office or Global Customer Interaction Center

6. Am I entitled for refund of my additional service request I have purchased along with my ticket?

Yes, you are eligible for a refund of any additional services that you purchased along with your tickets.

7. How can I purchase the service?

You can purchase the service on our website or mobile application along with your ticket purchase at the payment page.

8. Can I add this service after I have completed my ticket purchase or reservation?

No, this service can only be purchased at the time of booking your ticket.

9. What are the available forms of payment to purchase this service?

This service can only be purchased online using credit/debit cards.
