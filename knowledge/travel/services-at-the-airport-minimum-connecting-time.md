## Swift Connections Guidelines

When passengers transfer between flights, Ethiopian Airlines requires a minimum amount of time between landing and re-boarding to guarantee a smooth connection and reliable delivery of checked baggage. This is known as the Minimum Connection Time (MCT). MCT is not a fixed, single number — it varies by airport, by the type of connection being made (domestic or international on each leg), and by whether the onward flight is operated by Ethiopian Airlines directly (an "online" connection) or handed over to a partner airline (an "offline" connection). The table below lists every airport in the Ethiopian Airlines network together with its published Minimum Connection Time, in hours and minutes.
Ethiopian Airlines' Minimum Connection Times range from as little as 0.20 (20 minutes) for a straightforward domestic-to-domestic connection up to 3.00 (3 hours) for connections that involve immigration, customs, or a change of terminal — such as Delhi and Mumbai. At the airline's Addis Ababa (ADD) hub itself, the MCT is 0.30 (30 minutes) for a domestic-to-domestic transfer and up to 1.00 (1 hour) for transfers between domestic and international flights.
How to Read the Table: Times are written as hours. Minutes, not decimal hours — so 1.30 means 1 hour 30 minutes (not 1.3 hours), and 2.15 means 2 hours 15 minutes. Always check the connection time shown on your e-ticket or itinerary against the figures here before you book a tight transfer.

WHAT CONNECTING PASSENGERS NEED TO KNOW1. What Is a Minimum Connection Time?Minimum Connection Time (MCT) is the shortest time required between flights for passengers and baggage to transfer between aircraft.
It includes immigration, security, and baggage transfer. MCT varies depending on whether the connection is between international flights (I/I)
or between domestic and international flights (D/I or I/D).

2. Connecting Through Addis Ababa (ADD)Addis Ababa Bole International Airport is Ethiopian Airlines’ home hub. Terminal 2 handles all international flights, while Terminal 1 serves domestic flights.
Transfers between terminals may require more time. International-to-domestic connections can take up to 1 hour, while international-to-international transfers
take about 0.35 hours.

3. Baggage on Connecting FlightsChecked baggage on Ethiopian Airlines is normally transferred automatically to the final destination if the connection meets the published MCT.
If baggage is not submitted on time, ground staff will provide a written slip so passengers can track it.

4. Long Layovers: Free Stopover & Skylight HotelPassengers with layovers of 8 to 24 hours in Addis Ababa on Ethiopian Airlines may qualify for a complimentary hotel stay, meals, and transfers
under the airline’s stopover program. Accommodation is usually at the Ethiopian Skylight Hotel or Skylight In-Terminal Hotel.
Vouchers are issued at check-in or upon arrival in Addis Ababa.

1.What is Ethiopian Airlines' Minimum Connection Time (MCT)?

MCT is the shortest time Ethiopian Airlines needs between an arriving and a departing flight to move a passenger and their baggage safely and on time. It varies by airport and by connection type, ranging from 0.20 (20 minutes) to 3.00 (3 hours) across the network.

2.What is the Minimum Connection Time at Addis Ababa (ADD)?

At Addis Ababa Bole International Airport, the MCT is 0.30 (30 minutes) for a domestic-to-domestic connection, 1.00 (1 hour) for domestic-to-international or international-to-domestic connections, and 0.35 (35 minutes) for offline international-to-international connections handled with a partner airline (0.45 minutes on Ethiopian's own online international-to-international connections).

3.What do D/D, D/I, I/D, and I/I mean in the table?

They describe the type of connection: D/D is domestic-to-domestic, D/I is domestic-to-international, I/D is international-to-domestic, and I/I is international-to-international. The required minimum time is usually longer whenever a connection crosses between domestic and international status, because it typically involves immigration, customs, or a terminal change.

4.What is the difference between "Offline" and "Online" in the table?

"Online" MCTs apply to destinations Ethiopian Airlines flies to directly on both legs of the journey. "Offline" MCTs apply when the connection involves another airline that Ethiopian Airlines partners or codeshares with at that airport.

5.Is my baggage automatically transferred when I have a connecting flight?

Yes. On an all-Ethiopian Airlines itinerary that meets the published MCT, checked baggage is transferred through to your final
destination. On interline journeys, Ethiopian Airlines receives baggage handed over by the other carrier; if it is not submitted in
time, staff will inform you with a written slip so you know it has not travelled with you.

6.What happens if my scheduled connection is shorter than the published MCT?

Ethiopian Airlines' reservation system will not normally construct or ticket a connection that falls below the published
minimum for that airport and connection type. If you believe your itinerary shows a shorter connection, contact Ethiopian
Airlines or your travel agent before travel to confirm it is valid.

7.How early should I arrive at the airport for a connecting flight?

If check-in is required, arrive at least 2 hours before your scheduled departure time. This also applies if you have completed online check-in but still need to drop off checked baggage.

8.What if my layover in Addis Ababa is longer than the connection window?

Passengers with a layover of 8 to 24 hours on an Ethiopian Airlines ticket qualify for a complimentary hotel stay, meals, and transfers under the airline's stopover programme, generally at the Ethiopian Skylight Hotel or the 97-room Skylight In Terminal Hotel inside Terminal 2. Vouchers are issued at check-in at your origin, or at the transit desk on arrival.

This list covers every airport currently in the Ethiopian Airlines network together with its published MCT. If a destination is missing or you need the figure confirmed for your specific booking, contact Ethiopian Airlines' Support and Feedback team or your travel agent before you fly.
