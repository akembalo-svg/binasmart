Important Notice to Passengers to and from America and Canada

Passport Validity: Please make sure your passport is valid for at least six months beyond your travel date.
Travel Documents: Ensure you have the necessary visa and transit documents for your destination and any connecting airports.
Flight Details: Double-check your arrival and departure dates, flight times, and plan to arrive at the airport well in advance to avoid delays.
Special Meals: If you require a special meal such as vegetarian, fasting, medical, Muslim, or baby food please request it when booking your flight or purchasing your ticket.

11.2 Passengers traveling into or out of the USA with an amount of money or monetary instruments more than USD10,000.00(CAD12,500.00-13,500.00) are required to declare the amount by filling out customs forms designed for this purpose.

### U.S Department of Transportation (DOT) &The Canadian Transportation Agency (CTA) Baggage rule

When the origin or destination of a ticket is a U.S./CA (United states of America or Canada) point, the baggage provisions of the airline selected at the beginning of the itinerary must apply throughout the journey, regardless of stopover.
In the case of codeshare flights that include a point in the U.S/CA (United states of America or Canada), the baggage provisions of the marketing carrier applies.
