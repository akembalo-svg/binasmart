## Refund Rules and Regulations

- Complete Information:
Make sure to provide full and accurate details when
completing the refund form to avoid delays.
- No-Show Fee:
If the refund is requested on or after the flight date, an
additional No-Show fee will apply. A No-Show occurs when a passenger fails to either use the
ticket or cancel the reservation at least 24 hours before the scheduled flight departure.
- Ticket Issued by Travel Agency:
For tickets purchased through a travel agency,
please contact the agency directly to request a refund.
- Ticket Purchased with Cash or Bank Deposit:
For tickets originally purchased
with cash or via bank deposit, please visit your nearest Ethiopian Airlines Ticket Office or
contact your travel agency for assistance.
- Service Interruptions:
If your flight is impacted by a schedule change or
cancellation, please complete the refund form for any additional services purchased, such as:

- Seat selection
- Upgrades
- Extra baggage
- Wi-Fi services
- Lounge access
- Priority boarding

- Cancellation of Entire Journey:
Please be aware that the booking will be
cancelled for the entire journey if a refund request is processed.
- Non-Reversible Request:
Once the refund request is submitted, it cannot be
reversed. Make sure all information is correct before completing the form.
- Refund Process for Extra Services:
In case of service disruptions, you are
eligible for a refund on extra services like seat selection or baggage, provided you fill out
the form.
- Important
Remember to check all details carefully before submitting the form,
as the request is final and cannot be undone.
