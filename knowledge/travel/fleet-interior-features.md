1. How can my company enrol in the Ethiopian Airlines Corporate Travel Program?

To enroll in the Corporate Travel Program, your company must have a yearly travel budget of USD 50,000 or more. Simply fill out the Request for Proposal (RFP) form, and it will be reviewed by our Global Corporate Sales Desk within two working days.

2. What are the benefits of joining the Ethiopian Airlines Corporate Travel Program?

Members enjoy exclusive upfront discounts, priority services, and flexible travel options, ensuring comfort and savings for your company's business travel needs. You'll also have access to our extensive route network connecting five continents with more destinations in Africa than any other airline.

3. Can I book corporate travel tickets online?

Yes, you can book your corporate tickets online through our website, via a designated travel agent, or at Ethiopian ticket offices. Once enrolled in the program, you'll have access to special corporate rates.

4. How long does it take to process a corporate travel request?

After submitting the RFP form, our Global Corporate Sales Desk will review and respond within two working days. Once both parties agree and sign the contract, the program will become active.

5. Is the Ethiopian Airlines Corporate Travel Program renewable?

Yes, the program is renewable after one year, based on the fulfillment of the agreed-upon travel expenditure. Both parties must agree on the terms for renewal before the contract ends.
