Members enjoy exclusive upfront discounts, priority services, and flexible travel options, ensuring comfort and savings for your company's business travel needs. Yes, you can book your corporate tickets online through our website, via a designated travel agent, or at Ethiopian ticket offices. Once enrolled in the program, you'll have access to special corporate rates.

After submitting the RFP form, our Global Corporate Sales Desk will review and respond within two working days. Once both parties agree and sign the contract, the program will become active.

Yes, the program is renewable after one year, based on the fulfillment of the agreed-upon travel expenditure. Both parties must agree on the terms for renewal before the contract ends.
