Fill Out a Claim Form: fill out a damaged baggage report. Provide all necessary details, including your flight number, baggage claim ticket, and a description of the damage.

"What is a PIR reference number, and why do I need it?"

A PIR reference number, or Property Irregularity Report reference number, is a unique identifier assigned to a report filed to manage lost or delayed baggage in the World Tracer system, which is used by airlines and airports to manage lost or delayed baggage. When a passenger reports a missing bag, a PIR is created to document the incident and track the recovery process.

You need a PIR reference number because:
Tracking: It allows both the airline and the passenger to track the status of the missing luggage. You can use it to check for updates on the bag's location.
Record Keeping: It serves as a formal record of the reported issue, ensuring that there is an official acknowledgment of your missing belongings.
Claims Processing: If you need to file a claim for compensation due to the delay or loss of your luggage, the PIR reference number is typically required to process the claim.
