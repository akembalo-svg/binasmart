# Addis Ababa Bole International Airport

Addis Ababa Bole International Airport (IATA: ADD, ICAO: HAAB) is the primary hub of Ethiopian Airlines and the main international gateway to Ethiopia, connecting travelers across Africa, Europe, Asia, and the Americas. The ultra-modern terminal was inaugurated on January 21, 2003, with a second phase of expansion completed and inaugurated on January 28, 2019.
Handling more than 22 million international and 2 million domestic passengers a year, Bole is one of the busiest airports in Africa. As Ethiopian Airlines' home base and a Star Alliance member hub, it plays a central role in connecting Africa with the rest of the world with 21st-century facilities designed to speed up every departure and arrival.

## Airport Terminal

The terminal has arrival and departure halls for international and domestic passengers, a dedicated VIP salon building, an airport security office, and desks for the airlines that fly to Addis Ababa. It also houses immigration offices, customs, self-check-in kiosks, baggage drop-off counters, banks, and duty-free and specialty shops selling jewelry, cultural clothing, and souvenirs alongside pharmacies, emergency clinics, hotel booths, telecom services, a tourist information center, international brand bars and restaurants, free Wi-Fi, and airport information desks.
Terminal II handles all international flights, while Terminal I (currently refurbished and expanded) serves all domestic flights. Passengers moving through Terminal II benefit from streamlined immigration and customs processing, extensive duty-free shopping, and a full range of dining options making layovers and connections in Addis Ababa comfortable, even during longer transit windows.

## Getting Around & Making the Most of Your Time at Bole

Getting To and From the Airport
Bole sits about 6 km from central Addis Ababa's Bole Road area, making it one of the more convenient capital-city hub airports on the continent to reach.

- Airport taxis (meter taxis) can be hired directly at the Arrivals Hall before exiting the terminal.
- Hotel Contact Booths in the Arrivals Hall let passengers book a room before leaving the terminal building.
- Tourist information, telecom services, duty-free, forex banks, and ATMs, plus free Wi-Fi, are all available in the Arrivals Hall before you exit.
- Car rental desks in the terminal let passengers arrange a self-drive or chauffeured vehicle on arrival.
- Connecting passengers transferring between Ethiopian Airlines flights can proceed straight through the terminal without leaving the secure area.

## Dining, Shopping & Duty-Free

The terminal offers a wide retail and dining mix designed to make layovers
and pre-flight time enjoyable.

- Duty-free shopping for fragrances, spirits, electronics, and travel essentials.
- Jewelry, cultural clothing, and souvenir shops showcasing Ethiopian craftsmanship.
- International brand bars and restaurants alongside local Ethiopian dining, including coffee bars serving Ethiopia's own world-renowned coffee.
- Pharmacies and convenience shops for last-minute travel needs.

## Transit Service & the Skylight In-Terminal Hotel

Passengers with a transit time between 8 and 24 hours are accommodated at the Ethiopian Skylight In-Terminal Hotel, regardless of whether an alternative flight with a shorter connection is available. Eligible passengers can collect their hotel voucher at their origin station at no additional cost.

- The hotel has 97 rooms across five categories, located past passport control in Terminal 2, a short walk from the departure gates.
- Rooms include complimentary Wi-Fi, a fully stocked minibar, an in-room safe, a flat-screen TV with premium satellite channels, a walk-in shower, bathrobe and slippers, and complimentary coffee/tea and bottled water.
- Guidance for making tight connections is available on the Swift Connections Guidelines page, and stopover options that extend a layover into a short visit to Addis Ababa are on the Addis Ababa Stopovers page.

## Lounges & Premium Passenger Services

Premium and connecting passengers can access Ethiopian Airlines' lounges in Terminal 2, housed in the terminal's dedicated VIP salon building.
- The Cloud 9 Lounge welcomes passengers travelling in Business Class out of Addis Ababa.
- The ShebaMiles Platinum Lounge is reserved for Ethiopian's top-tier ShebaMiles Platinum members.
- Both lounges are located near Gate A13, reachable from the departures floor after security (or after immigration for joining/transit passengers).
- Priority check-in and immigration handling, comfortable seating, refreshments, and workspace are available for eligible passengers.
