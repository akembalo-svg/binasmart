# Arrival Services &mdash; Step by Step

## Meet & Assist on Arrival

All arriving passengers on Ethiopian Airlines flights are met by Customer Relation Officers, who guide them

through to the Passport Control and Baggage Claim area, or to the transit exit for connecting passengers.

## Immigration & Passport Control

Nationals of countries that qualify for a visa on arrival should check with the Airport Immigration Office to
enter Ethiopia. All other travelers must arrive holding a valid entry visa and pass through the Passport Control
counters.

## Applying for an Ethiopian e-Visa

Passengers traveling to Ethiopia can obtain an e-visa by applying at https://www.evisa.gov.et/. Entry is
permitted for passengers who hold a confirmation email and the one-page e-visa issued after approval.

Passengers who arrive with incomplete e-visa documentation, or with proof of payment only (without the approved e-visa itself), will not be allowed to enter Ethiopia.

## Finding Your Baggage Belt

While waiting for passport clearance, passengers can check the wide-screen display in the arrivals area,

which indicates which belt to use to claim baggage from their flight.

## Duty Free, Banking & Mobile SIM

A small Duty Free Shop is available at the Baggage Claim Area. Banks and a booth for local mobile lines are
also available there for arriving passengers.

## 24/7 Customer Service Desk

Ethiopian Airlines operates a Customer Service Desk and Office right at the Baggage Claim Area, staffed 24
hours a day, 7 days a week. In case of missing or damaged baggage, Customer Service Agents are on hand to
assist you.

## Customs: Green & Red Channel

Ethiopian Customs operates Green and Red Channels for arriving passengers. However, be prepared to have
your baggage and hand baggage pass through the Baggage X-Ray machine before you exit the terminal.

## Good to Know Before You Land

Four quick answers that fill in the practical details of an Addis Ababa arrival.

## Getting to the City

Bole International Airport sits about 8 km (5 miles) from central Addis
Ababa, roughly a 15&ndash;30 minute drive depending on traffic. Options include
the Blue Line SkyBus from Terminal 2, official yellow-cream taxis, licensed
private taxis, local ride-hailing apps (Uber does not operate in Ethiopia),
and hotel shuttles arranged in advance.

## Delayed or Missing Baggage

If a checked bag doesn't arrive with you, report it immediately at the
Baggage Claim Customer Service Desk. Ethiopian Airlines aims to deliver
delayed bags within 24 hours and, for international itineraries, offers a
one-time incidental payment (USD 150 for Cloud Nine/Business, USD 100
for Economy) if the bag misses the next day's flight.

## Money, SIM & Wi-Fi

Forex bank counters and ATMs in the arrivals hall let you exchange
currency or withdraw Ethiopian Birr on landing. An Ethio Telecom counter
sells local SIM cards for arriving travelers, and complimentary Wi-Fi is
available throughout the terminal.

## Customs & Security

After baggage claim, choose the Green Channel if you have nothing to
declare or the Red Channel if you do. All baggage and hand luggage
passes through the X-ray machine before you exit keep your customs
declaration and passport handy.

1. What happens right after I land at Addis Ababa Bole International Airport

Ethiopian Airlines Customer Relation Officers meet arriving passengers and guide them to Passport Control and
Baggage Claim, or to the transit exit if you're connecting to another flight.

2. Do I need a visa to enter Ethiopia through Addis Ababa

It depends on your nationality. Travelers from countries eligible for visa on arrival can obtain one at the Airport
Immigration Office; everyone else must arrive with a valid entry visa, such as an approved Ethiopian e-Visa, and clear
the Passport Control counters.

3.How do I apply for an Ethiopian e-Visa before I travel?

Apply online at evisa.gov.et. Once approved, bring the confirmation email and the one-page e-visa document with
you &mdash; a payment receipt alone is not sufficient for entry.

4.How will I know which belt to collect my luggage from?

Check the wide-screen baggage display in the arrivals area while you're clearing passport control; it shows the
belt assigned to your flight.

5. What if my baggage is delayed, lost, or damaged

Go to the Ethiopian Airlines Customer Service Desk at the Baggage Claim Area, open 24/7. Agents will help track
your bag; for international flights, delayed-baggage incidental payments (USD 150 Cloud Nine / USD 100 Economy)
apply if it doesn't arrive on the next day's flight.

6. Can I exchange money or buy a local SIM card at the airport

Yes. The Baggage Claim Area has bank counters and ATMs for currency exchange, plus a booth for local mobile
lines, and there's free Wi-Fi throughout the terminal.

7. What are the customs rules when leaving the arrivals hall?

Choose the Green Channel (nothing to declare) or Red Channel (goods to declare). All baggage, including hand
luggage, must pass through the Baggage X-Ray machine before you exit.

8.How do I get from Bole International Airport to the city center?

The city center is about 8 km away, a 15&ndash;30 minute drive depending on traffic. Choose the Blue Line SkyBus, an
official taxi, a local ride-hailing app, or a pre-arranged hotel shuttle.

9. Who can I contact for help with arrival services

The Ethiopian Airlines Customer Service Desk at the Baggage Claim Area is staffed 24/7 and is the fastest way to
get help immediately after you land; the airline's global contact center can also assist before or after your trip.
