# Travel Insurance Service

Travel Smarter. Travel Protected
Unexpected events can happen before or during your journey. Ethiopian Airlines’ Travel Insurance helps protect you against common travel risks, giving you peace of mind from the moment you book until you return home.

Travel Insurance is brought to you in partnership with Awash Insurance and through their different global reinsurers. It is an optional add-on service that provides protection and assistance in case of unforeseen travel-related incidents subject to policy terms and conditions.

## HOW DOES IT WORK?

- Book your flight on Ethiopian airlines online
- Select Travel Insurance during the booking process
- Complete payment and receive confirmation
- Refer details of your purchase and policies on the email you will get
- Coverage is provided by Ethiopian airlines&rsquo; authorized insurance partner

What is Travel Insurance?

Travel Insurance is an optional add-on that provides coverage for certain unexpected events such as trip cancellation, medical emergencies, and travel disruptions, subject to the policy&rsquo;s terms and conditions.

How can I purchase Travel Insurance?

Travel Insurance can be purchased only during the online flight booking process on ethiopianairlines.com or the Ethiopian Airlines mobile app.

Can I add Travel Insurance after booking my ticket?

No. Travel Insurance must be selected and purchased at the time of online booking and cannot be added later.

Is Travel Insurance mandatory?

No. Travel Insurance is optional and offers additional peace of mind for your journey.

Who provides the Travel Insurance coverage?

The Travel Insurance is provided by Ethiopian Airlines&rsquo; authorized insurance partner and is governed by the insurer&rsquo;s policy terms and conditions.

What does the Travel Insurance cover?

Coverage may include benefits such as trip cancellation or interruption, medical emergencies, and other specified travel-related risks. Coverage details, limits, and exclusions apply.

Is Travel Insurance available for tickets purchased offline or through agents?

No. This service is available exclusively for tickets purchased through Ethiopian Airlines&rsquo; official online booking channels.

How will I receive my insurance confirmation?

Once purchased, your Travel Insurance confirmation will be issued along with your flight booking confirmation.

Whom should I contact for insurance-related claims or questions?

All claims and insurance-related inquiries should be directed to the insurance provider, as outlined in the policy document.
