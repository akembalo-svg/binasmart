Kiosk Check-in

Kiosk check-in is a quick and easy way to streamline your travel experience. Follow these five simple steps to complete your check-in process at the airport:

- Locate the Kiosk: Find the self-service terminal and select Ethiopian Airlines. Choose your preferred language (English or French).
- Find Your Booking: Access your booking using one of three options: by Destination, Flight Number, or Reservation Code.
- Confirm Baggage Safety: Confirm that you are not carrying any hazardous or dangerous materials in your checked-in or carry-on baggage. The check-in process can only proceed once this is verified.
- Enter Personal Details: Provide personal information, select or change your seat from the interactive seat plan, and add any missing details like your passport information or frequent flyer number.
- Get Your Boarding Pass: Once completed, the kiosk will print your boarding pass with a 2D barcode.

Baggage Check in

● Checked Baggage: Each passenger is allowed a free checked baggage allowance, which may vary depending on your class of travel and the origin-destination of your flight. Be sure to check the specific limits for your journey.
● Excess Baggage: If your luggage exceeds the allowed weight or piece limit, you can carry additional baggage by paying the applicable charges.
●Cabin/Hand Baggage: For Business Class passengers on all Ethiopian routes, you're allowed two pieces of hand baggage, with a maximum weight of 7 kgs each and dimensions not exceeding 23x40x55cm (or 9x16x22 inches).
For more detailed information on baggage allowances or restrictions, please contact your nearest Ethiopian Airlines office.

Check-in/Reporting time

1. When do Ethiopian Airlines check-in counters open and close?

Ethiopian Airlines check-in counters open 3 hours before departure and close 1 hour prior to flight time. However, for some airports like London Heathrow (LHR) and Manchester (MAN), check-in closes 75 minutes before departure, while at Paris Charles de Gaulle (CDG), it closes 90 minutes before departure.

2. Can I check in online and go directly to the gate?

Yes, if you have completed online check-in and have no checked baggage, you can proceed directly to the gate. At Paris Charles de Gaulle (CDG), you must arrive at the gate at least 30 minutes before departure.

3. Is there a dedicated check-in counter for premium passengers?

Yes, Cloud Nine passengers and frequent fliers with Platinum/Gold ShebaMiles and Star Alliance membership have access to dedicated check-in counters and can enjoy the Cloud Nine Lounge after check-in.

4. How early should I arrive at the airport for check-in?

● International flights: Arrive 2 hours before departure.
● Addis Ababa departures: Arrive 3 hours before departure.
● Flights to/from the USA: Arrive 4 hours before departure.
● Domestic flights: Arrive 1 hour before departure.

5. Can I use a self-service kiosk for check-in?

Yes, Ethiopian Airlines offers kiosk check-in at select airports. You can check in, choose your seat, and print your boarding pass in five simple steps.

6. How do I use the self-service kiosk for check-in?

● Find an Ethiopian Airlines kiosk and select your language.
● Retrieve your booking using Destination, Flight Number, or Reservation Code.
● Confirm you are not carrying hazardous materials.
● Add personal details, select your seat, and update passport or frequent flyer information.
● Print your boarding pass with a 2D barcode.

7. What is the baggage allowance for Ethiopian Airlines?

The free checked baggage allowance depends on your travel class and route. Business Class passengers can carry two pieces of hand baggage, each up to 7kg, with maximum dimensions of 20x40x55cm (8x16x22 inches).

8. What happens if my baggage exceeds the allowed limit?

If your checked baggage exceeds the allowed weight or piece limit, you will be required to pay excess baggage fees.

9. How much hand baggage am I allowed to bring?

● Business Class passengers: 2 pieces of hand baggage, max 7kg each.
● Economy Class passengers: Check the Ethiopian Airlines baggage policy for specific allowances.

10. When does check-in start for night flights from Addis Ababa to the USA, Canada, and Europe?

For night flights departing from Addis Ababa to these destinations, check-in begins at 13:00 local time.

11. What should web-checked-in passengers do at the airport?

Web-checked-in passengers must arrive at the boarding gate at least 30 minutes before departure. If they have baggage to drop off, they should go to the baggage drop-off counter at least 45 minutes before departure.

12. Are local passengers in Addis Ababa allowed to leave the airport after check-in?

No, for security reasons, local passengers who have checked in at Addis Ababa are not allowed to leave the airport to meet relatives or friends. They must proceed directly through immigration.

13. What happens if I arrive late for check-in?

Ethiopian Airlines reserves the right not to accept passengers who arrive later than the required check-in time. Make sure to arrive at the airport early to avoid any issues.

14. Can I check in at the counter if I need assistance?

Yes, Ethiopian Airlines staff are available at check-in counters worldwide to assist with baggage, special requests, and check-in procedures.

15. How do I get more information on baggage policies?

For detailed baggage allowance and restrictions, contact the nearest Ethiopian Airlines office or visit the official Ethiopian Airlines website.
