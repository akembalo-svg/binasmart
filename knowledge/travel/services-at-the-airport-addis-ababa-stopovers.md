# Addis Ababa Stopovers

Ethiopian Airlines' Addis Ababa stopover program turns a long layover into a genuine introduction to Ethiopia's capital instead of hours spent
waiting at the gate. On qualifying 8–24 hour connections, eligible passengers enjoy a complimentary hotel stay, convenient transfers, and the
option of a curated city tour — so you can experience Addis Ababa's culture, history, and energy while your journey with Ethiopian Airlines
continues smoothly to your final destination.

A stopover with us is more than a wait between flights — it's a short, easy way to discover Africa. Want to turn
a few extra hours into a full day of sightseeing? Browse bookable Ethiopian Holidays stopover tour packages and add one to your itinerary.

## Transit Service

- Passengers with a transit time of 8 to 24 hours are accommodated at the hotel, regardless of whether an alternative flight with a
shorter connection is available.

- Eligible passengers can collect their hotel voucher at their origin station, at no additional cost

## Skylight In-Terminal Hotel Service

Ethiopian Skylight In-Terminal Hotel has 97 tranquil rooms across five categories, each featuring modern essentials for a seamless stay. Pick a Skylight Suite for extra space with living or dining area, catch up on work or connect with complimentary Wi-Fi, and raid the fully stocked minibar.
All rooms feature a rollaway bed/cot, crib, iron and ironing board, in-room safe, bathroom amenities, hair dryer, separate walk-in shower, flat-screen TV, minibar, premium satellite TV channels, bathrobe, slippers, phone in bathroom, and complimentary coffee/tea supplies and bottled water.

## Facilities at the Arrivals Hall

Facilities and services available in the arrival hall include:

- A number of hotels have Hotel Contact Booths for passengers who want to book rooms
before exiting the terminal building.
- Airport taxis (meter taxis) are also available and can be hired at the Arrivals Hall before
exiting the terminal.
- Tourist information center, telecom services, duty-free, forex banks, ATM, and free
Wi-Fi services are available before exiting the terminal building

Planning Your Stopover

## Who Qualifies for the Free Stopover Package

The free hotel stay applies when Ethiopian Airlines operates both your inbound and outbound flights through Addis Ababa on a single ticket, with a total connection time of 8 to 24 hours.
No separate booking is needed &mdash; the hotel voucher is issued automatically for eligible itineraries.
Collect your voucher at check-in at your origin station; if it isn't issued there, ask at the transit desk in Addis Ababa on arrival.
The benefit typically covers one night's hotel stay, meals, and transfers between the airport and the hotel.

## Do You Need a Visa to Leave the Airport?

If you remain in the international transit area, no visa is required, regardless of your layover
length. If you want to leave the terminal to visit the city, entry rules apply:

- Passengers on the Ethiopian Airlines 8&ndash;24 hour stopover program are generally assisted
with a transit visa as part of the free package, so a separate application usually isn't
needed.
- Travelers leaving the airport outside that program typically need an Ethiopian eVisa
(applied for in advance at the official government portal) or a visa-on-arrival, depending on
nationality.
- Ethiopia's Council of Ministers approved a broader "stopover tourism" policy in April 2026
that would let Ethiopian Airlines passengers exit visa-free for 24 hours up to 7 days. As of
August 2026 it is still pending parliamentary ratification and is not yet in force

## Curated City Tours During Your Stopover

Beyond the complimentary hotel stay, stopover passengers can add a curated city tour to see
more of Addis Ababa in the time they have. Tours are arranged through Ethiopian Holidays,
Ethiopian Airlines' official travel partner, and can be booked online in advance or alongside
your stopover.

- Half-day, full-day, and multi-day guided tours cover Ethiopian culture, history, and the
country's famous coffee ceremony.
- Packages are built specifically for transit passengers, with pickup and drop-off timed to
your layover window.
- Airport pickup and drop-off are coordinated so you're back in plenty of time for your
onward flight.

## Make the Most of a Short Stopover

Even a few hours in Addis Ababa can be worthwhile. Popular stops for stopover passengers
include:

- A traditional Ethiopian coffee ceremony &mdash; coffee is believed to have originated in Ethiopia.
Book it as the Coffee Journey package.
- The National Museum of Ethiopia, home to the famous "Lucy" hominid fossil &mdash; covered by
the National Museum of Ethiopia Tour.
- Entoto Hill, for panoramic views over the city &mdash; see the Entoto Park & Cultural Shopping
Tour.
- The Merkato, one of Africa's largest open-air markets &mdash; join the Merkato Open Market Tour.
- The "I ❤ Ethiopia" landmark and surrounding gardens, a popular spot for photos near the city
center included on the Friendship Square Attractions tour.

What is the Ethiopian Airlines Addis Ababa stopover program?

It's a program that gives eligible transit passengers a complimentary hotel stay, meals, and transfers in Addis Ababa during a qualifying layover, plus the
option to add a paid guided city tour booked through Ethiopian Holidays.

How long does my layover need to be to qualify for the free hotel?

Between 8 and 24 hours, on a single ticket with Ethiopian Airlines operating both your inbound and outbound flights through Addis Ababa.

Do I need to book the stopover separately?

No. Eligible passengers are accommodated automatically; the hotel voucher is normally issued at check-in at your origin station, or at the transit desk
in Addis Ababa if not already issued.

Which hotel will I stay at?

Stopover passengers are typically accommodated at the Ethiopian Skylight In-Terminal Hotel or an associated Ethiopian Skylight Hotel property,
which has 97 rooms across five categories.

Do I need a visa to leave the airport during my stopover?

If you're on the 8&ndash;24 hour stopover program, Ethiopian Airlines generally assists with a transit visa as part of the package. Outside that program, a
standard Ethiopian eVisa or visa-on-arrival is normally required to leave the terminal, depending on your nationality.

Is Ethiopia introducing a visa-free stopover policy?

A visa-free stopover policy (24 hours to 7 days) was approved by Ethiopia's Council of Ministers in April 2026, but as of August 2026 it is still pending
parliamentary ratification and is not yet in force. Confirm current rules before you travel.

How do I book a guided tour for my stopover?

Guided tours are sold by Ethiopian Holidays, Ethiopian Airlines' travel partner. Browse and book Stopover Addis Tour packages directly on
ethiopianholidays.com, either before you fly or during your layover.

What can I do during a short stopover in Addis Ababa?

Popular options include a traditional coffee ceremony, the National Museum of Ethiopia, Entoto Hill's viewpoints, the Merkato market, and the "I ❤
Ethiopia" landmark each available as a bookable half-day or full-day package on Ethiopian Holidays.

What if my layover is shorter than 8 hours or longer than 24 hours?

You won't qualify for the free stopover hotel package. Shorter layovers are best spent in the transit area (no visa required); longer stays follow
standard visa and entry requirements, and you can still book an Ethiopian Holidays package around your own hotel arrangements.

Is airport transfer included with the free stopover hotel?

Yes, the complimentary package for eligible passengers includes transfers between the airport and the hotel.
