# Credit/Debit Card Restrictions

Booking a flight with Ethiopian Airlines? Here&rsquo;s everything you need to know about accepted payment methods, including credit card rules, third-party payments, and how to verify your card if needed. All details below apply to both online and in-person purchases.

Ethiopian Airlines accepts the following credit and debit cards for online and in-person bookings

● Visa

● MasterCard

● American Express

● Diners Club

● China UnionPay

● JCB

● UATP

Other accepted payment options include:
● Local debit cards and mobile payments from select African countries

● Elo and Hiper cards issued in Brazil

Note: Anonymous prepaid cards are not accepted.

### Third-Party Payments and Card Verification

You can book a flight for someone else using your credit or debit card. However, for security reasons:
● The cardholder may be contacted by email for verification.

● In some cases, the cardholder will need to verify the card in person at the airport or at the nearest Ethiopian Airlines ticket office.

If you are in a country without a local ticket office or sales agent, you can still authorize a third-party payment:

- Download and complete the Credit Card Authorization Form.

- Send the completed form to the first Ethiopian Airlines operating station.
