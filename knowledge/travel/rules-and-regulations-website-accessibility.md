# Website Accessibility Policy

At Ethiopian Airlines, we believe that everyone deserves equal access to our services. Our website is designed to be inclusive and user-friendly for people of all abilities. This page outlines our dedication to accessibility standards and how we support users who may need assistance navigating ethiopianairlines.com.

Ethiopianairlines.com accessibility policy

Ethiopian is dedicated to ensuring www.ethiopianairlines.com is accessible to all users, regardless of ability. We adhere to the WCAG 2.0 Level AA standards for our website.

For accessibility feedback or concerns, please contact us via Support and Feedback.

Individuals with disabilities who cannot access the website or prefer phone contact may reach us at:

● Global Call Center (US/Canada): 1-800-445-2733
● Washington DC: 1-703-682-0570
● New York: 1-646-476-9391
● Toronto: 1-800-445-2733

For other locations, please refer to our Call Center Numbers.

More information on WCAG 2.0 Level AA is available at https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG20/ to assist your browsing experience.
