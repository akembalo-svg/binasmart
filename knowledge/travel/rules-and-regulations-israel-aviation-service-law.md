Notification to passengers traveling to/from Israel

Ethiopian Airlines is required to comply with the Law on the Powers of Collection and Analysis of Data Regarding Passengers Entering or Leaving Israel (hereby &ndash; The Law). Under this regulation, we are obligated to submit passenger data collected during the ticket reservation process to the National Centre for Passenger Data Analysis (Hereby - the Centre).
This data may be used by the relevant Israeli authorities, as specified in the Law, for the following purposes:

- Combating terrorism

- Securing air travel

- Combating serious crime

- Enhancing border control

- Protecting public health

All submitted data will be retained in accordance with the timeframes established by the Law.
For more information, including data protection measures and the full text of the Law, please refer to this ASL summary.
