# Ethiopian Airlines MICE

Ethiopian Airlines Conventions is a service that helps make organizing and attending international conferences easier. As one of the continent's leading carriers, Ethiopian currently serves more than 145 global destinations across five continents, as well as over 20 domestic routes, connecting Africa with Europe, Asia, the Middle East, and the Americas.

Ethiopian Airlines Conventions privileges are offered based on the number of participants, destination, event time / season and the content of your conference, congress, seminar, symposium, panel, open session, forum, fair and festival organizations. Ethiopia meets the needs of your meeting, incentive, conference or event (MICE) activities

Events at Ethiopian Skylight hotel

### Requirements for sponsorship

- Event should be International & attract international participants
- Event should have at least 50 and more international participants
- The venue can be on Ethiopian Airlines online city or beyond
- The event must be covered by one of the categories of conference, congress,
seminar, symposium, panel, open session, forum, fair and festival

### Advantages for organizers

By using Ethiopian Airlines Conventions product, organizers will enjoy the following
benefits:

- One point of contact for all event travel: manage and plan your travel through
one central contact
- A global conference discount: for your delegates, no matter where they are
traveling from on Ethiopian Airlines network
- Convenient 'Online hyper link' for air ticket discount
- Extra Incentive: extra free tickets depending upon the event evaluation process

- Announcement of the Event on all ET's Social media and website

### Advantage to the delegates

As Ethiopian Airlines Conventions delegate, you can access the flight schedules of
127 International and 22 domestic flights across the world in one place, researching
your options is fast and easy.

- Attractive discount for Business and Economy Class tickets;
- Flight period covering seven days before and after international events;
- Book your flight any time before your event date
- Purchase of discounted online tickets with an exclusive promotional code.
- Sheba Miles program to earn miles

1. What is Ethiopian Airlines Conventions?

Ethiopian Airlines Conventions is a specialized travel program designed to offer discounted flights and exclusive benefits for international conferences, congresses, seminars, and other large-scale events.

2. Who can apply for Ethiopian Airlines Conventions?

Event organizers hosting international conferences with at least 50 participants can apply. The event must fall under categories such as congresses, fairs, forums, or symposiums.

3. What benefits do event organizers receive?

Organizers enjoy a single point of contact for all travel needs, discounted group fares for attendees, free incentive tickets based on evaluation, and promotional support on Ethiopian Airlines' digital platforms.

4.How can delegates book discounted flights?

Attendees can book flights using a unique promotional code provided for the event, allowing them to access exclusive discounts on Business and Economy Class tickets.

5. How far in advance should I apply for the program?

It&rsquo;s recommended to apply at least a few months before the event to ensure smooth planning and approval. Ethiopian Airlines will review the request and provide confirmation.
