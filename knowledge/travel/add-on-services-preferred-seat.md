# Upgrade Your Economy Class Experience

Make your trip more comfortable with Ethiopian Airlines Preferred Seat selection. Choose your seat in advance and travel with the peace of mind that your spot is secured before boarding. Whether you prefer extra legroom, sitting by the aisle, or a window view, you can reserve it when booking your flight or managing your ticket.
Selecting a preferred seat is simple and available for most fare types. Add it to your booking online, during check-in, or through our mobile app, and enjoy a flight experience tailored to your comfort.

## Extra leg room

These seats are available in both Premium Economy and Economy Class.

## Preferred Zone Seat

As a rule, the preferred seating area is located in the front part of Economy Class, directly behind Business or Premium Economy Class. These seats enable you to leave the aircraft quickly after landing, making your journey even easier and more flexible.

Do I have to pay to choose my preferred seat on Ethiopian?

If you're travelling in Business Class (cloud 9) or economy class and choose standard seat you don&rsquo;t have to pay for seat selection. But if your selection is a preferred seat such as; Premium seat at front row and bulkhead seats or exit row with extra legroom, you will pay a little extra. You can view the cost of seat through Manage a Booking. Anyone who chooses exit row seat must meet the criteria for Exit row seat assignment criteria.

Are preferred seats selection charges refundable?

Preferred seats are non-refundable except for involuntary changes occurred due to operational reasons.
Customers are not eligible for seat selection refunds when;

- Voluntary or involuntary upgrading to c-9
- Selecting a lower value seat after purchasing a higher value seat
- And when other voluntary changes like Itinerary and routing changes
Please note that the refunds will include the total amount paid for preferred seat selection minus the non-refundable taxes, if any applicable
In case of involuntary changes to your seat due to operational, safety or security reasons, you are eligible for a refund only in the following cases:

- Premium seat re-seated on a Regular or Exit Row with extra legroom
- Exit Row with extra legroom re-seated on a Regular or Preferred seat that&rsquo;s not the exit row nor premium seat.
Refund requests can only be submitted after flight departs

I have purchased my seat in advance; however, I am cancelling my trip. Will I get a refund value for the paid seats?

Preferred seat value is non-refundable in such cases.
