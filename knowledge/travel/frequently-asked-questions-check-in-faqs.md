Can I check in for a flight on a mobile device?

Yes, Mobile check-in for Ethiopian Airlines is available and allows passengers to check in for their flights using a mobile device, typically through the Ethiopian Airlines mobile app or website. Here&rsquo;s how the process generally works:

Download the Mobile App: If you haven't already, download the Ethiopian Airlines mobile app from your device&rsquo;s app store.
Check-In Option: Look for the check-in option, which is usually prominently displayed on the homepage.
Enter Details: Enter the required information, such as your booking reference or ticket number, and your last name.
Select Your Flight: Choose the flight you wish to check in for, and follow the prompts to complete your check-in.
Choose Seat and Baggage: If available, you can select your seat and add any checked baggage during the process.
Boarding Pass: Once check-in is complete, you can either receive a digital boarding pass on your mobile device or choose to print it later.

What is the mobile boarding pass (QR code)?

A mobile boarding pass is a digital version of a traditional boarding pass, typically accessed via a smartphone app or digital wallet. It allows to check in for flight and receive boarding pass electronically, rather than printing it out on paper.
