## Take the Train to Your Plane

Ethiopian Airlines, in partnership with Deutsche Bahn, offers seamless train-to-flight connectivity, allowing passengers to travel from almost any DB station in Germany to Frankfurt Airport with ease.

### Important Information About Rail&Fly

- Rail&Fly tickets cover train travel to/from Frankfurt Airport from any Deutsche Bahn service in Germany, with a surcharge automatically added to your booking.
- Your train segment will be included in your flight itinerary. However, journey times listed are fictitious&mdash;you must select a specific train connection during check-in.
- Rail&Fly only applies to DB trains and is not available for multi-stop bookings.
- The ticket must be booked alongside your flight&mdash;it cannot be added online later.
- Business Class passengers enjoy 1st-class train travel, while Economy Class passengers travel in 2nd class.
- If you rebook your flight, you must check in again for your new train segment at www.rail-checkin.com.
- Seat reservations: Free for Ethiopian Airlines flights. 1st-class train tickets include a free seat reservation, while 2nd-class passengers can book one separately at www.bahn.com, the &ldquo;DB Navigator&rdquo; app, or any DB travel center.

1. Check-in on www.rail-checkin.com

To ensure a smooth journey, passengers must check in at www.rail-checkin.com before departure to retrieve their Deutsche Bahn (DB) train ticket. We recommend checking in as early as possible to secure your preferred train connection.

### 6 Simple Steps to Get Your Train Ticket

- Visit www.rail-checkin.com
- Enter Passenger Details &ndash; Provide your name and either your flight e-ticket number or record locator
- Select Your Trip &ndash; Choose your outbound/inbound journey and add up to four additional passengers (if they are on the same route and in the same flight booking)
- Choose Your Train &ndash; Select a preferred train connection
- Confirm Your Booking &ndash; Review and confirm your train details
- Get Your Ticket &ndash; Download your train ticket or receive it via email

1. What is Ethiopian Airlines' Rail&Fly service?

Rail&Fly is a service offered in partnership with Deutsche Bahn, allowing Ethiopian Airlines passengers to travel seamlessly by train from any DB station in Germany to Frankfurt Airport.

2. How do I book a Rail&Fly ticket?

Rail&Fly must be booked at the same time as your Ethiopian Airlines flight. It cannot be added separately online after booking.

3. Is the Rail&Fly train ticket included in my flight itinerary?

Yes, the train segment will appear in your flight itinerary. However, journey times listed are fictitious&mdash;you must select an actual train during check-in.

4. How do I check in for my Rail&Fly ticket?

Visit www.rail-checkin.com, enter your details, select a train, confirm your booking, and download your train ticket or receive it via email.

5. When should I check in for my Rail&Fly ticket?

It is recommended to check in as early as possible before your departure to secure your preferred train connection.

6. Can I use Rail&Fly for a multi-stop trip?

No, Rail&Fly is only valid for direct travel to/from Frankfurt Airport and does not apply to multi-stop bookings.

7. Which class do I travel in with Rail&Fly?

- Business Class passengers travel in Deutsche Bahn's 1st class compartment.
- Economy Class passengers travel in 2nd class.

8. Do I need to reserve a seat on Deutsche Bahn?

- 1st class passengers: Seat reservations are included for free.
- 2nd class passengers: You can purchase a seat reservation at www.bahn.com, via the &ldquo;DB Navigator&rdquo; app, or at any DB travel center.

9. Can I rebook my Rail&Fly ticket?

If you rebook your flight after collecting your train ticket, you must check in again for your new train segment at www.rail-checkin.com.

10. Why does my itinerary show incorrect train details?

Due to system limitations, your itinerary may display fictitious train numbers (e.g., W2), departure points (QYG), and times. You must complete check-in to select an actual train connection.

11. Can I travel on any Deutsche Bahn train?

Yes, but only on DB-operated trains. The Rail&Fly ticket is not valid on other train providers.

12. When can I select my train connection?

- For departure: You can choose a train on your departure day or the day before.
-
- For arrival: You can select a train on your arrival day or the day after.

13. How early should I arrive at the airport with Rail&Fly?

Make sure your train arrives at Frankfurt Airport at least 2 hours before Ethiopian Airlines&rsquo; check-in counters close to allow sufficient time for flight check-in.

14. What happens if my train is delayed?

Train delays may occur, and passengers are responsible for reaching the airport on time. Ethiopian Airlines is not liable for missed flights due to train delays.

15. Who can I contact for assistance with Rail&Fly?

If you have not received your train ticket or need help, contact Ethiopian Airlines' Germany reservations team at +49 69 770 673-052 or email onlinebooking@ethiopianairlines.com.
