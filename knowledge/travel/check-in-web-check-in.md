# Online Check-in

Enjoy a smooth and efficient travel experience with Ethiopian Airlines' Online Check-In. Our easy-to-use platform lets you check in, choose your seat, and save your boarding pass ahead of time. Online check-in is available 48 to 2 hours before your flight, allowing you to skip the long airport lines and begin your journey hassle-free.

## Web Check-in

Simplify your journey with Ethiopian Airlines Web Check-In. This easy-to-use platform allows you to check in for your flight, select your preferred seat, and download your boarding pass, all from the comfort of your home.

## Mobile App Check-In

For added convenience, use the Ethiopian Airlines Mobile App to check in on the go. With a simple and intuitive interface, our app puts flexibility and control in your hands, enhancing your travel experience at every step.

## Self Bag Dropoff

Skip the long lines with our Self-Bag Drop Off service. This fast and easy process allows you to check in your luggage in just a minute. Simply tag and drop off your bags, and you're all set to head to your gate quickly.

## Flyaway Kids' Beds for Ages 2-7

For young children aged 2-7, passenger may come with flyaway kids bed for their children comfort while flying long distance. The flyaway kids bed shall be used only at the window seat, so that it doesn’t block the egress of others and only at cruise level or after takeoff.
Please note, these cannot be used during takeoff, landing, or whenever the seatbelt sign is on.
