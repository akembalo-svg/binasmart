# Ethiopian Customs Regulation

- Ethiopian Customs regulation dictates that passengers continuing their journey further to Ethiopian domestic destinations via Addis Ababa are required to go through customs and clear their baggage at Addis Ababa. Baggage can be through checked-in up to final destination but passengers are required to collect their bags at the transit point and clear customs before dropping their baggage at domestic check-in counters.

- Any passenger leaving Ethiopia is only permitted to take up to 2 kilograms of coffee products, unless the Ethiopian Coffee & Tea Authority issues a special authorization.

To ensure the safety of passengers, crew, and aircraft, Ethiopian Airlines implements important safety rules regarding carrying lithium-powered devices.
While some lithium-powered devices are completely prohibited, some are accepted under a specific safety protocol. These devices are permitted in checked and carry-on baggage with the approval Of Ethiopian Airlines as follows:

- The use of power banks onboard any Ethiopian Airlines flight is strictly prohibited. Power banks and any electronic devise with lithium-ion batteries must be kept under the seat, not in overhead bins and must not be used in any way while onboard.
- Lithium battery powered vehicles/motorized vehicles (which are not mobility aids) and commonly referred as air-wheel, solo-wheel, electric skateboards, hover-board, mini-segway balance wheels both as carry-on or checked-in baggage are prohibited, whether with or without lithium battery as the materials pose risk to flight safety. These items will be offloaded if they are discovered inside the cabin.
- Lithium battery powered-powered lighters without a safety cap or means of protection against unintentional activation fall under forbidden goods for carriage.
- Defibrillators (AED), portable oxygen concentrators (POC) and continuous positive airway pressure (CPAP), containing lithium metal or lithium-ion cells or batteries may be carried by passengers for medical use as follows: Portable medical electronic devices (PMED), such as automated external.
a. For lithium metal or lithium alloy batteries, a lithium content exceeding 2 g, but not exceeding 8 g; or
b. For lithium-ion batteries, a watt-hour rating exceeding 100 Wh, but not exceeding 160 Wh;
c. Batteries shall be of a type that meets the requirements Of the UN Manual of Tests and Criteria, Part Ill, subsection 38.3.
- Portable electronic devices, such as power tools, video cameras and laptops containing lithium-ion batteries as follows:
d. Lithium-ion batteries with a watt-hour rating exceeding 100 Wh, but not exceeding 160 Wh;
e. Batteries shall be of a type that meets the requirements Of the UN Manual of Tests and Criteria, part 111, subsection 38.3.
Remember the 7 simple steps:

- Pack light: Only bring the devices and batteries you really need.
- Stay alert: If a device is hot, smoking, or damaged, tell the crew (or airport staff) immediately.
- Keep devices with you: Always carry phones, laptops, cameras, vapes (if allowed) and other battery-powered items in your hand baggage, not in checked baggage.
- Protect loose batteries: Keep spare batteries and power banks in their original packaging or cover the terminals with tape to prevent short-circuits.
- Gate check reminder: If your hand baggage is taken at the gate to go in the aircraft baggage hold, remove all lithium batteries and devices first.
- Check battery size: For larger batteries (over 100 watt-hours, such as those used in larger cameras, drones, or power tools), check with your airline as approval may be required.
- Check airline rules: Always confirm your airline&rsquo;s policies, as requirements may differ in compliance with local regulations.

Travelling to the European Union for non-EU nationals
The Entry/Exit System (EES) will serve as a digital database of the arrival and departure dates of non-EU nationals travelling to the European Union for a short stay (that is for up to 90 days within a period of 180 days). It will also keep records of the travellers&rsquo; personal data including photo of the face and fingerprints.
Travellers&rsquo; data will be collected and stored in full compliance with EU data protection rules and rights.
