- In the USA, the importation of Bushmeat is generally prohibited under the Animal Health Protection Act and various regulations enforced by the U.S. Department of Agriculture (USDA) and the Animal and Plant Health Inspection Service (APHIS).Read More
- Key Points:
1. Definition: Bushmeat refers to the meat of wild animals hunted in tropical forests, often including species that may be endangered or protected.
2. Prohibition: Importing bushmeat is illegal due to concerns over wildlife conservation, public health, and the potential spread of zoonotic diseases (diseases that can be transmitted from animals to humans).
3. Enforcement: U.S. Customs and Border Protection (CBP) actively inspects luggage and cargo for illegal wildlife products, including bushmeat.
4. Penalties: Violations can result in confiscation of the goods, fines, and potential criminal charges.
5. Public Awareness: Travelers are encouraged to declare any wildlife products and to understand the laws surrounding the importation of animal products.
