# What is MySheba Neighbour Free Seat?

Get the most comfortable seat in Economy: MyShebaSpace. You can now guarantee that no one sits next to you on your flight. Reserve one or more extra seats beside you now, and look forward to your journey.

● More comfort

● Extra personal space

● Room to stretch out, lay-down, or anything in between

## How it works:

1. Check if your flight is eligible. After booking, you can place a bid on up to two adjacent empty seats, with notification between 72 and 2 hours before departure, or secure them immediately via instant purchase starting 72 hours prior to takeoff. 2. Select your seat and the seat(s) you’d like to block 3. Enter your payment information 4. Confirmation of your reserved seats will be sent via email Please be prepared to show this email at the time of travel. 5. Enjoy the extra space and comfort!
