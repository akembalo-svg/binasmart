# What’s the Experience Like at Our CIP Lounge

Step into a world of comfort, privacy, and personalized service designed exclusively for CIP passengers. Your journey begins at the CIP Terminal, where agents assist with check-in, baggage handling, and customs procedures in a calm and private environment away from the crowds of the main terminal. After completing these procedures, continue to the exclusive lounge within the terminal, where you can relax in a comfortable setting with a quiet work area, relaxation zone, and private meeting space. Enjoy a premium atmosphere with elegant design, freshly prepared meals, and refreshments suited to your preferences while staying informed with flight status updates before departure. When it is time to board, benefit from a seamless transfer from the lounge to your aircraft, ensuring a smooth and stress-free journey.

I am transiting through Addis Ababa; can I purchase a CIP lounge pass at the airport?

No. The CIP Lounge service is available only for passengers departing from Addis Ababa (ADD). Transit passengers are not eligible to purchase the CIP Lounge pass.

What is included with a CIP lounge service? Are there any extra fees at the lounge?

The services included in CIPM lounges all complementary

- Complementary meal, drink and Ethiopian Coffee service
- Free Wi-Fi, TV for news and entertainment
- Peaceful and comfortable area including couch to relax, meeting room
- Assist with baggage handling on departure
- Assistance with immigration and customs process
- Escort service from/to CIP lounge/aircraft with dedicated transport
- Assist with baggage handling on arrival

Where can I book/purchase my CIP lounge service?

You can purchase your CIP lounge service online; through our website & mobile app, from any of our offices at local ticket offices and Addis Ababa International Airport.

What form of payment is accepted to purchase a CIP lounge service?

All forms of payment currently applicable for ticket sales is also applicable for lounge pass purchase. These options include credit/Debit card, through bank transfer, bank deposit and cash.

I’m part of a loyalty programme. Am I exempted from paying CIP lounge service?

No. Loyalty program membership does not include free CIP Lounge access. Payment is required to use the service.

Can I request access for CIP lounges all over the world?

No, this service is currently available for Addis Ababa Bole international airport. CIP Lounge located at Addis Ababa bole international airport

Can I make a change to my CIP lounge service booking, if I want to change my travel date?

Yes, you can make a change to your lounge service along with your flight booking within a week subject to availability of the service on the changed date.

What happens if I need to cancel my lounge service booking?

If you cancel your lounge service voluntarily, the fee will be non-refundable. Refund is only applicable for involuntary changes due to operational reason such as.

- Flight cancelations/rerouting
- Denied service due to over sale
- Schedule change
You can request refund you can contact our global contact Centre by emailing at reservation@ethiopianairlines.com Or by calling on our Global Contact Centre Numbers.

Where should I go to get my CIP lounge access receipt re-issued?

Visit the nearest Ethiopian Airlines sales office/Ticket offices or contact us through our Global Contact Centre Numbers.
