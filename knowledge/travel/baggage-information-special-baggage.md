Sports Equipments

- The following procedure shall apply for special baggage charge (sporting equipment).

Special baggage (Sporting Equipment):
Any sporting equipment shall be transported for free on Ethiopian flights, provided that the below three criteria are fulfilled.
I. If carrying only 1 checked in baggage, making the sporting equipment the 2nd piece
II. The dimension of the sporting equipment is less than or equal to 158cm or 62 inches

III. The weight of the sporting equipment is less than or equal to 32 kgs even if it is the 2nd PC.

- Any sports equipment that exceeds the allotted free baggage allowance but remains within the size and weight limits (280 cm or 110 inches) shall pay a special baggage charge (sport equipment) only.
- Any sports equipment that exceeds the size limit of 280 cm or 110 inches shall be transported as cargo.
- Advance notice is mandatory for all domestic flights within Ethiopia.
- For equipment measuring between 203-280cms, advance notice is required when making the ticket reservation.
- Before accepting sporting equipment, the originating station must verify the type of aircraft that will be used for the passenger's entire journey.
- For a 737 aircraft, accepting sporting equipment exceeding 280 cm (110 inches) is limited to two. If additional requests are made, the request handler or booking office must coordinate with the station on which the sector operated by the 737 aircraft.
Please note

- As an exception, Sporting equipment such as Kayaks and canoes, which are indivisible in nature, may be accepted with &ldquo;Heavy&rdquo; label to a maximum of 46 Kgs/ 101.41 lbs by charging applicable excess charge.
- The excess baggage charge for sporting equipment can be collected in local currency of the departure station.
- Payment is non-refundable, non-reroutable, and non-endorsable.
- Ethiopian shall not carry Jet Ski
- All other rules and regulations concerning sporting equipment shall be governed by Ethiopian baggage handling and excess baggage handling policies and procedures.

Musical Instruments

Musical instruments shall be permitted to be carried in cabin as a carry-on baggage so long as the dimension of the instrument (inside or outside of its package) do not exceed the carry-on dimension standard set by the airline. That is to say 23*40*55 (cm)...... 4.15.4

- Block seat baggage service assures passengers safe transportation of fragile or bulky baggage which should not be checked or carried in the baggage compartment.

- Such service is accomplished by allowing passengers to reserve a seat for such items subject to normal reservation procedures.

- Generally, the following list of items are considered in this category;

- Base viol and cello

- Musical instruments other than normal sized stringed instruments

- Typewriters

- Tv sets

- Radios

- Electronics Instruments

- Cosmetic Cases

- Tape recorders

- Cameras and related equipments

- Pictures and paintings

- courier / Diplomatic Bag

- Block seat baggage service is provided to passengers traveling solely on Ethiopian and ET/LH codeshare flights. And also Ethiopian portion of interline trips, or in case a similar service is offered by other carriers, to the entire portion of the interline trip involving such carriers

- Securing, loading and lashing of bulky, oversize, fragile or valuable baggage should be handled by approved staff.

- Block seat baggage must be placed forward of all seated pax in the aircraft. It must be secured in a seatbelt and seat belt extensions. Articles too large and bulky will not be accepted. Any block seat bag shall not protrude to the aisle or over the height of the seat.

- Weight of property carried as block seat baggage will not be considered when determining passenger&rsquo;s free baggage allowance and excess charge. Weight of such block baggage will be shown in total weight of checked baggage when international travel is involved.

Pets

Travelling with Pets
A. Policy

- Cats and dogs, as defined in current passenger tariffs, may be transported in the cabin or baggage compartment when in a container and accompanied by a passenger or shipped as cargo.
- Ethiopian Airlines accepts only dogs and cats in the cabin, with a limited number allowed per compartment:

Aircraft Type | C9 Class | Economy Class |
Wide Body | 2 | 4 |
Narrow Body | 1 | 2 |

Note: You must bring your pet in a box that does not exceed 55 x 40 x 20 cm and 8 kg (including the container). If the size or weight is more, the pet must be transported in the aircraft hold.

- If the cage is larger than allowed for cabin travel, or the total weight (pet + cage) is more than 8 kg and up to 45 kg, it will be transported in the hold.

- Pets (including cage) weighing more than 45 kg must be transported as cargo, following cargo procedures.

- Seeing-eye dogs (trained to assist visually impaired passengers), when harnessed and muzzled, may travel in the cabin on all Ethiopian Airlines aircraft at no charge. This depends on local or government restrictions.

- Hearing-ear dogs (trained to assist hearing-impaired passengers), when harnessed and muzzled, may also travel in the cabin for free. This requires advance arrangements and is subject to local or government restrictions.

- Service dogs for visually or hearing-impaired passengers travel free and can stay in the cabin. You must notify the airline in advance.

Snub-nosed dog and cat breeds are not accepted, due to increased breathing risks caused by stress and temperature changes during transport.
B. Procedure on carriage of pets
● Pets are accepted in the cabin on all Ethiopian Q-400 aircraft.
● Pets (in containers) accompanied by passengers or shipped as cargo may be carried in the baggage compartment.
● You must make advance arrangements for all pet transport.
C. Booking Procedure

- You must specify the type of pet and the size of the container when making a reservation.
- Ethiopian does not accept emotional support or psychiatric service animals in the cabin unless the passenger provides valid documentation (no older than one year) from a licensed mental health professional (e.g. psychiatrist, psychologist, clinical social worker, or medical doctor) on official letterhead.
D. Condition of Carriage

- Pet acceptance rules vary by country. Check with your nearest Ethiopian Airlines office or travel agent.

- For interline connections, check the policy of the other carrier.

- Charges for pets in the cabin or hold apply at excess baggage rates, regardless of other baggage.

Others

- Ethiopian shall permit transportation of cremated human ash as cabin and checked-in baggage.

Carriage of cremated Human remains
The following procedures shall be applied to accept cremated human ash on Ethiopian flights, either as cabin or checked-in baggage;

- Prior to travel, the Passenger shall be advised to contact the embassy of the destination country in order to ascertain that carriage of cremated ash into the country is permitted

- The passenger shall be advised to choose a container made of plastic or cardboard to carry the ashes. Urns made up of ceramic and metal are not permitted

- The passenger shall place the ashes in a sealed plastic bag inside the travel container or Urn to prevent the ashes from spilling

- The passenger should secure the deceased person&rsquo;s passport, cremation certificate, and death certificate

- All relevant documents, including the cremation certificate and death certificate, which are in a foreign language, need to be translated and authenticated into the local language to avoid misunderstanding with local authorities

- Passengers should arrive early to check-in and finalize formalities ahead of time, in case any eventualities may occur at the airport

- The size and weight of any cabin bag /checked-in bag should be handled per the Ethiopian baggage handling policy.

Restricted

- What are the safety concerns for cabin baggage?
Passengers are not allowed to carry the following items in their hand baggage for passengers&rsquo; safety and security reasons. To minimize inconvenience, you are advised to check in or put these items in your checked baggage instead:

Please note that these items will be retrieved at the boarding gate by the local authorities and depending on local rules and regulations, the items may not be returned to you.

- What are the rules and charges for travelling with animals?
Ethiopian shall accept only Dog or Cat into the cabin with limited number per compartment (2 in economy class and 1 in business class) with a box measuring dimensions not more than 55x40x20cms and does not exceed 8kgs including transport container, otherwise it should be loaded in belly.

- Is baggage transferred when I&rsquo;m on connecting Ethiopian airlines flights?
Yes, Ethiopian receives connecting passenger&rsquo;s baggage from other airlines. If luggage of connecting passenger is not submitted by the delivering carrier such connecting passenger should be informed using a slip prepared for this purpose that their luggage is not delivered to Ethiopian and hence not to expect at final destination.

- Can I carry firearms, weapons, and ammunition in my baggage?
Ammunition, firearms, sharp knives and/or other security-restricted items belonging to passengers shall not be carried inside cabin and/or flight deck (cockpit), and passengers must declare such items at the time of check-in. All ammunition shall be carried in the aircraft hold as checked baggage only and with approval of concerned Security Authorities.

Carry on baggage

- What is Ethiopian Airlines' allowed size and weight for carry-on baggage in Economy class?

In Economy class with Ethiopian Airlines, passengers are allowed 1 piece of hand luggage with a maximum weight of 7 kg and a maximum dimensions equal to 23 x 40 x 55 (cm) .

- What is the carry-on baggage allowance for Business class passengers with Ethiopian Airlines?

Business class passengers with Ethiopian Airlines are allowed 2 pieces of hand luggage, each weighing a maximum of 7 kg.

- Can I bring personal items in addition to my carry-on baggage on Ethiopian Airlines?

Yes, Ethiopian Airlines allows personal accessories in addition to the specified carry-on baggage allowance in both Economy and Business class.

- Are there restrictions on the types of items I can bring in my carry-on bag with Ethiopian Airlines?

Ethiopian Airlines, like other airlines, may have restrictions on carrying liquids, gels, and certain hazardous items in carry-on bags. Familiarize yourself with Ethiopian Airlines' specific policies.

- Can I bring food on board in my carry-on bag with Ethiopian Airlines?

Generally, you can bring snacks and non-liquid food items in your carry-on bag with Ethiopian Airlines, adhering to their specified policies.

- What happens if my carry-on bag exceeds the size or weight limit with Ethiopian Airlines?

If your carry-on bag exceeds the specified size or weight limit with Ethiopian Airlines, you may be subject to additional fees, or you may need to check the bag.

- Is there a difference in carry-on allowances for different classes of service with Ethiopian Airlines?

Yes, there is a difference. Business class passengers with Ethiopian Airlines have a higher allowance, with 2 pieces of hand luggage weighing 7 kg each, compared to Economy class.
