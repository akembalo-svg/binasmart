# Premium Lounge Access Ethiopian Airlines

Experience the ultimate pre-flight comfort at the Ethiopian Airlines Premium Lounge. Savor delicious meals, authentic Ethiopian coffee, and refreshing beverages, stay connected with high-speed Wi-Fi, and relax in a luxurious and serene environment designed to make your journey seamless and enjoyable.

## Services That Await You

Experience comfort and convenience at the Ethiopian Airlines Premium Lounge with:

- Delicious meals and refreshing beverages
- Authentic Ethiopian coffee
- Free high-speed Wi-Fi
- Television, reading materials, and more

## How to Purchase

You can purchase your Premium Lounge pass:

- During flight booking on our website or mobile app
- Through &ldquo;Manage Booking&rdquo; after ticket purchase
- At Ethiopian Airlines ticket offices

## Location

Premium Lounge services are available exclusively at Addis Ababa Bole International Airport.

I am transiting through Addis Ababa; can I purchase a premium lounge pass at the airport?

Yes, you can purchase your premium lounge access from Ethiopian Web/Mob app and Local offices

What is included with a Premium lounge pass?

The services included in the pass are:

- Complimentary meals and beverages
- Ethiopian coffee
- Free Wi-Fi access
- Television and reading materials
- Comfortable seating and relaxation areas
- Flight information display

Where can I purchase my premium lounge pass?

You can purchase your premium lounge pass online; through our website & mobile app, from any of our offices at local ticket offices.

What form of payment is accepted to purchase a premium lounge pass?

All forms of payment currently applicable for ticket sales is also applicable for lounge pass purchase. These options include credit/Debit card, through bank transfer, bank deposit and cash.

Can I request access for Premium lounges all over the world?

This service is currently available at Addis Ababa Bole international airport.
