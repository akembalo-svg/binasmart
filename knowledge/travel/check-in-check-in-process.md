## How to check in online with Ethiopian Airlines

Start your journey with ease using Ethiopian Airlines' Web Check-in service, available from 48 hours up to 2 hours before departure. Simply click "Web Check-in" from the quick links on our homepage, then enter your Booking Reference or Ticket Number along with your Last Name exactly as they appear on your ticket. Once submitted, you’ll be guided through key steps, including pre-check-in instructions, hazardous materials information, passenger details confirmation, seat selection, and optional extra baggage .

1. What is online check-in?

Online check-in is a service offered by Ethiopian Airlines in which passengers can check-in their flights using our website, mobile App or social Bots within 48 hours prior to the departure of the flight from wherever you are.
You can select your preferred seats, update your travel preferences, access our additional services and print your boarding pass online. This is a great alternative to avoid long queues at the airport.

3. Is online check-in applicable to group reservations?

Yes, it is possible to online check-in group passengers on the Ethiopian Airlines website but not on the mobile applications, and Social bot. The maximum number of passengers who can be checked in at once is 10, and any additional passengers will be checked in the next batch of 10 until every passenger is checked in.

4. Which types of passengers are not allowed to check-in online?

Online check-in is unavailable for specific passenger categories who are required to proceed to the airport for check-in. This includes:

- passengers needing medical assistance,
- unaccompanied minors,

- those with tickets requiring additional payment,

- individuals with travel documents needing further verification at the check-in counter,

- pregnant mothers,

- and those requiring special assistance. For these passengers, the check-in process at the airport is necessary.

5. What time does online check-in open and close?

Online check-in is available between 48 to 2 hours prior to the scheduled departure time of a flight.

7. What document do I need to use the online check-in service?

You only need your Passport (passport is not valid up to a minimum of six months), the E-ticket number or Booking Reference to make use of our online check-in services. Visa and other travel documents may also be requested as per the ADC rule.

8. What happens if I experience a problem while checking in online?

You can Contact Us via email or contact the nearest Ethiopian airlines office at any time with regards to your check-in related issue. Please note that you may encounter issues at online check-in if your passport is not valid up to a minimum of six months.

9. I have purchased my seat in advance; however, Can I also check in for connecting flights with other airlines?

Yes, you can check in for connecting flights with other airlines as long as the first sector is operated by Ethiopian Airlines. Please note these check-ins are subject to other airline policies and restrictions.

10. What about check-in online for return / onward flights?

Online check-in for return flights can only be completed 48 hours before flight departure and after you have departed on the first sector of your flight. However, if one of your sectors is with another airline and you were not able to check in successfully on ethiopianairlines.com, you will need to visit the other airline&rsquo;s website in order for you to check-in.

12. Is online check-in applicable to domestic flights and connecting flights?

Yes. Boarding pass can be printed for each flight sector.

14. Can I do Online Check-In for my travelling companions as well?

Yes, you can check in your travelling companions if you make your bookings together, provided they share the same booking reference as yours in our record.

15. What if I fail to arrive on time at the airport after check-in in online?

If you fail to check in on time, you may be refused for carriage on your flight. This is in the interest of maintaining Ethiopian on-time performance and to ensure those on the flight who have connecting flights can make it on time.

- Depending on your fare conditions, a failure to check in on time may also result in: The loss of the original fare value. You may be required to make a new booking and pay for a new ticket if you wish to travel, or
- Pay a change fee penalty plus any difference between the fare of your new booking and the original fare

16. How soon before my flight should I arrive at the airport after I check-in online?

Passenger with no luggage shall arrive at the gate 30 minutes prior to boarding. If the passenger with baggage drop off shall arrive at web check-in counter at least 1 hour before departure for international flights and 45 minutes before departure for the domestic flight.

18. What is Your reservation is not eligible means?

This is when the passenger booking is not qualified for online check-in. This could be when the booking is cancelled or it is not confirmed. Such error response will be displayed when there is a schedule change or last minute change on your flight.

19. What does Invalid segment found in your reservation means?

Such error response will be displayed when the booking has a status of waiting list, reconfirmed or unable to confirm status. All flight segment should be in OK status to be able to qualify for online check-in.

20. Restricted Passenger

This means when a given passenger is restricted to make online check-in and in this case he/she has to check-in at the airport. This happens when the passenger travel document requires further in person verification or when a given passenger is restricted to enter to the destination country.

21. What is partially checked-in?

Partial check-in happen when the first flight segment is checked in but the second flight segment is not. Some times if there are more than one passenger, the system will allow to check-in one passenger but not the other passenger. When this happen the traveler should check-in the remining flight segment at the airport.

24. What Does Check-in System Error mean?

This includes various system error behind the scene and one or more system may not be working as expected. When the passenger get this response he/she should proceed and make their check-in at the airport.

23. Why I get “We found your reservation but there is a problem with your ticket” response?

This error happen when the passenger ticket is not eligible or it has status code other than OK such as NOGO,LFTD,RFND or CTRL. When the passenger ticket is used or refunded such error response will be displayed.
