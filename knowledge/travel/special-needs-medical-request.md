# Medical Information Form (MEDIF)

If you require medical assistance or need travel clearance, please complete the Medical Information Form (MEDIF).
Our medical team will review your submission to assess your fitness to fly and determine any special support or arrangements needed to ensure a safe and comfortable journey.
To begin:
1. Enter your booking reference and last name.
2. Complete the MEDIF form in full.
3. A confirmation will be sent to your email address upon submission.
You can track the status of your request using the reference number provided after submission.
