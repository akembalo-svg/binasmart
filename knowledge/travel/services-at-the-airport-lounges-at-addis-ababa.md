# Lounge services at Addis Ababa Bole International Airport

Ethiopian Airlines operates four lounges in Terminal 2 of Addis Ababa Bole International
Airport (ADD), all located near Gate A13: the Sheba Cloud Nine Lounge for Business Class travelers, the
ShebaMiles Platinum Lounge and Star Alliance Gold Lounge for top-tier elite members, and the ShebaMiles Silver Lounge for Silvertier frequent flyers. Eligible passengers may enter starting five hours before their scheduled departure.

Ethiopian Airlines' lounges at Addis Ababa Bole International Airport offer comfort, privacy, and
premium services for travelers passing through the airline's home hub. Guests can enjoy refreshments,
workspaces, and relaxing environments while waiting for their flight, with exclusive lounge services
designed to make every connection through Addis Ababa smoother and more enjoyable.

Step into an Ethiopian Airlines lounge at Addis Ababa Bole International Airport and relax in style. With
cozy seating, delicious refreshments, and peaceful surroundings, every moment before a flight becomes
a more comfortable part of the journey

### Location

Ethiopian Airlines' lounges are located on the terminal 2 airside, opposite Gate A13.

- For joining passengers – after immigration and the security check, turn left and walk until
reaching Gate A13. The lounge is located opposite Gate A13.
- For transit passengers –after the security check, go one floor up to the departure floor.
Once on the departure floor, turn left until reaching Gate A13. The lounge is located opposite
Gate A13.

## Sheba Cloud Nine Lounge

Open 24 hours a day in Terminal 2 near Gate A13 Welcomes all passengers traveling in Cloud Nine (Business) class out of Addis Ababa, with a traditional Ethiopian coffee ceremony and spacious seating.

## ShebaMiles Platinum & Star Alliance Gold Lounge

Open 24/7, located opposite Gate A13. Reserved for Ethiopian's own ShebaMiles Platinum members and Star Alliance Gold members from any partner carrier.

## ShebaMiles Silver Lounge

Located in the inner corridor of the departure hall in Terminal 2. Dedicated to Ethiopian's own ShebaMiles Silver members only.

## Shower Facility

First-come, first-served with registration and boarding pass required. Allow at least 60 minutes before departure; each session is capped at 15 minutes.

# Terms and Conditions

## General

- Passengers are allowed to enter the lounges they are entitled to only. Please do not enter a
lounge you are not entitled to.
- Customers should stay aware of their flight departure times while in the lounge

## Personal Belongings & Baggage Carts

- Carrying an abnormal type or size of bag into the lounges, beyond a normal handbag, is not
permitted.
- Customers must keep their personal belongings with them at all times — Ethiopian Airlines does
not take responsibility for lost items of any kind in the lounges.
- Unpacking or repacking commercial items in the lounges is strictly forbidden.
- Baggage carts of any kind are not permitted inside the lounges.

## Food Items

- Passengers may not bring outside food into the lounge unless a valid reason is discussed with
the team leader on duty.

- Guests are welcome to fully enjoy the lounge's food and beverage offerings on-site, but food,
drinks, and periodicals may not be removed from the lounge.

## Pets

- Animals permitted in the cabin may be brought into the lounge and must remain in their locked
carriers, except for trained and properly documented service animals assisting passengers
physically, psychologically, or emotionally.

- Lounge tableware may not be used to feed animals.
- Animals are not permitted to walk freely around the food and beverage areas or occupy lounge
seating.

- Customers may be asked to remove an unruly pet for the comfort of other guests.

## Smoking

- Smoking, including the use of electronic cigarettes, is prohibited in the lounges except in
designated smoking areas.

## Usage of Mobile Devices and Musical Instruments

- Playing a musical instrument or media at loud volume is not permitted.

- Using loudspeakers for personal calls is discouraged; headphones or earbuds are recommended
for the comfort of other guests.

## Children

- Children must be accompanied by an adult at all times and should behave in a manner suited
to the quiet atmosphere of the lounge.

## Alcoholic Beverages

- Guests are asked to drink responsibly.
- Ethiopian Airlines staff reserve the right to refuse alcoholic beverages to any guest who appears
intoxicated or behaves unruly.

- Guests under the age of 21 are not permitted to consume alcohol in any Ethiopian Airlines
lounge

## Informal Dressing and Inappropriate Behaviors

- Walking in the lounge in nightclothes or bare feet is not permitted.
- Iying down on the floor or sofas, or resting feet on tables, is not allowed, out of respect for the
cleanliness of the lounge and its furniture.
- Other examples of inappropriate behavior include drunkenness, extreme rudeness toward staff
or other guests, aggressive behavior, and any act considered harmful or unsuitable for the
environment.

## Shower Facility

- Showers are offered on a first-come, first-served basis; guests must register for a turn.
- Guests may use the shower only during their assigned turn and only while holding the next
number on the list — trying to bypass the queue may result in losing access.
- Guests should keep their boarding pass with reception staff for monitoring against their
boarding time.

- The minimum time required to enter the shower room is 60 minutes before departure; using the
shower with less than 60 minutes before departure is not recommended.
- The maximum allowable time in the shower is 15 minutes, to allow efficient use of the facility.
- Please leave the shower clean after use.
- Littering water or insoluble items in the showers is not allowed.

- Please hang towels on the towel hangers rather than throwing them on the floor.

1. How many lounges does Ethiopian Airlines operate at Addis Ababa Bole International Airport?

Ethiopian Airlines operates four lounges in Terminal 2, all near Gate A13: the Sheba Cloud Nine Lounge for
Business Class, the ShebaMiles Platinum Lounge and Star Alliance Gold Lounge for elite members, and the
ShebaMiles Silver Lounge for ShebaMiles Silver members.

2. Who can access the Sheba Cloud Nine Lounge?

Passengers with a Business (Cloud Nine) class boarding pass on their onward flight out of Addis Ababa can
access the Cloud Nine Lounge. Transit passengers connecting onward in Economy class are not eligible, even if
their inbound flight was in Business Class.

3. Are the lounges open 24 hours?

Yes. The Sheba Cloud Nine Lounge and the Sheba Platinum/Star Gold Lounge are both open 24/7 throughout
the week.

4. How early before my flight can I enter the lounge?

Eligible customers can access the lounge starting five hours before their estimated departure time.

5. Where exactly are the lounges located?

All lounges are on the airside of Terminal 2, opposite Gate A13. Joining passengers turn left after immigration
and security until reaching Gate A13; transit passengers go one floor up to the departure floor and then turn left
until reaching Gate A13.

6. Can I use the shower facility in the lounge?

A. Yes, showers are available on a first-come, first-served basis with registration at reception. Arrive with at
least 60 minutes before departure, since each shower turn is limited to 15 minutes and boarding pass details
are checked against your departure time.

7. Can I bring my own food into the lounge?

Outside food is generally not permitted unless a justifiable reason is discussed with the team leader on duty.
Guests are invited to enjoy the lounge's own food and beverage offerings on-site, but items may not be
removed from the lounge.

8. Can children and pets enter the lounge?

Children are welcome if accompanied by an adult at all times and kept to a manner suited to the lounge's
quiet atmosphere. Pets permitted in the cabin may enter but must stay in their locked carriers, except for
trained, documented service animals.

9. Is there an age limit for alcoholic beverages in the lounge?

Yes. Guests under the age of 21 are not permitted to consume alcohol in any Ethiopian Airlines lounge, and
staff may refuse service to any guest who appears intoxicated.
