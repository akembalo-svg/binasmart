1. What is a cargo charter flight?

A cargo charter flight is a dedicated service where an entire aircraft is reserved exclusively for transporting cargo. It offers flexible capacity and routing, allowing shipments to be delivered to any destination based on the customer&rsquo;s specific needs.

2. What are the Advantages of Using an Air Cargo Charter Flight?

Air cargo flights are the ideal choice for customers looking to save time, reduce costs, and streamline logistics, ensuring fast and efficient delivery of goods.

3. What kind of services Ethiopian provides on a cargo charter flight?

We provide a charter flight to any destination carrying any cargo (Per IATA standard and A/C capacity) with options for part charter or full charter option.

4. How can I book a charter flight?

You can book a charter flight by filling out the online form on our website or by contacting us via email or phone for assistance.

5. What are Ethiopian cargo charter payment terms?

Payment terms are based on agreements between the parties. However, for any charter flight, payment must be fully settled at least three days before the scheduled operation.

6. How reliable is Ethiopian Cargo charter flight?

Ethiopian Cargo Charter offers highly reliable services, ensuring safe and timely deliveries. Our trained staff handle shipments with special care, providing a seamless and secure transportation experience.

7. How much does it cost to ship by air?

The cost of air shipping depends on factors such as route, distance, and the nature of the cargo. Our cargo charter team is always ready to provide detailed pricing information. Please contact us for a customized quote.

8. Will charter rates include meals, taxes, and other charges?

Yes, our charter quotations include a standard economy meal, all airport fees, and related government taxes, except for cargo-related taxes if cargo is part of the charter.

9. How fast can you arrange a charter flight?

For domestic charter flights, we can typically arrange the flight within 6-12 hours. For international flights, it generally takes 24-72 hours, depending on aircraft availability and required permits from certain countries.
