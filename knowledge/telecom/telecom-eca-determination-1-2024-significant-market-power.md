Determination of Significant Market Power (SMP)
             in Relevant Markets
          Determination No. 1 of 2024

April 2024

1.    Introduction

The Ethiopian Communications Authority (ECA) is publishing this determination on
Telecommunications Operators with Significant Market Powers in relevant market
areas following the market review study undertaken on the Telecommunications
market in Ethiopia.

2.    Legal Background

Pursuant to article 47 of the Communications Service Proclamation No. 1148/2019,
the Authority is fully mandated to regulate market competition in the
telecommunications sector. Notwithstanding the provisions of any other law, the
Authority shall have exclusive power to determine, pronounce upon, administer,
monitor and enforce compliance of all persons with competition laws and
Directives, whether of a general or specific nature, as it relates to the Ethiopian
Communications Service market.

Pursuant   to   sub   article   1(c)   of   Article   5 of   the   Telecommunications
Interconnection Directive No. 791/2021, the Authority shall monitor competition in
Telecommunication Services, and from time to time, define relevant markets, and
designate operators with Significant Market Power in those markets;

Pursuant to Sub-Article (1) of Article 7 of Telecommunications Competition
Directive No. 798/2021, the Ethiopian Communications Authority shall endeavor
to perform a Market Review every three (3) years to define markets susceptible to
regulation, and designate operators as having SMP in those markets.

Pursuant to Sub-Article (2) of Article 7 of Telecommunications Competition
Directive No. 798/2021, the purpose of a Market Review shall not be to define all
telecommunications markets. Instead, the Market Review shall define only
markets that shall be susceptible to regulation.

3.      Ethiopian Telecommunications Market Review Study

In line with the powers provided in the Telecommunications Competition Directive
No. 798/2021, ECA has conducted market review study of the Ethiopian
Telecommunication sector based on the above legal and regulatory frameworks.

The objectives of the study are:

a. To identify relevant markets in the mobile telecommunications value chain
        and assess whether they are susceptible to ex ante regulation.
     b. For those markets susceptible to ex ante regulation, to examine the existing
        competition dynamics in order to understand whether any of the existing
        operators hold a position of SMP.
     c. Where SMP exists, to identify corrective action or pro-competitive remedies
        required to encourage effective competition in the markets identified.

The approach used in this market review study is in line with articles 10-14, 16, 26(2)
of the Telecommunications Competition Directive No. 798/2021, as well as with
international best practices.

The     Telecommunications      Operators       -   Ethio   Telecom   and   Safaricom
Telecommunications Ethiopia PLC were actively participated in providing input
data for the Market review study. This involvement entailed sharing essential
information and insights relevant to the telecommunications sector, contributing
significantly to the study.

Furthermore, stakeholder consultations on the draft market review study were
conducted with Ethio Telecom and Safaricom Telecommunications Ethiopia PLC.

These consultations were instrumental in gathering feedback, addressing
potential concerns and ensuring that the perspectives of the stakeholders were
considered. Such engagement with industry stakeholders enhances the study's

validity, relevance, and effectiveness in informing regulatory decisions and
      promoting fair competition within the telecommunications market.

4.       Relevant Markets

The Market Review Study has identified the following five relevant markets and
      found them to be susceptible to ex ante regulation.

i) Wholesale Transmission Markets;
               ii) Wholesale access to cell site passive infrastructure market;
               iii) Wholesale mobile and fixed termination services market;
               iv) Wholesale mobile access and call origination market;
               v) Retail mobile services market.

6.    Enforcement of Proposed Remedies

Operators with significant market power shall have the duties to comply with the
specific measures indicated under the proposed remedies.

Regarding remedies on Potential account separation, Telecommunications
operators shall Separate the account by type of service in accordance with the
criteria and procedures for telecommunications accounts reporting on type of
service which shall be prescribed in due course by the Ethiopian Communications
Authority.
