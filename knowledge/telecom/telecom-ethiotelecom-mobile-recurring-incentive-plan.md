## Stay Connected without interruption!

###

Unlock Growing Bonuses with Every Auto-Renewal Packages!

Keep your favorite Data, Voice, or Voice + Data plan active with auto-renewal and receive a FREE, growing bonus each month.

The Recurring Package Incentive Plan rewards subscribers of limited recurring packages by promoting consistent usage and enhancing customer retention. It’s the easiest way to get more value and stay connected without interruption.

Features:

- Applies to Customers who have successfully renewed their subscription for the second time without falling into pending status.

- It provides a progressive bonus reward:

- ⇒ 5% bonus on package resources for the 2nd month

- ⇒ 10% bonus for the 3rd month

- ⇒ 15% bonus for the 4th month

- Eligibility

- To start receiving rewards, you must subscribe to an eligible recurring plan and maintain it for at least two consecutive months without any renewal failures (pending status).

- Auto-Renewal Failure

- If your renewal fails due to insufficient balance, you enter a pending status.
- You have a 5-day grace period to recharge your account. If you recharge within these 5 days, your plan will be renewed, and you will receive your incentive.
- If you fail to recharge within 5 days, you will be removed from the incentive plan.
- If your renewal fails and you later reactivate, the system will treat it as a new purchase. Your bonus-earning cycle will restart, and you will need another two consecutive successful renewals to begin receiving bonuses again.

- Included Plans

- This incentive plan applies to a specified list of existing recurring Data, Voice, and Voice + Data
- It does not include Social, Unlimited, or Flexi package plans.

- Bonus Rules & Criteria

- If customers fail to recharge within more than 5 days, they will not receive the incentive, and the system will remove the offers automatically from their account.
- Your monthly bonus is valid for 7 days.
- Any unused bonus resources will expire after 7 days and will not roll over to the next period.
- The bonus cannot be transferred to another user or converted to cash.
- All other existing business rules for our recurring packages remain unchanged.

- Renewal & Reset Rules

- From the fourth month onward, customers will continue to receive the fourth-month bonus with a successful renewal until the service is interrupted.
- If the service is interrupted, the incentive cycle resets.
