### Eligibility

- Disability package plan is dedicated for selected whitelisted customers based on their disability type (Blindness, deaf, physical disable, Mental and Leprosy).
- Whitelisted prepaid, postpaid and Hybrid customers are eligible to purchase the package.
- Customers shall be registered for the whitelist by visiting the nearest Ethio telecom service center in person.
- Disabled persons package out of whitelisted customers eligible to purchase and send gift package only for whitelisted disabled customers and not eligible to buy themselves and gifts for customers out of the whitelisted.

- All the resources are not convertible and transferable.
