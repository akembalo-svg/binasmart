## How to Activate International Roaming?

To easily stay in touch with your loved ones during your stay, activate your roaming service on the telebirr SuperApp, My Ethiotel App, or e-Care services before your trip. Once activated, you can purchase Hajj roaming packages by dialing *999# or using the telebirr SuperApp.

## How to select preferred network manually?

## Android

Settings > Connections / Mobile Networks > Network Operators > Search Networks > Select Preferred Network.

## iPhone

Settings > Carrier > Turn Automatic OFF > Wait for available networks > Select Preferred Network.

## Windows

Settings > Mobile network > Mobile & SIM > Network selection/SIM settings > Wait for networks > Select Preferred Network.

## How can I browse the internet while roaming abroad?

## Ensure data roaming is turned ON.

## Android

Settings > Mobile Networks > Turn Data Roaming ON.

## iPhone

Settings > Cellular > Cellular Data > Turn Data Roaming ON.

## Windows

Settings > Network & wireless > Mobile & SIM > Data roaming options > Select Roam.

## How to call or send SMS to Ethiopia?

- Start with “+251” or “00”, the dial the full number.
Example: +2519******** or 009********

## How to connect to the internet?

## Android

Settings > Mobile Networks > Access Point Names > Check APN, change to et.com and save.

## iPhone

Settings > Cellular > Cellular Data Options > Cellular Network > Make sure APN is et.com.

## Windows

Settings > Mobile & SIM > SIM settings > Turn on Manual Internet APN > Enter APN as et.com and Save.
