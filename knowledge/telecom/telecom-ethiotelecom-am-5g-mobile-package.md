Frequently Asked Questions for 5G Service

What is 5G?

✓5G is the fifth generation of cellular technology. It is designed to increase speed, reduce

latency, and improve the flexibility of wireless services.

What is the difference between 4G and 5G?

✓The main differences between 4G and 5G are faster speed, higher bandwidth, and lower

“latency”, or the communication lag time between the device and the server.

What are the benefits of 5G?

The 5G network provides enhanced performance, increased capacity, and network efficiency in

every industry.

- Faster downloads

- More devices & and device types for both personal and enterprise use

- Lower end-to-end network latency

- Flexibility with network slicing

- Massive IoT with a high density of smart devices

What is the requirement to get 5G service?

- 5G Enabled SIM card

- 5G Supported Mobile Phone

- Available in 5G network coverage

How do I activate my SIM to support 5G Service?

- Can upgrade the existing 4G SIM card to 5G service

- Subscribe the new 5G SIM card

Will every smartphone be able to use 5G service?

✓No: - The 5G service can only be accessed by mobile devices having 5G capabilities.

How do I know that my phone is 5G support?

✓You can check if your phone is 5G capable or not on the device setting/about the phone

or on the “Preferred Network type/mode” list

Are all customers eligible for this service?

✓Yes, the 5G service is available for all prepaid and postpaid as well as hybrid 5G SIM

card customers.

What is the price of 5G service?

✓The price for 5G service is the same as the subscription fee for 4G service.

How to activate 5G service in our device?

For Android Phones:

- Go to Settings

- Select “SIM Card and Mobile Network”

- Select SIM.

- Select “Preferred Network type/mode”

- Select “5G/4G/3G/2G (Auto)”

For iPhone phones:

- Go to Settings on your phone.

- Tap on “Cellular”

- Tap on “Cellular Data Option”

- Tap on “Voice & Data”

- Select “5G On”

I have a 5G device. Do I need to check whether it supports or not?

✓Most 5G devices are compatible with the 5G network. Some devices might require a software

upgrade which will directly get pushed to your phone from the device brands. Please ensure

you install the update to make your phone 5G-ready. Steps to update software:

- Go to Settings.

- Tap on “Software update”

- Tap on “Download and install”

- Select “install”

What happens if I move from a 5G coverage area to a 4G/3G coverage area?

✓You will be able to move seamlessly between 5G and 4G/3G networks without any action or

intervention required on your part. The network displays on your mobile will show 5G or

4G/3G depending on the coverage available in your location

ስልኬ የ5ጂ አገልግሎትን እንደሚያስጠቅም እንዴት ማወቅ እችላለሁ

- የስልክዎ ዲቫይስ ሴቲንግ ላይ በመግባት “about the phone” ወይም “Preferred Network type/mode” ዝርዝር ውስጥ 5ጂ መኖሩን ያረጋግጡ፡፡

ሁሉም የኢትዮ ቴሌኮም የሞባይል አገልግሎት አይነቶች 5ጂ መጠቀም ያስችላሉ?

- የድሕረ-ክፍያ፣ የቅድመ ክፍያም ሆነ የጥምር አገልግሎት ደንበኞች የ5ጂ አገልግሎትን መጠቀም ይችላሉ፡፡

የ5ጂ አገልግሎት ዋጋ ልዩነት አለው?

- የ5ጂ አገልግሎት ዋጋ ከመደበኛው ነባር የሞባይል አገልግሎት ክፍያ ጋር ተመሳሳይ ነው፡፡

የ5ጂ አገልግሎትን በስልኬ ላይ እንዴት ማስጀመር እችላለሁ?
ለአድሮይድ ስልኮች:

- Settings

- “SIM Card and Mobile Network” ይምረጡ

- SIM ይምረጡ

- “Preferred Network type/mode” ይምረጡ

- “5G/4G/3G/2G (Auto)” የሚለውን ይምረጡ

አይፎን:

- Settings

- “Cellular” ን ይጫኑ

- “Cellular Data Option”

- “Voice & Data” ን ይጫኑ

- “5G On” ን ይምረጡ

5G የሚያስጠቅም ስልክ አለኝ፤ 5G የሚያስጠቅም መሆን አለመሆኑን እንዴት ማረጋገጥ እችላለሁ?

- አብዛኞቹ 5G የሚያስጠቅሙ ሰልኮች በቀጥታ የ5ጂ አገልግሎት መጠቀም የሚያስችሉ ሲሆን፤ አንዳንዶቹ ደግሞ የሶፍትዌር ማሻሻያ ሊያስፈልጋቸው ይችላል፤ የሚያስፈልገው ማሻሻያም ከአምራቾቹ የሚለቀቅ ሲሆን በሚከተለው ሂደት ሶፍትዌሩን ማዘመንዎን ያረጋግጡ

- Settings

- “Software update” ን ይጫኑ

- “Download and install” ን ይጫኑ

- “install” የሚለውን ይምረጡ

የ5ጂ አገልግሎት ካለበት ስፍራ ወደ ሌላ የ4G/3G ኔትወርክ ወዳለበት ስፍራ ብቀይር ምን ማድረግ እችላለሁ?

- የ5ጂ አገልግሎት ከሚገኝበት ስፍራ ወደ 4G/3G የኢትዮ ቴሌኮም ኔትወርክ ወዳለበት ስፍራ ቦታ ሲቀይሩ ምንም ማድረግ ሳይጠበቅብዎ ያለምንም ችግር የሞሞይል ስልክዎ በ4G/3G ኔትወርክ አገልግሎት መስጠቱን ይቀጥላል፡፡
