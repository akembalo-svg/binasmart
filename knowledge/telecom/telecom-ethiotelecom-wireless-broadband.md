Terms and Conditions

- The service is available only for enterprise customer with data only postpaid option.
- It is recommended for the customer living areas do not have coverage of fixed broadband network and having 4GLTE network.
- Fair Usage Policy shall be applied for speed based internet service if the customer finished the assigned volume, the speed shall be downgraded to predefined access speed. However, FUP is not applicable for volume based option and VPN service.
- The 4G Lte (WTTo) SIM card shall be activated to use only data/internet service.
- To make the service to office based only, the SIM binding locking feature shall be applicable in which location shall be locked with the nearest BTS. If the customers want to relocate the office location, the customer should request move/relocation service.
- The 4G Lte (WTTo) device shall be provided with installment basis for 12 months or upfront as per the customers’ preference, the customer shall have the right to choose.
- All package prices are monthly based and recurring
- Up/down grade will be possible as per the customer request
- Proration shall be applicable for the first month subscription and termination.
- All prices are VAT inclusive.
