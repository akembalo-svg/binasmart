FAQs
Q1: What is VoLTE?
▪VoLTE or Voice over LTE is a service that allows customers to make high-quality voice calls over 4G LTE network. Unlike traditional voice calls that use 2G or 3G networks, VoLTE utilizes the data network to transmit voice calls.
Q2: What is the difference between 4G and VoLTE?
▪4G provides high-speed data connectivity, VoLTE specifically focuses on improving the quality and efficiency of voice calls over an LTE network.
Q3: What are the benefits of VoLTE service?
▪VoLTE offers several advantages over traditional voice calls, including:
✓High-definition (HD) Voice and Video Quality
✓Faster Call Setup times:
✓The ability to use simultaneous voice and data services.
✓Improved battery life
Q4: Who can use VoLTE service?
▪It is available to all Mobile Postpaid, Prepaid and Hybrid customers, except for Data Only SIM customers.
Q5: What is the requirement to get VoLTE service?
▪To use VoLTE service, a customer should meet the following requirements:
✓Have VoLTE capable devices and 4G/5G SIM.
✓Ensure that the handset has been updated with the latest software from the handset manufacturer for VoLTE calls.
✓Need to be in a 4G/5G network.
✓Verify that the VoLTE voice calling feature on the smartphone is turned on.
Q6: Will every smartphone be able to use VoLTE service?
▪Not every smartphone will be able to use VoLTE (Voice over LTE) service. The VoLTE service can only be accessed by mobile devices having 4G/5G capabilities.
Q7: How do I know that my phone has VoLTE support?
▪You can check if your phone is VoLTE capable or not by checking Phone Settings: On some devices, you can check for VoLTE settings directly in the phone's settings menu. Here's a general guideline to locate the VoLTE settings:
✓Go to the "Settings" menu on your phone.
✓Look for the "Connections" or "Network & Internet" section.
✓Locate the "Mobile Networks" or "Cellular Networks" option.
✓Look for an option related to "VoLTE," "HD Calling," or "Enhanced 4G LTE Services. The presence of such an option suggests that your phone supports VoLTE.
Q8: Will VoLTE calls consume data?
▪No. VoLTE is a voice service and will not consume any data. Calls will be charged as per the existing normal call rates.
Q9: Do I need to have data bundles to make video calls on VoLTE?
▪No. Customers can make video calls on VoLTE without data resources. The video call will be charged as a normal call from airtime or package resource minutes.
Q10: What are the PAYGO tariffs for VoLTE Call?
▪For On-net VoLTE Voice call /Video call tariff:
✓Peak hour: Br. 0.50/minute
✓Off-Peak hour: Br. 0.35/minute
▪For Off-net VoLTE Voice call tariff:
✓Peak hour: Br. 0.75/minute
✓Off-Peak hour: Br. 0.75/minute
Q11: Does VoLTE require a separate subscription or plan?
▪VoLTE has no separate subscription or package plan; customers will call using airtime or minutes (voice package resource), and normal charges will apply.
Q12: Can customers use VoLTE internationally?
▪No. This service can be used only nationally (wherever LTE is available). However, a customer will be able to make regular calls on 3G and 2G as well.
Q13: How to activate VoLTE service in our device?
▪For Android Phones Enable VoLTE in Settings:
1.Go to the "Settings" menu.
2.Tap on "Connections" or "Network & Internet".
3.Select "Mobile Networks" or "Cellular Networks."
4.Depending on your device and Android version, you may find an option called "VoLTE," "HD Calling," or "Enhanced 4G LTE Services." Enable this option to activate VoLTE on your device.
▪For iOS (iPhone) Enable VoLTE in Settings:
1.Go to the "Settings" app.
2.Tap on "Cellular" or "Mobile Data."
3.Select "Cellular Data Options."
4.Tap on "Voice & Data."
5.Choose "LTE" or "Data Only" to enable VoLTE.
Q14: What happens if I move to an area with no LTE/5G coverage during a VoLTE call?
▪If the customer moves to an area with no LTE/5G coverage during a VoLTE call, the call may switch to a non-VoLTE connection (such as 3G or 2G) to maintain the call without interruption.
Q15: How will I know if I am on VoLTE service?
▪For Android users: The “HD/VoLTE” icon will be shown on the top bar of the screen.
▪For iOS users: There is no visual change. However, they can verify the VoLTE status through the settings under mobile data.
Q16: Is VoLTE available everywhere?
▪VoLTE is available in areas where there is LTE/5G coverage.
Q17: Does VoLTE work for both caller and receipt if only one has VoLTE?
▪If your mobile device supports VoLTE, you will be able to make and receive VoLTE voice calls when in 4G service regions, regardless of whether your caller or recipient is on VoLTE. However, to experience VoLTE Video calls, both parties must be on VoLTE service.

- If the primary number/SIM card is lost and the customer requests suspension, all secondary SIM cards will be suspended; if the customer requests for activation, all secondary SIM cards also become active.

Life Cycle of the Services

-
- If customer do not have enough balance to pay the daily or weekly or monthly service subscribed after normal service time, subscriber will enter Grace period (7 days) while using the service and system shall wait till, they have the required balance to charge. With this stage they can access the 604 IVR and use the default voice changer.
- If the customer does not have balance within the 7 days of the grace period, they will enter the suspension period (60 days) where they cannot use the service at all, and service suspended.
- At last, if the customer did not pay within the suspension period, they will be automatically terminated from the service.
- If customers fail to pay monthly rental fee, the system will let them use the weekly package to enable user can access the service for 7 days’ and minimize the user experience impact. This shall be applied in case the charging is failed for weekly service as well.
