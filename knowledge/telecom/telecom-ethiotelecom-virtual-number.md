## Virtual Number

One SIM, Two Identities

A Virtual Number is a digital, non-physical, secondary line linked to your primary SIM card. It lets you manage personal and professional calls on a single phone without needing an extra SIM card for local calls.

Why Choose a Virtual Number?

- Different mobile Numbers with One SIM Card: Get a second 9 or 11-digit number on your current phone—no extra SIM or device needed.
- Enhanced Privacy: Keep your primary number private by using your virtual line for business.
- Work-Life Balance: Easily separate personal and professional calls on a single device.
- Instant Activation: Simple, hassle-free setup that works immediately on your existing phone.

Connectivity

- Outgoing Calls: You can call any mobile network from your Virtual Number.
- Incoming Calls: Receiving call accepted only from other Virtual Numbers.
