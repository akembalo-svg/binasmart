FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions for 5G Service
What is 5G?

✓5G is the fifth generation of cellular technology. It is designed to increase speed, reduce
latency, and improve the flexibility of wireless services.
What is the difference between 4G and 5G?
✓The main differences between 4G and 5G are faster speed, higher bandwidth, and lower
“latency”, or the communication lag time between the device and the server.
What are the benefits of 5G?
The 5G network provides enhanced performance, increased capacity, and network efficiency in
every industry.

- Super Fast downloads
- More devices & and device types for both personal and enterprise use
- Lower end-to-end network latency
- Flexibility with network slicing
- Massive IoT with a high density of smart devices
What is the requirement to get 5G service?

- 5G Enabled SIM card
- 5G Supported Mobile Phone
- Available in 5G network coverage
How do I activate my SIM to support 5G Service?

- Can upgrade the existing 4G SIM card to 5G service
- Subscribe the new 5G SIM card
Will every smartphone be able to use 5G service?
✓No: - The 5G service can only be accessed by mobile devices having 5G capabilities.
How do I know that my phone is 5G support?
✓You can check if your phone is 5G capable or not on the device setting/about the phone
or on the “Preferred Network type/mode” list
Are all customers eligible for this service?
✓Yes, the 5G service is available for all prepaid and postpaid as well as hybrid 5G SIM
card customers.
What is the price of 5G service?
✓The price for 5G service is the same as the subscription fee for 4G service.
How to activate 5G service in our device?
For Android Phones:

- Go to Settings
- Select “SIM Card and Mobile Network”
- Select SIM.
- Select “Preferred Network type/mode”
- Select “5G/4G/3G/2G (Auto)”
For iPhone phones:

- Go to Settings on your phone.
- Tap on “Cellular”
- Tap on “Cellular Data Option”
- Tap on “Voice & Data”
- Select “5G On”
