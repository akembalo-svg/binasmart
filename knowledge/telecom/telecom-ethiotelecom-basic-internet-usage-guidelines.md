## Basic Internet Usage Guidelines

## Understanding The Internet

The Internet is a vast, global network that connects millions of computers, smartphones, smart future phones and servers worldwide. Using phone lines, satellites, and undersea cables, it allows users to access an ever-evolving range of information and digital services. To get started, you simply need a compatible device and a data connection.

## Core Internet Services

The internet has become the foundation of modern life by providing essential services such as:

- Information: Instant access to news, research, and general knowledge.
- Communication: Email, instant messaging, and video calls.
- Social media: Platforms to connect with friends and family.
- Entertainment: Streaming music, movies, and gaming.
- E-Commerce & Mobile Banking: Online shopping and secure financial management.
- E-Learning: Online courses and digital classrooms.

## How to Get Online ?

To start browsing, you need three basic components

## A Device

A smartphone, smart future phone, tablet, or computer.

## A Connection

An active Mobile Data plan or a Wi-Fi connection.

## A Web Browser/App

An application used to view websites (e.g., Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge and you tube, Facebook, WhatsApp, etc).

## Steps to Browse the Web . . .

## Visit a website

Open your browser, type a specific address (e.g., www.ethiotelecom.et) into the address bar at the top, and press Enter

## Search for Information

If you don’t know the address, type keywords or questions (e.g., “latest news” or “ethio telecom packages”) into the Google search box

## . . .Installing Mobile Applications

For specialized services like social media or banking, it is often better to use a dedicated App. These must be downloaded from your device’s official store.

## For Android Users -Google Play Store

Open the Play Store
Locate the icon on your home screen.

Set Up an Account
If you haven't signed in, tap Sign In and select
Create Account -> For my personal use. Follow the prompts to enter your name, birthday, and choose a Gmail address and strong password.

Search
Use the top search bar to type the name of the app (e.g., "Facebook" or "Telegram")

Install
Tap the green Install button

Permissions
When you first open the app, tap "Allow" when prompted for necessary permissions (like Camera or Notifications).

## For iOS -iOS App Store

Open the App Store
Tap the blue "A" icon.

Create an Apple ID
Tap the Profile Icon and select Create New Apple Account.

o Enter your email, create a password, and select your region.

o Agree to the Terms and Conditions.

o Enter your personal details. For payment, you can select "None" if you only plan to download free apps.

Verify Your Identity
o Phone: Enter your number and type in the SMS code Apple sends you.

o Email: Check your Gmail for a 6-digit verification code from Apple and enter it on your phone.

Search & Download
Tap the Search tab (magnifying glass), type the app name, and tap Get.

Authenticate
Confirm the download using FaceID, TouchID, or your Apple ID password.

Launch
Once the progress circle is complete, tap Open or find the app on your Home Screen.
