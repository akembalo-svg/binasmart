## Safaricom Ethiopia – Voice over LTE (VoLTE)

Safaricom VoLTE lets you enjoy high-definition voice calls over our 4G network, offering crystal-clear sound and less background noise. With VoLTE, you can also continue using mobile data at full 4G speed while on a call, allowing you to browse, stream, or use apps without any interruption.

To access VoLTE, you need a Safaricom Ethiopia 4G SIM, a VoLTE-enabled 4G smartphone, and to be within a 4G coverage area. Both the caller and receiver must be using VoLTE-compatible devices on the Safaricom network.

## Advantages of VoLTE

### HD Voice Quality

VoLTE delivers high-definition voice, making conversations clearer and more natural with reduced background noise.

### Use 4G Data While Calling

You can browse the internet, use apps, or stream videos at full 4G speed during a voice call without interruption.

### Better Network Experience

VoLTE provides more stable calls with fewer drops, especially in areas with strong 4G coverage.

### Faster Call Connection

Calls connect almost instantly — no long waiting time when you press the call button.

### No Extra Cost

VoLTE uses your existing voice plan or bundle—no additional charges apply for this service.
