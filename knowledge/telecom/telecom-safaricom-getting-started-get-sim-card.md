###
Manual Option:

For Android devices, depending on your phone type and android version:

Go to 'Settings' -> click on 'Internet & Mobile Networks' or 'Mobile Networks' or 'Connections' or
More... or 'All Settings' or 'Advanced..'' or SIM Cards' -> click on Access Points Name (APN)

Click on 'Add' or '+' and enter the below details:

- Name: Safaricom

- APN: Safaricom

- Save and select the newly added APN and then switch on mobile data

For Apple devices, you can follow the below general steps:

Go to 'Settings' -> click on 'Cellular' -> 'Cellular Data Network' -> click on Access Points Names (APN)

Under Cellular Data, enter following detail:

- APN: Safaricom

- Settings will be saved, and service is ready for use
