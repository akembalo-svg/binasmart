### 💰 Salary Rights

- ✅ Salary paid monthly on agreed date

- ✅ Protected by Saudi Wage Protection System (WPS)

- ✅ Employer cannot deduct salary without legal justification

### 🏠 Accommodation & Living Standards

- ✅ Private, suitable accommodation — employer's obligation

- ✅ Meals or meal allowance must be provided

- ✅ Access to clean water, sanitation, and privacy

- ✅ Passport cannot be confiscated — illegal under Saudi law

- ✅ Worker must have freedom of movement on rest days

🚫 Passport confiscation is ILLEGAL in Saudi Arabia. Any employer holding a worker's passport commits a criminal offence. Workers should contact MLSD at 19911 or their embassy immediately.

### 🏥 Medical & Health Rights

- ✅ Employer must provide health insurance

- ✅ All medical treatment costs are employer's responsibility

- ✅ Work-related injuries — full treatment at employer's expense

- ✅ 30 days paid sick leave per year

### ✈️ End of Contract Rights

- ✅ Return air ticket — paid by employer

- ✅ End of service gratuity — 1 month salary per year after 2 years

- ✅ All outstanding wages settled before departure

- ✅ Proper Iqama cancellation completed by employer

### ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

##### Can a domestic worker resign in Saudi Arabia?

Yes — workers can resign with 1 month notice. If the employer has violated the contract (non-payment, abuse), the worker can terminate immediately with MLSD protection.

##### What if my employer doesn't pay my salary for months?

File an urgent complaint via Musaned or call MLSD at 19911. Saudi's WPS system flags unpaid salaries and MLSD can take immediate enforcement action.

##### Can my employer prevent me from leaving the house on my day off?

No — restricting freedom of movement on rest days is illegal. Workers can report this to MLSD or call the worker protection hotline.

##### Does Saudi law protect non-Muslim domestic workers?

Yes — Saudi domestic worker law applies to all workers regardless of religion or nationality. All protections apply equally.
