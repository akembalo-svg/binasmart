## Is It Legal to Hire Ethiopian Maids in UAE?

Yes. Ethiopia is an officially approved source country for domestic workers in the UAE. Hiring must go through a **MoHRE-authorized Tadbeer centre** or licensed recruitment agency. All workers must have a valid residence visa — hiring on tourist or visit visas is strictly illegal.

## Step-by-Step: How to Hire an Ethiopian Maid in UAE

### Step 1 — Contact a Tadbeer-Licensed Agency
Only use MoHRE-authorized Tadbeer centres or licensed agencies. Browse verified agencies on GCC Domestic: www.gccdomestic.com/en/agencies/

### Step 2 — Select a Candidate & Sign the Contract
Review profiles, conduct an interview, and sign the **MoHRE Unified Standard Employment Contract**. Verbal agreements are not legally valid.

### Step 3 — Apply for Entry Permit via GDRFA
Your Tadbeer centre submits the entry permit application. The worker can enter UAE within 60 days of approval.

### Step 4 — Ethiopian Government Clearance
Ethiopian workers require clearance from the **Ethiopian Ministry of Labor and Skills (MoLS)** before departure. Your Tadbeer centre handles this coordination.

### Step 5 — Worker Arrives & Medical Test
Medical fitness test must be completed within **30 days of arrival** — blood tests, chest X-ray, and health clearance.

### Step 6 — Emirates ID & Residence Visa
Biometric registration at ICP centre. Final 2-year residence visa stamped in passport.

### Step 7 — Register Contract with MoHRE
Submit the signed contract to MoHRE within 30 days of arrival. Verify status on the MoHRE portal.

> ⏱️ **Timeline:** 4–8 weeks from abroad. 2–4 weeks if worker is already in UAE.

## Legal Rights of Ethiopian Workers in UAE

Under Federal Decree-Law No. 9 of 2022, all domestic workers — regardless of nationality — are entitled to:

- **Annual leave:** 30 days after one year of service
- **Weekly rest day:** At least one day per week
- **Medical insurance:** Mandatory, paid by employer
- **End-of-service gratuity:** 14 days' salary per year
- **Right to resign:** After 6 months with 30 days' notice
- **Protection from abuse:** MoHRE complaint line: 600 590 000

> ⚠️ All forms of physical, verbal, or psychological abuse are criminal offences under UAE law.
