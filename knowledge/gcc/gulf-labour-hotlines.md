# Gulf labour authority hotlines for domestic workers

A domestic worker in trouble — unpaid wages, a withheld passport, abuse, being made to work elsewhere —
can call the labour authority of the country she is in. These lines are free from a local phone and most
answer in several languages. They are the labour authority, not the police: for immediate danger, the
emergency number comes first.

## United Arab Emirates — MOHRE (Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation)

- **+971 600 590000** — MOHRE call centre. The same number takes **WhatsApp** messages, which matters
  for a worker who has no credit but has internet.
- **80084** — the **labour claim** line, for a complaint about wages or a contract. Free from within the UAE.
- These two are different services and are not interchangeable: 600590000 is where to start, 80084 is for
  filing the claim itself.
- Police **999**, ambulance **998** — for immediate danger, before any labour line.

## Saudi Arabia — Musaned / Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development

- **19911** — the unified MHRSD line, also the Musaned line for domestic-worker contracts.

## Kuwait — PAM (Public Authority for Manpower)

- **1884880** — PAM's domestic labour line.

## Qatar — Ministry of Labour

- **16008** — the Ministry of Labour's complaint line.

## Bahrain — LMRA (Labour Market Regulatory Authority)

- **17506055** (from outside Bahrain: **+973 17506055**) — the LMRA line.

## Oman

- Ministry of Labour. No single hotline is published consistently in GCC Domestic's guides, so none is
  stated here rather than guessing one.

## Ethiopia — the embassy, and what is still missing

An Ethiopian worker abroad also has the right to complain **through Ethiopia**: to the Ministry of Labour
and Skills, in writing or verbally, and a relative up to the second degree may file it for her
(Proclamation 1389/2025, Article 53). She can also go to the Ethiopian mission in the country she is in.

**United Arab Emirates**

These are two different missions in two different cities, and the city code is what tells them apart —
Dubai is **04**, Abu Dhabi is **02**. Never give one city's number for the other, and when a caller is
inside the UAE give the local form starting 04 or 02; the +971 form is for calling from abroad.

- **Dubai — የኢትዮጵያ ቆንስላ ጄነራል (Ethiopian Consulate General), ዱባይ**:
  **04 269 9111** inside the UAE, **+971 4 269 9111** from abroad.
- **Abu Dhabi — የኢትዮጵያ ኤምባሲ (Ethiopian Embassy), አቡዳቢ**:
  **02 665 5111** inside the UAE, **+971 2 665 5111** from abroad.

በአማርኛ፦ የኢትዮጵያ ኤምባሲ አቡዳቢ ስልክ **02 665 5111** (ከውጭ ሲደወል +971 2 665 5111)፤
የኢትዮጵያ ቆንስላ ጄነራል ዱባይ ስልክ **04 269 9111** (ከውጭ ሲደወል +971 4 269 9111)።
ዱባይ 04፣ አቡዳቢ 02 — አንዱን በሌላው ቦታ አትስጥ።

**Not the embassy:** Ethiopian Airlines city and airport offices publish their own Dubai and Abu Dhabi
numbers, and they appear elsewhere in this knowledge base. An airline ticket office cannot help a worker
whose wages are unpaid or whose passport is held. When someone asks for the embassy or the consulate,
give the mission numbers above — never an airline office number.
