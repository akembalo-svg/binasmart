# Hiring an Ethiopian Housemaid in Bahrain (2026): LMRA Route, Costs & Steps

Short answer: hiring an Ethiopian housemaid in Bahrain runs through an office licensed by the LMRA (Labour Market Regulatory Authority). Expect a one-time recruitment cost of roughly BHD 900–1,400 (often including a refundable guarantee of around BHD 500) and a monthly salary of about BHD 90–130, with the whole process taking 4–8 weeks. Ethiopia is one of the most active corridors into Bahrain, so licensed offices usually have real candidates with verifiable files.

### The legal route: LMRA-licensed offices only

Every legal domestic-worker recruitment office in Bahrain operates under the LMRA. Before paying anything, ask the office for its CR / LMRA licence number and check that the name on the licence matches the name on your invoice. A legitimate office shares its licence without hesitation.

The paperwork on your side is straightforward: the office arranges the LMRA work permit and the worker's CPR residence card under your sponsorship, on the standard tripartite contract registered with the LMRA.

### What it costs (2026)

- One-time recruitment: roughly BHD 900–1,400 for the Ethiopian corridor — usually including the LMRA fees, flight and medical, and often a refundable guarantee of around BHD 500. Get the breakdown in writing.

- Monthly salary: about BHD 90–130 depending on experience; workers with previous Gulf contracts sit at the top of the range.

- Your obligations: food, accommodation, medical coverage and a return ticket home per the contract — budget these on top of the salary.

### The process, step by step

- Choose an LMRA-licensed office and agree the candidate profile (experience, language, skills).

- The office matches candidates through a licensed Ethiopian agency registered with Ethiopia's Ministry of Labour and Skills; you review files and interviews.

- Contract signing on the standard tripartite terms.

- Wafid (GAMCA) medical in Addis Ababa before travel.

- LMRA work permit + visa issuance, flight, and handover — typically 4–8 weeks end to end.

### Communication and settling in

Amharic and Oromo are the main first languages; many candidates speak basic Arabic, especially with previous Gulf experience. Ask the office specifically about language level and prior contracts — an experienced worker settles into a Bahraini household noticeably faster. Treat the first month as an investment: clear duties, patience and respect decide how the two years go.

### Red flags

- Cash requested outside the office's official payment channel.

- Prices far below the ranges above — corners are being cut somewhere.

- "Visit visa" arrangements — illegal for both sides, and the worker has no protection.

- Any suggestion that the office or family holds the worker's passport — it stays with her, by law.

### Frequently Asked Questions

#### How much does an Ethiopian housemaid cost in Bahrain?

Roughly BHD 900–1,400 one-time through an LMRA-licensed office (often including a refundable ~BHD 500 guarantee), plus a monthly salary of about BHD 90–130.

#### How long does hiring take?

Typically 4–8 weeks from signing: candidate matching, contract, Wafid medical in Addis Ababa, LMRA permit and travel.

#### Can I verify the recruitment office?

Yes — ask for the CR/LMRA licence number and check it against the official LMRA register at lmra.gov.bh before paying anything.

#### Does the worker pay any fee?

No. The employer pays the office. An office charging the worker — in Bahrain or Ethiopia — is breaking the rules of both countries.

#### What are my obligations as the employer?

The standard contract: salary paid on time, food, accommodation, medical coverage, a weekly rest day, and a return ticket. The worker keeps her own passport.
