On 29 September 2026, the National Bank of Ethiopia announced: "The presentation was followed by a panel that highlighted encouraging progress, including…" — as reported by its official Telegram channel @nbethiopia.

## What was posted

The presentation was followed by a panel that highlighted encouraging progress, including the expansion of retail lending by banks to previously underserved segments over the past five years. The discussion also focused on the opportunities ahead to strengthen data and coordination, improve interoperability, connect fragmented systems, and close persistent regional and gender gaps.

NFIS III represents an important step toward a financial system that works for more Ethiopians and supports broader, more inclusive economic participation.

Read the draft National Financial Inclusion Strategy III at nbe.gov.et/nfis.
