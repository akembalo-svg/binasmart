On 30 September 2026, the National Bank of Ethiopia announced: "Insurance markets play a critical role in protecting households and businesses,…" — as reported by its official Telegram channel @nbethiopia.

## What was posted

Insurance markets play a critical role in protecting households and businesses, mobilizing long-term capital, and strengthening economic resilience.

At the Ethiopia Finance Forum 2026, Belay Tulu, Director of Insurance Supervision at the National Bank of Ethiopia, opened the session with a presentation on the evolving insurance landscape and the reforms shaping its future.

A central focus was the proposed transition of insurance regulation from the National Bank of Ethiopia to a dedicated Ethiopian Insurance Regulatory Commission (EIRC). The discussion emphasized that this represents more than an institutional change. It is an opportunity to build a modern regulatory framework that strengthens supervision and consumer protection while supporting market development, sound governance, and capital formation.
