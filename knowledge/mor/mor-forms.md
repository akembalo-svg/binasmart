The Ministry's file name says it is based on Proclamation 1395/2017 E.C. (2025) and is to be used from Nehase 1, 2017 E.C.
ለምን ይጠቅማል፦ ከተከፋይ ሂሳብ ላይ የተቀነሰ ግብር ማሳወቂያ ቅጽ፦ ግብር ቀንሶ ገቢ አድራጊው ስም፣ TIN፣ የግብር ሒሳብ ቁጥር፣ የግብር ሰብሳቢ ጽ/ቤትና የታክስ ጊዜ፣ ክፍያዎችና የተቀነሰው ግብር። የሚኒስቴሩ የፋይል ስም በአዋጅ 1395/2017 መሠረት ከነሐሴ 1 ቀን 2017 ዓ.ም. ጀምሮ እንደሚሠራበት ይገልጻል።

### Withholding Tax On Payment Declaration

English name: Withholding tax on payment declaration (English)
Category: ከተከፋይ ሂሳብ ላይ የተቀነሰ ግብር ማሳወቂያ ቅፆች (Withholding Tax Declaration Forms)
What it is for: English version of the withholding tax on payment declaration: the withholder's details and withholding month, then each withholdee's TIN, name and address, the taxable amount, the tax withheld and the receipt number.
