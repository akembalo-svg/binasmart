Why building management in Ethiopia is still mostly manual

Ethiopia's commercial real estate has grown far faster than the tools used to manage it. In most buildings — even new towers — the daily reality looks like this:

  • Paper tenant registers. One notebook (or one Excel file on one laptop) holds every contract, deposit and phone number.
  • Missed renewals. Nobody notices a lease expiring until the tenant mentions it — or leaves.
  • Rent chasing by phone call. Every month the owner or a staff member calls tenants one by one.
  • One electricity meter, many tenants. Utility costs get split by argument instead of by measurement.
  • Lost visitors. A customer looking for an office on floor 7 asks three people before finding it — or gives up, which costs the tenant business.

Existing property software in Ethiopia mostly addresses the back office: rent ledgers and lease records. What has been missing is the front door — the building's public face — and communication that reaches tenants where they actually are: WhatsApp and Telegram, in Amharic.

What is a QR building directory?

A QR building directory turns the building entrance into a digital reception desk. It works like this:

  1. An A4 poster with a QR code hangs at the entrance.
  2. A visitor scans it with any phone camera. No app to install.
  3. The phone opens the building's live page: every shop and office, floor by floor, with photos, phone numbers, opening hours — and a green "open now" badge computed on Addis Ababa time.
  4. The visitor taps one button to call the shop, chat on WhatsApp or Telegram, or be walked to it in 3D.

You can try the real one now: the JJ Darule Building live directory is public, in Amharic and English, and updates the moment the owner's records change — because it is generated from the same database that runs the owner's dashboard, not a separate website that goes stale.

3D navigation with Amharic voice guidance

The part visitors remember is the 3D walking navigation. Choose any of the 71 tenants and a small guide figure walks you through a 3D model of the actual building — in through the entrance, to the elevators and stairs where they really are, up to the correct floor, to the unit door — while a voice speaks each step in Amharic: which way to turn, which floor to press, where the shop is.

For a G+10 building this changes daily life in small but constant ways: deliveries find offices, first-time customers don't give up, and the guard stops being a human directory.

💡 Why it matters for tenants: a shop on floor 8 pays the same rent as street level but gets a fraction of the walk-in traffic. Navigation that takes visitors straight to upper floors makes upper-floor space more valuable — which is ultimately the owner's revenue.

Accounting built for Ethiopian rules, not imported ones

Generic international property software does not know Ethiopian tax law. BinaSmart's accounting module is built around it:

  • VAT per Proclamation 1341/2024: 15% VAT on commercial rent, output-VAT and input-VAT tracking, and a printable monthly VAT return — matched to the filing deadline at the end of the following month.
  • TIN and bank details on every invoice, under the building's own name.
  • Staff payroll: guards, cleaners and facility staff paid monthly with one idempotent action (salaries are VAT-exempt and recorded as expenses).
  • Audit trail: every payment, reversal and change is logged — nothing silently disappears, which is exactly what a tax audit wants to see.
  • Late-payment penalties applied automatically per the contract, only after the owner switches them on.

Frequently asked questions

What is BinaSmart Ethiopia?

A QR-powered smart building management platform made for Ethiopia: a public digital directory with 3D voice navigation for visitors, and a full management dashboard (rent, VAT, payroll, sub-metering, maintenance, WhatsApp/Telegram notifications) for building owners — in Amharic and English.

Is BinaSmart Ethiopia related to "Bina Smart Business" (Romania)?

No. BinaSmart Ethiopia (bina.et) is an independent platform built for the Ethiopian market. Bina Smart Business is an unrelated Romanian retail-management ERP.

Do visitors need to install an app?

No. Scanning the entrance QR opens the directory in the phone's browser instantly — including the 3D navigation and Amharic voice guidance.

How do tenants receive invoices?

On WhatsApp or Telegram, sent under the building's own name: amount, due date, the building's bank accounts and a payment reference code. A bilingual e-receipt follows automatically when the payment is recorded.

Does it handle Ethiopian VAT?

Yes — 15% VAT per Proclamation 1341/2024, output/input VAT tracking, a printable monthly VAT return, expenses and staff payroll, with a full audit trail.
