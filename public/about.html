Who we are

BinaSmart is an Ethiopian company headquartered at the JJ Darule Building in Arada, Addis Ababa, founded by Ibrahim Kedir Bedru. We build everyday digital services for people in Ethiopia and for Ethiopians abroad who handle things back home.

We are a neutral platform. Cinemas, shops, insurers, dealers and agencies supply their own products and prices. BinaSmart does not invent a fare, a price or a seat, and every guide names the official source it was checked against.

Not to be confused with “Bina Smart Business”, a Romanian retail software company, or “Smartlife Bina” in Turkey. We are not related to either.

What we run

  • Ride — fixed upfront fares in Addis Ababa, no surge, no app to install; moto, bajaj, economy, comfort and XL. Also inside Telegram.
  • BinaPool — shared commute: up to four riders share one car and pay per seat, including women-only groups.
  • Drive with BinaSmart — drivers register free in Telegram, with 0% commission during launch and Amharic turn-by-turn navigation.
  • Cinema and Watch — tickets for Addis Ababa cinemas and events, and Ethiopian films online.
  • Business pages — a free page for any shop, café, restaurant or office, with products, prices and orders.
  • Building management — rent invoicing, VAT accounting, sub-metered utilities, maintenance and tenant notices, used in our own JJ Darule Building.
  • Tenders, Amharic news and Digital Ethiopia guides — Fayda, telebirr, passport, TIN, VAT, business registration, customs and more.
  • Bini, plus Dr Afiya for the health system and Asmat for legal procedure — assistants that explain where to go and what to bring. They do not diagnose or give legal advice.
