ከኦገስት 28 እስከ ሴፕቴምበር 22 ቀን 2026 (ነሐሴ 22 ቀን 2018 – መስከረም 12 ቀን 2019 ዓ.ም) ቢናስማርት የሰበሰባቸውን 340 የጨረታ ማስታወቂያዎች ተንትነናል፦ ማን እንደሚገዛ፣ ምን እንደሚገዛ፣ ለመወዳደር ስንት ቀን እንዳለ፣ ምን ያህል እንደሚያስከፍል።

ምንጩ በአብዛኛው (325 ከ340) የሪፖርተር ጋዜጣ የጨረታ ገጾች ናቸው። የፌዴራል መንግሥት ጨረታዎች በeGP ስለሚቀርቡ በዚህ ሪፖርት በአብዛኛው አልተካተቱም — ዘዴውና ገደቦቹ ከታች ተገልጸዋል።

🇬🇧 English summary

BinaSmart analysed 340 unique tender notices first seen between 28 August and 22 September 2026, mostly from The Reporter's tender pages.

  • Who buys: NGOs, churches and UN agencies posted 37% of notices and banks, insurers and microfinance institutions 33%; private companies 18%; government bodies and state enterprises (including 10 World Bank-financed projects) 9%. Federal bodies must take bids through the eGP system, so most federal tenders are outside this set.
  • What: supply 57%, services 17%, consultancy 11%, construction 8%, asset disposal 6%.
  • Time: the median time to bid is 10 days from first sighting; 30% of notices close within a week and only 11% allow 21 days or more.
  • Cost: the median bid-document fee is ETB 300 (most often 500); 46% of notices require bid security, and across 70 hand-checked amounts the median is ETB 100,000 (half between 50,000 and 300,000).
  • Submission: at least 42% ask for sealed envelopes; only 8 notices mention eGP.

🔬 ዘዴ · Methodology

  • Source: BinaSmart's daily tender harvest: 325 notices via The Reporter's tender pages, 12 World Bank procurement notices, 2 from Ethiopian Airlines and 1 court notice.
  • Period: notices first seen 28 August – 22 September 2026 (Addis Ababa time), including notices that have since closed. 13 notices hidden from the site while still open were excluded.
  • De-duplication: one notice per buyer + title (340 unique notices).
  • Buyer type: each of the 249 buyer names was assigned to one of the five groups with a hand-built list, checked name by name; 5 notices with an unclear buyer are not shown in the chart.
  • Days to bid: closing date minus the date BinaSmart first saw the notice; 294 notices with a closing date. Notices seen late would make these periods look shorter than they were.
  • Bid security: amounts were extracted from the notice text and every one was checked by hand; bid-document fees and percentage-based requirements were excluded; for multi-lot notices the first lot's amount is used.
  • Limits: this is not the whole Ethiopian procurement market. Federal eGP tenders, regional gazettes and tenders not published online are largely missing. The stored text is a summary of each notice, so shares of documents and submission methods are minimums. Weekly counts are not shown because the first week includes the harvester's backlog.
