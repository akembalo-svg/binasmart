Every extra seat lowers your price and raises the driver's income, so everybody wants a full car. Prices come from the same fixed-fare engine as a solo ride; they never change with traffic or rain.

እንዴት ይሠራል? How it works

1ማቆሚያ ይምረጡPick your stop

Open Ride, tap ጋራ ጉዞ · Pool. We show the corridor nearest you and its stops. Walk to the stop; the car follows the line, it does not zigzag.

2አሁን ሂድ ወይም ጠብቅGo now, or wait to pay less

"Go now" leaves with whoever is in the car at today's price for that count. "Wait" holds up to 8 minutes while others join and your price drops.

3ታርጋውን ያረጋግጡMatch the plate, pay your seat

You see the driver's name, car and plate before boarding, and the car live on the map. Pay only your seat, in cash to the driver.
