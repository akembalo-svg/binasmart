Document checkየሰነድ ማረጋገጫ

Paste the text of a licence, certificate or official letter — or photograph it with your phone, or upload its PDF. We check what the document claims — the law it stands on, the fees it quotes, the office that issued it, its dates and links — against our library of official Ethiopian sources. የሰነዱን ጽሑፍ ይለጥፉ፣ በስልክዎ ፎቶ ያንሱ ወይም PDF ይስቀሉ። ሰነዱ የጠቀሰው አዋጅ፣ ክፍያ፣ ሰጪ ተቋምና ቀን ከኦፊሴላዊ ምንጮች ጋር ይመሳከራል።

⚠️ This is a consistency check, not proof. It cannot see paper, seals or signatures, and a forged document that quotes the right law will pass. Confirm authenticity with the office that issued the document.
