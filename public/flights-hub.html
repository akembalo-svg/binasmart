Questions · ጥያቄዎች

Can I pay with telebirr?

Yes — for Ethiopian Airlines. Their website accepts telebirr and CBE Birr. Other airlines listed here need an international card, so for those use a partner agency instead.

በtelebirr መክፈል እችላለሁ?

አዎ — ለኢትዮጵያ አየር መንገድ። ድረ ገጻቸው telebirr እና CBE ብር ይቀበላል። ሌሎቹ ዓለም አቀፍ ካርድ ስለሚፈልጉ በወኪል በኩል ይሂዱ።

Does BinaSmart issue the ticket?

No. A licensed travel agency issues your ticket, or you buy direct from the airline. BinaSmart connects you and does not hold your money.

Is the price final?

No price on this page is final. Fares change until the ticket is issued — the agency or the airline confirms the real price before you pay.
