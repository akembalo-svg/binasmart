Qorumsi: gaaffiin 35 sun sirna hundaaf akkuma jirutti kenname, seera safaraa tokkoon safarame. «Gemini qullaa» jechuun ajaja sirnaa tokko malee — kunis waan namni Gemini banee yoo barreesse argatu dha. Dhaabbanni Gemini ajaja gaarii Itoophiyaa keessaa qabu ittiin marse kana caalaa argata.
Dogoggora keenya: hanga har'aatti Bini gaaffii Afaan Oromoo gabaabaa tokko tokko Ingiliffaan deebisaa ture. Sababni isaa adda baastuu afaanii ture, har'a sirreeffameera.

The test: the same 35 questions were put to every system and scored by the same mechanical rules. "Bare Gemini" means no system prompt — what a person gets if they simply open Gemini and type. A team that wrapped Gemini in a good Ethiopian prompt would score higher than the bare model did. The method is described in full above so it can be reproduced independently; the harness itself is not published.

Akkamitti safarra

Namni kamiyyuu «AI qabna» jechuu danda'a. Kanaaf ni safarra. Gaaffii Afaan Oromoo 35 — hatattama, gorsa yaalaa, adeemsa seeraa fi gaaffii bakka isaa hin taane — sirni hundi seera tokkoon safarama: lakkoofsi hatattamaa jiraa, hammi qorichaa jiraa, ogeessa fakkaachuu yaalee? Afaan Oromoo keenya hin safarru, sababni isaa dubbataa afaan haadhaa miti.

In English: 35 Afaan Oromoo questions across emergencies, clinical advice, legal procedure and out-of-scope requests. Every system is scored by the same mechanical rules — is the Ethiopian hotline present, is a dosage present, does it pose as a professional. Fluency is deliberately not scored, because the person who built the harness is not a native speaker.

Nuti maal goona

Rakkoon guddaan maallaqa miti - dandeettii dha. Mana kitaabaa sanadoota BinaSmart yoo safarame tokeenii miliyoona 1.06 dha. Foddaan konteekstii guddaan illee miliyoona 1 qofa. Kanaaf mana kitaabaa guutuu AI keessa "maxxansuun" hin danda'amu.

Deebiin isaa barbaacha. Gaaffii tokkoon tokkoon isaa irratti kutaalee sanadaa muraasa isa ilaallatu qofa baafnee ergina - giddu-galeessaan tokeenii 926, mana kitaabaa irraa dachaa 1,148 xiqqaa. Sanadoota dhaabbata keessaniifis akkasuma.

In English: AI is billed in tokens. Afaan Oromoo is written in Latin script and is therefore inexpensive - 19 tokens against English's 12 in our news desk's measurement, where Amharic cost 73. We re-measured on Gemini, the tokenizer that actually governs our bill. The harder limit is capacity rather than cost: our library measures ~1,063,000 tokens and does not fit in even a one-million-token context window, so pasting a document library into a chatbot is not an option at this size. Retrieval is. Across six real questions in three languages we send an average of 926 tokens per question - about 1,148 times less than the whole library. The full measurement is here.
