Application for registration for VAT

ለተጨማሪ እሴት ታክስ (ቫት) ለመመዝገብ የሚቀርብ ማመልከቻ። የግብር ከፋዩ ስም፣ TIN፣ የንግድ ስም፣ አድራሻ፣ ስልክና ኢሜይል፣ ንግዱ የተጀመረበት ቀን፣ የሥራው ዓይነትና የድርጅቱ ሕጋዊ ሁኔታ ይሞላበታል። በሁለት ቅጂ ይሞላል፤ ቅጹ የቀድሞው የኢትዮጵያ ገቢዎችና ጉምሩክ ባለሥልጣን ስም ያለበት ስካን ነው።

Registers a business for value added tax. It asks for the taxpayer's name, TIN and trade name, address (region, city, sub-city, woreda, kebele, house number), P.O. box, telephone, fax and e-mail, the date business commenced, the type of business activities, and the business legal status (public enterprise, share company, private limited company, joint venture, sole proprietor, cooperative, partnership, other). This scan still carries the former Ethiopian Revenues and Customs Authority heading and says to fill it in duplicate.

VAT and buyer-withheld VAT declaration form (with detailed transaction schedule)

የተጨማሪ እሴት ታክስ እና በገዢው ተይዞ የሚከፈል የተ.እ.ታ ማሳወቂያ ቅጽ ከዝርዝር የግብይት መረጃ መግለጫ ጋር። የመጀመሪያ ገጹ የሚኒስቴሩ ደብዳቤ ነው፤ በአዋጅ 1157/2011 እና በመመሪያ 60/2012 መሠረት የተሻሻለው ቅጽ ከመጋቢት 1 ቀን 2012 ዓ.ም. ጀምሮ በSIGTAS ላይ ተግባራዊ እንደሚሆን ይገልጻል።

The VAT declaration that also reports VAT withheld by the buyer. Its first page is the Ministry's cover letter: after Proclamation 1157/2011 amended VAT Proclamation 285/1994 and Directive 60/2012 on VAT withheld by the buyer was issued, the VAT declaration form and the detailed transaction schedule were changed, to be used from Megabit 1, 2012 E.C. on SIGTAS; the changes include the taxpayer's VAT withholding, the value of capital goods, and a 50% withholding of the VAT on transactions.

የተጨማሪ እሴት ታክስ ማሳወቂያ (ቫት ሪተርን)፦ ያሳወቁበት ቀን፣ ዋና ወይም የተከለሰ ማሳወቂያ፣ የግብር ጊዜ፣ የግብር ከፋዩ ስም፣ TIN እና የቫት ምዝገባ ቁጥር፣ እንዲሁም ማሳወቂያው በታክስ ወኪል ከተዘጋጀ የወኪሉ መረጃ።

The periodic VAT return: date of filing, return type (original or revised), the tax period, the registered taxpayer's name, TIN, sub-TIN and VAT registration number, and details of the tax agent when an agent prepared the return.

VAT Withholding Tax Declaration

VAT withholding tax declaration (English)

ቫት ቀንሶ የሚያስቀር ሰው ወይም ድርጅት የሚያቀርበው ማሳወቂያ (በእንግሊዝኛ)፦ የቀናሹ መረጃ፣ የወሩ ጊዜ፣ ለእያንዳንዱ ክፍያ የተከፋዩ TIN፣ ስምና አድራሻ፣ ግብር የሚከፈልበት መጠን፣ የተቀነሰው ታክስና የደረሰኝ ቁጥርና ቀን።

Filed by a person or organisation that withholds VAT: withholder's name, address, TIN, tax account number, tax centre and withholding month, then for each payment the withholdee's TIN, name and address, the taxable amount, the tax withheld, and the receipt number and date.

የታክስ ከፋይ መለያ ቁጥር ቅፆችTax Identification Number Forms

የግብር ከፋይ መለያ ቁጥር ለመመለስ የሚቀርብ ማመልከቻ ቅጽ

TIN de-registration request (returning the TIN certificate)

ንግድ ሥራ ሲቋረጥ የግብር ከፋይ መለያ ቁጥር (TIN) የምስክር ወረቀቱን ለመመለስ የሚቀርብ ማመልከቻ። የግብር ከፋዩ ስም፣ TIN፣ የተ.እ.ታ ቁጥር (ካለ)፣ ስልክ፣ ንግዱ የቆመበት ምክንያትና ቀን፣ የባለቤቱ ወይም የዋና ሥራ አስኪያጁ ስም፣ ፊርማና ማህተም ይሞላበታል፤ የውዝፍ ግብርና ታክስ እዳ ማጣራት (ክሊራንስ) ክፍልም አለው።

Used when a business stops: asks the Ministry to take back the TIN certificate. It asks for the taxpayer's name, TIN, VAT number (if any) and phone, the reason for ceasing business and the date it ceased, the owner's or general manager's name, signature and seal, and has a tax clearance section (tax arrears) that the tax office completes.

በግብር ከፋይነት ለመመዝገብ (ድርጅት) የሚቀርብ ማመመልከቻ ቅፅ

Non-individual (organisation) taxpayer registration application

ድርጅትን በግብር ከፋይነት ለመመዝገብ የሚቀርብ ማመልከቻ። የቀድሞ ምዝገባ መረጃ (TIN፣ ሕጋዊና የንግድ ስም፣ የንግድ ፈቃድ ቁጥር)፣ የአዲሱ ድርጅት መረጃና ሕጋዊ አደረጃጀት (ኃ/የተ/የግል ማህበር፣ አክሲዮን ማህበር፣ የሽርክና ማህበር፣ የሕብረት ሥራ ማህበር ወዘተ) ይሞላበታል። የመጀመሪያ ገጹ አዲስ TIN ሰርተፍኬት ለማውጣት የሚያስፈልጉ ሰነዶችን ይዘረዝራል፤ ለምሳሌ በውልና ማስረጃ የፀደቀ መመስረቻ ጽሁፍ፣ የዋና ሥራ አስኪያጁ ፎቶና የታደሰ መታወቂያ፣ የባለአክሲዮኖች TIN እና የፋይዳ ቁጥር፣ በድርጅቱ ስም የፀደቀ የቤት ኪራይ ውል።

Registers a company or other organisation as a taxpayer: past registration details (earlier TIN, legal and trade name, commercial licence and principal registration numbers), then the new business details and legal status (private limited company, share company, joint venture, partnership, holding company, public enterprise, cooperative, NGO, religious organisation, micro and small enterprise, diplomatic mission, other). Its first page lists what to bring for a new TIN certificate, including the name registration, the memorandum of association authenticated by the documents registration office, recent photos and a renewed resident ID of the general manager, the shareholders' TIN and Fayda numbers, and an authenticated lease in the company's name.

Registers an individual as a taxpayer. Type of application: new, change of details, biometric, or replacement TIN card. It asks for the existing TIN (if any), full name with father's and grandfather's name, date of birth (Gregorian or Ethiopian calendar), country of residence, nationality and country of birth, work permit number for foreigners, occupation (student, employed, trader), home address, main business address and the tax centre.

For a change of address or name its first page lists: the completed form with the organisation's seal, an authenticated copy of the minutes the members agreed, a copy of the main registration and trade licence changed to the new address, the original TIN and VAT certificates, an authenticated copy of the lease, the bank account number and branch, and a direct line telephone number.

Reports VAT that a buyer deducted and paid to the tax authority under the reverse-taxation rule for services received from non-residents: the taxpayer's TIN, name, address and VAT number, the declaration period, and for each transaction the type of service, the value before VAT and the VAT withheld and paid. The Amharic heading cites Article 23(4) and 21(1) of VAT Proclamation 285/1995 E.C.; the English heading cites Article 23(4) of VAT Proclamation 1341/2016 E.C. The Ministry's own file name marks it as the old-proclamation version.
