ባንኮች ስንት CGPA ይጠይቃሉ?

ከ2.50 እስከ 3.50፤ በብዛት 3.00። ቢያንስ 7 ባንኮች ለሴቶች ከ0.25–0.50 ዝቅ ያለ ነጥብ ያስቀምጣሉ።

የመውጫ ፈተና ያስፈልጋል?

ለአዲስ ተመራቂዎች በአብዛኛው አዎ፤ አንዳንዶቹ 50 እና ከዚያ በላይ ይጠይቃሉ።

የዕድሜ ገደብ አለ?

ለተመራቂ ሥልጠና ብዙ ጊዜ 25–30 ዓመት እና ከዚያ በታች።

ማስታወቂያው ስንት ቀን ይቆያል?

8 ከ10 በሳምንት ውስጥ ይዘጋሉ።

ደመወዙ ስንት ነው?

ማስታወቂያዎቹ አይገልጹም፤ «በባንኩ ስኬል» ብቻ ይላሉ።

የትኛው የትምህርት ዘርፍ በብዛት ይፈለጋል?

ማኔጅመንት፣ አካውንቲንግ፣ ኢኮኖሚክስና ቢዝነስ አስተዳደር፤ ከዚያ IT፣ ባንኪንግና ፋይናንስ እንዲሁም ምህንድስና።

ዘዴ · Method: BinaSmart's daily harvest of seven Ethiopian job boards. Set: job adverts from employers whose name contains "bank", first seen between 1 July and 22 September 2026 (World Bank and African Development Bank excluded). Name variants of the same bank were merged and duplicates (same bank + same title) removed: 575 unique adverts from 32 banks. Role shares are read from titles; degree shares only from the education sentence (520 adverts that have one). Deadlines: 249 adverts carried a deadline 31–60 days after first sighting, almost always exactly 40–41 days: ETCareers gives every advert that default closing date regardless of the employer's deadline, so they were excluded, leaving 229 with a real closing date. The requirement table quotes the adverts' own text. Coverage reflects what the boards publish, not every vacancy a bank fills. Please credit BinaSmart.
