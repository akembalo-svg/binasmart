How to write a CV for Ethiopian jobs

A recruiter looks at your CV for a few seconds before deciding. This page shows you what to put in those seconds, with a one-page sample you can copy.

🇪🇹 በአማርኛ ያንብቡ →

Hundreds of applications arrive for one post. Nobody reads them all closely: the first pass is fast. So your CV has one job — to show at a glance that you fit this post.

That takes no design skill. It takes a clear order, true details and a clean file.

📌 The seven sections

  1. Name and contact — full name, a phone number that works, an email address, and the city you live in. Nothing else.
  2. Short summary — two sentences: who you are and what you can do.
  3. Work experience — newest first. For each: job title, employer, dates, and two or three things you actually did.
  4. Education — field, institution, year.
  5. Skills — software and practical abilities, not "hard working".
  6. Languages — Amharic, English, Afaan Oromo and the level of each.
  7. References — two people with name, role and phone. Ask them first.
Leave out: marital status, religion or ethnicity, your life story, salary expectations (unless asked), and your ID number.

🧾 The format Ethiopian employers expect

  • Reverse chronological. Your most recent job first, because that is what an employer checks first.
  • One page for a fresh graduate, two if you have real experience.
  • Black text on white. Colour blocks and decorative templates usually hurt.
  • No photo unless the advert asks for one.
  • Send a PDF. A Word file re-flows on someone else's computer and your layout breaks.

English or Amharic?

Follow the advert. Many vacancies on bina.et — especially NGOs and banks — say plainly "upload a cover letter and an updated CV in English". If the advert does not say, English is the safer choice, though plenty of local employers are happy with Amharic.

✍️ A one-page sample

This is a sample. The name and numbers are invented — put in your own.

Selam Abebe
Addis Ababa · 0900 000 045 · selam.abebe@example.com

SUMMARY
Sales and customer service professional with two years of experience.
I organise sales data in Excel and prepare the monthly report.

EXPERIENCE
Sales Assistant — Abyssinia Trading PLC
September 2024 – present
· Track daily sales for three shops
· Prepare the monthly sales report for the manager
· Built a customer record system

Cashier — Meseret Supermarket
July 2023 – August 2024
· Served more than 120 customers a day
· Balanced and handed over the daily takings

EDUCATION
Diploma in Marketing — Addis Ababa University, 2023

SKILLS
Excel · Word · Sales record keeping · Customer service

LANGUAGES
Amharic — native · English — good · Afaan Oromo — basic

REFERENCES
Mr Dawit Tesfaye — Manager, Abyssinia — 0900 000 046
Mrs Senait Girma — Supervisor, Meseret — 0900 000 047

The difference is in the wording

Weak: "I worked in the sales department."
Strong: "Tracked daily sales for three shops and prepared the monthly report."

The second says what you did; the first says only where you were. Add numbers wherever you honestly can: how many shops, how many customers, over how many months.

🚫 Nine common mistakes

  1. A phone number that does not work. If one call fails, the recruiter moves on. Check it twice.
  2. A childhood email address. Open one with your name in it.
  3. No dates. "I worked at…" is not enough — say from when to when.
  4. One CV for every advert. Reuse the advert's own words.
  5. Spelling mistakes. Have someone read it before you send.
  6. A photo nobody asked for.
  7. References who were never told. An unprepared referee damages you.
  8. A file called "CV final final2.pdf". Name it Selam-Abebe-CV.pdf.
  9. A photograph of a printed CV. Blurry scans get dropped. Send a PDF.

✉️ The cover letter

Many adverts ask for a short letter with the CV. Three paragraphs is enough.

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am applying for the Sales Assistant position advertised by your company.

For the past two years I have worked in sales and customer service, organising
sales data and preparing the monthly report for management. I believe this
experience fits the post.

My CV is attached. You can reach me on 0900 000 045.

Kind regards,
Selam Abebe

🌍 If you are going abroad for work

For overseas employment a CV is not enough. Recruitment must go through the LMIS system, and you need a Labor ID and a COC certificate of competence.

The rule to remember: under Proclamation 1389/2025, an agency may not charge the worker recruitment fees. If someone asks you for money, that is not lawful.

The details are in our guides: Labor ID and LMIS · COC certificate.

❓ Questions people ask

Should my CV be in English or Amharic?

Follow the advert. NGOs and banks usually ask for English; many local employers accept Amharic. If it does not say, send English.

What do I write if I have no work experience?

Your final-year project, internships, volunteer work, a family business you helped run, and the software you can use. Experience is not only paid work.

Should I put a photo on my CV?

Not unless the advert asks for one.

How many pages?

One for a fresh graduate, two with long experience.

CV or resume — what is the difference?

In Ethiopia they mean the same document.

Can I make a CV using only my phone?

Yes — answer the questions on a job page, or speak them in Telegram. You get a PDF.

Why do I never hear back?

Hundreds apply for one post. Tailoring each application, meeting deadlines and applying widely improve your chances, but a good CV cannot guarantee a reply.

A recruiter gives your CV a few seconds — but you decide what is in those seconds. Write one page today and you are ready for tomorrow's advert.
