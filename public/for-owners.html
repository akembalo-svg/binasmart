ግልጽ ነንStraight answers

  • Free during launch: no setup fee, no monthly fee, no commission. We will tell you in writing before that changes.
  • No online payment on BinaSmart yet: customers pay you directly, at your counter, table or door. BinaSmart does not deliver food.
  • Every change you make reaches our team, which can take down a page that breaks the rules: fake listings, false claims such as "guaranteed cure", or someone else's business.
  • Your own mobile is shown only where you chose it as the number for customers. A doctor's licence number is never shown.
  • Questions: bina.et/support, or ask Bini on any page.
