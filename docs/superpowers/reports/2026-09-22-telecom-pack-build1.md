## 2. Excluded, and why

- Ethio telecom: 517 pages, 163 kept. Kept on disk but not indexed: the 15 Oromo, Somali and Tigrinya pages (Oromo comes later). Out of scope: about 58 English enterprise-IT, media, shopping and corporate pages. No text in the HTML (JavaScript): `4gcoverage`, `fbb`, `our-shops-2`, `personal-packages`, `harar-5g-launch`, so the shop locator and the 4G coverage map are not in the pack. About 170 Ethiopic-slug Amharic pages are copies of kept pages; about 45 more are older or duplicate translations (two kept because they hold something unique: the Umrah roaming package and the 2017 tariff-revision notice).
- Telebirr overlap: all telebirr, mobile-money, credit and saving paths are denied by name and tested. One overlap found (Amharic general FAQ embeds the telebirr FAQ, containment 0.99); a `dedupAgainstPacks` option removed 30 paragraphs banking already holds word for word. About 25 telebirr-family pages (Siket Bank terms, sinq and enderas terms, promos, device finance) are in neither pack: a follow-up for banking.
- Safaricom: 6 soft-404 pages, company pages, `business-faq` and `privacy-policy` (no text in the HTML), `device-offers` (thin).
- ECA: index and empty pages, login page, consultation notices, the Ethio telecom and Safaricom wholesale offers (52-256 thousand characters each), superseded 2023 draft fee directives, press notices, organogram.
- Correction to the harvest README: the Amharic PDFs 1024/2017 and the draft consumer code do NOT need OCR (16,404 and 18,175 Ethiopic characters read by eye). Only Regulation 585/2026 has no text layer.
- Hand-written documents already in the index stay (`law/telecom-consumer-complaint-eca-directive-832-2021`, `law/stolen-phone-police-report-sim-block`, an abridged 1321/2024).
- `curatedHosts` now claims `safaricom.et` and `eca.et`; `ethiotelecom.et` stays with banking.

## 3. Rendering fixes (all opt-in per site, other packs unchanged)

- Safaricom prices were deleted by the shared "repeated on 15% of pages is template" rule: `keepNumericParagraphs`.
- Package cards read as unrelated lines: `htmlPrep` joins each card as `100 MB - 5 ETB`.
- 19 price pages were "thin" (site `minChars` 150) and 18 Amharic price pages were called English (site `amFloor` 80).
- Two-column gazettes (Amharic left, English right; 2019 Amharic font unreadable): `pdfCrop` reads the English column (4,902 of 4,926 words survive for 1148/2019, 5,842 of 5,847 for 1321/2024).
- Text-layer PDFs were capped at 60,000 characters: `pdfSplit` reads them whole; `pdfLangFromText` labels Amharic PDFs.
- `maskPhones` now masks the local 09 form; a dialling example with a digit stuck to a number is separated by `htmlPrep`.
- 34 documents read by eye. Known flaws: `international-visitors-plan` puts the currency unit on its own line; `privacy-policy` is WordPress default text (`app-privacy-policy` is the real one); the 1024/2017 Amharic fee tables wrap cells (figures present, column association not guaranteed); package tables drawn as pictures are not in the HTML and every header says so.

## 4. OCR

Only Regulation 585/2026 (16 pages) on the server with PyMuPDF at 300 dpi and Tesseract 5.3.4 (system `amh` model, not `tessdata_best`), tool `ops/packs/ocr-pdfs.py`, no external service. English mean word confidence 88.5, Amharic column 85.4, 95.8% valid Ethiopic syllables. Checked against the page image (Articles 7-9): levy 1.5% of gross annual revenue, three-year grace period for new entrants, collection within three months. Not verified: the late-payment figures, the fiscal-year dates, every figure in the Amharic column. The document states in both languages that a machine read it. Not in the pack: the Amharic column of 1148/2019 (unreadable font) and of 1321/2024.
